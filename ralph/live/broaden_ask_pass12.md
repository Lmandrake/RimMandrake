# Graffiti for RimWorld 1.6: what haven't I imagined? (pass 12)

The mod: colonists paint marks on walls (tags, throw-ups, stencils, pasted posters, pieces, tallies, crude vandal scrawls, ideoligion sigils, and "subject" marks: a mockery of a named colonist with their painted head, a tribute to one, a memorial to the dead). A mark is filth drawn in the plane of the wall face and climbing onto the wall top. Pawns who see a mark get mood and opinion effects (capped: ordinary wall art at most +3 mood total); a mockery's subject, their friends, the Kind and the subject's enemies react differently. Pawns answer marks: scrub it, cross it out, paint a reply, or confront the painter (words, or blows with a chance scaled by the confronter's temper). Raiders leaving tag the outer wall; visitors read insults to their faction once per visit (goodwill capped). Art skill sets craft (awful to legendary): Beauty -6 to +30, a "clumsy paint" or "good work" thought, and the look (awful is muddy and smeared double; from this pass good+ pieces get a 3D block shadow, masterwork+ get shine glints). Personality: psychopaths feel nothing, sensitive people feel more, clean freaks are troubled and can break into compulsively scrubbing. The player has a Paint order and a Scrub graffiti order (drag over walls); a wall the player had scrubbed is left alone by the colony's own painters for 10 days.

## The owner's bars (draft, his words in quotes)
- A mockery names someone, hurts or amuses the right people, and changes how they regard its painter.
- A tribute can warm someone, make someone jealous, or annoy an enemy; a memorial comes from someone actually grieving and helps them feel better.
- Pawns answer what is on the wall: the player sees the scrub, the cross-out, the reply or the confrontation finish.
- A raid's mark left standing troubles the colony; taking the wall back ends that claim.
- Visitors read an insult to their faction; one passing trader never charges the same wall twice.
- "Graffiti makes occasional stories in an ordinary colony. It must never become a permanent mood tax or a stream of messages."
- A fight has people and a reason; paint never manufactures violence from nothing or bypasses a pawn's restrictions.
- Works alone without an ideoligion, survives saving, stops when its settings say it stops.
- Making a mark is its own fun (small mood buff, counts as recreation). Rereads affect mood at most once a day; opinion changes once.
- "A poor artist will paint such a bad graffiti mark that it is ugly... a highly skilled artist is actually making art. It is beautiful."
- Psychopaths don't care; sensitive people feel more both ways; clean freaks take extra debuffs and can break into compulsive cleaning.
- Visual: marks appear, read as deliberate punk/urban paint at play zoom (64 px per cell), sit on the wall in its perspective, vary, show their ugliness, never read as dirt, contain no real-world English.

## What I have tested and how
Two colonies on game B with only this mod, its bridge and all DLC.
- "Ashgate": a quicktest colony with a hand-built base (bedrooms, corridor, hall, kitchen, workshop), six colonists, run 9+ in-game days. One painting spree forced by me; everything else unscripted. Measured over two unstaged days after a cleanup (ticks 517k-637k): 2 messages, about 6 log lines, marks 45 to 60, one vanilla mental break (a Tortured Artist's insulting spree). The vandal Mo does not spiral (mood 82%): -13 from walls, of which a 3-day humiliation expires. Shots attached: the bedrooms and hall at play zoom, the spree corridor, the scrub order.
- "Rustwall": a staged colony I set up with forced relations and traits; it is in bad shape (starvation, a raid, two dead). Seen there: a psychopath mocked with no thought; a Volatile pawn "Mocked on the wall by Brandt -13" who then scrubbed it and painted an awful mockery back unprompted; a lover's jealousy of a tribute (message); a memorial by a grieving friend easing +1 against "My friend Pell died -2". Earlier passes: a fight with blows between a Brawler+Bloodlust pawn and the mocker, with a message naming both and the reason; visitors' goodwill -12 once for two insults; a raid exit tag that troubled the colony until cleaned; one save/load mid-feud.
- Visual: six wall materials in daylight at play zoom and 128 px (attached), side faces carry a vertical scrap, corners and doors stop the paint. A craft ladder of one piece per craft (attached, 40 px per cell).

Not seen on this build: the scrub hold over days, classic mode (no ideoligion at all), the clean-freak fit on screen, making-a-mark joy on the Needs tab, a reread a day later, settings switched off live, save/reload, a raid mark retaken, visitors, children/prisoners/slaves/guests/other xenotypes.
