# Graffiti for RimWorld 1.6: what haven't I imagined?

The mod: colonists paint marks on walls (tags, throw-ups, stencils, pieces, tallies, crude vandal scrawls, ideoligion sigils, and "subject" marks: a mockery of a named colonist, a tribute to one, a memorial to the dead). A mark is filth drawn on the wall face (and, by the owner's pick, climbing onto the wall top). Other pawns who see a mark get mood and opinion effects; the subject of a mockery, their friends, the Kind, enemies of the subject all react differently. Pawns answer marks: scrub it, cross it out, paint a reply, or confront the painter (words, or rarely blows, the chance scaled by the confronter's temper). Raiders leaving tag your outer wall; visitors read insults to their faction once per visit. Art skill sets the craft (awful to masterwork), which sets Beauty and a "good work / clumsy paint" thought. Personality: psychopaths feel nothing, sensitive people feel more, clean freaks are troubled by marks and can break into compulsively scrubbing them.

## The owner's bars (draft, his words in quotes)
- A mockery names someone, hurts or amuses the right people, and changes how they regard its painter.
- A tribute can warm someone, make someone jealous, or annoy an enemy; a memorial comes from someone actually grieving and helps them feel better.
- Pawns answer what is on the wall: the player sees the scrub, the cross-out, the reply or the confrontation finish.
- A raid's mark left standing troubles the colony; taking the wall back ends that claim.
- Visitors read an insult to their faction; one passing trader never charges the same wall twice.
- "Graffiti makes occasional stories in an ordinary colony. It must never become a permanent mood tax or a stream of messages."
- A fight has people and a reason behind it; paint never manufactures violence from nothing or bypasses a pawn's restrictions.
- Works alone without an ideoligion, survives saving, stops when its settings say it stops.
- Making a mark is its own fun (small mood buff, counts as recreation). Rereads affect mood at most once a day; opinion changes once.
- "A poor artist will paint such a bad graffiti mark that it is ugly... a highly skilled artist is actually making art."
- Plus visual bars: marks appear, read as deliberate punk/urban paint at play zoom (64 px per cell), sit on the wall in its perspective, vary, show their ugliness, never read as dirt or contain real-world English.

## What I have tested, and how (all in one staged colony: a quicktest map with a hand-built base of bedrooms, corridor, hall, workshop; 8 colonists with traits I assigned)
- Mockery: forced relations (I set opinions with a hook), then let a pawn paint. Subject humiliated -8.8 (x1.5 on a volatile pawn), opinion of painter -33; friends defend, enemies gleeful, Kind disapproves, psychopath nothing. Seen in the Social tab (attached).
- Answers: subject crossed out and scrubbed; a friend confronted; a humiliated pawn painted a revenge mockery. A fight with blows: a Brawler+Bloodlust pawn I made hate the painter, chance 0.85 -> blows, message names both and the reason (attached). Seven earlier confrontations were words only.
- Clean refusal (a pawn who hates the subject won't clean the insult) and a cleaner bond.
- Memorial: I killed a colonist with a hook; her friend painted a memorial and got "+5 grief eased".
- Clean freak break: forced with a hook; it scrubs marks one by one.
- Visitors: two insults to a faction on the outer wall, a caravan came, goodwill went 0 -> -12 once and stayed for 4000 ticks.
- Raid: a real raid; I switched its lord to "exit map" by hook; a raider tagged the wall on the way out; every colonist got "Enemy marks on our walls" -1; within 1500 ticks the mark was gone and so was the thought (who cleaned it is unknown).
- Save/load mid-feud: 101 marks and an open grudge survived.
- Rate: this build, 8 idle pawns, about 4 marks in the first 21000 ticks (a third of a day), all by the one skilled artist.
- Visual: play-zoom shots of each wall material, side faces, corners, a door in the run (two attached).

Untested: tribute (honoured / jealous lover / enemy scoffs), rereads a day later, settings switched off live, whether recreation actually rises while painting, a multi-day ordinary colony with no hooks, and anything with children, prisoners, slaves, guests, mechanoids, animals, other xenotypes.
