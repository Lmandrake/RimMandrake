# Graffiti (RimWorld 1.6), pass 18: broaden

The player should get: graffiti that reads as punk/urban paint on the walls at play zoom (64 px/cell), in the plane of each wall (option D: bolder paint on the face, marks climbing onto the wall's top), never as dirt; marks that show how well they were made; and a social layer (mockery, tribute, memorial, answers, raid tags, visitors, personality) that makes occasional stories without a mood tax or a stream of messages.

22 bars. 20 are proven on recorded live evidence (numbers below), two are still open by a blind judge's reading of play-zoom shots: `never_reads_as_dirt` and `mark_register_is_punk_urban`.

What I have tested, and how:
- One colony, Ashgate (8 colonists, day ~18, dev-quicktest start, colonists' traits cast by me: an abrasive mocker, a nervous target, a psychopath, a brawler, a neurotic, a kind one, a tortured artist). Social bars were played over unstaged days (3 days: 9 messages, Karen's mockery answered once by a reply; a death: one mourning message), plus staged triggers: an ordered mockery, a forced raid exit (lord_set_job), a forced trader caravan, a forced painting spree start, a forced clean-freak fit.
- Every look bar was judged on the same 8-9 shots of that colony's walls (south wall, a corridor, a storeroom, and a staged "dirt vs paint" granite wall with vanilla filth in front), by a fresh blind judge each pass. Pass 17's judge: dirt = claw-gouge scratches, chalk-and-ash memorial wings, half-weathered white paint, a rust-red throw-up; badges = the ideo sign in a halo ring (twice on one wall), fists ("emoji-style"), a horned mockery ("winged skull, biker patch"), crowned and rose tributes, cogs, a gold Loyalist glyph.
- This pass (shots attached, current build): scratches redrawn as scratchiti (a scratched tag, X-eyed face, heart); memorial wings deep blue; the ideo sign is now each writer's own sprayed copy (no outline, drifts per hand, a clumsy hand's wanders and runs) under a tilted saint's halo instead of inside a ring; fist sprayed freehand; mockeries are the subject's head on a rat's or plucked chicken's body; tributes a tilted crown with an arrow, or a crowd of arms lifting the head. Found and fixed live: an ordered mark went to a colonist without the skill, who silently painted a different mark.
- Owed checks this pass: a colonist painting over another's work gives the maker "X painted over my work" (mood -2.3, opinion -20) and a play-log line; the maker's answer is a roll (confront 30% if hot-tempered); I saw None once. A visitor reading an insult to another faction: running now.

What I plan next: a fresh blind judge on these shots; then the done claim.
