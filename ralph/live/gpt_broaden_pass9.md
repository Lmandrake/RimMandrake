# Graffiti, a RimWorld 1.6 mod: six shots, three questions, and which promised lines no shot proves

The mod makes colonists paint graffiti on walls (tags, throw-ups, tallies, stencils, posters, pieces, ideoligion sigils) and makes those marks
matter between people: a mockery names someone and hurts them, a tribute warms its subject, a memorial comes from someone grieving, pawns
scrub, cross out, reply in paint or confront the painter, art skill sets how beautiful or ugly a mark is, and personality changes how a
pawn takes it. All six attached shots are from the live game at normal play zoom (about 64 px per cell), on the current build except for one
change noted below.

Two colonies appear. The first was staged with one feud and then left alone. The second is a fresh colony with nothing staged. Both have
pawns called Brick, Lark, Pell, Mo and Vex, but they are different people.

The images, in order:
1. lived_in_colony_bedrooms.png: the fresh colony's bedrooms (Mo, Karen, Brick, Fringe), nothing staged. Every wall in the frame has marks.
2. social_mockery_wall_playzoom.png: a bedroom in the first colony, at play zoom. Look for a mockery before reading shot 3's caption.
3. social_mockery_victim_needs.png: Brick's mood list in the first colony.
4. social_fight_blows.png: the fresh colony; a message at the top and two pawns fighting.
5. dirt_vs_paint_b.png: a test wall: vandal scrawls on the left, six sets of tally marks in different paints, vanilla filth on the floor below.
6. perspective_sweep_wood.png: one wood-walled room with marks on every wall facing.

Measured, not shown in any one shot: the game rates each vandal scrawl at Beauty -15 and each tally at Beauty -3. When these shots were
taken, most pawns carried about +4 to +6 mood from ordinary wall art (funny doodle, good work, our sign, a friend's tag, "left my mark").
Since then the build caps that kind of wall pleasure at +3 total (times a personality factor up to 1.5 for sensitive pawns), and a memorial's
comfort is held under half the grief it eases.

## The three questions (answer each from the images)
1. In shot 2, would a player notice the mockery without clicking the wall? What would make it noticeable, if not?
2. Is +3 total from ordinary wall art (down from +4 to +6) still free mood, too little to notice, or about right for a flavour mod?
3. In shot 5, is the vandal scrawl clearly uglier than a tally at a glance, or only louder and more colourful?

## The promised lines (the owner's checklist). Which of these does no attached shot prove? Say what shot would.
Must show:
- mark_actually_appears: after a paint job or spree, a mark is visibly on the wall.
- mark_reads_as_deliberate: a mark reads at play zoom as something a hand made on purpose, not dirt or a smudge.
- mark_reads_at_play_zoom: every mark reads as a mark at ~64 px.
- mark_sits_on_the_wall: a mark reads as on the wall face, aligned to it, not a decal floating over the floor.
- marks_visibly_various: several marks in one colony are visibly different kinds, not recolours.
- mark_register_is_punk_urban: the set reads as urban/punk graffiti, not clip-art icons or modern signage.
- mark_carries_no_earth_signage: no mark reads as a contemporary real-world sign.
- mark_ugliness_is_visible: a Beauty -15 vandal scrawl looks worse than a Beauty -3 tally at a glance.
- mockery_changes_regard: a mockery names someone, hurts or amuses the right people, and changes how they regard its painter.
- tribute_and_memorial_mean_it: a tribute can warm someone, make someone jealous or annoy an enemy; a memorial comes from someone grieving and helps them.
- pawns_answer_the_wall: the player sees the scrub, the cross-out, the reply or the confrontation finish.
- raid_mark_troubles_until_retaken: a raid's mark left standing troubles the colony; taking the wall back ends that claim.
- visitors_read_without_recharging: visitors can read an insult to their faction; one trader must never charge the same wall over and over.
- stories_not_a_tax: occasional stories in an ordinary colony, never a permanent mood tax or a stream of messages.
- fights_have_people_and_reasons: a fight has people and a reason; paint never makes violence from nothing or bypasses a pawn's restrictions.
- stands_alone_and_obeys_settings: works without an ideoligion, survives saving, stops when its settings say so.
- making_a_mark_is_its_own_fun: every mark maker gets a small mood buff, and painting counts as recreation.
- rereads_affect_at_most_daily: rereading a mark affects mood at most once a day; opinion changes once, on the first read.
- art_skill_goes_into_the_art: a poor artist's mark is ugly and hurts mood and room beauty; a skilled one's is beautiful and helps; a well-made slander angers and pleases at once.
- each_takes_it_like_themselves: psychopaths feel nothing from any mark; sensitive people feel more both ways; clean freaks take extra debuffs and can break into compulsive cleaning.
Cannot show:
- never_real_world_english: no legible real-world English word or third-party author's tag in a mark.
- never_reads_as_dirt: no mark indistinguishable from vanilla filth at play zoom.
