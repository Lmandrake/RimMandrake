# Graffiti (RimWorld 1.6), pass 14: broaden

The player should get: graffiti that reads as punk/urban paint on the walls at play zoom (64 px/cell), never as dirt, and a social layer that makes occasional stories (mockery, tribute, memorial, answers, raids, visitors) without a mood tax or a stream of messages.

Tested this pass, played on a real day-18 colony (Ashgate, 6 colonists) unless marked staged:
- 3 days unstaged: 6 messages, the Karen-Lark feud ended at one scrub (no paint-back).
- A colonist killed: two friends painted memorials within an hour; before a fix this pass, 7 "X stopped at the memorial" messages in an hour; now one mourning message per death per day (not yet re-seen with a mourner reading it).
- A 3-colonist painting spree (staged start): before a fix, 16 "X painted over Y's work" messages in 5 game-hours; now one summary per spree painter at its end (seen: 3 messages).
- Edge-on walls (N-S walls show only a strip): 24 of 77 marks were there, pictures squeezed into blots that read as blood/mould. Now pictures and social marks never go there; seen live, the spree's 11 new edge-on marks were all tags/scrawls.
- A blind judge on the day-18 shots: punk register MIXED (teal ideoligion sigil repeated 3x, a tribute's laurel/sun frame read as badges), no earth signage TRUE, ugliness visible TRUE, never reads as dirt FALSE (weathered flakes read as debris/mould; a dark tag on a dark wall reads as soot). Fixes this pass: weathered paint keeps a faint stain of its whole shape; the fist stencil and the HighLife glyph redrawn.
