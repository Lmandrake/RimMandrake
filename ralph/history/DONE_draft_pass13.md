## Painted on, not stuck on (owner, 2026-11-07)

**True for the set as it stands, fairly sure.** The paint has patchy coverage, spray falloff and drips that run down the face, and the wall's own brick shows through the paint. Poor hands paint a muddy smeared double; good and better hands put a block shadow under their letters. This pass added two fixes. Weathered marks now flake and wash off in patches rather than thinning to a ghost. A mockery's portrait of its subject is now painted in broken brush and muted tones, not the game's crisp sprite with its die-cut outline. Sigil icons are cut as two-tone stencils. Colours sit in the wall's light.
