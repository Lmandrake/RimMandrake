# First look: an ordinary landing, every mechanic on, a live storyteller

Shots are in `D:\ClaudeWork\rimmandrake\ralph_pyrelands\shots\`, map `NS_Pyrelands_play_c`, at about 64 px per cell.

- `first_look_1_landing.png`: the landing party on tick 0, orange ember grass and green quickgrass on black ash.
- `first_look_2_front.png`: the opening fire five hours in, a ring walking outward with a black scar and fire-born pods inside it.
- `first_look_3_scar.png`: the same scar after about a day in clear light, grass returning, pods standing.
- `first_look_4_blackrain_noon.png`: the same ground under black rain at noon: a dark, drained sky.
- `cd5.png`: the whole map about five days in: a burned patch regrowing, a second burn at the top right.

**What I'm least sure about:**
- Whether scorch-fruit pods really rot on time. I have not measured it, and one exception on a pod is still unexplained.
- Whether the fire's solid yellow blobs read as a line up close, because neighbouring flames merge. Vanilla draws them that way.
- A colony that actually defends itself. My test colonists were left unmanaged: they got bitten and burned, and several died, so nothing here says how a run plays.
