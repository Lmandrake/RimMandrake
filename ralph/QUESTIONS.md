# Questions for the owner

1. **Every Pyrelands map now gets some standing water.** Water came only from a noise layer, and some maps got none. Map `n` (fresh, 38 C) had no water, no mud and no sowable rich soil. Under FlowWorks thirst, colonists were empty by day 1 and would have died by about day 4: food gives no water, there's no well without a lucky underground roll, and nothing warned. So a map left with fewer than 150 water cells now gets two or three small ponds (radius 4.5 to 7, ringed with mud and rich soil), away from the edges, rock and the landing spot. That is still rare: about 0.4% of the map. My recommendation is to keep it. The alternative is a guaranteed fresh-water bleed well for Pyrelands, which only helps a player who already knows FlowWorks.
2. **Explosive Growth's dry-crop rule is off for Pyrelands only.** It multiplied any vanilla crop more than 2 cells from water by 0.1, on top of the ash field's own rule (dry 70%, watered 140%). Rice on dry ash grew at about 7%, and rice on the "rich soil" by a pond crawled at 10%. That was the "crops at a tenth" you asked about. The biome now opts out through a new `RM_ExplosiveGrowthBiomeExtension`; no other biome changes. Watered ash still grows crops twice as fast as dry ash.
3. **Crops on watered ash now shrug off the heat ("wet roots"). Built, not yet seen in game.** The Pyrelands' tiles have a median of 53.6 C. A vanilla crop grows fully only up to 42 C, stops at 58 C, and grows only from 06:00 to 19:00, which are the hottest hours. On a 50 C landing (map `q`), the day ran 45 C at 05:00 to 58 C at 17:00. Rice read a 0 to 16% heat multiplier at midday and gained about 4% growth a day, whether it stood by water or not. That is your "crops at a tenth", alongside the Explosive Growth rule fixed earlier. It also made the biome's own text false ("wherever water reaches it, it grows almost anything"), and a canal changed nothing a player could see. Now, a plant on ash-field ground that counts as watered (beside dug water, near a pond or mud, or after black rain) reads its heat multiplier as if its cell were 15 C cooler. Dry ash keeps vanilla's heat. The plant's inspect pane says "Wet ash keeps its roots cool…". Watered rice should grow most of the day; dry rice hardly grows at all. My recommendation is to keep it: water becomes the heart of farming in a fire biome. The alternative is to paint Pyrelands tiles nearer 35 C, which would make the biome mild.
4. **A downed colonist here dies of thirst unless someone has a water bottle (FlowWorks, not Pyrelands).** Heatstroke downed a colonist on day 2 of map `q`. FlowWorks' "give patients water" job needs a bottle, and a crashlanded colony has none, so their thirst ran to 0 in bed beside a pond. I left FlowWorks' thirst alone. I recommend a doctor be allowed to carry water by hand from open water to a patient within a short distance.
5. **Animal mix: no change made.** Your ruled roster weights give grazer-heavy maps at landing. On a model of vanilla's spawner, small grazers fall to the big predators' number or below on under 1% of maps. But several maps this week read predator-heavy after a few days: fire-wasp swarms, anooba packs, flamefangs. The Iriaz (weight 0.5) can never appear on a 50 C tile, because its comfort maximum is exactly 50 C. I'm watching whether fire or hunting thins the grazers over days before I touch your weights.
