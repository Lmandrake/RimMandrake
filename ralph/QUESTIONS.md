# Questions for the owner

1. **Every Pyrelands map now gets some standing water.** Water came only from a noise layer, and some maps got none. Map `n` (fresh, 38 C) had no water, no mud and no sowable rich soil. Under FlowWorks thirst, colonists were empty by day 1 and would have died by about day 4: food gives no water, there's no well without a lucky underground roll, and nothing warned. So a map left with fewer than 150 water cells now gets two or three small ponds (radius 4.5 to 7, ringed with mud and rich soil), away from the edges, rock and the landing spot. That is still rare: about 0.4% of the map. My recommendation is to keep it. The alternative is a guaranteed fresh-water bleed well for Pyrelands, which only helps a player who already knows FlowWorks.
2. **Explosive Growth's dry-crop rule is off for Pyrelands only.** It multiplied any vanilla crop more than 2 cells from water by 0.1, on top of the ash field's own rule (dry 70%, watered 140%). Rice on dry ash grew at about 7%, and rice on the "rich soil" by a pond crawled at 10%. That was the "crops at a tenth" you asked about. The biome now opts out through a new `RM_ExplosiveGrowthBiomeExtension`; no other biome changes. Watered ash still grows crops twice as fast as dry ash.
