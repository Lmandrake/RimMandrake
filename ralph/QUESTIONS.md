# Questions for the owner

**Burned ground carpets over in about a day and a half, much faster than the north star's "about a week".** I burned a 40x26 patch on a real map and counted: 35 plants at the end of the fire, 303 at day 1, 351 at day 1.5, where unburned ground holds about 410. Raising `wildPlantRegrowDays` from 3 to 9 made no difference (441 by day 1.25), so the bursts are doing it, not the wild spawner: sprouts ripen in a day, and one in three pops and sows 6 to 9 more. That matches your "grow violently quickly and spread itself super-fast" and the kept burst, so I left it. The cost: a black scar reads for about a day, not several. I recommend keeping it. If you want scars to last, the fix is to stop bursts sowing on ground burned in the last few days. Tell me if you want that.

**Dry deep ash now reads 70% fertile instead of 40%, so it can actually be sown.** Every crop needs at least 70% (the game's own base), so on 40% a player could not sow anything on 85% of a fresh map: zone it, and it says "Need more fertile terrain". That also made the north star's "the same crop on dry ash" impossible. At 70% dry ash behaves like stony soil, and watered ash stays 140%, so water still doubles it, and a fresh scar still reads about 120%. The cost: dry ash is a little more generous than "feeds little" suggested, so I changed the text to "feeds thin crops". The other way would be to keep 40% and patch sowing to ignore the minimum on ash; that keeps a harsher field but fights every other mod that reads the rule. I recommend 70%. It is one slider, "Dry deep-ash fertility", if you want it lower.

**The native cast no longer gets thirsty.** With thirst on, a wild animal searches 60 cells for water. A Pyrelands map has a few ponds, so most of the herd sat at empty and reached 65% dehydration by day 3 (fatal at 100%). I exempted the Pyrelands cast, wild or tamed: they take their water from the grass. The cost is that they do not gather at ponds, and a tamed one never needs a drink. I recommend this over widening the search for all animals, which is a rule outside this biome and still fails on a map with no water.
