# For the owner

## "Why did I see three separate cables coming out of a doorway to a single terminal node?" (2026-11-07)

Because of a dice roll, and it was wrong. Every ordinary floor connection rolled for 1, 2 or 3 parallel cords (the Mod Setting `cordsPerConnection`, shipped at 3). Your earlier ruling on "very odd triple cords" made a machine's own lead one cord, but left conduit ends, junctions and wall stubs rolling. So a plain run out through a door to where the conduit ends could draw three cables for one line of conduit. Nothing about the door or the grid asked for it.

Fixed: the shipped setting is now 1, so one connection draws one cord everywhere. Your settings file never stored the old value, so you get the new one. Overhead lines still carry their wires, and tangles are still heaps of many wires. The slider stays in Mod Settings for a player who wants bundles. Nothing here needs an answer from you unless you want the slider removed altogether.

**Second cause, found 2026-11-07 (pass 5).** Modern power strips could still look like several cables going to one place. When a strip sat right beside a machine, its sockets faced that machine. The cord passing through to the next node then had to run along the machine's edge to reach a socket, so from above it looked like a second cord going into the machine. The machine now takes the strip's own single lead, and the sockets face open floor. This is a change to how strips sit in a look you approved. Seen working beside the batteries in the Modern look; other spots and looks not yet checked. Before and after shots still owed.

**Third cause, found 2026-11-07 (pass 6), the biggest one.** Batteries and generators carry power themselves, and the mod drew a separate cord to every conduit cell touching them. A conduit run alongside a solar panel drew seven cords and a row of power strips into that one panel. The staged test site never ran conduit along a machine, so nobody saw it until I built a colony on a generated map. Now each run gets one cord. A battery joining two separate runs keeps one cord per run, and a conduit end beside the building still plugs in, so it doesn't spark. Seen fixed at the solar panels and batteries in the Modern look (`p6N_Modern_yard`, against `p6L_Modern_yard` before). Other looks not yet checked.

**Fourth cause, found 2026-11-07 (pass 7), and probably the closest to what you saw.** Sometimes it was never several cables. One cord with too much slack for a short hop ran out past the machine, doubled back almost to where it started, then went out again. That lays three nearly parallel lines between one junction and one machine, and from above it reads as three cables. The graph held one connection, which is why the earlier fixes and every proof row said "one cord". I found it by looking at a kitchen in a played colony: a heater 3 cells from its junction, the same shape in every look. Now a cord that runs back alongside itself for more than about a cell is laid again on its next random draw with a little less slack, and all other cords stay exactly where they were. Seen gone at that heater in Scrapper and Modern (`p7d_Scrapper_house` before, `p7f_Scrapper_house` after). I can't prove this is the doorway you looked at, because I don't have your map. The other two looks are not re-shot yet.

**Fifth cause, found 2026-11-07 (pass 8), and the one that matches your words best.** A cord's spare slack could wander under a door. The mod keeps its loops out of doorways, but not the broad wander of the cord or the way it settles, and a door counts as floor you can walk on. So one cord going from a junction to a heater in the kitchen could slip under the door, loop round in the next room and come back. From above, that shows as several cables coming out of a doorway, with nothing in the room behind them to explain it. I saw it at the kitchen door in `p7f_Modern_house`. Now a cord may go under a door only if its route really runs through that door, and only once. A cord that strays is laid again with a little less slack. In the rebuilt colony the probe reads 0 stray passes in 66 strands, and no cord ends at that door any more (`p8a_Modern_house`). I can't prove this is the doorway you saw, since I don't have your map. Only the Modern look has been re-shot since this fix.

## Cords in tall grass look dashed: keep this? (2026-11-07, pass 6)

On a real map the grass draws over the cords, so a thin cord through grass shows as broken dashes (`p6N_Modern_cutspan`, `p6L_Modern_house` bottom right). It reads as a cord lying down in the grass, but a player could take a dashed stretch for a break. Drawing cords above the grass would fix that, but they would look laid on top of the plants, and it changes a look you approved. I recommend keeping it as it is, and I have left it unchanged. Tell me if you want it the other way.

## A short stump left by a blast drew no end at all (2026-11-07, pass 7, no answer needed unless you disagree)

The mod hides needless conduit: a dead end of 1 or 2 cells off a junction becomes a small loop in the cord, so a stray stub doesn't spark forever. It spared a stub only if it faced more conduit within 3 cells, on the idea that this means a break. A mortar in a lived-in colony blew a 3-cell gap, one more than that test could see across, so both stumps counted as needless and the cut showed nothing: no spark, no limp end, just two cords gone.

Fixed: a stub now counts as a break if it faces conduit across a gap of up to 5 cells, straight along its own line. What remains: if a blast takes a corner or the whole far side, so the stump faces nothing in line, a stump of 1 or 2 cells still reads as needless and draws a loop instead of an end. Telling those apart would mean remembering which conduit was destroyed, and this mod saves nothing of its own in your save. I recommend leaving it that way. Longer stumps always draw their end.
