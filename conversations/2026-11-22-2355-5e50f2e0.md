- **All drawn shadows come from the sun.** Their direction and strength follow the time of day, and they fade out at night.
- **FlowWorks pits follow only the sun.** The pit's shading reads the sun shadow plus a fixed edge shade. No pit drawing code reads lamp light at all `[measured: a source search]`.

**What that means for a lit pit**
- **A strong lamp at the rim:** the cells brighten, but the pit's shadows stay where the sun puts them. They do not move to follow the lamp.
