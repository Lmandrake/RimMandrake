## Case 2: the prisoner gets back up

**Why it's revealing.** Most of the care you've built is tied to the prisoner being downed: feeding without a bed, suppressing `TakeToBed`, and probably tending. A raider downed by pain shock or a minor wound stands up within hours, so the 60000-tick run very likely contains a moment where the prisoner stops being downed. The other risk runs the opposite way: a bleeding raider who stays downed dies within the run if the rim path doesn't work for tending. The statement mentions rim reachability only for the two feeding jobs.

Assert the prisoner still counts as in a prison cell on the first tick after load.
