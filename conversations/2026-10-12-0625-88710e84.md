Yes. It's built, and the parts that use dug channels passed live checks on 2026-10-11. The pump-into-tank route uses the same code, but no live check has measured it yet.

**How it works** (`D:\Luke\dev\RimMandrake\src\RimMandrake\FlowWorks\Source\RM_StockMath.cs`):
- **Each natural pond or lake holds a fixed amount of water** when the map first sees it. That amount scales with the pond's size and a "source budget" slider in Mod Settings.
- **Very large bodies never run down.** A body counts as endless if it's too big to measure (an ocean), or if it touches the map edge and is big enough that it plausibly continues off-map. That decision is made once and never revisited.
- **Drawing water lowers the pond's stock.** Water drawn out by a dug channel, or lifted by the pump into a tank beside it, is subtracted from what the pond holds. The pump code is in `D:\Luke\dev\RimMandrake\src\RimMandrake\FlowWorks\Source\LiquidTypes\Building_LiquidPump.cs`.
- **The shoreline shrinks as the pond drains.** Visible water tiles go away to match what's left. The cells feeding an outflow channel dry up last, so a channel isn't cut off with water still stranded in the pond. When the pond is empty, the water stops flowing.
- **It refills slowly.** There's always a small background seep, sized to the pond, and the rate goes up with rain and with the season: spring refills most, high summer barely at all. Refill can be turned off in Mod Settings.

**What's been checked in the game.** On the 2026-10-11 run, all seven pond checks passed on a small 5×5 pond drained through a dug channel: the pond starts out limited, it draws down, the shoreline shrinks, flow stops when it's empty, it visibly refills, and refill can be switched on and off.
