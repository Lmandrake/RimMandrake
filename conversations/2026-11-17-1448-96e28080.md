### definition.md Physical boundaries and transitions
BEFORE:
## Physical boundaries and transitions

Outdoor ambient Dark density follows the map's shared ordinary outdoor temperature; each room's density follows its own ordinary room temperature. The transition band remains tunable checks, not a new heat rule. Outdoor air therefore thins or closes together as that shared temperature changes; Unveiling clears it map-wide. There are no spontaneous drifting open-air pockets in this first version. Those built Perlin offsets move to a labelled next-version switch, off, alongside the already deferred fold lanes and phantom pockets. A heated room can show clear indoor air against the Dark through its open doorway while maintained heating keeps the room warm. Opening or removing its enclosure can cool the room and close its air through ordinary temperature updates; it creates no warm outdoor lane. This is a settled scope choice from the current engine's room/outdoor temperature model, not a measurement left for a simulator.

The Dark reduces fine detail and contrast of explored terrain and relief in proportion to local density, while retaining the relief silhouettes and usable walking/building ground. Buildings are dimmed with readable outlines and footprints. Player-owned colonists, tame animals and mechs remain fully drawn. Wild animals, hostile pawns and neutral visitors are dimmed with density but retain a discernible silhouette; they are never culled, removed from view or replaced by an invisible threat. Selection outlines, inspect access, controls and warning text remain readable for all these classes. The predator's dim silhouette and its existing close-attack text/sound tell keep danger perceptible; no new warning system is introduced. A sky tint alone, an unchanged predator sprite and a completely hidden predator each fail this definition. Ordinary unexplored fog still hides what it normally hides: the Dark neither reveals that fog nor adds sprite hiding inside explored country. Contrast/opacity tuning may vary only while preserving these class distinctions and readable silhouettes.

Unveiling clears all first-version ambient particles everywhere, then ambient Dark returns according to room/outdoor temperatures. No carried knot or release is active. No detection ray gains clean targeting merely because its shooter stands inside warm air: intervening Dark matters for simulated sight/aim even though the player can still discern dim creature silhouettes. Ordinary walls remain occluders. A common density query must feed presentation, targeting, glowers and muffling; the geometry model must expose any contradictory exception.

Unveiling's apparent brightness is presentation, not solar elevation or UV. Direct Sun is the standing world-geometry rule; a weather lightening must not move the sun.
