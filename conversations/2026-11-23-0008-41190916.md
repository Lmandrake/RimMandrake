Both fixes are landed and both items are closed. The fix landed in `09594beb08`; the follow-up commits are `9004eb0a49` (test table, one check repaired) and `3f6074e9d8` (recorded red run). Nothing is deployed to ArchBig.

**Cause:** every pass that shapes a cord keeps the last bit at each end exactly as laid (at a plug, a power-strip socket, a wall plate) and only reshapes the cord in between. Where the reshaped part left that end bit at an angle, the joint stayed a sharp corner. The shape rule also excused any turn within 0.75 cell of a socket however sharp, so the builder never tried another layout. At station 3 this produced two hooks where pile cables leave the power strips (114° and 129°) and one sharp bump on the wall-plate lead. Under the battery the same thing happens, but the battery art hides it.

**Fix:** a new last step rounds every such kink with a smooth curve no tighter than 0.2 cell. It keeps the cord's direction on both sides and never moves the end pieces or adds any other fault. The loose loops stay.
