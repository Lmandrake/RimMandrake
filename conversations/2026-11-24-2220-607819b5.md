## Change 2: the plan behaves as a sensible person would

1. **Stay.** The live account stays live until it reaches its own line. No switch happens because another account
   stands earlier in a list or an alphabet. Order answers one question only: when the live account is finished, which
   account is next.
2. **Fewest switches.** A switch is costly: every window restarts and live runs are interrupted. The plan makes the
   fewest switches that still use the allowance that a reset would otherwise erase.
3. **No blocked starts for a preference.** New runs are refused only when the live account is at its own warning line.
4. **Resets are planned for.** Allowance that a reset would erase is a reason to act (use that account before its
   reset), weighed against the cost of a switch. The plan says its reasoning in one line that a person can check.
5. **No markers.** State comes from fresh measurements each time.
