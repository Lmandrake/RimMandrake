## Claude

Section 2 — the object model. This is what gets built.

**Fixture.** One light the framework controls. Discovered **by capability** — does this thing expose a settable glow colour — never by a def whitelist, because the mod list carries at least four separate light mods and a whitelist would rot within a month. Each fixture has a position, a colour, a brightness, and a role: *mass* or *flasher*. Lights the framework doesn't recognise are simply not fixtures and keep doing whatever they do — which law 8 now actively depends on.

**Group.** A named set of fixtures, from three sources in precedence order: a hooking mod registers one with any membership it likes (Shrine, internal, external); the player selects one in settings (whole ship, per-room, halls versus rooms); or the framework derives defaults (rooms, corridors, exterior). Groups may overlap.

**Scheme.** The authored look, and exactly your four axes — a **palette** of quantised colours in order, a **pattern** that is a pure function of *(fixture, group, time)* returning a palette index, a **brightness envelope**, and a **rate** given separately for mass and flashers. Because a scheme is data rather than code, it can be declared in XML, which is what makes the declarative hook possible at all.

**Claim.** A request to show a scheme on a group, of one of two kinds. A **mood** is indefinite, held until replaced, and carries a god's identity. A **gesture** is finite, plays over whatever is underneath, and on completion restores the substrate exactly — that restoration *is* the Narrator's slow dim back.

**Compositor.** The single thing that decides what every fixture shows, on a real-time clock. It takes the live claims; gives **territory** to the top two moods over the union of their groups, with the boundary advancing on its own slow clock; turns moods ranked third and below into **tremor** contributions in their own palettes, capped at three; composites gestures on top in rank order with the Narrator above all; and emits a quantised colour-and-brightness pair per fixture. Then the critical part: **only fixtures whose pair actually changed are written and dirtied.** That single rule is what makes the whole thing affordable, and it's inherited from the twinkle spike rather than invented here.

**Rank comes from canon, not from us.** `in_front` already rules that engagement makes a god louder and the loudest holds actuator priority. Loudness *is* rank — the framework doesn't need its own priority scheme, it needs to read the one the campaign already computes.

**Witness ledger.** Per gesture and per mood transition: was an awake colonist in a lit affected room. Deliberately narrow — awake, on the map, in a room containing an affected fixture, and everything else counts as unwitnessed, because that's the honest direction to be wrong in.

**Two hook doors.** Code: register a group, push a mood, play a gesture, query the ledger, ask what's showing. Defs: declare a group by room role or fixture marking, and bind a named signal to a scheme-plus-group-plus-kind — enough for an XML-only mod to make the lights answer an event.
