## The language
- A score is one utterance: its intent, its emotion, and a list of events (gesture, length, gap, pitch curve, timbre). A separate voice sets size, register, speed and formants.
- A dialect is a set of parameters for register, timing, articulation, timbre and cadence. One score plays on any voice.
- A short EDM-style notation, borrowed from Tidal/Strudel, compiles to a score:
  `language/pattern.py 'notice [double_blip*2 ~] <glide_up query_fall_rise>' --pitch '0 7 12 ~ 5' --warble 'sine 0.2 0.8'`
