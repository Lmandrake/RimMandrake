## What the skill actually recommends

Its framing is the opposite of ConsultJev's. ConsultJev treats Jev as a **second opinion you consult**; the official skill treats it as a **programming primitive you embed in the product**. Its recommendations, condensed:

- **Read the live docs as part of the task** — `https://docs.typesafe.ai/llms.txt`, targeted reads, append `.md` to page paths. It says explicitly the skill gives direction and the docs are the source of truth.
- **Work backward from the behavior, not forward from the model.** Keep rules, calculations, exact lookups and execution in code; add Jev only at the seams where semantic understanding is needed.
- **Pick a primitive by what the answer means:** Choice (one of a set), Noul (probability a statement is true, one per label), Score (position on ordered, concretely-described levels).
- **One narrow judgment per question, with enough state**; batch all independent questions over the same state into a single request — they run in parallel and can't see each other.
- **"Typed output guarantees the interface, not truth."** Thresholds must be evaluated on your own data and consequences; validate performance in the target domain before trusting it.
- Of its six patterns, the two that fit this repo are **"find and judge evidence"** (rerank/relevance) and **"verify and escalate"** (check a claim against its evidence, send uncertain cases to a human or a reasoning model).
