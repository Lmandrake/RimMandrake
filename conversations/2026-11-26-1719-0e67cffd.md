## Claude

This run built Direct Sun as one model for every map, as the owner ruled on 2026-10-19: a pure function of the sun's geometry, with no per-biome sun. Three of the five tickets are closed, and the two tree-shade tickets go to the next run.

**What a player now gets**
- Direct Sun adds 55 °C × sin(sun height) on every sunlit map of a world with a fixed sun. A world with a day and night has no Direct Sun.
- A roof, a shade tent or a parasol protects at every sun height. A shadow is height ÷ tan(sun height) cells long: about 4 cells behind a wall when the sun is at 14°, none at 85°.
- The heat kinds and every per-biome sun number are gone. The standing sun shield is no longer shade gear.
- The volcanic heat of the Forge, the steam of the Scald and the hot fog of the Greentide are now hotter air: a permanent vanilla temperature condition, shown on the thermometer, that no shade cools.
- Overcast, rain, fog, snow and storm weathers dim Direct Sun.

**Settings:** the master switch now says "Direct Sun". New rows: the zenith value (default 55 °C), "weather dims Direct Sun" (default on), and a label that day/night worlds are unsupported. The Forge, Scald and Greentide each have an on/off and a °C slider for their hot air.

**Something the build found:** the plant check that the design asked for showed that the planned 12 °C of hot air would slow all 24 Greentide plants (its trees from 78% growth to 3%), and 15 °C would stop 7 of the Scald's 8 plants. So each hot-air biome's own wild plants now have their upper growth limits raised by the offset (widen only, as you ruled for plant bands). After that, none of the 36 plants in those three biomes grow slower.
