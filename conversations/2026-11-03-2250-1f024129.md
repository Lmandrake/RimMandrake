## Relentless: agent or pipeline?
I think the name sits on the pipeline, the reverse of how you remember it. The relentless part is the supervisor program plus each item's record on disk. It's what never lets go.

**Is it modular?** More than you feared:
- The worker is a plain `rm-builder` told "You are one worker in a line", and it's meant to be able to die.
- The slots are settings in `relentless_rules.json`: `worker_agent`, `checker_agent`, `help_agent`, `pilot_agent`.
- A worker only has to use six commands: `beat`, `learn`, `contract`, `snag`, `finish`, `help`. Anything that uses them can sit in a slot.
- The limit is one worker type at a time. Mixing styles per item would be a modest change.

**Subagents: no, workers can't make them.** Their brief says "no subagents", and `rm-builder` has no Agent tool. A stuck worker runs `help`, which sends a fresh consultant whose advice lands on the item's record for the next worker. Unnamed subagents belong to this Conductor window.

**Naming:** I recommend keeping **Relentless** as the pipeline and calling what's in it **workers, checkers, consultants and test pilots**, with the worker style named separately (today, `rm-builder`). That needs no rename, and it fits your idea of other styles in those slots.

```
ledger item → QUEUED → worker in one of 8 places → CANDIDATE → blind checker → CLOSING → DONE
                ▲          │ snag or restart              │ FAIL or WEAK
                └──────────┴── back to the top ───────────┘
side tracks: GAME (one at a time) · HELD (for you) · BLOCKED (on another item)
```
