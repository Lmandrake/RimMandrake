Regaining shade after a deposit has been removed does not recreate it: another actual wetting and retreat is required. If shade returns before an already-wet deposit dries, that still-present deposit may persist as ordinary Soil; no new deposition occurs. Player floors/excavations replace the flood's ownership, so neither shade loss nor expiry undoes them.
