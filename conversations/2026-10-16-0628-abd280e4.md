Here's how mineral finds work when you dig, step by step:

- **When it can happen:** each time a colonist digs a square one level deeper. A square only gets a chance the first time it reaches a given depth, so filling a canal in and re-digging it pays nothing.
- **How likely:** 1.5% per square, per level dug. A Mod Settings slider scales that.
- **What comes out, checked in this order:**
  1. **Deep cuts only (depth 3 or 4, the pit):** if there's a deep-drill deposit under that exact square, there's a 50% chance the find is that deposit.
  2. **Otherwise:** an ore that is actually in this map's rock, picked in proportion to how much of it the map has. A map rich in steel veins mostly turns up steel.
  3. **If the map has no ore at all:** a chunk of the map's own stone.
- **How much:** a lump of 10–25 units, dropped on the nearest undug ground beside the hole, with a message. The first find of each kind of mineral on a map also gets a letter.
- **The cap:** each map can only give up so much this way, a share of the ore in its rock, set by a slider. Once that share is used up, finds become stone chunks, so digging up the whole map can't out-mine the land.

**So what kinds can appear?** Whatever ore this map actually has: steel, silver, gold, uranium, jade, plus any mod ores in its rock. Deep cuts can also reach whatever the deep-drill scanner would find under that square. Components and plasteel never come out of a hole; you called that the "absurd thing in Vanilla", and it's excluded on purpose.

**Two caveats:**
- Every number above is a placeholder, waiting for your review sheet.
- The plan is to draw minerals from a planet-wide list of which minerals belong in which biomes (`MINERALS_WHERE_THEY_BELONG_1`). That list isn't built yet, so until it is, "local" means this map's own geology.
