**Why the Dalgo mares overlap wrongly:** the game picks which of two overlapping pawns is in front with a random number tied to each pawn's ID (`Verse/Pawn_DrawTracker.cs:43` and `:60` in the decompile, `SeededYOffset`). So between any two pawns it is a coin flip, fixed for good.

**The fix** lives in `src/RimMandrake/MandrakePatches/Source/SouthDrawsFront/` and applies on every map:
- **Pawns among pawns:** the random number is replaced by a height based on how far south the pawn stands. It stays inside the pawn's own draw layer, and a pawn in full flight is lifted above pawns on the ground.
- **Buildings among buildings:** each building is drawn higher the further south its footprint starts, within its own layer.
- **Plants:** I moved BiomesShell's plant fix into this mod and dropped its "our biomes only" limit. BiomesShell's copy and its setting are gone, so nothing is patched twice.

**Setting:** "Southern things draw in front", on by default; off restores the game's normal order.

**Cost:** pawn positions are read many times per frame, and the new code there only reads a few values and does some arithmetic; it creates no objects. The building and plant change runs only when the map's graphics are rebuilt, never every frame.

**Not fixed yet** (all written into the ticket):
- A pawn still draws over every building and tree, so a pawn standing north of a tree is not hidden by it.
- Within about 15 cells, a northern human's head can still draw over a southern pawn's body. For an animal's wound or overlay graphics the distance is about 3 cells. Two animal bodies, the reported case, are always ordered correctly.
- Buildings that are animated each frame rather than drawn into the map's graphics are not covered.
