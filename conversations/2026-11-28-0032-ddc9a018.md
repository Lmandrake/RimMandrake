The account plan now takes a leave line that changes through the week: a line schedule. Every reader of the line gets it from one function, `line_of_step`. The live plan is unchanged, and the item is closed with the timer's live promise.

**To set your Codex example:**
`./acct line codex --schedule "Sat 23:59=40 hold; Tue 13:30=0 ramp" --owner-said "…"`
Try it first with `./acct whatif -- line codex --schedule "…"`, which writes nothing. `./acct line codex 25` sets a flat number again.

I changed one word of the brief's design. Each point now says the line up to its moment: `hold` keeps that percent up to the point, and `ramp` is a straight line from the point before. With that reading, your example string means exactly your sentence. Under the brief's wording the same string was broken: it held 40% until Tuesday, then ramped towards nothing.

**What `./acct status` shows:** a line `SCHEDULE codex: 40% now, held until Sat Nov 28 11:59 PM PST, then a ramp to 0% at Tue Dec 01 1:30 PM PST`, and each point as hours before the reset. `./acct plan` and `./acct forecast` show the same. `./acct measures` now also gives the minutes under the line, the lowest gap, and dark hours for each week.

**Three corner cases from the sweep, each with its rule:**
1. **The reset moves.** A move of up to 2 hours is absorbed. A daylight-saving week shifts a ramp by an hour, which changes the line by 0.7 points or less. **Rule:** if the reset moves by more than 2 hours, type the schedule again; status says `OUTSIDE THE WEEK`.
2. **A falling ramp on the last Claude account** stops and restarts the fleet up to 12 times in 96 hours. **Rule:** on Claude accounts use a few `hold` steps, not ramps. Codex suits a ramp.
3. **A line that rises while a run never ends** lets the fleet burn up to 61 points under the new line before the 2-hour drain limit ends the run. **Rule:** raise a Claude line only on an account that is not live, or set the rise 2 hours early.

**Comparison headline:** at today's Codex burn, your example erases 2 points a week at the reset instead of 8.7. It also cuts dark hours from 34.5 to 24.4 a week and adds about 7 points a week of fleet work. With your own use of 0.3–0.7 points an hour on Wednesday–Saturday, your example never left your Codex empty. Under the flat 25%, a fast fleet left it empty 6.1 hours a week.
