The retained plain arrival explanation says rain scalds from above, roofs stop that direct scald, shade reduces only common geometric Direct Sun, and ambient hot air still needs ordinary heat management. No new site quest is the entrance fee to seeing the mod’s land.

**Foundry works — Designer physical contract.** Whole standing towers are permanent solid scenery: their occupied base cells are impassable, with no enterable interior, usable floor or roof area. They cannot be mined, deconstructed, salvaged or destroyed by ordinary damage; this preserves the_forge.md §5’s whole towers through a long-settled colony. Ordinary Wreckage salvage/deconstruction applies only to separate remains, never to the towers. Placement must leave permanent connections even with all salvageable remains removed. Towers cast shade through the shared Direct Sun caster geometry; they create no roof over neighbouring ground, so a tower’s shadow alone does not stop falling scald. Ordinary player-built roofs still use vanilla roof rules. This adds no Forge shade exception or tower shelter aura. Open melt must also show visible nonliving machine motion during ordinary observation; a static red seam fails the frozen image even if towers and steam are convincing. No task or interactive tender is revealed.

**Life — Designer feeding contract.** Small and greater bulloos metabolize vapor while aloft within an active column, using the actual habitat field’s InColumn membership at their horizontal position. Feeding bouts restore their Food need without consuming a plant, item or gas stock; a plant-free column feeds them. Their existing VegetarianRoughAnimal diet remains an alternative for ordinary plant/compatible hauled feed, including tame bulloos away from columns and caravan feed. Away from an active column no vapor nutrition is granted: food falls normally, feed must be supplied or reached, and unfed animals can starve. Taming changes neither metabolism nor diet and never forces a tame animal back to a column. Greater bulloos share this feeding eligibility without acquiring an escort or permanent home leash; seeking a reachable feeding site when hungry is nutrition, not a new roaming restriction. Missing/disabled habitat columns grant no vapor food; hiding the display alone leaves the active habitat field unchanged. Ordinary feeding remains available and urgent needs outrank homing. Fleet fliers instead consume actual edible vegetation, including admitted fireweed, within their existing foraging reach; a bare column does not feed them. Jossurs eat prey through ordinary carnivory. The frozen sheet §4 explicitly separates vapor-metabolizing herds from fleet fliers eating fireweed; current bulloo/greater-bulloo/fleet-flier XML all declare rough herbivory, which supplies the fallback but does not implement vapor feeding. The shared habitat/feeding adapters are dependencies to wire in T18, not a gas economy to design here. Inspect/need-source information must distinguish vapor feeding from plant or supplied-feed consumption.

Early priorities are permanent shelter, food/feed access, safe routes and ordinary temperature management. A brief unexpected burst can injure or down an otherwise healthy exposed adult; it must not execute them before a prompt response can occur.
