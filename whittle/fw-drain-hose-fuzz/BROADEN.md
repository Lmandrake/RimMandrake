- A 1×1 reel's nominal outlet iteration has no nozzle. Require the deterministic seed-2 witness to have a real nozzle, and exercise outlet-only and free-only mutants. The repair checks `Reel.Outward.HasValue`, and both mode mutants are included in the fast controls.

- The family and VV matrix need failure tallies plus ordinary grouped diagnostics. Witness faults increment `t.Failures` and enter `fails`. The normal summary always prints witness coverage. `Report` remains the assertion point.
- A tiny-scale original reproduction can look degenerate. Add an original total-refusal run at full scale and its repaired counterpart.
- The lightweight probe's solver argument adapter differs from the real CLI parser. Add focused builds that compile every actual SelfTest project input and execute the actual `Program.cs` with `--hose-fuzz`, plus positional `g2` or `g2off` where applicable. The ordinary wrapper separately executes the normal full entry point.
- Keep random case/step counts honest. Witnesses have their own printed coverage and leave the sampled case/step/laid counts intact. The final repair follows this suggestion.

The suggestion to run witnesses during explicit single-seed debugging was declined to preserve the requested refusal-only single-seed workflow. Such output explicitly states that successful lay coverage is optional. Normal hose runs at every positive scale require both witnesses. A sampled ratio, unknown-filter validation, extra skip messages and trail-state coverage would expand this item, so the existing scale/filter semantics remain.

One fixed seed per actual mode is sufficient for this guard. Seed 2 is checked against the production planner with both solver modes. The guard also rejects a future generator change that removes its nozzle. It does not establish a minimum random-sample success ratio.
