The original assertion in `item-before.txt` is therefore **FALSE as an effective pawn-size/T3 assertion**. Its base-size 32 and Miasma weight 0.15 components are **TRUE**. Its T3 component survives only for the separate def-level classifier.

## Consequences and unresolved gameplay concerns

The design response's classifier concern is real as a numeric discrepancy, but its consequences require precision. [TitanPassage.cs](../../src/RimMandrake/HugeThings/Source/Titanic/Events/TitanPassage.cs):55–73 uses `AdultTier`, home/commonality, water classification, season and passage-list gates. The calf's source home is land-classified Miasma; the adult's is water-classified Grey Sea. This permits the calf on a suitable Miasma map when other gates allow, without admitting the adult there. Correcting the calf's classifier from T3 to its supported maximum T2 would still pass the event's T2 minimum.

There is a concrete difference in spacing: `RM_TitanicKernel.cs:212–217` rejects a T3-classified candidate when a live T3 stands on the map, whereas a T2 candidate would remain eligible. `TitanPassage.cs:118–120` counts existing T3s using current `GetTier`. Thus the calf can be blocked as a T3 candidate without ever counting as a living T3 after arrival. This deserves a classifier-semantics review; it cannot be dismissed as merely a display label or fixed by pretending the calf has BodySize 32. Conversely, source comments explicitly intend calf-lineage eligibility and conservative def qualification, so the discrepancy alone does not establish that a lineage-based event classifier is unintended. No classifier correction or bug-free gameplay certification is included here.

The suggested scavenger consequence is overstated for this animal: [CorpseSiteVisitors.cs](../../src/RimMandrake/HugeThings/Source/Titanic/CorpseSite/CorpseSiteVisitors.cs):25–28 requires a predator with AdultTier None. This calf is non-predatory and would fail even if classified T2. Its exclusion is not evidence of a new scavenger regression.

The inherited combat concern remains substantial: the calf retains tail-sweep power 480, bulk power 120, health scale 20 and manhunter-on-damage chance 0.2 (`RM_SeaBeasts_Invented.xml:1328–1362`). Core stage melee factors are 0.5/0.75, leaving nominal tail-sweep products 240/360 before other combat mechanics. This sits poorly with the nursery sheet's “cheap to hunt.” Actual hits and combat outcomes were not tested. **This is an unresolved balance/specification concern, not accepted gameplay and not silently waived by KEEP.** Changing body size alone would not correct inherited tools or health. The mother's size inversion likewise remains visible rather than being claimed resolved.

The design response's spawn-age follow-up can be narrowed offline. The wild spawn IL calls ordinary `GeneratePawn` (`engine-selected.il.txt:213–218`); stranding (`RM_MapComponent_StrandingPools.cs:263–265`) and crèche young generation (`RM_SetPieceElement_AnchoredPawn.cs:124–130`) do not force a newborn age. With no selected age-generation curve and life expectancy 500, the retained default curve spans normalized ages 0.05–1.25 (`engine-selected.il.txt:1135–1184`), yielding proposal ages 25–625 years, already beyond the child's last stage. Under that normal generation branch, successful proposals are juveniles at size 16. This is not an observed stage distribution, and special newborn/fixed-age requests are separate branches. Nursery travel, maturation into another species and real encounter frequency remain unproved.
