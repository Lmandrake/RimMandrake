# Bedless pit prison — delegated FlowWorks trial, 2026-11-10

A drained hole should be a usable prison, without furnishing a hidden bedroom.

- The player can capture a downed raider from the rim of a drained D4 moat or pit with no prisoner bed in the hole; the raider becomes a prisoner and stays in that hole.
- The prisoner can be fed and kept through ordinary prisoner care without adding a bed; a prisoner bed elsewhere does not cause capture to move the prisoner out of the hole.
- With pit prisons disabled, the same bedless capture is refused, and ordinary bed-based prisons continue working.
- A repeatable acceptance scene asserts its starting conditions, distinguishes the enabled behavior from its disabled control, and restores terrain, liquid, things and changed settings after the test; relevant offline checks and a real game run support the claim.
