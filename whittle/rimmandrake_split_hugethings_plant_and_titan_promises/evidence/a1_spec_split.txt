  <description>Huge plants and huge animals that behave like their size. Mass has consequences.

The engine gives every plant and every pawn one cell, however big it is drawn. A
mushroom twenty cells across can only be clicked on the cell it grows from,
pawns walk straight through its stem, and walls get built inside it. A beast
the size of a house walks through a doorway, hides behind a sandbag and gets
penned like a chicken.

Huge Things makes two promises for anything that opts in.

GIANT PLANTS are as big as they look.
- A huge plant gets a solid trunk. The cells where its art touches the ground
  are solid (measured per species, and grown into as the plant grows), so pawns
  walk around it and nothing can be built inside it. You still walk under its
  cap. Clicking anywhere on its picture selects it. Trunks give partial cover,
  and shots into them hurt the plant.
- A trunk that grows across a doorway seals it: cut the plant down to get in
  or out.

TITANS have bodies with consequences.
- A huge animal gets a selection hitbox the size of its drawn body.
- A titan (any creature of body size 4 or more, or one a mod opts in) gets a
  size tier. Its crossing breaks what it walks through: heavy, colossal and
  titanic creatures crush crates and plants, breach walls, hole thin roofs and
  leave rubble, struggle under overhead mountain, and the biggest smash through
  giant plants while smaller titans go around the trunks like walls.
- Its death leaves remains: the largest become a harvest site worked over
  days, and scavengers and visitors come to compete for them. Smaller titans
  butcher for a curved yield.

With Large Pawns (neku.largepawns) loaded, titans also occupy 2x2 to 4x4
cells, using this mod's tiers; without it they stay one cell and everything
else still works.

Mod Settings: one master switch for giant plants and one for giant animals,
each with every detailed behaviour and its tuning beneath. All off is vanilla.

This is the engine only. A plant opts in with the RM_HugePlantExtension, a
race with the RM_HugePawnExtension or RM_TitanicExtension modExtension (or
simply a large enough body size). This mod replaces "RimMandrake: Titanic
Creatures" (mandrake.rm.titaniccreatures), which is now part of it.</description>
