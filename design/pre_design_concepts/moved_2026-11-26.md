# Tickets moved out of the list into ideas stores, 2026-11-26

This page is a record of one scan. It is not a concept (it has no concept fields) and it is not owed work. The scan ran
on the order of the owner (ledger item `RIMMANDRAKE_MOVE_IDEA_TICKETS_TO_PRE_DESIGN_CONCEPTS_HE_SUSPECTS_MANY`), through
the Conductor. His words: "Please scan for other tickets that are entirely ideas for new mods or new capabilities for a
mod that does not clearly yet exist. Add them to the new pre-design concepts (that is what that new reservoir should be
called and used for). They can be for mods, new agents, new pipelines, anything really. The more detailed the better."
(OWNER `file`, 2026-11-26T21:32:55Z.)

## What was read

778 open tickets on the list (`list_home.py json`, total 778 at 2026-11-26 about 6:50 PM PST). Method, measured:

- Every ticket was read by its id, kind, state, target, home and title. Its ledger text was read whole when any of
  these held: kind design, decision, feature, innovation or docs; target v2; or an idea word in the title or the id
  (idea, v2, concept, new mod, future, capability, brainstorm, and similar). That is 79 tickets.
- Of the 79, about 30 were read in full (prose file and events). The others were read to the point where one fact decided
  the class (a built state, a failure named, a blocked owner word, a work bundle, or a design file that holds the work).
- The other 699 tickets name a failure, a fix, a test, an audit, a redraw, an install, or a build of a thing with a
  design; or they are first-touch Designer passes or first north-star scripts of mods that exist. None was moved on a
  title alone, and none of these was read in full. A title can hide an idea; a later sweep may read them again.
- Tickets excluded by the brief: 149 parked by a block (23 kind owner, among them the 14 faction tickets of
  commit `e047237d5c`), kinds owed, standing, and the alarm tickets (OPS_*, STEVE_LANE_*), and the tickets that the Conductor filed on 2026-11-26.
- Most idea-shaped tickets of the list were already moved or placed before this scan: the 2026-11-22 ticket sweep of
  FlowWorks and the 2026-11-23 grouping by home (`RIMMANDRAKE_GROUP_OLD_TICKETS_BY_HOME_AND_KEEP_THE_LIST_FOR_GOOD`) had
  already put their ideas into next-version lists or given them a home. So few tickets are pure ideas.
