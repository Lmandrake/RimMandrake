The earlier record is a dry-run question rehearsal, not an approved root or waiver. The current concept folder had no record, sheet, log, next-version file or tree leaves to amend; the CASE explicitly establishes empty old bars. ## Scope and the place

1. **“What makes this mod itself?”** Readings: all approved mechanisms form a mandatory package; or land/life plus the stocked-pool cause/effect chain form this version. Consequence: the former requires mobile terrain, sale fulfilment and two quests before farming can pass. I take the latter under the present one-signature authority. The September husbandry ruling and October selected experience centre keeping a pool healthy. The October crab approval remains next-version authority, not rejected lore. No question asks him to choose again.

2. **“Weeping” / “secret shade.”** Readings: descriptions suffice; or the map shows dark wet faces and associated green against dry pale ground. Consequence: ordinary sand plus a labelled puddle could pass a def read yet disappoint on arrival. Take visible spatial contrast (B1). Frozen sheet §§1,5,9 explicitly require the mark. No fluid/humidity solver follows from the adjective. A static wet-face treatment tied to habitat can pass; an arbitrary wet decal on dry sun-facing stone cannot.

3. **“Every cold shaded face.”** Readings: all shaded cells are wet at any scale; or eligible exposed cold rock faces in the moisture habitat show the mark. Consequence: a literal cell rule wets colony interiors and underground mines. Take habitat-qualified exposed faces; natural overhang/face placement, not arbitrary roofs, defines where condensation belongs. The sheet's cold-face/wind story supplies the qualification. Shared shade supplies heat geometry, not a licence for a new water source under every constructed roof. Open seep oases are explicitly exempt from a visible-cliff requirement.

4. **“Oasis biome” / architecture.** Readings: the biome itself randomly creates all pools; or the already-authored named Oasis landmarks supply pools, while the biome defines stone country. Consequence: requiring every local map to contain a new oasis silently commissions placement/generation. Take the frozen September biome-plus-hand-placed-landmark ruling. Test the actual mutator handoff on existing tiles, and include non-landmark terrain as a dry control. No world assignment, worker-score sweep or alternate planet.

5. **“Concentric rings.”** Readings: exact circles of fixed widths; or legible water→green→scrub→dry bands following the actual pool edge. Consequence: geometric circles flatten seep/rock shapes and make a rectangular pen a failed oasis. Take edge-following bands, with breaks at rock/buildings and broader dry ground outside. The frozen sheet calls the bands a plan-view read, not an engineered circular collision shape. B1's pictures make the open seep exception explicit. Do not confuse these habitat bands with the farm's stock-health ripples.

6. **“Wind-hour” / weather.** Readings: old sheet prose commissions a daily fog/animal/harvest appointment; or later typed rejection kills that mechanism. Consequence: a daily scheduler fights weather and requires timed jobs. Existing rejection wins (shine portfolio verdict 4). Ordinary fog, wind and condensate fiction remain land. “Every dawn” in later descriptions must not secretly reinstall the clock on a tidally locked world. Born-and-dying water is also already dead (verdict 2), not a future task. The map's wind direction and solar direction are different quantities; neither fixes the other.

7. **“Low sun” / “safe shade.”** Readings: this biome has a local low-sun heat type and roofs do nothing; or the tile's geometry controls the one Direct Sun rule. Consequence: the shipped arrival text can direct a colonist away from a useful roof/parasol and toward a non-shade sun shield. Take the later standing rule. The local XML still contains `heatKind/heatOffsetC/minScaledHeatOffsetC` and the false advice, so this is concrete corrective work (T18), not an invitation to tune another biome sun. Hot steam is ordinary ambient temperature. Wet land is not automatic heat protection.

8. **“Own strange natives” / “comb.”** Readings: new art review or special custom AI for every exotic description; or keep accepted native silhouettes, ordinary behaviour and actual flight. Consequence: the former commissions dances, climbing, crests opening in wind and more variants. Take ordinary life with the existing comb identity; source headers explicitly leave optional AI unwired. Installed art is accepted by standing ruling. Incorrect disappearance/misrendering is still a functional issue. No yield or variant count becomes a bar.

9. **“One home.”** Readings: evict every cross-biome species now; or retain and annotate current placements. Consequence: a cleansing pass could overturn a prior casting. Take the stopped-evictions ruling. Bantha's pilgrim multi-home is already documented in the fauna roster. Census the other effective memberships, do not infer a generic algorithm or force a migration simulation. `RM_Dewshrooms` is currently shared with TheRot: the standing duplicate-and-diverge ruling requires a distinct variant; sharing is not silently authorised forever (T16).

10. **“The native is built.”** Readings: a race/kind def proves it appears naturally; or a spawn route must also work. Consequence: mirrik can remain an invisible taming/flight promise. Current biome XML omits it despite its def and the legacy inventory saying native fauna are wired. Take an unproven spawn route, retain the species and reconcile loaded wiring (T16). Source presence is current implementation evidence; historical load close is stale gameplay proof. Zero world tiles tells neither story.

## The farm

11. **“A pool.”** Readings: one natural connected water body; or a player-designated pen with stable identity. Consequence: feeding a small zone can either credit a whole lake or just that farm. Take the explicit named contiguous water zone; code tracks zones, not flood-fill. Adjacent separate zones remain independent. Refuse disconnected extensions; do not silently invent split/merge identities. Terrain/bridge edits invalidate covered/dry cells, keep surviving identity/debt and show the change. Delete-zone releases residents; redraw is not a debt-erasing cure. R3 is an attackable implementation choice.

12. **“Stocking.”** Readings: any nearest pen; or the pen the farmer selected. Consequence: a nearest-pen scanner puts breeders in a crowded pool while a new empty one waits. Take a selected destination with an explicit no-valid-destination result. Existing automatic nearest-pen code is insufficient (T13). Reservations must survive a concurrent worker, destination removal and reload. A capture/release replacement must preserve breeding-relevant state or an explicit equivalent, not reroll its fertility.

13. **“Breeding stock” / “sustainable.”** Readings: meat comes from repeating net/release; or a founding group replenishes itself so chosen surplus can be harvested again. Consequence: the laziest implementation is a cumbersome hunting pipeline. Take real repeat production with retained breeders. Existing neutral released pawns have reproduction data but no demonstrated working cycle; don't call reproduction absent merely because no custom kernel exists. Verify normal mating, nutrition and residency before adding local integration. Whole-map model is mandatory because changing one birth/yield number cannot settle pathing and work competition.

14. **“Harvest” / “overdraw is a choice.”** Readings: every reachable stocked animal is an automatic work target; or player-requested surplus respects a breeder reserve with deliberate warned overdraw. Consequence: enabling the work type currently empties the farm without a decision. Take the latter, with the smallest per-pen controls: destination, viable reserve, harvest request. No scheduling dashboard or species optimisation tree. Protect a reproductive group, not merely arbitrary survivors. A player who explicitly chooses complete overdraw may empty the pen; that consequence is not a defect. T13 covers the missing intent.

15. **“Feed.”** Readings: destroy any nutritious item and reset the clock; or deliver permitted suitable food that supports the actual residents. Consequence: a fed-looking farm can starve while consuming meals intended for colonists. Take real delivery and real nutrition under player food policy, safe bank access and no credit for an invalid pen. Existing feed amount is independent of population and currently consumes food even after destination deletion. The model determines workload/feed demand; numbers remain tuning. No cocoon-waste item, rain-grown bloom or water-budget income is required.

16. **“Healthy” / “thin” / “silent.”** Readings: current population fraction plus inspect text is enough; or visible cause-aware cues allow ordinary intervention. Consequence: a fed crowded pen currently looks healthy until emergence, and an empty overlay can look like missing graphics. Take visible productive/thin/crowded/empty/vhorrin distinctions with inspect explanation and an explicit empty marker. “Silent” is figurative absence of living-stock activity, not a commissioned audio system. T12 builds the gauge; T20 reads it. A warning arriving in the same mutation that removes stock is too late if ordinary care could have prevented that loss.

17. **“Nasty and way too active.”** Readings: arbitrary attacks on drinkers; or catching/handling costs and traceable escape pressure in the farm. Consequence: the former contradicts the frozen ambush ban. Take real minor catch injury, moving targets and recapture, with safe feeding. No scripted maiming/death lottery. Ordinary wound treatment can still carry ordinary game risk. The exact injury odds never pass or refuse the mod. Negative controls isolate handler-job injury from enemy fire/heat.

18. **“The net lands.”** Readings: freeze the target before a touch channel; or aim, projectile travel and coverage at impact. Consequence: speed becomes irrelevant under a guaranteed channel. The October net ruling settles this already (OWNER:1791380355976654559). Keep moving-target misses. The handler-fate proof explicitly tolerates capture failure and is not a capture-success certificate. Skarrin is not assumed to fly because a legacy proof calls it a flier: jumping is not sustained flight. Actual mirrik/dewglider and campaign flyers obey the flight ruling and owner-presence limit.

19. **“Vhorrin grows” / “eats the farm.”** Readings: any random monster appearance and a state label; or stressed pen stock becomes the predator and its presence causes additional resident loss. Consequence: a decorative vhorrin beside an unaffected farm cannot deliver the selected experience. Take the latter, confined to designated pens, with a readable conversion and causal stock pressure. Healthy, adequately spaced, fed pens do not roll emergence. No ambient wild monster. Existing kernel only flags emergence and excludes vhorrin from starvation; actual extra consumption is not implemented there. The ruled stocked-pool sheet §1 explicitly places pens outside the truce, and §3 Pens and pool zones separates husbandry violence from drinkers by geometry. R6 names the shared dependency's required narrow pen-cell exclusion hook. R14 uses ordinary hunger-driven predation on residents inside that pen, leaving a partly eaten carcass and a cue naming eater and victim. The protected drinking margin remains subject to shared hunt suppression and guilty-hit retribution. Culling ends predator pressure. T13 integrates ordinary predation, T18 supplies the exclusion need, and the Situation and transaction generator calibrates field membership, boundary crossings, concurrent jobs and save continuity. This corrects my prior assumption that pen water remained protected and removes the custom pulse-feeding deadline.

20. **“Crowded” / “neglected.”** Readings: visual impression of busy water; or an actionable management state that warns before avoidable loss. Consequence: equal populations in different pen areas can be judged differently without the player knowing why. Take explicit cause text and consistent warning state, derived from resident population/eligible footprint/feed need. Baseline kernel thresholds are checks, not sacred ecology. A pen resize must re-evaluate its capacity without resetting debt. The season model can change the chosen rules/thresholds if warnings and viable reproduction cannot coexist.

21. **“It leaves” / “nearby.”** Readings: teleport to any standable cell in a radius; or traceable traversal to a reachable moist-bank/water route. Consequence: current despawn/respawn can put an eel through a wall on dry ground with only a dev log. Take visible movement and origin/destination cue, retain a blocked-route eel in the pen. Existing chance is not crowding-gated; occasional uncrowded escapes remain within the active-stock promise. No invented daily event, pot/barrel incident or path through closed walls. R12 is attackable if real locomotion cannot support its wet-route assumption; geometry and state reads decide (T14).

22. **“Cull saves the pool.”** Readings: remove the debug-spawned vhorrin and give meat; or end actual predator pressure and allow viable survivors/restocking to return to production. Consequence: the former pays a feast while an empty farm remains dead. Take causal emergence→pressure→deliberate cull→care/restock→production. Keep the documented single-handler action; multi-handler coordination is later. No owner waiver was found in the dry-run record, and none is invented or re-asked. The choice is delegated small scope, not attribution of a waiver.

23. **“Crashes for a year.”** Readings: save a hard annual embargo after overdraw; or lose the stock and future production until the player restores a viable farm. Consequence: the annual lockout adds a punitive timer unrelated to actual remaining breeders. Take stock-loss/recovery now, annual-lockout proposal later. The selected experience specifies wrong life from unhealthy water, not a duration. This is a scope decision about an unimplemented draft, not a change to a standing tuning bar.

24. **“Doubt” / “Rimcuisine.”** Readings: descriptions or a third-party food mod; or actual ingestion memories in an independently usable free-tier kitchen. Consequence: eating the alien beasts can have no game cost or be unavailable without another package. Owner card requires the real mood economy. Keep direct/ingredient and callous-trait differences, modest existing recipes, eater-specific feast/broth memory. No keeper multiplier, ideology ban, named roasts, automatic fat quality or colony feast event. Those are separate extras. Huldu's later hunter description cannot nullify its explicitly commissioned doubt food; correct contradictory narrative text without a behaviour rewrite.

25. **“Every native drinks its own water.”** Readings: a pool display is enough; or applicable native thirst systems must recognise accessible environmental water. Consequence: live natives can dehydrate beside the actual source. Take the standing November ruling. Test every effective native race whose active needs include drinking, and match an inaccessible-water control. This creates no new universal thirst system when none is installed. Bladder-fruit food text is not proof of a custom thirst-restoring effect; no such special consumable power is commissioned.

## Shared boundaries, defaults and proof

26. **“Truce” / “full water.”** Readings: all combat is forbidden in a wide safe zone; or existing hunt suppression plus faction-specific response to actual guilty damage at standing water. Consequence: an invulnerability zone can nullify raids and mask farm injury interactions. Take the shared radius/guilty-hit law, tame-predator suppression included, with designated pen cells excluded as required by the ruled stocked-pool sheet §1 and §3. The dependency needs that narrow exclusion in the field read shared by suppression and retribution, including overlapping water radii and zone edits/reload. Drinkers at the margin remain protected. A thin fish population does not mean environmental water has dried. No new scarcity-water event. The engine can punish drafted defensive fire if its guilty flag says so; do not promise a new battle-level moral judge.

27. **“Farm violence at truce water.”** Readings: every removal/injury rouses the herd; or designated farm conversions and factionless-stock handling scratches do not manufacture a player guilty hit. Consequence: ordinary harvesting may become unusable with truce on. Source currently uses `Destroy(Vanish)` for conversions and stock as injury instigator; shared retribution needs positive guilty faction-attributed damage. This argues for compatibility but is not a measured callback trace. Default is no retaliation from farm care. Designated pen cells are outside the field under the stocked-pool ruling, and player weapon attacks on the protected drinking margin retain normal guilt and response. T18/model generator checks both field consumers, farm callbacks, zone edits and reload against that ruled geometry.

28. **“Optional” / defaults.** Readings: a checkbox plus default-on machinery; or next-version content cannot create things, jobs, letters, incidents or persistent gameplay by default. Consequence: hiding the old crab UI still lets its pool/quests change the test. Take callback-level switch-off and positive on-controls. Built code/defs stay resolvable. Existing saves keep readable suspended unique content and reversible state; never delete a living crab because a toggle changed. Full functionality of enabled next-version content is not certified by a gate test.

29. **“Extras coupled in code.”** Readings: an extra stays whenever kept code names it; or unused production is gated and the kept caller is decoupled. Consequence: dewsilk could be smuggled back as feed, or the condenser could be claimed necessary to get water. Current feed accepts ordinary nutrition; pens use existing water; no kept job calls the old crab. Gate the fat recipe without disabling needed broth/feast or their thought defs; gate found-tech plant callbacks without disabling shared shade; keep scanner targets as passive resolved strings. T1–T11/T21/T23 name actual callers.

30. **“Scanner” / “shade lessons.”** Readings: both are a new experience to certify; or distinguish a standing all-biome scanner dependency from a local optional research interaction. Consequence: deleting the scanner hookup would overturn its owner card, while retaining research would keep an unnecessary mechanic. Keep existing read-only scanner interface (patch header cites the 2026-10-06 all-biome decision), silent for absent deferred targets; move local dripfringe/shadefern study effects off. 31. **“Proven” / “loads clean.”** Readings: a closed item, mock, source field or existing DLL certifies gameplay; or current matching real-mechanism evidence is required. Consequence: the forced harvest proof can go green while all ordinary work liquidates breeders. Take source as implementation evidence, historical proof as stale, unsupported experience as missing/unknown. Existing `validation.py` bypasses READ, sustainable breeding, live truce and autonomous work in crucial places; its mocks do not cure those limits. 32. **“The season.”** Readings: accelerated pulse arithmetic with everything else frozen; or actual game ticks preserve needs, AI competition, weather, growth, random scheduling, serialization and two action orders on a full map. Consequence: a beautiful isolated pen misses whole-map grass depletion, stock flooding or job storms. Take the latter. Offline model output remains a prediction until calibrated, and never replaces B9's full running map. Save at the middle and resume; continue the stress schedule to the hundredth day. “A season” is the game's quadrum-length observation interval, not a day/night cycle on Ash'karr.

33. **“Bars” / “all rows.”** Readings: a requirement per def value/variant or old check; or experiences that survive renormalisation. Consequence: a balance pass would relock the entire mod. Re-derived B1–B9 from the frozen place, owner husbandry/truce words, selected experience and standing laws, then compared the old walk line by line. Def fields, density/fish tables, source wiring, rot ticking, yields, probabilities, job durations, settings ranges, negative probes and recipe counts are checks C1–C13; zone/conservation/algorithm choices are R1–R14. Optional dewsilk/condenser/study/rare-loot/fat work moves with its extra. Worker scoring is inert and has no player bar. The old tree has no leaves, so none are fabricated for the mapping.

34. **“Whole native life” / exceptional products.** Readings: every unusual native needs a new personal system; or native life uses ordinary gathering/taming/food/timber/caravan routes with only the farm signature adding bespoke behaviour. Consequence: a dewsilk factory or ssurr aura could hide in life. Take the latter: the deletion arguments in census distinguish ordinary leather/forage/timber from independent cocoon manufacture, aura, terrain digging and fat processing. Keep the existing kitchen routes only because the owner explicitly made raised alien stock into a food/mood choice. Do not commission more dishes merely to enrich the roster.

35. **“Scarce timber.”** Readings range from no harvestable native trees to forest-wide timber abundance. The former forces imports for any wooden construction, while the latter removes the desert material choice. I take gatherable timber in separated native trees, with local cutting limiting further wooden building. This is my delegated reading of the ordinary timber cost in the frozen flora roster and the existing definition. B2 marks “scarce” as figurative and includes both wrong maps as reject pictures. The full-map natural tree distribution and ordinary harvest output must support the comparison across authored oasis and non-landmark maps, before cutting and after regrowth. A cleared corner alone proves nothing.

## Second attack: the cheapest wrong whole mod

A builder could preserve every def, add a pretty ring overlay, stop autonomous harvest, and demonstrate a forced net/stock/harvest cycle. It would still fail if neutral released stock wanders out or never breeds, feeding only alters a timestamp, a healthy crowded cue offers no intervention window, or truce/cull callbacks retaliate on handlers. Therefore B3 explicitly demands reproduction without fresh capture for each harvest, B5 demands early distinct danger, B6 demands actual stock pressure and recovery, and B8 runs the farm care actions under active truce. B9 forbids weather/AI/growth locks. The model/instrument debt remains visible rather than turning these experiences into a def-count checklist.

The cheapest wrong *land* is uniform sand with a decorative pool. B1 requires the actual map composition, including the open seep counterexample and usable dry shoulders. The cheapest wrong *food choice* is exotic names plus flavour text; B7 requires exact ingredient memories in actual eaters with ordinary fish and trait controls. No further owner question is needed to reject those substitutes.
