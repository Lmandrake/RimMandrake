# Cracked Lands — proposed Design Version 0.1.0

Status: Designer proposal, 2026-11-17; no implementation or acceptance claimed. Every new scope choice and construction rule below is **delegated**, the Designer's call under this cleanse brief. This folder does not amend his words or activate work. The concept directory contains no record.json, sheet.md, tree.json, log.md or next-version file; the historical inventory and dialogue are in design/RimMandrake/designer_codex_2026-11-09/CrackedLands/. The CASE specifies no old leaves. The historical record's injury ceiling is stale against ANSWERS_OWNER.md, 2026-11-10.

## The mod in a player's words

The Cracked Lands are dry canyon country where shade and high ground decide where you can live. Water chimes announce a flood that fills the canyon floor from bank to bank, so you leave useful work behind to get people out. When the water drains, wet mud feeds a short burst of plants and small animals, then the ground dries and the canyon grows quiet again.

Land: cracked pale pans and red-brown flood stains, cut rock, shaded strips of vegetation, dry weather, and usable upper ground with refuges cut into cliff faces.

Life: the existing alien roster, with ordinary forage, materials, predators and calls, plus real flight wherever the effective roster contains fliers; dormant pan animals and flood-born irqit make the wet/dry change visible.

**Signature:** water chimes interrupt work before a potentially lethal bank-to-bank flood, whose retreat briefly feeds the canyon before dry ground returns.

Runner-up: discovering hidden water and carrying the Swale technique away. It loses because it adds prospecting, an item economy, a persistent unlock and a water-source loop; none is necessary to evacuate, survive, use the wet ground and see it dry. Its earlier approval remains a promise for a later version, with its tier and unlock rulings intact.

## Why a player reaches for it

A gravship crew gets a place worth reading before it works: open floor is an opportunity on borrowed time; the bank is where one can trust a camp. A settled colony gets a recurring interruption rather than a second permanent fertile biome. Prediction, not a bar: that interruption and short return should make a remembered departure and a useful visit. No minimum enjoyment, crop profit, spectacle size or commodity price is asserted.

The source is the compose line, the owner-selected experience in biome_experiences_2026-10-20.md, the cracked-lands sheet, and the November 10 answers: damage may be lethal from drowning and impact; prompt evacuation prioritises people; slow transport on the floor is a gamble; wet fertility ends at drying. Vanilla flood/temporary-terrain systems and FlowWorks already provide water and restoration vocabulary. This mod earns its place through the canyon's readable geography, warning and wet/dry sequence, not a replacement liquid engine.

## Using it: first hour, routine, colony scale

On arrival the player can distinguish the low floor, cliff bank and reachable high ground. A concise ordinary arrival explanation names chimes, low ground and the wet window, and correctly says roofs provide sun cover but not elevation. High-water stains, real chime anchors and visible access to high ledges supply the same information in the map. No carving, survey, quest or scanner is required to understand it.

The player camps and builds on an upper tier, works the shade edge, forages existing nabbuq where mature, harvests ruqqal or dye if useful, and can hunt or tame ordinary roster animals using existing interfaces. Uttaqar is a dangerous crag predator, not mandatory stock; the untrainable muttavaq is not a tame work machine. No promise of a new mount or all-purpose domestic beast is invented. Ordinary food, textile and minor dye yields are life; tuning belongs to checks. Harrovaq does not regain its removed wood yield. Thornwood's yield must obey the standing plant economy, not an obsolete local comment.

Routine: hear chimes, abandon the current haul if necessary, move to the nearest genuinely high reachable bank, wait for the marked flood to drain, use the wet interval, then resume dry routines. No automatic recall of colonists is added. Neutral human visitors and trained player animals need the existing refuge behaviour because the player cannot command visitors and cannot sensibly micromanage every trained animal. It sends only eligible, mobile, undrafted, nonhostile seekers, holds them during danger, then releases them. Prisoners, mental breaks, hostiles and downed pawns need player or vanilla rescue. No unreachable pawn teleports or silently despawns; a blocked seeker reports why shelter cannot be reached.

At a 40-colonist colony, simultaneous recall from ordinary reachable work sites must clear mobile people if ordered at the first chime. It need not finish loaded hauls, evacuate prisoners unaided, repair a deliberately blocked stair or rescue every downed pawn. Workable access means a high destination reached by a legal path before the local front; the geometry model tests it using actual path costs. Broad bank refuges and distributed routes, not a tiny decorative pocket with a limitless refuge flag, must accommodate the traffic. The warning is tuned to that task; keeping an old lead-time number is never a reason to reduce the flood.

## The running contract

**Local landform, not world generation.** The shipped world and its biome assignment remain fixed. Constructing or loading a local encounter map may supply its canyon layout, as the current ledge and wreck GenSteps already do. No planet worker, rarity setting, new world, tile paint or alternative planet is part of this proposal. Zero world tiles is expected. The existing worker is recommended for retirement and must be made ineligible, not relabelled as next-version worldgen.

A local map layout must supply a saved landform record: a main, non-self-crossing reach with upstream entry and downstream outlet; ordered cross sections; low-bed cells including covered ground and built-floor locations, with a dry mineral baseline separate from generated or deposited soil; solid natural rock; upper banks and cliff-cut refuge pockets with legal access; and readable matching high-water marks. This is a new **required specification**, not a claim that such a record exists. The present random seed/BFS is not this record. The cheapest producer is local canyon layout data produced together with the rock layout, not interpreting raw vanilla elevation noise as a physical water level. Single main reach, bends and varying widths suffice; tributary hydrology and terrain erosion simulation do not ship.

The bed is the union of the recorded low cross sections. Cross sections cover the full opening between opposing natural banks, including bends and widenings. A cell shared by sections belongs to the earliest section along the reach. Solid natural rock is not water, but mining low rock opens its already recorded bed cell to the next front; a low roof or floor never turns it into upper land. High bank mining does not invent a lower elevation. No minimum global percentage or random cell cap substitutes for this topology. Outside the main bed, upper banks stay dry apart from independent FlowWorks liquid. The mask, inspect information and marks must agree. An invalid or absent landform is an explicit defect/readable diagnostic, never a fallback patch advertised as a canyon flood.

A front advances in section order, wetting the entire open section, then the water stands and drains. This is a discrete river event, not fluid dynamics. Warning must precede the earliest contact at upstream work sites. Chimes are anchored in the canyon's actual crack/water line and increase urgency before contact; they never point along a fictitious far-corner-to-random-seed line. Accepted vanilla clips remain acceptable (owner October 3); no new audio commission. Continuous roar belongs to present water. The optional wind/ticking/hush dramaturgy moves to next version; ordinary tarruq calls remain. Peakstorm Light remains dry land weather, and its existing probabilistic clock bias stays part of the same runoff chain; it is neither rain nor an exact timer and cannot shorten an announced escape interval.

Living, grounded pawns contacted by the front receive an impact consequence; continued immersion exposes breathing pawns to drowning. No downed/low-health blanket immunity, injury ceiling, guaranteed death or compulsory scripted casualty. Health, bodies, protection and ordinary damage resolution decide outcomes. Real airborne flight is above the surface hazard until landing; an animation or a race label alone confers no immunity. Nonbreathing machines take appropriate impact but do not acquire biological drowning. Shared trait immunities must be enumerated in the damage adapter and exposed by the instrument. Floor buildings do not promise flood protection: low accessible rooms remain hazardous behind roofs/doors. The first version need not simulate structural collapse, item sweeping or a new water pressure solver. Existing FlowWorks seals still govern its channels; they do not cancel the externally arriving river on other bed cells.

“Clay” means the pale cracked natural bed surface identified by the local landform/substrate record; no chemistry system or specially named Clay def is required. Only actually wetted, eligible natural ground receives flood fertility. While wet, it behaves as visibly wet, highly fertile ground; once dry the **extra** fertility ends. **Flood-laid ordinary soil (delegated rule R7a):** at retreat, each actually wetted natural bed cell still under the flood writer's ownership receives ordinary Soil if the shared Direct Sun reader classifies it as geometrically shaded. Walls, rock and roofs can supply geometric shade; low glow, artificial darkness, weather attenuation and the temporary water layer cannot. A roof can therefore shade eligible natural ground without making it safe from the flood. Built floors, bridges, ore, solid rock and FlowWorks-owned excavations are ineligible. This deposits soil even over original Sand; restoring a Sand snapshot is not deposition. Keep the deposition record separate from the temporary wet-fertility layer.

Ordinary deposited Soil survives drying indefinitely while geometric shade and natural-ground ownership remain; it never becomes SoilRich through repetition. The extra wet fertility alone ends at drying. If shade is lost, a dry deposit returns at the next terrain update to its recorded dry mineral baseline; while still wet the temporary benefit lasts to its own expiry, then the exposed cell returns to that baseline. Regaining shade after a deposit has been removed does not recreate it: another actual wetting and retreat is required. If shade returns before an already-wet deposit dries, that still-present deposit may persist as ordinary Soil; no new deposition occurs. Player floors/excavations replace the flood's ownership, so neither shade loss nor expiry undoes them. Repeated floods refresh the same Soil without stacking fertility or spreading sediment to neighbours. New deposition can extend only to newly wetted, geometrically shaded, eligible cells inside the recorded natural bed; under unchanged wet footprint and shade its extent cannot grow.

The contract is identical for the free tier's Sand/Soil fertility palette and the campaign's pure-Sand override (WildAnimals_CrackedLands.xml Op 5). In flood-managed natural bed cells the layout's dry mineral baseline is Sand in the current palettes, including where the free generator supplied Soil or SoilRich without flood provenance; after the cycle, eligible cells shaded at retreat retain their deposited Soil while continuing shade holds, and exposed bed returns to that mineral baseline in either tier. Terrain outside the flood-managed bed is not rewritten. Snapshot original terrain, mineral baseline, ownership and deposition provenance before a temporary layer; resolve expiry to the deposition outcome, not blindly to the old terrain. Preserve intervening player edits. This is bounded ordinary habitat soil, not the permanent rich-soil windfall forbidden by the later wet-window answer. No comprehensive farming ladder: a wet interval must support useful native gathering/browsing and visible growth; it need not mature a newly planted vanilla long crop. Utinni's no-farming campaign is not assigned a farming loop.

The same per-cell wet-until record drives fertile benefit, qirra opening/dieback and the flood's pushed soak. Rewetting extends only cells reached again. ExplosiveGrowth may also see real water supplied independently by FlowWorks; those cells remain wet because of that supply, not an expired flood. Distinguish flood-derived wetness from other sources. The engine owns swelling and terminal moments; this mod records the exact accepted handoff. The model must include terminal fires or spreading behaviour of the loaded roster, not freeze growth below its terminal state to get a clean picture.

Qirra is a red-brown wet-ground plant, opening only on natural recently wetted clay; dye is an ordinary minor harvest. Irqit are bounded cohorts on actual retreating wet clay, marked flood-born, living until their own local wet interval ends; the next hatch represents their reproduction, not an unbounded second breeding simulator. Each cohort keeps its own deadline; rewetting its nursery may extend its wet period, a flood elsewhere may not. A transported, tamed or carried irqit still carries a readable remaining lifespan; death leaves a corpse where it is, including inventory/world-pawn handling. No rescue-by-pickup immortality. Muttavaq remains a pawn with a readable sealed state, not a vanished creature; wet contact wakes it, dry ground lets a wild mobile animal seal again, damage wakes it, and ownership/drafted/downed/mental-state guards prevent involuntary sealing. It is neutral and never becomes a scripted raid. Its ordinary wading/feeding remains; a depth-editing walking weir is next version. Uttaqar stays in the crag band; muttavaq in the pans. Spawning and ordinary habitat preferences enforce the contrast; chasing prey, fleeing or player orders may cross bands without teleporting the animal.

## Colony story, costs and failures

The remembered change is a job abandoned at the chimes, a person rescued ahead of water, and the return to living mud. Costs are lost work time, exposed travel, damage when late, ordinary harvesting labour and a temporary opportunity. No bespoke currency, offering, shame hediff or funeral mechanic. Killing a sleeper remains an ordinary hunt/butchery choice with existing wax/meat outputs; its moral description is lore, not a new punishment.

A flood missed has a condition, water, roar and damage/drowning information. A dead pawn is a corpse, a sealed animal has inspectable identity, and spent irqit leave carrion with an aggregate dry-out message. No repeated per-cell letter storm. A broken refuge route reports blocked access; it does not promise a safe place that cannot be reached. User disabling damage is a supported preference and clearly described; that configuration is not evidence for default danger.

## Edges and neighbours

No worldgen; no new permanent river/lake; no local rain or snow; no flier nests. Existing fixed open-water locations and their catch remain ordinary imported land/life, not a reason to create water elsewhere. Only Moisture Farmers are resident lore; Junkers, Hutts, Jawa and tribes are road traffic. Existing Farmer templates are ordinary land dependencies with upper-tier siting, not a new faction simulator. Hutt toll encounters, bloom-market pricing and the guaranteed campaign witness quest are outside this version's biome gameplay. The reusable arm service remains a dependency and must preserve the same warning/danger semantics for its external caller; it does not create a quest by itself.

The base and the campaign are not two designs with different hazards. RM owns invented content with a sufficiently complete ordinary roster; canon species ride the Utinni patch layer. No roster eviction is authorised here. The patch currently replaces fauna with RM_Lirrith/RM_Ommok plus its added cast; migration must preserve membership and fix ownership, not trade one sparse roster for another. Flagged multi-homing: RM_Lirrith is defined by Abyss, RM_Ommok has other biome consumers, Cauldron now has its own thornwood copy (RM_TwistingThornwood versus RM_CrackedThornwood), so the historical shared-thorn warning is not a current thornwood multi-home, and can-cell/convor feeding travel has an explicit in-game reason. The aspirational Alien_Bestiary is naming/lore context, not evidence those proposed creatures exist. The current free roster has no flier; airborne feeding is required only for an effective roster containing a flier, currently the Bestiary-gated campaign cast. The free tier must have no walking fictional flier and no resident flier nest; it owes no new species in this version. A new invented RM flier to fill that parity hole is recorded for next version, not borrowed from a neighbour or silently added by T29. Flight acceptance uses deterministic Pawn_FlightTracker state for airborne feeding travel and grounded landing, never a visual hunt; visual flight: joint owner session only. Ordinary life/plant looks and recordings exclude flight. Woolamander remains a walking resident. Keep current shipping labels; the full internal rename is owed, not a request to reopen the earlier save gate.

Dependencies, not local redesigns:

- FlowWorks: excavation identity/depth/fill, driver outcomes and normal liquid/pathing. Honour NotOwned/Refused without a fallback terrain write; never turn a canal or SUPERDEEP into soil. Independent pumps/build/edit actions must survive recede. Swale's own mechanism remains FlowWorks-owned; canyon discovery/refill integration moves together to next version. Free-tier normal Swale availability remains; campaign discovery gating is not waived. Until discovery ships, the campaign must not silently grant it. The whole bar unlocks all **kept Cracked Lands mechanics**, not future campaign technology.
- CreatureBehaviors/Sun and Shade: shared sun geometry, exposure/path-cost readers, flight safeguards and existing creature traits. No biome heat kind, local substitute shade grid or flyer fiction with a walking pawn. Veqma's existing def-wide glow-limit eligibility remains; the local neighbour-count proxy must additionally agree with geometric shade, not redefine Direct Sun. No sun value is inferred from a biome label.
- ExplosiveGrowth: accepted per-cell soak, visible growth and authored terminal actions; no second growth engine.
- EnvironmentalHazards: ordinary arrival explanation and existing hazard visibility/services; consume a supported drowning/damage service if available, otherwise a thin canyon contact adapter is required, never a second hazards framework.
- Wreckage: ordinary canyon wreck field, inherited deconstruction and weathering. Map-start wrecks remain shared world texture; repeated recede wreck rewards and their claim systems move. Global Wreckage rules are not rewritten.
- Bones: ordinary butchery byproducts of existing corpses; no fossil-equals-bones conversion or extra fossil-crafting engine. Include its output in the whole food/material accounting.
- Acoustic Scanner: independent service; this mod's special prospecting payload is off for next version, with no false claim that static veqma proves hidden water.
- Shared SettingsKit/HarmonyResilience and composed Biomes packaging: settings and patch-health infrastructure. The source folder is not evidence of a standalone shipping contract. All DLC are assumed; no DLC-absent test matrix.

## Settings

Keep searchable, grouped, resettable, persisted settings with effective scope and current status. Master flood, damage, warning staging, plant soak, wet aftermath life, sleeper wake/seal and refuge automation each have explicit controls; ordinary land, existing roster and references remain loadable with all off. Timings are tunable checks, not bars. Peakstorm weather needs its own major-feature weather control as well as the bias switch; all-off leaves dry ordinary land/weather and passive roster.

Shipped defaults enable all kept mechanics; next-version sections explicitly say “Next version — off by default”. Existing cross-biome toggles remain off with the standing unsupported label; add no new one. Dev map controls remain dev tools, never natural flood suppliers in acceptance. A control cannot silently replace the natural river's geometry. Disable during warning cancels cleanly; disable during water performs ownership-safe recede, stops damage/roar, and lets already created living things finish a readable lifecycle. Re-enabling after a long idle or loading an old idle clock gives a full new warning. No stale immediate hit. Existing saves retain defs and entities; optional systems stop producing new things and safely release jobs, not delete existing pawns. Fresh-map absence is the switch-off acceptance test. New-map effects are labelled local map creation; no “worldgen” knob.

## Four stories

**First hour.** A small crew lands above a cut. They see the flood stain below their camp and a path into a high cliff pocket. A worker finds mature food at the shade edge. The flat floor looks easier for a loaded return; the player now knows what the chimes would interrupt.

**Ordinary week.** The canyon is mostly dry. Tarruq calls cross the slots, a crag predator makes one route unattractive, and dormant pans remain where the crew learnt them. Clear weather changes to dry Peakstorm Light without delivering local rain. The crew can leave without a flood; no ritual forces the schedule.

**Worst day.** Chimes ring with workers spread along the floor. Recall gets mobile people up; a player who insists on finishing a slow load risks impact and drowning. A visitor takes a real refuge path. A blocked path is reported; the game does not invent a rescue. A late pawn may die, but a flood does not demand a death to complete its story.

**Season's turn.** Water crosses the entire low reach, drains, and leaves wet ground busy with swelling plants and irqit. The colony gathers what it can; its dug channel remains its channel. A save made mid-cycle reloads into the same phase. The wet benefit ends, irqit leave corpses, pans seal and the next quiet stretch begins. A larger colony returning in another order meets the same geography and costs, without accumulating permanent rich soil or reward incidents.
