# Instruments specified, not built or measured

Priority is the order below. An output is a prediction until its calibration agrees with the actual effective mod/engine build. Record the assembly/def/settings/fixture fingerprints, raw comparison and disagreement; historical selftest passes and a timestamp do not establish currency. Changing a footprint/clock/consumer invalidates the relevant calibration. Numeric agreement thresholds below are instrument tolerances, never passing bars.

There is no credible offline proxy for whether a chime is audible in the normal mix, a canyon reads as a canyon, a plant visibly swells or the full sequence remains understandable. Those require B1/B2/B6/B8 non-flight looks/playback with state capture; flight is read only from actual Pawn_FlightTracker state, and any visual flight check is a joint owner session only; T26 specifies them. No model invents a body damage formula to replace real engine damage resolution.

## M1: Canyon geometry and evacuation

**Question:** Can every open section fill from bank to bank while real upper ground remains reachable by promptly recalled mobile people, including a crowded colony, ahead of the earliest local water contact?

Informs B1/B2/B3/B4/B7/B8, R1/R2/R4/R5/R6/R7a/R12; unknown meetings `landform` and `warning_clock`, and the crowd contribution to `population`. Build **first**, before tuning coverage or chime lead.

Inputs: an exported local rock/layout record, independent bank contours, ordered bed sections and dry mineral baseline, entry/outlet, access paths, doors, floor/roof ownership, refuge capacity, shared terrain and sun path costs, natural/armed warning clock and each station's contact time. No world generation. Run the actual geometry/clock kernel where available, plus an independent expected section-union oracle from the layout boundary, not a second invocation of FloodCells.

Range: maps with axes 150–500 cells; all integer cell translations of each layout's bank/access/roof/door relationship, rotations and reflections; section widths 1–80, bends, necks and widening, rocks/pillars, clipped edge entrances, low mined rock and high pockets. Sweep legal path lengths from adjacent refuge to the longest route. Parties 1–6 and 7–40 colonists, distributed and clustered starts; normal unladen recall, finish-current-job/loaded transport, blocked approach, downed rescue as an explicitly different case. Sweep warning/front timing at shipped value and ±25%, then values needed to remove any counterexample; no selected value becomes a bar. Process one geometry equivalence class at a time; retain each distinct failure, not every duplicate tile.

Assumptions: the local-map record is honest and persists, cell movement is represented by real game path costs, the discrete front follows sections, no player edits a path between sampled events. A static shortest path alone is a lower bound; reservation/door/crowding overhead comes from actual path replay or calibrated upper-bound queue time. Do not claim crowd fairness from independent paths with no contention. Roofs are not banks. This is not pressure, fluid-volume, erosion or planet hydrology.

**Calibration before belief:** on actual native disposable maps export the layout independently of flood state, observe contact cells/ticks and real pawn paths/arrival with the default mechanics all on. Include a straight reach, bent reach, bottleneck, covered low room, longest route and crowded bank approach in all distinct orientations. Require exact agreement on bed/high/solid membership and event order; contact tick agreement within the runtime adapter's documented update interval. Predicted escape times must conservatively cover actual times, with a positive reserved margin after measured worst crowd/door overhead. Any underprediction or layout/visual-bank mismatch invalidates that class. A look verifies the exported contours match the apparent bank, rather than blessing arbitrary tags.

Outputs: expected/observed/subtraction maps, station-time strips, evacuation margin heat maps, high-space occupancy traces, smallest failing placement per B2/B3/B4 and a warning-lead versus lost-haul-work table. Rough size [guess]: a small Verse-free sweep plus a game exporter/path recorder, hundreds to thousands of reduced local configurations and a streamed translation sweep, not a second geometry engine. Scale before claiming coverage; if exhaustive full-map layouts are infeasible, enumerate all producer primitives plus adversarial composed layouts and label sampling.

Decision changed: widen/reposition upper access or change the local layout rule; extend warning or adjust the discrete front; reject the random-patch algorithm. Never shrink the bed mask or grant low roof immunity to rescue a timing check. Trigger: existing unknown meetings now; again after a first geometry/evacuation live failure or a second coverage/lead retune.

## M2: Situation and lifecycle generator

**Question:** Do the same contact, ownership, animal identity and deadline rules survive every relevant transition and order, rather than only a staged dry-to-flood example?

Informs B2–B8; R5–R10 (including R6a/R7a)/R12; unknown `pawn_contact` and `identity_save`; regression of the argued `terrain_ownership` and `external_consumers` meetings. Existing Kernel/RM_FloodKernel.cs, RM_PanKernel.cs, RM_RefugeKernel.cs and Source/SelfTest/CrackedLandsFuzz.cs are reusable pieces, not a complete instrument.

Inputs: explicit phase/event machine, settings effective values, nullable/loaded legacy state, per-cell wet/source/owner versions, original terrain/mineral baseline/deposition provenance, shared geometric shade and effective free/campaign terrain palette, engine outcome classes, entity/cohort IDs, pawn kind and R6a flood-adaptation membership, grounded/flight/health/trait categories, nursery and inventory/world-carrier location. Export actual starting states, not arbitrary “valid” states invented by the model. Kernel functions execute offline; operations that depend on Verse damage, ownership callbacks or cross-reference loading emit actual-engine replay cases.

Range: Dry/Herald-legacy/Warning/Front/Standing/Recede/Wet/Dry; save/load before/at/after each boundary; master/damage/soak/life/refuge/sleeper toggle edges in each state; enable after long idle; natural/peakstorm/quest arm/already-warning/already-flooding; no seed/invalid record; one and repeated flood, same/disjoint/part-overlap wet cells. Living healthy/injured/downed/dead/owned/drafted/mental, grounded/flying/landing; pan wake by damage/water versus dry seal. Include RM_Muttavaq in the front and continued immersion through sealed/waking/awake and wild/owned/injured/downed states, paired with unadapted subjects at the same contact. Irqit live/predated/dead/captured/carried/loaded inventory/world exit, carrier death, destroyed map. Terrain natural/built/engine-owned, initial Sand/Soil/SoilRich and deposited Soil, free Sand/Soil palette versus campaign Op 5 pure Sand, wall/rock/roof shade present/lost/regained before/after recede and drying, changed weather/glow without changed geometric shade, repeated unchanged versus newly shaded wetted bed, D=1..4, empty/partial/full water, every supported foreign-liquid refusal, player edit/pump/fill between flood and restoration. Hit all single transitions, both orders of noncommuting actions, and transition loops of length up to 6 before seeded long walks of 100–1000 steps. These are exploration sizes, not claims to enumerate unbounded play.

Leaves out art/audio, actual human understanding, long-run economy and emergent full-map AI. Offline health categories do not predict damage magnitude/death. The actual game runs contact replays on real bodies/traits; a pure model can only enumerate them and check which adapter operations should occur. If the engine cannot expose causal damage or world-pawn death state, mark those cases unmeasured and build the read surface before judging them.

**Calibration before belief:** replay the smallest case for each transition/outcome class in game on the same effective build, including real save/loading phases, owned-cell edits and transported irqit. Require exact entity IDs, owners, source deadlines, phase and event multiplicity after each discrete action; time may differ by no more than the declared update interval without reordering expiry/contact. Match integer liquid units exactly to engine reads at the same pulse. A plain JSON serialization roundtrip is not Scribe/cross-reference agreement. Damage replay requires actual injury/exposure/death causes, not just a request counter. R6a replay requires muttavaq contact and wake with no flood injury/drowning and later living dry sealing with the same ID, alongside attributable flood consequences for unadapted controls. This verifies compliance with the argued adaptation rule. Deposition replay requires exact Sand-to-ordinary-Soil transition/provenance at recede, retained ordinary Soil after wet expiry and the R7a repeated-footprint bound in both tiers; compare against shared shade and actual terrain state. Flier replays require T31’s RM_Qevrit in both tier manifests and cover each effective campaign flier. They use deterministic Pawn_FlightTracker airborne feeding-travel ticks and grounded landing state; no flight look/capture or unattended visual hunt. A visual flight review is joint with the owner only.

Outputs: transition coverage graph, reachable-state ledger, mismatch table by bar, minimal shrunk action sequence and a replay fixture/request for every engine-only case. Rough size [guess]: several kernel/adaptor test extensions with thousands of reduced states and seeded walks, plus a compact native replay suite. No gameplay code need be redesigned to get these reporters.

Decision changed: use per-cell/per-cohort deadlines, change event ordering/wake guards, add ownership-version checks or migrate a legacy save field; tighten adapter handling of carried/exported entities. Do not remove a bar because an instrument is missing. Trigger: unknown meetings now; any reload/disable/death/ownership failure; a clock constant changed twice to satisfy a regression count.

## M3: Whole canyon season and resource budget

**Question:** Does the complete default canyon return from wet activity to dry work without accumulating rich ground, self-sustaining wetness, runaway animals/materials, unusable shelter or constant event/log traffic?

Informs B1–B8, especially B5/B6/B8; R5/R7/R7a/R8/R9/R10/R11/R12; unknown `wet_ledger` and `population`, including the multi-way loop water -> swelling/terminal behaviour -> plants/food -> irqit/predators/corpses -> food/Bones -> colony traffic -> evacuation.

Inputs: real local map/roster/terrain/owner export, M1 geometry/path classes, M2 transitions, actual weather scheduler/bias, per-cell flood and independently supplied water, dry mineral baseline and ordinary-soil deposition ledger with shared geometric shade, effective free/campaign initial terrain palette, accepted ExplosiveGrowth soak/terminal outputs, ordinary growth/gathering/predation/decay/butchery and Bones, sleeper states and guards, current ordinary Wreckage state/weathering and per-event costs. Next-version reward/dramaturgy systems off. Use the shared engine kernels where credible; use game-recorded distributions/traces for full AI/diets/terminal effects instead of inventing a food-chain rate from names.

Range: full 150–500-axis native maps, narrow/broad/bent main cuts, sparse/dense effective ordinary plant and animal populations; visiting crews 1–6 versus resident colonies 7–40, leave native wet life alone versus gather/harvest/butcher, ordinary water channel with/without an independent supply, natural storm success/failure with earliest/latest schedule jitter. A season is 15 game days; run long enough for a natural complete cycle and rescheduling if the default 20-day period outlasts it. Extend to day 100+ with retained history. Repeat same and shifted footprints, unchanged shade versus added/removed wall or roof shade, both initial terrain palettes including campaign pure Sand, overlapping nursery wet intervals, extreme weather overlaps, save mid-water and repeat with recall/gather/channel editing ordered differently. Stress period/duration/soak at shipped and UI extrema for regression; explicitly distinguish default results from user-created near-continuous flood settings.

Assumptions: recorded reach geography stays fixed; wall/roof/player edits and their resulting shared shade are explicit events, not unrecorded geometry changes. The modelled kernel agrees with engine pulses, imported rate/AI summaries are representative within their observed range. Timestamp independent water; do not infer it from a global “soaked” count. No frozen clear sky, no disabling growth terminals, predation, flight or hazard, no pausing ordinary tick work while advancing a special flood clock. Reuse event traces for terminals where offline fidelity is inadequate. A season predictor with uncalibrated AI rates can suggest where to look but cannot certify B8.

**Calibration before belief:** take native game exports at dry start, first warning/contact, full standing, retreat, mid-wet, dry expiry, after reload and next scheduling. Compare exact masks/owners/phase/source/deadlines for deterministic state; compare matched-seed hatches/terminal events exactly where RNG can be replayed. For nondeterministic AI, observe multiple cycles at sparse/dense bounds: predicted food/population/traffic envelopes must contain the observations and predict the same direction of change and worst failure regime; disclose interval widths. If an envelope spans both sustained runaway and clean return, it is too weak to decide and the actual whole run decides. Require exact per-cell Sand-to-Soil deposition/provenance and ordinary dry fertility in both tier fixtures; unchanged wet footprint and shade cannot add soil area or fertility across cycles. Newly eligible cells must be actually wetted shaded natural bed, and loss/regain of shade must match R7a without undoing player edits. Record sampled cells/entities and log/job/tick-work deltas. Require the same qualitative benefit/dry-return and no unexplained entity loss; do not substitute a fixed yield or population threshold for that experience.

Outputs: full-map wet/green/fertility and ordinary-soil deposition-area/provenance maps over time, phase/population/corpse/material trajectories by source, gathered useful native resources versus labour, peak refuge occupancy/path margin, event/log/work growth and worst example per bar. Classify flood (banks/ordinary work remain overwhelmed), desert (no usable wet response), stall (no return/reschedule) and error storm (continuing repeated failures). Numerical safety budgets remain tunable checks; genuine absence/permanent change is judged by the pictures. Rough size [guess]: a medium integration harness, several map exports and dozens of season/history traces; a reduced event model precedes an expensive full-game season.

Decision changed: shorten/align wet-source expiry, cap representative cohorts without erasing the wet-life picture, fix feedback into growth terminals or corpse resources, improve upper-ground capacity, or reduce the retained behaviour set if logically unnecessary; never turn off a kept system only for its test. Trigger: current unknown meetings; first B8 flood/desert/stall failure; any wet/population constant changed twice to chase one check.

## M4: Shade and plant eligibility sweep

**Question:** Do visible shaded vegetation, flood-deposited ordinary shade-soil and sun-safe routes agree with the one geometric Direct Sun rule rather than the biome's old light-neighbour proxy?

Informs B1/B5/B6/B8, R3/R3a/R7/R7a/R12; unknown `shade`; affects M1 sun path costs and M3 vegetation/wet ground. Uses shared Sun and Shade geometry; does not redesign that engine.

Inputs/range: exported shared sun elevation/bearing/weather factor and geometric exposure masks; plant-def glow thresholds and actually sampled daylight/artificial glow; roof/rock/building bounds and shadow heights. Sweep the actual fixed-world map coordinates available to the biome, then synthetic local geometry only: sun elevations 0–90 degrees, bearings over a complete turn at 1-degree steps plus exact grazing-edge cases; open floor, all wall faces/ends/corners, narrow slots, roofs, parasol cover, artificial luqqim-on as a separate next-version control, other lights, planted/debug-invalid placements, natural and mined wall, wet/dry substrate. Add original Sand/Soil/SoilRich versus deposited Soil, both free and campaign Op 5 terrain tables; at recede compare wetted shaded natural bed to shaded unwetted/exposed wetted controls. Repeat unchanged shade, newly shaded wet cells, shade loss while wet versus dry, shade regain without new flood, and save/load around these changes. Enumerate every retained flora def against the complete R3a manifest, including the thorn family, all six expansion plants, Veqma, Qirra, Talus and passive hardy grass/lithops. Every listed def is shade-bound regardless of pigment. Sweep each def through natural placement, supported sowing, loaded invalid sites and shade-loss dieback. Retain Qirra’s wet-substrate, Talus’s natural-wall and Veqma’s additional glow restrictions. Cell translations/rotations/reflections cover boundary aliasing. No alternative planet is generated.

Assumptions: the shared engine is the authority for Direct Sun, roof cover is real, glow-limited plant eligibility remains an additional def law. Geometric shade is not artificial darkness and artificial illumination is not sun. Leave out tree-crown model redesign and unsupported day/night planets. If the shared shade reader cannot expose a trustworthy mask, do not invent a parallel one: export it or report the dependency blocker.

**Calibration before belief:** compare model mask to the game's shared exposure reader cell by cell on actual native walls/roofs at recorded bearing, and visually inspect grazing boundaries. Require exact exposed/covered classification except a declared shared-engine edge convention, which must be identical for the plant consumer. Def maxGlow/glow reads must agree at their threshold; nonmatching threshold cells invalidate eligibility, not Direct Sun. Compare the exact deposited-soil cell set and baseline/provenance to R7a eligibility and the actual terrain writes at recede, expiry and shade loss; atmospheric/glow changes alone cannot change that geometric mask. For every R3a def, inspect naturally placed, player-sown where supported, loaded and debug-invalid sites through normal dying/placement callbacks. Require agreement with the shared shade classification at placement and after shade loss, while additional fertility/wet/wall/glow laws remain effective. The manifest comparison must expose any retained def omitted from the sweep. The current local LightBlockers count is a comparison target, never the oracle.

Outputs: mismatch heat map and sunward/leeward pass/fail pictures, maximal eligible plant footprint, a list of bad placements by consumer, deposited-versus-restored-Sand contrast maps, repeated-flood footprint bounds and a corrected-reader requirement. Rough size [guess]: a small geometric-consumer harness over wall/roof primitives plus a native mask exporter, much smaller than art or an entire ecology simulation.

Decision changed: replace the local consumer's proxy with shared shade plus the preserved glow law; apply R3a siting to every retained flora def and correct arrival text. Do not invent a special canyon heat kind or silently delete the shade requirement. Trigger: existing unknown; a green plant observed in geometric sun; second retune of maxGlow to hide placement disagreement.
