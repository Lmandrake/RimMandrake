## Second attack on the small definition

The cheapest bad build now is a visually convincing canyon whose flood is still a small patch, whose roofs are invulnerable, whose cliff pockets cannot take the crowd, or whose mud remains rich after the irqit die. These no longer satisfy the words in the bars. The remaining risk is not an unanswered taste question: the geometry, path times, wet clocks and growth terminals may not fit. Those meetings are explicitly unknown, with M1, M2, M3 and M4 named, and are the first implementation work. Sound and visual recognition have no credible offline substitute; count reports alone will not pass them.
