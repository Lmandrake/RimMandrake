## does
- [leaf:bloom_reads_sick] Under the standing Bloom the sky is a red storm lid, not grey overcast
- [leaf:tells_then_burn] Gawpsacks sink, rattlegrope shakes and red puffs rise before the Burn, never after
- [leaf:shelter_in_lead] Colonists within one dash of a refuge get in during the tells; a far work party is caught out
- [leaf:one_dash_at_cost] A prepared colonist crosses between refuges in a Burn and lives, hurt; staying out is grave
- [leaf:refuge_is_shade] A refuge is whatever the shared shade calls shade: a roof, a tree's cast shade, water
- [leaf:natives_take_burn] UV-shy natives are hurt by the Burn; Scaldhide and Crispling are not
- [leaf:preburn_dive] Natives dive for shelter during the tells and sun lovers come out, before the Burn lands, owed
- [leaf:bloom_quiets_sundash] No sun dash under the Bloom; it returns when the Burn clears the sky
- [leaf:repulsor_warns_and_clears] The Repulsor warns that its Burn covers the whole map, then clears the sky, and lapses without power
- [leaf:touched_reads_early] A worker out in the Bloom shows an early touched state and one message when change begins
- [leaf:mutations_both_ways] Changes come from a mixed deck, harmful and mixed, capped, never twice on one body
- [leaf:sunbeam_arrest_treats] Holding the Sunbeam's lens stops the change, keeps the mutations and leaves a burn
- [leaf:unfinished_malformed] Each budded Unfinished is half-made its own way and dies into slime and mulch
- [leaf:genome_brings_tissue] A gestation shows its odds first and, when the wanted part misses, still brings matched tissue
- [leaf:bodyprints_phased] A corpse becomes skin, then a lasting print, and a Bloody Mess eats the rest, owed
- [leaf:flora_flesh] The plants read as grown flesh, and zhool haze turns plants in our biomes only
- [leaf:cast_lives_here] The natives spawn at density and each reads as its own creature
- [leaf:airborne_stats] Every flyer carries its stat, flags and flip-book; its flight is never live-tested without him
- [leaf:fauna_specials_declared] Each native carries the kind of death, flag or body its description promises; no number is a bar
- [leaf:biome_def_declared] The biome declares its own terrain, density, diseases and no forage; RustPuff only with TheRot
- [leaf:zhooliza_lives_here] The new smaller Zhool'Iza lives here in both its variants, owed
- [leaf:walk_red_lid] A reload mid-way and a second order of the main actions give the same Burn, body and batch
- [leaf:settings_superb] Every major feature has its switch and tuning, and each switch stops only its feature
- [floor:mod_settings] Superb Mod Settings
- [floor:biome_scope] Biome mechanics are promised inside our biomes only
- [advisory] Burn doses, tells lead, stage timings, deck weights, yields and creature numbers are tuning; scenes check sign and order, never a value
