# Contagion — proposed Design Version 0.1.0

This is the Designer's proposal, not an applied change, an owner ruling, a build authorization or a passing verdict. The existing biome hold remains. All scope/default/interpretation calls here are delegated Designer calls unless a particular owner source is cited. No game, bridge, art or source changes were made. Nothing is written into his words.

## The mod a player recognizes

You settle a wet valley of flesh-plants and unfinished animals beneath a red storm that slowly changes exposed people. When the natives warn that the cloud will tear, bring workers into shade or risk a purposeful crossing through the burning light. You return for useful donor tissue, arrest unwanted change with a Sunbeam, and watch failed bodies return to the valley through visible remains.

Land: alien sand and buildable valley ground, flesh-shaped vegetation and a storm-dark red sky on the rain-bearing peaks above the green; eroded ordinary wrecks remain part of that ground.

Life: the admitted grotesque cast, ordinary diets and predation, actual flyers, host budding and unstable Unfinished, Wombpod hatching, readable deaths, and the commissioned smaller Zhool'Iza. No edible native forage or products; an anatomical organ is not food.

Signature: **The covered Bloom changes and renews bodies until native warnings herald a burning clear-sky window, making refuge, a costly tissue expedition and arrest of unwanted change parts of the same colony routine.**

Runner-up: the controllable biological laboratory — Coalescence, chosen/exotic limbs, print collecting and Helix contracts. It lost because it is a second progression/economy, adds producers and state machines without improving the basic warning/refuge decision, and the owner explicitly deferred its large parts. Ordinary donor organs remain under his useful matched-or-sellable tissue promise (OWNER:1794364405453876478; record.d_other_tissue). Including grown natural arms/legs and matched-mood feedback is my delegated Designer call, inferred from that promise, current ordinary tissue/surgery and the filer's retention line in `later_version.md`; the line is not his express exception to the Grown Limbs deferral. Deleting restoration makes these ordinary outputs decorative; deleting matching feedback hides the donor relationship. This is an exception with a deletion argument, not permission to retain the rest of the laboratory.

Prediction, not a bar: learning the native tells and choosing whom to send will be more interesting than merely waiting indoors for weather to end. The useful-crossing proof decides whether that prediction has a playable foundation.

## Why a player reaches for it

This is a colony location with a repeatable weather/body decision. Vanilla toxic fallout primarily makes the open undesirable; ordinary weather changes shooting and movement. Here the bright interval offers clearer shooting and fewer shy natives in the open while injuring a traveller. Bringing home a useful ordinary tissue batch gives a purpose beyond watching a sky effect. Vanilla surgery, reservation, hatching and hediffs do the ordinary jobs. We retain only the custom links needed for the particular experience, rather than replace those systems.

The Assailant weapon is water-starved elsewhere and runs too aggressively to finish bodies here. The campaign explanation for its confinement is the cloud's protection and sterilizing descent toward the desert; it is not a request for a new downstream infection simulator. The campaign owns the Helix base, posts, politics and authored rivers. No quest, rite, new faction economy or worldwide infection is added.

## Using it: arrival, routine and scale

Arrival: bring food and medicine. Find buildable dry ground and make a roofed refuge; mountain roofs, real cast shade and traversable water use the shared shade contract. A refuge protects against the UV exposure it actually blocks, not predators, contact infection or hot ambient air. Inspect native bodies and the touched health entry. Purchase or receive a Sunbeam through ordinary item trade/reward paths; no crafting recipe or guaranteed arrival is invented. Device availability must be proven without special Helix trade switched on.

Routine: the natural clock supplies a warning window. Gawpsacks sink in place without despawning, Rattlegropes visibly shake, red puffs rise and rustle is audible. A player orders colonists; there is no automatic colony rescue or teleport. Shy natives begin their own reachable-shelter jobs before damage, while Scaldhide and Crispling come out. Scorchpod is a leaker: it can remain exposed and cook. The fog clears before Burn pressure starts; shooting loses the Bloom penalty but retains ordinary accuracy limitations. The sky later closes and the cycle resumes. Wet vanilla rain lulls are covered intervals, not unannounced clear Burns; their ambient exposure must follow the same covered/Burn distinction.

A purposeful crossing is ordinary path travel or a brief rescue between real refuges, not a new dash ability. For the proposed proof envelope use healthy adult travellers, ordinary mobility, ordinary gear, actual open routes and doors, early/mid/late departure and rain movement. `bars.json` declares the numeric regime; it is a testing envelope, never a claim every pawn survives every gap. Repeated open work must have a serious health consequence, not merely a decorative dose label. Both Burn UV injury and the shared vanilla heat path run; sheltered but already overheated people can remain ill.

Exposure: open covered sky and stronger Burn exposure affect eligible fleshy humanlikes. Adjacent Unfinished contact is independent of shelter. An early touched state precedes active change; a saved onset latch produces a noticeable transition once for that progression, not a message per dose. Ongoing change does not stop merely because the pawn moves indoors. The actual deck includes all-negative and benefit-with-cost outcomes; each stated cost must apply. Eligibility, no duplicate card, finite cap and unavailable body parts are explicit rules/checks. Exhaustion means readable no-room state, not endless failed messages. No scalar claim that every mixed outcome is worse for every imaginable play style is defensible; the ruling means no benefit-only mutation and real unavoidable costs.

Treatment: another colonist carrying a Sunbeam reserves and approaches the patient, holds a visible beam for the whole job, and arrests progression only after completion. Existing changes stay; a treatment injury remains and re-exposure restarts change. Movement away, death, loss of weapon, lost reservation and cancellation must not produce a completed treatment. A fully blocked injury must not silently report priced treatment success: retain progression or expose the blocking reason until an actual completed priced intervention is possible. The same projector's ordinary weak weapon/projectile path stays because the ruled arrest tool is that weapon; its extra native-damage multiplier is deferred; there is no new accuracy exemption or independent gun system. The live beam is owed: current hold tick only faces the target and has a progress bar.

Tissue: ordinary extraction costs blood loss and creates a stable donor-labelled sample. Carry it to a living ungestated Bloody Mess (wild or hatched from a Wombpod sac). The menu states the resolved ordinary pool and desired-part odds before commitment, with no promise of a selected outcome. Injection rechecks eligibility/reach/settings at commit; an interrupted approach cannot spend the sample. The host gestates over actual elapsed time, then is consumed for one ordinary batch. Natural arms and legs remain distinct from the deferred bizarre limbs. Outputs carry donor ID/name; mismatched recipients can use them with no invented penalty, and successful matched surgery gives the retained feedback. Dead/absent donors do not invalidate sale or installation in someone else. Completion with nowhere to place output retains a readable blocked-birth entitlement and retries; it cannot silently kill the host and lose the promised ordinary batch. Premature violent host death may lose the batch once, with a sign, and still uses the shared acid death.

At a forty-colonist scale, refuges form a working network. Path length and crowding matter; a global order is not a guarantee everyone fits through a door. Treating a worker, injecting a moving host and consuming remains compete with other work. The signature must allow ordinary hauling, treatment and retrieval alongside colony work, not fill the queue with repeatedly cancelled escape jobs. Every onset message is per progression, every actual new mutation is a new event, and a common clock cue is not a letter for every animal. The whole-season instrument records queue/health/population evidence; limits are not guessed from one quiet pad.

## What changes in the colony's story

An exposed worker is a changing person whose history survives arrest. A trip for a missing leg can come back with a useful heart instead; the next expedition requires another consumable host. Natives teach the weather, then leave a visible history of failed bodies. These are persistent consequences, not scripted lore events.

Ordinary life stays ordinary where possible. Peeper, ikee, Ogleknot, Doublemaw, Gnashling and the grubs are the small/browser/picker cast; Bloodlurk, Eyestinger, Gorekite, Scaldhide and the slow Danglemaw supply predator risk; Fleshsop is an ordinary retaliating pack weapon, never a needy pet. Meltgut is a scavenger, Sloshbelly a pictured swollen native, Skinflap a real skin flyer. Gawpsack is a grounded floater, distinct from the zhool and Blisterfloat. Zhool and infected bulloo retain their declared bodies, ordinary floating and guarded VEF traits; this pass does not redesign the shared behaviour engines. No new special taming benefit, milk economy, electrical feeding or formation controller is inferred. Taming uses current ordinary defs and does not grant immunity.

Flora includes eyed canopy wood, inedible filament cover, shrubs, columns, tentacular forecast plants, both halfmade alien tree appearances, Meatvine, Toothmoss and host-bearing Wombpod. Bodies remain even when an attached optional product/behaviour defers. Sapblister no longer produces a default inert medicine promise; Bloody Fist no longer produces a default duplicate fertilizer promise. Halfmade means alien on both halves, never a half-oak. Variant count is an art regression check, not a requirement to re-pass this place.

## Costs

Food must come from supplies, farming or external sources; native life is not a hidden dinner. Refuge construction, carrying, time outdoors, ordinary extraction blood loss, a consumed host, treatment UV injury and irreversible mutations are concrete costs. Prices, yield, hatch/gestation timing, movement and damage values are tuning/checks. The Bloom's existing accuracy factor is an explicit standing numeric exception kept in a check, not a new bar to re-pass on every renormalization.

The deferred plain-mulch fertilizer remains code/defs only; no item payout or soil designator is active by default. Body traces do not need a profitable byproduct. Raw/sunned mulch infection is not resurrected: the owner ruled plain fertilizer only.

## When it goes wrong, and the readable sign

A distant worker can miss shelter; health shows UV injury and/or heatstroke and touched exposure with separate causes. A shy native with no route remains visible, burns and leaves remains. A blocked animal is not teleported, deleted or silently exempted. A missed treatment does not declare success. A host killed before completion loses its tissue opportunity visibly; a blocked healthy birth stays an inspectable pending transaction.

Native death handling is owed. A recognizable carcass or non-butcherable goo remnant becomes collapsed skin, then a glossy anatomical Bodyprint. A Bloody Mess must walk to it to consume it. An uneaten print stays readable through an ordinary trip out from the colony and back, then fades with a visible sign. Final Bodyprint sampling moves to next version, off, with its only defined consumer, Draftprints; no replacement record item or sampling job is invented. No duplicated right to butcher, collect Bones or consume the same reserved material. Existing corpse butcher/fire destruction can end the chain early with its own visible sign. Player-colonist corpses remain vanilla. Acid deaths still burst and leave a trace; birth still suppresses acid. Unfinished and other goo-buds do not become persistent ordinary butcher corpses. Flying departures leave the promised ridge husk; no pawn disappears to imply a dive.

Disable behaviour without deleting history. Already living animals live out their readable lives, existing mutations stay, and saved ordinary batch/remain entitlements remain resolvable. Toggle transitions are enumerated, not improvised as destructive cleanup. Persistence duration and cleanup scheduling are checks to tune against ordinary return-trip time and accumulated work, not an indefinite promise of an infinite number of persistent objects.

## Edges

No worldgen, second planet, biome scorer or rarity slider; no finding from zero world tiles. No world painting, hydrology rewrite, green-tile capture or new lowland rain. The peaks' weeping look belongs to world-look work; rules wiring remains deferred until asked under the existing ruling.

No Coalescence, exotic limbs/abilities/removal, Monstrous selection/fallback, Draftprints, Helix curators/contracts, Repulsors/hardpoint, plant-converting haze, custom sap medicine, target steering or new animal/plant sub-engines by default. The installed code is coupling evidence, not a reason to keep these. Rawshade and duplicate seed-fist utility are retirement recommendations only; no roster eviction is proposed. The explicitly waived true Danglemaw canopy ambush is not raised again as missing first-version work.

## Neighbours and dependencies

CreatureBehaviors supplies one geometric shade/cover service, Direct Sun feeding vanilla heat and cover-aware escape. Contagion consumes it for UV refuge; no biome-specific heat field or exact-trunk alternative survives. Water follows the already delegated refuge ruling; it grants no new potable-water property.

ExplosiveGrowth supplies ordinary growth and Rupture for the current Contagion plant roster, including births of Bloody Mess and zhool with its existing malformation pool. These are dependencies, not an extra we quietly turn off. Rupture can produce more hosts than CompSpawnerUnfinished's local cap accounts for. The coupled model is mandatory before treating the ecology as bounded; any required change to an engine goes to that engine's owner, not a covert redesign here.

EnvironmentalHazards supplies scaled acid deaths, disease references, render helpers and external Forge creep handling. Wreckage supplies local eroded fragments, scatter, decay and deconstruction; local map content is distinct from planet generation. Bones owns its normal butcher patch; the remains system must claim/destroy a source only once and cannot issue invisible duplicate bone loot. TheRot owns RustPuff; a composed package guard alone does not prove a separately enabled biome feature. Shared scanner payloads are standing integration: retained pod/wreck/cave signals, no dormant Coalescence heartbeat. SettingsKit remains the existing UI engine. All DLC are assumed. Shipping identity remains the franchise-free RM tier inside `mandrake.rm.biomes`; campaign proper names/faction wiring stay beneath it.

Roster audit: no eviction. The source comments record earlier multi-home Sytheclaw history, but it is no longer a current Contagion row; do not re-admit it to fix history. Zhool and bulloo were owner-moved from Cauldron; keep their current Contagion rows. ExplosiveGrowth can create them outside ambient roster selection; that is an in-game mechanism, not an accidental second home to purge. Any multi-home row discovered in the resolved roster is noted and left alone. No new giant admission is invented from the Titan passage discussion.

## Settings

Retained major switches cover natural Burn, tells/Dive, touched exposure/progression, Sunbeam arrest and ordinary projectile access, Unfinished budding/variation, ordinary genome growing, Bodyprints, haze appearance and ordinary land/life integration. Reuse existing fields where they express these functions; add absent switches for Dive, Bodyprints and currently ungated major behaviours. Defaults on express the retained version; off stops new effects at a documented now/next-event boundary. No local setting disables the shared sun ruling or rewrites the planet. Independent shared-engine settings remain that engine's, documented as dependencies.

Deferred controls are visible but labelled “Next version — off by default.” Change field initializers, Scribe fallbacks, reset snapshots, producer gates and test-suite defaults together. Existing `draftprintsEnabled=false` alone cannot turn off curator stock; `grownLimbsEnabled=false` alone cannot hide recipes/removal powers; `cloudRepulsorEnabled=false` alone cannot remove quest rewards or ship gifts. Default absence is a state read of objects/jobs/letters/incidents, not absent XML or a checkbox screenshot. Clean-config and persisted-settings paths must agree. Old opt-ins remain explicitly labelled opt-ins, not a way for the default proof to inherit active extras. All-off leaves an inert but recognizable place and readable saved state; it need not satisfy the on-default signature bars.

## Additional deletion and dependency boundaries

The same deletion test applies to the necessary Sunbeam: the item, ordinary weak projectile, visible medical beam and arrest remain, but the independent native-damage bonus is off by default as next-version combat scope. Its numerical factor is neutral unless that extra is explicitly enabled. This removes an independent offensive mechanic without blocking intervention.

Shared VEF floating/fission are ordinary borrowed locomotion/reproduction of the admitted zhool/bulloo bodies, declared guarded in their race defs; they are dependency behaviour, not locally redesigned animal powers. Keep their producer events visible to the full-season model. Shade tells are not flight, and ordinary ecology running with flyers present does not authorize unattended targeted flight tests or screenshot hunts.

The storm-shadow confinement bans an independently viable open-sky native colony or spreading lowland infection economy. Transported shielded natives remain physical animals with visible injury/death outcomes; no off-map disappearance is a containment mechanism. Shared exposure must supply that susceptibility consistently on supported owned maps; if it cannot, report the engine contract gap instead of inventing another sun. Campaign downstream sterilization remains authored world lore, not a new simulation commissioned by this pass.

## The first hour

A food-laden group roofs a place on alien sand. A Gawpsack settles, a tentacular plant shakes and red puffs rise. The player calls the nearby builder inside, sees a far hauler still outside, and chooses a short refuge route rather than assuming clear skies are relief.

## An ordinary week

A worker carries the early touched warning after repeated outdoor duties. The colony extracts a donor sample, takes it to a Bloody Mess, reads the random-batch odds and prepares a return route. A completed Sunbeam hold stops the worker's change with a visible burn; the mutations already gained stay. The returned tissue is useful even when it is not the wanted leg.

## The worst day

A roof is lost and a doorway jams while the cloud tears. A shy animal without a route burns; a rescue is attempted within the prepared crossing envelope. An interrupted medic has not cured anybody. The native body later leaves skin and a print, and a distant Bloody Mess must physically reach it before it is eaten. The outcome is costly, visible and recoverable without an error cascade.

## The season's turn

The colony's refuge network and bodily scars now record its decisions. Newly budded unstable bodies replace earlier failures, the storm still tears and heals, and another ordinary host is needed for another batch. Save mid-cycle, reload and perform treatment and host collection in a different order: the same choices remain, without replayed payouts, a flooded map, an empty cast or hidden next-version machinery.
