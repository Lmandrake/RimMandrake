When the natives warn that the cloud will tear, bring workers into shade or risk a purposeful crossing through the burning light. You return for useful donor tissue, arrest unwanted change with a Sunbeam, and watch failed bodies leave visible remains.

# Page two — what to check

Settlements that matter:

- “One dash”: ordinary purposeful traversal/rescue within declared normal route/health conditions, no immunity token. Long outdoor work must have serious consequences.
- “Refuge”: the shared geometry result, including ruled water; no exact-trunk shortcut or local heat offset. Shade does not stop contact, predators or ambient heat.
- “Before”: visible sink/shake/puffs/rustle must allow near workers to arrive before harm; Dive begins before injury, armoured natives come out, covered Bloom does not provoke sun-escape.
- “Never upgrades”: every mixed mutation has an actual applied cost; metadata alone cannot prove it. Early warning precedes change, arrest preserves mutations, later exposure can restart it.
- “Useful miss”: precommit ordinary-batch odds; donor-labelled installable/sellable alternatives; no cancelled sample spend or healthy host consumed into lost output.
- “Lasting print”: visible native carcass/remnant, skin and anatomical trace; eater physically arrives; final sample works without the deferred commerce system.
- “Feature off”: gate producers, recipes, stock, rewards, jobs and messages, while preserving readable living/saved history. Your existing deferred switches still ship on in source; changing their defaults alone is insufficient.

Questions: 0. No evidence requires you to predict a measurement or repeat a settled choice.

Models specified, first to build first:

- Situation and transition generator: warning, job competition, cancellation, setting and save edges, plus real mutation effects.
- Coupled valley season: weather, growth/Rupture births, hosts, unstable offspring, remains and colony work together.
- Refuge geometry and crossing envelope: shared shade plus actual routes and combined UV/heat/body costs.
- Body and tissue ownership walk: arrival, birth, death, sampling, butchery and reload without lost or duplicate entitlements.

There are 4 models, 6 unresolved interaction groups and 52 tickets. Unknowns concern weather ordering, geometry, combined health, competing jobs, remains ownership and ecological accumulation.
