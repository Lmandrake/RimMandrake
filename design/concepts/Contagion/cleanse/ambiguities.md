# Second lazy-build attack

A builder could satisfy “red storm and changing bodies” using only a tinted sky, randomly assigned text, silent delete-on-death, and a lethal clear period. The final bars require drawn/audio tells early enough for orders, applied bodily costs, a completed useful crossing, physical remains/eater movement, real treatment and ordinary tissue transactions. A second cheap loophole is to prove them on empty pads with dependency births disabled: the single whole bar records producers, weather, jobs, health and identities during an unrestricted season. A third is to park everything behind renamed switches but leave rewards and recipes running: each switch ticket reads default absence through the full acquisition graph. These attacks moved the definition; none is answered by adding a tuning quota to a bar.

37. **The same weapon hides an extra.** Keep every offensive property because the lens is necessary versus retain only the item, ordinary injury/projectile and arrest, deferring the additional native multiplier. Consequence: a necessary medical device would smuggle in independent enhanced hunting. Take native bonus default off/neutral and separately labelled next-version opt-in; ordinary damaging-hit and held-lens arrest remain. DamageWorker_RM_UV.Apply has an independent factor read. This second deletion attack changed the initial draft disposition.
