# The Contagion: what the rewrite chose, what it lost, and why

Companion to `design/concepts/Contagion/design.md`, written 2026-11-18 by the same pass. Its job is to let the older
files be deleted without fear: every distinct idea found in any source has a row saying what became of it. Nothing
has been deleted. The older files this design replaces are listed at the end.

**How the choices were made.** The owner's words of 2026-11-18: the biomes *"are going to be visited by a huge ship
on the run"*, crewed by Jawa *"way in over their head"*; *"anything that takes seasons to observe will simply be
missed"*; for 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 the *"OMG did you see that?"* of Jawa meeting alien creatures at close range is *"the
overriding priority"*. The Contagion needed the least bending of the five biomes in this run. Its experience line
is already about a visit (*"When the sky clears, run"*), its sheet already calls it *"refuge priced in biology"*,
and he has already cut its three slow or costly systems to the next version in his own words. The pipeline holds
two readings: the locked sheet (0.1.0, certified by him on 2026-11-15 and corrected on 2026-11-16) and the cleanse
proposal of 2026-11-17, which cut further on a Designer's authority. Where they differ I followed the locked sheet,
because the creature tricks, the Repulsor and the Bodyprints the cleanse switched off are his keeps and are the
close-range moments. The one large change is whose refuge it is: the ship's hull, not a colony's roofs. Four things
he ruled on today's card are stated per biome: the stop's outer edge, cover from orbit, fuel, and what the place
does to the hull.

**Words used in "what happened".** *kept*; *kept, reshaped* (changed as stated); *moved* (scope bucket changed, from
and to given; "cleanse next" is the cleanse's switched-off list, "locked sheet" is `sheet.md`; this design uses
0.1.0, 0.2.0, later, dropped); *folded into shared machinery*; *lost*.

## The ones I would look at

1. **The Coalescence.** *"A tower of almost-anatomy, dozens of eyes, on the point of standing up"*: one organism
   that grows through staged forms during long Blooms, absorbs the Unfinished, emits manhunters and dies in any
   Burn. It is the biome's giant and the clearest reason a crew would *want* to force a Burn. It is 0.2.0 by his
   word of 2026-11-16 and stays there; nothing here argues with that. I list it first because under the lens it is
   also the one thing that punishes staying, which is one of his questions.
2. **The Helix.** The only people in the valley, buyers of samples, sellers of both devices, and the makers of the
   one ship part this biome has. Deferred by him with the trade. The consequence I could not design around is in
   `NEEDS HIM`: a 0.1.0 crew has no way to come by a Sunbeam.
3. **The Rawshade.** *"When the Burn comes it does not run. It stands still in the open and lets the light cook
   its back, and the small things fleeing the sky crawl into its shade. Then the skirt comes down."* Designed
   2026-11-02, judged KEEP three rounds. The cleanse recommended retiring it as a duplicate of the Danglemaw. I
   moved it to 0.2.0 instead: a Jawa choosing between the Burn and standing under a predator that *"does not hunt
   anything as large as a person"* is the lens, and a Jawa is not as large as a person.
4. **Sunned water and infecting mulch.** *"A day in the open sterilizes a tank"*; *"un-sunned, it infects your
   field and ocular grass takes the farm."* The sheet's two best uses of the Burn as a tool. He ruled mulch plain
   fertiliser on 2026-10-16, so the infecting half is lost; sunned water I put at 0.2.0 because a ship short of
   water above a desert would use it at once.
5. **The planet drinks from it.** *"Every dayside river the shrubland and desert depend on rises in the
   Contagion's valleys."* World lore a single stop cannot show. Lost from the design; it should live in a world
   document, because it is the reason the place matters.
6. **The Ashfall Research Base.** One line of plot (the Helix are trying to make the weapon *"more robust and
   productive, at the risk of unleashing it planet-wide"*). Campaign layer; "later".
7. **Bodyprints.** The cleanse deferred the phases; the locked sheet owes them in 0.1.0 by his card. I kept them
   in, because *"no animal or pawn ever vanishes without a readable sign"* is a standing rule and this is the
   biome where everything dissolves.
8. **The goo on the hull.** Not a loss: an addition. No source says what this place does to the ship, and today's
   ruling wants every biome to say. I chose the nearest thing the sources offer and flagged it.
