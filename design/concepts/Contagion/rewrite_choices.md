## Second reader, 2026-11-18

**The three most valuable things the first read missed**

1. **What this place does to the ship is ruled and built.** The first read wrote *"No Contagion source says what
   this place does to a landed hull"* and proposed goo creeping aboard. His shield sheet of 2026-09-02 says it:
   *"The environment is hostile and immediately goes to work on the ship"*; the proposal he was ruling on names
   *"the ocular forest's spore/toxin load"*; and his answer is the light screen, which handles *"bio contamination,
   spores... and rain damage completely."* In source, the Contagion is one of three biomes marked as fouled air,
   and the shared frame already wears an unscreened ship there. On the same sheet he cut the spore membrane, a ship
   part meant for exactly this biome: *"spores aren't magical, just particulates."*
2. **The question the first read could not design around had an answer.** It asked where a 0.1.0 crew gets a
   Sunbeam with Helix trade deferred. He ruled the sellers by card on 2026-10-15 (*"Quests plus a visiting Helix
   trader"*), and the built Sunbeam is an ordinary trade good with a reward tag, so it can be bought with Helix
   trade off. The same packet holds two sentences he typed that the design now quotes: *"Bloom time, stronger in
   burn, and contact"* and *"Stops current mutations. No protection if you get exposed again later."*
3. **Creatures, and whose they are.** The Rawshade is his commission (*"Make (C) into a new creature, it's very
   cool"*, 2026-10-24), which outranks the cleanse's advice to retire it. The Unblinking is his too (*"Of course it
   can get another creature. Just make one"*, 2026-10-08) and had no row. The ogleknot and the blistered bulloo are
   built, rostered and were in no design file. The Shambles is gone to the Rot by his note. And the cast bible's
   one-line portraits, read only by heading the first time, are the best prose in the biome: the Meltgut's belly
   *"shows the last three things it swallowed, at three stages of not existing any more"*; the Eyestinger's tail
   *"ends not in a sting but in an eye — and it stings with it anyway."*

Also recovered: his thirst ruling that red slime drunk in desperation is *"full mutation exposure right away"*; his
card that *"Contagion rupture is their burst"*, which means a flesh plant can split and give birth to a Bloody Mess;
the ship's acoustic scanner reading for this biome; that the hardpoint Repulsor clears the weather on any other map
the ship lands on; and that a tissue batch takes about four days, which sets the shortest stop that brings one home.

**What I changed in the design, and why**

- *What the place does to the ship* is rewritten on his words and the built frame. Nothing in it is this page's
  proposal. The goo on the hull and the Repulsor as a cleaning lamp are withdrawn.
- *NEEDS HIM* is rewritten and tagged by theme. The device question is narrowed to what is left of it. A new hull
  question asks whether the screen's bubble protects the crew inside it. The cut spore membrane is listed and not
  revived. The Rawshade and Bodyprints questions carry his dated words. The locked tree is named as not edited.
- *"OMG"* took four scenes (the Meltgut, the Eyestinger, the stare, the tree that gave birth) and a plain statement
  that two of the first read's scenes (the Danglemaw's drop, the Fleshsop's line) describe the creature and not yet
  the build.
- *The first day and week* took the three ways a body is touched, treatment at the warning stage, the red that is
  not a drink, the plants that give the walker away, rupture, the bulloo, wrecks and the scanner, and the four
  days a batch takes.
- *What the crew takes away* names the deck part. *Scope* lists the built hull wear, the two built creatures and
  the Unblinking, and *Dropped* lists what he cut or declined with dates.
- Checked and left alone: the clock. The design's figures (one step of cover; probe in the second week; about four
  and a half to seven weeks to the bombardment) sit inside the Greentide design's arithmetic for one visibility
  band. Fuel: no source names a fuel here; "emergency-slow only" stands.
- Whose word wins, checked: the cleanse's retirement of the Rawshade ran against his word of 2026-10-24; its
  deferral of Bodyprints runs against his card of 2026-10-16 as the locked sheet reads it (the design keeps them
  in, and asks); its deferral of the ground Repulsor runs against his certified warning of 2026-11-15 (the design
  keeps it in). The first read had made the last two calls already.

**How the choices were made.** The owner's words of 2026-11-18: the biomes *"are going to be visited by a huge ship
on the run"*, crewed by Jawa *"way in over their head"*; *"anything that takes seasons to observe will simply be
missed"*; for 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 the *"OMG did you see that?"* of Jawa meeting alien creatures at close range is *"the
overriding priority"*. The Contagion needed the least bending of the five biomes in this run. Its experience line
is already about a visit (*"When the sky clears, run"*), its sheet already calls it *"refuge priced in biology"*,
and he has already cut its three slow or costly systems to the next version in his own words. The pipeline holds
two readings: the locked sheet (0.1.0, certified by him on 2026-11-15 and corrected on 2026-11-16) and the cleanse
proposal of 2026-11-17, which cut further on a Designer's authority. Where they differ I followed the locked sheet,
because the creature tricks, the Repulsor and the Bodyprints the cleanse switched off are his keeps and are the
close-range moments. The one large change is whose refuge it is: the ship's hull, not a colony's roofs. Four things
he ruled on today's card are stated per biome: the stop's outer edge, cover from orbit, fuel, and what the place
does to the hull.

## The ones I would look at

1. **The Coalescence.** *"A tower of almost-anatomy, dozens of eyes, on the point of standing up"*: one organism
   that grows through staged forms during long Blooms, absorbs the Unfinished, emits manhunters and dies in any
   Burn. It is the biome's giant and the clearest reason a crew would *want* to force a Burn. It is 0.2.0 by his
   word of 2026-11-16 and stays there; nothing here argues with that. I list it first because under the lens it is
   also the one thing that punishes staying, which is one of his questions.
2. **The Helix.** The only people in the valley, buyers of samples, sellers of both devices, and the makers of the
   one ship part this biome has. Deferred by him with the trade. The consequence I could not design around is in
   `NEEDS HIM`: a 0.1.0 crew has no way to come by a Sunbeam.
3. **The Rawshade.** *"When the Burn comes it does not run. It stands still in the open and lets the light cook
   its back, and the small things fleeing the sky crawl into its shade. Then the skirt comes down."* Designed
   2026-11-02, judged KEEP three rounds. The cleanse recommended retiring it as a duplicate of the Danglemaw. I
   moved it to 0.2.0 instead: a Jawa choosing between the Burn and standing under a predator that *"does not hunt
   anything as large as a person"* is the lens, and a Jawa is not as large as a person.
4. **Sunned water and infecting mulch.** *"A day in the open sterilizes a tank"*; *"un-sunned, it infects your
   field and ocular grass takes the farm."* The sheet's two best uses of the Burn as a tool. He ruled mulch plain
   fertiliser on 2026-10-16, so the infecting half is lost; sunned water I put at 0.2.0 because a ship short of
   water above a desert would use it at once.
5. **The planet drinks from it.** *"Every dayside river the shrubland and desert depend on rises in the
   Contagion's valleys."* World lore a single stop cannot show. Lost from the design; it should live in a world
   document, because it is the reason the place matters.
6. **The Ashfall Research Base.** One line of plot (the Helix are trying to make the weapon *"more robust and
   productive, at the risk of unleashing it planet-wide"*). Campaign layer; "later".
7. **Bodyprints.** The cleanse deferred the phases; the locked sheet owes them in 0.1.0 by his card. I kept them
   in, because *"no animal or pawn ever vanishes without a readable sign"* is a standing rule and this is the
   biome where everything dissolves.
8. **The goo on the hull.** Withdrawn by the second reader. The first read said no source covers the hull; his
   shield rulings of 2026-09-02 do, and the wear is built. See the second reader's section and rows.
