# The Twilight Sea: what was chosen, what was lost, and why

Companion to `design/concepts/TerminalBiomes/TwilightSea_design.md` (2026-11-18). This page exists so the older
Twilight files can be deleted without fear: every distinct idea found in any source has a row saying what became of
it. None of the Twilight's older source files has been deleted.

**Why this page exists.** The same day's first run wrote the four seas as one design ("the combined design";
retired on 2026-11-18 and kept only in git history, commit `b2530669e5`) and read the Twilight's largest sources
by heading only; its own report said the seas were not safe to delete on. This
run read the Twilight's sources in full. What that changed is the next section. Which files were read to their end,
which were read in part, and which were only searched is stated at the bottom, by name.

**Whose word won.** His latest dated words first; then the frozen sheet; then the cleanse of 2026-11-17, each of
whose Twilight lines was checked against his answers dated before it. Three cleanse lines failed that check (the
selling of light parked as future work; the panes on the ship switched off; the well clock asked as an open
question) and are marked in the cleanse table.

**Columns.** *What happened* is one of: kept · kept, reshaped (what changed) · moved (from → to) · folded into shared
machinery · lost. Every lost, reshaped or moved row gives the other choice he could make and what it would cost.

## Where this differs from the combined design

Five corrections, each from a source the combined run did not read through. The combined design is retired; this
list is the record of what was wrong in it for the Twilight.

1. **There is no fishing on the floor.** The combined design promised *"the only ordinary fishing on the planet"*
   as *"nets in the light columns. It works like fishing."* He corrected that in his own words on 2026-09-26: *"you
   don't fish at the bottom of the ocean, I had meant the shoreline fishing"*. On the floor, fish are hunted and
   beds gathered by hand; the catch is fished from the beach.
2. **Selling light is dropped, not "later".** The combined design listed *"tenancy, claim buoys, ageing charts"* and
   *"the Compact's permits"* under Later. His typed ruling of 2026-09-27 is *"The Compact does NOT sell light,
   that's not a mechanic we accepted. Remove."*, and the code was removed that week.
3. **The week is his.** The combined design answered the cleanse's question by argument. The record has his words:
   *"the light can move around slowly over a week"* (2026-09-26).
4. **A source does speak to a ship under a sea.** The combined design said none does. His hiding concept of
   2026-08-13 names under water as a place to hide the ship; it is the evidence under the cover question.
5. **Several things it called built or simple are not.** None of the fourteen seaweeds has a def (measured
   2026-11-18); the gardener's pass is unbuilt; the floor's weather is still Clear and Fog; the float harness needs
   a weir downstream to be of any use, so it moved from the first version to the second.

Also new here: the gripping terror's own description names a hull; a rare animal on this floor weeps a gel *"that
can be refined into fuel"*; the cast of seven residents was blessed by his card on 2026-10-31.

## The ones I would look at

1. **The river is in 0.1.0, and his line for this sea does not mention it.** *"A ceiling of cathedral living glass,
   where every creature is bedecked in reverent light. Shame most of them are so big, hungry, and quick."* The
   mechanics review of 2026-10-22 said of the current: *"largest Twilight cost and not in the line"*. The cleanse
   made the river the whole mod's signature. I kept it in the first version because it is built, it is his ruling
   that the rivers carry, and a Jawa sliding away down a dry gully is the lens. But the first version's weight is on
   the river and the panes, and the *"big, hungry, and quick"* half of his sentence mostly arrives in 0.2.0. If he
   would rather the first version be the animals, the liar and the gardener's rain come forward and the surge and
   sink detail go back.
2. **The Ark Seed is lost.** *"I carried the last sea back in a tank and it lived."* It is the most beautiful idea
   in the marquee pass and it is exactly a thing a ship carries away. It is lost because he answered the ranking it
   sat in with *"no we need to vary this"* and it was never ruled in, and the brief for these rewrites says not to
   revive a declined ship idea without him. It is under NEEDS HIM with a small version.
3. **"You never own a golden well. You hold one, and the roof decides when your lease ends."** The sentence survives
   and the lease does not: his cut. Free ground (*"A well belongs to whoever is standing under it"*) is the ruled
   replacement and it suits a crew that owns nothing here.
4. **The gardener is reshaped.** The cleanse specified a giant animal swimming at the ceiling, grazing rims that
   overgrow on a stated interval. Its own second review found that is a flyer in engine terms (no flyer is
   live-tested without him) and would be carried by the current. This design goes back to his words (*"The shadow
   of the great whale analog would be welcome"*) and the built lanternwhale: overhead it is a shadow and a sound;
   on the floor it is the largest animal in the seas, protected. The loss is a visible worker at the lid.
5. **The Compact is not in the first version.** The frozen sheet says *"v1: the dwellings, plots, moorings and
   lamplight EXIST and are inhabited — the reveal is real on any dive"*, his drop says *"(very cool!)"*, and he
   blessed the cast. The cleanse deferred it and so do I, because the houses are unbuilt and campaign-tier and the
   free biome has to stand alone. It is the first thing I would pull forward: the seven residents teach every
   danger on the floor by doing chores, which is how a crew that reads nothing learns a place.
6. **Five unbuilt seaweeds are in 0.1.0.** The towers, the fan, the turf and the lace. Without them the wells have
   no kelp and the river loses three of its five tells. It is the largest unbuilt piece I put in the first version.
7. **The afterglow and the wilt are back in 0.1.0** against the cleanse, which switched all plant behaviour off. He
   ruled that the wilt *"SHIPS"* on 2026-09-27, and without these a closed well is just dark.
8. **Which famous monsters live here is not settled.** His review notes place the opee sea killer, the colo and the
   sando in this sea; the live roster has only the sando, by the campaign patch. Under the lens these are the
   creatures the Jawa *"heard of or seen before but never THIS close"*. I did not add any.
