# The Chill: what the rewrite chose, what it lost, and why

Companion to `design/concepts/TerminalBiomes/TheChill_design.md`, written 2026-11-18 by the same run. Its job is to
let the Chill's older files be deleted without fear: every distinct idea found in any source has a row saying what
became of it. None of the Chill's older source files has been deleted. The files this design stands in for are
listed at the end.

**Why this page exists.** The same day's first pass wrote the four seas as one design and one choices page ("the
combined design"; retired on 2026-11-18 and kept only in git history, commit `b2530669e5`) and said of itself
that it read thirteen sources by heading only and was not safe to delete on. This page is the Chill alone, with its own sheets, passes,
agendas, specs and ledger items read through. The reading list at the end says, file by file, what was read whole
and what was read in part; the parts not read are almost all the other three seas' sections of shared files.

**How the choices were made.** The owner's words of 2026-11-18: the biomes *"are going to be visited by a huge ship
on the run"*, crewed by Jawa *"way in over their head"*; *"anything that takes seasons to observe will simply be
missed"*; for 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 the *"OMG did you see that?"* of Jawa meeting alien creatures at close range is *"the
overriding priority"*. Whose word wins when sources disagree: his latest dated words first, then a locked Designer
sheet (there is none for this sea), then the cleanse of 2026-11-17.

**What the full reading changed against the combined design.** Six things, each a row or a scope move below. The
combined design is retired; this list is the record of what was wrong or missing in it for the Chill.

1. *His latest words on this sea were not in the combined design.* Two pages of his own card answers, dated
   2026-11-10, sit in a Designer rehearsal folder. They say what a floor visit and a fuel stop are for. This design
   is built on them.
2. *The cleanse had switched off what three of those answers describe.* It moved the garden's defence and the
   warmth trails to "next version, switched off" on 2026-11-17, a week after he had described both as what the
   floor does. The combined design left them at 0.2.0. They are in 0.1.0 here.
3. *The hull wear was already built.* The combined design wrote "cold" and "power and insulation". The campaign's
   shield mod has had the cold case and the cold field since September, on his own ruling, and its code comment
   names the propane sea.
4. *A whole lakebed he asked for was found orphaned.* On 2026-09-10 he seeded a stinging stationary animal, a
   crystal drifter and its rooted stage, and three plants, and ruled on them by card. None was built and no later
   sitting mentions them. It is the first entry under "The ones I would look at".
5. *Two things he cut were not on the combined page:* the ulkhoss (2026-10-06, his words) and keelgrass
   (2026-10-06, his words). The combined design listed keelgrass among the ten plants; it is not in source.
6. *Fuel has two more sources than the combined design gave it,* both in his words: catch rendered to fuel as a
   mechanic (card, 2026-09-24) and propane rain on the beach *"equivalent with chemfuel"* (2026-10-19).

His four rulings of 2026-11-18 (3:10 PM PST) are applied, not asked: stop length as an outer edge (about six weeks
on the floor, a month on the beach); cover from orbit (one step on the floor, none on the beach); fuel (replete);
wear on the hull from the sources (cold, answered by the cold field, power and one warm room).

**Words used in "what happened".** *kept*; *kept, reshaped* (changed as stated); *moved* (scope bucket changed,
from and to given; the cleanse used "first version", "next version, switched off" and "recommend retiring", and
this design uses 0.1.0, 0.2.0, later, dropped; a cleanse "next version" item that lands in "later" is not a move);
*folded into shared machinery*; *lost*. A row whose last two cells are empty is a *kept* row, or a *lost* row whose
reason is the one line given under its table heading.

## The ones I would look at

1. **The bottlearm, and the rest of the lakebed he seeded on 2026-09-10.** His words: *"A stationary animal that
   stings/attacks at the bottom of the propane lake. Weird plant-like growths underwater."* The answer was the
   vhaal: *"A pale green bottle-shaped stalk the height of a standing man, rooted to the lakebed, crowned with a
   spread of slow-curling arms that do not move until something does. The divers who work the fuel call it a
   bottlearm and give each one two arm-lengths of respect, because the arms are longer than they look and what
   they touch stops swimming... It has never been seen to let go of the bottom, and nobody has asked it to."* With
   it came the lakestar (*"from shore it is a point of violet light moving under the mirror"*), its rooted crown,
   bead fields, a combed felt and a pale spire, and one sentence for the whole floor: *"a museum robbery where some
   exhibits sting."* He ruled on it by card that day. It was never built and never retired. I put it at 0.2.0
   pending his word, and I think the stinger is the best slapstick the floor could have.
2. **"Wonder before extraction."** His answer of 2026-11-10, and the reason the garden's defence, the trails and
   the procession are in 0.1.0 here. If he reads that answer as a mood only, three scope moves below revert.
3. **The lab under the hill of the oldest garden.** *"The garden is the scar tissue of the old war. The stillest
   place on the planet has spent a hundred centuries growing closed over the wound the war left, molecule by
   molecule, the way the sea does everything — patiently, beautifully, and without any interest in whether someone
   might one day need the wound open again."* Six routes, each a different price, the cheapest costing the world.
   All of it is the campaign's and none of it is in this design beyond a line. A reader who deletes the routes
   spec on the strength of this page deletes the ending's only home.
4. **The scrapers.** They are in the descriptions and nowhere else: shacks furnished with set wax, *"chairs,
   hafts, window-lattices... all of it furniture that was briefly an organism"*; the oddu carried home *"in
   mittens to eat by the tap-wire, which is as close to a good evening as the nightside offers"*; tarspool bands
   read *"the way farmers read clouds."* No source says who they are. They are the only neighbours a crew could
   have here, and the only people a Jawa could haggle with.
5. **The electrojet tap.** *"Long grounded wire loops across the ice harvesting auroral ground currents"*,
   surge-prone, *"the sky that feeds it fries unprotected circuits"*, with *"the wire-loop ruins of everyone who
   learned the aurora pays"*. It is land, so it is "later" here. It is also junk wire laid on ice for free power in
   a place where power is what ends the stay. I would argue for it on the beach in 0.2.0.
6. **"The pole's undertaker."** *"The dead of the Blue Desert arrive here and are eaten."* The frostmite working
   the fallen, the dead preserved on the lakebed, *"the planet's cold archive"*. Kept as one line of salvage; lost
   as an ecology.
7. **The six lines of world physics.** The exhaust port of a machine; the magnetosphere slamming back together;
   the aurora's ash blown to the crags as the Dark; the factory one-tenth built and *"slowly grinding down, falling
   down, collapsing inward on itself over the ages."* Lost from a design about a stop. They belong in a world
   document and they are the best prose in the sheet.
8. **Keelgrass.** Rooted in the ceiling, *"its tips reaching toward the floor it will never touch"*, and across a
   descending hull it *"reads, for one bad moment, exactly like something reaching up."* He cut it (*"cut no longer
   needed"*). Listed here only because the combined design still counted it.

## Scope moves

Each line is one feature whose bucket changed against the cleanse proposal of 2026-11-17, with where the combined
design had put it. Revert by moving the line in the design's Scope section.

- **The garden's defence, both steps (arcs that warn; lattices that wake):** cleanse next version → combined 0.2.0
  → **0.1.0**. His answers of 2026-11-10 describe both.
- **Warmth trails that the defence reads:** cleanse next version → combined 0.2.0 → **0.1.0**. His answer of
  2026-11-10: *"Return ready for resistance."*
- **The fuselight's real explosion:** cleanse next version → combined 0.2.0 → **0.1.0**. Ruled real by card, twice;
  it is the sea's one-line joke (*"the only fire at the bottom of the world is a flower"*), and a promise that
  nothing burns needs the one thing that does.
- **The wax procession and its sheets:** cleanse next version → combined 0.2.0 → **0.1.0**. His card of 2026-10-02
  made it the floor's giant; its first pause falls inside a stop; it is wonder first and scavenging second.
- **Catch rendered to fuel:** cleanse next version → combined 0.2.0 → **0.1.0**. His card of 2026-09-24 (*"a
  MECHANIC"*), and his fuel ruling of 2026-11-18.
- **The pumped fire room:** not in the cleanse census → **0.1.0**. Built and checked; it is the other half of the
  fire rule.
- **Cold wear on the hull and the cold field:** not in the cleanse → **0.1.0**. His ruling of 2026-11-18, his shield
  rulings of 2026-09-02, and the built code.
- **The beach as a fuel stop (pipes, pump, valve, the lake creature's answer, a gas emergency):** combined
  "campaign layer" with no version → **0.1.0**, campaign layer. He named the beach as replete; his three shore
  answers of 2026-11-10 are its promises.
- **Aurora surge storms and the collector:** cleanse next version → 0.2.0 (as the combined design).
- **The raid in the gas; gas vents:** not in the cleanse (campaign layer) → 0.2.0.
- **The polar Burner's place; the lake creature at scale; a passage-sized sea giant:** open items → 0.2.0.
- **The lakebed set of 2026-09-10:** in no bucket anywhere → 0.2.0, pending his word.
- **Stayed "later" from the cleanse's "next version":** the cryoponics vat and floor bed, hydrocarbon flesh and its
  refining, tarspool fractions and the drug as an industry, the powered stonewater extractor, the crater biome and
  the lab's ending. Not moves.
- **Forty colonists on a floor and the whole-sea season model:** first version → later (as the combined design).
- **New, not in any source:** stating cover from orbit for the floor and the beach separately; the reason given
  for one step (a ship here cannot run cold); the question of what an orbital bombardment does beside the lake;
  the reading of the procession as the crew's own trade.
