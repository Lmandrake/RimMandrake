# The four seas: what the rewrite chose, what it lost, and why

Companion to `design/concepts/TerminalBiomes/design.md`, written 2026-11-18 by the same pass. Its job is to let the
older files be deleted without fear: every distinct idea found in any source has a row saying what became of it.
Nothing has been deleted. The older files this design replaces are listed at the end.

**Read this first: this page is thinner than its sources.** The four seas have more than a megabyte of design
behind them, about thirty-five files, and several of the largest were read here only by their headings and rulings
sections (the list is at the end, by file name). The rows below are honest about every idea I found; they are not a
guarantee that I found every idea. The Twilight Sea's content drop (110 KB) and the three underwater flora passes
are where a second reader should look first.

**How the choices were made.** The owner's words of 2026-11-18: the biomes *"are going to be visited by a huge ship
on the run"*, crewed by Jawa *"way in over their head"*; *"anything that takes seasons to observe will simply be
missed"*; for 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 the *"OMG did you see that?"* of Jawa meeting alien creatures at close range is *"the
overriding priority"*. The seas were already written for a ship: his rulings made the ship the only door, and the
cleanse proposal of 2026-11-17 already described a sea stop as *"a supplied expedition"*. So the lens changed
little about what a sea is and a good deal about what the first version must show.

Three changes against the cleanse. First, it chose one signature for the whole mod (the Twilight's carrying
current) and left the other three seas with land and ordinary life only; this design gives each sea its own way of
wearing on the parked ship in 0.1.0, because he ruled on 2026-11-18 that every biome does, and because for the Grey
and the Twilight he had already typed how. Second, the creature encounters the cleanse switched off come back at
0.2.0. Third, the cleanse's one question to him (may the Twilight's wells turn over in weeks, when the sheet says
years) is answered by scope: a visit lasts weeks.

His four rulings of 2026-11-18 (3:10 PM PST) are applied, not asked: stop length as an outer edge (about six weeks
on a floor, a month on an open beach); cover from orbit by sea (every floor one step; the Scald's beach one step
under its steam; the other beaches none); fuel by sea (the Chill replete, the other three emergency only); and wear
on the hull from the sources (rooms heating, crust and salted doors, a laden deck, cold).

**Words used in "what happened".** *kept*; *kept, reshaped* (changed as stated); *moved* (scope bucket changed, from
and to given; the cleanse used "first version", "next version, switched off" and "recommend retiring", and this
design uses 0.1.0, 0.2.0, later, dropped; a cleanse "next version" item that lands in "later" is not a move);
*folded into shared machinery*; *lost*.

## The ones I would look at

1. **The Deepwater Compact on the Twilight's floor.** *"The surface world knows them as sellers of water; only
   those who dive learn what the water bought — an ark, kept."* The frozen sheet promised the reveal in the first
   version (*"the dwellings, plots, moorings and lamplight EXIST and are inhabited — the reveal is real on any
   dive"*). The cleanse deferred it; I put it at 0.2.0, in the campaign layer, because its implementation could not
   be found. It is the most affecting thing in any of the four seas and the only neighbours the crew meets below.
2. **The Brine Elders.** His own paragraph, entire, in the Grey's content drop: salt-crystal minds *"older than
   every faction"* that pay for novelty *"once, then nearly worthless thereafter."* Built, switched off by the
   cleanse, 0.2.0 here, and asked as `NEEDS HIM` for 0.1.0. Under his "very Jawa" test nothing in these seas scores
   higher: a customer for junk, as long as it is junk it has never seen.
3. **The Twilight's light economy.** *"You never own a golden well. You hold one, and the roof decides when your
   lease ends."* Tenancy, light cages, the cultured sun-sphere, the Ark Seed (*"carry a piece of the last living sea
   home, alive, and keep it alive"*). All "later": it is farming, and a crew on the run does not farm. The Ark Seed
   is the exception I would argue about: breeding stock carried away aboard is exactly what a ship is for.
4. **The fessk at the edge of the lamplight.** *"It stands at the edge of lamplight and watches — never entering,
   never attacking."* Built, 0.2.0 with the rest of the Grey's lamp life. It could come into 0.1.0 alone; it is
   the cheapest dread in the mod.
5. **The pillar law.** *"All travel below is pillar-to-pillar: waymark navigation through blindness... a lost diver
   is someone who missed one pillar."* He ruled it story only on 2026-09-27. Lost as a mechanic, rightly by his
   word, and it is the best idea the Grey's sheet had.
6. **The Scald's two-faith shore.** *"The margins hurt and heal simultaneously, and both faiths claim it as proof of
   their own reading... two churches, one shore, no concession."* Campaign layer and rites, both deferred. Lost
   from this design except one line.
7. **"The planet's water purification runs through the weapon: the Contagion is the filter stage of the
   distillery. Nobody planned it. It works anyway."** World lore, lost from a design about a stop. It belongs in a
   world document and it is too good to drop on the floor.
8. **The Chill's ending.** The war lab under the lake, six routes into it, the crater that changes the map. It is
   the campaign's ending and I left all of it to the campaign layer, with one line in the design. A reader who
   deletes the Chill's sheet on the strength of this page would be deleting the ending's home.

## Scope moves

Each line is one feature whose bucket changed against the cleanse proposal of 2026-11-17. Revert by moving the line
in the design's Scope section.

- **Twilight falling panes and the laden deck:** next version → 0.1.0. The same.
- **Scald Immersion Berth:** not in the cleanse census → 0.1.0. His card of 2026-10-02.
- **The Grey's lamp life (watcher, light-seekers, the giant that breaks lamps); pool sentinel; contact venom;
  Brine Elder trade:** next version → 0.2.0.
- **Vaulisk; suulk; lid-dark:** next version → 0.2.0.
- **Scald staged sails and walkers, steam devils, vent flashes, steam-catch:** next version → 0.2.0.
- **Chill warmth trails and immune shocks, fuselight explosion, wax procession, aurora surge, catch-to-fuel
  refining:** next version → 0.2.0.
- **Campaign inhabited Twilight holdings:** next version → 0.2.0 (campaign layer).
- **Twilight well clock:** the cleanse's question, answered: the built week-scale clock.
- **Stayed "later" from the cleanse's "next version":** the Scald's exposure clock and boil-suit, sponge on wrecks,
  cargo floats, harsher sink outcomes, light cages and the sun-sphere, tenancy and charts, the Grey's reactive art
  and elder relics, the Chill's cryoponics, tarspool, hydrocarbon flesh, stonewater extraction, the crater and lab
  ending, plant charge and wilt. Not moves.
- **Forty colonists on a floor and the whole-sea season model:** first version → later.
- **New, not in any source:** stating cover from orbit sea by sea; the open question of how the Empire reaches a
  floor; the reading of the Brine Elder as a customer for the crew's junk.
