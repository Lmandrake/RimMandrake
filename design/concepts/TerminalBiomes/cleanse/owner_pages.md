# Page one — what the mod now is

Visit alien sea floors by gravship: the Scald boils, the Grey grows salt, the Chill holds dark fuel, and the Twilight shelters gardens beneath living glass. Their native plants, prey and predators belong to those liquids, and the fish caught at the beach have living counterparts below. In the Twilight, harvestable banks tempt you beside a current that can carry a careless worker into a sink, so routes, warnings and rescue shape the visit.

**Land:** distinct floors, materials, visibility and ordinary weather; beach steam stays above the submerged Scald floor.

**Life:** existing native rosters, ordinary forage, prey, predators, moving light and environmental survival; no evictions.

**Signature:** productive Twilight banks tempt workers beside carrying water whose warned surges can take them to a findable, rescuable sink.

My delegated choice: keep the ruled sea bundle but choose that current chain as its signature. The cultivated-light economy is the runner-up; it adds another progression system. Natural temporary wells and their gardener stay because the frozen roof laws require them. Minimal peaceful inhabited holdings stay as a campaign dependency because the sheet explicitly promises that reveal; a full town does not.

# Page two — what to check

My consequential defaults:

- “Sea surface”: no playable location there; only floor or neighbouring beach. Travel is ship-only, without a hatch.
- Scald “perched/outflow”: stale prose; keep the terminal basin, change no world.
- “Clean steam”: no fouling/poison currency; ordinary air heat and paid liquid contact remain. The cooler margin is still undrinkable.
- “Sparse Grey”: solitary animals, not an empty mineral floor. Twilight shoals are allowed below the lid.
- “Current”: ordinary visitors cannot cross the centre harmlessly between registration ticks. Bed, stakes and actual field must agree.
- “Safe arrival”: protect actual hull, unloading and return access from surge; excluding an obsolete hatch is insufficient.
- “Rescued”: outside dangerous water in safe shelter/carriage; a roof over the sink does not qualify.
- “Temporary light”: retain finite years-scale drift and keeper; do not let short-lived source tuning or permanent lamps replace the frozen law.
- “Life”: keep ordinary bodies, growth, harvest and natural glow; code calls do not make every special behaviour necessary.
- “Off”: stop availability, dispatch, running effects and reload reassertion while preserving saved animals and contents.

No owner question is needed. All these are my stated defaults from the rulings and source, not new words attributed to you.
