# Miasma cleansing proposal

Design Version 0.1.0. Every scope choice and implementation default in this proposal is the Designer’s delegated decision. The owner’s November cleansing brief supplies the authority to choose a smaller first version. Earlier approvals establish the deferred features’ intended behaviour. Applying this proposal requires his reading and approval after the separate fresh review.

## The mod in three sentences

You settle among salt-white banks, stilt-root trees and honestly beautiful flowers under sickly green-gold haze. Storm-driven brine reaches up the channels and retreats, leaving sea young in shrinking pools their water-bound warden mother cannot reach. Return them and earn her tolerance, or sell them and lose her trust, while unharmed young may choose your colony and continue a weaker watch when old age takes her.

Land is a difficult-to-cross brackish mangal with a readable fresh-to-seaward gradient, dry haze, cultivable fresh mud and salt-damaged margins.

Life is the admitted alien canopy, benign rainbow blooms, wild scuttler prey, plant and animal predators, composters, flying swarms and juvenile sea fauna, with ordinary feeding and husbandry products.

The signature is storm recession creating nursery rescues whose treatment determines maternal tolerance and the chance of a juvenile inheritance.

The runner-up is rot becoming electricity and then a productive spent bed. It lost because it is a complete independent industrial progression, while the moving shore and mother-child constraint explain both the land and the player’s relationship with its life. The experience row in `design/RimMandrake/biome_experiences_2026-10-20.md` includes “Rot is power”. Deferring that clause is an explicit Designer version-scope choice under the later land/life/signature instruction. It remains a documented next-version promise. This proposal carries no claim that the owner independently selected that deferral.

## Why a player reaches for it

This is a delta settlement with construction and relationship decisions attached to the same changing water. A player reads a salt-marked tree, keeps a farm on the fresh side, builds a channel, and learns that the giant able to crush a raider cannot cross a strip of dry ground. Protection follows geography and earned trust. Prediction of enjoyment is that rescue and restrained engineering produce attachment through ordinary colony work. Enjoyment itself is no passing bar.

Vanilla swamps already provide slow mud, disease, trees and hunting. Vanilla animal taming already provides training and faction changes. Miasma earns its signature through recession causing an actual rescue problem, a mother whose route has the same water limitation as the child’s future work, and a durable nursery record governing trust and inheritance. Wreckage already supplies ordinary salvage. A second loose-goods spawner adds an independent reward loop and moves to the next version.

## Using it

### First hour

The player can identify fresh water, brackish channels and sea-side salt on the colony map. Stilt-roots dominate the canopy and leave passages and buildable patches. Colourful benign blooms remain recognisable through the haze. An inspectable nursery names its mother, known young, colony standing and the mother’s failing age. The warning is available through ordinary selection and a bounded first-encounter notification, with the campaign vigil wording supplied through its own tier. The player learns that water routes constrain protection and that exposed crops at the salt margin are a gamble. Ordinary recruitment uses the existing gradient bands. Ilbareen recruits in fresh ground, brelloch in the brine band and thessamor in the brackish band, with quennath allowed in fresh/brackish ground while its specialist host gate is deferred. Salt-tolerant natives keep their ordinary survival traits. A surge never teleports established plants to the preferred band. These are delegated recruitment defaults using the current fresh/brackish/brine thresholds. Model calibration may retune them without changing the geographic experience.

### Routine

Colonists cut ordinary wood, gather brelloch salt, harvest scuttler meat and karrobel castings, and keep liliana for silk and eggs. Standard foraging remains the current BiomeDef food entry, with no new medicinal forage item. Existing vanilla item labels in resources never imply a new Earth wildlife roster. Ordinary disease remains dangerous and receives ordinary tending. Outdoor haze exposure builds gradually and recovers under roofs. The surge darkens and changes the haze, announces itself, advances water/salinity and then recedes. The player can recall nearby workers after the warning, while saving an entire badly sited farm requires earlier siting and construction. Some recessions produce no occupied pools. Across the declared cycle range, occupied pools and genuine interventions must occur.

A stranded young is an existing juvenile with a condition. Its condition, inspect information and locatable call identify distress. Each rescue-eligible young belongs to a persistent nursery, allocated once from its spawning site or reconciled once on loading an older save. Nearest distance alone never transfers a known child to another nursery. The mother moves to reachable water near her own calling young while retaining urgent defence. Multiple cries cannot repeatedly restart her path so that she never arrives.

A colonist can carry an unresolved distressed young across land as the established rescue action. Engineering a FlowWorks connection is the other rescue route. A successful return requires the correct nursery and mother, water she can actually reach, and the young’s survival. The return clears distress and its call but preserves identity, rescue history and eligibility for future consent and inheritance. Legitimate offered return is evaluated before routine maternal targeting of that carrier at the acceptance water, so the actual rescue action is executable. It grants colony-wide tolerance for that mother. Tolerance excludes ordinary colonists from her targets until the colony harms or harvests the nursery. She remains wild and uncommandable.

A held distressed young receives the existing meaningful premium and can be offered to an ordinary eligible trader. Only a completed sale commits permanent betrayal for that young’s nursery. Cancelled trade, rejected trade and a trader who cannot pay leave the record unchanged. Custom buyer scheduling and its arrival letters move to the next version. Ordinary trader access and refusal remain readable through the normal trade interface. Sale voids later return-based forgiveness and succession for that nursery. Foreign nurseries remain independent. The ordinary hostile act of attacking, killing, harvesting or butchering its young also ends a clean record and revokes tolerance. Accidental damage from a player-controlled pawn or turret counts as colony harm. Injury caused solely by unrelated wildlife or disease does not. Sale is permanent, even after a setting cycle or reload.

### At a forty-colonist scale

A prepared small rescue party can complete reachable rescues while the rest of the colony keeps working. Whole-map travel, concurrent ordinary disease, path congestion and several pools run together. Unreachable or delayed rescues can fail with a locatable warning and visible outcome. Notifications group by nursery and transition. A failed return reason appears when its reason changes and remains available on inspection, rather than repeating at every poll. Breeder pressure, predation, natural regrowth and ordinary hunting prevent either endless pawn multiplication or routine extinction of the food floor. The seasonal model establishes whether these requirements fit the actual full-map rates. This is an open measurement risk.

## What changes in the colony’s story

The nursery becomes a remembered place. Rescue can earn a protector whose reach the player can extend by connected canals. A sale pays at the cost of that relationship. Unharmed registered young may self-tame without conventional taming work. Their useful work is confined to water, including guarding, release/attack and hauling water-side items to reachable water-side destinations. The existing general land Haul and Rescue options remain unavailable to these animals.

The mother is visibly old from first encounter, with plain inspect text and notice that her protection will end. She slows as she declines. The Designer default is an old-age loss reachable within an ordinary campaign, with a model-tuned horizon. A guarded heir remains a juvenile and is conspicuously weaker in reach and combat than her. The age event transfers an actual anchor/duty to a living local colony-affiliated clean-record juvenile with an actual successful rescue/return history and a legitimate water route to the nursery. Consent can occur without prior rescue, but that alone never supplies inheritance eligibility. The promotion decision records an absent heir if eligible young are dead, away or unreachable at that moment, with no teleport or replacement. Rescue nothing, lose the eligible young, or betray that nursery, and inheritance fails with an intelligible reason. No adult sea-beast growth arc is introduced. Succession runs once, is saved, and cannot manufacture replacement animals or duplicate guardians.

The mother’s natural death does not mark the colony as her killer. Direct nursery harm retains its relationship consequences. The separate map-wide manhunter-chance multiplier is recommended for retirement and defaults off pending that decision.

## Costs

Mud costs travel time. Haze costs outdoor working capacity and ordinary sickness costs tending and labour. Surge exposure costs salt-sensitive plants and unprotected crops. Rescue costs travel, carrying or canal construction and a nursery risk the player can read. Canal extension costs the FlowWorks engine’s ordinary labour and materials. It grants no remote command over the mother and no additional guard radius beyond her ordinary anchor reach. Sale costs trust and inheritance. Inheritance costs continued care and keeps a much smaller protector after the mother’s death. These costs are comparisons and consequences. Tuning values remain checks and rules, outside the bars.

## When it goes wrong and the readable sign

- A new pool shrinks through an identifiable edge, with its occupants and unresolved distress visible. A saved rescued, traded or departed animal cannot be destroyed by the original pool’s expiry list.
- A wild unresolved young either reaches legitimate water, remains endangered, dies with an ordinary body, or leaves through the historically permitted fallback with an explicit player notification naming the young and pool. A developer log line alone is insufficient. Claimed successful rescue always leaves the actual animal in a safe, identifiable state.
- A canal severance can trap the mother in still-wet water. Existing animals remain in that component and move within it. They cannot acquire a new route across land or disconnected water. Drying around them gives warning and an observable survival outcome. Repairing a connection restores legitimate routes.
- Brine reaching ilbareen leaves a dead-standing gauge. Old gauges record earlier reach and need not resurrect when salt recedes. Distinguishable water and ground reveal current salinity. Receding salt does not guarantee instant fertility restoration.
- The wrong mother cannot accept a child by proximity. A known nursery lost from an older save is explicitly unresolved until reconciled and shown on inspection. No arbitrary remote colony-wide benefit is granted.
- Disease, incapacity, death, interruption, filled stockpiles, multiple rescuers or simultaneous trade cannot duplicate the young, payment, return credit, item stacks or an heir. Save/load preserves the last committed transition.

## Edges

Version scope contains the land, ordinary life and the nursery chain above. Deferred industrial systems, luxury interactions, fever rewards, reactive animal powers and the rite are listed in `next_version.md`. Stable definitions and assets stay available for old references. Their executable behaviour and new availability are gated by settings that default off and say “Next version”. Existing objects remain inspectable and safe through a toggle or reload. Their saved progress stays inert while disabled. Disabling the biome’s active features suspends their producers and interventions while retaining ordinary terrain, objects and honest status information.

The sea-connected route requirement applies to water-bound mothers and the affectionate nursery young taking these duties. An unresolved disabled youngster being carried for rescue is the established exception. Once returned or self-tamed into ordinary water life, no carry-home upgrade exists. Salt-tolerant native flora can withstand the brine their species inhabits. Rainbow bloom benignity prevents attacks, toxins and deceit. It does not guarantee immunity from salt, fire or ordinary plant damage. Special host/germination gates, plant-fed graphic states, acid spit, regeneration, lockjaw disease, fire spit, fermentation output and lastvine rearing move to the next version. Their species stay on the admitted roster with usable ordinary diets and attacks.

## Neighbours

Ash’karr is the fixed authored desert world. Miasma makes no alternate planet and changes no world tile or inter-basin river. The world definition forbids connecting its basins and terminator rivers. The sheet’s river-mouth language is read as local delta/backwater ecology with surviving dayside fresh inflow. It authorises no planetary hydrology repair. A sea-facing island such as Bitterleaf can receive the local brackish nursery without an invented road or river link. On an authored local site lacking a marked sea boundary, the signature reports that missing local input and awaits the site author’s existing geography, never guesses a fresh river exit to be the sea. A biome on zero world tiles remains expected.

Multi-homed references such as saluksis and bileworm and any patch-added residents remain annotated in their admitted rosters. ## Settings

The settings floor remains inherited. The default active groups are gradient/surge, pools/distress, nursery relations and young work, ordinary life interactions, and haze/exposure. Each major behaviour has a working off switch, search, reset and an accurate application-time label. Existing shared controls may be linked or explained from Miasma’s page, provided a player can find and operate them and their cross-biome impact is explicit. No duplicate silent control is acceptable. Shared settings remain owned by their engine. New cross-biome switches are excluded by the standing ruling.

Returning and consent must remain operational when a sale-specific control is off. Pool identity and water constraints remain safe when pool creation or the next event is disabled. Disabling succession suspends consent/promotion, preserves records, and provides no new heir on re-enable unless the recorded eligibility still holds. Feature off never erases the player’s pawns or resets betrayal. All-off produces a playable ordinary map. The forbidden world-rarity slider is removed from active presentation and the scorer is made inert, with no deferred worldgen mode.

## Stories

### First hour

A settler crosses dark mud toward a white salt bank, then turns back to the wood-bearing roots on the fresher side. Through the haze she sees brilliant flowers and a half-submerged elder. The nursery’s ordinary selection tells her plainly that this protector is already failing and can never cross the bank beside her.

### An ordinary week

A hauler gathers castings, a hunter takes scuttlers and a keeper collects silk. The next storm pushes the channel margin past the old dead ilbareen. After recession a young calls beyond the mother’s route. A rescue party carries it to its own nursery. Its distress ends, its identity stays, and the mother passes a colonist she previously threatened.

### The worst day

Several pools are drying while sickness fills the infirmary. A buyer offers an easy escape from the rescue burden. The colony sells a young from one nursery and returns another from a different site. The first mother’s record closes permanently and the second grants tolerance. The severed canal leaves the first mother in a pond. Every animal’s outcome remains visible, including a lost wild young whose old pool expires.

### The season’s turn

The full map has survived repeated salt advances, regrowth, hunting and tending, with a mid-cycle save and reload. A clean-record child has chosen the colony and carries ordinary loose salvage between water-side points. The mother’s failing age, already known, finally ends her watch. A much smaller juvenile takes that nursery’s duty. Elsewhere an empty nursery remains empty because the colony saved nobody there.
