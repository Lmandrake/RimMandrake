# Proposed instruments

These are specifications for future instruments. None was built or run in this pass. Outputs are predictions until the stated game agreement is established. The existing pure Miasma kernel and fuzz runner can supply transition primitives, with runtime adapters and new inputs where noted. They cannot supply trustworthy geometry, job execution or ecology by themselves. Models consume snapshots of local maps from the fixed authored world. Synthetic grids exercise topology and produce no alternate planet or playable-world generator.

Build order follows the headings below. The shore model comes first because the signature presently lacks its causal water-footprint transition. The generator follows because rescue must preserve nursery credit and affiliated young into inheritance, including a never-rescued sibling.

## Shore and route sweep

**Question.** Can the same local water and salt transitions create new orphan pools, preserve construction and keep all mother/young movement water-bound through canal cuts and repair?

**Informs.** B1, B3, B4, B5, B6, B7 and B9. R1-R7, R9 and R10. The unknown surface and water routes meetings in bars.json, plus placement inputs to the root-line goods meeting. It also provides region inputs to Whole-map season.

**Assumptions.** The fluid engine identifies an authored seaward boundary and a cell’s underlying fluid independently of the traversable top surface. Orthogonal contact carries connectivity. Salinity is a scalar and the shared surge can publish actual temporary wetting/recession, its affected natural terrain and completion events. Existing salt/crust history persists according to that engine’s contract. Route legality uses the engine’s movement plane and the relevant pawn’s actual path checks. The model must never treat the largest component or a freshwater edge as a sea source. If the engine cannot expose a reliable water transition, record the assumption as false and return the witness to T11. A simulated substitute cannot establish that the engine already does it.

**Inputs and sweep.** Exhaustively enumerate the water/land/source/bridge-throat categories on small grids up to 4 by 4 cells after symmetry reduction. Include diagonals, fresh edge only, a closed pond larger than the true sea-connected branch, walls, constructible under-terrain and dry shortcuts. Enumerate each transition in which a single throat is wetted, dried, bridged or obstructed, then multi-throat cuts. For 150, 250 and 325 cell-square local-map snapshots, sweep actual seaward bearings, widths 1-8, saline thresholds immediately below/at/above the shipped boundaries, front extent min/default/max, recede min/default/max, salinity residual min/default/max and 100 repeated allowed surges. Import larger authored sites as supplied without generating new planetary locations. Use crops and ilbareen at growth stages, native fresh/brackish/brine recruitment with bare versus established plant cells, player floors, bridge top/under layers and gravship foundations. The sweep records the mother’s and eligible young’s real route shapes during wander, defence, calls, following, hauling and repair. Include naturally wet root-lines, transplanted inland roots, root removal, water-side pickup/storage and blocked/full destinations. Sweep harvest targets just inside/at/outside anchor reach, separating target acquisition from executable water-only attack paths.

**Leaves out.** Ocean fluid pressure, wave physics, alternate planets, new geometry engines and final art judgement. Biological hunger and population variation belong to the seasonal model. Where the engine cannot export a path request’s intermediate cells, the geometry prediction alone leaves B5 unverified.

**Game agreement before belief.** T19 captures a disposable map’s exact top/under/foundation terrain, fluid-source cells, salinity and actual path-cell traces immediately before, during and after an ordinary surge. Compare model and engine against the same stored RNG state and event parameters. Natural terrain class, constructed-surface preservation, sea-connected component membership, pool identity and permitted/rejected path membership must agree exactly at every changed cell and transition. Float salinity may differ only within the engine’s actual serialization round-trip tolerance established by an unchanged save/reload, and may cross no band boundary differently. Executed movement must contain no forbidden dry step. Use at least a direct sea arm, a larger closed pond, a fresh-only edge arm and a bridged canal with a dry shortcut. Wet root-line placement and pickup eligibility must agree exactly with real deposited-stack cells and paths, including the inland-root negative control. A mismatch invalidates the relevant assumption and the model’s passing prediction.

**Produces.** Before/advance/recede connectivity maps, constructed-layer difference pictures, legal-path overlays and the smallest counterexample per touched bar, including an ordered trace of how a pool became isolated. It also reports maximum changed-ground extent over the repeated-surge sweep and whether fresh habitable regions recover.

**Rough size.** Medium, roughly a few days of instrument work [guess, existing gradient/pool kernels and map snapshots provide part of the representation]. Enumerated category combinations should be symmetry-reduced and counterexamples minimized, with sampled large-map sweeps clearly separated from the exhaustive small-grid portion.

**Build trigger.** Immediate. Both relevant meetings are unknown and current RM_GradientAxisRepaint changes water grade or mud to crust without creating the required wetting footprint. Build/calibrate before accepting a recolour-based surge proof. A second retune to front extent or residual drift also reruns this model.

**Decision changed by output.** Whether the shared transition contract can support the nursery at all, which topology/terrain case the builder must repair first, and which authored wet-margin ranges are compatible with intact floors and viable fresh ground. Its counterexamples can attack R2-R4 before any bar is weakened.

## Nursery transition generator

**Question.** Does each actual young keep the correct nursery identity and committed outcome through rescue, trade, harm, consent, death, settings changes and inheritance?

**Informs.** B4-B7 and B9. R4-R7, R10 and R11. The unknown nursery record meeting and settings checks. Feeds legal scenario manifests to the other models and to the ordinary-entry-point live driver.

**Assumptions.** A stable pawn identity, nursery identity and mother identity survive loss of the distress hediff. Trade has a committed outcome distinguishable from an attempted PreTraded callback. A pool’s occupant list is bookkeeping and conveys no authority to destroy a rescued or transferred animal. Saved settings and RNG state are part of the transition state. Nursery-level rescue credit is distinct from each candidate’s own rescue history and consent. Current harvest targeting is separate from lasting trust/clean-record state; non-nursery harvest inside reach does not itself dirty the record. Existing kernel rules are reusable only after they implement this definition. Current NoteReturned removes the young and TryPromote does not attach a duty, so the present kernel is an implementation under examination.

**Inputs and sweep.** Generate maps with 0, 1 and 2-8 nurseries, 0-16 named young, swapped proximity and different actual parentage. Pawn states include wild, distressed, carried, returned, self-tamed, downed, dead, sold, away and heir. Nursery states include clean, harmed, betrayed, mother alive/declining/dead and successor attempted/active/absent. Walk the complete reduced legal graph for one nursery, then pairwise interacting nurseries and targeted three-way schedules combining pool expiry, return and sale or death. Events include carry pickup/drop/interruption, canal rescue, wrong mother return, physician treatment, player pawn/turret harm, wild-predator harm, harvest/butcher, completed/cancelled/rejected/unfunded sale, ordinary trader unavailability and, in a separately labelled deferred-setting branch, custom buyer absence/retry, mother age death/violent death, eligible child death, repeated same callback, settings off/on and save/reload. Generate old saves missing each newly needed field and use actual available records for reconciliation. Explicitly test no historical identity and preserve visible unresolved status. Use bounded interleavings of simultaneous colonist and trader actions, then targeted depth extension until no new transition class appears. Record the search bound with the result. Pair a successfully returned young with a never-rescued self-tamed sibling; let the rescued young live, die or depart while the sibling remains eligible. Contrast no rescue, rescue only elsewhere, harmed/betrayed record and candidate dead/sold/away/no water route. Walk plant/wood/salt/animal harvest start/stop/cancel just inside/at/outside anchor reach, tolerated or not, with concurrent idle colonists; nursery-young harvest additionally closes the record. Consent roll branches test logic, not frequency; ordinary occurrence uses the whole-map histories.

**Leaves out.** Pathfinding performance, actual audio perception, art, market-price tuning and long-term food balance. Calls and jobs are abstract requests until ordinary engine traces agree. Exhaustive single-nursery reduced-state coverage never claims exhaustive unbounded multi-pawn scheduling.

**Game agreement before belief.** Reuse the generator’s event manifests on a disposable game map through real carrying, training, trade and guard actions. Before/after each event read pawn ownership, actual payment, distress, parentage, pool membership, mother target set, ledger, actual duty/anchor and notifications. Compare exact discrete state against the model, including an uninterrupted trace and a replay saving immediately before and after commits. Calibrate clean return, wrong-parent attempt, interrupted return, completed/cancelled/unfunded trade, sale then return, return then harm, consent after return, mother death with/without an eligible child and expiry after rescue. Require exact target-set and executed-defence agreement for tolerated woodcutter/salt-gatherer controls, without lasting record change from ordinary harvest. Replay a never-rescued sibling inheriting on nursery rescue credit, plus no-rescue and foreign-rescue negatives. A raw method return or a congratulatory letter supplies no agreement. Any mismatch disqualifies that transition’s predictions.

**Produces.** A machine-readable generator of situations with expected accepted/rejected transitions, a coverage matrix of reached states and excluded impossible states, minimized failing event sequences and timeline pictures showing identity and ownership. It reports each notification and each durable change separately from callbacks.

**Rough size.** Medium [guess, the existing MiasmaFuzz runner and CrecheLedger reduce pure-logic work, but transaction and duty adapters are missing]. A bounded finite-state implementation plus ordinary action replay manifests suffices.

**Build trigger.** Immediate after the shore instrument exposes the causal scene. The nursery record meeting is unknown, known parentage is erased on return, and promotion currently supplies a name without a guard. Any later change twice to a timer/chance to make a rescue or consent check pass reruns the generator.

**Decision changed by output.** Which identity and commit paths must be repaired, whether gates may remain coupled, and whether the mother’s death yields the defined choice-dependent outcome. It attacks nearest-mother fallback and combined return/consent state storage first. It never manufactures a guaranteed heir to hide a failure.

## Whole-map season

**Question.** With all kept mechanics active, can an ordinary colony live through repeated surge/nursery cycles without the food floor, habitat, duties or event traffic running away?

**Informs.** All bars, especially B2, B8 and the whole bar B9. All rules. The unknown life biomass, weather and root-line goods meetings, and the cross-effect between the unknown surface, water routes, nursery record and colony effort meetings. It is the whole-mod model required by the brief.

**Assumptions.** Use exact engine-exported initial plant/pawn/item/terrain/roof/canal state and actual growth, breeder, predation, weather, disease, age and job timing inputs. Drive the shared deterministic kernels where they exist. Model ordinary animal and colonist decisions using empirical action/time distributions calibrated below. Preserve light-driven native growth, vanilla disease/immunity and sun/ambient heat independently. Exposure, storms and permanent haze retain their real priority ordering. Wall-clock compression advances genuine game ticks in their original order. An abstract event-skip model can explore risk but supplies no final B9 pass until event ordering is shown equivalent. Kept deposits expose recession-to-stack provenance and saved stock accounting. Startup seeding is off and initial Wreckage salvage distinct. Placement requires wet roots and executable water-side work, not planted-root proximity.

**Inputs and sweep.** Authored-site snapshots at 150, 250 and 325 cell-square maps plus larger supplied sites. Colonies 1, 8 and 40, initial native/predator densities at the shipped expected low/default/high regimes, intact and partially cleared canopy, fresh/brackish/brine proportions from the shore sweep, min/default/max permitted storm/recede/residual settings, ordinary hunting/gathering/building/tending schedules, at least two rescue/canal orders, ordinary traders with available/blocked routes, with the custom buyer incident off and product stores spare/full. Run 15 game days minimum, extending through a maternal age/consent/inheritance outcome. Use 30-60 days as the delegated age-tuning target and extend observation to 120 days where needed. Save/reload during an unresolved pool, after sale, during an active job and before promotion. Sample 24 initial RNG histories per input regime as a risk screen [guess, a tractable first sweep], then investigate each discovered failing region to its boundary. Random sample count is a check, never a bar. Keep recurring deposits on, startup off and no staged haul items. Sweep natural/removed/isolated roots, stored/consumed stacks, full/blocked water-side storage and exhausted initial salvage; record recurring work versus source starvation or accumulating goods. Observe eligible consent opportunities and natural outcomes by clean nursery across independent histories over this same stretch, contrasting harmed records. Forced rolls cannot support frequency.

**Leaves out.** Unlimited future play, planetary hydrology, art approval, hostile mods outside the supplied framework and adversarial extermination. Uncalibrated NPC AI and rare medical events remain uncertainty bands, not confident simulated truths. If no credible empirical AI/incident adapter can match the game, retain the model as a scenario selector and use the full game trace to decide B9.

**Game agreement before belief.** On an actual disposable full map with shipped defaults, collect daily habitat area and species population, categorized births/immigration/deaths/harvest, ordinary products, hunger/thirst/medical need, weather, exposure, job completion/interruption and notification totals. Record genuine recession deposits, root cell, stack identity, merge/move/consumption, outstanding stock and trained pickup-to-store completion, plus consent opportunities/outcomes per nursery. Capture cell-level changed terrain and individual nursery histories at each event. First compare a normal week and a full season from identical saved initial state. Kernel-driven transitions and terrain/identity membership must match exactly. For empirical ecology/job projections, observed daily values must stay within the model’s declared prediction intervals, the trend sign in each low/default/high regime must agree, and every flood/collapse/stall classification must match. Tune intervals from recorded variance, disclose them before evaluating another trace, and never widen them to relabel an observed failure. Use separate calibration and holdout saves. Deposit event identity, placement, item conservation, cap state, consent eligibility and nursery rescue credit must match exactly. Consent counts on independent holdout histories must fit prediction intervals fixed from shipped chance/cadence and calibration before evaluating holdouts; never widen intervals to accept almost-never consent. B7 frequent-versus-almost-never and B5 recurring-versus-staged classifications must agree with real traces. Probability tuning/statistical advisory thresholds remain C11 checks. A discrete success/failure classification disagreement invalidates that regime even if aggregate counts look close.

**Produces.** Habitat and water maps over time, population/food/time series by source and sink, weather/exposure and illness timelines, nursery outcomes with persistent identities, duty interruption traces, and worst-case witness per bar. Include deposit-to-work item-flow tables, worst uncollected-stock accumulation, source-starvation maps and per-nursery consent timelines over the full stretch. Explicitly flag a flood as scuttlers/events displacing ordinary play, a desert as ordinary life failing to recover in the habitable fresh region, a stall as accessible duty/rescue work repeatedly failing to complete, and an error storm as repeated new errors in the run log. The words name observable failures, their advisory tuning thresholds are recorded in checks.

**Rough size.** Large [guess, geometry and state kernels can be reused, coupled ecology and ordinary-job calibration are the missing work]. Start with event-budget projections and reuse the game as the scheduler where a faithful offline AI is uneconomic. This fallback is a credible instrument specification, with no claim of a fully predictive ecological simulator today.

**Build trigger.** Immediately after the first two models and calibrated event traces. Life biomass, weather and root-line goods meetings are unknown; the restored source needs calibration before seasonal work can be claimed. Also run on the first whole-map live failure or when a breeder/growth/residual constant is changed twice to move an isolated check.

**Decision changed by output.** Which rule or tuning regime causes cumulative loss, whether exposed ground can recover without erasing history, whether call priority must yield to defence, and whether the signature’s rescue workload is compatible with ordinary colony life. Any conflict is resolved in rules/inputs or implementation before asking to weaken an owner experience.

## Food and rescue budget

**Question.** Can a prepared party finish reachable nursery work while the rest of a full colony feeds itself and tends the ordinary Miasma sick?

**Informs.** B2, B4-B9. R5-R11. The unknown colony effort meeting, plus timing pressure that can couple all other meetings. It is a smaller projection of the seasonal run, with explicit labour and route costs.

**Assumptions.** Ordinary game jobs consume measured travel, manipulation, carrying and waiting time. A party has real capacities, needs and competing work. Post-recession deposits, not staged items or once-only wrecks, supply recurring haul work. Harvest inside maternal reach can interrupt a tolerated worker and consume response time. Juvenile haul conserves an actual item stack and requires a valid water-side source and destination. The model uses future pool drying time supplied by the shore/pool engine and known current signs. It assumes no hidden teleport, guaranteed buyer arrival or direct state-changing proof hook.

**Inputs and sweep.** Colony 1, 8 and 40, parties 1-4, pool routes in the measured shortest-to-longest reachable distance deciles on each supplied full map, ordinary unburdened and carrying travel, roof/shelter distance, exposure bands, disease cases 0 through the largest simultaneous ordinary case in the calibration trace, 0/1/3/8 concurrent distressed pools and young within configured small/big draws. Sweep min/default/max allowed drying, worker congestion, full stores, juvenile carrying capacity and source/destination eligibility. Include ordinary trader visits during a return, mother defence during cries, shelter before/after rescue, interrupted job followed by replacement worker, and both canal-first/rescue-first orders. Compare time remaining at the first available sign against time to complete the actual permitted response.

**Leaves out.** A numerical fun score, price optimisation, an obligation to save every inaccessible child, and a replacement colonist AI. If a hazard’s real timing cannot be inferred from a visible sign, the model flags an information defect rather than assuming player omniscience.

**Game agreement before belief.** Obtain ordinary event timestamps from pickup, travel, canal construction, deposit, treatment, consent, haul completion and return acceptance in both an uncluttered local fixture and an occupied full map. Route cells and item/pawn conservation must agree exactly. Each action’s observed duration must fit the interval predicted from its recorded path, speed/capacity and actual interruptions, using tick-rounding tolerance from that engine call path. Validate on a held-out different route and crowd level. The model may never declare a prompt rescue feasible where an ordinary prepared party fails for a missing job, inaccessible target or later unmodelled interruption. Those are implementation/state witnesses, not interval tuning.

**Source and defence sweep.** Include wet root-lines with recurring source on and startup off, source exhaustion/restock, harvested/isolated roots, full stores, consumption and competing haulers. Combine wood/salt/animal harvest inside/outside anchor reach with rescue preparation and clean/harmed records. Calibrate source arrival, trained hauling and defence interruptions against ordinary-entry traces; item identity and target inclusion must agree exactly. A manually placed stack cannot establish seasonal usefulness.

**Produces.** Travel-and-action timelines, remaining response margin by pool region and party, food/tending/work balance, worst overload witnesses and water-haul source/destination rejection pictures. Advisory values guide tuning. Passing rests on observable recoverable ordinary work and intelligible loss.

**Rough size.** Small to medium [guess, it primarily reads trace data and sums known job phases]. Use the generator’s manifests and shore routes instead of inventing another simulator.

**Build trigger.** The colony effort meeting is unknown. Build once real normal-job traces are available. Prioritise it immediately if the first full-map rescue fails despite prompt action, calls restart mother duties repeatedly, or a drying/travel/exposure constant is changed twice to repair a check.

**Decision changed by output.** Whether to tune warning lead/drying time, restructure call interruption priority, correct water-job eligibility or reduce event pressure. It establishes whether a small ready party can respond and leaves genuine delayed/blocked losses possible.
