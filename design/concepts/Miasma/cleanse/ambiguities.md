# Ambiguities resolved by the Designer

Each entry records an alternative build, its concrete consequence and the delegated default taken. These are scope or acceptance differences. Ordinary tuning values stay in checks. Frozen bans and later standing rulings supply boundaries. Older draft questions are resolved here without representing their answers as the owner’s.

## What counts as the signature

**Two readings.** A bundle of every approved economic miracle, or the shore-to-orphan-to-relationship chain with one consequential nursery choice.

**Consequence.** The first requires independent power, corpse, cosmetic and health progression before the biome can pass. The second can tell a complete delta story through rescue, sale and succession.

**Taken.** The nursery chain. The October experience row explicitly also says “Rot is power”, which is concrete evidence for retaining that feature in the mod’s eventual promise. The later November land/life/one-signature instruction delegates version scope. I move the whole rot chain to next version, with its semantics preserved in the tickets. I keep sale as the alternative outcome of the same young, and succession as its long-term payoff. Those use the same individual nursery history. Source evidence that moved me is the September mother/young geography and the October mother’s-price choice. The prior delegated fluff decision explicitly kept succession for that reason.

## “Every surge brings orphans”

**Two readings.** An occupied orphan pool after absolutely every event, or a recurring nursery outcome over irregular recessions, with some empty pools.

**Consequence.** A mandatory orphan quota can manufacture young whenever the map has no plausible isolation. Empty-weight and small/big draws already make individual events variable.

**Taken.** The sentence is figurative in the older experience row. It means that recession is the recurring cause of nursery rescues. Actual wetting and newly cut-off water are required. Any single event may leave no occupied pool. The B4/B9 range must actually observe ordinary occupied outcomes before it can pass. Sources are RM_Miasma.xml’s emptyWeight and the frozen undirected, uncertain ecology. No direct forced juvenile spawn can prove this causal claim.

## “Surge” and “salt line”

**Two readings.** Recolour existing water and push a scalar salinity field, or temporarily extend channel water and let recession disconnect wet low spots.

**Consequence.** The recolour-only build cannot strand something through changing water geometry. This is what the current RM_GradientAxisRepaint implementation does.

**Taken.** The signature needs the actual water-footprint consequence described by the sheet and condition text. The shared-engine contract is ticketed. A salt line is a broken ecological front and historical dead-tree band, never a straight geometric line. “Miles” refers to the large planetary fiction, with no literal local-map distance bar. An irregular branching front passes, a cosmetic wave over unchanged ground fails.

## “No map state is permanent”

**Two readings.** Every surge can permanently turn more of the map into sterile crust, or disturbance keeps changing habitable and saline regions while history persists.

**Consequence.** The former can leave a season-wide desert even when every isolated repaint check passes.

**Taken.** Construction and historical gauges can persist, and currently habitable fresh ground must still support ordinary regrowth through repeated allowed events. No instant salt-to-fertile-soil reversal is promised. This wording comes from treating the frozen gradient and nursery as a running habitat. Residual drift plus regrowth is unknown until the shore and seasonal models agree with the game.

## A river mouth on this particular planet

**Two readings.** Repair the world’s rivers so one joins the Grey Sea, or keep the authored world and provide local delta/backwater ecology at its legitimate sites.

**Consequence.** The former breaks ASHKARR_WORLD_DEFINITION.md’s separate-basin and terminator-river rulings and can “fix” Bitterleaf’s deliberately roadless island.

**Taken.** The fixed authored world wins. The biome describes local brackish delta ecology, with surviving dayside fresh inputs and sea-facing margins, and edits no world links or coordinates. Missing local sea-facing input is reported to the site author. The seasonal instrument consumes local-map records, with no planetary repaint or alternative-world generation.

## Sea connected versus simply wet

**Two readings.** Any large component or water touching any map edge qualifies, or only a component connected to the actual declared sea boundary qualifies.

**Consequence.** The current largest-component/any-edge proxy admits a closed lake or freshwater river exit as a sea and gives a sea elder illegitimate routes.

**Taken.** The September FlowWorks sea-connection ruling is literal. Use explicit seaward sources in the existing shared representation. A source connected diagonally alone fails. Synthetic topology sweeps are local instruments, with no world generation capability.

## What a bridge means to the mother

**Two readings.** A bridge removes the fluid below it, or the underlying water remains connected but the top movement surface can block the swimmer.

**Consequence.** Erasing the fluid strands the wrong pools. Treating every under-water cell as a swimming surface can path the mother across a constructed dry top.

**Taken.** Preserve hydrology beneath constructed surfaces. Apply the actual movement plane and pawn’s legal water route separately. Bridge blocking remains an ordinary construction consequence. No new under-bridge layer or multi-cell pawn-body geometry is commissioned. Evidence is the real top/under/foundation split already addressed by RM_GradientAxisRepaint and the still-incomplete WaterLocked path constraint.

## “She cannot reach dry land”

**Two readings.** She may cross dry cells between valid water endpoints and recover periodically, or no ordinary executed movement traverses dry ground.

**Consequence.** Endpoint validation plus a recovery walk can let her solve the very rescue the colony is supposed to perform.

**Taken.** Full-route water restriction. The source itself admits that current periodic checks cannot establish this. B5 reads executed intermediate cells across calls, combat, following and work. A post-hoc return to water fails.

## A mother stranded in water

**Two readings.** Lost sea connection freezes or deletes her immediately, or traps her in the wet component until repair or visible drying consequences.

**Consequence.** Immediate immobilisation conflicts with the stated pond tragedy and may silently kill a tolerated defender.

**Taken.** She may move within the trapped wet component, cannot acquire routes to disconnected water, and receives a readable stranded state. Drying has an observable survival outcome. A repaired connection restores legal routes. The September canal ruling explicitly describes a pond with a still-swimming mother.

## Carrying versus the water-bound affection

**Two readings.** The no-carry-home fiction bans the colonist’s established rescue action, or it bans a transport upgrade for the young once they enter ordinary affectionate water-bound life.

**Consequence.** A blanket ban makes the October “carry it home to her or sell it” choice impossible.

**Taken.** An unresolved distressed young can be carried for rescue or held for the ruled sale. It keeps its distress sign while carried. Once returned/self-tamed into water life it stops at the waterline and receives no carry-home upgrade. This combines the September no-land solution boundary with the subsequent explicit carry-or-sell branch.

## “Its mother” and “nearby”

**Two readings.** The nearest living warden accepts the nearest distressed pawn, or each known young belongs to its own nursery.

**Consequence.** Current CheckReturns can credit one mother while NoteReturned updates another marker. A second close nursery becomes a trust exploit.

**Taken.** Persistent site identity created at spawn and preserved after distress is cleared. Wrong mothers refuse without committing a rescue. Old saves reconcile once from reliable records. A genuinely unresolvable child is visibly unaffiliated pending reconciliation, with no proximity-based remote trust grant. The ordinary range includes several adjacent nurseries and swapped proximity.

## Return ending the condition

**Two readings.** Remove the hediff and all of its state, or clear distress while retaining the individual’s nursery/rescue history.

**Consequence.** Current TryReturn plus NoteReturned can remove the self-tame comp and the ledger membership, breaking rescue-to-succession.

**Taken.** Separate durable identity from transient distress. Returning does not itself grant an instant heir or guarantee consent. This is needed for the September self-taming and succession payoff to be reachable through the October rescue action.

## “Sell one”

**Two readings.** Any sale callback or opened trade commits betrayal, or the completed paid transfer commits it.

**Consequence.** The current PreTraded prefix can punish an attempted or failed transaction and produce a letter about payment that never happened.

**Taken.** Commit the actual transaction once, with the actual child transferred and payment recorded. Cancelled/rejected/unfunded attempts leave the record unchanged. Retrying the same completion cannot duplicate payment or betrayal notices. The source proves callback coupling, the owner’s choice names an actual sale.

## Tolerance and harm

**Two readings.** Tolerance protects ordinary harvesting unless nursery members are harmed, or any harvest inside anchor reach makes its actor a target even when tolerated.

**Consequence.** The first reading lets a tolerated woodcutter or salt gatherer work beside the crèche untouched, contrary to both the frozen sheet and the September fauna amendment. Treating that local exception as permanent colony betrayal would also punish ordinary forestry as if a child had been sold.

**Taken.** Corrected after review: any colonist harvesting plants, wood, salt or animals inside the anchor reach is targeted immediately, tolerated or not. Outside-reach harvesting does not trigger her. This per-act exception ends when ordinary non-nursery harvesting stops and does not erase trust or the clean nursery record. Defence still obeys anchor reach and water-only path legality. Separately, player-controlled pawn/turret damage, killing, butchery or harvesting nursery young breaks that nursery’s clean record and revokes tolerance; ordinary harm keeps consent closed in this version, without a new-rescue forgiveness reset. Wildlife and disease alone do not count as colony harm. Completed sale permanently closes tolerance and succession. Evidence: the_miasma.md section 4 explicitly says harvesting inside reach returns you to the target set; fauna roster section 6 says harvesting anything, while section 6a ties clean-record loss to harm to young. These are distinct conditions. The mother remains wild and uncommandable.

## Frequent consent versus a guaranteed animal

**Two readings.** Every rescue yields an immediate tame pawn, or eligible unharmed young can choose the colony through probabilistic ordinary checks.

**Consequence.** Guaranteed affection removes the frozen “rebirth, maybe” premise, while a chance too low to occur during colony play makes the payoff unreachable.

**Taken.** Frequent probabilistic consent, with no ordinary taming grind. “Frequently” means consent recurs among unharmed clean-nursery young through the whole-bar observation window; a clean nursery whose young almost never consent fails. It is not a guaranteed animal on a particular roll or a regular schedule. B7 marks the frequency word figurative and contrasts the pictures; chance and cadence remain C11 tuning checks. Generator branches cover roll outcomes, and whole-map histories measure ordinary occurrence rather than a forced lucky draw. Nursery affiliation and water eligibility are mandatory. Consent without rescue is possible. Inheritance needs the colony’s successful rescue record at this nursery, not the candidate’s personal rescue history: a living self-tamed sibling can inherit even if it was never rescued. A child dead, away or unreachable at the promotion decision supplies no local heir. Evidence: the owner’s “frequently self-tame” words and section 6a’s sibling payoff.

## An heir in name or an heir on watch

**Two readings.** Save a name and emit a succession message, or transfer actual guarding behaviour to the existing eligible juvenile.

**Consequence.** RM_CompCrecheYoungLedger.TryPromoteSuccessor currently supplies the former. Protection disappears while the game claims continuity.

**Taken.** Actual anchor/duty and executed guard behaviour, a visibly weaker juvenile, and an explained absent-heir branch. Eligibility belongs to the colony’s record at that nursery: at least one successful rescue/return, a clean record and a living self-tamed affiliated young with a legal water route. A never-rescued sibling qualifies; rescue nothing, harm or betray the nursery and there is no heir. Requiring the heir’s own rescue was my erroneous narrowing, corrected on the September section 6a sibling evidence. No new adult, instant maturation or second full-sized mother. Old-age inheritance is anticipated, with plain age information on first encounter. The delegated tuning target is loss within 30-60 game days of encountering an already-old mother, subject to calibration of actual age/health progression. Earlier ordinary violent or disease death is a separate readable outcome, with no clean inheritance reward for colony killing.

## “Trainable” at the waterline

**Two readings.** Hide broken Rescue/Haul options and call training finished, or offer real water-side Guard, release/attack and water-side haul that can execute.

**Consequence.** Current hiding and periodic land interruption leave no useful dedicated hauling job.

**Taken.** The ruled water work stays because removing it defeats the affectionate child’s ordinary usefulness in the signature. Keep its recurring post-recession wet root-line deposits as needed_by signature: no staged items, no source creation by the haul job, and eligible pickup, destination and complete water route. Initial seeding alone moves. RM_MiasmaWreckField.xml scatters wreck buildings once at local-map creation, with no water-side placement; it cannot sustain the ruled root-line work through the season. T15 must prove repeated natural source-to-store hauling after initial salvage ceases to supply the route. No general land Haul or Rescue is offered.

## Pool expiry and the previously allowed fallback

**Two readings.** Every tracked pawn is destroyed at the original pool’s expiry, or only unresolved wild occupants still subject to that pool reach the permitted fallback.

**Consequence.** The present prune/expiry path can destroy a rescued colony pawn elsewhere and leave only a log line.

**Taken.** Keep the existing fallback permission. Exclude owned, rescued, returned, sold, departed and out-of-pool individuals from its authority. Unresolved loss gets a player-visible notification naming the young and place. No new question about the old waiver is raised. A trace follows individuals and rejects a replacement pawn as proof of survival.

## Thick, crowded and “eaten by everything”

**Two readings.** Fill the map with breeders and let every entity kill scuttlers regardless of diet, or sustain a noticeable renewable prey floor with honest consumers.

**Consequence.** Unlimited breeding floods the map, universal feeding overrides vanilla diets, and high plant predation can erase the floor.

**Taken.** Crowding is the ordinary visible presence of scuttlers and young across workable channels. Plant predators and ordinary animal predators consume eligible prey. The phrase “everything” is ecological emphasis, without changing every race’s diet. The full-map model resolves joint growth/breeding/hunting rather than a spawned-corner density example. Deliberate total extermination is outside ordinary replenishment.

## Benign rainbow colour versus predatory flora

**Two readings.** Every colourful plant must attack, or genuinely benign blooms coexist with a separate visibly carnivorous clade.

**Consequence.** Conflation breaks the frozen absolute hope premise or makes the expressly carnivorous plants inert.

**Taken.** Preserve the two clades from the September card. Predators take wild scuttlers only and show feeding, including wall/obstruction restrictions. Blooms cause no toxins, traps or damaging harvest effects. Normal salt/fire death remains ordinary environmental damage to the plant. Species-specific fed graphics move because a visible ordinary kill/feeding cue already makes predation readable.

## Multi-cell roots, host trees and castings-only ground

**Two readings.** Bespoke multi-cell collision plus germination gates for every plant, or a readable prop-root canopy and ordinary plant placement with the specific salt gauge.

**Consequence.** Hidden gates repeat the cost of the removed pollination system and can make ordinary plants fail silently. Strict solid-root footprints can eliminate passages.

**Taken.** Retain the native silhouettes and walk/build constraints of ordinary plants, with no new collision engine. Retain ordinary fresh/brackish/brine recruitment preferences, gradient-sensitive ilbareen and native salt tolerance. Current fertility-only definitions leave broad recruitment sorting as a kept implementation gap. Quennath’s extra host-only germination still moves. Move additional host-only and castings-only germination mechanics to next version. Their fiction remains source material, with active descriptions limited to the behaviour actually promised for this version. This is my scope reduction. The November delegated cut is evidence that invisible ecological gates can leave the first version.

## Native growth and native water

**Two readings.** Test the map with growth/animal needs disabled, or include the actual shared growth and water-needs engines.

**Consequence.** Disabled growth hides seasonal regrowth or canopy floods. A native can die thirsty beside its own brackish water while generic hunger checks pass.

**Taken.** ExplosiveGrowth’s active Miasma classification and CreatureBehaviors pressure run alongside actual native drinking. No biome-only shade, heat or drinking exception is invented. The standing November native-water rule supplies the admitted-roster test range.

## Haze and sickness

**Two readings.** Exposure, disease, pollination and fever rewards are one mandatory mechanic, or the ordinary atmosphere and disease remain independently usable while extra rewards are gated.

**Consequence.** A single off switch can silence the kept air or a recovery patch can quietly grant deferred powers.

**Taken.** Keep dry haze, shelter-recoverable exposure and ordinary disease. Defer all fever-forging and strange-tier effects, including already-owned aura/inspiration execution. Leave the already-cut pollination gate retired. Both dry haze and dry surge weather use their real priority, with no clear-sky proof. The shared Direct Sun geometry remains intact. Source evidence is the separate condition, exposure, disease and forge paths.

## Animal life versus secondary powers

**Two readings.** Any behaviour in an admitted animal’s def is automatically first-version life, or the animal’s ordinary existence, feeding and products can remain while independent powers wait.

**Consequence.** Regeneration, lockjaw disease, acid/fire abilities and fermentation introduce separate combat/economy loops without eviction ever having occurred.

**Taken.** Preserve every admitted species and its usable ordinary diet, attacks and standard products. Defer those independent powers and lastvine’s reactive rearing. No fairy-tale immobile hunger failure can result from removing a weird-food comp. T20 explicitly requires ordinary drake meat feeding and removal of active descriptions that promise the disabled ability. Existing art remains available.

## Flotsam and salvage

**Two readings.** Once-only Wreckage salvage can supply water-only haul forever, or recurring natural wet root-line deposits are necessary for that work while initial flotsam seeding is dispensable.

**Consequence.** Deferring all flotsam leaves a trained child idle after any initially reachable salvage is exhausted; staged haul items disguise the missing natural source. Conversely, current root-proximity-only placement can generate goods beside inland transplanted trees and inflate the economy.

**Taken.** Corrected after review: split the existing flotsam capability. Keep post-recession wet root-line deposits as needed_by signature, since deleting them defeats recurring water-only haul. Defer only the initial seed windfall. MapComponentTick reads LastRecedeCompletedTick and calls FlotsamStep then Place; those paths currently share flotsamEnabled and must be decoupled. T6 separates startup gating without consuming or replaying recession events; T15 requires actual wet root-line pickup cells, not root proximity, and saved outstanding-stock limits. Existing Wreckage remains a dependency. Whole-map item-flow modelling must settle sustainable work versus accumulating goods, including full stores and harvested roots. This is my revised deletion argument, not a new owner ruling.

## Cosmetics, medicine and rot

**Consequence.** Lowering permanent injury severity can change pain and capacity while claiming to be cosmetic, violating the no-medicine promise.

**Taken.** Attar production/glaze move to next version, current injury-modifying balm defaults off with retirement recommended. A future genuinely cosmetic approach remains possible. Rot research/power/spent-bed/corpse/Cuisine systems move together, with all entry points gated. Bones stays a dependency for stable references. Existing raw-food refuelling and direct proof hooks do not establish the intended rot-feed economy.

No world-rarity slider remains active, and worldgen gets no next-version mode.
