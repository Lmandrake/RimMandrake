## The second attack on the definition

The laziest implementation paints caps, raises all room temperatures and relies on ordinary warm-temperature rot. It loses the floor/enclosure cause, frozen digestion, and stored-food thermal load. B4, B5 and B6 each contain a mechanism-isolating counterfactual while all kept mechanics remain enabled.

The next laziest implementation makes only the staged clearing workable and spawns a static herd. B2 ranges over full-map placements and growth, B3 requires ordinary feeding/lifecycle/products, and B7 carries all retained mechanisms through the season and a saved transition. A display-only forest, an unbound heat marker and a helper-forced gas cloud each fail their respective pictures.

The remaining uncertainty is coupled thermal balance, wet-native turnover, pathing and food-chain stability at scale. These are model questions with game calibration, followed by the whole live reading. Their unknown meetings are explicit in bars.json. No current gameplay proof, deployed fingerprint or fresh external review is claimed by this proposal.
