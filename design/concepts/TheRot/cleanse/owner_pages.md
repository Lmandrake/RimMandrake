# Page one — The Rot proposed first version

A pale forest of enormous fungi rises out of glowing ground and milky ponds on Ash'karr's night shoulder. Its living floor helps heat your shelter, while the surrounding decomposer ecology consumes supplies left exposed. Food harvested from that ecology keeps giving off heat in storage, so settling here means arranging shelter, harvest and refrigeration around the same metabolism.

Land is dense pale caps, usable ground contacts, biological light, fungal milk shallows, the actual tile sky and Sheen weather, plus ordinary digested wreckage.

Life is fungal forage and timber, the existing hybrid roster, ordinary predation and husbandry, real native flight, and dangerous resident Prime/Skulltop patches.

The signature is metabolic colony life. Living floor supplies warmth, exposed stores feed decomposition, and living harvest heats storage. My runner-up was shared wounds and kin-mending. It adds an independent herd combat/healing loop, so I moved it.
