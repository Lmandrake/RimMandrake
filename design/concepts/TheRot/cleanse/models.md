# Proposed instruments, in build order

These are specifications. None was built or executed on this pass. A source-derived forecast becomes credible only after the stated game comparison. Current kernel/fuzz covers mostly the deferred giant/vat/core/root chains. It supplies a reusable sequence/shrinking pattern, without establishing any of the retained colony physics. No model predicts art readability or enjoyment. Bars B1, B3 and B7 retain their live obligations.

## M1 situation and transition generator

Question answered is whether the retained mechanisms and dormant extras preserve the right effects and entity ownership through every supported state change.

Informs B2 through B7, R1, R2, R4, R5, R7, R10, R11 and R12, and the unknown corpse_ownership meeting. It generates inputs for the room, terrain, population and feature-gate meetings as well.

Inputs are the resolved def/settings manifests, actual room metadata snapshots, tick intervals, stack membership, entity/holder IDs, product/lifecycle state and production predicates or kernel methods where those are callable outside Verse. Enumerate exposed/lean-to/enclosed/breached/map-edge rooms, floor fractions below/at/above the configured boundary, fresh/rotten/frozen items, split/merge/haul/consume/destroy, living/downed/dead animals and corpses, queued/reserved/running/cancelled haul or butcher jobs, feature on/off and fresh/legacy settings. Add save/reload before and after each effect commit, at the same tick as destruction, and while job reservations or old extra holders are populated. Include master-off with each retained feature on individually and each moved feature explicitly enabled with its declared prerequisites. Include default missing fields and explicit old true fields. The accepted empty guardian alarm is a valid next-version control.

Partition only genuinely equivalent situations. Interior translations with identical room membership and occupancy may share a representative. Freezing boundaries, door/roof changes, different holder types and deferred-feature gates stay separate. All critical three-way combinations of corpse ownership, temperature and room transition are enumerated. Reachable longer walks are generated from legal transitions, with failed sequences minimized using the existing TheRotFuzz approach. This generates test situations, never planetary layouts.

Assumptions are that exported room membership and ownership refer to one atomic saved tick, production pure predicates share game semantics, and scheduler ordering is explicitly represented. An unavailable production seam requires a game read. A guessed engine stub is insufficient to settle it. Leave art, autonomous animal choices, rendered flight and thermal evolution to other instruments.

Calibration uses a disposable game's own thing IDs, holder tree, job/reservation state, actual room flags, RotProgress, saved tick and destruction causes before/after each boundary. Expected agreement is exact eligibility, identity conservation, enablement and state transition. A side effect may lag by at most its declared production tick interval, with the lag explained in the output. Corrupting a roof flag, doubling a holder transfer and forcing an extra gate open must each fail the relevant oracle. Calibration itself uses normal jobs and ticks and the real save loader. A serializer round-trip in a stub cannot stand in for a real reload.

Produces a situation matrix, a legal transition graph, coverage gaps and minimal counterexamples labelled by the affected bar. Rough size is [guess] hundreds of boundary partitions and several thousand short walks, based on room, object, holder and toggle products. Keep the exact generated count in the future model report.

Build trigger is immediate. The corpse_ownership meeting is unknown, and required default-off/legacy migration proof has no current instrument. T61 commissions it first. A second constant adjustment to rescue an ownership/enablement check also triggers it.

## M2 thermal and storage balance

Question answered is whether the same metabolism can meaningfully help cold rooms, threaten ordinary refrigeration and still permit competent food storage throughout the authored shoulder.

Informs B4, B5, B6 and B7, R2 through R5, R8, R11 and R12. Settles the room_heat and storage_decay unknown meetings. M1 supplies enclosure/stock transitions and M3 supplies changing room geometry.

Inputs are actual engine heat push/equalization rules and measured wall/roof/door leakage, room area/perimeter and outdoor-temperature history, live warm-floor set/fraction/ceiling, actual marked food stacks and per-unit contribution, cooling capacity/power, room changes, tick phases and the operative digestive contract. Sweep ambient minus 42 through plus 24 C, including a dense boundary partition around freezing and the warm-floor target. Sweep room interiors from 3 by 3 through 30 by 30, irregular equal-area rooms, roof/perimeter gaps, floor fractions throughout zero to full coverage and immediately across the threshold, closed through heavily trafficked doors, empty through the normal maximal stock of a forty-person colony. Initial support of zero through two thousand marked units is a [guess] stress bracket until normal stock is measured. Adjust the bracket after tuning to cover both preservation outcomes, recording the new boundary rather than fixing a cooler/stock count as a bar.

Sweep mat/inert floor with matched produce, ordinary/marked food with matched floor, normal/expanded cooling, power loss/restoration and harvest/haul/cook order. Align and misalign the 250-tick native mat/rot sweep and 2000-tick produce sweep from current source. Include a save/reload at each phase and a changed room whose cached membership must rebuild. Keep every retained ambient heat source enabled. Isolate cause by matched geometry and contents.

Assumptions are ordinary well-mixed room temperature and the production engine's actual heat-transfer formula. Exclude rendered biolight, direct pawn thermal injuries, exact AI hauling demand and hypothetical new heat generators. Direct Sun uses the existing geometric input. An exposed digestive clock is a contract input required by T67. The current frozen-zero multiplication intentionally follows temperature scaling. It fails the proposed Q1 frozen-digestion default, rather than proving a code defect. Treat the chosen digestive contract as an explicit input; a cold-edge answer changes the expected frozen outcome and requires recalibration.

Calibration uses actual game traces of outdoor/room temperatures, individual source heat pushes, stack identity/count, power/cooler state and item RotProgress/destruction over cold startup, steady heat, stock changes, door/roof breach and reload. Require identical eligibility and heat totals per contribution. The forecast must preserve the ordering of matched rooms and preservation outcomes, with temperature agreement within one C after each full equalization interval and digestive loss within the production sweep lag. These tolerances are [guess] provisional instrument tolerances, chosen to distinguish preserved from spoiling stores. If a boundary lies within that tolerance, refine against a game trace before declaring its outcome. Independent swapped-room/product controls rule out site warmth. Never fit and validate using the same trace.

Produces response surfaces of backup heating demand and preservation state, timeseries around transitions, stock/cooling trade-offs, the failing region for each bar and a conservative worst case for B7. Rough size is [guess] several thousand room/stock regimes and a day of trace data per calibration regime, chosen from the cross-product above.

Build trigger is immediate after M1. The room_heat and storage_decay meetings are unknown and current source has both a missing food attachment and a frozen rot exemption. T64 commissions it. A thermal constant changed twice to make an isolated check green triggers rebuilding its comparison surfaces.

## M3 contact and reachability sweep

Question answered is whether the full dense fungal landscape preserves matching visible contacts, native substrate affordances and ordinary work routes through every placement and growth change the supported game can make.

Informs B1, B2, B3 and B7, R2, R3, R6, R7, R9, R10 and R12. Settles forest_geometry and terrain_substrate unknown meetings. Use it to bound the geometry changes supplied to M2 and the route topology supplied to M4.

Inputs are current art/texture fingerprints, HugeThings production contact and selection math, all current giant variants and flips, actual growth-to-contact rounding transitions, terrain affordances, map boundaries, walls/doors/roofs, wreck footprints, ordinary plant coexistence and milk/bridge/sowing/native-drinking contracts. Enumerate the finite collision topology partitions by every growth value at which an occupied cell changes, testing both sides and the exact boundary. Interior translations may share a representative. Every map-edge/corner clipping class, root parity/flip and actual terrain-affordance boundary is separate. Enumerate relative offsets between overlapping contacts through their full support, including a third contact closing an otherwise open corridor. Read all current variants from the manifest each run, with no bar requiring a fixed count.

Use existing authored full-map fixtures at each supported map size from two hundred to four hundred cells per side, preserving actual giant density. Evaluate every walkable origin/destination connectivity region, food/work reservation access, construction affordance and selection bounds. Apply growth/cut/build/bridge/save-reload transitions around choke points. Do not repair a failed dense stand by shrinking its art or population.

Assumptions are that the mask corresponds to the art fingerprint and live occupancy uses the same production transform. Exact path scheduling belongs to B2 ordinary-job calibration and B7. Exclude fresh art creation, taste approval, solar shadow redesign and hypothetical alternatives to the one planet. Local disposable geometry fixtures are instruments.

Calibration compares the model with actual path-grid blocked cells, computed selection rectangle, clicked object identity, placement acceptance and completed ordinary work jobs. Demand exact cell membership and selected identity. At every transform/edge category include a stem-contact refusal and an open-overhang success. Use real growth and saved reload on a boundary case. For milk, compare actual terrain, bridge support, marsh-fungus planting and native hydration state. A missing substrate/affordance has no plausible geometry oracle until T60 supplies the contract. Model output remains provisional at that seam.

Produces collision/route maps, selection overlays, unreachable work regions and minimal obstacle sets for a failed bar. Rough size is [guess] millions of inexpensive cell/contact classifications, based on current mask support, growth boundary partitions and pair/triple offsets, with a small normal-job game sample per equivalence class.

Build trigger is the current unknown geometry/substrate meetings, before accepting any mature dense-map result. T63 commissions it. A first blocked worker, mismatched click, re-grown contact or milk bridge failure also triggers its relevant partition.

## M4 whole Rot season

Question answered is whether the complete default biome retains usable fungal life, storage and colony work over the season and avoids a delayed food, growth, gas or hauling collapse.

Informs B1 through B7 and all rules. Settles ecology_population, emission_wet_growth and colony_workload unknown meetings, with M1 through M3 supplying the other meetings' boundary traces. This is the whole-mod forecast. B7 still requires normal whole live play.

Inputs are actual saved full-map terrain/plant/animal/room/job state, resolved weather probabilities and timing including rainfall and temperature factors, authored tile sun and ambient regime, shared ExplosiveGrowth wet-native Churn and immortal enrollment, gas lifetime/emission/affliction rules, native food needs/reproduction/predation/products, plant regrowth/harvest, corpse/filth decay, Bones/Wreckage outputs, ordinary task capacity and M2 room results. Milk wet classification and actual source attachment must be resolved first. Exports are from existing disposable maps, with fixed planet geometry left alone.

Sweep supported full-map sizes and flat/rocky/milk-pond substrates across cold/middle/warm shoulder, actual default mature density, sparse and ordinary animal cohorts growing toward natural spawner admission, and colonies of three and forty. Run a complete fifteen-day season and a hundred-day extension from the season endpoint. Treat time compression as numerical integration only after cadence-preserving agreement is shown. All kept features, all DLC and normal weather selection remain enabled. Cross work order, mid-season saved reload, roof/door changes, harvest load, stored food, ordinary raids' corpse inputs, wet cycles and native gas exposure. Report realized coverage rather than forcing a rare weather into every natural season. M1 adds rare-state boundary cases separately.

Assumptions are calibrated rates for autonomous animal behavior and colony task allocation, actual room physics from M2 and reachable-region structure from M3. Stochastic ecology needs an ensemble. It can forecast resource pressure and hazard coverage but cannot certify decisions made by autonomous pawns or player-visible readability. Stop at that limit and use B3/B7. Exclude all next-version encounter chains. A full regional gas or growth flood is a model failure candidate even if pairwise checks passed.

Calibration uses independent complete-game traces at cold, middle and warm regimes, with telemetry for plant area/age/renewal, native births/deaths/predation/starvation, emitted gas and affected pawns, weather history, harvested/consumed/spoiled food, initial resources, heat/cooling, completed/failed work and logs. Compare M4 to game records in daily bins. Require exact saved identity/stock reconciliation and matching transition/eligibility causes. For stochastic totals require the game traces to lie inside the forecast's declared confidence envelope, using an initial ninety-five-percent envelope as a [guess] working tolerance. More importantly, require the same sign of food/heat/work pressure and the same flood/desert/stall classification. If error can reverse a bar decision, the forecast is untrusted for that region and an additional live trace is required. Hold out one full regime and reload/order sibling from fitting.

Produces daily map layers for route access, gas and fungal renewal, food/energy/work budgets, distributions with uncertainty, and the worst observed regime per bar. A flood is persistent map-wide gas or overgrowth that prevents ordinary work. A desert is loss of the fungal cover, forage or sustaining native food web. A stall is persistent reservation/path/production deadlock. An error storm is repeated new errors from the enabled mod/dependency path during normal actions. Numeric alarm thresholds are diagnostic check choices. Final rejection follows the player-visible whole bar.

Rough size is [guess] tens of complete starting maps and an ensemble of seasonal event walks with a hundred-day tail, based on climate, map layout and colony scale. Use the cheapest calibrated aggregate simulation before any spatial refinement. The driver T62 collects real-game agreement while preserving full normal ticks.

Build trigger is immediate once M1's ownership boundary and the M2/M3 contracts have credible calibration. All three whole-ecology meetings are unknown. Any first B7 failure, new wet-source class or repeated tuning of a colony-scale constant also triggers its relevant sweep. If a trustworthy aggregate instrument cannot reproduce animal/task dynamics, state that limitation and use the normal whole-game trace for those decisions.
