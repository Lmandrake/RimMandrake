# Instruments specified, not built

Build in the order below. A model output is a prediction until checked against the actual composed game. No source, raw live proof, benchmark or saved trace was executed or produced in this pass. Existing Kernel/SumpFuzz only models mere geometry, shell and vault decisions; the latter systems move off. The exact-clock Glasswalk runner offers a useful calibration pattern, not a whole-mod proof. All calibration records identify source/assembly, composed defs, all DLC, settings, fixed site, starting state and ticks.

## Situation walk

Question: can every retained actor complete its permitted transition without losing state, reapplying a cure from dirty boots, duplicating material or running a disabled extra?

Informs B2–B7 and R1–R12; directly settles the carried-source and saved-state unknown meetings and supplies counterexamples to the whole model. Build first because a resource forecast using a false cure is worthless. T46 supplies the instrument; T40/T43/T44/T45 supply missing implementation/read seams; T50/T51 supply tar-hydration and finite carried-water readings. This is a generator of situations, not a generator of worlds.

Inputs and sweep: actual resolved terrains/affordances, carried-filth data, exposure stages, native growth profiles, actor/food/ingredient identifiers, work policies, reservations, dependency states and local feature gates. Enumerate clean → contact → carry → deposit → exposure → reserve/reach → debit → clean → work → re-exposure; interruption/death/downing/despawn at every transition; save/reload immediately before and after each state mutation. Cover at least the next giver scan. Use ordinary, tar, glasswalk and impassable cells; reachable, sealed, forbidden, stolen-reservation and destroyed ingredients; none/sufficient stock; capable/incapacitated tar carrier; Cleaning priority and Auto solvent cleaning setting on/off; juvenile/adult/wild/tame where that changes the actual transition. Include real-flight state and seated animal-body preservation when custom extras are off. Off/on/reload uses named local gates, never global neighbour ablation. All triple intersections of carried tar, nonaccepting floor and cleanup/save boundary are exhaustive; remaining independent axes may use pairwise generation only after a written failure-equivalence argument. Reversing cleanup/harvest and route/solvent order must generate distinct traces. Include human drink selection with reachable/forbidden/empty bottles and barrels, partial-container debits and reload, no legal tar fallback, and native versus imported non-native animal membership. Imported non-native dehydration is an unsupported boundary, not a permitted thirst exemption.

Assumptions: state identity and event order, not a fixed tick rate, determine reservation/material ownership and recovery. Extract any pure decision logic from the actual implementation rather than duplicating it in a second supposedly authoritative model. Vanilla carried-filth pickup/drop and job interruption are explicit external assumptions. Gate reachability includes producers, menus, jobs, map components and post-load callbacks. A declared field existing is not evidence that its consumer ran.

Game calibration before belief: actual ordinary walk/work/cleanup traces from a disposable save, with identity-bearing state reads just before and after pickup, exposure scan, ingredient reserve/debit and cure. Re-read after the next exposure scan and reload. Require exact agreement on ingredient ownership/debits, carried-filth membership, hediff presence, pending jobs, pawn existence and enabled-extra state for every calibrated boundary class; event timing may differ by one owning tick/scan interval, recorded explicitly. No lost pawn/item, duplicate debit or wrong branch is tolerable. Include a deliberately absent tracking binding, cure leaving carried tar, failed reservation and deferred callback mutant. A mismatch invalidates the model's affected region until fixed/recalibrated.

Outputs: transition coverage table by declared range, rejected mutant, shortest failing trace with actor/item/cell identity, and worst counterexample for each informed bar. No coverage percentage becomes a passing bar. Estimated size: a few thousand boundary fixtures, a small serialized trace and comparator, approximately a modest test module plus adapters; cases grow with actual distinct transitions, not all unrelated Cartesian products. Runtime should be seconds offline; that is an estimate, not a benchmark.

Leaves out visual legibility, stochastic colony scheduling, food-chain equilibrium, artistic animation judgement and a complete Verse emulator. It can validate cleanup logic but cannot alone prove a colony's ordinary AI actually chooses and completes that job; B6 still needs a game state trace. Flight fields cannot prove takeoff; B3 has an actual tracker read. Trigger: immediately on implementation because carried/saved meetings are unknown; also any new consumer, serialization change or cure that reappears after a later scan. Decision changed by output: repair state/gate order, release semantics or consumer wiring before tuning any rates.

## Whole map

Question: with every retained mechanic and dependency on, do land, native food web, routes and ordinary work persist together over a season and the slower crop cycle?

Informs B1–B7, R2/R5–R12, and ground/growth/animals/liquid/stock/cleanup/saved meetings. Builds second, using the situation trace vocabulary. T47 is the implementation/instrument ticket. It is a map-state/flux forecast with event replay and a game trace reader, not an alternative-planet generator and not a substitute for the actual whole bar.

Inputs/range: local map arrays at 250×250, 300×300 and 400×400 from existing fixed-site layouts and enumerated obstacle masks; no world seeds. Read real tar/glass/dry/build cells, actual initial plants/ages, native home-tar hydration bindings, finite imported RM_Bottle_FreshWater / RM_Barrel_FreshWater cargo, resolved thirst factors, capacities, partial draws, deterioration/losses, physical storage/transport and drinking/haul/search job costs, resolved ExplosiveGrowth archetype/top/soak/rate data, ordinary animal diets/needs, source/sink/fire states, light/temperature, work allocations, available benches/research, pitch/wax/solvent stocks and food reserves. Simulate 60 days at colony 3/12/40, actual site ambient traces and threshold bracketing over -15..22 C, low/high outside-work policy, prey/predator and harvesting/hunting pressure. Use planned starting food when cold prohibits growth. T51 must establish actual off-site filling and ordinary caravan transport before treating the water input as obtainable; budget and load finite water cargo for the entire declared horizon with supported heat/work maxima, sip waste, losses and recorded reserve. No unrecorded mid-run replenishment, guaranteed trader or locally generated fresh water. Continued settlement beyond that horizon costs further player-arranged imports. Seated natives hydrate at tar; imported non-native livestock is an explicitly unsupported boundary, not an assumed bottle consumer. Run ordinary default complete ecology and work first; separately branch a reachable generic channel/closed-open gate and player ignition of natural tar. Include source replenishment, ignition-to-continuing-fire fuel consumption and ordinary cooling; persistent burning versus an immediate flash into harmless paving is a qualitative B7 distinction, with no frozen burn duration; no Sump belch or post. Save/reload with dirty worker, a reservation, growing bed and active dependency state; reverse route-first/solvent-first and harvest/cleanup actions on matched initial copies.

Assumptions: no ongoing petroleum delivery from another map; finite mapped ordinary resources except sources the actual FlowWorks engine treats as inexhaustible. Habitat, effective thermal/light growth, animal intake/hunt and current fire/front rules come from owning engines; do not replace them with convenient constants. The cheapest initial surrogate uses coarse labour and sampled feeding actions plus shared/pure growth/geometry kernels where available. Mark all omitted AI dynamics uncertain. No rate tuning is believed if a model ignores sleep/hauling/cleanup contention. All-off and future-extra configurations are separate control traces, never a substitute for the default whole run.

Game calibration: compare the initial map's own terrain/plant/actor/source records to model input exactly. Read repeated real-tick intervals that include a full exposure scan, a plant long tick, ordinary feeding/hunting/drinking, actual crafting/cleanup and a liquid pulse/burn/cooling transition. Deterministic reused kernels must match exact state transitions and resource debits. Named potable cargo litres/partial draws must reconcile exactly against actual drink, loss and reload records. Calibrate tar ignition, ongoing burning, fuel debit and cooling through normal ticks against the same actual region; separately identify fresh source input so pumping cannot masquerade as slow fuel consumption. If ordinary FlowWorks only produces a flash conversion, report the lore/engine conflict and change the attackable integration rule before forecasting success; do not invent a Sump fire engine. Effective growth accumulation must agree within one engine growth increment; path/job boundaries use the Situation walk tolerance. For stochastic births, forage, hunting and immigration, compare repeated matched-configuration intervals and uncertainty envelopes; do not call an empty or unbounded envelope agreement. If predator/worker scheduling is omitted and changes the sign of net food/solvent/work, the surrogate is not credible for that decision: use the actual full-run trace instead. No chosen percentage of disagreement is allowed to hide a failed experience. Normal ticking with wall-clock speed-up is the initial authoritative run; coarser time steps need convergence and real-tick validation, including overlapping fire, traffic and feeding.

Outputs: daily maps of usable connected land/vegetation and traffic/tarred workers; food/prey/solvent inventories and finite potable litres/time-to-exhaustion with drinking/hauling labour; completed ordinary jobs versus outstanding reservations; fire/source region and callback/log signatures; reload/alternate-order differences; worst flood, empty-habitat, no-progress and error-storm trace per bar. A flood means ordinary actions remove the required usable settlement/margins, not merely that tar moved in a channel. An empty habitat means feeding/hunting/growth cannot continue under supported resources/temperature, not a player-hunted-out map. A stall means repeated intervals with runnable ordinary work but no progress because of the mod's queue, not sleep or a cold crop. Repeated identical log errors are reported with their first cause.

Estimated size: several hundred local-layout/policy runs, dense cell arrays up to the largest declared map and daily/event traces; a small simulator/reader, perhaps a few modules, with the real-game run the long part. Exact workload depends on resolved shared-engine APIs. Leaves out combat strategy, all future extra semantics, arbitrary player destruction and any unsupported claim that a finite model proves enjoyment. A full offline RimWorld AI clone is not credible or commissioned.

Trigger: growth/food-web/liquid unknown meetings immediately, especially before any second opposed tuning change; any bar's first live season failure or a change to growth classification, source/fire or habitat. Decision changed: correct data/gating/geometry first, then tune resource/work density if the model is credible. If an inherited engine rule makes the retained experience impossible, surface the conflict with its actual record; do not silently switch off the engine or alter the ruling.

## Route geometry

Question: across placements and bearings, can workers use solid routes with the promised clean-floor/travel tradeoff without trapping carried tar or severing usable margins?

Informs B1/B3–B7, R1–R4/R8/R11 and ground/routes/carried/liquid meetings. T46 extends existing exact-clock/mere-kernel work. Builds third; simple route fixtures may be used during the earlier situation work without waiting for the full sweep.

Inputs/range: actual path costs, terrain/filth acceptance, traversal types, colony build affordances and mere masks. Sweep cardinal/diagonal bearings, turns, edge/door/roof thresholds, narrow/wide 1..12-cell corridors, route lengths 1..200 cells, map edges and obstructed mere candidates on the declared local sizes. Use ordinary clean/fouled paving, glasswalk, dry margins and shallow/deep tar. Compare same pawn at base move cost 5..60 ticks with identical load, injury, glow and job; adapted mouse/stilt/flying classes use their actual traversal rules. Vary accepting cleanup pads present/absent and dirty-carrier entry to a nonaccepting route. A floor's footprint follows legal game affordances; do not claim that a buildable Light floor is a bridge across liquid. Initial mere geometry and habitat ring use actual post-initialization cells, including the initializer's skip/no-seed case. Skip is reported as unsupported site/land defect if the site otherwise claims a healthy Sump, never as a successful mere.

Assumptions: engine path costs can be modeled from recorded per-step costs and obstacle graph, while actual job/path selection needs calibration. No exact universal speed ratio: additive terrain cost interacts with the pawn's base. Geometry can establish the existence of a route and habitat, not which route a busy AI will choose.

Game calibration: compare an actual local-map terrain/affordance mask and reachable components cell-for-cell; walk the same pawn through representative straight/diagonal/corner/door/glow boundary cases using exact positions and ticks, including actual dusk. Require actual traversed path to match its replay and total ticks within one movement transition's scheduling tick; otherwise retain the observed path and repair the cost adapter. Floor deposition and carried-filth membership must match exactly. Paired mouse/colonist and skellarn flight-state evidence checks adaptation rather than mere stat speed. Compare real initial mere masks with RM_MereKernel output for recorded RNG inputs; artificial masks alone do not prove the game creates them. Check a wrong filth mask, free-speed glasswalk and severed-margin mutant.

Outputs: component/habitat maps, path-cost/deposition tables and smallest failing placement/bearing per bar, including locations where no accepting shedding surface exists. Estimated size: tens of thousands of tiny path fixtures and a few full-map masks; roughly one focused geometry module and trace adapter. Leaves out population/scheduler economics, visual black-on-black distinction, and impossible build placements the game refuses. Trigger: unknown ground/routes meeting before terrain acceptance, any mere skip on a supported site, first speed/tracking failure, or terrain/affordance/cost changed twice to move a check. Decision changed: adapt allowed placement, startup geometry or cleanup access; numbers remain rules/checks and no art quota is introduced.

## Solvent queue

Question: can a supplied colony's automatic recovery relieve ordinary exposure without consuming all labour or depending on unbuilt mounds, imported acid or bottled-gas production?

Informs B2/B4–B7, R3–R5/R9/R12 and cleanup/stock/animals/routes meetings. Build fourth, using calibrated movement and transition costs; run an early conservative paper bound if the whole model shows likely starvation. T47 owns the instrument.

Inputs/range: actual broodmother ordinary butcher wax, rendering and solvent recipes, pitch/bitumen recipes, existing benches/research, reachable stock, hauling distance 0..150 cells, available labour fractions 0..1 after observed food/water hauling and actual drinking time, colony 3/12/40, travel policy ranging from all-solid to repeated shallow crossings, initial shortage and adequate reserve cases, interruptions, sleep and danger. Sweep actual configured recipe/work/exposure values at 0.5×,1×,2× for sensitivity, not bars; explicitly finite wax, animals and stockpiles. Compare selective policy and over-cleaning policy. Model clean-ground slow recovery as a competing action and carried-filth shedding separately from floor cleanliness. No assumed strong-solvent trade; no broodmound productivity or new drop. Material for glasswalk must compete for ordinary crafting labour rather than arrive free.

Assumptions: recipe/debit arithmetic is exact; labour availability and exposure arrivals are taken from observed colony traces with min/max bounds. A queueing model does not prove that the actual WorkGiver/AI runs. Do not hide an unstable default behind a hypothetical perfect staffing allocation. Finite local hunting can be a provisioning phase but cannot be called inexhaustible daily supply.

Game calibration: real ordinary research/bench/haul/cleanup sequence, with source item identities, product counts and reservations exact to the item; actual path/job time before/after relief including sleep/interruption. Use repeated default colony intervals, with the same clock and policies, to bound arrival/completion rates. Require the predicted direction of backlog change and the dominant bottleneck to match each observed supply/shortage/traffic regime; predicted counts must include observed counts and have a bound narrow enough that “recovers” and “stalls” are not both possible. Otherwise report indeterminate and measure longer. The same stocks with cleanup disallowed and a cure-retains-boots mutant are negative controls. No model's suggested yield becomes an owner bar.

Outputs: stock/labour flow table, stable/unstable/indeterminate regime map, maximum wait and idle retry traces, finite-resource exhaustion time, and concrete bounds on staffing/recipe/policy changes that could change the result. Estimated size: a small discrete-event queue plus a resource ledger, hundreds to a few thousand policy points; not a colony simulator. Leaves out wealth/market balance, future mounds/vaults/gas/trade and enjoyment. Trigger: cleanup/stock unknown meetings, actual retry starvation, or a recipe/cleanup constant retuned twice. Decision changed: fix reservation/recovery logic if the queue feeds itself; otherwise tune ordinary local supply, work or policy. If basic routine cannot coexist with fixed source constraints, report that conflict, do not quietly restore a second signature.
