## What moved the definition after attack

The laziest visual build is current dark-tinted donor grass with a mere and inert tell plants. That moved ordinary habitats, terrain presentation and honest text into required work. A rich version disguising every unusual animal behaviour as life failed the deletion argument: plain bodies already meet life, so shell/dread moved. A “free mod” tested only with campaign patches could fake the tar loop; the RM tracking seam must be independent. A surgery test that reads the hediff immediately can fake recovery; carried tar and the next scan must be read. A clear, lit Glasswalk lane can fake usefulness by hiding dusk; same actual lighting and same pawn are now part of the control. No numerical speed ratio or asset count is a bar.
