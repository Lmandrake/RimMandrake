## 3. Three calls that I made without a card

Each is logged for your daily digest. For calls A and C I found nothing against the obvious answer. Call B is a
reading of your statement where I first went the other way; it says why I changed.

**Call A. A repair is ordered on the machine and made on the machine.**

- You select the machine and order its next repair there. No level of an onboarded machine is in the build menu, the
  Wrecked level included.
- The machine stays where it stands, at its lower level and in sight, while colonists bring resources and work. When
  the work is complete, the machine changes level.
- A repair that is cancelled or runs out of resources never costs the colony the machine.
- Why: in the built mod the base game takes the lower machine apart before the frame of the upper one is built. The
  look saw that in play. Between the two steps no machine stands there. I read the base game's source, and nothing in
  what I read puts the wreck back if the order is then cancelled. Nobody has cancelled such an order in play, so the
  loss itself is read from source and not yet seen. In 0.1.0 a machine of this mod cannot be built again, and it took a
  journey to find. Your sentence of 2026-09-15 calls repair in place "excellent". Station E of the keeper save shows
  what the built mod does today.

**Call B. While a machine's line in Mod Settings is on, the world hands out no working one. That includes the base
game's own vanometric power cell and psychic emanator.**

- Your statement: "Original (no longer attainable through research or repair, used only for comparison)", and a
  machine "must be found and retrieved to a safe location, studied to enable research, and then slowly repaired".
- For the 18 factory machines, turn 4 already said it: no trader, quest or map gives a working one.
- For the two base-game machines it is new, and it changes the base game. Measured: today the base game gives a
  working vanometric power cell or psychic emanator as a quest reward, as stock of a trader of exotic goods, and as
  loot on generated maps. With this call, and the machine's line on, it gives none of the three. A colony gets a
  working one only by repair of a wreck.
- Where I first stood: leave the base game's cell alone, because a change to base-game loot is a large act in a
  stranger's game. The fresh reader struck that on your words "used only for comparison": a working Original that a
  trader sells is used for more than comparison, and it passes over every repair level. I found no words of yours for
  my first answer, so your statement decides, as it did for "a wreck does nothing".
- What stays: a machine that already stands in an old save. With a machine's line off, the base game hands that
  machine out as before. The infinite chemreactor is not one of this mod's machines and is not changed.
- This is my reading of your statement, not your words. If you want the base game's working cell and emanator to stay
  in the world, say so; it is one line.
