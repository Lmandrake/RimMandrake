## What I took from your three answers

### 1. "All 18 machines" (10:38 PM PST)

Every production machine of the factory mod is ancient in 0.1.0. A colony finds its wreck, brings it home, studies it
and repairs it.

- **The 18, measured from the factory mod's own defs:** autofarmer, drill platform, smelter, biofuel refinery, masonry
  saw, mincer, conveyor oven, assembler, neutroamine synthesizer, ammunition press, autoloom, alloy forge, distillery,
  medicine granulator, machining bay, conveyor crematorium, fish farm and cannery. The cannery exists only when Vanilla
  Cooking Expanded is loaded; without it the count is 17.
- **What stays in the build menu:** the parts that are not production machines. They are the conveyor, the two
  underground conveyor ends, the hopper, the booster and the heatsink. The factory mod's two research projects still
  open these parts.
- **A colony that already has a factory:** a machine that stands in an old save is not changed. The colony cannot
  build another one.
- **A switch for each machine.** Mod Settings get one on/off line per machine, on as shipped. With a line off, that
  machine is researched and built as in the factory mod. This is the built smelter switch, made general.
- **Where I stood.** I recommended a handful of three. My test was whether a second machine can come in by the written
  onboarding steps. All 18 pass that test 17 times, and your card named the cost. I have no argument left against it.
- **The size, so that nobody is surprised later.** 136 new pictures of big machines (Wrecked and Kludged, four facings
  each, for 17 machines), 17 onboardings by hand, and two research projects for each machine. The build takes the
  smelter first and then the machines in the order in which the campaign needs them (your words: "we must first
  prioritize Utinni service").

## Ask 4: what gives a colony plasteel from the forge

**Claim.** Plasteel is the recipe that the forge's last repair brings. The Kludged forge makes the easier alloys; the
Refurbished forge makes plasteel too.

**Attack.** Your words of 2026-10-09 put the unlock on research: "we will need an alloy forge. Maybe even earlier, but
the recipies will unlock later in the game for more difficult alloys. Durasteel earlier, plasteel later". The built
mod does that today: a late research project opens the plasteel recipe. Your words of tonight also bear on it, and
they do not decide it: "specific recipies blessed per repair tier". The forge is the campaign's one recipe that makes
plasteel from raw materials. Otherwise plasteel comes from salvage, from slag re-melted on the smelter, and from trade
(`design/RimMandrake/canon_materials_design_2026-10-09.md` §3.3). The last repair costs an Ancient Component, and you
ruled tonight that one is "Only found in ruins or via plot". So my claim puts the campaign's fabricated plasteel
behind a find. Your card for all 18 named no consequence for the forge.

**Picture.** A colony has found a wrecked alloy forge, carried it home, studied it and made it Kludged. It makes its
first alloys.

- If research gives plasteel: colonists finish a late research project at a bench, and the Kludged forge makes
  plasteel. No Ancient Component is spent. A Refurbished forge makes the same alloys faster, and a colony has little
  reason to spend a component on it.
- If the Refurbished forge gives plasteel: the colony studies the Kludged forge, finishes its second project, and
  spends resources and one Ancient Component. Then the forge makes plasteel. A colony with one component chooses
  between plasteel and another machine. A colony that gets no component makes no plasteel at the forge; it has only
  salvage, re-melted slag and trade. If "via plot" is the campaign's story (my reading, section 3), the campaign can
  hand one out at the moment it wants plasteel to begin.
- If both: the Refurbished forge and the late research project. Plasteel comes last of all.
