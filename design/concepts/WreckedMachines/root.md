### must show
- [ ] `wrecked_machines_root` - Wrecked Machines changes specified machines that install like furniture, such as those of Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Factory, into advanced, ancient machines. A colony can no longer simply research and build one. A colony must find one and bring it to a safe place. Colonists study the machine, and the study opens its research. Then they repair the machine slowly, through levels of function. A Wrecked machine does not work. A Kludged machine works at a minimum. A Refurbished machine has full function, but is slower than the original. The Original is a fourth level, there only for comparison: no research and no repair reaches it. Each repair needs study of the machine, time at a research bench and considerable resources. The last repair, to Refurbished, also needs a rare Ancient Component. The idea can be applied to any game tree. This first version supports only Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Factory in this way, and some versions of vanilla expanded items such as archotech power supplies and psychic emitters.
