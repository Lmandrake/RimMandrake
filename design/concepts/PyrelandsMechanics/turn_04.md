# PyrelandsMechanics, Designer turn 4 (stage 2: argue the edge)

Run `rm-role-designer-1793901065`, 2026-11-05. Two questions. The tree, sheet and scenes are written from your answers;
these are the last two things that need your word before the Deep Desert Tribes' fire rite goes to the builder.

**Claim.** The moment you judge this mod by is the plain rite: a small file of Tribes walks in off the sand, the
grass catches where they stand, they pick scorch-fruit in the black behind the front, and they walk off carrying it.

**Attack.** That sentence is mine. It sat inside the root you approved as "Judged first", but approving the root was not
picking the mod's face. The fruit defence is the only part where the Tribes act *on you*, and a rite you merely watch
could read as scenery, not people who own their harvest.

**Picture.** Each option is a scene on a Pyrelands map with the burn forced to arrive (otherwise you could wait days
for the roll):

1. **The rite party.** The letter names the Tribes; a small party walks in, lights the line where they stand, works
   the black behind the front without stepping into flame, and leaves with the fruit.
2. **The rite, with you in it.** The same rite, and one of your colonists picks in their scar: a warning naming the
   Tribes, then harvesters drive the picker out of the scar and go back to the fruit. No goodwill lost, no raid.
3. **The visit.** A big fire is already burning; a peaceful Tribes group walks in and picks in the black around it.

All three get built and checked either way; your pick only decides which one is the face, judged first. The drafted
showcase is (2). Picking (3) means the showcase is rebuilt around the visit.

## 1. The face of the mod (`a_flagship_pick`)

**Which of the three is the moment that says "that is the Pyrelands Tribes"?** My recommendation is **(1) the rite
party**: you see it in ordinary play without doing anything, while (2) only happens if you send someone into their
scar. (2) is the stronger scene if you think the Tribes must push back to feel real; (3) only happens when a big fire
is already standing.

## 2. The mod's north star (`a_validate_north_star`)

This mod had no north star; one is drafted from your answers today at
`D:\Luke\dev\RimMandrake\design\validation_walks\RimUtinni\PyrelandsMechanics.md`. Its bars, short:

- **Must show:** now and then a scheduled burn arrives with a small Tribes party and a letter naming them · they light
  the line where they stand · they pick scorch-fruit behind the front, never in a burning cell, and none burns · they
  leave carrying it · with a lot of fire standing, a peaceful group walks in, picks in the black and leaves with it ·
  picking in *their* scar during a rite or visit gets a warning naming the Tribes; if the colonist keeps picking,
  harvesters attack that colonist until it leaves the scar, then return to the fruit; no goodwill change, no raid,
  even if the colonist fights back · picking anywhere else draws
  nothing · attacking them openly turns them hostile and they raid you, possibly again later.
- **Cannot show:** a raid, goodwill loss or warning caused by your colony's own fires · the Tribes counting anything an
  animal does (a fire-hawk's twig, a furnace-beast's bed) against you.

**Validate it as written?** ("yes", or correct any bar.)

**Four of those lines are my readings, which you can undo:** fighting back while being driven off is self-defence (no
raids); the visit defends the burned ground it is harvesting just as the rite defends its scar; the warning is a
message naming the Tribes plus a harvester walking to the picker; and their raids after an open attack are ordinary
faction hostility, with nothing extra built.
