# Pyrelands 0.2.0 (the Deep Desert Tribes): the vision

Written only from the root paragraph he approved (`record.json` root, OWNER:1793895903357725756), `sheet.md` and his cards of 2026-11-05. Sentence 8 is the sheet's Mod Settings floor line, not his words. Convergent and Divergent are measured against this text.

1. The Deep Desert Tribes are the Pyrelands' fire harvesters: they come into the Pyrelands now and then for their fire rite and to reap what the fire leaves, then go home, and the Pyre burns with or without them.
2. Some of the fire's scheduled burns arrive with people: a small Tribes party walks in off the sand, and the grass catches where they stand.
3. They pick scorch-fruit in the black behind the front without ever stepping into the flames, and walk off the map carrying what they picked.
4. When a lot of fire is already standing, a peaceful Tribes group may walk in to the burn, pick in the black and carry it off.
5. A colonist picking in the scar they are harvesting is warned, then attacked until it leaves: a defence of the fruit, not a raid, and no goodwill is lost.
6. The Tribes keep no count of your colony's fires, and never blame you for anything an animal does.
7. Attack them openly and they raid you back, and may go on raiding you later.
8. The rite, the visit and the fruit defence each switch off cleanly in Mod Settings.
9. It is judged first on a rite party seen in ordinary play: lighting the grass, picking scorch-fruit in the scar behind the front, and walking off the map with it.
