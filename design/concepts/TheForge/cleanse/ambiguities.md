## Ruined works versus whole towers

Readings: “ruins” permits only rubble and broken silhouettes; or abandoned foundries retain whole standing towers among their remnants. Consequence: a rubble patch could satisfy generic wreck placement but erase the frozen image. Take whole towers and worked ground remains: the_forge.md §§5/8 says towers stand whole. Designer physical choice: towers cannot be mined, deconstructed, salvaged or destroyed by ordinary damage; ordinary salvage affects separate remains only, so a long-settled colony still meets standing towers. Tower base cells are solid and impassable, with no usable roof/floor/interior and no roofs spawned over neighbouring ground. Their shade follows the common Direct Sun caster geometry, not a roof-like scald shield or temperature aura. This distinction preserves the frozen whole towers and the shared geometry ruling without a dungeon or new salvage economy. T16/R9 now state the physical contract and B1 rejects salvage-empty settled maps.
