## Three sentences

You settle among dark foundry towers, steaming banks and open seams of lava on a mountain that rains boiling water onto itself. After warned fire and rain, the lava wears a temporary crust where floatstone gardens ripen, giving a prepared crew time to gather strong, light building stone and leave before the cracks reopen. Heat-adapted animals feed, drift overhead or wake with the rain, making ordinary hunting, husbandry and travel part of reading the same place.

Land: a recognisably manufactured volcanic massif, with whole dark towers, worked remains, ash, permanent buildable banks, visible vent-steam, local hot air and open lava. The ordinary ground trip must meet the old works; no dungeon visit is necessary to identify the Forge.

Life: heat-adapted ordinary fauna and flora, ground life using the wet interval and aerial life around actual vapor habitat; usable local grazing/hunting/husbandry, real flight/floating and ummisk bank movement. Every existing admitted species stays on its roster. Food, local water and jobs must actually work, not just appear in descriptions.

Signature: **Warned fire and boiling rain temporarily crust the lava and wake a harvest interval, so crews gather floatstone and retreat before the crust melts back.** Gas, rain, temporary terrain, natural garden growth, departures, readable warnings, melt consequences and rain-linked plant/native adaptation are parts of that chain. The additional moving plume, flowing-water floods, injected animal swarm and bespoke research are not required by any causal link and move out.

Runner-up: the dhokkur’s remembered roads and wall shoves. It loses because it is a separate settlement/animal-road system; a rain-drinking giant can exist, wake, move and yield hide without deforming terrain or displacing walls. Elaborate aerial hunting is another independent enhancement; ordinary column habitat and real flight stay because removing them contradicts the existing smoke-herd life, rather than because they share its file.

## Why a player reaches for it

The player wants a dangerous industrial volcanic home with periodic resource expeditions, rather than generic barren lava or a collection of unrelated tricks. The desired tension is deciding when to leave shelter, whether to cut another globe and when to abandon a load. Enjoyment is a prediction: the contrast between permanent safe ground and expiring opportunity should create decisions the player can understand. No bar claims fun, dread or wonder as a measurement.

## Using it: first hour, routine and a large colony

On arrival, the player sees actual tower forms, volcanic banks, steam and red open seams. The retained plain arrival explanation says rain scalds from above, roofs stop that direct scald, shade reduces only common geometric Direct Sun, and ambient hot air still needs ordinary heat management. Lava is a present danger with vanilla terrain effects, not a creature habitat. Manufactured bases/yard edges affect normal paths; they must leave a permanent connected shelter/work region. No new site quest is the entrance fee to seeing the mod’s land.

Early priorities are permanent shelter, food/feed access, safe routes and ordinary temperature management. A brief unexpected burst can injure or down an otherwise healthy exposed adult; it must not execute them before a prompt response can occur. This does not promise long exposed survival or immunity under a hot open roof. No required scald gear is invented and the struck fireweed economy stays struck.

Routine follows the actual phase clock. Still heat may contain brief closed-cycle bursts. A readable hiss precedes fuel ignitions; workers interrupt jobs and recall to roofed nonflammable shelter or bare permanent safe ground. The longer boiling phase wets the local cycle and opens flash growth. Quenching produces walkable owned crust and ordinary steam/fog. Gardens seed only on valid crust, mature through ordinary growth, are harvested through ordinary plant jobs, and are hauled to permanent ground. At cracks, unharvested gardens depart with a visible sign/message, the deadline remains inspectable, and a prompt crew retreats before progressive melt removes its return. Work during cracks means salvaging carried loads/retreat, not an invisible extension to garden harvesting.

At forty colonists the mod remains one system. Dispersed workers need usable shelter routes and warnings that buy a prompt recall; it does not teleport or auto-draft them. Cutting, hauling and shelter improvements are tested in both orders on a safe established base. Tamed julmox are ordinary animals the colony feeds, handles and slaughters; ownership is not a trigger for sealing them out of control. Natives’ actual needs and ordinary predation run while the colony hunts and constructs. Bounds and worst routes are explicit in bars.json; no staged tiny pad qualifies as this operating regime.

## What changes in the colony’s story

The colony learns the difference between ground that belongs to it and ground it is borrowing. Foundry forms and unexplained, nonliving motion beneath the melt preserve the history of unfinished manufacture; no inspect text reveals what the machines are doing. Permanent banks become the place for roofs, stores and work. Floatstone accumulates from legitimate trips and enters ordinary construction without a separate study ritual. Existing animal bodies, feeding, hunting and taming provide ordinary life; the special reactions and plots are next-version choices.

The local map does not have to contain a summit to belong to the single massif. Cone/flow/skirt contexts are terrain/height regimes of the fixed place, not a new planet generator. No claim here changes the planet’s placement or the disputed older Scald-rim versus later Dune-Sea sheet geography: take the later Forge-specific frozen sheet’s Dune-Sea massif context; do not repaint either record. No historical tile statistic is asserted as present geography.

## Costs

The costs are ordinary work, exposure, temperature management and the option to abandon harvest or cargo when ground expires. Open lava, scald, gas-driven fire and ordinary predators remain dangerous at shipped defaults. Floatstone is actual light, strong, nonflammable attractive building stuff, not guaranteed profit or a gravship fuel saving. Comparisons use equivalent ordinary structures/materials. Its normalized harvest and existing plant-economy constraint remain; no bar pins output per trip, price or a number of gardens.

A successful trip must make genuine material progress. It need not finish a building alone. If current timing/cost/growth cannot support that across the declared operating regimes, change attackable placement/order/tuning rules under the economy constraints, rather than pretending a debug-spawned stack proves an expedition.

## When it goes wrong and the readable sign

Rain injuries have real health state and direct-source evidence; an overhead roof stops falling scald, while geometric shade and ordinary air temperature remain distinct. Gas uses actual exposed flammable plants and vanilla spread, so a bare map does not invent fuel. Warning plus intact routes must buy time; a hiss at the same instant a distant return is already lost is a failure.

Crust is temporary skin over lava, never a permanent new continent. Cracks and the saved phase deadline are sufficient to recall crews even with audio muted or another map viewed. Deliberate delay can kill, destroy possessions or lose a harvest. Death state, corpses/ordinary loss evidence and located summaries remain accessible; a pawn never silently vanishes. Garden departure may use the existing dust-plus-counted-message abstraction; it need not instantiate a flying garden, and it is not a creature flight exemption.

Reload must continue the same pawn identities, terrain ownership and causal events without reseeding free rewards. Disabling/ending the cycle must resolve pending work and leave no owned hazard or stuck native. Gentle cleanup is not falsely advertised as harming nothing: rooted plants may be lost and unsupported buildings deconstructed with an explicit account and movable leavings preserved. For surviving pawns/items, search the actual permanent safe region. If no valid landing exists, keep an explicit, visible pending safe-support state until resolution is possible instead of removing the floor under them. That emergency state must not permit a new reward cycle, buildings farming free ground or indefinite duplicate seeding. This is the Designer’s safe fallback, specified for implementation, not a claim the current radius-limited relocation already does it.

Whole-system failure includes recurring default exceptions, no natural opportunity on eligible connected maps, feedback that wipes ordinary food/native viability, or fire/growth/material pressure that prevents repeated workable intervals. A naturally bare lava region or deliberate player overhunting is not evidence of cycle-caused desertification. The models and actual state traces distinguish those causes; no seasonal balance conclusion is guessed here.

## Neighbours and dependencies

The current per-biome ambient heat extension conflicts with the standing rule and must be removed/replaced by compliant shared wiring. The sun’s position is read from the fixed tile, never a biome name. Ambient volcanic warmth belongs on the ordinary thermometer.

EnvironmentalHazards: shared pulse/weather snapping, heat-armour scald, flash window, arrival text, vapor geography/immunity and mechanic gates. Its found-tech engine remains for other biomes, while both garden study and externally patched fungus study are gated off for Forge. Do not switch off that engine globally to hide a Forge extra.

ExplosiveGrowth: its actual resolved everyday/soaked profiles, tree lifetime and moisture semantics remain enabled. The current implementation has replaced older multiplier bands; do not assume the old dry/wet numbers or use a stale Python mock as the final rate. Flash growth and shared growth must be read running together. Fix local-off release without changing another biome’s profile.

Wreckage: existing local placement, terrain/occupied-footprint legality, deconstruction and ordinary salvage contract for needed surface remains; no new salvage economy. Forge-specific physical content has not been found in this source subset. Bones: existing eligible butchering adds bone beside ordinary meat/hide, not a second Forge product system. VEF: permanent floating currently depends on its guarded comp for bulloos; the required locomotion must survive actual supported mod guards, through an explicit dependency or existing shared support. FlowWorks: current type/assembly coupling exists even though default rain releases are deferred; isolate/gate the adapter, do not redesign water.

Vanilla already supplies ordinary roofs/rooms, fire spread, heatstroke, scald armour response, temporary terrain, jobs, stuff construction, dormancy state and normal predators. Prefer those contracts to custom work rules. Ordinary danger remains, but no extra bespoke JobDriver is required to cut/haul/build. Native drinking of its own environmental water follows the standing ruling and does not turn scalding water into safe player drinking water.

Optional campaign cast and ore remain external, with no eviction or new mining system. Tower dungeons/tenders/salvage-study, tibanna tap/embargo and dynamic creep ring are external extras to gate locally. Beldons remain the only lawful tibanna source even in the deferred feature; code sharing cannot expand that source. RM invented exotic names remain RM; genuine canon content belongs in the campaign tier. The bestiary is aspirational naming context, not evidence of a built species or an instruction to rename these admitted animals.

## Settings

Meaningful retained controls are master, weather pulse, grand cycle, gas, freeze, destructive melt, gardens, flash response, native dormancy, ordinary sky habitat/column visibility, ummisk bank/steam movement, surface content and warnings where separately adjustable. Defaults describe the proposed shipped mod, all retained mechanics on. Exact current field coverage and required additions are in T24. Keep useful tuning and reset/save support. Disabling a warning sound never removes inspect/terrain/death information; disabling a parent feature never leaves a harmful child penalty running. Startup-only menu changes state their restart requirement.

Every extra has a clearly labelled next-version switch, default off in declarations, load fallbacks and reset. New map state must show no extra-produced thing, job, letter, incident or ability; enabling must reach the existing entry point with dependencies installed. Old defs/stacks/installed buildings and keys remain loadable. No worldgen toggle and no new cross-biome option; any existing unsupported extension stays default off. Settings existence, load health and clean log are inherited floors with regression checks, not artificial extra experience bars.

## Four stories

**First hour.** A newcomer’s wood trip detours around a tower footing; the shape is visibly worked manufacture, not just rock. The crew roofs a permanent bank. A surprise burst injures the worker outside; a prompt retreat or rescue works. Shade helps common Direct Sun, but the roofed open air remains hot. This story is an acceptance picture, not a claim already measured.

**An ordinary week.** Hissing interrupts outdoor work. Fire and rain pass, an outcrop stands up as a dhokkur, julmox crop the wet interval and sky animals feed around steam. The crew uses newly cooled crust, cuts ripe gardens and hauls floatstone to permanent stores. The ordinary construction menu spends it; nobody needs to study a globe or enable special hulls. At cracks the crew abandons remaining growth and returns before the melt.

**The worst day.** A delayed hauler has cargo on a threatened crossing. The visible deadline was real; they can abandon the load and retreat, while a late rescue stays risky. Goods deliberately left behind burn with a readable account. On a separate shutdown copy no valid landing exists: the occupied support stays explicitly pending rather than exposing the survivor to lava. Disabling the feature is not a secret kill command.

**The season’s turn.** The colony reloads from its live growth-phase save and continues the same trip. Later it improves shelter before hauling where it previously hauled before building; the cycle remains usable. Native needs, real fire, geometric sun, growth, rain damage and ordinary products all keep running. Whether food, habitat and opportunities actually persist for this whole stretch remains an explicit unknown for the calibrated whole-season model and real-tick trace, not a rosy numerical guess.
