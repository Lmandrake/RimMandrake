## Final lazy-build attack

The cheapest wrong whole build is still a random metal field, working scalar mood and several impressive direct proof buttons. It satisfies many old noun lists but fails B1's connected choices, B4's causal location and cancellation, B5's target bounds, B6's paid return and B7's concurrent season. The proposal therefore keeps missing maze, event-location, debt/restitution and response instruments as build work rather than deleting the bars. It removes the temptation to satisfy them by enabling the repair arc, readers or ship witnesses.
