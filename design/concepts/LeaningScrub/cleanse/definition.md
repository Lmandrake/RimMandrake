## The mod in three sentences

A low sun hangs over a crusted plain of leaning fuzz, steady wind and tall venomvine islands. You build a wind-powered home, keep ordinary work on maintained safe ways, and deliberately prepare journeys into the vines for venom or slow fuel claims. Native grazers and predators live around rare named sweetline trees, where coated animals rub for health and leave useful wool.

Land: buildable crust and soil, spaced knee-height ground cover, shared sunward lean, windy/foggy ordinary weather, exceptional Stall and Gale, tall vine obstacles and rare ancient tree landmarks.

Life: preserve the effective native animal and ordinary plant rosters, their food, reproduction, natural defense, products and genuine flight; retain sweetline grooming and foraging. Fiction flyers stay flyers even when special mass-launch events are off.

Signature: venomvine advances across wild ground, so the colony maintains safe routes and explicitly prepares dangerous trips to harvest venom or bank slow fuel claims.

Runner-up: wind masks motion under the fuzz until Stall exposes it. It loses because invisibility, lee geometry, freeze/hunter AI and traffic cascades form another full cause-and-effect chain. Ordinary wind remains the land and power resource, without this second signature. Fire stamping/attribution and the tree diary are not smuggled into ordinary life.

## Why a player reaches for it

Choose this place to make a stable home beside a wild boundary that must be understood and tended. Vanilla arid shrubland supplies outdoor space and ordinary grazing/hunting; this biome adds a continuous visible wind gesture and a specific travel discipline. Vanilla path cost is inadequate when an otherwise ordinary haul may choose poison after the detour closes: here the consent is explicit and expires with the trip. A normal tailoring bill, crop/animal products, regrowing harvest and vanilla refuelable work are preferred over new jobs except where the signature actually needs blanket placement and intent.

Prediction, not a bar: a small deliberate expedition whose risks the player can read will be more interesting than an exhaustive tour of plant tricks. No enjoyment judgment is disguised as a regression threshold. No web comparison is needed: the relevant baseline is the repository’s actual vanilla integration seams and shared engines.

## Using it: first hour, routine and a large colony

Arrival uses the actual local player start, not map centre. The start is buildable and connected to an ordinary safe way out; vines are already a visible part of the wider ground, not isolated collectibles. Ordinary cover is low enough that a standing person is visible above it. Build shelter, connected wind generation and storage, and plan food under the existing protected-growing rule. Ordinary native forage remains forage; it does not imply unprotected colony crop farming is effective.

Choose a few routes by clearing actual plants. Roofs, existing buildings, laid floors or underterrain, and sown plants in growing zones protect their cells from new runners. Bare stockpile, dumping, growing and other zones leave the dirt open to creep, as do Home areas, blueprints and cutting designations. Dirt routes need actual repeated maintenance, with labor measured alongside food, hauling and crafting.

Ordinary work includes hauling, construction, food, rescue, tending, firefighting and animal hauling. It takes a safe way or waits with a clear reason. If a vine matures during the job, stop and replan before the hazardous cell. One safe detour chosen once does not prove the policy.

Select a colonist, right-click the target and choose “Order risky trip.” Confirm the named target and risk of injury from venomvine contact. The option is available only to harvest ripe Dripping venomvine, place a smother blanket, pick up dry cane already paid out by a due claim, cut an armed vine stand or rescue a downed pawn. A Harvest or blanket-placement designation stays ordinary work. If every physically traversable route would cross armed venomvine, it waits with “No safe route: armed venomvine contact.” Both Dripping harvest orders use the same vanilla regrowing harvest on completion. Acknowledgement applies to the selected actor, job and target and still obeys physical size barriers. Consent expires on completion, cancellation, target change, downing, death or mental break. Current-schema reload preserves only a validated unfinished job link and recomputes the path. Drafting or emergency forced movement grants no permission for later work.

At forty colonists, the same rules apply to simultaneous harvesters, haulers and rescuers. Tree reservations, blanket ownership, blocked product placement and ordinary circuit demand must not be hidden by hand-spawned supplies or invulnerable actors. A path retry loop or all available labor spent keeping a modest dirt route open fails the home experience. Numerical maintenance budgets are checks to attack, not bars to defend after every retune.

## What changes in the colony’s story

The ordinary windy routine powers work, fog changes visibility, Stall is visibly and audibly calm, and Gale drives dust and stronger actual wind. Batteries bridge a sensible calm period or the player sees and manages a power shortage. The fictional turbines never stop phrase means reliable wind outside exceptional calm, not infinite power, guaranteed factories or a hidden energy grant. Custom surge/breakdown, deafness and raid weights are off.

Vine trips have an immediate renewable venom harvest and a delayed land claim. First-version active forms are thicket, Dripping and Hollow. A Dripping edge is an actual harvest with regrowth; a blunt Hollow gallery is a recognizable safer passage within the shared size band. No special crawl animation or pocket map is needed. Other form defs and art remain in the source, but default spawn paths do not place them. This deliberately narrows the prior each-ruled-form current showcase; it does not dispute the recorded earlier commissioning of those forms.

The player weaves FuzzFiber and SweetlineWool at an ordinary tailoring bench, or uses a clearly labelled ordinary Cloth alternative when local wool is absent. This alternative is a Designer choice, specified work, not claimed current behavior. A tree-free map is legal: there is no free cloth, guaranteed trader, guaranteed tree or required immediate smothering. A colony may import cloth or grow its inputs under ordinary protection. These are optional expedition rewards, not prerequisites for survival or route clearance. The model must expose that supply cost.

A completed blanket placement consumes its blanket and records a local claim. The plant visibly remains for years and still warns of any contact hazard until it actually falls. Smothering halts its creep; it does not make a safe shortcut by label. Save claim duration, due time and yield basis at placement. Current tuning proposal is 120 engine days, adjustable 120–600; those are delegated checks, not his numbers. Changes affect later claims only. Actual death/destruction of a claim loses it with a sign; it does not pay a secret duplicate. At maturity, deliver the physical cane once through a safe outside-Plant.TickLong transaction. Default source currently recomputes duration from settings and needs TCLAIM.

Dry cane must perform as premium fuel in matched verified consumers; accepting wood’s filter is insufficient. Implement only the needed supported adapters with accounted mixed-fuel credit; do not redesign a fuel engine. Current source premium behavior is unproven, ticket TFUEL. A larger yield or exact heating multiplier is not the bar.

## Ordinary life, its deletion tests and the tree boundary

Native roster membership is retained. Base animal entries, patched-in campaign species, absorbed ports and ordinary products remain content. The active roster must be read from current composed defs, not only this folder’s BiomeDef. Historical campaign giant/scavenger binding and canopy-engine source paths could not be located by a full src filename/symbol search in this checkout. Existing local comps and XML bindings are present; external realization remains unknown. Absence from this checkout is never labelled proof that deployed behavior is absent. No animal is evicted to simplify tests, fix a large-band conflict or enforce single-homing. Existing multi-homes are human-review notes, not an algorithm. A source scan found no repeated inline wildAnimals entries for this biome’s inline animals in other biome files in this checkout; this is not proof of unique effective homes, because patch-added and inherited memberships remain to be resolved by TNATIVE. The campaign roster and rehome/library work remain explicit.

Keep ordinary diets, hunting, growth, eggs, live birth, butchery and existing innate defense, including thornhold melee venom. These form ordinary life, not a new population engine. Removing the defense would turn the existing naturally venom-armed animal into a false safe prey; adding a special den retaliation schedule is not required. Cruststar remains edible and grazed by ordinary eligible animals; no exclusive grazer selection/spawn engine is promised now. Ordinary hunger/flight/corpses remain when bloom, camouflage and corpse rolling are off. A native’s environmental water works for it under the standing rule; this says nothing about colonist drinking safety.

Keep actual flyer flight and its directional body movement because of the standing flight rule. Shirrel, dustflutter and other fiction flyers launch and land during ordinary movement with Gale glide, sweetline tether, crown and bloom off. Interruption and death resolve visibly. Visual flight observations require owner attendance.

A sweetline is a rare named large landmark, not a guaranteed resource installation, an aura or an incident. Its persistent name is a location label. Its trunk/canopy use BiomesShell and shared shade geometry. Removing the landmark defeats the stated land; removing its wool/grooming defeats the stated ordinary tree-forager-animal relationship. Once-mature free shedding provides ordinary forager material. A coated animal can reach the tree and rub when its coat is ready or it has an eligible illness and needs another care pulse. A completed rub consumes only a ready coat, dropping part and banking the conserved rest. It gives the personal, nonstacking temporary health condition required by OWNER:1794835478275153823. A sick animal with a regrowing coat can rub for care alone and return after the effect wears off until the eligible illness clears. No injury repair, missing part, addiction, permanent genetic condition, resurrection, remote cure or colony hospital service.

Tame colony animals can get animal flu and plague through vanilla incidents. Wild animals get neither from those incidents. Gut worms and muscle parasites are tested on staged eligible animals, with no new infection source in this version. Use mechanism-specific adapters to clear the named condition over time sooner than matched untreated recovery. Healthy animals with ready coats may groom. Animals without a shearable coat use ordinary medicine. Roped, pen-blocked or area-restricted animals do not escape to rub. Unsupported disease mechanisms remain untreated with a truthful readout. The bank debits only delivered output, conserves fractional credit, and physically drops excess rather than silently erasing it. Wool shed naturally by an ever-mature tree is accounted separately from animal coat conversion. Current Rub has no located healing call; TCARE is required. Deleting the bank is not pruning a perk: it would destroy or duplicate the coat transaction.

The diary, pilgrim traces, apparel thought and special furniture comfort all move. Actual timers, current bank and condition inspect are necessary feedback and stay; no narrative history, bespoke letter or simulated visitor is required for that feedback. The recipe’s local felt ingredient is not a reason to retain unrelated felt bonuses.

## Costs and failure signs

Costs are land allocation, turbines/storage and ordinary maintenance work, time spent safely reaching resources, blanket ingredients, years of delayed claim and medical/food risk during explicitly dangerous trips. The ordinary land is mild relative to this world, not a new biome-specific heat mechanic. Sun exposure is the same shared geometric rule with roofs/parasols and tree shade as elsewhere.

Hazard warning distinguishes a young unarmed runner, armed venom-bearing stand, blunt gallery and still-hazardous blanketed claim using inspect/current state plus visible form. No hidden off-cell thorn litter exists at defaults. A route rejection names why work is waiting; a lost claim names the cause; a dead/removed pawn or animal leaves a readable body/exit/cause. Output blockage retains custody for retry, not an endless error or paid-but-missing reward. If a shared-engine integration cannot supply these truths, keep the needed bar and build its instrument, rather than soften the wording.

## Four short stories

First hour. Wind is audible before anything asks for attention. A colonist stands above low fuzz; a dark vine stand beyond the landing ground interrupts the open view. The player places shelter and plans a wind-only circuit without walking through a poisoned shortcut. A tree may be absent; that is ordinary Scrub, not a broken start.

Ordinary week. Haulers use the cleared detour. A runner approaches its untended edge, and a worker cuts it during ordinary upkeep. The forager explicitly accepts a vine trip and harvests venom from a recognizable edge. A tame coated animal with flu rubs at an accessible sweetline and recovers sooner while wool remains available to gather. No pilgrims, lore tokens or mass traffic show is needed to make these events legible.

Worst day. Stall arrives with a visible and audible loss of wind. The battery reaches its planned margin. Growth closes a natural bottleneck and a hauling job waits rather than inventing permission to cross poison. The player clears/reopens a route or explicitly orders a dangerous rescue trip; a downed pawn does not carry that permission into its rescuer’s next job. A destroyed claim is visibly lost, not resurrected on loading. These are comprehensible setbacks, not guarantees of survival.

Season’s turn. The colony has changed its work and route layout around the vine margins. Animals have fed, aged, hatched and died with signs. A saved unfinished trip resumes with the same identities. A blanketed stand still waits through the seasonal cycle; the follow-on long-horizon observation later sees its original due date and useful dry cane. Doing the main actions in another feasible order changes the story without doubling coat, venom or claim reward.

## Adversarial second reading by this author

A pretty empty map could satisfy wind artwork: vine_country requires route-scale encounters from the actual start. A single detour could hide dangerous autonomous work: safe_routine closes it and reads intermediate cells. A cheap model could freeze weather or supply actors: whole_season requires unlocked progression, IDs and no debug relief. A no-tree map could deadlock the recipe: the legitimate Cloth alternate is explicit proposed work. A wool-heddiff label could fake care: tree_care compares actual illness progress after completed AI rubbing. A flyer stat could pass while all launches moved: real_flyers requires ordinary flight independent of the extras. A high cap could still choke a bottleneck: Geometry and route sweep and Whole-map season model are unknowns to build first. None of these arguments claims a current pass, a fresh reviewer approval or a gameplay measurement.
