# Page two — what to check

The decisions that mattered:

- “Never stops”: ordinary wind is dependable; Stall is genuinely calm. Storage and actual connected work decide useful power; no hidden energy or scripted surge.
- “Tall/cover” means small bodies within fuzz height, standing people above it, mature vines towering over both.
- “Safe”: ordinary work detours or visibly waits even when the detour closes. A specific acknowledged expedition can risk traversable vines; permission cannot leak to another job or rescuer.
- “Maintained”: developed cells resist creep; dirt routes require measured labor. Home-area paint and blueprints confer no invisible immunity.
- “Varied”: poisonous harvest edges and blunt galleries suffice now; other commissioned forms remain later.

- “Fly”: shirrel and other flyers launch in ordinary movement with Gale glide, tether, Crown and runway reactions off. Flight looks remain attended.
