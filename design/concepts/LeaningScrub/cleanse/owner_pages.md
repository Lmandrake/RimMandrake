# Page two — what to check

The decisions that mattered:

- “Never stops”: ordinary wind is dependable; Stall is genuinely calm. Storage and actual connected work decide useful power; no hidden energy or scripted surge.
- “Tall/cover”: people stand above ordinary fuzz; vines interrupt their view. Existing art stays accepted; functional legibility still matters.
- “Safe”: ordinary work detours or visibly waits even when the detour closes. A specific acknowledged expedition can risk traversable vines; permission cannot leak to another job or rescuer.
- “Maintained”: developed cells resist creep; dirt routes require measured labor. Home-area paint and blueprints confer no invisible immunity.
- “Varied”: poisonous harvest edges and blunt galleries suffice now; other commissioned forms remain later.
- “Years”: capture each claim’s due time and payout basis at placement; settings changes affect later claims. A blanket is not instant safe clearance.
- “Rare tree”: no tree is legal. Add a labelled ordinary Cloth recipe alternative; no free inputs or guaranteed trader. This is proposed work, not present behavior.
- “Healing”: completed rubbing gives a personal temporary benefit against eligible animal disease/parasites; no proximity cure, injury repair or pen escape. Actual severity progress must be observed.
- “Fly”: ordinary launches must work with Crown and runway reactions off. Flight looks remain attended.
- Source conflicts: ordinary Sand and lightning-strike weather violate crust/no-natural-ignition constraints. Preserve the requested wind twin while removing ignition; use shared geometric Direct Sun.
