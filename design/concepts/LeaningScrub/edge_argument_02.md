## Attack

The cheapest wrong version has a pretty windswept map, one successful detour and isolated proof hooks. A later automatic job crosses the poison; walking growth invades the maintained way; a reload forgets a claim; the premium fuel is ordinary wood with another label; the ritual calls nobody on the franchise-free list. Any of these can survive a collection of green hook tests. Current source establishes several gaps, and proves none of these as a current observed play failure.

## Picture

Written exploration, not a capture: at settlement the player sees a shared lean and tall islands around usable ground. Routine workers keep supplies moving along maintained ways. During a failed journey, an unavailable safe path leaves the routine haul unfulfilled instead of silently authorizing a harmful crossing. The player orders and prepares a separate expedition, obtains a useful resource and brings it home. A blanket converts scarce fiber and felt into a visible, saved future claim; its delayed payoff must agree with the intended horizon. These are starting situations for later scripts, not a claim that this play has been demonstrated.

## Cost assumptions and finite observations

The Designer assumes a maintained route means deliberately developed or repeatedly cleared ground, not all unexplored land. Ordinary creep and walking need one coherent developed-ground guarantee; a merely cleared dirt way may need continued upkeep, whose cadence is not measured. Routine jobs must still obey the no-dangerous-crossing policy when that way becomes unavailable. This does not promise immunity to raids, fire or weather.

The explicit recipe costs are 40 fuzz fiber and 15 sweetline felt, with 2400 work in the current definition; these are implementation values, not owner-picked numbers. Wind's supply and repair cost, clearing labor, time to locate a rare tree or recover farmstead plans, and a 40-colonist colony's aggregate work and tick costs remain unmeasured. No claim of an affordable economy or acceptable tick rate follows from the definitions. [Recipe](../../../src/RimMandrake/LeaningScrub/Defs/ThingDefs_Items/RM_SmotherCraft.xml#L97).

For every family the table names concrete wrong implementations and a finite set of discriminating situations. Equivalent ordinary workers can share a fixture; add a pawn variant only for a different policy, geometry, job or state failure. A completed journey and reload/order-change interaction do not replace independent feature scenes. Removed wardens and farmer camps stay absence checks. The existing plain vaporator remains learned from farmstead plans with no drying scar; complete recover→research→build→power→water as a separate scene, since ProofVaporator reads definitions only. [Vaporator proof limit](../../../src/RimMandrake/LeaningScrub/Source/RM_ScrubInjections.cs#L436).
