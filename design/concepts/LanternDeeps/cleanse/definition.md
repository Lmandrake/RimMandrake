# Lantern Deeps — proposed Design Version 0.1.0

This is a Designer proposal, not an applied definition or an owner approval. Every new scope, trigger, boundary and build choice below is delegated by this cleansing brief. No text is added to `his words`. The concept directory contains no record, sheet, tree leaves, log or next-version file to amend; the historical inventory is `design/RimMandrake/designer_codex_2026-11-09/LanternDeeps/record.json`, with its later owner answers in `ANSWERS_OWNER.md` and ledger event `OWNER:1794375005355305543`. It is grounding, not a locked passing tree. No current gameplay verification is claimed.

## Say the mod

Below the frozen surface is a persistent blue cavern of mineral wealth, fungal pasture and excellent equipment left by failed expeditions. Your expedition light attracts the living cave’s attention, and the dead machines reveal that the crystals have minds of their own. You can learn the peaceful inhabitant’s greeting or take from a protected gallery and face its remote bodies, then return to the same place with those consequences still there.

Land: enclosed mineral passages, native light patches, still air, ordinary cave temperature, shallow water, accessible technological ruins and roofs that warn before failing.

Life: native fungi and forage, cloth and mineral harvest, ordinary cave hunters, luminous hydrocarbon grazers and usable fuel animals, with existing roster admissions preserved.

Signature: Expedition light draws the living Deep’s attention, revealing crystal minds through the dead machines they use to greet visitors and defend their galleries.

Runner-up: a managed light economy of safe Lanterns, lamp-draining vermin and aurora-powered animal hazards. It loses because the owner’s selected experience turns on recognising inhabitants, reinforced by his November defence/greeting answer. The frozen ordinary-light consequence and safe Lantern trade remain necessary parts of the approach to that encounter; Sipper radius drain, electrical feeding and storm surges do not. Another runner-up, a moving-crystal predator ecology, is likewise independent and deferred.

## Why a player reaches for it

A cold-surface expedition seeks mineral material, edible cave forage, cloth, useful fuel animals and the excellent equipment of previous visitors. The cavern offers an inhabited destination worth revisiting, rather than a resettable loot room. Its native light and metal should make discovery attractive before the player understands who is looking back. “Riches for kings” is figurative abundance and temptation in the owner’s October experience, not an exact sale price or guaranteed wealth engine. The protected gallery holds concentrated native lanternstone: RM_LanternstoneWall yields RM_ChunkLanternstone, cut into RM_Lanternstone. It must be more desirable to take for actual luminous construction or trade than reachable ordinary rock outside it, with no equivalent easy unguarded haul nearby. Scattered unprotected lanternstone may remain. This is a deposit/use comparison, not a new mineral or campaign progression. Bar B5 rejects useful salvage elsewhere masking worthless protected stone; its real mining/hauling/cutting comparison must establish the temptation before defence. Existing price data do not establish it, and price retuning does not change the bar.

The predicted pleasure is the change from prospecting to recognising company. That prediction is not judged as an enjoyment metric.

## Using it

**First hour.** On a supported cold host the player finds a glowing natural mouth or a ruined shaft visibly set in failed technological mining works. These are local map features on the existing world, not world sites or planet authoring. Either leads to a persistent cave with a reachable return exit, navigable galleries and actual initial life. Each independent mouth keeps its own destination unless an authored link explicitly says otherwise. No multi-map dungeon chain is added.

Native patches leave navigable darkness between them. A limited naturally placed living Lantern is discoverable on suitable reachable ground; ordinary sowing is a later replacement route, not the only way a new expedition learns the light choice. This is a delegated availability correction: current source defines the sowable Lantern but does not naturally place it. Initial generation must also place ordinary fauna through existing game APIs; a `wildAnimals` list is not proof of an initial population.

Carried lamps permit useful work, but sustained ordinary illumination at occupied colonist cells accumulates attention. Dim work and light contributed only by living Lanterns avoid that trigger. Harvesting a Lantern turns its material into ordinary luminous stone and removes its safe light. “Safe” never means safe from a hungry predator, a fight already underway or a roof fall. Risk is inspectable as quiet/building/drawn/cooling and has a local approach sign. Native predators arrive by legal traversable routes; no unexplained manhunter appears inside a sealed room. A draw recruits only an existing alive, wild, mobile RM_BloodropMoth not already drawn, with a legal route to the exposed worker. It never creates a pawn. With none eligible or reachable, inspect says the cave is quiet and distinguishes absent hunters from blocked routes; no attacker appears. Ordinary births and existing population replenishment are independent of attention, which cannot request, accelerate or reserve them. Player-caused eradication can remove this danger; unintended ecosystem depletion still fails the season bar.

**Routine.** A light brought near a mind’s dead chassis makes it stand and face the visible source. Its inspect identifies the controlling rooted crystal; the crystal inspect identifies associated bodies. This is control through the local crystal/electronic network, not eyesight through walls. Light-facing itself uses received, unblocked light. The associated machines worry at rock without mining out the land for the player. Ordinary vanilla hostile fauna remain distinct from these bodies.

The living gallery’s protected concentrated native lanternstone cells are marked when a mind or a mining designation is selected; hover/inspect explains that extraction provokes its occupants. Mere designation is not aggression. Completed extraction in that marked gallery, or actual damage to a mind or its associated chassis, activates finite existing dead bodies after a visible wind-up. The bodies protect their gallery using ordinary combat, stay within its controller reach, and return to guard when intruders leave it. No retaliatory raid, endless respawns, off-map pursuit or global faction hostility is added. A blocked route leaves a visible guarding body; it does not teleport. No surviving body is silently replaced, duplicated or erased during a building/pawn representation change. Stable controller/body identity and visible transformation account for it. A destroyed controller visibly releases its surviving shells. When all shells have already been destroyed, the living crystal is visibly undefended; it cannot invent replacements.

Ordinary rock outside the marked gallery, puffer, lace and standing Lantern harvest do not provoke territorial defence. A friendly visitor is warned by the same rules as an unknown visitor: friendship does not grant ownership of living mineral. The player always has a route to retreat when the system starts defending; the system never seals it. Deliberately walling oneself in or being immobilised by a separate injury does not promise an escape.

Orun-Ghal is the peaceful representative of the same inhabitants: a visible skeleton in a walking mining suit, never a miner, tunnel cutter, worker, recruit or combat boss. Real Research-work study visits reserve, reach and spend time with it. Only a completed visit earns saved colony acquaintance, at most once per day; interruption, death, lost reach or a downed suit grants nothing. Simultaneous researchers cannot duplicate credit. A later familiar return prompts its chosen approach, pause facing the visitor and a brief distinctive blue greeting pulse before it resumes harmless company or its own rounds. This gesture is my proposed reading of the requested greeting, not a new owner quotation. It does not stop urgent player jobs. Colony acquaintance survives the original studier’s death and a reload; suit death leaves its corpse and ends future greetings. No resurrection/replacement is promised. Gallery defence suspends greeting until a peaceful reachable visitor is available, without making Orun fight or erasing acquaintance.

Living colony-machine listening moves to next version, switched off, including interruption of Biotech colony mechs. Crystal-linked dead chassis facing light and defending valuable mineral already show electronic agency; removing the listening job does not defeat the signature. Its code/defs remain, with job assignment, inspect and mote effects gated separately from kept crystal control. Permanent rogue/wipe/Kindled routes remain later campaign work; no standing lore is rewritten.

**At a colony of forty.** There is no multiplied risk merely because more people share the same lit work area: the brightest exposed colonist supplies the map’s current light signal, with saved decay and cooldown. Work parties, pasture competition, reservations, path clutter and evacuation still matter. The cave supplements a large colony; it is not guaranteed to feed it. Wide use can consume ordinary resources and kill inhabitants. The interface must identify that player-caused depletion rather than disguise it as a generator failure or replace it with automatic loot/inhabitant resets.

## What changes in the colony’s story

The valuable objects answer back. Mining can now be an intrusion against visible inhabitants, not an undifferentiated resource job. Returning can mean meeting the same familiar suit or seeing the empty shell of a creature the colony killed. The world’s unfinished terramanufacture supplies the old technological remains and independent crystal cousins; no first-version dialogue reveals the alive Cathedral or claims the Kindled already exist. Free invented fauna, plants and minds remain RimMandrake content; genuine canon names and campaign plots retain their existing owners.

## Costs, failure and readable signs

Travel, carried provisions, light management and support work cost ordinary colony time and risk. A native fuel animal is worth taming for its usable product; it is not ordinary meat. The older gembug/drinker ports retain their own insect biology rather than being silently swept into the wave animals’ no-meat premise. Native animals must drink their own environmental water. Fictional flyers must use real flight; no owner-absent live flight demonstration is specified.

An unsupported roof gives cell-local dust, sand and rumble while an ordinary miner can leave or a timely support job can finish. Vanilla determines support and the eventual fall. The warning does not suspend the roof forever or ignore a completed prop. The moved Knocker alarm must not lengthen this default window, and disabled galuush blasts cannot inject forced roof marks. Mining, light attention, animals and study continue while a warning is pending; their combined path/job competition is an explicit model risk.

Permanent mouth sealing remains the explicit vanilla destructive choice with a contents warning and a meaningful cancel path. No automatic test or gameplay cleanup may destroy an occupied persistent cave. Bodies, animal deaths, fallen roofs and sealed access leave readable physical or textual signs; normal damage/corpse rules apply.

## Edges and neighbours

No planetary worldgen, repaint, alternate worlds, surface farmability, new heat/sun rule, underground gravship transfer, new multi-layer travel network, rites, quests, bespoke letters, psychological Chorus, route-changing titan, Kindled manufacture, kyber trade or Force route belongs to this version. Existing natural cave-map generation is local map content, not an exception allowing planetary generation. A zero-world-tile cave BiomeDef is expected.

Vanilla/Odyssey owns portals and persistence, job reservations, roofs, ordinary animal life and damage. Compared with a vanilla undercave or the formerly borrowed Caverns biome, this version needs a persistent native landscape and an observable mind/body relationship; neither a generic undercave nor a pretty crystal formation provides the signature. No outside mod feature claim is needed for this comparison.

CreatureBehaviors supplies existing fluid feeding, wrong-blood and creature wiring; gate this biome’s deferred link/soothe/breeder consumers without redesigning the engine. ExplosiveGrowth retains its existing roster policy, including the sowable stone’s `top None`; no burst crystal lifecycle is inferred. EnvironmentalHazards remains the compatibility boundary for any existing environmental effects; no direct warm-wax implementation was established here. Wreckage supplies its existing cave field/density policy and SleepingIce children, not a second salvage system. Bones keeps ordinary remains lifecycle, not new resurrection. Shared LightLedger owns composed light adjustments; SettingsKit and HarmonyResilience remain implementation dependencies. Minerals retains its existing abundance/home policy. Scanner annotations and Pyrinth integration move off.

The campaign companion files described by the historical inventory are outside this checkout. Their current implementation is unknown, not newly declared unbuilt. The proposal tickets require locating and gating their cave integrations before default absence can be accepted. Likewise, `RM_ColdWax` is referenced by current fauna XML but no provider def was located in available `src`; resolve its ordinary product/dependency before shipping, without inventing a new warm-reactivity engine. No multi-homed active species was found by the available-source XML reference search outside LanternDeeps; the complete loaded patched roster is unmeasured. Record any later discovery in place and leave rosters alone. Dormant soulchime/grub/megapleura and Ossk definitions stay at their existing admission status.

## Settings

Default on: natural and shaft entrances on supported cold hosts; luminous land and formations; native life/renewal and ordinary products; light attention and standing Lantern exemption; aware crystals, dead-body animation and bounded defence; Orun presence, real study and remembered greeting; roof warnings; shared wreckage policy at its own default. Every major feature gets an individual labelled switch. Rates, thresholds, warning duration and densities may be tuned as data, not bars.

Existing host selection remains, labelled unsupported outside shipped cold hosts; default support is fixed nightside climate at or below minus forty Celsius plus the supported host list. An empty list stays empty on reload rather than silently resetting. “World generation” labels must become “new local maps”; no setting may imply another planet. Master-off stops new entrances and preserves all existing maps/routes. Life/formation placement controls say new-map, renewal says live. Disabling attention clears custom draw activity gracefully; disabling control releases custom defence jobs without deleting shells; disabling friendship suspends custom interaction and preserves acquaintance; disabling warnings says explicitly that vanilla immediate roof behaviour resumes. All-off retains return paths and contents.

Default off and clearly labelled next version: every extra in next_version.md, including colony-machine listening and effects wired in XML, global map components, comp branches and companion patches. Disabling listening releases any owned listen jobs without changing mech ownership or crystal/chassis control; its enabled experimental mode never counts as default bar evidence. A kept component’s call to an extra is coupling to decouple, not an excuse to keep that extra. Native animal bodies and ordinary injury blood remain when their special powers are off. All default bar evidence enables every kept contribution together.

## Four stories

**The first hour.** The survey party leaves the surface cold through a blue geode. Its lamp makes a passage useful for work; a native hunter begins an intelligible approach. A standing Lantern offers another choice. The party finds good equipment and a workable native-crystal deposit more desirable than the ordinary rock outside it, then sees dead metal turn toward its lamp. A generic loot-room explanation no longer fits.

**An ordinary week.** The colony returns to gathered puffer and lace patches and brings provisions for its workers. Its researcher completes actual visits with the suit while the others work outside the marked living gallery. On a later entry Orun comes to greet them. The same injured animal, excavated face and stored supplies anchor the visits to the same place.

**The worst day.** A miner proceeds after the living-gallery warning. Existing shells stir to defend it while a support removal starts dust and a roof rumble. The party withdraws; defenders remain with their crystal, and a completed prop saves the ordinary unsupported span. The already-wounded worker must still be carried by the player. No invisible army, forced galuush blast or automatic cave deletion compounds the crisis.

**The season’s turn.** The colony leaves and reloads the save before returning. Native life has used the remaining pasture, the gallery remembers its surviving shells, and Orun remembers the colony if it survived. The second expedition studies after retreating rather than before mining: the same light, territorial and peaceful rules still apply. Depletion the colony caused stays visible; an unintended flood, empty ecosystem, stalled job/warning or error storm refuses the whole bar.

## Proposal status

A fresh external review refused the protected-resource temptation, ambiguous predator creation, default-on colony-machine listening and unmarked B5 metaphors. This revision addresses all four; no second-look acceptance is claimed. No required game reading, model calibration or switch-off test has been performed here. The proposal’s seven unknown shared-state meetings are named build-first work, not evidence of failure or a blocked design. All earlier concept waivers remain respected. Historical shared compile-source references also extend outside this checkout; the product/dependency ticket verifies the actual build inputs before the builder claims a clean composed load.

Additional historical promises are individually inventoried for later work: formation-parent maturation/offspring, permanent droid departure/wipe, the cousins’ late narrative route, illegal kyber trade and the unidentified imported orange mineral. None is assumed unbuilt outside this checkout or identified by guessed donor provenance. Photonic metabolism remains the lore explanation; this version does not add a separate crystal energy/plant-consumption simulator.
