# Page two — what to check

The decisions that matter:

- “Persistent”: actual saved place, occupants and consequences; never a regenerated lookalike or destructive test cleanup.
- Cold host: fixed nightside climate, intersected with supported hosts; a cold room or cold snap never qualifies. Existing unsupported customization stays labelled.
- Ruined shaft: visibly inside local technological ruins; no world site generator.
- “Safe light”: only the standing Lantern’s actual contribution exempts light-triggered attention; it grants no general immunity.
- Blocked/mixed light: real occlusion and source contributions decide, not nominal radius.
- “Intelligent”: visible controller-linked bodies and actual defensive action; labels, dust and an unrelated raid cannot pass.
- Protected mineral: marked gallery extraction or direct attack provokes finite existing bodies; mere designation warns. Ordinary unprotected resources remain usable.
- Retreat: defenders stay with their gallery and cannot close the route or pursue through the portal.
- “Friend”: completed study visits produce remembered greeting and chosen company; interrupted work grants nothing. Colony acquaintance survives the studier’s death, but a dead suit stays dead.
- Crowded cave: all kept systems run together; no isolated clear-sky feature demonstrations.
- Historical “built”: companion source outside this checkout is unknown, not newly declared absent. Dormant roster definitions are neither restored nor evicted.
