# Page one — what Lantern Deeps becomes

Proposed Design Version 0.1.0. These are my delegated choices under your cleansing instruction, not new words attributed to you. Nothing changes until you have read these pages and accepted the proposal.

Below the frozen surface is a persistent blue cavern of mineral wealth, fungal pasture and excellent equipment left by failed expeditions. Your expedition light attracts the living cave’s attention, and the dead machines reveal that the crystals have minds of their own. You can learn the peaceful inhabitant’s greeting or take from a protected gallery and face its remote bodies, then return to the same place with those consequences still there.

Land: enclosed mineral passages, blue light patches, still air, accessible technological remains and roofs that warn before failing.

Life: forage and cloth fungi, useful stone, ordinary cave hunters and luminous hydrocarbon animals worth keeping for fuel; existing roster admissions stay.

Signature: expedition light draws attention, revealing the minds through machines that notice, greet and defend. Your November answer makes actual remote defence indispensable. # Page two — what to check

The decisions that matter:

- “Persistent”: actual saved place, occupants and consequences; never a regenerated lookalike or destructive test cleanup.
- Cold host: fixed nightside climate, intersected with supported hosts; a cold room or cold snap never qualifies. Existing unsupported customization stays labelled.
- Ruined shaft: visibly inside local technological ruins; no world site generator.
- “Safe light”: only the standing Lantern’s actual contribution exempts light-triggered attention; it grants no general immunity.
- Blocked/mixed light: real occlusion and source contributions decide, not nominal radius.
- Light draws recruit existing reachable hunters only; never new pawns. No eligible hunter means a readable quiet cave.
- “Intelligent”: controller-linked bodies actually defend; labels, dust or unrelated raids cannot pass.
- Protected mineral: worthwhile concentrated native lanternstone; selection/designation warns, extraction or attack provokes finite bodies. Ordinary unprotected resources remain usable.
- Retreat: defenders stay with their gallery and cannot close the route or pursue through the portal.
- “Friend”: completed study visits produce remembered greeting and chosen company; interrupted work grants nothing. Colony acquaintance survives the studier’s death, but a dead suit stays dead.
- Crowded cave: all kept systems run together; no isolated clear-sky feature demonstrations.
- Historical “built”: companion source outside this checkout is unknown, not newly declared absent. Dormant roster definitions are neither restored nor evicted.

Questions: 0. I found no evidence requiring an owner choice against these defaults.

Models: 4, first-to-build first:

- Situation generator: entrances, full cave geometry, crowded routes and real roof/prop transitions.
- Light geometry: Lantern, lamps, luminous ground and mobile sources across walls and mixed light.
- Encounter walk: finite controllers/bodies, real study, defence, retreat and greeting through interruption/reload.
- Whole cave season: native life, depleted hunters, protected mineral versus unguarded alternatives, work and returns together, with reload and reversed action order.

Unsettled: 7 shared-state meetings require those models; no model result is claimed. Protected-mineral temptation is now explicitly unmeasured. But the bar keeps no anchor for 'riches', so the owner's own promise is weakened to beating the worst comparison available. Would pass: B5 still compares against reachable unprotected rock, and it also requires the gallery haul to be a prize. The colony would count it among the most valuable things the expedition brings home, worth the trip and the risk on its own.
