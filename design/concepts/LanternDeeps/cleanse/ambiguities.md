# Decisions that change scope, construction or passing

All choices here are the Designer’s delegated defaults under this cleansing brief. Earlier prose is evidence, not silently converted into owner approval. The important adversarial question is whether a lazy build could preserve the nouns while omitting their consequence.

1. **“A layer beneath the surface.”** A blue surface biome versus a reachable underground map: the former could satisfy the compose sentence without going below. Take persistent vanilla pocket maps reached through mouths. Evidence: frozen sheet hard bans on surface placement/regenerating dungeons. Local cave-map generation does not author the fixed planet.

2. **“Persistent.”** Same seed or same actual saved place: identical cave art can conceal deleted people and excavation. Take actual map/occupant IDs, work and relationship state, on empty and occupied returns and reloads. Evidence: frozen persistence ruling and current harness’s destructive default cleanup. Tool cleanup is not an exception to gameplay persistence.

3. **“Every mouth leads to a Deep.”** All mouths on a source share a cave versus each has its own persistent destination: these need different linking code. Take separate vanilla portal identity unless an authored link says otherwise; both entrance styles lead to the same kind of place, not secretly the same instance. Evidence: current MapPortal defs, no shared-map linkage found. No new network.

4. **“Cold nightside” / “biome temperature.”** Current indoor/surface thermometer or fixed host climate: a cold snap could inject a Deep under a warm region. Take fixed source-tile annual climate at or below minus forty, nightside, intersected with supported loaded host biomes at defaults. Current code checks only strings and contains the stale-looking BiomeGRimond name; the correction is ticketed. No live map climate has been measured here. The later selectable-host setting survives as existing unsupported customization of the biome list only; every selection must pass the same fixed nightside temperature test. The frozen sheet’s section 6 hard ban on injection above minus forty applies to custom hosts too.

5. **“Old mineshafts inside ruined facilities.”** A bare scatter with a corpse or a shaft in readable technological ruin remains: the former loses the promised discovery. Take local ruin dressing enclosing/contextualising the shaft, using existing remains/wreckage; no new world settlement/site generator. Evidence: sheet section 8 and GenStep_ScatterMineshaftPortal’s explicit unimplemented-placement comment. No claim that the existing corpse/rubble already suffices.

6. **“Warm by comparison.”** A new geothermal heat kind or ordinary cave air: the former violates Direct Sun. Take current ordinary pocket temperature; intact roof excludes direct sun geometrically. Evidence: standing one-sun rule, current 17 C def. The source tile used for a grazing estimate must not become the cave’s actual air temperature.

7. **“Wondrous” / “riches for kings.”** Required feeling or bank balance versus readable luminous inhabited land and worthwhile ordinary resources: the former cannot survive retuning or be objectively judged. Take the latter; wonder is a prediction. The marked protected native lanternstone deposit must offer a better practical construction/trade opportunity than reachable ordinary rock, without an equivalent easy unguarded haul nearby. The haul must also be a prize the colony would mount an expedition for, among the most valuable things it brings home and worth the trip and risk on its own; slightly better building stone fails. B5 marks “riches” figurative and holds both requirements; useful salvage elsewhere cannot substitute. RM_LanternstoneItems.xml’s current MarketValue of 1 proves no fortune. Tune native utility/placement, not a required price or added campaign mineral. Evidence: October owner experience, November defence answer and no-number-in-bar ruling.

8. **“Alive and intelligent.”** Sentient labels or intentional observable responses: static dust-emitting props could pass the shallow version. Take controller-linked dead bodies that notice light, defend a protected gallery and coexist with a peaceful remembered inhabitant. Evidence: OWNER:1794375005355305543 explicitly ties recognition to remote defence; current Building_RM_WorkingDead cannot fight. This is an indispensable missing implementation, not permission to downgrade the promise.

9. **“One signature.”** Everything crystalline and every light/heat animal becomes one themed system versus a causal expedition encounter: the former hides independent mechanics in a label. Take light attracts attention -> embodied recognition -> study or protected extraction -> greeting or bounded defence -> remembered return. Frozen light and collapse constraints survive; aurora, moving crust, duplication, battery feeding and radius drain do not become necessary merely because they share files or nouns.

10. **Frozen “v1” and “everything at once.”** Preserve every old build package now versus choose a small current Design Version: the former defeats this expressly authorized cleansing pass. Preserve hard bans, persistence, safe-light trade, collapse signs, admissions, nonviolent Orun and world/canon truth. Defer secondary runtime systems under this brief’s version authority; do not change their lore, retire them or pretend the old instructions never existed. This is a scope proposal, effective only after the owner reads and accepts it.

11. **“Safe Lantern.”** Invulnerable zone versus exemption only from light-triggered attention: the former introduces a general protection engine. Take only its own light-draw exemption; ordinary hunger, existing attacks and roofs still apply. Evidence: Lantern build item explicitly excludes its glow from exposure. This qualification is in B4’s figurative shelter note and definition.

12. **“Light from a Lantern alone.”** Any Lantern within nominal radius versus actual source contributions: the former misclassifies a lamp behind rock and ignores luminous floor/items. Take engine-compatible occlusion and source provenance, including terrain and mobile light. Evidence: LitOnlyByLanterns currently checks radius and omits terrain. Equal-brightness Lantern-only/mixed/blocked controls are required; radius fuzz alone cannot decide it.

13. **“Light now, sold later.”** Debug-spawnable/sowable only versus an obtainable first-visit choice: the former can leave inexperienced visitors without the defining comparison. Take a naturally discoverable standing Lantern and later ordinary sowing; harvesting removes safe shelter and gives ordinary luminous material. Evidence: current RM_Lantern def is sowable but no natural placement was found. It is not RM_Lanternstone_Sowable, which is ordinary light.

14. **“Beacons” / “drawn out of the dark.”** Arbitrary newly spawned manhunters versus readable native attention: unexplained enemies in sealed bedrooms would satisfy a weak attack count. Take brightest occupied exposure, decay/cooldown and legal approaching residents, with cause/sign readings. Evidence: source currently generates moths near the target, not path-following native response; frozen words describe attraction. No predators remaining means visibly depleted danger, not conjuration. Whole-map replenishment remains a model question.

15. **“Nearby” control.** Walls block all crystal/electronic control versus crystal-network control crossing rock within finite reach: these yield different defender sets. Take a finite horizontal reach with stable nearest-mind ownership, declared as an attackable rule; visual facing still requires actual unblocked light. Evidence: current mind radius is distance-only; immobile crystal networks wearing electronic bodies are frozen lore. It does not justify infinite range or off-map transfer.

16. **“They defend themselves.”** Any mined stone provokes all crystals, versus an inspectable protected living gallery: the former makes ordinary cave economy inexplicably hostile. Take marked gallery mineral plus mind/chassis damage dealt by a colony pawn or through a player order; selection/designation warns, actual damage/extraction provokes against the instigating intruder. A roof fall caused by completed protected extraction counts as that extraction against its miner; environmental, wild-animal (including drawn moth) and unprotected-work roof damage never provoke. Otherwise ordinary mining or animal attacks could start unwarned retaliation against an uninvolved colony. Cancelled designation does not. Ordinary stone and Lantern harvest outside that territory stay usable. Evidence: owner requires defensive agency but gives no universal land ownership; this boundary is my minimal inference.

17. **“Droid bodies remote controlled.”** Spawn a raid, seize the colony’s machines, or mobilise finite supplied dead shells: these differ radically in loss and scale. Take existing local shells with stable IDs and a readable controller/activation; overlapping minds cannot duplicate them. No human corpse resurrection, automatic body replacement or colony pawn conversion. Evidence: well-provisioned-dead and Shard-mind source; no readable-vanishing ruling.

18. **“Avenue to retreat.”** Cosmetic warning versus an actionable route: pairwise valid defenders and roof warning may block the same passage together. Take a naturally reachable exit/retreat that the defence does not close, bounded pursuit and visible guarding when blocked. Ordinary deliberate player sealing or unrelated immobility is excluded explicitly. Geometry generator and full-map season decide congestion; no guessed throughput guarantee.

19. **“Working Dead work.”** Actually mine resources versus visibly worry at rock: the former would give automatic excavation and undermine the peaceful inhabitant. Take non-extractive activity plus light response; real fighting is a separate state of those shells, not Orun. Evidence: RM_WorkingDead comments, owner’s explicit not-a-miner revision. No free mining output bar.

## Second lazy-build attack

A protected gallery with no better extraction opportunity than the unguarded rock fails B5 even if a tester forcibly mines it. A new predator spawned off-screen fails B4 even if it walks a legal approach; an exhausted cave must visibly stay quiet. Default listening jobs on colony mechs fail the T39 switch-off check, not a new bar. A beautiful cave with static chassis, a friend counter, harmless default lamps, a single held roof and a pristine staged room would satisfy the nouns in the compose line and much old proof prose. It fails B4’s ordinary-light comparison, B5’s actual controller-linked defence, B6’s real visits and greeting, B7’s expiry/prop contrast and B8’s complete season. Conversely, no bar demands variant counts, a fixed economy number, permanent mind transfer or all deferred hazards. These changes are intentional tightening, not disguised new flavour requirements.

## Fresh-review repairs

All four refusals are accepted as gaps in my proposal, not new owner rulings.

**Where drawn danger comes from.** Resident recruitment only versus bounded or unbounded pawn creation changes both ecology and whether danger survives extermination. I choose existing eligible reachable residents only (R3/B4/T25): no direct or indirect attention spawning, and a readable quiet state on absence or blocked reach. The current DrawSomethingToTheLight calls PawnGenerator.GeneratePawn with forceGenerateNewPawn true; comments calling it a resident do not establish residency. Ordinary independent replenishment is allowed only before a pawn can be recruited, never as a draw fallback. Ecology and action-order models include depleted cases; intentional eradication is an explicit whole-bar exclusion, unintentional depletion is not.

**Living gallery / dead machines.** A room full of fungi versus marked mineral protected by an aware crystal; arbitrary wrecks versus supplied powerless mindless chassis. I take the latter readings and mark both phrases figurative in B5, including a bare gallery without fungi and a moving controlled chassis as acceptable departures from literal readings. No colony-machine possession is implied.

**What still needs evidence.** Protected native lanternstone must be a better practical opportunity than reachable unprotected rock under actual loaded uses, work and hauling, not just a higher declared price. The resources meeting is now unknown and assigned to Whole cave season. T24 specifies placement/utility repair, T22 tests extraction/defence, and B5 requires the comparison without campaign minerals. This is proposed design, not a claim that the current material already meets it.
