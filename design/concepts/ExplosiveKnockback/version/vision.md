# Explosive Knockback 0.1.0: the vision

Written only from the root paragraph he approved (`record.json` root, OWNER:1794468835221748501, his card "Approve as written"), `sheet.md` and his quoted words there, including his cards of 2026-11-13 on mass and blast power. No sentence is inferred. Convergent and Divergent are measured against this text.

1. Every harmful explosion in RimWorld now throws what it hits, other mods' explosions included and fire at half strength; melee and bullets move nobody.
2. A mortar shell beside a colonist hurls them about three cells straight away from it, clean over sandbags, until a wall, a closed door or another pawn stops them hard; they land alive, briefly stunned, and go back to work.
3. Heavier flies less and a bigger blast throws farther: one blast throws a rat farther than a colonist and a colonist farther than a muffalo, and no living creature over the mass limit flies at all.
4. Anything light goes too: loose goods and corpses fly wild through the air, spinning and rebounding off walls, and no open shelf keeps them safe, while closed containers keep their goods and may themselves be shoved a little.
5. When a flammable gas cloud catches, it goes off clump by clump, each burst scattering what lies near it and shoving people one cell unhurt, the Cauldron's gas included.
6. Where a blast meets a pit, its cover breaks and whoever it throws in falls, and goods thrown into a pit can be fetched back out.
7. A shield belt that absorbs the blast keeps its wearer in place, at extra charge.
8. It is judged first on his flagship: a bomb in a storeroom with people in it beside a pit, some goods and some people falling into the pit.
9. Every new part switches off cleanly in Mod Settings; there is no worldgen and no multiplayer support.
