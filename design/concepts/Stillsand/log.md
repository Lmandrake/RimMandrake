### Root synthesis

Stillsand is an ocean of apparently empty sand beneath a sun that never sets, crossed by giants. Fortunes scattered among distant rock islands and uncertain caves draw repeated expeditions. Exploring and securing an island makes a dependable harbour, and a useful cave discovery supplies or funds the next expedition or outpost. Learning to live here uses sunlight and desert resources to make glass and lenses, cook food and recover water.
