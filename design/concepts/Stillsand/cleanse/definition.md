# Stillsand — proposed Design Version 0.1.0

Stillsand is an exposed ochre plain of deep sand, sparse living things and travelling giants under a fixed sun. Distant rock islands offer caves to explore and secure, rather than automatic safety or guaranteed treasure. Useful discoveries and ordinary sun-fed glass, cooking and water work supply or fund the next expedition or outpost.

These are the Designer's definitions and scope choices under this cleanse brief. They are proposals, not an applied root change, owner certification, current proof or enjoyment verdict. No game, bridge, art or source work is part of this pass. Earlier Conductor instructions are authoritative historical decisions: this proposal deliberately replaces their blanket retention of built capabilities using the later cleanse mandate. It preserves their practical expedition emphasis and the distinction between current water and later shared film. It does not revise a standing ruling, reopen a recorded waiver (none is recorded), or claim the earlier Cold Reader exam passes the new contract.

**Land:** broad walkable sand and deep drift, corrugation, sparse rock, occasional physical bones and wreckage, dry weather and shared geometric sunlight/shade. Roads already allowed remain allowed; no new road or river is authored.

**Life:** existing patched native residents and ordinary plants, their food, reproduction, predation, burrowing, flight, shelter seeking and ordinary products. Giants carry moving shade; small things use shelter or depth. Roster membership is retained, including campaign animals. A species being kept does not keep a special quest or incident about it.

**The signature:** exposed crossings lead to uncertain caves which, once explored and physically secured, supply refuge and practical discoveries for ordinary sun-fed resupply and the next undertaking.

**Runner-up:** the dune-gale carry/return/aftermath cycle loses because it creates another hazard, recovery and renewal economy alongside the expedition loop. Its ordinary wind and sand dependencies remain; the custom cycle defaults off for next version.

## Why a player reaches for it

Choose Stillsand to make distance, carried supplies and a known refuge matter. The prediction is that returning with something useful makes the next crossing feel earned; enjoyment remains a prediction. This version is whole without rituals, scripted leviathans, new quests or specialist sensing weapons. It still has ordinary predators and an uncertain cave. Vanilla extreme desert supplies scarcity and normal work; Stillsand adds the rock/cave/discovery loop and depth-and-shade life.

“Ocean”, “island”, “harbour” and “fortune” are metaphors. Ocean is extensive exposed sand between destinations, not literal water or empty terrain with nothing living. An island is a rock outcrop, not a generated sea tile. A harbour is a place the player has inspected, sheltered and defended; it is not an immunity area. Fortune is a usable change in provisioning or trade, not a wealth threshold. The counts, speeds and weights currently in defs are regression readings, not the definition of these words.

## Using it

**First hour.** Arrive carrying ordinary starting provisions. Locate actual cover, read exposure, inspect the nearest approach and the rock mouth, and decide whether exploration is affordable. An unfamiliar cave may contain a predator or hatchable clutch; a mouth is not a safety certificate. A roof or parasol blocks Direct Sun even at low solar elevation, but the thermometer and vanilla heatstroke still matter. The arrival guidance says this and never claims rock cools the air. No Debt obligation, swarm, Muurrok visit or gale starts at default settings.

**Routine.** Cross sand while reading wakes and listening to nearby movement. Swimmers surface on hard ground unless active drift makes it sand again. Oommok shade moves with its body and is temporary cover, not a taxi or a safe convoy command. Explore the chamber, remove actual threats, retain a protected route and make an ordinary stockpile/sleeping space. Harvest, fish, hunt, mine, salvage, haul, craft or trade what is useful; accept that a taken cave may add no supplies. Revisit to rest or collect the remaining finite contents, not to reroll its reward.

**At a 40-colonist scale.** Use ordinary hauling, work priorities, storage filters and bills. Workers share finite inputs and work sites; no new autonomous sieve chore is required. A prepared colony can queue furnace/lens/cooking work and plain-still feed hauling, recover after an interruption and furnish another trip without repeatedly resetting jobs. This does not promise a sparse desert feeds such a colony indefinitely: imported provisions, trade and deliberate agriculture remain normal colony decisions. Wildlife density is not increased to feed the settlement. A busy stockpile cannot silently eat a still's output when placement is blocked.

**Day one and day one hundred.** Resource scarcity and ordinary hunger persist. Some plants grow slowly; a century of ticks cannot reroll a cave or create supplies on reload. A saved empty cave stays empty. A player who roofs or walls a work site may protect workers while shading the apparatus; its inspection text must identify that separate receiver constraint.

## What changes in the colony's story

The colony acquires a known route, a secured refuge and a material reason to return. A discovered brine source can support water recovery; a grotto supports glass work or trade; eggs can be eaten or carried; a sealed cache supplies ordinary components, steel or food. A cave already taken redirects effort instead of pretending every expedition succeeds. Those are the retained ordinary cave rows, not a promise about their numeric weights or an entitlement to useful loot on every map.

Basic sun work stays only as the adopted root's provisioning route: furnace, lens bench, plain solar oven, plain solar still, ordinary glass/lens recipes and dry-bank brine extraction. Delete their player-facing work and the described conversion from desert resources into food, lenses and recovered water no longer exists. Delete the turret, horn, geophone, upgraded oven, corpse still or lens multiplier and that route still exists; those move. Fine sand is a traded input, not a renamed sieve product. A fortune may fund that trade; this is not a closed local economy. Lens production is an opportunity, not compulsory on every start.

## The costs

Crossings cost time, ordinary food and exposure; attacks cost health and can kill. Discoveries cost exploration, defence, carrying capacity, extraction and work. Sun tables need construction inputs, appropriate work skills, a usable receiver and bills. The plain still trades existing brine, eggs or meat for water, never creates both the original feed and its water. Optional DBH owns thirst/ingestion when present; without it, RM still-water is carried/traded physical stock (its def has no ingestible block), and existing edible eggs/sacs keep their actual ingestion. No new drinking action is invented. This proposal adds no thirst need and does not imply vanilla pawns die because an optional thirst mod is absent. No live crop-water pool is inferred from a wetness expiry timestamp.

## When it goes wrong and the readable sign

Exposed pawns show ordinary heat/exposure status; adapted natives can use their own environmental water, including brine, under the standing ruling. A visible wake, breach or rumble identifies concealed movement without identifying the exact species or promising safety when silent. Ordinary attacks and kills leave readable body/remains/messages; damage cannot be credited to a disappearing actor. A flyer flies under the standing flight system. Offmap disappearance is not a default Stillsand danger now that gale carry and event takes are off.

A shaded or storm-blocked sun appliance reports why it stopped; ordinary work can resume after sun returns. A dead worker releases reservations; another worker can continue a valid bill. A blocked still output waits or preserves an accountable pending product, rather than consuming feed into nothing. A roofed cave preserves eligible contents only in its recorded cells after actual occupancy no longer holds the den. Taking an object out, carrying it, losing its roof or disabling preservation releases only the state this feature owns. Items already disabled by another owner are not acquired. The status/tooltip must distinguish cave preservation from refrigeration and from ordinary ambient heat; T_CAVE supplies that reader.

## The edges

No weather-cleared acceptance scenes count for the whole bar. Ordinary Clear, DryThunderstorm and Sandstorm are land; the custom dune gale is an extra. There is no magical safe-island flag, new enemy AI, research tree, planet generation, tile repainting, faction settlement work, alternative planet or multiplayer promise. Existing local-map furnishing hooks may lay out rock/cave/ordinary debris when a local map is supplied; that does not authorize planetary generation or a seed search. Models enumerate synthetic *situations*, not worlds. Zero world tiles for this biome are expected and irrelevant to acceptance.

No cave is promised on a local layout too thin or blocked to fit one safely. The existing largest suitable outcrop and optional rockless tor policy remains an attackable implementation choice. Such a case must be reported as no cave, not a painted doorway into an unusable room. At regional scale the authored desert must still have destinations separated by exposed travel; isolated no-cave layouts do not excuse a desert with no useful destinations anywhere. The layout model identifies this boundary rather than rolling new planets until it passes.

No general outdoor no-rot rule is retained. Ordinary dead giants remain readable under normal corpse handling; optional seasonal conversion and special desiccation move together. Existing HugeThings-classified titan sites remain solely HugeThings-owned with normal temperature-driven meat spoilage and its existing competition; this is a dependency boundary, not permission to rewrite it. Basic static bones remain landmarks and geometric cover. Harps, extra fossil scatter and new conversion effects are not needed to read them.

## The neighbours

CreatureBehaviors supplies the one geometry-only Direct Sun/heat path, shade including moving giants and plants, sand submersion, shadow followers, tracks and feeding. Stillsand supplies data and consumes those engines, not a biome heat kind. The existing legacy heat regime and glare floor violate the later ruling and must become unreachable through T_SUN; their presence is source evidence, not a new design question. Bare rock, roof, rib gaps, plant crowns, worker gear and appliance receivers must all query that one geometry policy. No art variant fixes a geometry discrepancy.

MovingDunes supplies baseline wind, depth, transport, movement events, clearing yield and the active-field query; no second sand store or custom gale multiplier is kept. FlowWorks supplies brine/deep-sand terrain and, solely for completion of old records, TakenByLand. ExplosiveGrowth supplies existing ordinary plant/wet-source compatibility; shared agricultural thin-film work stays later. EnvironmentalHazards supplies set-piece elements, existing shade gear and arrival guidance. Wreckage supplies normal salvage/weathering; Bones supplies ordinary butcher bones. ProximityHatch supplies existing hatch-on-approach behaviour for fertile trap clutches in the supplying composition; a missing service is a composition/spec discrepancy, not permission to silently turn dangerous eggs into inert free loot. HugeThings remains the owner of titan geometry and corpse sites.

LongShade source still supplies Ulgga, Vosska and Ultriss Pad; the current Stillsand roster determines their home. Canon animals and bloddle remain patch-added by the campaign. Vaalok remains the native pack carrier; Karrask is existing livestock, not an invented new wild row. Vozzik remains defined here for its other home; no eviction is proposed. Multi-home notes from actual HEAD patch source: Kreetle also appears in LeaningScrub; GraniteSlug in Cauldron; Scurrier in LeaningScrub; Gizka in Greentide, LeaningScrub and Pyrelands. These are source memberships, not a current live-resolution measurement. No eviction or rehoming is proposed. Other resolved multi-homes are noted by C_ROSTER without changing membership. Tier identity follows the standing naming scheme, not whether an invented name sounds exotic. No franchise name is added to an RM def.

Sparse checkout note: FlowWorks, MovingDunes, LongShade and tier source directories are not materialized here; read-only `git show HEAD:<path>` and `git grep ... HEAD` recover their source objects. Failure of a working-tree path is not evidence of an unbuilt feature. Census records historical proof as stale/missing/unknown, never current. Existing proof wrappers largely call validation.py; its `_prep` locks Clear and strips roof, `_setting` restores defaults, and internal forced helpers bypass ordinary work. T_OBSERVER replaces those assumptions for acceptance while retaining useful regression checks.

## The settings

Shipped defaults enable land, ordinary resident behaviour, geometric sun/shade, ordinary sand signs, basic cave/discovery/preservation and the plain provisioning chain. Settings group those major features with per-feature on/off and appropriate tuning, search, scrolling, collapse and reset. Each control reports whether it applies now, next activation or next local-map furnishing; never “worldgen”. Reset uses the proposed defaults, not the older source defaults. All-off leaves loaded defs and a readable ordinary desert; turning a feature off does not delete pawns or corrupt existing saves.

Every deferred extra has a separately labelled “Next version” default-off gate. The campaign tier cannot reactivate Debt or a cave/event row merely by loading its def. Recommended retirements are parked off until the owner decides; obsolete heat semantics are prohibited at every setting. Existing outside-biome controls stay off and labelled unsupported; no new cross-biome switch is built. Exact prior user settings, not shipped defaults, are restored after any test. Existing active extra entities on an old save finish or are safely released with a sign; there is no promise of enabling an unfinished extra as a supported first-version experience.

## First hour story

The party sees a low fixed sun and bare sand between itself and a rock mouth. A wake crosses the approach. They put up a quick roof, check remaining ambient heat, and send an equipped scout to inspect the cave instead of moving every bed into an unseen den. The roof works despite the low angle; finding rock has not yet won safety.

## Ordinary week story

After clearing the real occupant, they stock the roofed chamber and haul out a useful clutch and biosilica. Ordinary bills turn resources and traded fine sand into working equipment; brine feeds the plain still. A second sortie passes the same already-emptied chamber without a fresh reward. The colony keeps its prepared refuge and plans the next destination from actual supplies.

## Worst day story

A sandstorm blocks the appliances while a moving drift reaches an approach that had been hard rock. Their inspect text explains the stopped production; a wake shows that hidden movement is possible again. A worker dies in an ordinary attack and leaves readable remains. Other workers release the failed reservation, secure the approach and resume useful work when the sun returns. No ritual or scripted rescue is necessary to recover.

## Season's turn story

The sun still occupies its original place. Living things have fed, sheltered, reproduced or left; the settled cave retains its history and scoped preserved contents. Supplies have actually been spent and transformed. Reloading a save from the middle and doing workshop preparation before the next exploration leaves those consequences intact. Emptiness still separates destinations; neither a carpet of new life nor an endless cache fountain has replaced the desert.

## Second attack: the laziest build

A screenshot of a beautiful empty desert could satisfy the opening sentence while hiding a dead food web, unroofed chamber and inaccessible recipes. The bars therefore distinguish a full-map look, actual life transitions, connected chamber/approach, ordinary input-to-use work and scoped preservation, then require the season with everything enabled. A reward added by reflection cannot satisfy provisioning. A static shade blob cannot satisfy moving cover. A no-op ritual toggle cannot prove scope removal. The models settle unresolved geometry, ownership, provisioning and whole-map feedback before expensive live tuning; none is claimed built or calibrated here.

Definition boundary for the whole run: all viable supplied cave layouts, including viable tor layouts, receive the full-season/reload/reordered undertaking. A physically unsuitable no-cave local layout is a negative geometry control, not a passing cave-use scene. The region cannot be excused if the model finds no viable useful destinations at all. This restriction follows the existing local furnishing policy and does not authorize a new world generator or seed selection.
