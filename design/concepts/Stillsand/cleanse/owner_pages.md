# Stillsand — page one: what I propose

Stillsand is an exposed ochre plain of deep sand, sparse living things and travelling giants under a fixed sun. Distant rock islands offer caves to explore and secure, rather than automatic safety or guaranteed treasure. Useful discoveries and ordinary sun-fed glass, cooking and water work supply or fund the next expedition or outpost.

Land: open corrugated sand, actual geometric shade, dry weather, sparse rock, bones and wreckage.

Life: the existing roster, including buried predators, shade tenants, real flyers and usable ordinary plants and animal products. No animal is evicted.

Signature: cross exposed sand, explore and physically secure a cave, then use its discoveries and ordinary sun-fed work to provision the next undertaking. The dune gale loses the signature choice because its carry, return and renewal economy is another complete cycle.

I retain basic glass/lens work, plain solar cooking and water recovery as practical provisioning. I remove their dependency on optional upgrades, special chores and religious accounting. These are my scope decisions under this cleanse mandate, including replacement of the earlier delegated blanket retention of built water features. Standing rulings remain binding.

# Page two: what to check

The ambiguity decisions that changed the build:

- “Empty ocean”: exposed travel between destinations, with functioning sparse life.
- “Harbour”: explored, currently secure physical shelter; never a quest flag or automatic immunity.
- “Fortune”: an actual provisioning/equipment/trade use; never guaranteed wealth or renewable loot.
- “Cool cave”: blocks Direct Sun; ordinary hot air remains. Remove the obsolete drip/cooling claims.
- Low sun: roofs and parasols still cover. Replace legacy heat branches and false arrival advice.
- Moving shade: follows the actual giant; its former position cannot remain protected.
- “Preserved cave”: recorded roofed cells and owned state only; carried/outside contents return to ordinary decay.
- “Current water”: quantity and ordinary provider use stay; poured response and religion move. Legacy wetness expiry is not measured film water.
- Useful lenses: basic precision chain; no sieve, rare upgrade or event crest required. Supplier access must be measured.
- “Off”: stops producers, jobs, offers, queues and tier activation; hiding UI is insufficient.
- Saved extras: finish/recover old entities visibly; never delete a pawn to meet default-off absence.
- “Whole”: full map, all kept mechanics on, ordinary weather unlocked. A cleared showcase pad proves nothing about a season.

The cooling hediff exists, but source search found no ordinary drink hookup despite its header claiming one. I record its missing positive test rather than building an unwanted potion. Without a thirst provider, plain still-water is carried/traded stock; existing edible eggs and sacs retain actual ingestion.

Models specified, first to build first:

- Situation generator: enumerate cave, creature, item, work and save transitions; find the shortest ownership or activation failure.
- Whole-map season: expose coupled sand, growth, animals, work and supply floods, starvation or stalls.
- Geometry sweep: test cover, moving shadows, chamber approaches and worker/appliance receivers across legal placements and bearings.
- Provisioning model: find inaccessible suppliers, recipe/bootstrap loops, contention and lost output without extras rescuing them.

There are 4 models, 8 unknown interaction groups and 50 tickets, including every built-extra switch-off and missing instrument. Models need agreement with real game reads before anyone believes them.
