# The Abyss — proposed Design Version 0.1.0

Designer proposal, 2026-11-17, with the owner marks of that date folded into the release scope. No game, bridge, art, source, roster or standing ruling is changed. Nothing here is an owner quotation unless explicitly sourced. This proposal replaces neither `## his words` nor a frozen sheet; it proposes a smaller release schedule under the cleanse delegation. Enjoyment is a prediction: maintaining small islands of working light should make shelter feel earned and a clearing worth watching. It is not a passing bar.

## Say the mod

You settle among obsidian crags where material Dark dims the country and nearby predators, and shortens artificial lamplight. You cultivate living lamps, warm a shelter and roof exposed work while the same Dark settles into corrosive grain that can feed your heaters. When the air clears you can read the country around you; when it closes, the light you tended is your way home.

Land: black etched highlands, dark buildable sand, still air interrupted by gusts, chemical grain and brief Unveilings, with the existing near-black swirling world face.

Life: the current owned native roster and campaign additions, feeding and breeding without sky dependence, with edible luminous berries/fungus, light-turning vines, grazing cover, timber, fiber, pack animals, milk, ordinary predation, actual moth flight and a fishery of living dark pool creatures.

Signature: **The Dark makes the colony depend on living light and warmth, threatens that light through its native feeders, and settles into corrosive grain that can be recovered as heater fuel.**

Runner-up: the hidden gravship tested by searching probes. It loses because a cover clock, trade isolation and scout/report encounters are an independent stealth system, while ordinary optical shelter and the existing campaign survey shadow already express protection. The brood-and-rescue-wreck expedition is another independent reward/risk chain. Both move intact to the next version, off. No species is removed.

## Why a player reaches for it

This is refuge with restricted information and a build/maintenance cost, on Ash'karr's fixed world. Dark is a material condition, not ordinary night or a universal invisibility buff. You can keep a workshop usable by warmth, a farm productive by cultivated light and exposed steel intact by roofing. A defended light can fail locally when krizzaks feed, allowing an ishvarith closer. Etchfall turns a long stay into roof and repair choices, with useful residue rather than a punishment clock. This carries the protection and nearby-terror clause of the owner's 2026-10-20 experience through ambient obstruction, light-feeder pressure and ordinary predation. Unveiling reveals etched relief as a partial expression of the beauty clause. Its close carries the swift turn back to danger through returning Dark, shrinking artificial lamps, krizzak feeding and an ishvarith approaching the weakened colony edge; B8 requires that readable sequence. Only the dread of staying long enough to discover the nightmares' source is deferred with cryptid sites, phantom events, lore and dreams; erosion is a material cost, not fulfillment of that dread. This is an explicit first-version reduction of that experience, not a claim to carry all of it.

## Using it

First hour: land or enter the existing authored biome. Ground and clear/Dark edges must be visible without selecting a hidden debug field. Inspect tells you that local temperature, grain exposure and light matter. Bring food and fuel, find berries and a native lamp, put ordinary shelter/heating in service. With heat observation switched off, a silent load-time grant opens ordinary heat-folding research at the bench. Actual materials and ordinary work are still required, and crops must grow before harvest. A new colony is not assumed to own a refinery. Ordinary campfire/heater mechanics remain the first warmth source.

Routine: roof exposed work, harvest berries and hollow fungus, transplant wickwood using vanilla actions, manage crop temperature and light, herd or hunt native stock and use pack animals. Wind is not continuous generation capacity: stillness and pulses are shared by visible wind, feeding and sound. Grain is a normal haulable stack with a vanilla refinery bill, not a new cleaning designation/job. Its existence does not mean unlimited free fuel. A working farm needs warmth as well as light; an Unveiling is not a new star, a temperature change or a free crop season.

At forty colonists: the same rules operate across dispersed farms and shelters. No map-wide lamp count determines first-version safety. No per-lamp custom tending job or discovery letter is required. Krizzak pressure remains local; an intact lit refuge and a reachable withdrawal route remain a feasible response with ordinary colony tools. Forty pawns gathering at a lamp do not multiply sight penalties, grain production, ambient temperature or audio events. Population/food/light/repair pressure must remain recoverable with normal work; the whole-map model decides tuning rather than an invented numeric balance verdict here.

## What changes in the colony's story

The crew chooses imperfect cover over open visibility. The threatening boundary is light and usable air, not a proven cryptid faction or an expedition quest. The Unveiling exposes already unfogged country, gives an opportunity to scout/haul under ordinary sight and restores the ambient air's optical clarity; it does not reveal unexplored fog or guarantee a predator attack. Returning Dark closes that opportunity. Wind-torn ordinary wreckage provides familiar salvage and evidence of the building cost. The existing campaign pursuit slowdown remains real, but is not immunity, an active concealment meter, or proof that dynamic cover suppresses trade.

## The costs

Roofing, fuel, growing time, animal food, crop warmth, the loss of artificial lamp reach, restricted long sight and exposed steel/rock deterioration. Deepfire keeps its ruled exemption from ambient Dark dimming (`RM_AbyssMod.cs`, owner card comment of 2026-10-08); this is not immunity to physical walls or a claim that an animal cannot feed on its light. Living lamps resist ambient Dark, not feeding krizzaks. Killing native stock gives ordinary meat/hide/bone via the shared engines. Great SummBone and rescue-ship fittings are off. Giant fibre stalk is ordinary old plant matter, not a source of newly invented premium fiber; resolve inherited harvest against its explicit unworkable-fiber description.

## When it goes wrong and its readable sign

A lamp contracts while a visible light-feeder settles beside it; inspect names the feeder contribution and sound/text supplies an alternative cue. There is no feeding through a wall or a sealed room. Removing the feeder or excluding it restores its contribution without erasing ambient Dark or another mod's light share. Heat stops feeding only through actual ordinary temperature; the setting is not a new heat type.

An ishvarith already in usable light breaks toward reachable darkness. It is deterrence, not a magic impassable lit border; a trapped or committed attacking animal may still be dangerous. Its state is inspectable, not advertised as a guaranteed safe camp. Ombrathia's quill rattle/text precedes an actionable close attack, rather than being exhausted on a distant hunt job; it is not a projectile ability. Saevitha's grip/drain is its primary attack and leaves a health/log cause. Ordinary killing/downing leaves corpses and combat evidence. No pawn/animal is deleted as scenery, a timer expiration, a weather transition or a disabled feature.

Grain falls on exposed surfaces, with inspectable deterioration and tholin residue. Roofed controls are spared. Natural rock eaten away leaves the hollow; etchcap admits that actual substrate, not any fertile soil. This does not erode all stone-stuff furniture, damage pawns with acid rain, raise water wetness or trigger an ExplosiveGrowth soak.

Nevarithia remains ordinary native life, with its current appearance, grazing and melee. Its unbuilt carried knot of Dark and death release move to next version, off. Removing those behaviors does not break land, ordinary life or the ambient Dark/light/feeder/grain signature. The frozen halo description still constrains that future mechanic; it does not authorize a new first-version system. Shipped descriptions must distinguish the deferred promise from current behavior.

## Physical boundaries and transitions

Outdoor ambient Dark density follows the map's shared ordinary outdoor temperature; each room's density follows its own ordinary room temperature. The transition band remains tunable checks, not a new heat rule. Outdoor air therefore thins or closes together as that shared temperature changes; Unveiling clears it map-wide. There are no spontaneous drifting open-air pockets in this first version. Those built Perlin offsets move to a labelled next-version switch, off, alongside the deferred phantom pockets. The owner-retained fold lamp opens its directional clearance lane through the existing wall-bounded columns and distance fade while ordinary temperature remains unchanged. A heated room can show clear indoor air against the Dark through its open doorway while maintained heating keeps the room warm. Opening or removing its enclosure can cool the room and close its air through ordinary temperature updates; it creates no warm outdoor lane. This default conflicts with frozen abyss.md §5's real, sharp-edged, unexplained clear pockets: the shared outdoor temperature cannot provide the adjacent thermometer difference described in §3. Owner-page Q1 carries that conflict and the alternative; no lore amendment is claimed.

The Dark reduces fine detail and contrast of explored terrain and relief in proportion to local density, while retaining the relief silhouettes and usable walking/building ground. Buildings are dimmed with readable outlines and footprints. Player-owned colonists, tame animals and mechs remain fully drawn. Wild animals, hostile pawns and neutral visitors are dimmed with density but retain a discernible silhouette; they are never culled, removed from view or replaced by an invisible threat. Selection outlines, inspect access, controls and warning text remain readable for all these classes. The predator's dim silhouette and its existing close-attack text/sound tell keep danger perceptible; no new warning system is introduced. A sky tint alone, an unchanged predator sprite and a completely hidden predator each fail this definition. Ordinary unexplored fog still hides what it normally hides: the Dark neither reveals that fog nor adds sprite hiding inside explored country. Contrast/opacity tuning may vary only while preserving these class distinctions and readable silhouettes.

Unveiling clears all first-version ambient particles everywhere, then ambient Dark returns according to room/outdoor temperatures. No carried knot or release is active. No detection ray gains clean targeting merely because its shooter stands inside warm air: intervening Dark matters for simulated sight/aim even though the player can still discern dim creature silhouettes. Ordinary walls remain occluders. A common density query must feed presentation, targeting, glowers and muffling; the geometry model must expose any contradictory exception.

Unveiling's apparent brightness is presentation, not solar elevation or UV. Direct Sun is the standing world-geometry rule; a weather lightening must not move the sun. The existing Summ UV/regen mechanics are deferred, with their rules intact. No fantasy-dragon tells are introduced. Native finite age stages stay but do not satisfy the deferred continuous lifelong growth promise.

Leaving the map clears visitor murk, including caravans and carried/world pawns. Native dead/downed/despawned feeders cease to affect lamps; a mid-feeding reload may restore the krizzak share as the existing documented safe-reset rule, then only a still-present feeder can dim again. Ambient Dark must reapply independently. Old maps/settings load without silently opting players into deferred features. The one whole bar repeats main actions in another order and includes a save made mid-cycle.

## Edges: deliberate exclusions

No worldgen in any version, no new planet, paint pass, tile assignment, new planetary layer or bridge work. Local-map placement of content on an existing authored tile is distinct from planet generation. Zero painted Abyss tiles is expected and irrelevant to capability proof. No geothermal, steady laminar wind, water rain, permanent Clear, clean long-range sensing or sun-dependent resident ecology. No confirmed Nhaleth pawn/faction/structure. No spontaneous outdoor pockets, carried halo or death release. No new rites, cryptid signs/exchanges/dreams, sorter ring/caches, fold-lamp discovery, scheduled Summ call, active cover/probe loop or brood/egg/wreck/bond mechanics at shipped defaults. New corpse-processing, spine spray and the spore hazard remain deferred. Vine turning and mobile berry light are retained by the owner marks. Original natural stock and neighbour-owned Taozin remain rostered.

The older world documents say the nightside never becomes farmable; the later explicit lamp-crop ruling permits maintained localized warm/light farms. It does not authorize naturally sun-fed fields or a nightside greenbelt. The older donor roster/cut history is not a present casting instruction: the later owned sheet controls, and evictions are stopped. `RM_Lirrith` is also referenced in `UtinniPatches/Patches/WildAnimals_CrackedLands.xml`; note multi-homing, do not evict it. Aspirational `Alien_Bestiary.md` names do not create a roster. The owner already cut the gust-turbine echo and several old species; they are not proposed again.

## Settings

Shipped on: ambient Dark and its strength, Unveiling, grain erosion/recovery, lamp crops, krizzak feeding, light aversion, gharrek pulse feeding, readable native attack warnings, land soundscape, Dark muffling including placed map sounds, and the ruled deepfire exemption. Shared engines retain their shipped defaults and their own settings. Missing physical presentation requires a major-feature control; outdoor noise pockets and future halo/release controls are labelled next version and default off. Feature off must clean its effect or let it decay with a stated scope; all-off remains terrain/life/weather without scripted penalties, not an error or deleted animal.

Every extra is under a labelled next-version switch, off, including Scribe/load defaults, reset and composed defaults. Lair/wreck/eggs/bane, cover/probes, cryptid subfeatures and discovery/research/build affordances inherit parent gates. Existing toggles that only stop placement are insufficient. New maps/restart/now scope is honest. No worldgen or rarity option is shipped. Muffling remains on by the owner's recorded ship-on card; no new listening question. Settings themselves are inherited floor obligations, not another gameplay bar.

## Four short stories

First hour: the crew spots a berry lamp at the edge of black sand. The builder encloses and roofs a small workroom, then heats it with an ordinary heat source. As its room temperature rises, its air clears; outside, the lamp they brought cannot reach as far. They gather food rather than wait for a research letter.

An ordinary week: a transplanted wickwood farm supplies food when warmed. Gharreks open at gusts; grazing and predation continue between them. Tholin from exposed work goes to the refinery. A light-feeder settles on the approach lamp, the caretaker excludes it and the light recovers without a new recurring job.

The worst day: Witchfire lightning threatens an exposed roof edge while an eaten lamp gives an ishvarith an approach. The crew retreats toward an intact lit room. The ombrathia rattles before its close attack; combat leaves a cause, not vanished stock. They repair, roof and restore light. No obligatory boss, probe or egg storm is secretly adding pressure.

The season's turn: an Unveiling exposes etched black relief and the living-light country already explored. Workers move a haul while the air is clear; grain still settles, faster under the shipped rule. They save midway, reload, and complete the same work with heating and planting in another order. Dark returns, the roofed work and tended farms remain usable, native life continues, and the country has not become an empty quarry or free-fuel carpet.

## Retained light tools under the owner marks

The owner marks of 2026-11-17 retain fold_lamp, vine_turning and glowberry_item in 0.1.0. The heat observation feature stays next version, switched off. Its existing FinalizeInit off-state grant completes the observation project silently and idempotently on fresh load and reload before lamp availability is queried. Discovery checks, offers and letters stay dormant. The grant completes RM_DarkFoldsFromWarmth. Ordinary bench research of RM_HeatFolding unlocks the fold lamp for building, fuelling and rotation. Its lane fades forward with distance and stops each column at a wall. Rotation, fuel exhaustion, removal and reload update the common density query. Ordinary heat and light still apply. T4/T5 carry availability and geometry acceptance.

Paddle vines visibly turn toward useful local light while retaining normal growth and fiber harvest. My delegated default selects the strongest reachable source, retaining facing when no source qualifies and keeping a stable choice on ties. Moving a source, rotating a fold lamp or dimming it with a krizzak can change the chosen bearing. The facing change must appear in the plant rendering. T39 covers growth, harvest, occlusion, ties and saved transitions.

Harvested glowberries remain edible and usable by existing recipes and native diets, and cast actual mobile light as dropped stacks or from the current local-map carrier. Reuse item/holder ownership and existing light services. Stack changes and transfers reconcile contributions. Consumption of the last berry, destruction, despawn or feature-off removes that source. T41 checks for duplicate and ghost light through split/merge and reload. Berry food/light values and turning cadence remain tunable checks.

These tools ship on, each with an effective named setting. Heat observation has its separate next-version off setting. Master-off and individual disable preserve ordinary objects, edible stacks and saved identity while safely withdrawing special effects. Costs are lamp fuel/material/work, plant growing time and edible berry stock. Routine at colony scale includes rotating and refuelling route lamps, tending light-facing fiber plots and carrying luminous forage. A lamp running dry or a consumed berry has a readable loss of clearance/light. A reload restores ownership and lane state before resumed work.

## Creepy dark fishery

The owner adds fishing in 0.1.0. My creature designs are invented franchise-free RM_ natives, specific to the Abyss. The tracked Abyss roster records no fishery, and neither species name appears in the tracked source or biome rosters. T51 requires confirmation against the resolved roster before building.

- RM_Veiljaw is a fish-sized translucent black ribbon swimmer with pale hinged jaw seams and blind pits. It undulates through the deeper parts of mineral pools, taking young suturefins and carrion. Its catch is a recognisable veiljaw carcass usable through ordinary food preparation.
- RM_Suturefin is a fish-sized flattened bottom swimmer with a pale stitch-like seam around its dark body. Rippling fins carry it between pool shelves as it grazes native biofilm. Its catch preserves that outline and supplies ordinary edible flesh.

Every declared catch has its matching living moving animal admitted to the same biome habitat. Normal ticks must produce real position changes through water. Mineral-fed biofilm supports the suturefins without sky light. Ordinary feeding, renewal, predation and readable death or catch causes connect both creatures to the food chain. Static decorations and loot-only catch entries fail B5. Population and food values are tuning choices. Existing species placements remain as recorded.

Use authored mineral-fed standing pools on the fixed world's local maps, with readable dark water, shallow shelves, deeper refuges and accessible bank work cells. Local pool provision and compatible native food substrate are build obligations under T51. Ordinary fishing eligibility, movement, harvesting and food handling reuse the project's existing services after their APIs are confirmed. Pools add no planetary generation, water rain or hydrology simulation. An unreachable bank gives the ordinary unavailable-work sign. A supported authored fishery map needs a usable bank and resident aquatic life, including when preparing a legacy map fixture.

In the first hour the crew can see a veiljaw pass below a bank and take an ordinary fishing job there. In an ordinary week they haul and cook catches alongside forage, fuel lamps and harvest vines. The worst day may interrupt a fishing job or darken its return route, leaving normal job/catch identity and recoverable work after rescue. Across the season, fishing and natural renewal must coexist with food demand, predators and routine labour. B8 includes those actions and a saved mid-job reload with reversed fishing/harvest order. M1 checks catch/animal/job ownership, M2 tests seasonal food/population/work pressure, and T51 supplies the manifest, movement traces and a short live look at both swimmers and their catches. The fishery setting ships on. Disabling it safely withdraws offers/admission while preserving living saved animals and caught items.
