# Warscar: what the rewrite chose, what it lost, and why

Companion to `design/concepts/Warscar/design.md`, written 2026-11-18 by the same pass. Its job is to let the older
files be deleted without fear: every distinct idea found in any source has a row saying what became of it. Nothing
has been deleted. The older files this design replaces are listed at the end.

**How the choices were made.** The owner's words of 2026-11-18: the biomes *"are going to be visited by a huge ship
on the run"*, crewed by Jawa *"way in over their head"*; *"anything that takes seasons to observe will simply be
missed"*; the *"OMG did you see that?"* of Jawa meeting alien creatures at close range is *"the overriding
priority"*; and *"always look for solutions that make you say Very Jawa"*. The Warscar bends easily to this: the
frozen sheet already describes it as a place Jawa raid and leave (*"The Jawa call the place cursed and keep coming
anyway"*) and already carries the one ruling in any biome written about the ship (*"the biome follows you home"*).
The cleanse proposal of 2026-11-17 chose the Settling as the signature and switched off 37 other things. This
design keeps the Settling as the signature and brings forward the parts of the switched-off list that a crew meets
in a week and that are about scavenging or about the ship: the shells the film reveals, the invisible hunter the
film betrays, the rings the ship wakes, and the mynocks.

**Words used in "what happened".** *kept*; *kept, reshaped* (changed as stated); *moved* (scope bucket changed, from
and to given; the cleanse used "first version", "next version, switched off" and "recommend retiring", and this
design uses 0.1.0, 0.2.0, later, dropped; a cleanse "next version" item that lands in "later" is not a move);
*folded into shared machinery*; *lost*. Where a *moved* row's last two cells are empty, its reason and its other choice are the line
for that feature under "Scope moves": the other choice is to move it back, and the cost is the scenes and checks
that come with it. Where a *lost* row's last two cells are empty, the reason is in the outcome cell itself (a ban,
a card, his word) and the other choice is to reopen that ruling.

## The ones I would look at

1. **The hospice.** *"Walk in, carry one out, wake it."* ... *"The tragedy is the economy."* Jawa carrying a dead
   droid home and coaxing it awake over a week, one flickering light at a time, to find out why it ran, is the most
   Jawa thing in any of these five biomes, and his own words made each machine a story. I left it in 0.2.0 because
   it is the largest single feature in the mod, has never been seen live, and its repair inputs run through the
   reagent he said he did not recognise. If one thing is pulled forward into 0.1.0, I would pull this.
2. **"That's salvage man!"** Pulling a working shield generator off the old line and carrying it home is in 0.2.0
   with the rest of the ring work. In 0.1.0 the rings only hum. A crew that can see a working generator and cannot
   take it will feel the gap.
3. **Glower black and the war-black hull.** Out by his card of 2026-09-30, before he stated the ship lens and
   before his ruling that every biome wears on the hull and has an armour or shield that answers it. It was the
   crust's use as fuel, as a pigment and as *"war-black hull plating"*. Lost, with the glower's shielding panels
   (which he did rule in) carrying the armour role in 0.2.0.
4. **The tetchik in a jar.** *"A living pollution counter"* a caravan carries, removed from source on 2026-10-23 as
   fluff. For a ship that chooses its next landing, a beetle that ticks faster over poisoned country is not fluff.
   Lost; the picture he kept is still in the mod.
5. **The invisible hunter in 0.1.0.** The cleanse deferred it. I moved it in because its whole design is the
   film's (*"unseen things leave prints"*), because his answer of 2026-11-10 about lone travel was given for it,
   and because a line of prints with nobody in them walking toward a lone Jawa is the lens. Its hunt state is not
   saved across a reload, which the census names as a risk.
6. **No hide at all.** I wrote that the Warscar gives no cover from orbit and almost nothing that helps a crew
   stay. That is what the sources say. It makes this the one biome of the five where the answer to "what helps
   them stay longer" is "nothing; leave". If every biome is meant to have an answer, this is the gap.
7. **The Sentinels.** Defend-only machines that strike whoever breaks their structures and never march are a
   ready-made third side when the Empire blasts its way in. They are campaign content with no attachment to this
   biome today, and I did not promise them.
8. **The bearing.** *"Every kneeling chassis, every pilgrim body, every humming ring faces one bearing."* A pawn who
   has seen three learns it and the world map gains a dotted line. He left that card blank twice. It is the
   quietest good idea in the file and it tells a ship where to go next.

## Scope moves

Each line is one feature whose bucket changed against the cleanse proposal of 2026-11-17. Revert by moving the line
in the design's Scope section.

- **Buried shells revealed by the film; defuse or trigger:** next version → 0.1.0. Scavenging, slapstick, and a
  minefield the crew maps for itself before the Empire comes.
- **The invisible hunter and its signs (prints, the quiet ring, shimmer, drag):** next version → 0.1.0.
- **Humming rings that screen the fall, and the ship waking dead rings:** next version → 0.1.0. It is the answer to
  the fall on the ship that needs nothing built by the crew, and it makes where to land a choice.
- **Mynocks aboard:** the cleanse listed the vermin kit as a neighbour, off → 0.1.0, as the hull-wear his ruling of
  2026-11-18 asks every biome to state.
- **Chatrak plate as a material:** kept in 0.1.0 (the cleanse kept it); only its no-dye rule is recommended for
  dropping.
- **Ring evaluation and salvage, the buildable screen, glower shielding, the hospice (rings, histories, revival,
  failure, walk-in), the totchak, the lacquer cloak, turret refit, the pools and their reagents and what they do to
  a corpse, the old tongue, war dust and its uses, the mark's trade bonuses, loosened panels, the bileworm's
  stench, scanner readings:** next version → 0.2.0.
- **Stayed "later" from the cleanse's "next version":** rest transcriptions and their premium buyers, the screen's
  calibration, the campaign kit and lore, the campaign's wake swap, the Open Boast. Not moves.
- **The label question the cleanse asked:** answered ("Warscar", the later card).
- **New, not in any source:** the Empire's approach written on the film (a reading of the built track grid, which
  records every pawn); marked shells set off under a strike team (a reading of the built ranged trigger); the
  Sentinels as a third side if the campaign attaches them; "no cover, fuel by the slow route only, nothing sold
  here" (statements his rulings of 2026-11-18 require, drawn from the sheet's weather, flora and settlement lines).
