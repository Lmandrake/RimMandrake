## Second attack: the laziest plausible build

A builder could keep flat grey floor and a single pretty dust overlay, report that counters and caps exist, then show a roofed pawn with toxins disabled. That satisfies the old root's atmosphere but fails W1's geometry, W3's real feeding/remains, W4's actionable disease, W5's source-attributed health, W6/W7's actual records/coupled clearing and W9's all-on season. The revised says/contrasting pictures make those failures explicit. Each lazy field is the exact reject picture, not a claim that the picture now satisfies the tightened words.

A second shortcut is to turn off all specials and call that a small biome. It fails because silent tracking, disease onset, real carrion feeding and native lasting contamination have explicit deletion arguments. We did not count all custom code as extras. Conversely, roof shelter works without salvageable rings, and ordinary sealed salvage works without privileged mark caches. The bare mark remains necessary for the explicit marked-in-mind promise, independently of its deferred rewards. The proposals retain 10 experience bars without requiring a taste vote on every species stat or variant.

Residual uncertainty is measured-system uncertainty, not an owner question: five shared meetings are unknown, each with a named model in bars.json. Actual geometry, native renewal/forage viability, coupled saved sweeps and complete-profile default gates remain unproved. A fresh reviewer refused the earlier proposal on seven points. These repairs await that reviewer’s second look. No second-look verdict or game measurement is claimed.
