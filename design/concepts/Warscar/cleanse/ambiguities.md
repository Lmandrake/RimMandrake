# Settled ambiguities and the attack on this definition

All readings below are Designer decisions under this brief, not his new words. No question is left pending. Earlier draft questions are not permission requests; the later ANSWERS_OWNER.md already answers curse/repair-consent/hunter-warning choices. The exact concept has no locked leaves or recorded waiver to reopen.

1. **One mod or all of the old bedazzle slate?** Reading A keeps every once-commissioned interaction; B makes the first version land/life/one signature and defers the rest. Consequence: screen→gel→pools→manuals→hospice either becomes compulsory build scope or does not. Take B, per the current cleansing authority. Earlier broad-tech and accepted-history rulings constrain their eventual behaviour; deferral does not reverse them. Fabulous recovered technology is a future promise, not falsely declared present acceptance.

2. **Signature means weather, curse, or machine rescue?** A says every memorable system is part of one “cursed battlefield”; B names a single causal cycle. Consequence: an arbitrarily large feature bundle can be called one system. Take B: calm→dust/toxicity/record→wind/lift/erase. Hospice is the named runner-up. The event loop is already in RM_Settling.cs; no existing code dependency makes a cradle necessary to it.

3. **Poisoned forever: label or enduring substrate?** A retains RM_PollutedBiomeExtension and says it is done; B requires an actual contamination source plus infertile native substrate after cleanup. Consequence: a player can permanently recover the biome in A. Take B from the_scarlands.md sections 3/5/6. Marker only detects; missing source is T17. Use ordinary pollution/toxin state and the season model to select minimal implementation; no isotope/novel radiation engine is implied.

4. **No fertile cell: no player food technology?** A bans all farming devices; B forbids native-ground rehabilitation while permitting building-based hydroponics and imported meals. Consequence: A invents a colony technology ban. Take B: the source ban describes land, and ordinary buildability must coexist with it. Terrain removal, imported soil and generic clean-water growth on the native mask cannot bypass it. The source mask is local geometry, not new worldgen.

5. **No soil versus glower growth.** A treats zero ordinary fertility as a reason the native crusts need not exist; B allows native crust growth without creating generally fertile soil. Consequence: all-floor maps can become a dead zoo in A, because glower/scorched-stars fertility minima and ordinary spawn/growth routes need checking. Take B from the food web. T26 must reconcile actual native eligibility with sterile ground, using existing plant data/seeder routes; do not “fix” this with fertile soil.

6. **Forage: caravan berries or human food actually here?** A accepts RawBerries because BiomeDef declares them; B says humans bring food/hunt, while grazers eat crust. Consequence: A fabricates a plant economy. Take B from the no-green/no-Earth flora and actual flora defs. T26 removes/changes unsupported forage claim without inventing edible tetchik or a berry plant.

7. **Scaria always comes: every grazer is doomed?** A secretly arms healthy/tamed animals; B arms only wild scaria-bearing chatrak, with existing cure/tame rules. Consequence: a clean livestock purchase becomes a hidden curse under A. Take B from kit ruling and RM_ChatrakSnap.Armable. “Always” applies to an eligible untreated infection, not every individual. Revalidate after cure; old incubation cannot linger as a trap. This is necessary ordinary-life disease, not a second colony curse system.

8. **Visible warning: decorative stage or usable local warning?** A only changes text immediately before attack; B lets an able nearby worker on an open path retreat. Consequence: A cannot affect a player decision. Take B, the minimal meaningful warning, as delegated inference. No global evacuation timer, remote letter or exact stat is a bar. Downed/trapped workers are excluded from the escape promise, not from correct health accounting. Blocked circling never stalls disease. T20 observes actual progression, not ProofStage.

9. **Ordinary fauna: a blank body is enough?** A removes electric attacks and special corpse feeding as “code”; B keeps each animal's ordinary means of attacking/eating but moves independent rewards/auras. Consequence: A changes a weapon-descendant into an unrelated harmless insect. Take B by classification of player experience. Bileworm gas/drinking and scar-roach waste eating are feeding, not optional industry. Bileworm's separate stench memory moves. Normal plate hide stays; the special no-dye veto is recommended for retirement.

10. **Bodies leave signs: any vanish with a log?** A treats missing corpses plus a debug record as readable; B requires remains in-world, carried items recoverable and a colony-body notice where appropriate. Consequence: named dead and quest gear disappear silently under A. Take B from the standing sign rule/cast bible. Existing bileworm drops gear; pallbearer has only vanilla ingest. Bones only hooks butchery. T26 must prove or supply the final-ingest remains path rather than claim vanilla desiccation always happens before eating.

11. **Rosters: stale register or resolved current load?** A evicts outdated/multi-homed entries to simplify; B preserves actual inline and patched rosters, notes overlap on this biome's sitting. Consequence: A violates stopped evictions. Take B. The available inline vozzik stays even though its source home is Stillsand. Campaign files are missing in this checkout: unknown is neither an empty patched roster nor permission to invent replacements.

## Second attack: the laziest plausible build

A builder could keep flat grey floor and a single pretty dust overlay, report that counters and caps exist, then show a roofed pawn with toxins disabled. That satisfies the old root's atmosphere but fails W1's geometry, W3's real feeding/remains, W4's actionable disease, W5's source-attributed health, W6/W7's actual records/coupled clearing and W9's all-on season. The revised says/contrasting pictures make those failures explicit. Each lazy field is the exact reject picture, not a claim that the picture now satisfies the tightened words.

A second shortcut is to turn off all specials and call that a small biome. It fails because silent tracking, disease onset, real carrion feeding and native lasting contamination have explicit deletion arguments. We did not count all custom code as extras. Conversely, roof shelter works without salvageable rings, and ordinary sealed salvage works without mark caches: code coupling is decoupled, not promoted to need. The proposals retain 9 experience bars without requiring a taste vote on every species stat or variant.

Residual uncertainty is measured-system uncertainty, not an owner question: six shared meetings are unknown, each with a named model in bars.json. Actual geometry, native renewal/forage viability, coupled saved sweeps and complete-profile default gates remain unproved. No permission or first-look review is fabricated; the external fresh reviewer has not run in this authoring pass.
