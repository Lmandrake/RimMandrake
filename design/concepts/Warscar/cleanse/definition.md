# Warscar — proposed Design Version 0.1.0

Status: Designer proposal, 2026-11-17. All scope choices, defaults, rules and bar wording here are mine under the cleansing brief. They are not new owner rulings. Nothing is applied until the separate review and owner reading. No game, bridge, build, art or ledger operation was performed.

## The mod in three sentences

You scavenge a cratered plateau where shattered floors and stripped defenses still show a last stand, but no trace explains the enemy. Crust grazers, living munitions and carrion cleaners survive on what the war left, while untreated wild plated grazers eventually turn dangerous and prolonged salvaging leaves a mental scar. When the wind dies, toxic dust falls and records movement; returning wind lifts the dust and erases the record, so ordinary roofs and deliberate routes matter to every expedition.

Land: shattered megastructure floor, slag hills, crater bowls, stripped outward-facing defenses, enduring contaminated substrate, a lingering mental cost of residence, dry grey sky and harmless tracking gun barrels on native maps.

Life: retained roster and ordinary lifecycle, feeding, weapons, butchery and taming; glower/scorched stars/wreck-lichen, scaria chatrak onset with local warning, carrion processing with remains, and waste eating. It is a web a player encounters, not a closed ecosystem that must support a colony without imported food.

Signature: sustained calm brings airborne toxic dust and a film that records walkers; sustained returning wind lifts that film and its movement record away.

Runner-up: recovering named deserters through the hospice. It lost because waking a machine needs a repair/research/material/history chain independent of the weather cycle. The 2026-11-10 answers make fabulous technology a real future promise, not a reason to smuggle that entire chain into this first version. Its accepted memory text, strength, repair-consent principle and broad technology scope survive the deferral.

## Why a player reaches for it

A legible cursed battlefield offers sealed salvage under bodily exposure and a lingering mental cost. The bare mark is needed by land. Deleting its accrual, mood, nightmares or lasting floor would erase the frozen sheet sections 5 and 7b promise that salvagers return marked in mind. Trade bonuses and mark-only panel caches remain separate next-version rewards. The selected experience in design/RimMandrake/biome_experiences_2026-10-20.md, Warscar row, says poisoned ground, mad beasts and silent guns; the owner answers in design/RimMandrake/designer_codex_2026-11-09/Scarlands/ANSWERS_OWNER.md connect radiation seeping in with fabulous technology. Prediction, not a bar: seeing a crew's route appear on the ground while sheltering from falling dust will make expeditions feel exposed and the ruins worth learning. This version earns that prediction with terrain, ordinary salvage and the dust cycle. It does not claim the later technology recovery story has already passed.

## Using it

First hour: inspect the crater plateau, identify traversable floor and a roofable shelter, bring food and ordinary protection, choose a route through stripped wrecks toward a sealed prize. A working projector is not required or generated by defaults. The condition notice and falling dust identify a Settling. Inspect an exposed worker's toxic buildup and move them under a roof; shade from a wall alone is not a dust roof. The plate-lifting grazer nearby is a warning about that animal, not a detector of toxic air.

Routine: import food or hunt edible fauna; harvest crust as ordinary raw trade material if useful. Lichen beside wrecks supports grazers. Clean the work area normally; this yields no war dust. A scavenger eating carrion leaves readable physical remains; a bileworm leaves bile and drops the dead pawn's equipment before consumption, with a notice for a colonist body. Scar roaches eat filth/waste using their shared engine; this can remove film locally. A new clean patch therefore proves neither shelter nor ordnance. During calm the source-local wind sound stops; tetchik clicks still come from their actual local sources. When strong wind returns, a sweeping lift clears film and tracks together. Its brief airborne exposure obeys ordinary protection and does not strike the same pawn repeatedly during that lift.

At 40 colonists: roofs, zones, work priorities and ordinary hunting/cleaning remain the management tools. No new dust job, harvest schedule, repeat letter for each worker or second research economy. A condition transition can be announced; ordinary accumulations are inspected through the existing health/condition UI. Clean routes maintained by workers and roaches are legitimate; the remaining uncleaned filmed routes must still show movement. Capacity pressure may evict old shared-grid records, never disable all new tracks. The player must not need to babysit a trace pool or an ambient sound loop.

## What changes in the colony's story

The colony learns that an apparently quiet ruin still responds: broken guns follow movers harmlessly, sick armour changes before a rage, and the ground briefly shows who passed. A full map need not be arranged into a literal perfect ring. Defenses must share approaches and layers so the last stand is visible; scatter that could be any abandoned town fails. No text or shape identifies the unseen enemy. Sealed vanilla/Odyssey salvage is the current reward. Ancient danger sites, including contents supplied by Odyssey ruins, are already opened and destroyed. Sprung clusters and cracked-open threat vaults lie spent beside sealed salvage containers. Sealed or dormant threats that wake on opening fail the land promise. Surface wrecks remain stripped. A colony may roof and build on the floor, but cannot rehabilitate the native ground into soil.

## Costs and failure signs

Ambient heat and Direct Sun keep their shared geometry-to-vanilla-Heatstroke contract. Conventional chemical/radiological fiction uses ordinary pollution and toxic-buildup paths. The saved native-substrate mask remains sterile through clearing, wetting and terrain replacement. Imported meals and building hydroponics remain available.

Renewal acts only on saved native cells that have neither a player-built floor nor a roof. Either covering stops native-source pollution writes beneath it. Removing the covering restores renewal eligibility on the same saved mask. Covering does not erase existing pollution, which still needs ordinary cleanup. An exposed worker on renewed polluted ground receives vanilla pollution effects according to ordinary resistance and health rules, separately attributed from the falling-dust and lift doses. The native crust flora RM_Glower, RM_ScorchedStars and RM_WreckLichen require plant.pollution Any and native-substrate placement, so they can establish and survive both clean and polluted native cells without admitting ordinary soil crops. A selected cell's inspect text identifies the native source as active or covered, and the ordinary pollution overlay/tint shows contamination returning after cleanup. No repeated renewal letter is needed. T17 owns this specified coverage, sign and effect contract. The whole-map model selects cadence and checks the combined exposure cost.

Damage shows in existing health state. Dust has a condition label, falling overlay and pale film. Rage has lifted plates, appetite change and circling/inspect state before attack. Every untreated wild chatrak remaining on the Warscar must eventually progress, including an animal initially spawned without scaria. T20 extends current scaria-only arming to add ordinary Scaria and incubation where needed. Successful medical cure cancels incubation and records an exemption from automatic biome reinfection. A later independently acquired Scaria infection can arm again. Already factioned clean livestock is outside wild arming. Scaria carriers retain vanilla taming prohibition and factioned rage under the kit's actual owner ruling. Death/despawn ends pending local jobs, and blocked circling cannot stall disease. A nearby able worker with an open retreat route can withdraw after the visible warning.

The bare mark accrues in living spawned humanlikes on the Warscar, including visitors and prisoners, with the existing non-mech eligibility and family no-stacking rule. Roofs, floors and wind do not stop this mental cost. Shallow exposure can fade after early departure. Prolonged exposure arms a saved permanent floor that leaves a small enduring mood burden. Deep marks can cause the existing Wander_Sad distress episodes, the fiction's nightmares, during residency. Leaving ends any mark-origin episode and prevents new ones until return, while the established mood scar persists. Other causes of mental states keep ordinary rules. T05 must repair the current ungated XML mental-state route and prove actual thought and episode effects. Trade offsets and loosened-panel rewards stay off through their own gates. At colony scale the whole-season model must check that this cost remains compatible with ordinary work.

## Edges

The fixed planet is supplied by savegame; map-local ruin/terrain placement is permitted. No planet generator, second planet, world-site generator, tile repaint or world rarity knob is a capability of any version. A biome with zero world tiles is expected, never a finding. Imported building-based hydroponics do not turn native ground into fertile soil; no new hydroponics prohibition is invented. Player buildings and ordinary roofs stay usable. Local floor removal, water, mutation and soil-import interactions must respect the native-substrate ban; the proposal does not silently take ownership of every terrain engine.

No roster eviction: preserve the current inline and actually patched rosters. The sparse checkout has no src/RimUtinni or src/RimStarWars tree; historical patch rows are unknown, not absent. ## Four short stories

First hour: the crew sees guns aimed along the same broken approaches, empty hull ribs and lichen where metal meets floor. They shelter a pack animal and supplies before investigating a sealed store. The sky begins falling; this is a reason to use the roof they found, not a scripted letter that gives them a new quest.

Ordinary week: the hunter brings meat home, a medically cured chatrak grazes, roaches work discarded filth and the corpse slug leaves bile beside dropped equipment. Workers keep the home path clean. Beyond it, a crossing animal leaves prints in pale dust. The wind erases the outer record and the week continues; nothing feeds an automatic reagent factory.

Worst day: the sick grazer's plates have risen beside an exposed salvage crew. The player withdraws to roofed floor while wind lifts the dust elsewhere. A worker who stays outside is poisoned; the roofed worker avoids the airborne contribution. A blocked retreat or ignored warning can still be fatal, with health/corpse/remains accounting. A reload at this moment preserves front progress and the medically cured animal’s exemption. A deeply marked resident may need care for distressed wandering even under shelter.

Season's turn: successive calm and wind spells leave and erase different trails. The settlers have built shelter and learned routes. Cleaned covered work areas stay usable, and exposed native substrate remains contaminated and infertile. The primary season itself includes a complete natural calm, fall, returning-wind and finished-lift cycle at shipped defaults. A season missing that cycle is a stall and cannot pass through a forced replay. Returning salvagers carry an established mood scar, while mark nightmares cease once they leave. Crust/grazer/carrion turnover persists in the parts they have not harvested bare. The map is neither coated with immortal records nor emptied of all forage by the combined toxin/animal rules. A second expedition that inspects salvage before roofing, rather than after, pays the same understandable exposure costs. This is the whole bar, not an assertion that the present code already does it.
