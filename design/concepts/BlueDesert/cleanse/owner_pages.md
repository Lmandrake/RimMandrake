# Page one — what Blue Desert becomes

This is my proposed Design Version 0.1.0, not a change to the mod or your rulings. Nothing applies before you read these pages and say so; independent review still follows this proposal.

Blue Desert is an open, ancient ice plateau whose blue distance resolves into pale ice and transparent, branching life. Its useful native growth and creatures are cold-stable fuel: damage or warming can turn a connected patch, and the cargo taken from it, into an explosive chain. Crews prepare a work site, keep an exit clear, mine ordinary water ice and recover old salvage, then keep native cargo cold at home.

**Land:** water ice, ice-grain drifts, obstructing fog, static abandoned workings and salvage.

**Life:** the existing low plants and native food web; ordinary grazing, hunting, wild harvesting and taming, with actual vrisk flight. No animal eviction.

**Signature:** cold preserves useful native fuel; disturbing or warming it can spread a blast, so preparation and cold handling matter.

Archaeological progression was the runner-up. It lost because study unlocks and awakening siege forms would make another defining system. Physical old workings remain; their absence cannot be covered by generic random loot.

I took your later answers literally: ordinary mineral mining, preparation by separating growth and securing escape, and cooling from loose ice without a rack. No special pick-speed heat model. The later giant exposing buried ground stays future work, separate from the cut rime road.

# Page two — what to check

These are my calls where your words did not settle implementation:

- “Hydrocarbon ice”: water-ice ground, hydrocarbon life; the mined water never explodes.
- “Careful”: real containment before normal work; a cleared gap must account for animals, corpses and carried wax too.
- “Everything becomes a bomb”: connected fuel can chain; empty ice is not an unexplained propagation path.
- “Warning”: locatable warming state and a usable pre-ignition response; audio alone is insufficient. Reload preserves the danger.
- “Long” cooling: finite reserve buys actual repair time under declared room/traffic loads; no guessed duration becomes a bar.
- “Ancient secrets”: a readable abandoned extraction attempt with accessible ice and salvage, without research or campaign history prerequisites.
- “Deep drift”: actual obstructing deposits against structures, not an overlay or a declared sand rate.
- Ordinary plants stay while special outputs/timers switch off independently. Native chemistry persists through newborns, corpses, hauling, splitting and processing.
