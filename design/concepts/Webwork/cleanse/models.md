# Instruments to specify before building

These are specifications, not completed instruments, measurements or passing evidence. Existing kernel fuzz, forced C# proofs, mock responders and the harvest runner cover slices; they do not already implement these contracts. Build in the order below: transition generator, geometry, material ledger, then the calibrated whole-map model. The existing model names stay stable for cross-references. No model generates or alters a planet. An exported local situation or weather trace is input, not a new world seed. Uncalibrated output is a hypothesis; incompatible production/source/settings fingerprints invalidate prior calibration.

## M1 Situations and event-to-job transitions

Question: can each allowed contact, carrier, hunter, nest and shutdown state reach the promised next state through the production caller, without a fabricated mark, job or reward?

Informs: B3 event attribution and pursuit lifecycle (not simultaneous convergence), B4 predator, B5 life tools, B6 extraction, B7 refuge, B8 whole; R2 detection, R3 targeting, R4 movement-source/exposed-retreat transitions, R5 nest, R6 modes, R7 life jobs, R8 shutdown. Meetings `detection_state`, `nest_state`, `predator_population`, `settings_state`. Its transition traces feed M2, and its spatial preconditions come from M3.

Inputs and ranges: states grounded in actual resolved content/adapters, never just the intended spec. Pawn alive/dead/downed/dormant/despawned; owner awake/dormant/exposed/shaded; movement source think-tree hunting/mark-directed pursuit/idle/Manhunter/hive-lord AssaultColony or DefendHiveAggressively, including guardian escalation, exposed-start retreat and no-retreat hold, source/exposure changes on a running path and reload; target reachable/unreachable/behind closed door; no/one/multiple marked targets; ordinary/generic preferred flesh type; new/renewed/expired mark; contact through walking, deliberate cutting, clutch mining, ship landing, egg inventory, haul-in-hands and caravan arrival. Cargo dropped, transferred, sold, rotted, destroyed, in corpse/container, or off map. Nodes absent/single/overlapping, saved and re-registered; nest present/removed, initial cohort budget unspent/spent, awake intact wall after guardians die, clutch empty/present, due/not due, no live owner/dormant owner/unrelated surviving owner/new natural arrival. Settings on/off/reset at each advertised effective time, including old save with existing job/cocoon. Sweep colony cardinality 0–40 for list/queue/limiter boundaries, with 0, 1, 4, 12, 40 as actual calibration anchors; arrivals on an inhabited map and a deliberately cleared one. Enumerate event orders up to length 6 around each boundary; longer cycles reduce only when the full relevant saved state matches. Deterministic choices at probability thresholds are inputs, not fabricated chances of success.

Assumptions: finite explicit state graph; runtime adapters faithfully report the same event/actor/cargo/geometry to the shared service as the offline transition. Production functions must be reused where available; extracting small decision functions is a builder option, not a design requirement. Model the scheduling gap explicitly. If a retained mechanism cannot be represented without simulating engine internals, mark its transition live-only and retain the game trace as the decisive instrument; do not invent a model implementation of a different AI.

Leaves out: sound/image quality, combat damage distribution, unbounded event histories, off-map caravan battles, campaign quests, deferred features, actual pathfinding geometry (M3). Real combat and movement scheduling remain integration calibration, not inferred from job names. In particular, M1 does not model simultaneous owner positions, reservations or route contention; it cannot decide B3 convergence. T40’s live multi-owner trace is decisive for that clause, with distinct-approach and single-crossing controls; a successful lifecycle table is insufficient.

Game calibration: record actual ordinary walk/cut/land events and job transitions without `StartJob`, direct mark injection, forced wake or due-time calls. Read actor, source node/cargo ID, registration counts, target choice, mark expiry, job, due time and settings fingerprint. Match state and transition order exactly; allow at most one observed production scan interval of scheduling latency, then use the measured interval as an input rather than a guess. Include negative controls: unregistered crossing, lamp, shelf eggs, dead target, duplicate load, ordinary predator with no mark, sterile map. A real landing and hand-held haul must traverse their own adapters. A model cannot stand in for an adapter that has no calibration trace. Flight compares actual `Pawn_FlightTracker` states, never unattended flyer pictures.

Produces: reachable-state table, predecessor chain and smallest counterexample for each failed bar predicate, missing/unreached adapter list, and replayable precondition/action bundles. Rough size [estimate]: a few thousand state representatives and tens of thousands of short walks after symmetry reduction, not the blind Cartesian product. Record discarded classes and why their failures are equivalent; do not say merely “the family.”

Trigger: build first because integration is already absent/broken in the source and multiple shared states are unknown. Tickets T40 and the preceding implementation tickets name the work; identify tickets by their titles if numbering changes. Decision changed: whether to repair an adapter, split a state, attack a rule, or move an actually independent behaviour later. It cannot delete a required bar just because its state reader is missing.

## M3 Web, canopy and refuge geometry sweep

Question: over every supported local placement and geometric boundary, does visible web correspond to traversable sensed ground and does actual sunlight protect the clearing until real shade returns?

Informs: B1 hierarchy, B3 touch, B5 beetle routes, B6 nest access, B7 refuge, B8 whole; R1, R2, R3, R4, R7, R9. Unknown meeting `web_geometry` and geometric input to `exposure_geometry`; informs the already shared tree-shade/Direct Sun services without redesigning them.

Inputs/range: production `RM_WebNetKernel` site/edge/raster plans plus actual `RM_GenStep_WebworkWeb.CanHold` masks and `RM_GenStep_WebworkNest` legal-position predicates. Sweep legal nest/site positions and translations through map boundaries, edge margins, narrow accessible clearings, full/empty/legal-space-sliver masks, rocks, walls, items, trees, roofs, slopes, cable overlaps, removed nodes and carpet borders. Enumerate line bearings, coincident/short/long segments, sag signs and supported map dimensions 200–400 (only shapes the engine actually permits); small toy masks are counterexample tools, not additional supported maps. Shade: sample all actual shipped crown/height archetypes and canopy gaps; use shared sun geometry continuously across each algorithmic threshold and fixed authored tile positions. Weather sun factor ranges from actual clear to the lowest supported opaque-weather value, including its changing edges; lamp versus sun; roof/parasols, newly felled and regrown crowns, doors opening, load-time grid rebuild.

Assumptions: use real geometry functions and exported crowns, not painted proxy circles that “look close.” Registration is a reference count of occupied mechanical cells. Path admissibility must use the actual retained consumers for think-tree hunting, marked pursuit, idle movement, Manhunter and hive-lord AssaultColony/DefendHiveAggressively (including enraged guardians), not straight-line distance or only the custom hunter. Include source switches, cached running paths after exposure change, exposed-start retreat-only paths and no-retreat holds under R4. If cached engine reachability differs, model result is a counterexample candidate until that source’s production path is read. This remains a single-owner geometry/admissibility sweep: it does not model simultaneous route contention and cannot decide B3 convergence; T40’s live multi-owner trace does.

Leaves out: growth timing, animal combat, sound, geometry outside supported local maps. “Thick” and “still jungle” have no credible numeric proxy; output drawings for B1's perceptual reader instead of a made-up coverage quota.

Game calibration: compare enumerator output with the naturally generated map's exact node IDs/occupied cells, path cost/passability, actual nest footprint and access, per-cell shade and `RM_Hediff_SunScald.ExposedAt`; compare before/after an actual tree cut, weather boundary, node despawn and reload. Match registration counts, nest access, passability and exposure booleans exactly; numerical shade may differ by at most 0.001 away from thresholds, and any disagreement at a threshold is a failure. For each counterexample, read the relevant production consumer’s chosen path/refusal and a pawn crossing the cable. Calibrate each R4 source separately, including a Manhunter chase and a guardian escalated by ordinary combat, plus already-exposed retreat/no-retreat controls; hunter-only agreement does not calibrate the other sources. No roof stand-in calibrates tree shade, no grid exists merely because the source declares one. Wrong/stale cache invalidates the sweep until fixed.

Produces: contrasting local map drawings, connected-component/route masks, exposed-edge overlays, shortest counterexample per rule/bar and a list of legal sites the current finite random sampler can miss. Rough size [estimate]: thousands of representative masks, exhaustive translations/rotations where equivalent, hundreds of thousands of predicate evaluations; start with the sliver-site and shade bridge boundaries. Trigger: initial unknown geometry meeting, any first real-canopy refuge failure, any placer/raster/crown change. Decision changed: legal-space fallback, raster/visual join, registration or path-consumer repair; never invent an arbitrary biome sun offset to hide failure.

## M4 Material and carrier provenance ledger

Question: can every usable fabric/egg and every refreshed carry alarm be traced to an allowed production action, with no disabled extra or duplicate transaction supplying it?

Informs: B3 mark, B5 ordinary forage and product use, B6 treasure, B8 whole; R2, R5, R6, R8, R10 source boundary. Meeting `material_sources` and extraction feedback into M2.

Inputs/range: paired expedition and ordinary work/trade runs from the same early or established departure save over the same elapsed days, matched market opportunities, starting needs, actual completed workshop/refit and whether the control can afford it, cargo use/sale receipts and trip costs including lost labour; actual destruction-event modes and actor provenance, deliberate cut/forced combat/beetle/fire/cleanup, butchery, ordinary natural egg mining, repeat calls and save/load at event boundaries; hands/inventory/transfer/stack split/merge/rot/corpse/shelf/caravan/ship cargo; no/one/many destinations. Read resolved ordinary trader stock generators, quest reward tags, random-stuff gear generation and recipes with the base strip and approved rare `Orbital_Exotic` resale on; campaign layer absent/present with its later stockers off; specimen, remains, siege, bounty and assassination settings off. Compare allowed resale draws at probability threshold values, and actual trade actions at unchanged negotiator/market conditions. Rates and prices are inputs/checks, not bars.

Assumptions: a faithful event log can identify source, recipient and conserved amount; production adapters expose actor/source even when the destroy callback loses its pawn. A stale list of XML stock entries cannot prove generated trader supply. Egg definitions do not establish that a real buyer accepts them. Do not model unseen trader algorithms by guessing a distribution.

Leaves out: strategic market equilibrium, optimal farming profits and campaign quest design. Resale is already approved; the model does not ask whether to ban it again.

Game calibration: match actual generated stock lists, actual cut/mine/butcher drop events, successful vanilla sale receipts, hauling/inventory and carrier mark traces, disabled feature eligibility and settings fingerprints. On deterministic local actions, event/source/count/recipient and tradeability match exactly. On random generated stock compare exact seeded production decisions where supported, otherwise calibrate distribution and retain a state read of each actual generator type; no green result from sampling only a lucky stocked trader. Expensive resale compares the same material/negotiator conditions with and without the price tier, not an invented market-value bar. Match the paired colony receipts, ordinary work and trade, expedition costs and completed improvement against game records. A token payout or an improvement already affordable without the trip fails. Deliberately plant a force-broken cable yield, doubled callback and an inventory-only hauling detector; all must be refused.

Produces: source-to-output table, shortest leak/duplication trace, carrier/mark timeline, authorized-versus-disabled supplier list, exact evidence for each B6 accept/reject picture. Rough size [estimate]: hundreds of deterministic event walks and generated stock/reward cases, extended when content adds a supplier. Trigger: existing `killedLeavings`/deferred-caller coupling, first extraction failure, or a price/yield constant changed twice to move a check. Decision changed: transaction/actor adapter or gate repair, source whitelist correction and reward tuning sufficient for the November 10 substantial-progress comparison; never make a reward-count check into a bar.
## M2 Whole-map living web over time

Question: with all retained systems active, does a colony's maintained refuge coexist with a living, huntable jungle and meaningful extraction, instead of growth, harvest and predation feeding a flood, desert or stall?

Informs: B1 land, B3 contact, B4 predator, B5 tools, B6 extraction, B7 maintained refuge, B8 whole; R1 layout, R2 detection, R3 targeting, R4 exposure, R5 nest, R6 harvest, R7 life, R8 shutdown, R9 growth. Unknown meetings `predator_population`, `canopy_growth` and `exposure_geometry`; also consumes calibrated M1/M3/M4 outputs. This is the main three-way loop: more cutting -> more material and contact marks -> more pursuit by existing owners -> less clearing work -> more shade -> more reachable hunting. Beetle loss of sensing surfaces can interrupt that loop. Natural owners and the one-time guardian cohort can reduce scarce prey without ending the living cycle.

Inputs/range: local full-map state from actual supported Webwork maps, 200–400 cells per side where those shapes are permitted by the engine; flat/large hills/mountainous local masks, fertile/poor margins, naturally sparse/dense canopy and connected/severed web. Do not synthesize alternative worlds. Fixed tile sun positions and hot/dry versus storm-fed weather regimes from the authored world; include actual weather transitions, webfall and ordinary vanilla fire, disease, growth and death. Use resolved engine profiles, not growDays alone. Sweep current shipped defaults plus 0.5x, 1x, 2x growth settings as sensitivity only; bar judging uses defaults. Local colony schedules: no occupation, foot party, settled cardinalities 1–40, using 4, 12 and 40 as calibration anchors and splitting at any actual work/queue regime boundary; maintain a compact compound plus haul routes, abandon maintenance, and reverse harvest/clear/haul order. Record a footprint in cells, ordinary pawn work capacities and transit times rather than granting perfect labour. Simulate at least a season, extend through natural nest renewal and no-mother branch, and include days 30, 60 and 100 plus a midway saved-state restart. Run event seeds for stochastic LOCAL mechanics, not worldgen seeds; extend until regime classification is stable or mark uncertainty.

Assumptions: engine-observed growth transition rates, the saved one-time guardian budget, finite prey, actual work/travel costs, exposed-route refusal, and saved clocks accurately summarize the retained interactions. Explicitly include tree maturation/end-of-life, node destruction without automatic reconstruction, ordinary animal influx/exit, disease losses, eggs eaten/rotted/sold and owner starvation. Do not add breeding, irrigation, border expansion or adaptive work automation. If poor inputs make refuge survival unknowable, output unknown rather than tune hidden rates to a preferred story.

Leaves out: rendering, hearing dread, player tactical skill beyond declared ordinary schedules, general RimWorld raid balance, deferred siege/giant/lance/rites and campaign quest economy. It predicts workload and ecology; it cannot certify the whole bar alone.

Game calibration before belief: on matching-fingerprint natural maps, capture full entity lists/IDs and positions, canopy/exposure masks, growth/age distribution, fire area, health/death causes, animal arrivals/exits, each guardian spawn and its wall’s lifetime spend, deconstruction mode/actor/yield, clutch consumption/due times and work-job durations. Compare at start, day 1, day 7, season end, and around a naturally due renewal; repeat across the declared weather/terrain extremes, not a roofed stage. Event conservation and identity accounting must match exactly (no duplicated/lost pawn, node or reward); categorical route/exposure decisions must match M1/M3. Predeclare a calibration tolerance of 10% for aggregate occupied/cleared-area and work-hour predictions on deterministic replay, with stochastic traces enclosed by the model's declared prediction intervals. Failure near a regime boundary invalidates that regime's prediction even inside tolerance. Those tolerances are ours and attackable, never owner bars.

Produces: time-lapse local maps of trees, silk, shade, fire and reachable hunting; area/population/work/cargo timelines; survival/maintenance regime map; worst case and event chain per bar, especially owner flood, prey desert, nest stall and repeated-error storm. Rough size [estimate]: local grids at supported map resolution, about a dozen calibrated starting maps and hundreds of coarse event-driven runs; retain original ticks for boundary replay. There is no credible cheap offline sound/appearance verdict: B1/B2 still need looking/listening. B8 still needs actual full-mod season play with natural clocks.

Trigger: build immediately after M1/M3/M4 calibration because aggregate coexistence is unknown. Also rebuild when a constant is changed twice to move a check, when any full-map live reading floods/empties/stalls, or when growth/weather/spawner defaults change. Decision changed: tune declared settings/checks, limit an implementation feedback loop, revisit the guardian budget or job scheduling, or revisit scope. Do not repair a flood by disabling a retained mechanic during proof.
