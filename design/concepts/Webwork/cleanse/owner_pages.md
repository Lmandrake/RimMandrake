# Page one — what Webwork now is

The Webwork is a quiet green jungle threaded with white silk, with its water held inside its vegetation. Taking its silk or eggs tells the untameable ollathrix where you are, and the web becomes a hunting ground. You prepare and guard a clearing and a return route, because regrowth and weather can take that protection away.

Land: buildable jungle, canopy and internal water, substantial cables, finer strands, carpet at spider sites, ordinary weather and drifting webfall.

Life: existing invented flora and fauna, ordinary usable forage/products, the hunting ollathrix, web-cutting quarrok, egg-seeking vennick and flying sivvern; living nests and inert perishable eggs.

Signature: touching or robbing the web brings hunters; maintaining sunlit ground breaks their approach as the canopy returns.

The successful expedition must fund substantial colony progress, as your later answer requires. Preparing the escape matters. Neither ordinary loot nor a lucky improvised retreat is the intended experience. These requirements stay without pinning yields or prices.

The runner-up was the neighbouring spider siege. It is a separate incident system and moves later; the home biome works without it.
