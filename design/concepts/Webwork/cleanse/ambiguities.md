The fresh reviewer’s refused points are accepted; these are Designer repairs, not owner answers:

- **“Together”: valid jobs versus actual convergence.** A job-only trace can pass while owners queue forever. B3 is now live-only for convergence: T40 records every responding owner’s position, paths, reservations and retries from acquisition to contact/hold/cancel. Separate usable approaches must be used where present; a single-crossing control must show progress, reroute or release rather than a queue past the next completed retry. M1/M3 settle lifecycle and individual geometry only. This changes the live-only count, not the promise or capability scope.
- **“Live listening”: an unnamed pipeline agent versus a human who can hear.** B2 names the owner at a staged save as listener. A real thrum under a loud rainforest chorus must fail the separate control despite identical event attribution. Until the owner listens the acoustic result is UNMEASURED; keep the save and restage later, without holding a game session. This follows the November 16 owner-look ruling and does not ask him to predict a measurement.
- **“Production hunter”: one custom consumer versus every movement source.** The term now covers every R4 consumer, including mental-state and lord-duty movement; T38 cannot pass only the custom hunter. Existing emergent and guardian census rows name that integration work. No kept mechanic is switched off to conceal the crossing loophole.

Final attack: the laziest plausible mod is a quiet textured rainforest, a manually placed nest, instant debug research, and a spawned spider moving only because manhunter was forced. It no longer passes: land reads natural layout, touch reads the actual event-to-job chain, refuge reads production exposure/path choice and growth, tools read autonomous consumers, and the whole run forbids staged replacements. The remaining risk is not another unanswered adjective; it is calibration and aggregate behaviour, explicitly unknown in the meetings and models.
