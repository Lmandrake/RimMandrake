# Webwork — proposed Design Version 0.1.0

Designer decision, 2026-11-17; not an owner approval or a change to his words. Nothing here applies itself. The owner hold stays. No game, bridge, deployment, art, or source change was made.

The Webwork is a quiet green jungle threaded with white silk, with its water held inside its vegetation. Taking its silk or eggs tells the untameable ollathrix where you are, and the web becomes a hunting ground. You build in a clearing and keep the canopy back, because regrowth and weather can take that protection away.

**Land:** a buildable, waterless jungle floor, heavy canopy, main silk cables, local sticky strands, carpet confined to spider sites, ordinary weather and visible webfall, rapid shared-engine vegetation growth.

**Life:** the existing invented plant and animal rosters, with the ollathrix as the predator, quarrok as the web cutter, vennick as the egg finder, and sivvern as a real flier. Nests, inert clutches, ordinary forage, timber, animal products and disease belong to living here. No roster eviction is proposed.

**Signature:** touching or robbing the living web brings its hunters, and maintaining open sunlit ground is how a colony breaks that pursuit as the jungle grows back.

**Runner-up:** the spider siege of a neighbouring colony. It loses because it is another incident system, on another map, with door binding, spinning budgets and rescue clocks; deleting it leaves this biome's land, life and trespass cycle intact. It moves intact to next version, off.

## Why a player reaches for it

For expeditions whose risk can be read and changed, and whose successful return funds substantial colony progress: hear the web, see the hunting route, cut a way out, bring back valuable fabric or perishable eggs. For settlement it offers a maintained clearing rather than a passive wall. Enjoyment is a prediction: learning the signs should turn fear into planning. No bar promises an emotion.

The source experience is the owner's 2026-10-20 sentence, preserved in `design/validation_walks/RimMandrake/Webwork.md`: “So quiet that you know you're in terrible danger. It makes treasure, it eats all, and it's coming for you.” October 22 question cards refine silence, ships, the material economy and the deletion of automatic creep. The frozen September sheet supplies canopy, light-moat and web ecology; later specific rulings override its old lethal-sun, absolute sole-source and border-creep language. All further specifications below are Designer defaults, explicitly delegated from those sources.

## Using it

First hour: a foot party hears its steps and silk rather than a rainforest animal chorus. It can see trunks and undergrowth behind the silk: cables span between trees, thinner strands occupy local approaches, carpet identifies sites. Open terrain can be built on normally. The world map's absent river is not a hidden local lake. A naturally placed nest and accessible clutches exist on a supported inhabited Webwork map. A landing ship causes a readable hostile response even if its landing footprint is bare.

Routine: harvest ordinary plants and hunt the thin resident wildlife using vanilla work and inventory. Deliberately deconstruct a cable or raid a clutch for material; do not need a lance, specimen, quest, scanner or reading job. A new touch, including a clutch crossing or successful deliberate clutch mining, makes a thrum and an inspectable felt mark; awake hunters acquire the marked target and approach through shade. The mouth's binding spit creates a medical problem, not unexplained deletion. Retreat by a prepared, guarded return route toward genuinely exposed ground or burn the approach; the November 10 escape answer says improvising retreat is unreliable. Inspect the canopy and weather before trusting yesterday's refuge.

At 40 colonists the same map has more contacts, not a multiplying incident per colonist. Detection coalesces repeated contacts on a pawn. Hunters converge using available shaded approaches, not an indefinite queue at a blocked crossing or independent permanently queued jobs for every mark. The November 10 answer says existing ollathrix hunting together are enough and their coordination must be convincing; no additional caste is added. One hunt can be reassigned when its target dies, leaves the map, becomes unreachable or loses the mark. A crowded egg store is not a map-wide alarm; carrying eggs is. Burning denies harvestable silk rather than printing it. The colony can keep a compound and work routes clear with ordinary cut/burn jobs. The definition does not promise that every colonist can forage unattended.

## What changes in the colony's story

“We found valuable cloth” becomes “we cut the line and were followed home.” Clearing work acquires a purpose beyond room space. A quarrok can remove a sensing crossing without making a tame guard; vennick can show a real nest rather than decorative insect traffic. Losing the last living ollathrix changes the nest's future, not the egg item into a new spider. There is no truce, tame owner, talking spider or spontaneously advancing border.

## Costs

Heat in exposed ground, normal jungle disease, movement through vegetation and silk, tending of bound pawns, maintenance of cleared ground, and the opportunity cost of carrying perishable cargo. Direct Sun feeds vanilla felt temperature and heatstroke; ambient air is vanilla. No new biome heat number, radiation damage or second sun model. Spiders' species-specific sun-scald is a physiological response to the shared exposure geometry, never an additional source of heat.

The ordinary material is still vanilla `Hyperweave`, labelled thrixweave, with its normal material uses. Local deliberate harvest and butchery provide it. The already ruled rare, expensive trader resale stays: this is a necessary exception to the restriction, not a new trader economy. Eggs are inert, perishable cargo, not food, medicine or a tame-owner hatch path. The free-tier sale route is an ordinary orbital trader that accepts Sellable Items, as specified by September nest §4b, without a campaign quest. The actual buyer kind and receipt must be identified during M4 calibration; a generic tradeability flag is not proof. The November 10 owner answers (`ANSWERS_OWNER.md` beside the rehearsal record) choose a major windfall, stronger than the earlier draft's merely noticeable equipment gain. A serious successful trip must fund new usable crafting capacity or a coherent equipment refit, beyond replacing its own expedition supplies. For an early colony that can be a new working workshop; for an established colony a squad equipment refit or major workshop expansion. Predeclare that next improvement; a token stack does not qualify. Choose the improvement and record starting needs before testing, then trace what the recovered material or sale actually buys. This comparative promise is kept; exact yields, prices, rot durations and spawn weights are checks/tuning, not bars.

## When it goes wrong, and the readable sign

Touch: thrum once on acquisition, visible mark and source/expiry information, then hunters visibly change jobs. A repeated scan does not restart a chorus. Mark-directed pursuit expires; ordinary visible-prey hunting can still continue by vanilla rules, so expiry is not pacification or a truce. Egg carry sustains a mark; dropping eggs leaves ordinary expiry from the last refresh. Include inventory, hands carrying a haul, and caravan carriers once spawned on a local map; shelves, ground, corpses and off-map transport do not acquire pawn marks. Transfer refreshes the new carrier, not a dead pawn. No world-caravan combat simulation is added.

Hunter: a dormant animal must have a visible debris concealment/tell before a burst and become a normal targetable animal when it wakes. Mouth binding, wounds and venom have named health entries, tending and ordinary death/corpse signs. Droids are preferred among otherwise equally reachable valid prey only when the campaign's generic flesh-type preference is supplied; unreachability and sunlight refusal win over preference. No RM def names a Star Wars flesh type. One race and kind; no giant caste or hatchling population.

Clearing: exposed cells repel voluntary hunting routes; an exposed spider already forced there can move back to shade and accumulates crippling scald, never downing solely from that hediff. Clearings protect against owners' voluntary entry, not ranged spit from a reachable shaded edge, unrelated raids, fire or hot air. A roof or parasol protects a colonist from Direct Sun, but roof shade can also admit a spider. A roofed room inside the compound still needs ordinary doors and walls. Lamps never make a light-moat. Overcast protects spiders only if the shared exposure predicate actually changes; webfall's silk overlay does not itself darken the sun. Clearing safety is conditional, not a permanent terrain flag.

Loss of protection: canopy growth visibly returns shade to an unmaintained gap; altered weather changes the exposure read. Show the same shade/exposure read to player and hunter, using existing shared inspection if possible. If tree-shade calibration fails, this signature is not demonstrated: do not pass by replacing trees with roofs or switching Direct Sun off. Respect the standing tree-heavy-map fallback for Direct Sun, but report the resulting Webwork evidence gap rather than inventing a local heat exception.

## Edges and settled transition contract

These are construction rules, attackable if the bars disagree; see `bars.json`.

* Web sense uses registered occupied cells, not an invisible radius around every drawing. Overlapping nodes retain reference counts. The clutch must register its occupied cells and mining must ring the miner; the current clutch def lacks that comp, so this is an explicit integration ticket. The nest wall still needs no extra SenseWeb-node claim: its guardian wake is separate. Connections must be truthful where silk is drawn; visual bridges over rock do not create a walked sensing cell. Nearby in the old sheet is operationally the awake, shade-reachable owners on the loaded local map, not adjacent world tiles. No safe-by-label canopy zone is generated; an actual beetle-cleared gap can be blind while still exposed to ordinary hunting.
* Proposed ordinary felt duration: one game day since last real contact; carried eggs refresh it on the normal scan. Persist expiry and origin; reconstruct node registration from spawned things after load, exactly once. The old missing `RUT_Webwork_FeltMark` and `RUT_Webwork_Egg` soft lookups are broken integration, not working behaviour. Bind the existing service to current content; do not duplicate the shared engine.
* Touch and a ship landing recruit actual extant owners or wake a dormant owner before considering emergency emergence. Landing on a living Webwork produces an observable response within the normal acquisition window (initial check bound: one game hour); a map cleared of owners and nest does not resurrect them merely because a ship lands. That deliberate sterilised-map boundary reconciles guaranteed arrival response with finite life. A still-live dormant nest can answer. Response is an acquisition/approach or visible refusal at the exposure boundary, never only a letter. Landing orders change no world tile.
* Harvest emergence is the existing separately rolled hidden ambusher mechanism. Its initial check values remain those in the defs; require a visible emergence sign and avoid a duplicate response for the same destruction event. Combat destruction, beetle chewing, burning, cleanup and reload are not harvest. Ordinary cuts ring the line even when emergence does not roll. The full-map model must expose whether repeated cuts generate a predator flood.
* Quarrok choose reachable chewable nodes and their destruction deregisters only the destroyed node; it grants no player silk. No new lure item, tame combat order or beetle steering UI. A player can exploit a naturally cut route; guaranteed directed herding moves later. Vennick seek an existing reachable clutch along actual silk when such a route exists, eat from a finite clutch, and fall back to ordinary wandering when none exists. Do not invent a trail to an absent nest. Sivvern fly and retain an ordinary mite diet; a bespoke circling “nest radar” is later.
* Nest placement must search legal space to completion before declaring none, prefer fertile ground, and never overwrite colony structures or pawns. Range: ordinary landable maps with accessible buildable ground; a wholly blocked artificial map is not a supported settlement. Require an inspectable placement refusal there, not silent success. A nest wall is a guardian structure, not a nest-bound mother identity. The September sitting explicitly allows re-lay while any living ollathrix is on the map, including dormant ones; do not reopen that answered choice. Destroyed nest walls do not reappear. Existing nearby clutches prevent further accumulation. The current guardian wall also leaves finite local eggs when destroyed; retain that nest-salvage source, not an off-map reward or new hatch path. Fire/combat exclusion concerns fabric and harvest-emergence, not a new promise about all egg destruction modes. If all owners die, no re-lay; a later natural owner can resume it while the wall remains. Save the due time; no catch-up shower after load. The ruled initial interval is 20–30 days; the whole reading extends past a season until it actually includes a natural re-lay and its no-mother control.
* No automatic reconstruction of cut web is added in this version. Regrowing trees restore shade, not silk. The existing natural nest guardian/spawner must remain population-bounded; it is not a perpetual manhunter dispenser. Its production cadence is an input to the whole-map model, not a guessed assurance.
* “Water is inside” is land/lore plus plant products, not a fluid-network simulation. No surface river or permanent pool is supplied by Webwork; player tanks, spilled water and temporary vanilla rain wetness are outside that ban. Native drinking uses the shared environmental-water ruling wherever water is actually available. No artificial dehydration exception is added to a dry map.

Major retained groups: web placement/carpet/local strands (new maps), nest placement (new maps), sense and hunter response, adhesion and binding, sun-scald, local harvesting/emergence, egg renewal, material-source restriction/resale, hush/thrum and webfall. Every later feature has its own off-by-default, “Next version” control with a named switch-off ticket; startup and save defaults must agree. All-off leaves normal terrain, roster, plants and weather with scripted interactions quiescent. Turning a feature off safely finishes/cancels its jobs and preserves people/items; disabling scald removes its active penalty rather than freezing a cripple forever. A new map is needed to judge generation-only switches. Existing saves keep their objects without ticking disabled jobs or killing occupants; cocoon release belongs to the siege switch-off ticket. No destruction of saved objects is part of this proposal.

## Four stories

**First hour.** A walking scout hears a step, a drip, then silence. Cables cross the undergrowth without turning the ground into a white floor. A fresh contact thrums. An owner leaves cover toward the scout, stops at real sunlight, and remains a visible danger at the edge. The ship alternative wakes an answer before anyone has cut a strand.

**Ordinary week.** The colony cuts a compound margin and hauls ordinary timber. A quarrok removes a crossing, and the route loses its web signal without yielding free cloth. Vennick lead a raiding party to a real clutch. They leave carrying perishable eggs, still marked, and tend the carrier caught by mouth silk. Dropping cargo buys eventual release from pursuit, not instant amnesia.

**Worst day.** Rain and a neglected canopy edge remove part of the light-moat. A hunter enters by the newly shaded route; the medical pawn is carrying stolen eggs. Workers burn the approach and abandon the haul. Fire sacrifices material, the bound pawn has a readable health problem, and nobody silently disappears. A lamp and an arrival letter could not have saved them.

**Season's turn.** A maintained compound still functions while abandoned clearings become jungle. Huntable traces and surviving owners remain, rather than all fauna vanishing or every cut becoming a new siege. The saved mid-season map resumes marks, nodes and nest clocks without duplication. The same actions done in the opposite order still form a legible trespass/retreat cycle. Natural egg renewal may require extending the run beyond the season; it is not forced due to make a demonstration pass.
