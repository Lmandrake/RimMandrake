# Next version, default off

All release dispositions are Designer calls under this brief. Prior approved mechanics retain their design constraints; none is retired here. Species and ordinary life remain. Code/defs stay, with runtime and availability gates. Recommended retirements remain recommendations for the owner.

  Why outside the first version: Timed contaminant-bezoar shearing is a special refinery economy; ordinary taming survives without that output. Switch-off/spec ticket: T1.
