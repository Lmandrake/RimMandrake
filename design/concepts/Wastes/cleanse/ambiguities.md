## Final attack on the proposed minimum

The laziest apparent implementation would retain decorative plants, dose during ash only, put a clean roofed test pad in the center and call that a refuge. It fails quiet-day land danger, rough comparison, stock-bearing roots, the warm preservation branch and the occupied seasonal comparison. Those omissions became explicit in B3–B8 rather than additional tuning bars. The reviewer's further lazy builds use the vanilla pollution overlay to bypass decorative radmoss, overwrite cleanup from a stale projection, preserve wastepacks through an unrelated rot rule, supply only player-dropped finds or claim uniform sky colours as a directional horizon. Revised route controls, outside-write accounting, real dissolution, the entry-hoard manifest and the absent horizon-binding ticket now expose each failure. The opposite lazy implementation would let the plants clear every polluted cell and indefinitely sustain all native creatures from scripted food; the stock and food controls reject that too.

No real measurement is required from the owner. No unresolved question has evidence against the stated default strong enough to justify asking him to decide it now. The unknowns are empirical: complete-map viability, rendered cue legibility, current preservation and engine adapters. They are the first instrument work, not hidden waivers.
