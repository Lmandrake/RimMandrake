# Pyrelands, Designer turn 6 (stage 2: argue the edge)

Run `rm-role-designer-1793981883`, 2026-11-06. One question: the Pyrelands sheet and the built mod disagree about worlds
other than your Ash'karr save, and your words do not settle which one is right.

**Claim.** The sheet should say what the mod does on a world someone generates themselves, because the mod already does
something there.

**Attack.** The sheet's "does not" list says "No worldgen of any kind", taken from your ruling of 2026-08-15 (no worldgen
feature in any version; players receive the one frozen world). But the mod ships a Mod Settings checkbox,
"Pyrelands can generate on new worlds", on by default and labelled WORLDGEN-AFFECTING (as your 2026-09-12 settings
ruling asks of such toggles). With it on, vanilla RimWorld's own world generator can place Pyrelands tiles on a brand-new
world (hot grass savanna tiles, about 33 °C and up, 550-1000 mm rain). We wrote none of that generator; the biome only
tells vanilla where it fits. Either the sheet's line is false, or the switch's job is outside what the Pyrelands promise.

> says the mod makes no promise about any world but yours, and nothing checks the switch.
