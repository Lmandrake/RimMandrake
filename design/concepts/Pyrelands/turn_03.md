## 1. Black rain on a burn scar (`a_blackrain_compound`)

Today a fresh burn makes the ash 50 points richer for 6 days. Black rain keeps the ground wet for the whole rain and about a day after. That lifts deep ash from 40% to 140% fertility, but the rain also wipes the burn bonus within about half a day. So a rained-on fresh scar reads **140%**.

The cheapest "compound" is to stop the wipe. The same scar then reads **190%** while wet (watered plus burn), and the bonus fades on its own 6-day clock.

**Is that what you meant, or should black rain do more on a burn scar** (stretch the bonus while wet, or add a further bonus of its own)?

## 2. What a firebreak must do (`a_fire_control_scene`)

The firebreak is a floor your colonists build (2 chemfuel a cell, after Firefoam research). It is as wide as you paint it, and fire will not burn on it.

Picture the walking burn line reaching a firebreak you laid around a field. What must you see to trust it?

- **(a)** A line two or three cells wide always stops the ground fire. Only a fire-hawk's dropped twig ever gets across, and a colonist with the firefoam sprayer puts it out. The biome never starts a new fire inside the ring.
- **(b)** It usually holds. The odd spark gets across, and you send someone out with the sprayer.

My recommendation is **(a)**. "Usually holds" turns every passing burn into a chore, which reads as punishment, not solace.
