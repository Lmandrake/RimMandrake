## Second attack: the laziest remaining build

A named barren map, three working proof helpers and a high dial is not enough. It could keep indoor comfort irrelevant to insulation, emerge through a floor at expiry, report seeking without moving, reveal later cracks through a hidden arrow, preserve every isolated test while accumulating a hidden army, and become entirely silent after reload. Those attacks moved the definition: the explicit exposure proxy and its calibration; per-cell release/movement validation; metadata-aware warning; capacity reservations with identity accounting; and the all-on saved season with preparation order reversed. The bar pictures now reject those builds.

The strongest residual objections are measurable. The proxy may inadequately represent the cold exterior; actual path execution may disagree with its planner; the bounded population/repair/work feedback may still flood the colony. These are the three unknown shared meetings in bars.json, with M3, M2 and M4 respectively. No owner answer could substitute for those observations. No other unresolved scope choice requires a question.
