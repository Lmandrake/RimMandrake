# Instruments specified, not built or measured on this pass

Build in this order. A model predicts only under its stated assumptions; even production-kernel reuse is not a measurement of the game. Every proposed tolerance below is a Designer calibration criterion, not an existing agreement. No tool may generate alternative planets. Inputs are disposable local-map fixtures and records of the fixed world; the source situation generator creates cases, not worlds. Default bar readings keep every retained mechanic active; feature-off cases verify the inherited settings floor separately.

## M1: Situation and lifecycle generator

**Question.** Can every declared heat/breach/animal/settings/save transition produce a state that the bars' own observers can distinguish from the contrasting wrong build?

**Informs.** B2–B8, R3–R7,R9–R12, the warning-state argued meeting and inputs to the three unknown meetings (ice-boundary, thermal-pressure, colony-cycle). The generator catches missing regions before a geometry/season model samples them. It is required even though a production math fuzz suite already exists.

**Inputs and sweep.** Read current resolved settings and state vocabulary, the explicit proposed rules and masks exported from full 200/250/300 square maps. Enumerate no camp/cold/warm/exposed/insulated/saturated states; active/off/destroyed/out-of-range/unreachable/tied pushers, fires, warm boundary and downed-living sources; healthy/downed/dead/fighting/wild/externally tamed shivven; untaught/taught with no crack, warned pending, attacked pending, sealed, blocked release, released and cancelled. Cross each source/cell eligibility boundary with master/heat/pursuit/confinement/strike/breach/warning transitions, including off/on before and after expiry and during a Goto/AttackMelee job. Include saved warning flags, cooldown, capacity reservation, source cache invalidation and room split/merge. Include a victim dying, a downed source recovering or leaving its cell, and a floor constructed underneath an existing pawn. Generate ordinary mining, milk, pack and deconstruct orders with finite/blocked inventories. Preserve all current memberships, the cave-lemming waiver and all DLC; use RM-only and campaign-patched compositions as different source-resolution contexts, not DLC-absent tiers.

Each transition is exercised before, at and after its scheduler boundary: think step, hourly dial, hourly breach, crack look, first reminders, expiry, capacity release and construction completion. Include actual serialization/reload at pending, warned, seeking and striking states. Walk both heat→floor and floor→heat, plus salvage→warning response and warning response→salvage. Group pairs only when the same predicate and outcome apply; emit why each grouping is sound. Within a case, run all enabled kept mechanics normally rather than calling a pure method in isolation.

**Assumptions and exclusions.** A finite symbolic transition graph can represent gates and identity custody. It does not establish how clear an audio/visual cue is, realistic temperature transport, tolerable event cadence, or global path completion. It assumes foreign mods do not arbitrarily delete pawns or replace the map; default shared engines are included. Feature-off paths are not default bar evidence. A simulated reload flag reset is not accepted as a real save load.

**Real-game calibration before belief.** Export actual component fields, pawn ids/jobs/needs/occupied cells, room clusters, letter text plus target metadata and settings before/after the corresponding ordinary game transitions. Reload real disposable saves for serialization cases. Require exact agreement on identity, taught flags, reserved cohort and terminal outcomes; timing must agree within the observer's existing polling interval, which is recorded (the current crack poll is 60 ticks, source RM_BreachCracks.cs). Events must have a natural or explicitly staged-transition provenance, and staged opens never prove natural breach probability. Test negative controls: destroyed source, sealed opening, cancelled feature and death of a candidate source must change the observation. Existing ProofDestroyStops is not sufficient because it reads before any post-deadline ticking.

**Produces.** A complete situation/transition table, grouping rationale, minimal replay per contrasting bar picture, observer blind spots, unreachable-state list with proofs, and a worst counterexample per bar. Missing calibration data says UNCALIBRATED, never PASS.

**Rough size.** [Guess] Several thousand meaningful cases after predicate grouping, under a hundred thousand expanded combinations; two small offline adapters plus ordinary game state export/replay fixtures. Reuse kernel math and existing fuzz assertions where they truly observe the same state. No new generic test platform is proposed.

**Trigger and changed decision.** Build first for T8: no current instrument covers settings changes, actual serialization, metadata uncertainty and after-deadline stopping together. A counterexample changes a lifecycle/gating rule or observer before a builder tunes cadence; it cannot remove a needed warning or hull bar. Outputs select calibration cases for M2–M4.

## M2: Ice boundary and source geometry

**Question.** Does every supported source/crack/defence arrangement allow legal actual pursuit and legal emergence while constructed protection holds through changes?

**Informs.** B4–B8, R3–R5,R7,R12 and the unknown ice-boundary meeting. M1 supplies transition sequences, M3 supplies source strengths, M4 consumes its route and eligibility results.

**Inputs and sweep.** Actual terrain/standability/roof/floor/hull/edifice/fog masks, source footprints and rotations, sensing boundary and live pawn locations. Enumerate every translated location and valid rotation of each loaded candidate footprint on each exported map; sweep cardinal/diagonal bearings, legal strike adjacency, distance just inside/at/outside sensing radii10–150 and full-map disconnected regions. Enumerate tiny corner arrangements exhaustively, then lift them into full-map rings, concave and U-shaped floors, corridors, bridges of Ice, door openings, obstructions, map borders and competing sources. For the current source/camp perimeter rule include nearest-building distance just below/at/above3 and12 cells as regression boundaries, without prescribing those distances as an experience. Test large source footprints obtained from resolved defs, not an invented maximum. Cover all candidate emergence cells around an opening and occupancy conflicts; zero legal slots must block release safely. Change floor/hull/roof/source state before/during/after path execution and release, including multiple release animals occupying available cells. Include real path searches exceeding the current6000-cell search budget and detours whose first moves increase distance to the heat source. Examine a sealed strong source plus a reachable weak source and a remote strong source plus a local weak source in the same map.

**Assumptions and exclusions.** Ice cell eligibility is exactly the shared swim-terrain query and resolved game geometry. Connectivity can prove a route exists; it cannot prove vanilla follows the proposed multi-cell Goto endpoint. Therefore two readings are mandatory: an independent flood-fill oracle for reachability and recorded actual pawn steps to calibrate the executor. An abstraction based only on planned endpoints is not credible. It does not infer sound visibility, heat flux, combat balance or the season's event rate. It does not vary planet geometry or create new worlds.

**Real-game calibration before belief.** Export the actual masks and compare the offline legality verdict at every read placement. Execute the chosen route cases as normal Goto/attack jobs with every kept feature on, recording every occupied cell and planned leg; compare legal release cohorts from ordinary expiry and sealed branches. Require exact source eligibility, actual-cell legality, absence of corner cuts and completion whenever the independent oracle finds a supported route; no permitted tolerance for entering a floor/hull or spawning there. The calibrated leg executor may allow normal job scheduling delay, explicitly reported; repeated zero progress with a reachable target is disagreement. RandomClosewalkCellNear success without eligibility validation is not a positive control. At least one unsealed release must actually produce animals, ruling out a universal spawn suppressor.

**Produces.** Maps of legal ice, source eligibility and actual routes; a counterexample image/table for every mismatch; worst detour length/progress/stall per topology and footprint. State whether the consumer's leg size/path execution needs correction. No numerical route speed is a bar.

**Rough size.** [Guess] Tens of thousands of small masks plus translated checks over up to90000 cells per local map; reusable pure-grid adapter and narrowly instrumented actual path recorder. Geometry-state reading, not an expensive live visual sweep, does most work.

**Trigger and changed decision.** Build before fixing T6 because the ice-boundary meeting is unknown and the current code separates a safe BFS from vanilla execution. Any successful floor/hull penetration or reachable-target stall changes release validation, source filtering or path-leg rule. If a path budget must change repeatedly to move a check, run the sweep again rather than raising it by habit. A constant changed twice is a mandatory rebuild trigger.

## M3: Exterior heat and pressure

**Question.** Does the explicit exposure proxy let a comfortable cold-exterior refuge reduce breach pressure without making exposed warmth irrelevant or forcing saturation in ordinary use?

**Informs.** B4,B6,B8; R2,R3,R9,R12 and the unknown thermal-pressure meeting. M1 defines lifecycle cohorts, M2 supplies actual geometry, M4 uses calibrated pressure histories.

**Inputs and sweep.** Read the complete formula in definition.md as a candidate, including current kernel target/easing, source group weights and proposed transmission coefficients. Import room topology and actual temperatures, wall thickness, open/closed doors, roofs, nominal duty histories, fire sizes, consumer draw and geometry from game records. Sweep outdoor stress temperatures -80 through -20 C, stable indoor0/10/21 C as fixture inputs, source duties0–100 percent, current sensitivity25–600 and frequency scale0.1–4. Room shapes include compact, elongated, nested clusters and distributed workrooms at traveller1–6 and colony40 scale. Pair equal-indoor-comfort rooms with open/closed exterior and wall depth1/2; expose, extinguish, switch off, destroy, reopen or reconstruct sources. Include a continuing warm room after source-off, fire-only camps and machines with power draw but no heat pusher. Sweep below/near/above breach threshold and both pressure and capacity saturation. Track de-duplication of nominal indoor heat and warm-room volume; no room emits each estimate as unrelated extra heat.

**Assumptions and exclusions.** The proxy is explicitly a Designer sensor approximation and need not predict real thermodynamics. Vanilla still owns air temperatures; models do not change them. A warm-room boundary can be an approach target without pretending it is a melee object. Downed living pawns have a weak local signal for existing shivven, not a base population/count trigger; death stops that candidate. No biochemical heat simulation, new material-insulation system, calving, radiation storm or per-biome Direct Sun. The model cannot settle the visual readability of an alert or the human tolerability of the whole game.

**Real-game calibration before belief.** Use normal vanilla heat/room updates in paired actual room fixtures at matched indoor comfort; record input duty, indoor/outdoor temperatures, topology changes and the resulting proxy/dial components over heating and cooldown. The candidate's formula applied to exported input should agree with live pressure readings to a declared absolute dial tolerance0.02 and correct event-gate boundaries; temperature evolution is imported, not fabricated by the proxy. In addition, observe the vanilla cooling/heater-duty ordering of the equal-comfort insulating fixtures: the better protected room must actually hold heat better or require less heating, and the proxy must order its outside exposure lower. Exact formula agreement alone would be circular and is insufficient. Record any fixture that cannot hold equal comfort. Require consistent source/dial ordering on held-out room shapes and unchanged signs after door/floor changes/reload; allow no inversion hidden in an average. For event counts use paired natural rolls and enough cohorts for their uncertainty intervals to separate the pressure regimes where the bar requires comparison. Current ProofDial snapping and a fire contribution are not this calibration.

**Produces.** Tables of room input, exported thermal exposure, predicted/live dial and natural breach pressure; equal-comfort comparison plots; saturation maps; worst ordering inversion and slowest recovery. Label empirical temperature/duty records separately from model predictions. Publish parameters and calibration fingerprints with every plot.

**Rough size.** [Guess] A few dozen calibrated room/source arrangements and a few thousand offline traces. Candidate calculations are small; acquiring stable actual heat histories is the cost. No credible completely offline replacement exists for those initial vanilla room histories.

**Trigger and changed decision.** Build for T10 before relying on the new insulation definition or any monotonic breach claim. An unknown thermal-pressure meeting, a first failed insulation scene, or a coefficient retuned twice requires it. A failed proxy changes R2's aggregation/transmission rule, not the standing cold-exterior promise. Saturation maps change scales or the source presentation; no new biome heat kind is an allowed repair.

## M4: Whole plateau season

**Question.** Does the complete default plateau remain a usable crossing and costly supplied refuge over a season, with readable incursions and useful work instead of a flood, resource desert, stall or error storm?

**Informs.** Whole bar B8 and its B1–B7 components; all rules; unknown colony-cycle meeting, and the thermal-pressure/ice-boundary meetings after their own calibration. This is the test for loops and three-way interactions, not just pairwise compatibility.

**Inputs and sweep.** Full map terrain and placed-object state at200/250/300 sizes, the B1 hill/topology regimes, current resolved memberships including patched visitors, livestock and hessarund, Frozen wreck field, shared-engine setting snapshot, calibrated room/source/route outputs and seed/event histories. Run15 in-game days, from both new-camp and day100 saved starting contexts, at traveller1–6 and colony40 scale. Supply food/feed/fuel for the intended actions, with recorded finite stocks. Policies are: ordinary crossing and selected salvage; exposed warm work camp; cold operation; equal-comfort insulated/floored refuge; deliberate ignored and stopped breaches; withdrawal and repair. Include external vanilla raids/ordinary visitor/death scenarios as declared input histories rather than relabelling them heat incidents. Preserve natural weather and fixed geometry; ordinary aurora is on its actual scheduler, not a locked screenshot preset. Include main actions in the second order and reload a midpoint save while pending or seeking. Retain explicit naturally quiet trajectories. Trace single opening/reservations, pressure-saturated and capacity-saturated states, deaths/ordinary departures and no unexplained disappearances. Deeps host entrance and optional scanner data coexist at their defaults; an optional entrance is not an obligatory new cave ecology simulation owned here.

**Assumptions and exclusions.** Job labour and combat service-time estimates start from real recorded ordinary game jobs, not invented instant repair or an infinite defender. Supply bookkeeping includes hunger, fuel use and job interruptions. The model can replay a fixed external threat history; it does not claim to simulate arbitrary player tactics, enjoyment, every raid faction or all of Lantern Deeps. Default enabled engine effects on the surface are included, even if their expected contribution is zero. Changing only the wall-clock playback speed is valid compression; skipping normal weather/room/AI/event/needs ticks is not. State-reading absence or a crashed recorder is UNMEASURED, never a quiet successful season.

**Real-game calibration before belief.** Record actual ordinary complete season traces in the declared source/geometry/colony regimes after M1–M3 agreement. Compare event eligibility, source/dial histories, opening/outcome ordering, pawn identity ledger, finite salvage provenance, completed/interrupted job categories, supplies and logs. Identity, prohibition and lifecycle transitions must agree exactly; saved continuations must preserve active deadlines/taught history and outcomes, with source caches recomputed. For stochastic traces use the exported actual roll history for exact replay, then held-out seed cohorts for uncertainty/shape comparison; do not require an exact random count from unrelated RNG streams. Proposed initial service-model tolerance is10 percent per ordinary job-category completion total over a matched season and no misclassification between work-with-interventions, continuous intervention and no signature execution. Failure of those thresholds means the model is not yet trusted. Perceptual B1/B7 samples come from these same all-on runs, not a calm separate sky/AI-off scene.

**Produces.** A season timeline, heat/pressure/work/animal-capacity and resource plots, final preserved/worked map, causal event ledger, reload/action-order divergence report, error-log signature counts, and worst case per bar. Flood means sustained intervention/population accumulation displaces ordinary work; resource desert means unworked preserved opportunities or the plateau's land/life identity disappear; stall means legal reachable actions repeatedly make no progress or the signature never executes in eligible warm cohorts; error storm means repeated runtime exceptions/log errors under normal retained operation. Numerical warning/work/capacity counts diagnose these pictures and never become new passing bars by themselves.

**Rough size.** [Guess] Dozens of actual calibration cohorts and hundreds to low thousands of offline seasons, depending on event uncertainty; each is a full-map state evolution, with representative policy/task queues rather than full RimWorld emulation. Reuse existing trace exports. If that reduced labour/heat model cannot reproduce held-out actual traces, say no credible offline surrogate yet and use ordinary game-ticked season records; do not rename the emulator's output a measurement.

**Trigger and changed decision.** The unknown colony-cycle meeting requires this before acceptance. Also build/rerun after the first full-map failure or repeated tuning of heat/cooldown/population to move checks. The result changes cadence/scale/capacity/path/work rules or reveals a bar failing. It cannot be solved by disabling a retained mechanic, forcing clear skies, adding the larder to dispose of excess animals, or dropping the whole bar.
