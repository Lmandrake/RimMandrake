# The Sleeping Ice — proposed Design Version 0.1.0

All choices labelled delegated here are the Designer's. This is a proposal, not an owner approval, changed ruling, applied feature switch or gameplay acceptance. The existing hold and normal fresh-review/application process remain in force. Nothing outside this folder is changed.

A traveller crosses dirty-white upland ice beneath a cold sky, passing frozen wrecks and ridges that do not look alive. Stopping brings useful salvage and a place to shelter, but the warmth escaping the camp draws warned breaches and blind shivven travelling inside the ice. A prepared refuge can endure by protecting its warmth and its perimeter, while a supplied party can take its haul and move on.

Land is hard impure white ice, open expanses, low geological ridges and still-air hoarfrost, ordinary cold overhead sky light and preserved failed expeditions. Life is the admitted scarce visitors, imported milk and cargo animals, the behaviourless mineable hessarund and concealed shivven; there is no plant or grazing economy. The signature is: **Warmth exposed to the ice raises pressure, opening warned attackable breaches whose shivven approach reachable warmth under the ice and can be stopped by cold, constructed protection or fighting.**

Runner-up: the sohl's single lifetime movement and permanently spent body. It loses because it creates its own trigger, reveal, movement, reproduction and aftermath, none of which is required for warmth, breach and defence to work. It remains admitted future content, switched off, with the rest of its lineage. This is a version boundary, not an eviction or a claim that the owner never wanted it.

## Why a player reaches for it

The frozen plateau is an expedition bargain: useful preserved material on land that supplies neither pasture nor a comfortable campsite. Heating decisions affect trouble. Ordinary vanilla ice-sheet survival already makes food, cold and shelter costly; this biome adds a readable consequence of making a stop comfortable. Its sources cite Anomaly pit-gate warning tiers and a siege-burrow staging object as shapes for that decision, not proof of current fidelity or claims of unique prior art. Blue Desert's chemical basins, Lantern Deeps' luminous caves and the propane country's fuel life remain distinct places. This plateau does not import their chemistry to manufacture content.

The intended enjoyment is a prediction: a quiet crossing interrupted by a chosen salvage stop should make a visible approaching crack matter more than another scheduled raid. The bars require the cause, choice, defence and readable outcome; they do not certify enjoyment.

## Using it: first hour, routine and a large refuge

First hour: arrive supplied, recognize bare hard white ice, no native forage, and choose a route or wreck. Shelter, clothing, fuel and food are ordinary RimWorld responsibilities. A camp may start cold and see no breach. The dial explains exposed warmth once there is enough to matter; the first natural crack, whenever it happens, teaches the consequence and points to its attackable object. It is not a guaranteed arrival event.

Routine: mine the hessarund or deconstruct a frozen hull, tank or expedition rig using ordinary jobs. Haul the usable material; the field is finite and does not regrow. Hessarund is a mineable ridge with catalyst crust, no thoughts, trigger, bargaining, displacement or new research. Hoarfrost is non-plant scenery with ordinary obstruction if its actual footprint requires it, never an invisible hazard or harvest. Crossing can remain the whole visit. Existing Lantern Deeps entrances, when that neighbour scatters them under its settings, provide the separate route below and back; no rescue warren is implied by an echo or a cave entrance.

At 40 colonists [declared scenario, not a measured colony], a lasting supplied refuge is valid, following the owner's recorded 2026-11-10 answer. Warm workrooms, insulated exteriors and constructed floors make ongoing work possible; exposed salvage and repairs still cost time outside. There must be useful work between interventions. A permanently comfortable, unprotected camp should draw more pressure than a prepared cold-exterior refuge. It need not lose a particular worker or suffer an attack on a fixed day. Warmth can attract shivven while a fully protected footprint prevents legal emergence; there is no promise that every heated camp gets a crack.

Life remains sparse, rather than being quietly replaced by an arctic zoo. Zhissa is an admitted milk grazer; keep ordinary milk, breeding and physical defence but move its independent electric-shock debuff to next version. It needs supplied feed because the ground grows none. Tellurox remains owned imported pack livestock with an ordinary permanent shell butcher product, not a newly added wild herd. Its shell continues as an ordinary stuff independently of the proposed switched-off hexplate floor. No new trading system or renewable shell-shearing job is introduced. Existing campaign visitors keep their memberships and conditions; visitors that die leave ordinary bodies. The cave-lemming population-ratio exception is already answered and never reopened. The roster's mahllik name is an admission record, not grounds to invent an additional current wild def. T28 checks its existing identity mapping before anyone claims it is present or missing.

## What changes in the colony's story

The same heater is comfort and an announced stop. The first crack is a physical object the player can find and destroy before it opens; stopping it changes the deadline's outcome. Ignoring it brings animals from legal ice cells. Later warnings preserve uncertainty; they do not secretly carry a map arrow. The animal travels concealed with a moving surface seam and rumble, surfaces to strike, and is accounted for when it stops, is hurt or dies. The surface is still the plateau after a month of use: finite worked finds, imported supplies, quiet stretches and a managed perimeter, rather than farmland or continuous monster manufacture.

“Frozen highway” and “nobody stays” in the owner's experience are figurative descriptions of the usual journey. They require neither a built road strip nor an anti-settlement mechanic. The later owner answer explicitly permits a lasting refuge. Crossing, mining/recovery and the neighbour's descent remain ways to meet the place; first version does not pretend to implement its own abducted-pawn rescue.

## Costs

Food and animal feed, fuel/power, cold protection, shelter/floors, salvage/mining labour and perimeter attention are the costs. Heat pressure is a sensor of exposure, not a second temperature, damage type or sun. Floors/hull stop emergence and transit; they need not prevent a shivven on adjacent bare ice from striking an exposed edge building. Cold reduces future pressure rather than deleting surviving animals or cancelling a pending warning by itself. An ignored crack remains a physical deadline until attacked, sealed or cancelled by an explicit setting change.

Salvage uses Wreckage's family/Frozen weathering results. No yield, market value or variant count is a passing bar. Density must leave open crossing ground and optional work rather than furnishing every cell; that is observed jointly with field distribution, not prescribed as a new numerical quota. Mining the ridge yields the named ordinary catalyst resource as building stuff accepted by existing stone-stuff structures and crafting filters; completing an ordinary wall with it is the usable-output control. No research unlock or material-dependent wall insulation is promised for that resource.

## When it goes wrong and the readable sign

Exposure pushes the thermal trend up; the readout shows contributing source groups and whether pressure is saturated by existing shivven capacity. The first warning identifies warmth, the crack and its exact countdown, permitting recall from the ordinary working radius and a response. Default first warning time remains the current provisional 24 game hours [source: RM_SleepingIceSettings.breachFirstCountdownHours]; later internal times remain 8–16 hours [source: RM_BreachLoop.TryOpen]. Later notification text, inspect and metadata do not give an exact time or direction. An unaided perimeter search or intentional scanner pulse can discover a crack's actual position.

Destroying a crack before expiry stops that opening. Construction of a floor, hull or roof over it visibly seals it; remove the staging object with a sealed message/sign, release its reserved capacity and retain the ordinary cooldown. Subsequent construction removal cannot revive it. If the opening stays legal but no legal emergence cell is available at expiry, resolve it as blocked with an account and release no animal; never spill into neighbouring floor or hull. Do not relocate an old deadline. A fresh future crack must satisfy a fresh eligibility roll and normal warning rules. Construction and expiry on the same tick use the real ordering: construction completed before release validation protects; completion after a valid spawn cannot undo that animal. Actual spawn placement is revalidated per animal.

Animals take legal ice routes, including around concave floors; a correct planned endpoint with an endlessly rejected vanilla shortcut is a failed defence/pursuit experience. If terrain changes under an existing shivven, surface it visibly, stop owned travel and leave it accounted for on that cell until a legal ice route is available; do not teleport it across the shelter. Off-ice debug or foreign forced spawns are recovery cases, never evidence of allowed emergence. Ice confinement also constrains surfaced retaliation: damage or combat jobs cannot create a hull shortcut.

A source enclosed away from any reachable ice strike cell loses eligibility. Local reachable warmth wins over stronger unreachable or out-of-range warmth, as the owner answered on 2026-11-10. With no eligible source the shivven waits on its actual ice, with an inspectable state and a last-known moving/stationary seam; it neither targets a fictitious source nor disappears. A source switched off or destroyed is invalidated; the animal replans or waits. A swallowed colonist, cut-down recovery, frozen-person theft or nest rescue is not implemented by a missing-pawn letter. The first version leaves casualties and their carried gear in the ordinary surface map, with the shared readable kill sign where applicable.

## Implementation choices: explicit, attackable rules

These are Designer defaults, not measurements or new owner rulings. They supply a concrete build target; models can show that a different rule is needed to hold the bars. The kernel's current numeric tuning remains a regression baseline until measured interaction requires retuning.

Pressure sampling uses a deterministic **exterior transmission proxy**, not a claim to simulate heat transport accurately. For each enclosed warm room, count exposed open boundaries O, closed doors D, wall faces W and roofed cells A. A wall face contributes 1/t, where t is the contiguous solid wall thickness from that face toward outdoor space; a wall between indoor rooms is not an exterior face. Open doors are O; a closed outside door is D. Room splits/merges recompute these quantities from actual topology. Use F = clamp((O + 0.25D + 0.1Σ(1/t) + 0.05A)/(O + D + W + A),0,1), with empty denominator meaning F=1. A room using outdoor temperature has F=1. Count each exposed boundary face once and count roof cells once. This is a cheap sensor weighting; material stats do not supply a new insulation physics. Internal room-to-room boundaries are omitted until the outer enclosing room cluster is reached; for nested rooms treat connected enclosed rooms as one cluster for exterior faces, use their heat sums, and use each room's warm-volume term in that cluster.

For a cluster, H is working player heat-pusher output, Ffire is any fire's size contribution and P is powered player-consumer draw, using current weights 20 for fire size and 0.01 for watts. R is Σ max(room temperature − outdoor temperature,0) × room cells × 0.002. Cluster raw contribution is F × max(H+20Ffire+0.01P,R), **not the sum of both estimates of the same indoor heat**. Exposed outdoor sources use F=1. Unowned fires and warm rooms count as physical exposure; inert unowned machinery adds no nominal colony power. Working player-owned machinery inside a cluster contributes its consumer waste through that cluster, not directly through the wall. Keep the current target 1−exp(−raw/scale) and hourly halfway easing. No source means pressure decays toward zero. Turn a source off and its nominal contribution ends; warm rooms can continue contributing while cooling. Sun never enters as a special SleepingIce term. M3 must check the proxy's ordering and magnitude against real room histories before anyone believes it; its coefficients are rules to attack first, not values the game has measured.

Local source candidates are working pushers, live fires, warm-room exterior boundary cells, and exposed downed living pawns. Healthy walking pawns do not trigger proactive hunts in this version; downed warmth realizes the sheet's lost/dying signal without adding larder custody. A cold dead body is not a heat source. Downed signal exists only while the pawn is living, with current vanilla life/temperature state read rather than an indefinite corpse tag. Already-present shivven can approach downed warmth; body warmth alone does not create the colony breach dial. No living pawn is removed by reaching it. A reachable downed pawn may be attacked on ice; a protected one cannot be reached through flooring. Warm room boundary and fire targets get a legal approach location; fire is never the target of a nonsensical melee attack. A room's target is an exposed reachable heat-pushing building if present, otherwise only an unobstructed open boundary directly connected to reachable bare ice, where the animal waits; it never attacks an abstract room. Wall and closed-door leakage still contributes pressure, but never creates a boundary target that lets a protected room monopolize pursuit of an accessible source. If every boundary is protected there is no local room target, even when the dial is high. Existing retaliation supersedes pursuit while respecting the movement boundary.

Rank candidates within sensing distance with legal reachable approach/strike cells by exposed thermal strength (working pushers use their positive output times room F, fires their weighted size times F, warm boundary cells share that room/cluster raw contribution equally among exposed boundary cells, downed living pawns use a provisional strength of 1). Equal strength breaks ties by persistent thing id, or cell index for a boundary. Settings can retune these weights; no native gains visual, sound or vibration sensing. Refresh source eligibility when a source/room/floor changes, after reload, and at the regular think step; stale cache invalidation cannot wait indefinitely. Need not redesign CreatureBehaviors: supply legal targets and movement legs through its current integration contract. Where the current kit cannot execute that contract, T6 requests a narrow consumer/engine interface correction, not a new shared engine design.

Breach cadence retains the current threshold, dial-squared chance, single pending opening and cooldown as provisional rules, with a new per-map wild-shivven capacity of 24 [Designer tuning default, not measured]. Reserve slots when a crack opens; its release cohort cannot exceed available reserved slots. Count live wild shivven, not just visible ones, plus outstanding reservations. A fully saturated map opens no additional cracks; status explains that state. Death or ordinary accounted departure frees slots. There is no breeding, hidden cull or timer despawn. Waiting living animals remain real pawns under the ice. This capacity prevents unbounded accumulation but does not prove that the resulting stable population, warning rate and labour load are tolerable: M4 is still required.

## Edges

No planet generation, world painting, alternative planets, biome placement toggle or zero-tile finding. Ordinary local colony-map scatter and geometry fixtures are allowed. “Young” means geologically impure/fractured, not an accelerating annual ice-growth system. No wind or falling-weather system is silently added to the interior. Full reconnection storms, margin ablation/rime-fall and drifting Dark are next-version event work; the cold ordinary sky and any existing neighbour aurora stay in land. Where the geometry dependency currently fails to provide aurora at a saved plateau position, T3 must provide ordinary cold-sky presentation without a new heat/radiation/threat storm or moving the world tile. A map cannot pass under a forced Clear/weather lock.

No native plant or self-sustaining pasture. Hydroponics and transported greenhouse farming are not a new biome feature and must not make the ground fertile; no new anti-vanilla-hydroponics patch is proposed. Imported animals and buildings are distinguished from native residents. No liquid map terrain, prefab puddle tolerance or geothermal exception is allowed. Player processing of dirty ice belongs to existing water/resource machinery, not a local melt-pool feature. Native environmental-water drinking ruling is vacuous here because no native environmental liquid exists; do not add water to exercise it.

No abduction, larder, nests, active sohl, thaw ecology, bespoke raid, rite or shell-floor bonus is required to pass. All admitted organisms keep their records and future design; no roster is edited. Deferring a special organism's encounter does not substitute a functionless imitation in first version. The historically passed Breath-Choir, Witness Method, kesshet, Keel-Press, bargaining/creeping hessarund and ridge research are already OUT, not a new retirement pitch and not things to revive next version. These two historical inventory groups are exclusions, not capabilities promised by this proposal, so they are not census rows.

## Settings

Keep the searchable/collapsible screen, per-section reset, master and current major feature switches: heat sampling, alert, pursuit, ice confinement, surfaced strikes, breaches and warnings, with tuning and scope labels. The required scope is local SleepingIce within the framework; add no cross-biome switch. Defaults are the shipped signature with next-version extras off. The existing Wreckage SleepingIce field toggle and salvage controls stay in their engine. Add local new-map-only hessarund and hoarfrost scatter controls; changes never regenerate a planet or remove existing life.

Master off cancels pending cracks visibly, discards reserved release counts, zeroes the owned pressure and ends owned seek/strike jobs. Existing animals are surfaced/accounted for, not destroyed. Heat-sampling off also cancels pending thermal cracks and prevents new ones; pursuit is separately switched, so already released animals may still seek if its setting remains on. Breach off cancels pending cracks but leaves independently enabled pursuit alone. Seek off ends seek jobs; strike off ends owned strike jobs and leaves the animals waiting. Ice-only off is labelled a relaxed movement configuration; it cannot establish the default hull bar. Alert off hides the optional continuous readout, and warnings off removes later notifications and first reminders, while the first teaching letter and physical tell remain. Re-enable does not retell the first warning or release an expired cancelled crack. Shared swim off is an explicit degraded configuration; default evidence requires it on. Keep defs and animal identities even when all mechanics are off.

Add “Next version: zhissa electrical defence” and “Next version: tellurox hexplate”, both default off. T1 decouples electric capacities/maneuvers from ordinary attacks and makes descriptions honest while retaining defs and accepted art. T2 hides floor construction and its custom jobs/hooks while leaving shell butchery and ordinary shell stuff intact. Existing opt-in saves retain their objects; turning a feature off never deletes an animal or floor. Retiring the floor is a recommendation only.

## First hour — a short story

The party can see farther than it can afford to walk unprotected. A frost-locked rig suggests a detour. They carry food and fuel; the pale grazer is not a promise of pasture. They mine a low ridge, build shelter and light a heater. The rising thermal explanation connects comfort to the ice beneath their feet. No staged crack has been scheduled for their arrival.

## An ordinary week — a short story

A taught crack gives the crew time to call a salvager home and break the opening. The next warning is a knock without an arrow. They search the unfloored edge, choose what to work today, and extend protection where their route crosses it. The milk animal eats from stored feed, the pack beast takes material back, and the undisturbed ridge never walks after them.

## The worst day — a short story

A working room is opened to the outside while repairs need heat. The crew misses a crack. Fresh seams and rumbling track the approach to an exposed heater. A stronger heater behind a fully protected floor does not hold the attackers' attention. The animal surfaces at reachable warmth; workers fight from protection. Someone downed on bare ice must be recovered in the ordinary surface map. Nobody is silently stored in an unfinished rescue system.

## The season's turn — a short story

They reload a save made while a crack was pending. Its warning history and remaining deadline are intact. Another crew lays protection before heating, then salvages after the warning instead of before it. Both sequences obey the same geometry and thermal consequences. The supplied refuge finishes its work, with finite finds and stretches of quiet. A departure leaves still ice, worked sites and whatever ordinary living animals or corpses remain, not a reset world or an ever-growing hidden army.
