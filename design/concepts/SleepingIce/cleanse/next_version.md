# Next version — default off, admissions preserved

These are this Designer's version calls under the cleanse brief. They do not revoke the frozen slate, rename species, remove a roster membership or alter an owner ruling. The owner's historical build order remains the future dependency order where applicable: heat core first; thaw/calving, sky, sohl, larder, apex, forms. The ordinary forms hessarund and hoarfrost remain in the proposed first version. Do not start a future system merely because it is listed here. Built extra code and defs remain behind an explicitly labelled default-off Mod Setting; T1/T2 give the state-read acceptance tests.

- **Zhissa electrical defence — built, T1.** The electric melee effect, shock severity/consciousness and heart-attack chain.
  Why moved: it is a separate combat hazard; ordinary milk, breeding, supplied feed and physical defence remain complete life without it. Keep current animal/art/admission; prevent default electric attacks and make text honest. Preserve the opt-in electric capacities/maneuver/damage/hediff definitions.

- **Thaw pulse and calving delivery — absent, T13.** Heat softens subzero ice, slumps slabs and exposes preserved inclusions.
  Why moved: terrain collapse and deliveries form a second cause-and-effect system; the first-version thermal signature already ends in warned shivven and defence. Never create a liquid pool. Future native insects and recovered occupants depend on this package.

- **Lost soul — absent, T14.** A dying traveller rescue incident with thermal danger already approaching.
  Why moved: a bespoke rescue event is beyond ordinary life/land and the camp-pressure chain. Preserve victim identity and ordinary surface recovery; any under-ice transfer must wait for the larder contract.

- **Surface reconnection storm — absent, T15a.** Radiation, circuit surges and tunnel-stirring during an energetic sky event.
  Why moved: these are an additional hazard mechanism under a weather name. Ordinary cold starlight/aurora remains land, supplied by actual geometry/presentation rather than this storm.

- **Escarpment rime-fall and ablation — absent, T15b.** Margin-only falling ice/drift weather tied to real escarpment geometry.
  Why moved: a margin-event scheduler and effects add behaviour beyond the ordinary plateau, and cannot be spread across the interior to make a quick weather test pass. No internal wind/snow/rain exception.

- **Sohl — absent, T16.** An indistinguishable landform spends its single lifetime movement and remains inert-alive afterwards.
  Why moved: this is the runner-up signature, with independent trigger/commit/reproduction/aftermath states. Do not implement a meaningless decorative version and call its movement finished.

- **Frissim — absent, T17.** Flightless inclusion insects awaken in thaw and return to dormancy afterwards.
  Why moved: their activity depends on deferred thaw ecology. They are not a native food harvest or a replacement for the separately ruled deposited dayside swarm. Death, if any, leaves readable remains.

- **Wyrmlets — absent, T18.** Young dhorrumak feed on inclusion insects and visibly surface/submerge where those insects stir.
  Why moved: this is foreshadowing within the deferred insect→young→nest→apex chain. Keep the lineage and shared readable movement contract; do not add unrelated ordinary wild predators.

- **Dhorrumak and nest eruption — absent, T19.** Sustained heat or disturbed eggs/young brings the breach apex.
  Why moved: it is an additional set-piece encounter, not necessary for ordinary breaches to be dangerous. Future release must obey the hull rule; ordinary walking titan passage remains excluded.

- **Saallen — absent, T20.** Heat-blistering catalytic crust adds a local placement preference removable by scraping.
  Why moved: a separate scrape/placement-management mechanic is not necessary to bare warm ice eligibility or readable warnings. Scraping removes only the added weight; it must never ward a still-warm unfloored cell absolutely.

- **Oolm — absent, T21.** Sessile sohl-lineage colonies with individual identity and passive hardening after local mining.
  Why moved: colony-linked mining response/reproduction is an independent life mechanic; ordinary stationary mining life is already supplied by the simpler ruled hessarund. No new wall-insulation or research promise. The biological admission stands.

- **Neshkoll — absent, T22.** Frost fans form approximately toward local heat leaks and decay when the source changes.
  Why moved: this adds an environmental leak-diagnostic mechanic beyond the retained pressure explanation and inspected source state. It has a possible later experience and is not recommended for retirement; it is not a requirement of first-version warning readability.

- **Ice-kept dead — absent, T23.** Cut out preserved bodies/gear, with heat-sensitive theft and possible tablet hooks.
  Why moved: a distinct occupant recovery interaction and its custody risks extend the basic finite wreck/mining stop. Cold exposure alone never emits heat, and no theft ships until larder recovery exists. Tablet remains an optional future prop, never a newly attributed rite ruling.

- **Larder and pocket rescue — absent, T24.** Shivven drag downed/dead victims into persistent custody; a party enters a bounded rescue site and returns.
  Why moved: abduction, custody, entry, rescue and return are a separate expedition system. Owner already chose pocket-map style with a way home, separate from Lantern Deeps; keep that answer. Until enabled, ordinary surface casualties remain ordinary bodies.

- **Insect-fall — absent, T25.** Freeze-dried dayside swarm deposition gives food and stirs the warren.
  Why moved: this is a new food-delivery incident; it is neither ordinary farming nor native-frissim harvest. Its future classification is deposition, never a reopening of the interior precipitation ban.

- **Heat-drawn assailants — absent, T26.** Raid and manhunter-pack forms arrive because of heat.
  Why moved: separate incident enemies repeat the heat consequence beyond the selected warned-shivven signature. No fauna roster additions or replacement for vanilla raids is needed to pass.

- **Cold Ledger — absent, T27.** The admitted campaign consolation rite associated with preserved dead.
  Why moved: a religious/campaign experience has no necessary role in land, life or the breach chain. Keep admission and the already-passed alternatives closed; future host/tablet/perform-anywhere details must be specified before build, without asking the owner to redesign the rite.

- **Drifting Dark — absent, T29.** The crags' tholin cloud can drift over the ice and interfere with sensors.
  Why moved: a visiting obscuration hazard adds a separate event/response loop; ordinary cold overhead sky does not need it. Do not copy the Abyss's whole ecology or change the animal's thermal-only sensing.

- **Active crevasse/collapse — absent, T30.** Crevasses change and their lips can collapse after frost-sift/rime-fall/grumble warning.
  Why moved: a changing-terrain collapse hazard is more than the retained fractured hard-ice appearance. Future geometry must coordinate with thaw and the shared collapse engine; it is not a second hidden ambush in first version.
