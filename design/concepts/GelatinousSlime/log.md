### Delegated answer — CONDUCTOR:1794805789204977373

GelatinousSlime is a universal living-body biome: the colony can take food, building material and poison relief from the same slime that absorbs unprotected people and can ultimately dissolve them. Native resistant life shares the body with outsiders already being absorbed; becoming slimified does not itself make a creature hostile. A chosen gene makes the bargain deliberate: select it from the archive, locate and extract its current with a handheld seeker, then inject a perishable dose that also brings an unwanted gene and a lasting social cost. The recipient falls into a coma and faces accelerated absorption unless a friend administers the toxic antidote in time. Ordinary exposure has its own slower course. Early ordinary film recedes away from slime ground; later film persists in ordinary country, so drying country or antidote provides the established way out. The giant Titanoslime engulfs and digests prey and grows permanently by default; a harvested, short-lived piece becomes a dangerous thrown bioweapon, including to nearby allies, and serves the campaign’s sealed-vault use. Expensive archive technology can return a dead person from their last recorded touch, while the colony’s later lives and relationships remain real. The player can use and survive this body, but its gifts carry readable, actionable consequences.
