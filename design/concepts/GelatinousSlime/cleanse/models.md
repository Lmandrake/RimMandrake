# Instruments to specify before further build

These are specifications, not implementations, game measurements or accepted behaviour. Reuse the existing production kernels and shared engine ownership. Every output carries source/def/settings/save fingerprints, explicit unknown inputs and calibration status. A model cannot manufacture missing game behaviour; a forecast using an assumed connection must say so. Build in this order. Unknown meetings in bars.json name these exact headings; their members identify all bars and rules they inform, including three-way feedback.

## M1: Situation and clinical transition generator

**Question.** Can every declared contact, use, travel, treatment, loss and feature-disable transition preserve the promised benefit, danger, escape, signs and belongings?

**Informs.** Absorption and escape; the clinical portions of use_body, residents, mouth and whole; R_contact, R_clinic, R_poison, R_population, R_engulf, R_information and R_settings; the unknown clinical_state meeting. It supplies the required generator of situations, not just a sampler of happy paths.

**Existing material.** Production `RM_SlimeLadder`, `RM_TitanKernel`, source health/holder wrappers and `Source/SelfTest/GelatinousSlimeFuzz.cs`. The existing fuzz reuses actual kernels and shrinks traces, but does not prove normal UseItem, caravan ticking or holder transfers. `northstar_mock.py` models expected results and `selftest_slime_suite.py` plants faults; these show observer sensitivity, not game truth. Do not build a second hand-coded mirror and call agreement evidence.

**Inputs and sweep.** Enumerate susceptible flesh, extension resistance, active/inactive resistance gene and non-flesh; faction/player/prisoner/slave/wild/tame humanlike or animal; healthy/downed/bed/held/caravan/dead state; initial severity 0..1 with exact boundaries and nearest representable floats around .2/.5/.9/1; current defaults and clock slider extrema 2..30 days; top terrain primary/legacy/floor/null, roof on/off, biome drying/neutral/body/invalid. Action alphabet: step on/off, raw eat, cooked eat, living-slime drink, rainwater drink, dose again, normal treatment order/start/cancel/finish, move map-to-caravan-to-drying-to-neutral, engulf/release, save/reload, disable/reset/re-enable, independent death and terminal tick. Include old saved injected fast-clock state despite no new default injection.

Walk every legal single transition and ordered pair, then longer sequences until the finite abstract state graph has no unseen edge, with severity/time intervals represented symbolically between thresholds. Use seeded longer walks to seek cycles, not claim exhaustive real-valued simulation. Include 1, 2, 6 and 40 simultaneous patients competing for 0, 1 and several helpers; treatment delays from 0 through and beyond derived remaining life. Labelled apparel/equipment/inventory/carried objects and current map/caravan holders must be conserved or assigned an explicit outcome. Duplicate/lost id is failure, not an absent-health success.

**Assumptions and omissions.** The same production rules govern the wrappers only after calibration shows they do. The generator can enumerate eligibility/timing but cannot predict real pathfinding, rescue AI, unknown mod hediff classification or terminal caravan inventory transfer without game witnesses. Stubbed transitions are explicitly UNKNOWN. Ordinary direct-UI aesthetics are outside this model; no gene expedition is required for a first-version walk.

**Check against the real game before belief.** T28 collects tick-stamped state reads from actual contact, early wipe-off, advanced neutral hold, drying on map and caravan, raw/cooked/drink comparisons, real UseItem completion/cancel and terminal map/held/caravan loss, plus save/reload and OFF at a boundary. Match exact pawn/item/holder ids, latches, stage and event type. Numeric severity must agree within 1e-5 plus the change from one production 200-tick scheduling interval; if real tick scheduling differs, derive and disclose the bound rather than widen it after a failure. Terminal transfer/signs and action order must agree exactly. For jobs, use measured travel/work ticks and valid cancelled-job receipts, not guessed seconds. A single uncalibrated transition cannot be accepted by extrapolating another location.

Before any M1 output counts for escape, T28 must provide separate running-game treatment rows for walking on map, in bed, downed on map bare ground, held/released and caravan. Observe real ordinary action availability, targeting, reach/travel, job start and completion, same patient alive with film cleared, antidote consumption and toxic cost, with delayed/cancelled controls in each class. For held prey, record targeting while held and actual release followed by helper completion if release is needed. Caravan uses its actual supported treatment action, not an assumed map UseItem job. No direct effect call substitutes for these receipts. An unavailable or uncalibrated class is UNKNOWN and blocks acceptance over the whole range. M1 covers timing and severity between calibrated classes; it cannot establish engine job availability.

**Output.** State graph, reachable/illegal transition table, worst time-to-action margin per patient contention regime, missing sign/item-transfer list and shrunk counterexample traces bound to the bars' accept/reject pictures. Planted missing-tag, never-targetable-cure, silent loss, gear destruction, false hold/OFF and wrong-location mutants must fail.

**Decision changed.** Fix consumer routing, safe OFF semantics, location/holder handling or warning context; attack R_clinic/source wrappers before changing an experience. If normal treatment cannot fit a prepared ordinary route, identify the time/work choice to change. Do not restore the deferred gene system merely to make treatment observable.

**Rough size.** [guess] 400–800 lines of orchestration/assertions reusing production kernels and the existing shrinker, plus compact recorded transition fixtures; branching reduced by threshold equivalence. No game code is written in this pass.

**Build trigger.** First: clinical_state is unknown now. Build before further core tuning or live staging; T28. Any newly discovered holder or poison class adds a situation and its calibration, not an owner question.

## M2: Whole Slime season

**Question.** With every retained mechanism active, do terrain, water, vegetation, resources and animal populations support the declared managed routine instead of erasing or saturating one another?

**Informs.** All bars, particularly fields/residents/whole; R_contact, R_farm, R_growth, R_water, R_population, R_engulf, R_settings and R_bootstrap. Resolves surface_state and animal_population unknown meetings and integrates M3's water and M5's work budgets. A coupled growth/terrain/forage/animal/resource model is required even if pairwise probes pass.

**Inputs and sweep.** Read existing authored-map surface/roof/floor/zone/plant/animal/liquid records and loaded defs, not planet seeds. Full cell arrays for 150x150, 250x250, 325x325, 400x400 maps, plus local arrangements spanning the same area limits; no world generation. Include primary and owned legacy ground, scarce/ordinary/abundant forage and shelf areas, 0/ordinary/capped/over-cap wild populations, 6/40-colonist routines, fields 1..2500 cells and low/high harvest effort. Engine defaults ON, slime farm sampling from production `ConversionAttempts`, ordinary/wet growth timing, Slime top produce/plant removal/rich-terrain rings, natural plant renewal, animal diets/hunger/predation, Wuum cloning/cap, all-wild-animal visitor cap and adjacent-tile inputs, giant feeding/irreversible growth and acid-caused losses. Oomb promotion/fission, titan shedding and every other moved extra OFF.

Run 15,60,100 in-game days. Within each crop-comparison run schedule a new attempt after field reversion and match it to the same ordinary food crop on compatible imported substrate or hydroponics under the same care/light/temperature. Include recorded sow refusal or substrate-caused growth stop, successful managed-control harvest and unzoned forage regrowth. If the control cannot mature within a run, extend and disclose the observation stretch; neither silence at a timeout nor a projection to indefinite farming decides fields. A merely slower but harvestable reverted-ground crop is a counterexample. Sweep weather onset/overlap from observed shipped natural process and replay worst valid event overlaps; vary labour/harvest/treatment order and save/reload half-way. Imported bootstrap food/wood/steel/antidote are explicit ledger inputs, never an invisible supply. Sensitivity bracket each uncalibrated amount rather than fill in a confident “balanced” default. Rain/growth writes must go through the correct layer-owner assumptions established by M3.

**Assumptions and omissions.** Discrete event/stock approximation, not a reimplementation of RimWorld. Mortality/diet/work demand and growth/top timing must be empirically parameterised; stratified population bands may replace individual AI only after they reproduce actual flows. Spatial terrain rings cannot be reduced to global percentages: preserve floors, water, edifices, islands and boundary connectivity. It does not judge art/enjoyment or guarantee survival through arbitrary raids; it records independent losses as external events. No model output authorizes world edits, engine redesign or a numeric passing bar.

**Check against the real game before belief.** T31 calibrates from the same disposable authored-map saves, all features and natural sky active. Match exact zone-driven conversion attempts and eligible-cell writes when replaying logged RNG choices; exact event/produce/animal/item ids for deterministic trace replay; zero unaccounted floor replacement, missing creature or terrain-layer overwrite. At checkpoints compare observed plant/terrain/population/resource traces against predicted bands; require actual values within the model's preregistered 95% prediction intervals across independent repeated branches. For stochastic counts use recorded draws for initial trace identity, then independent draws for validation, never call a familiar seed exhaustive. A wrong trend, missed species/forage collapse or persistent water/slime expansion invalidates the model even if aggregate intervals happen to overlap. First calibrate 1 day/pulse/top events, then a week, then the season; do not believe the season from a single plant. For fields, match actual attempted-sow job outcomes, substrate eligibility/growth-stop causes and same-crop managed-control harvest receipts exactly by cell/crop identity. Preserve the distinction between a failed new attempt and a crop that began before conversion; absent harvest by a finite deadline alone cannot calibrate failure.

**Output.** Cell maps and timelines of substrate conversion, productive crop cells and forage connectivity; animal births/arrivals/predation/deaths/emigration by origin and holder; stock and labour pressure; worst bar counterexample per region, state and action order. Explicitly label flood (persistent area/population takeover preventing routine), desert (lost renewal), stall (jobs/cycles blocked) and independent catastrophe. No arbitrary percentage turns a heatmap into an owner bar.

**Decision changed.** Retune local ground/crop compatibility, source zone/conversion choices or population budget within current standing laws; fix local integration that double-counts water/growth, not shared engine physics. If native count blocks outsiders, attack R_population's budget before the resident experience. If the coupled system has no feasible region, report the conflict rather than choose a convenient clear-sky sample.

**Rough size.** [guess] 600–1200 lines of event adapters/ledger and plots using existing kernels, with 22,500–160,000 surface cells per run; dozens of scenario regimes, hundreds of stochastic replicates only where uncertainty matters. Calibration and exact game record readers are more work than the event loop. T31 is the model; T30 is the distinct whole-bar runtime runner.

**Build trigger.** Immediately after M1: surface_state and animal_population are unknown now. Rebuild/recalibrate if a growth/conversion/population constant is changed twice to move a check, or whole bar first fails. This is the whole-mod model required by the brief, not a replacement for the whole bar's normal-action evidence.

## M3: Rain and liquid ownership sweep

**Question.** Does organism rain create naturally available potable collection and receding inundation without double-counting fluid, overwriting owned terrain or charging the raw-slime dose?

**Informs.** water, ground, fields, residents, use_body and whole; R_water, R_growth, R_contact, R_settings; unknown water_growth meeting and the water inputs of M2/material economy. Existing FlowWorks APIs are dependencies. Do not write a parallel fluid solver.

**Inputs and sweep.** First build a route inventory from loaded weather choice, rain amount/provider, liquid identity, collector/tank/bottle, excavation/natural-surface, layer owner, drainage and thirst APIs. Unknown links remain unknown. The target flood picture is definition.md’s shallow potable surface layer on every unroofed, unbuilt natural slime land cell, preserving substrate, movement/build support and contact, with normal direct drinking as well as collection; existing channels, imported substrate, floors, edifices and roofed cells are excluded. This is a required mask and state contract, not an assertion that FlowWorks already supplies it. Then sweep existing legal local catchments: flat/slope/depression/edge, undug slime and dug channels/beds, water/slime/empty/full mixtures, open/roofed/partial roof catchments, floor/door/edifice boundaries, blocked/open drains and sealed storage. Include natural crust/rich/grass/mud, planted versus unplanted fields, legacy equivalents and each excluded surface class; roof/floor addition and removal during an event, direct drinking before/during/after recession, and concurrent field conversion/plant-top writes. Sizes 1 cell through a full 400x400 map; rainfall tile field 0 and observed nonzero values; weather onset/end/overlap/disable; 0..capacity storage; wet growth before/during/after peak rain; save during the shared pending-rain pass and restore.

**Assumptions and omissions.** Use the actual engine water unit and conservation API as read, not guessed world millimetres. Model is initially a geometry/event accounting checker: it cannot predict an undug-ground flood or collector hook that source/loaded consumers do not provide. If no route exists, record the retained land gap T22 and withhold predictions for that branch. Fluid input/absorption/drain terms are accepted only after trace calibration. It does not redesign rainfall frequencies, evaporate water by fictional intent, alter canonical terrain or simulate entire planetary hydrology.

**Check against the real game before belief.** T22 establishes actual loaded event eligibility on zero-rainfall authored tile, not forced weather alone. T29 reads per-owner integer liquid stock/collector contents, terrain layers, rain-landed/overflow/drained totals, pending pass markers and completed drinking/thirst/health before, during and after an event. Use covered collector and no-event paired intervals, ordinary-rain diagnostic and raw-slime drink controls. Account exact integral-unit conservation through a saved pending pulse; where the engine uses a floating accumulator allow only its documented quantisation remainder, stated before the run. Composition, raw-dose presence/absence, terrain ownership and reload-once markers must match exactly. Match the exact eligibility mask and direct-drink surface-water debit/thirst receipt. Show preserved underlying contact on flooded bare ground, no water-caused crop loss or support change, no sideways flooding of excluded cells, and the same post-recession terrain as the prior terrain plus independently recorded conversion/growth writes. Mid-event roofs/floors stop new input and drain prior event water safely. A volume match with wrong fluid or no visible inundation is disagreement, not successful calibration. Check visually that the instrument's wet cells correspond to actual inundation, once per terrain-layer type; the model then reads state.

**Output.** Ownership diagram, conservation tables and maps of wet/collect/drain cells, overwritten-cell witnesses, potable versus raw-liquid ingestion outcomes, and worst unreceding basin per legal catchment. Mark absent/unknown route separately from overflow caused by deliberate sealed storage.

**Decision changed.** Repair the specific local weather/provider/collection/drain adapter, natural eligibility or liquid type; preserve shared engine ownership and fixed-world rain ban. If there is no credible undug-ground mapping, report that explicitly and implement the retained minimal local hook before claiming the model credible. No generic `rainRate` multiplication can settle potable/flood/drain behaviour.

**Rough size.** [guess] 250–500 lines of route enumerator/ledger adapters and cell pictures, using existing FlowWorks reads/math; no invented flood simulation. Route tracing may find part of the instrument already exists.

**Build trigger.** water_growth is unknown now; build the trace before anyone tunes rain or substitutes living-slime drinking for collection. Also triggered by first water bar failure, duplicate water after reload or a second rain-duration/amount retune. T29 provides the instrument; T22 implements any actual gap only after tracing.

## M4: Engulf and occupied-cell sweep

**Question.** Across every legal giant placement, size and holder transition, can actual occupied geometry and release keep prey and possessions accounted for?

**Informs.** mouth, absorption/escape release cases, ground and whole; R_engulf, R_contact, R_clinic and R_settings; unknown held_occupancy meeting. Consumes shared HugeThings/Large Pawns geometry, without redesigning it.

**Inputs and sweep.** Enumerate all current giant life stages 1..5, growth thresholds ±epsilon, prey size and capacity boundaries, held count 0..capacity, flesh/non-flesh/same-kind/self/other-holder filters, pawn facings supported by the engine, actual occupied-cell shapes, map edges/corners and every translation of local blocker arrangements. Generate corridors from blocked through wider-than-footprint, diagonal pinches, building/water/floor interfaces, completely blocked drop rings and corpse-site conversion. Same-stage growth/no change versus cross-stage, down/death/destroy, engulf OFF and save/reload while holding. Sweep fixed geometry neighbourhoods up to the maximum occupied footprint and release search radius read from current source; no arbitrary “nearby” radius. Legal geometry equivalence classes collapse translation, but every supported stage/orientation/boundary class is represented.

**Assumptions and omissions.** Kernel eligibility alone does not predict melee verb selection or normal pathfinding; those are real encounters in mouth. This instrument imports the exact existing footprint/drop algorithm or recorded decisions, not a round-sprite proxy. Absolute all-mod map contents are not enumerated; classify every blocker type by the relevant game walkability/occupancy semantics. An unmodelled type stays outside accepted coverage until calibrated. Giant caravan transport is not promised; held people's caravan/health consequences belong to M1 if a supported transfer reaches that state.

**Check against the real game before belief.** T32 state reads actual occupied cells and lookup/path cache registrations before/after a real fed stage change and reload, at an interior, edge, corner and narrow blocker fixture in each distinct footprint class. Match sets of occupied cells and pawn/item/holder identities exactly after the source cache interval; record the interval rather than guessing instantaneous refresh. Match actual allowed/blocked prey decisions and legal release cells on supported rescues. A fully blocked release must retain the same prey safely/readably or the earlier engulf must have been refused; model must never dispose of it to make the count balance. Check release-before-corpse-site ordering against actual conversion. Planted stale registration, early container disposal and drop-out-of-bounds variants must fail.

**Output.** Placement map, allowed prey/release table, worst blocked footprint and shortest failing transition trace. Saved occupant/item conservation is explicit. Actual engine geometry disagreements are evidence against this model's abstraction, not measurements of an alternative map.

**Decision changed.** Correct local registration sequencing, guard an unsafe supported transition, or preserve/communicate a blocked holder until a release location exists. Attack local drop/registration rules before reducing the natural engulf/rescue bar or violating permanent growth.

**Rough size.** [guess] 300–600 lines, tens of thousands of small local configurations after equivalence reduction, plus saved geometry witnesses. Reuse existing Titanoslime proof/gate readers where they really expose the state.

**Build trigger.** held_occupancy is unknown now; run before any size/gate retune or giant rescue acceptance. First ghost occupancy, lost held pawn or map-edge failure becomes a replay fixture. T32.

## M5: Work and material budget

**Question.** Can accounted ordinary harvest, processing, construction and treatment support the declared managed routine when native-ground farms cease to be permanent?

**Informs.** use_body, fields, escape, water and whole; R_bootstrap, R_farm, R_growth, R_poison, R_settings; unknown material_economy meeting. Supplies labour/consumption bounds to M2, not a promise that any colony is indefinitely self-sufficient.

**Inputs and sweep.** Loaded ingredient/output/work/research/cost defs and actual worker/job/path receipts. Sources: forage, bellows harvest, existing shared wet-top produce, ordinary imports and earned normal salvage; sinks: meals, blocks, construction, antidote, raw cures, animal feed, spoilage/consumption. No source for a deferred solvent dish, gene item, chunk, vat, ruin ledger loot or fission animal. Track source/holder ids to prevent produce counted once as harvest and again as food. Sweep 1/6/40 workers, worker skills across the ordinary game range, route lengths 0 through full-map traversal, safe-floor work versus exposed gathering, empty/limited/adequate bootstrap imports, low/ordinary/soaked forage and several active patients. Use source work demand with measured scheduling/path bands, not a guessed universal worker throughput. Run first setup, week and season balances and both harvest-first/treat-first orderings.

**Assumptions and omissions.** Flow network / discrete job queue, not a market economy or a “fun” score. It assumes job legality only where T27 normal-use receipts establish it. Raid losses and labour availability are explicit external inputs, never convenient omissions to claim universal survival. No numeric yield or sustained colony-size threshold becomes a bar. If actual AI scheduling cannot be bounded credibly, show the feasible material envelope and mark job completion as the live bar's responsibility.

**Check against the real game before belief.** T33 reconciles exact input/output/reservation/inventory ids and quantities for completed harvest, normal pit/compressor bills, block construction, raw ingestion and antidote treatment, on an existing authored map. Match integer material conservation exactly after accounting recorded imports/consumption/spoilage; match measured normal work/path timing within its recorded scheduling interval and do not silently count paused work as available labour. Validate a second action order and a held/cancelled bill; a deliberately resource-short branch must remain infeasible. Match predicted bottleneck direction to actual seasonal runner queue/stock traces, with no hidden replenishment.

**Output.** Source/sink and work-time tables, bootstrap bottleneck, feasible/impossible routine envelopes, worst exposure/treatment pressure and labels on assumptions that M2 must sweep. No chart is an observation of the game without its calibration receipt.

**Decision changed.** Tune local recipes/availability or choose explicit imported bootstrap allowances; fix reservation/consumer accounting; attack a recipe/terrain rule before redefining the signature as costless food. If the material envelope works but real jobs stall, T27/T30 diagnose the engine path rather than pretending the economic model proved normal use.

**Rough size.** [guess] 150–350 lines of schema reader, queue/envelope and ledger output, plus representative normal-job receipts. No full colony-AI simulator is credible or proposed.

**Build trigger.** material_economy is unknown now. Run before lowering multiple recipe/conversion numbers to chase a check, after first workday/whole-bar failure, or when a source constant changes twice. T33.
