# Gelatinous Slime — proposed Design Version 0.1.0

This is a Designer proposal, not a new owner quotation, applied definition, build authorization, passed mod or changed ruling. Only this folder is changed. The owner's hold remains. Prior accepted root/showcase selections are historical input; this proposal deliberately changes the first-version boundary under the cleansing brief. No prior runtime proof is upgraded.

## Say the mod

You settle on a living landscape whose slime supplies food, building material and poison relief, while bare contact and raw consumption begin absorbing unprotected flesh. Resistant native herds, scavengers, a floater and a feeding giant live where your workers need constructed floors, an escape route and toxic antidote. Farms succumb to the body, its rain supplies drinkable water and drains away, and a prepared colony can keep working through the season.

Land: crust shelves, slower soft ground and liquid currents; warm wet passage marks; no local bedrock; allowed existing roads; converted fields and organism-induced, potable, fast-draining rain.

Life: keep the current native roster and ordinary flora, their feeding, flight, reproduction, resistance, predation and corrosive death hazards, plus bounded vulnerable outsiders. No eviction, including from another biome.

Signature: **The living material supplies food, stone and poison relief, but touching or eating it raw absorbs susceptible flesh, making preparation and reversal part of gathering and using the body.** Its necessary parts are contact, progressive film, raw benefit/fee, ordinary processing, readable warnings, lethal completion with belongings accounted for, drying geography and toxic targeted treatment. They form one resource/contact/consequence/escape chain.

Runner-up: the chosen-gene expedition and friend rescue. The original owner instruction reproduced in `design/RimMandrake/RM_gelatinous_slime_mod.md` makes it the former centerpiece, and CONDUCTOR:1794825281765620778 selected it as showcase. It loses this version because archive choice, field localisation, perishable extraction, gene/rider effects, coma, inheritance and lasting social cost form another elective chain. Deleting it does not stop ordinary settlement on living ground. This is my scope decision under the new brief, not an assertion that he stopped wanting it. The source requests and prior UI/rider/family/weapon answers remain binding when their systems return. Page two of owner_pages.md carries the explicit choice, the contrary owner evidence and the default; no reply is needed to continue this proposal on that default.

## Why a player reaches for it

This is a place where resources and exposure are inseparable. A traveller can eat the terrain's harvest to solve poisoning and acquire a different problem. A settler makes stable work areas and processes the same material for ordinary food and stone instead of opening a quarry. The predicted pleasure is planning a workable colony inside a landscape that consumes its visitors; enjoyment is a prediction, never a bar.

The organism is the disarmed Assailant end-state in Ash'karr's lore, a relative of the Rot, with connected subsurface lobes and a circulating genetic history. That remains background. The player does not talk to it, provoke a scheming consciousness, unlock a planet-wide spread event, or build a strategic genome simulation in this version. “Reading” means assimilation, not intent or a database UI.

## Using it

**First hour.** Read the terrain and health warning. Hardened shelves can hold substantial construction but still expose bare feet to the body; “dry crust” is not a drying biome. A roof blocks sun/rain according to their shared systems, not contact through exposed ground. Lay ordinary non-slime flooring for the work area. Food, antidote, steel and wood carried in are legitimate preparation. The existing pit and compressor have normal construction/work/research costs: the current no-thumbstalk-wood rule is not repaired by inventing wood output. A fresh colony without imports is not promised a costless bootstrap.

**Routine.** Harvest or forage raw slime, haul it, cook it in the pit or press building blocks, and use the real products. Prepared food must not secretly apply the raw ingestion doer. Eating raw slime purges the declared poison-like conditions and charges film to susceptible flesh; resistant flesh still gets the benefit, without an invented penalty overriding resistance. It does not regrow a limb or remove addiction. Living channel slime is a separate, last-resort FlowWorks drink with the existing raw-slime bargain; collected rainwater is potable water and must not be misidentified as that drink.

Contact starts ordinary film; early film can recede off body ground. Established ordinary film remains in neutral country. Declared drying-biome data reverses it on a local map and during caravan travel, and a normal helper can administer the toxic antidote before terminal dissolution. Treatment does not resurrect the dead or cancel unrelated injuries, coma or genes. A patient may be in a bed or downed; UseItem must actually finish. There is no dedicated triage screen and no assurance that a long, blocked rescue route can beat the clock.

**At forty colonists.** Use existing work priorities, health/alerts, zones and item jobs. Several patients compete for helpers and antidote stock; no auto-treatment service or new emergency dispatch UI is added. The accepting routine records unfinished and finished jobs, real patient identities and toxin/film changes. Letter latches survive reload rather than repeating the entire cascade. A colony may import supplies or abandon an unsafe field; it may not rely on unlimited helper-generated food, artificially frozen animal needs or disabled growth.

## Land running whole

Primary hardened, rich, grass, mud and liquid terrain remain different travel/building surfaces. Hardened is still tagged living ground; current text promising slower exposure there has no different source rate and must be corrected or separately justified, not smuggled into a bar. Constructed flooring protects against contact while exposed body ground under a roof does not. Existing roads stay allowed, without repainting any road or world tile.

Warm wet footprints are a retained land gap. `SlimeTerrain.xml` itself states that `takeFootprints` only works with snow and does not deliver its promise here. T18 asks for the smallest passage-mark delivery and witness, not new flora art. The exact appearance/lifetime is build choice; actual recent passage on warm slime must be observable.

Cultivation on native slime substrate is a temporary bargain, not a permanent agricultural base. Source currently triggers on growing-zone designation, even before sowing; de-zoning stops future zone-driven conversion but does not reverse past changes. After reversion, a new ordinary food-crop sow-to-harvest attempt must fail because the reverted substrate refuses sowing or stops growth before harvest, while the same crop reaches harvest on compatible imported substrate or in hydroponics during the observed stretch. A crop already growing before conversion may finish. Merely delaying or reducing a harvest is insufficient. Current primary rich slime fertility is 0.85 (`SlimeTerrain.xml`), so observing the replacement or waiting without a harvest proves neither failure nor permanence. T25 must read the substrate-caused refusal/growth stop and the completed comparison harvest for each ordinary crop compatibility regime, then choose local terrain/conversion compatibility that delivers the law while retaining native regrowth. This is my operational definition of the frozen no-permanent-farmland law, not an added owner quotation. Hydroponics, imported substrate and off-body agriculture are distinct managed alternatives, not a waiver for permanent native ground farms.

Slime rain is organism irrigation, explicitly allowed by the frozen sheet despite Ash'karr's meteorological rain ban. It needs natural availability even on the fixed world's zero-rainfall tiles, usable collection, visible temporary inundation and recession. No new rain frequency or flood depth is guessed. Roofed collectors are controls; open normal collectors receive the event's potable output. Existing clean stored water stays when standing water drains. Raw liquid slime is not rainwater. FlowWorks owns volumes, composition, containers, excavated cells, wet surfaces and drainage. The existing `ApplyRain` route is evidence of an engine, not proof that it implements the authored undug-ground flood. T22 traces the actual consumer chain before any local gap is implemented. No new hydrology engine or planetary rainfall alteration is authorized.

**Flood picture — my specification of the frozen rain law.** During an organism event, shallow standing potable water covers all unroofed, unbuilt natural slime land cells: hardened shelves, rich mats, grass and mud, including planted fields and owned legacy equivalents. This is an accounted surface-water layer above the substrate under FlowWorks ownership, not a terrain swap, an elevation simulation, a widening slime channel or a wet tint. Existing liquid channels, imported substrate, constructed floors, edifices and roofed cells receive no ambient flood layer, and water does not spread laterally onto these exclusions; an exposed ordinary collector still collects rain by its own route. A pawn can use normal thirst jobs to drink this standing water in place or collect it for storage, without a raw-slime ingestion dose. Drinking while standing on bare slime still incurs independent contact exposure: the water never hides the contact-active substrate. Land stays traversable and retains its underlying movement costs and building support, with only the existing shared wet/slip behaviour; no impassable deep-water conversion or new swimming rule is added. Floodwater neither kills crops nor changes fertility: fields fail by substrate conversion, while native plants keep their shared wet-growth response. Roofed rooms and constructed floors remain unflooded. Adding a roof or floor stops further event input there and existing event water drains safely; removal makes the newly exposed eligible land receive subsequent input. Recession removes event surface water, exposing the prior terrain unchanged; independent farm conversion or shared-growth writes made meanwhile persist and must not be rolled back. Stored water remains. Exact layer depth, pulse frequency and duration are checks. The provider for this picture is still unknown; T22 traces it and supplies only missing local hooks, and M3 cannot be believed until actual cell/water/drinking records agree.

## Life running whole

Gelatid supplies harmless resistant herd/prey life. Lesser, ordinary and greater gappos graze/filter-feed; they do not need to pave ground to be filter-feeders. The three oomb forms scavenge refuse using the existing VEF feeding primitive; deleting that behaviour would erase their scavenger role and tame utility. Promotion and tame fission are a separable next-version utility on the same comp: code coupling does not make them necessary. Wuum remains a biting substrate species with its existing capped VEF asexual reproduction; without an ordinary reproduction route this genderless species has no biological renewal. Shared spawn budgets and its reproductive limit must be included in the season model, not assumed to prevent all population floods.

Fubbum hunts by vanilla permitted-prey selection. “Hunts gelatids” is not “only gelatids,” “never a child,” or “only at night.” Dwommo must really fly through `Pawn_FlightTracker`, not just be drawn above the ground; it is resistant by identity, so its resistance is not evidence that flight removes contact for all species. Read its state deterministically. Live visual flight trials remain owner-attended under the standing ruling; no unattended visual test is commissioned. Native animals can drink their own environmental liquid without dehydration under the shared FlowWorks contract. Ordinary oomb/wuum acid death bursts stay because corrosive handling danger is part of meeting those animals; EnvironmentalHazards owns them. No bespoke weapon output follows from that danger.

The giant is necessary life: a natural predator/lobe, with ordinary feeding, actual melee engulf, a named digestion countdown, struggle, release on supported rescue/off/death transitions, and lethal completion with gear and inventory recoverable. It grows through the current size ladder as it feeds and keeps that growth by default, including after starvation, dry ground and wounds; owner permanence is not retuned away. HugeThings/Large Pawns own size/corpse-site integration. Release must happen before corpse-site conversion; blocked edge cells, growing occupancy and several held victims need M4. No new boss incident or teleporting prey is allowed. Wound shedding, harvested chunks and vault use are separable extras; the giant does not need them to eat and grow.

Visitors read the fixed world's neighbouring biome roster and enter partly absorbed if susceptible, retaining resistant entrants where legitimate. They do not change home assignments or turn that neighbour roster into a new Slime fauna list. The current cap counts every factionless wild animal, including natives; this can suppress arrivals even while outsiders are promised. The population model must settle the regime before changing that policy. Empty eligible neighbours mean no invented substitute arrivals. Existing local initial-population hooks are local-map content, never planet generation or a new map-authoring capability; loading a save never reseeds it.

## What changes in the colony story, and what it costs

The colony's early advantage is an abundant-looking material that can feed and shelter it. Its routine becomes a floor plan, resource and patient-management problem. The field that looked like liberation becomes an unreliable food source; a giant fed or ignored early becomes a lasting presence. Rain is a useful interruption, not a permanent ocean or a hidden dose attack. The season may have loss, retreat and imported supplies. It must not require secretly shutting down half the biome to remain playable.

Costs are real work, inputs, time walking slow ground, raw-dose film, antidote toxicity, failed native-ground agriculture and exposure to normal animal danger. There is no promised numeric food surplus, fixed sustainable population, exact growth speed, yield, beast size or rainfall percentage. Those are model inputs/checks, with comparisons and causal experiences in the bars.

## When it goes wrong and the readable sign

Worsening film names the patient and stage in existing health/alerts before terminal loss; there is truthful information about the available action, not a universally decreasing timer while drying or safe. An absorbed animal needs an inspect/death/combat/event witness of cause and location; silently deleting a map row cannot count as ecology. Terminal caravan/holder handling must preserve belongings in a recoverable holder/location and report the loss; no fictional stack of items is spawned on a nonexistent map. The transition generator contains these boundary cases.

The giant names its held prey and rescue time; its destruction/off release cannot delete them. Farm ground visibly changes and a post-reversion crop attempt fails beside a successful managed-substrate crop; a renamed field that merely harvests more slowly or less abundantly is a failure of the definition. Water that keeps rising, a forage desert after wet growth, arrivals displaced by self-replicating natives, endless warnings after reload, or frozen jobs are whole-mod failures when they occur in the declared managed range. M2 and the whole runner distinguish them from ordinary unprepared starvation or independent attacks.

## Four short stories

**The first hour.** A worker carries steel, wood, food and antidote from the ship. A shelf accepts the workshop, but bare crust still begins film. The player lays a floor, sees the recent wet footprints outside, and orders the first harvest. The promise is preparation, not a free fully equipped settlement.

**An ordinary week.** Gatherers bring bellows slime to the pit and compressor. A poisoned traveller eats a raw portion, recovers from poison and needs a planned purge. A gappo keeps grazing without paving the colony; the fubbum hunts permitted prey. Rain adds usable stored water and the temporary wet ground recedes. After its ground reverts, a fresh crop attempt fails there while the same crop reaches harvest on imported substrate and native meadow forage regrows.

**The worst day.** A worker is swallowed while another has advanced film. The existing UI names both dangers. The prepared rescue group downs the giant and a helper completes the toxic injection; a delayed alternate branch records a real death and recoverable possessions. Nothing turns the body into a new enemy faction and no vanished pawn is counted as cured.

**The season's turn.** Shared wet-growth has come and gone, animal encounters continue and the fed giant keeps its size. The colony reloads a save made during a compound patient/rain/held-victim state, then treats before harvesting instead of after. Work and water resume without duplicate inventory or repeated warning storms. This is the whole bar; isolated showcases cannot accept it.
