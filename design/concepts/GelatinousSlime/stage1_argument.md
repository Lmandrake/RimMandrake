**Claim.** The mod’s central promise is a useful living body whose gifts come with absorption risk and lasting consequences; the selected-gene expedition is the clearest deliberate encounter with that bargain.

**Attack.** A list of rewards and hediffs could satisfy those words while the ordinary player cannot complete the seeker journey or order the rescue. Conversely, routine protection may make ordinary work safe: that alone is no reason to invent another penalty. Whether the bargain remains appealing is an enjoyment prediction for a later walk, not a new acceptance rule.

**Picture — written rehearsal, not a capture.** On an ordinary workday, colonists render slime for food or compress it for building material while exposed people acquire film. For the deliberate payoff, one colonist selects a gene, follows its mark and carries out extraction. After injection, the existing health UI and permitted critical-time message make the coma rescue legible; the friend actually uses the antidote. The cheap wrong version gives the gene through a helper and calls its def wiring a rescue proof. The returned-person reunion and the harvested-weapon/vault encounter are separate promises requiring their own scenes later. No spawn, injected health state or other cheat has been used for this written picture.

**Proposed root.** GelatinousSlime is a universal living-body biome: the colony can take food, building material and poison relief from the same slime that absorbs unprotected people and can ultimately dissolve them. Native resistant life shares the body with outsiders already being absorbed; becoming slimified does not itself make a creature hostile. A chosen gene makes the bargain deliberate: select it from the archive, locate and extract its current with a handheld seeker, then inject a perishable dose that also brings an unwanted gene and a lasting social cost. The recipient falls into a coma and faces accelerated absorption unless a friend administers the toxic antidote in time. Ordinary exposure has its own slower course. Early ordinary film recedes away from slime ground; later film persists in ordinary country, so drying country or antidote provides the established way out. The giant Titanoslime engulfs and digests prey and grows permanently by default; a harvested, short-lived piece becomes a dangerous thrown bioweapon, including to nearby allies, and serves the campaign’s sealed-vault use. Expensive archive technology can return a dead person from their last recorded touch, while the colony’s later lives and relationships remain real. The player can use and survive this body, but its gifts carry readable, actionable consequences.
