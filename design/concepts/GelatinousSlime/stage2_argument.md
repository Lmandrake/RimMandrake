## 1. A useful body on an ordinary workday (edge_body_work)

**Claim.** The colony can obtain food, blocks and poison relief from the body while managing exposure and unstable cultivated ground.

**Attack.** A green map and recipes in a list can appear generous while no one can produce or use their outputs; alternatively an invented penalty on every processed meal would make a stronger bargain than the sources describe.

**Dumb version.** Spawn finished blocks and meals; clear a poison by helper; call that the economy.

**Counterexample / picture.** A poisoned worker eats raw slime: the poison disappears, but no film is charged. Or a slime-grass farm quietly becomes permanently safe despite conversion being enabled.

**Other side / reuse.** Vanilla bills and ingestion already perform the work. Slime supplies inputs, the compressor/pit recipes and the coupled raw-slime dose; it needs no replacement production UI.

**Unsaid sweep.** First bill, repeated bills, insufficient ingredients and a 40-colonist workday test different problems: unavailable workflow, input/output accounting, refusal and aggregate chores. Compare a sown slime-grass plot with untouched slime grass and non-opted-in terrain.

**Boundary.** The coupled poison/film charge belongs to raw-slime ingestion and the established living-slime drinking service. It does not impose a new direct film charge on every rendered meal or block. Safe floors and resistance remain legitimate answers to ordinary contact.

**Disposition — does, with the stated boundary.** Retain ordinary production, the raw-slime cure plus absorption fee in the same ingestion, scoped farm reversion, solvent bills and ruins. Demonstrate the production and use of results. Do not require perpetual inconvenience once a colony prepares well.

**Pruning.** Reject a non-working useful-body loop or a free raw-slime cure. Individual recipe quantities, plant silhouettes and ledger prose are coverage or tuning, not new independent core leaves.

**Finite situations for later acceptance.**

- Normal compressor and pit bills with actual input consumption and usable output; solvent food cooked and eaten, with its toxic/drug effects checked separately.
- Poisoned raw-slime eater versus untreated poisoned pawn; verify poison relief and film together, with an injury/addiction exclusion.
- Sown versus unsown slime grass over repeated work, then one representative large-colony workday.

## 2. Absorption has a readable course and an exit (edge_ordinary_film)

**Claim.** Ordinary contact can impair and ultimately dissolve a colonist, but its stage and circumstances give the player an actionable response.

**Attack.** One hediff label can conceal different clocks, and a test which sets severity directly proves neither ordinary exposure nor a rescue.

**Dumb version.** Show a high-severity pawn and a passing rate calculation, without waiting for any actual contact, cure or terminal outcome.

**Counterexample / picture.** A heavily coated colonist leaves slime for ordinary soil and the player assumes all film will wipe away; or gear vanishes with a dissolved body.

**Other side / reuse.** RimWorld health information, letters and alerts already convey health races. The owner explicitly chose their existing UI; a critical-time message is permitted, not a new triage panel requirement.

**Unsaid sweep.** Group flesh/colonist and outsider contact by the shared ladder, then test resistance separately because it gates application. Early off-body reversal, advanced ordinary holding, drying-biome reversal, injected off-body progression and dissolution are different transitions. Map and caravan location resolution, save/load and a bed death can fail independently.

**Boundary.** Slimification itself adds no aggression. Early off-body wipe-off applies only below the existing threshold on the ordinary clock. Tagged drying country has priority even on the injected clock. Antidote before terminal dissolution clears film, not genes, the lasting mark, or an already dead person.

**Disposition — does, with the stated boundary.** Preserve the full ladder, ordinary-versus-injected identification, existing UI, drying geography and subterminal toxic treatment. Use actual contact and a completed untreated terminal arm; inspect exact victim gear and inventory. Numbers remain current defaults/tuning rather than a new owner-approved deadline.

**Pruning.** Reject an unreadable or inescapable authored race, colonist immunity, a missing terminal result or lost victim equipment. Do not add a dedicated triage interface or require immunity to every other game hazard.

**Finite situations for later acceptance.**

- Ordinary susceptible contact and a resistant control; early versus advanced film off-body.
- Advanced ordinary and injected patients in tagged drying contexts, including caravan location; save/load a progressing patient.
- Untreated dissolution in bed with labelled equipment/inventory; treated matching patient with actual item use and toxic cost.

## 4. The deliberate coma bargain can really be rescued (edge_friend_rescue)

**Claim.** Injection grants target plus rider, induces coma and accelerates absorption, leaving another capable pawn to administer the toxic antidote in time.

**Attack.** Seeing coma and a cure function does not establish that an ordinary friend can target, reach and finish the treatment job before death.

**Dumb version.** Invoke injection and antidote functions back to back; never order the friend to use the item.

**Counterexample / picture.** Two comatose patients share one friend; a treatment order is unavailable or fails to finish while the displayed clock continues. A solo recipient receives a warning but has no magic self-treatment.

**Other side / reuse.** UseItem and xenogermination coma supply the mechanics. The existing health UI and permitted remaining-time message supply the decision surface already chosen by the owner.

**Unsaid sweep.** Completed timely rescue versus an otherwise equivalent untreated terminal arm discriminate a real race. A late order tests job duration; an unavailable friend tests refusal/warning. Several patients within a 40-colonist scene test conflicting orders without inventing a bulk-rescue guarantee. Reload must preserve the injected flag and urgency.

**Boundary.** Rescue is possible with preparation, not guaranteed regardless of travel, missing medicine or competing needs. The warning currently occurs after injection; do not invent a pre-injection veto. Antidote kills film but does not promise to cancel the independent vanilla coma or erase acquired genes/social consequences.

**Disposition — does, with the stated boundary.** Use actual ordered injection and friend-administered treatment, verify completion/consumption before accepting cure, compare untreated dissolution, and retain the toxic price. An existing UI failure is delivery work, not a reopened owner UI question.

**Pruning.** Reject a race whose nominal rescue cannot be ordered or finished. Fair warning does not mean a guaranteed save after any delay.

**Finite situations for later acceptance.**

- Whole ordered injection and timely friend treatment with gene/rider/coma/film/toxicity observations.
- Untreated and too-late rescue outcomes, capable-friend absence, and a shared-friend multiple-patient situation.
- Reload a patient while the injected clock is active.
