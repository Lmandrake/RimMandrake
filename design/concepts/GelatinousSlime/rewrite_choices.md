# The Slime: what the rewrite chose, what it lost, and why

Companion to `design/concepts/GelatinousSlime/design.md`, written 2026-11-18 by the same pass. Its job is to let the
older files be deleted without fear: every distinct idea found in any source has a row saying what became of it.
Nothing has been deleted. The older files this design replaces are listed at the end.

**How the choices were made.** The owner's words of 2026-11-18: the biomes *"are going to be visited by a huge ship
on the run"*, crewed by Jawa *"way in over their head"*; *"anything that takes seasons to observe will simply be
missed"*; for 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 the *"OMG did you see that?"* of Jawa meeting alien creatures at close range is *"the
overriding priority"*. The pipeline holds two readings of the Slime. The locked sheet (2026-11-15/16) is everything
he has asked for at once: the body, the gene journey, the giant, the weapon made from it, a vault it opens, and
return from the dead. The cleanse proposal (2026-11-17) kept only the body and its bargain, switched off the gene
journey as *"another elective acquisition/rescue chain"*, and asked him whether that was right. Under the lens the
answer is no: one Jawa in a coma on open slime and the rest running with a needle is the stop, it takes a day, and
it was his first ask for this biome. So this design keeps the cleanse's body and puts the journey and the giant
back into 0.1.0; the weapon is 0.2.0; the vault and the return from the dead are "later", because one is the
campaign's and the other needs a laboratory. Four things he ruled on today's card are stated per biome: the stop's
outer edge, cover from orbit, fuel, and what the place does to the hull.

**Words used in "what happened".** *kept*; *kept, reshaped* (changed as stated); *moved* (scope bucket changed, from
and to given; "cleanse next" is the cleanse's switched-off list, "locked sheet" is `sheet.md`; this design uses
0.1.0, 0.2.0, later, dropped); *folded into shared machinery*; *lost*.

## The ones I would look at

1. **Return from the last touch.** His words: *"An ability to resurrect someone from the last time they touched
   the slime. Requires expensive helix tech."* And his question straight after, which the review calls the design:
   *"But are they the same?"* A leaf of the locked sheet. I moved it to "later" because it needs paid, powered
   archive growth and top-tier research, which is a settled laboratory. For a clan of six in which *every* death
   grieves everyone, it may be the most wanted thing in the biome, and a ship is exactly what would carry a body's
   last reading away with it. If one "later" is too late, it is this.
2. **The Unfiled Hull.** *"The oldest titanoslime swallowed a whole ship ages ago... a dark keel visible through
   the jelly, drifting as the colossus moves."* Got out only by leading the giant onto dry ground until it shrinks
   and the hull drops; then sold sealed to the Helix, stripped for *"a ship-grade part for the gravship"*, or
   opened. Recommended by the review and NOT CHOSEN by him on 2026-10-02, when he asked for the bomb instead. It is
   a salvage job on a living hill, for a ship part. Nothing in any of the five biomes of this run is more "very
   Jawa". Dropped, because the choice is his and he made it; listed first among the things the lens might change.
3. **The Landing You Cannot Take Back.** The only pitch about the ship landing here; also not chosen. Today's hull
   ruling reopens the subject, so it is `NEEDS HIM`, with a much smaller default.
4. **The Throat experiment.** *"Scoop tons of the living genetic database onto the gravship and pour it down the
   Glowing Throat — likely killing what you poured, maybe neutralizing a planet-scale hazard, with genuinely
   unknown results below."* Written into the sheet as something only a ship can do. Campaign layer; "later".
5. **The gene journey.** Not a loss: a reversal of the cleanse. If he reads the Slime as larder and hazard first
   and the gene as an extra, the row to change is in "Scope moves" and the question is in `NEEDS HIM`.
6. **The piece of the giant.** His ask, a leaf of the locked sheet, built. I put it at 0.2.0 and not 0.1.0: it is
   the biome's one answer to the Empire and an excellent accident, but a first stop is full without it.
7. **The dispute.** *"The four patches are lobes of a single body, joined beneath the basin floor... though the
   planet's residents dispute this."* And the fear that it becomes *"another Mnggal-Mnggal."* World texture that no
   stop shows. Lost from the design except for *"So far, it only reads."*
8. **Farms that fail.** *"Every ruin a legible story of someone who thought they'd beaten the conversion rate."* The
   ruins are in 0.1.0 as places; the conversion that made them is "later", since this crew does not farm.

Each line is one feature whose bucket changed against the locked sheet or the cleanse proposal. Revert by moving
the line in the design's Scope section.

  settles the conflict the cleanse flagged with the brief's first law.

- **The harvested piece, its short-lived ground and its shelf life:** locked sheet → 0.2.0 (the cleanse had it

- **A wounded giant shedding gelatids; the greater gappo's channel; the film shown on the body; pit rendering of
  toxic cargo:** cleanse next → 0.2.0.
- **Return from the last touch:** locked sheet → later.
- **The actual vault breach:** locked sheet → later (campaign layer).
- **Farm conversion as a promise:** cleanse first version → later.
- **Slime at the ship's contact points:** not in any source → 0.1.0, as a default pending his answer.
- **Stayed "later" from the cleanse's "next version":** the Helix as vendor, the Throat experiment, lingering
  footprints, oomb promotion and fission, Joining Water. Not moves.
- **Dropped as the cleanse recommended:** planet scoring and its rarity control. His to confirm.
- **New, not in any source:** the ship's deck as the first built floor; flying to dry country as the cure (asked
  as `NEEDS HIM`); the giant rolling over an Imperial emplacement; the Cauldron's poisoned crew curing themselves
  here. All four are readings of built or ruled behaviour.

## Sources read, and the count

figures are the last paragraph of this section.

the bedazzle review (60 KB: its plain-words page, the roster-fill section with the three giant stories, and the
turn-one rulings and ticket table; the rest by search for the ship; not its defect census, fauna census, scorecard,
slate table, the five consult ideas in full, or the two pitched rites); the mod brief (its opening ruling and
companion laws, and its headings; not sections 1 to 11); the titan spec (its opening ask and summary paragraph; not
the specification); the gene lists (the curation principles; not the lists). Read by heading only: the cleanse's
next-version list.

`stage2_argument.md`, `stage2_flagship.md`, `tree.json`, `scenes.json`, the stage reviews and `exam_evidence`; the
cleanse's `definition.md`, `ambiguities.md`, `tickets.md`, `models.md`, `bars.json`, `census.json` and
`review.json`; the designer rehearsal's other files; the mechanics review's Slime table; `edible_genepack_native_mechanism.md`;
and the other files under `design/` that mention the Slime in passing. This is the thinnest read of the five biomes
in this run against the size of its sources, and the mod brief and titan spec in particular should be read whole
before anything is deleted.
