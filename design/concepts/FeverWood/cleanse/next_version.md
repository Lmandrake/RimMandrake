# Deferred content and retirement recommendations

All allocations are the Designer’s delegated proposal for this first version. Next-version effects default off; code, defs, art and roster identities remain. A retirement is only recommended, never enacted here. The owner’s already-cut theft/raid-back/parasite and declined rite stay settled exclusions; they are not new future work.

- **Next version, switched off** — Standalone mirror-break incident raising agitation and hush. (fw_mirror_break).
  Why outside land/life/signature: Sentinel and actual encounters already supply the warning. A separate scheduled theatre incident is not necessary land, life or the signature. Ticket: T_MIRROR_BREAK.

- **Recommend retiring; held off pending decision** — Consumable mirror-list item, use effect and purchased intelligence overlay. (fw_mirror_list).
  Why outside land/life/signature: Recommend retiring the duplicate information economy: the core already needs readable current water signs, and buying a consumable adds no essential experience. No deletion is authorised by this proposal. Ticket: T_MIRROR_LIST.

- **Next version, switched off** — Crafted radioactive treatment, designation, hauling and map-wide adult quiet. (fw_suppression).
  Why proposed outside this first version: retain the combat-only branch-and-shore scope, subject to the owner-pages treatment question. The owner’s no-fight ladder and validated water-memory “treatment” wording are contrary evidence; this deferral is proposed weakening, not a settled extra classification or authority to loosen his bar. Preserve the ruled reagent and campaign restrictions. Ticket: T_SUPPRESSION, held from application pending that decision.

- **Next version, switched off** — Captive tank upkeep, products, teaching benefit and breach/deconstruction outcomes. (fw_tank).
  Why outside land/life/signature: The shore loop remains understandable and escapable without a prison industry. Keeping the teaching discount would covertly make the signature depend on a moved economy. Ticket: T_TANK.

- **Next version, switched off** — Armed escapee travels to water and currently installs an adult immediately. (fw_escape_install).
  Why outside land/life/signature: Only captive industry produces this cycle; defer it with that industry. Its immediate installation contradicts the approved delay and must never run by default. Ticket: T_ESCAPE.

- **Next version, switched off** — Several-day establishment at a particular pool; only the right poison there intervenes. (fw_maturation).
  Why outside land/life/signature: A future captive consequence, not a second core replenishment system. Preserve the November decision without substituting combat, removal jobs or immediate adult revival. Ticket: T_MATURATION.

- **Next version, switched off** — World-held-young census makes water encounters bolder. (fw_brood).
  Why outside land/life/signature: External trade stock and caravan counts are an independent pressure source; core danger must not secretly depend on an unused captive economy. Ticket: T_BROOD.

- **Next version, switched off** — Peaceful release, reachable water return and durable owed-gift queue. (fw_return_gift).
  Why outside land/life/signature: Returning captive stock is a bargain outside land, ordinary life and the shore loop. Its existing adult-revival side effect is coupling, not need. Ticket: T_RETURN.

- **Next version, switched off** — Campaign Sporefall display stock, goodwill and trade story hooks, claimed in history. (fw_display_campaign).
  Why outside land/life/signature: Campaign story is not necessary biome content. Lower-tier source is unavailable here, so current installation is unknown; generic support is separately inventoried. Ticket: T_DISPLAY.

- **Next version, switched off** — Live bait stake designation, nonlethal downing, hauling, restraint and release. (fw_lure_bait).
  Why outside land/life/signature: Deliberately attracting factions is a separate wager from living beside water. The shore loop does not call a stake. Ticket: T_BAIT.

- **Next version, switched off** — Delayed hostile fronts converge on bait; pending waves persist. (fw_two_fronts).
  Why outside land/life/signature: Faction raids and their second arrival are a separate encounter engine. Ordinary vanilla raids can still frighten herds without this subsystem. Ticket: T_FRONTS.

- **Next version, switched off** — Reactive inland ant-dungeon layout and walls/roof/queen rooms. (fw_hive_geometry).

Ordinary animal/plant bodies and harvests are not evicted with their moved behavior. In particular, seepril still yields ordinary oil, static boles still anchor routes, birds still fly/call, native predators still feed, and frightened thornbugs still refuse nectar. The forbidden local-heat declaration cannot be re-enabled even while its retirement awaits the owner. A literal whole-body adult rise remains reserved to the separate campaign plot and is neither an ambient feature nor a newly commissioned next-version event.

- **Next version, switched off** — Finite potable cistrel cup and its thirst/drink/reservation adapter. (fw_cistrel_drinking).

Keeping an undefined safe-water action is not necessary to the shore signature. Align default text with the absent action; normal water provision belongs to the loaded thirst owner. Ticket: T_CISTREL.
