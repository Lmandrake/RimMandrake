## Real construction choices

The direction is already ruled. These are implementation alternatives under it, not a new owner vote.

For metal treatment, prefer persistent Cauldron load plus the normal medical-operation seam, with a narrow bill-eligibility extension for saved low traces. Keep direct acid-liquid contact and item immunity under their actual source rules and prove them separately.

Changing the approved duration to the current 4000 ticks would reverse the owner ruling and is excluded. The builder chooses the smallest implementation satisfying the timed scene.

For gas continuity, serialize sufficient active-front state in the existing gas component, with bounded straight/diagonal neighbor offers. Reconstructing fronts from saved active cells may work only if the mid-burn scene demonstrates no second ignition or lost attribution. A new parallel combustion engine adds needless scope.
