# The Cauldron: what the rewrite chose, what it lost, and why

Companion to `design/concepts/Cauldron/design.md`, written 2026-11-18 by the same pass. Its job is to let the older
files be deleted without fear: every distinct idea found in any source has a row saying what became of it. Nothing
has been deleted. The older files this design replaces are listed at the end.

**How the choices were made.** The owner's words of 2026-11-18: the biomes *"are going to be visited by a huge ship
on the run"*, crewed by Jawa *"way in over their head"*; *"anything that takes seasons to observe will simply be
missed"*; for 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 the *"OMG did you see that?"* of Jawa meeting alien creatures at close range is *"the
overriding priority"*. The Cauldron had two pipeline readings before this one, and they pull opposite ways. The
locked sheet (2026-11-15/16) is a full settlement: seventeen leaves ending in *"a prepared forty-colonist routine"*.
The cleanse proposal (2026-11-17) cut that down to one signature (heed the warning, protect, cap and refine) and
switched off nearly every creature behaviour to get there. Under the lens the cleanse cut the wrong half: the
balloon that explodes, the giant that stamps out fires and the animals that freeze before a bloom are the "OMG",
and they are built. This design keeps the cleanse's spine, brings the creatures back into 0.1.0, and sends the
settlement to "later". Four things he ruled on today's card are stated per biome: the stop's outer edge, cover from
orbit, fuel, and what the place does to the hull.

**Words used in "what happened".** *kept*; *kept, reshaped* (changed as stated); *moved* (scope bucket changed, from
and to given; "next version" is the cleanse's switched-off list, "locked sheet" is `sheet.md`; this design uses
0.1.0, 0.2.0, later, dropped; a cleanse "next version" item that lands in "later" is not a move); *folded into
shared machinery*; *lost*.

## The ones I would look at

1. **The prepared day.** The adopted root ends: *"Preparation earns manageable daily work and worthwhile industry,
   with risky expeditions and wildlife still demanding choices"*, and his answer of 2026-11-10 chose *"Dependable
   industry, risky expeditions"* as what makes settlement worthwhile. It passed an exam at 30 of 30. I moved it to
   "later" because a ship does not settle. If he reads the Cauldron as the one place the crew does dig in for a
   long haul, this is the row to revert, and the tree stands as it is.
2. **The engine underfoot.** *"The ground here talks all day — rush and groan and gurgle, the slow engine of the
   forest's chemistry."* He reversed the sheet's silence to this on 2026-09-28, then took bespoke sound out on
   2026-10-03. What is left of the best idea in the biome (the alarm is the engine *faltering*) is a visual: puffs
   thinning and animals going still. Lost as audio; the rite the Engine Hour, *"Listen until you can count it. When
   it stops, run"*, is in "later".
3. **"Quiet in a way that is wrong."** The frozen sheet's original voice: *"almost nothing here is fast, and almost
   nothing here makes noise on purpose."* Overturned by him, then half-overturned again when seven fast imports
   were wired in. Lost, rightly, but it was the eeriest line in the sheet.
4. **The ship grows scabs.** *"The ship becomes the biome's newest tree: it is being plated too, and the biome pays
   you a little for the insult."* Neither taken nor struck on 2026-09-28, before the ship lens and before today's
   hull ruling. The built acid wear is a damage tick; this was a picture. Asked as `NEEDS HIM`.
5. **Rhossak renewing the forest.** Three kin rooting a new grove was the cleanse's runner-up signature and is
   named in the adopted root. It is seasons-long by nature. "Later".
6. **The suush's burst and the vexxiss's fire-warding.** The cleanse switched both off as second systems. I moved
   both into 0.1.0. They are built, the vexxiss was his own redesign (*"attacks igniters and puts fires out"*), and
   they are the two funniest things that can happen to a Jawa here.
7. **Oomo's grimace.** The wettest-looking land on the planet *"where no drop can be kept"*, a god who dislikes
   it, hauled-in water tanks read as *"offerings that failed"*, and the Filtered Cup: *"He will not keep water
   here. So we do it for him, through the cloth, slowly. Show him the cup."* Campaign layer and rites; "later". The
   ship's nine gods ride along as running personas, so a god's dislike of a landing site is closer to the lens than
   most rites.
8. **The seismograph.** Cut by him (*"I don't like that idea."*). It was the muted player's alarm. The timed
   message now does that job. Listed only so the cut is not mistaken for an oversight.

## Scope moves

Each line is one feature whose bucket changed against the cleanse proposal of 2026-11-17 or the locked sheet.
Revert by moving the line in the design's Scope section.

- **Suush burst:** next version → 0.1.0. Built; the first thing a nervous crew will do is shoot it.
- **Vexxiss drinking vents, prying taps, warding fire:** next version → 0.1.0. Built; in the locked sheet's
  `vent_competition` leaf; his redesign.
- **Native stillness before a bloom:** next version → 0.1.0. It is in the adopted root's own text (*"nearby
  animals' stillness"*). The suush's proposed hover-while-still rule stays out: flight is not judged unattended.
- **The corroded ruin and study of its rig:** next version → 0.1.0. Salvage with a lesson in it; four live checks
  passed 2026-10-21.
- **The acid-worn hull and its liner:** next version → 0.1.0. His ruling of 2026-11-18: hull wear is in 0.1.0.
- **Vexxiss water poisoning and tracks; zisska fire-spit; bedbug paralysis; garsulix death blast; fexxil venom;
  kissaveth gas:** next version → 0.2.0. Creature and plant moments a crew can meet in a week; each is a second
  hazard, so not the first version.
- **Lined machines and the plate-only door:** next version → 0.2.0. What vexxith is for.
- **Dew beads, dew-saturated plants, vent flower rings, shore nettles:** next version → 0.2.0. Built looks that
  change within days.
- **Acoustic sounder readings here:** recommended for retiring → 0.2.0. A ship part; the pilot kept it at 0.2.0
  for the Greentide.
- **The prepared forty-colonist day:** locked sheet → later.
- **Rhossak seeding and grove-rooting:** locked sheet (`rhossak_renewal`) → later, as the cleanse also moved it.
  Binding and galls stay in 0.1.0.
- **Stayed "later" from the cleanse's "next version":** ossrith uranium refining, the trunk coat, Oomo's shrine.
  Not moves.
- **Dropped as the cleanse recommended:** the tile scorer. Crystalmit bricks wait for his word.
- **New, not in any source:** the crew's salvage yard as what a gas blast throws; the crew choosing which vents to
  cap as its defence plan; the vexxiss going for Imperial troopers who ignite gas; a siege camp sitting through a
  bloom; the load leaving with the ship. All are readings of built or ruled behaviour.
