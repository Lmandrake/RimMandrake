# The Cauldron — proposed Design Version 0.1.0

This is the Designer's scoped proposal, not a lock, implementation, current proof, owner certification or permission to lift the build hold. The original concept/owner words remain untouched. The new cleansing brief authorises the version decisions below; prior owner keeps remain future scope, never rescinded. No existing waiver is reopened (the current sheet lists none).

The Cauldron is a wet mineral forest in permanent dusk, with rainless lightning and chemical pools that cannot simply be drunk. The ground's vents quiet before a metal-heavy bloom, giving workers a chance to shelter or seal themselves before the exposed acquire lasting injury. Capping that same chemistry turns its drifting, combustible danger into collected condensate and useful supplies, so preparation buys a working settlement while unprotected errands still cost something.

Land: dark ordinary/rich soils, mineral trunks and ground cover, scattered twilight, chemical fog/condensation, rainless storms, acidic ambient exposure and genuinely hazardous pools on the supplied local map. “Dusk” is the diffuse, shadowless dim weather tint, not a fixed clock phase or brightness; “wet” is the moisture impression from fog, dewfall and existing plant art, without deferred dew beads or texture swaps.

Life: the current admitted plant and animal cast lives, feeds, breeds and supplies native food, wood, metal, medicine and protective material; mineral diets, genuine flight and bound rhossak are ordinary native identity, with no roster evictions.

Signature: The vent cycle warns of poisonous release, and the colony turns the same chemistry from exposure and fire risk into dependable supplies by sheltering, sealing, capping and refining.

Runner-up: Rhossak-driven forest renewal. Sleeping seeders and kin-rooted groves form their own cause/effect cycle; deleting that cycle leaves native animals, ordinary regrowth and chemical livelihood whole. Binding and galls remain ordinary life, while seeding/rooting move off by default. The old wild-vexxiss competition branch also moves: production can be dependable without an additional animal suppression/prying clock.

## Why a player reaches for it

A scavenger colony reaches for a place whose organisms themselves yield metal, whose raw chemistry can be made useful, and whose danger can be understood and planned around. Unlike vanilla toxic fallout as an occasional global event, the native local sources and permanent metal load make routes and return trips consequential; ordinary roofs, clothing stats, animal husbandry and research remain the familiar verbs. Alpha Biomes' original mineral/poison aesthetics are a reference, not an authority to rebuild donor mechanics: the current owned roster and shared service engines are the actual implementation basis. Expected enjoyment is a prediction: recognising the warning and completing a safe shift should feel like earned competence. No bar pretends to certify fun.

The lazy counterproposal is generic fog, harmless coloured water and a long-lived poison meter, with a machine fed by debug tanks. It fails place, pool_use, lasting_cost and chemical_work. The less obvious lazy version passes isolated work but needs a clear-sky lock for a functioning full colony. That fails the sole whole bar. Models explicitly address this risk before anybody balances one isolated rate repeatedly.

## Using it: first hour, routine and scale

Arrival needs normal starting food, drinkable water, materials and a shelter plan; the mod does not promise a self-sufficient naked start. Before sending a worker outdoors, the player can recognise chemical ground/pools and inspect the vent and timber. A roof is the earliest ambient protection. Actual pool chemistry—not the word 'toxic' or its colour—governs drinking, contact and storage. Nearby means the declared reachable work route in the warning range, not the whole map. A disabled or absent vent on an old local save is not a demonstration of the normal shipped warning.

Ordinary work uses normal designations, construction, research, hauling, cooking and medical bills. The worker selects thornwood by growth, visible metal and inspect assay. A completed successful harvest pays wood and bioaccumulated steel; interrupted or failed harvesting does not. The assay reads expected yield and does not promise identical random rounding on a small trace. Demanding means more work than easy ordinary timber under matched worker/tool/light conditions, not a pinned work stat. Old age overlays and extra variants are not needed for this decision.

The routine chemical loop uses Electricity then ordinary Filter-Works bench research, a tap placed exactly over a vent, a compatible tank touching its footprint and an ordinary powered cartridge-fed converter. Tapping stops new local emission; it does not vacuum existing gas or cure workers. Condensate produces reagent. Hazardous water follows its actual detox route to fresh output; condensate is not asserted to turn directly into drinking water. The tap is not itself a FlowWorks network member: a distant tank connected only by a hose is not a working collector. Output room, input, power and cartridges are real requirements with readable stops and successful resumption. Tap saturation can leave uncollected future flow; accepted stored stock cannot silently disappear.

At forty colonists, roofs/complete gear protect routine labour, food and water work can actually complete, and source-derived output is useful enough to justify work. It is not a promise of automatic profit or freedom from hauling. Medical needs consume real advanced supplies. Imported starting stock is finite and recorded, not replenished by the harness. The coupled model searches the regime where illness reduces labour, labour shortfalls delay supplies/protection, and those delays increase illness. Default ecology, weather, damage, AI, shared engines and needs remain on throughout.

## Life as the player meets it

Keep the actual inline RM plants, including ordinary expansion species. Tsevrix supplies toxic pulp with its normal stove roast into safe food. Selvix yields herbal medicine, which is not an advanced metal purge. Xithess/sessarix supply ordinary fuel; sessarix's substrate restriction remains ordinary habitat. Ixalith is ordinary mineral beauty; fexxil keeps physical path obstruction and a shard harvest while venom is off. Kissaveth remains a plant with its gas-on-cut/damage behaviours off. The other mineral flora grows through the existing low-light/stalled-growth services; visual population cannot be supplied by deferred garden spawners.

Suush is a docile atmospheric feeder with no ordinary food need and real flight. Designer hush choice: while airborne it hovers in place; hush never forces it to land or resets/bypasses normal flight limits and cooldown. Ordinary AI resumes after falter. Grounded or naturally transitioning suush hold horizontal position as their engine flight state permits. Job plus Pawn_FlightTracker reads decide these transitions unattended; flight visuals wait for an owner-present sitting, with no images of flight. Vexxiss remains a large living plate source through tame shearing, without water conversion, fire policing, vent drinking/prying or tracking at defaults. Zisska remains a mineral rasper with toxic meat and butcher steel; fire-spit is future scope. Eskith's ordinary flock movement makes its eligible native hush a recognisable change. Rhossak and galled rhossak bind to existing thornwood, rebind when it dies, and read as unbound ordinary animals when none remains; no dryad disappearance. Galled animals retain their food product and never seed.

Bedbugs retain ordinary predatory bites, eggs/hatching; paralysis is off. Crystalmits retain real mineral feeding and ordinary reproduction/carcass products; periodic random manufactured bricks are recommended for retirement and switched off pending that decision. Garsulix retains floating predation, ordinary jelly and fission; its death blast is off. Ossrith retains milk and shed crystals; the extra uranium refinery recipe is off. Sikka retains eggs and silk. These ordinary food/material products and diets define a living resource ecology: deleting feeding starves the animals, deleting normal reproduction exhausts it, and deleting useful husbandry products removes a reason to keep it. Exact product counts/cadences remain checks. Body-excreted mineral products are retained because the sourced land/life is biological metal, not a conventional pasture.

Native animals can drink their environmental water under the standing ruling. Meat-bearing native species must expose the toxic/prized food truth in the appropriate service/tier; 'all animals are food' is not asserted for creatures without meat. The free RM roster must stand on its own; campaign species and prized-meat patches extend it. Shared cast remains with its existing behaviours and owners. Decay drake (Miasma), bileworm (Warscar), visceral (Utinni), mullgoth and campaign imports are recorded as shared roster obligations; no home is reassigned. Multi-homing of each resolved species must be read from the actual patched rosters (C_roster/T38); source locality does not establish exclusive habitat. Existing source and art keeps override older blanket no-grazer/no-fast/no-green descriptions where explicitly ruled; this proposal makes no import eviction or art redo.

## What changes in the colony's story

Outdoor work becomes a choice with a memory. Before a bloom, active vents visibly quiet and eligible awake idle wild suush/zisska/eskith stop travelling. The unattended visual warning review judges vent puffs and walking zisska/eskith; suush hush uses job and flight-tracker state, with its visual reserved for the owner-present sitting. A warning message is necessary readability, not an optional new event chain. Sleeping, fighting, downed, owned and otherwise ineligible animals do not become statues. Vanilla sounds remain; no bespoke soundscape is inferred. The owner’s about-hour warning is preserved in the timing rule/check; it is not the unmeasured weather interpolation length. The worker's shelter route must fit the actual cue, and the first harmful dose is separately timed.

A roof or functioning complete environmental seal prevents new ambient acid/bloom injury. Designer seal rule: RM_AcidAir and RM_VentBloomExposure both read max(final loaded VacuumResistance, final loaded ToxicEnvironmentResistance) >= 1.0 at each exposure opportunity. Apparel, genes, hediffs and race all count; full ToxicEnvironmentResistance without a suit qualifies. Full ToxicResistance alone is not this seal, though its existing bloom resistance route remains. Below full seal each hazard retains its actual partial-resistance calculation. Acid-proof cloth surviving does not prove the wearer is sealed; this item-versus-wearer choice is a rule/check, not an independent bar. Player, visitor and hostile bodies use the same ambient rule except actual species/condition immunity. Direct pool contact, material corrosion and combustion smoke are separate shared routes; a roof is not a force field against them. Moving from one state to another, removing a helmet or losing roof coverage is tested at the next real exposure opportunity.

Metal load remains after shelter, ordinary care, travel and reload. A successful normal advanced-medicine operation purges it, including small hidden saved traces; ordinary herbal/industrial care does not. Designer default is MedicineUltratech, with ingredients/skill/work costs tuned as checks. Failed/interrupted treatment follows real vanilla accounting and never grants a free cure. Actual acid can leave permanent facial disfigurement and sensory-organ damage or loss, with real Health/capacity consequences; a label is insufficient. This is possibility, not a guarantee on every burn. Vanilla/DLC exceptional healing or prosthetic replacement can still repair the consequence through its own rules.

An ignition burns the gas fuel, including over water and bare rock, with toxic smoke and readable shared blasts that displace loose goods. A gas-free corridor or sealed barrier stops combustion; a pool alone does not. 'Connected' means a current path of combustible cells through passable transitions, with both cardinal sides open for diagonal passage. 'Thick' means the tunable ignition threshold, not an extra artistic promise. A maintained clear gap is re-read across every propagation opportunity, not asserted from its starting arrangement. Gas drift, overflow and neighbour ignition must all obey the same seal boundary. Save/load preserves active front and original ignition attribution, even if vanilla ground Fire has already died on fuel-less terrain.

## Costs, failures and readable signs

Costs are exposed work time, finite safe food/water, gear and medicine, materials, power/cartridges, safe storage and colonist attention. Source-rich timber takes work; containment and protection consume effort instead of paying a free resource stream. Permanent load raises the cost of careless repeated errands. Ambient acid injury, hazardous pool contact and a gas fire remain attributable different risks.

Failure signs: vent output/puffs and state text explain source state; warning and native stillness explain bloom onset; Health describes persistent load and lasting part harm; gas/smoke/fire and blast source show ignition; tank contents, bank inspection and converter reason show shortage/blockage; bound/unbound text explains rhossak behaviour. No animal or pawn silently vanishes. Readable death, corpse/remains or a named ordinary departure explains loss. A setting change does not pretend it medically cured a worker or destroyed existing goods.

Known source gaps, not new questions: RM_VentMetalLoad.xml still naturally decays (-0.08/day); no purge recipe was located. Bloom reads toxic-resistance stats without the acid path's full-seal check. Falter is currently tied to weather blending. RM_VentGasFront is not serialized, and ignition/overflow neighbour offers require sealed-corner audit. Intended pool identity through actual use remains unknown. The old gear availability gate mixes suits/containers with moved upgrades/hull rig, and ordinary research currently depends on the moved rig. These all have construction/instrument tickets, rather than assertions of delivered proof.

## Edges

This version has land, life and a vent-cycle livelihood. It adds no rites, quests, bespoke lore incidents, special study journey, ship maintenance loop, animal fire police, water-converting beast, tracking game, alternative growth engine or extra plant/death ignition system. Existing code and defs for those things remain behind labelled off switches; no retirement is executed. The extra age-based coat is already specified but not found in runtime; it remains future scope. The excluded seismometer and bespoke audio remain excluded and are not asked again.

No worldgen feature, in any version. The old scorer/rarity UI contradicts that ruling: recommend its retirement, make it inert in supported builds, never park it in next version. Local-map content preparation and saved-site fixtures do not create a different planet. Zero tiles is expected and is not evidence of absence. All DLC is assumed. No unowned bridge/game/code/state change is part of this pass. Nothing here lifts the owner hold. The first fresh review identified definition/evidence gaps; these edits address them, and the second look remains pending.

## Neighbours

CreatureBehaviors supplies home tether and ordinary shared creature services; its track grid remains independent of deferred Cauldron print metadata. ExplosiveGrowth owns the stalled terminator growth regime. EnvironmentalHazards keeps shared condition/contact/scatter ownership; default Cauldron venom/ruin consumers are off. Wreckage supplies shared salvage where encountered, with no bespoke Cauldron ruin needed. Bones appends normal butchery byproducts without duplicating the corpse. FlowWorks owns fluid identity, compatibility, storage and conversion; use its actual registry, never a parallel table. ExplosiveKnockback owns blasts and throwing; Cauldron supplies gas ignition/front/instigator events. VEF services support mineral feeding, floating, reproduction and ordinary animal products under their existing guards. A missing optional provider can be load-safe without being evidence that a service-dependent behaviour works.

Scarlands/Warscar owns the aerosol-screen polluted-biome response and shared creatures/acid service. Keep the guarded existing consumer without making its independent behaviour a Cauldron bar. Forge's opted-in acid-air service is not redesigned or switched off as collateral to Cauldron hull deferral. Utinni owns canon cast, prized-meat layer and the deferred shrine. RM exotic names are franchise-free; canon and campaign content remain in their appropriate naming tiers. Protected art is not redrawn by this proposal.

The fixed planet supplies time-of-day/location and ordinary air temperature. Dusk is a scene/light/weather description, never an exception to shared Direct Sun. Where an actual supplied tail tile has direct solar geometry, use the same weather attenuation and shade rule as everywhere else; do not invent a Cauldron heat offset to match a poem. Chemical vents have no geothermal/steam heat. This reconciles old absolute-light prose under the newer universal geometric ruling.

## Settings

Retain persistent searchable/scrollable controls, reset affordances and clear application timing. Defaults mean this proposal: ordinary roster, timber/assay/flecks, native binding, weather/warning/local exposure, gas transport/ignition/smoke, tap banking, ordinary research/converter, ambient body acid, material immunity and craftable suits/containers on; listed next-version extras off in Cauldron. The existing Forge hull consumer keeps its shared behaviour and protection access; the new Cauldron hull opt-in is separate. Major feature controls must include the retained animal-specific diets/products/binding and necessary preparation routes where those are currently only defs; T33/T38 complete that coverage without removing roster membership. Machine toggles use the service owner.

New live effects stop on their advertised tick/job update; startup recipe/comp/availability changes require restart and are labelled. Local dressing switches affect only future local maps, never existing saved world tiles. All-off leaves stock, health, current animals and built things in the save, prevents new feature activity and resumes without duplicate output when re-enabled. Feature-issued jobs must not act after their off boundary; unrelated jobs continue. Switching persistence off is not treatment. Existing unsupported cross-biome toggles stay off; no new cross-biome validation promise. The obsolete rarity/worldgen option is unavailable, not renamed to local placement.

The next-version list and each switch-off ticket name every moved behaviour, its callers and the default/opt-in state test. Mixed code is decoupled: retained vents initialize without gardens, bindings without seeding/rooting, gas attribution without a warden, normal research without rig analysis, suits/containers without upgraded machines or hull maintenance, and ordinary expanded flora without toxic cut/contact effects.

## Four projected stories

First hour: The new arrivals stand on dark ground under rainless lightning. A forager identifies the hazardous pool and keeps the starting drinkable water. A worker judges thornwood by its mineral maturity, but first helps finish a roof. The vents quiet and idle natives still; recalling the nearby worker before the bloom is a real action, not a hidden test flag. These are projected scenes, not measured current behaviour.

Ordinary week: The colony completes ordinary research and builds a tap with a touching tank. A cartridge-fed Filter-Works gives actual reagent and treats the separately delivered dangerous water. Husbandry brings plate and food products; ordinary harvesting replaces used timber. A blocked output tank explains the halted machine, and making room resumes it without losing the stored input. The warning still matters to outdoor hauling, while roofed shifts proceed.

Worst day: A worker removes a necessary helmet on an exposed trip while a cloud bridges the pool. A normal ignition carries across the water and throws loose goods; a genuinely sealed compartment contains the combustion. Shelter prevents further ambient injury but does not remove acquired metal or repair the acid-damaged eye. The colony must provide a real advanced medical purge for metal, and treat lasting organ loss by existing extraordinary routes if available. Losses have readable causes and remains.

Season's turn: There is no terrestrial spring/light shift on this tidally locked terminator; 'season' is a game observation interval. Through a full such interval, weather, ecology, ordinary jobs and vent hazards run together. Halfway through, the colony saves and reloads with work unfinished. It later processes water before forestry instead of harvesting first; finite supplies and actual protection still govern the result. The forest remains living, prepared work remains possible and dangerous expeditions remain choices. If the season instead becomes continuous gas, ecological collapse, deadlocked work or recurring exceptions, the whole mod fails its whole experience regardless of how many isolated checks passed.
