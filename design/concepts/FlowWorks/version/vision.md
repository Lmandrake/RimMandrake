# FlowWorks 0.1.0: the vision

Written only from the root paragraph he approved (`record.json` root, OWNER:1793794915205594887), `sheet.md` and his cards of 2026-11-05. No sentence is inferred. Convergent and Divergent are measured against this text.

1. FlowWorks lets you dig canals, pits, moats and trenches into the ground, and the liquids already on the map (water, tar, fuel, slime) flow into what you dug and settle at one level.
2. Every liquid comes from a real supply: the pond you draw from visibly drops and refills slowly, and a body at the map edge is endless.
3. Its main job is deadly earthworks: moats, covered pit traps, sluices and drawbridges that you fill, drain and set alight at the moment you choose.
4. It is judged first on one raid: raiders swim a deep moat under a raised drawbridge and tire, a sluice drains it, the drained hole holds them as prisoners, and chemfuel pumped in from above is lit by a colonist tossing a flame from the rim.
5. A pit reads as a hole with deep walls; ladders, climbing gear and jumps are the only ways in and out, and raiders make a ladder to fetch comrades who fell in.
6. Ground within two squares of a filled canal is wetted, so plants there have water; wetted ground is never standing water.
7. Pipes, pumps, tanks and hoses are one network moving the same real litres from the same supply.
8. Every feature has superb Mod Settings; there is no worldgen and no multiplayer support.
