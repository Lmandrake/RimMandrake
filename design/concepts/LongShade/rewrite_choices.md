# The Long Shade: what the rewrite chose, what it lost, and why

## Second reader, 2026-11-18

A second reader, who did not write the design or this page, read the Long Shade's sheet, sitting, review and shade
ideation to their ends, his rulings on the ledger, and the mod's own definitions, and checked the design against
them (item `RIMMANDRAKE_RECHECK_LANTERNDEEPS_TO_RUSTCATHEDRAL_CHOICES`).

**Rows added: 103**, in the section "Added by the second reader" below. The page now has 232 rows. Eight of
the first read's rows were wrong or stale and are corrected in place; each says so.

**The three most valuable things the first read missed**

1. **The ship's shade filling up is his own pick, and the hull wear he asked for is already built here.** The first
   read presented the Shipfall Commons as a cleanse runner-up it was bringing back. It was picked by his card on
   2026-09-30 (*"small animals take the ramp shadow, herds press the hull, pirrik circle, eventually the gloomcast
   walks over"*). And where the first read wrote *"no shield is invented here"*, the built ship-shield frame
   already sees one hazard on this biome's own weather table: the sandstorm. A ship that sits through one without
   its particulate screen wears today. His ruling on that screen also says it *"repels small animals"*, which
   sets a clean hull against game at the ramp.
2. **Two ideas the design kept as "never ruled" he had cut; and he had admitted seven it barely mentions.** His
   words of 2026-09-29, on the ledger: *"No on 6 7. Will consider the rest later."* Six was the gnomon line and
   seven the farm on the horizon with its heliograph. On 2026-10-08 he admitted, by card, the empty patch, the
   tollok, the stampede, lure awnings, the mirror field, the harrok and the Jawa clan that comes back. The empty
   patch (*"a perfect, deep, dew-ringed shadow with nothing in it is the loudest warning on the map"*) is now in
   the design's first day.
3. **The biome on disk is not the biome in the September sheets.** On 2026-10-05 (*"Go ahead and do the moves and
   cuts now please."*) the ultracactus he invented went to the Stillsand with three animals, two more went to
   other biomes and four were cut. The design still sent the crew home with ultracactus pad. And three plants the
   first read never met are built and growing: the shadespire (*"the only vertical that grows"*), maidenbloom
   (his name), and pavecrust, whose scraps *"burn slowly and hot"* and harvest to chemfuel. The design said
   nothing here is a fuel.

Also found: his question of 2026-09-24 to every biome (*"how can this biome uniquely add something to the
ship?"*) and this biome's answer on that register, the long-shade mast, which the design had under "recommended
for dropping"; the swimmer's road in full (it heads for the largest ring of dew, which is the ship's; hardpan
stops it; droids are ignored); the unread three quarters of the shade ideation, with its best lines; the rule
that crew react to heat and never plan routes applied to one more line the ideation had the other way.

**How the choices were made.** The owner's words of 2026-11-18: the biomes *"are going to be visited by a huge ship
on the run"*, crewed by Jawa *"way in over their head, adapted only to their parent desert biomes along roads
trading scrap"*; *"anything that takes seasons to observe will simply be missed"*; for 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 the *"OMG
did you see that?"* is *"the overriding priority"*. And his later test: *"you should always look for solutions that
make you say Very Jawa."*

The Long Shade turns the lens round. It is the desert with roads, herd beasts and a dead sandcrawler in it, so the
crew is on something like home ground and the alien thing in the scene is their own ship: the largest shadow for
miles, on a map where shade is the only currency. The older documents had already found this and then set it
aside. The sheet's central player consequence is *"Building a roof is the most powerful act available here"*; a
2026-09-29 pass wrote *"a landed ship is already the largest harbour for miles by the grid's own logic"*; it was
built as the Shipfall Commons; and the cleanse of 2026-11-17 made it the runner-up and switched it off, because for
a settled colony ordinary roofs do the same job. For a ship on the run it is the stop. So this design keeps the
cleanse's signature whole (real and false shelter decide exposure; natives rest, dash, rest) and brings three
built, small, ship-and-scavenger pieces back into the first version: the ship's shade filling up, the heaps in the
shadows, and the loaded dead out in the sun.

**Words used in "what happened".** *kept*; *kept, reshaped* (changed as stated); *moved* (scope bucket changed, from
and to given; the cleanse used "first version", "next version, switched off" and "recommend retiring", and this
design uses 0.1.0, 0.2.0, later, dropped; a cleanse "next version" item that lands in "later" is not a move);
*folded into shared machinery*; *lost*.

## The ones I would look at

1. **The smoke calendar.** *"The calendar of this biome is set by fires it cannot see"* is, as an earlier pass
   said, the sheet's strangest sentence. Haze as relief: *"shade for everybody at once, and the whole biome moves,
   feeds and breeds while it lasts."* Its second half (ash that feeds the ground and locks the sand) was cut on
   2026-10-23. The haze survives, built, and I left it in 0.2.0. It can happen within a stop and it may be the
   only cover from orbit this biome ever gets. If one thing moves up, I would move this.
2. **The wreck road and the dead crawler.** *"Greed and guilt, together. Every hulk is a haul of steel and
   components, and every hulk you strip deletes a stepping stone."* He ruled it in with the words *"Cool
   inhabited vision."* A Jawa crew finding a dead sandcrawler with the whole country sheltering in its shadow is
   the lens exactly. It is built. I held it at 0.2.0 only to keep the first version small enough to pass, and
   because the clan that returns for the hull wants to arrive with it.
3. **The vorrel.** A fruit that *"hatches its seeds inside your belly"*, so the dying animal *"staggers toward the
   next shade — and dies in it"*, which is how the plant reaches new shade. And the prepared seeds, *"a controlled
   near-miss with a lethal parasite."* The best single organism in the sheet. 0.2.0: a quick death from eating
   something that looks safe needs its warning proven before it ships.
4. **The stratified commons.** *"The deep cool centre is held by whatever is strongest; everything else takes the
   rim, half-exposed... the young are in the centre."* And *"the worst moment available here is arriving overheated
   at a shelter already full."* Kept as what the ship's shade looks like when full; nothing enforces the
   centre-to-rim order, so it is a description, not a promise.
5. **Signalling before the run.** *"Nobody commits without asking"*: challenge and answer across hundreds of
   metres before a crossing. Lost. Nothing built it, and the trim law elsewhere says players are not much
   interested in advance warning. It was lovely.
6. **Wrong-axis countershading.** *"Dark on the sunward flank, pale on the shadow flank. Left-and-right, not
   back-and-belly."* Kept as art direction only; whether the art obeys it was not checked.
7. **Walking with the giant.** *"Nothing else on the planet lets you borrow an animal's survival adaptation by
   standing in the right place."* The map-scale half is kept in 0.1.0 (standing beside a gloomcast is standing in
   shade). The world-map crossing is the Stillsand's by an earlier boundary and is "later" here.
8. **The dew line.** *"The most valuable ground in the biome is a rim, not an area."* Third in his own ruled
   build order of 2026-09-27. It bends the shape of buildings, which is a settler's pleasure. The fringe plant
   stays as forage; the rim rule is 0.2.0; farming it is later.

## Scope moves

Each line is one feature whose bucket changed against the cleanse proposal of 2026-11-17. Revert by moving the line
in the design's Scope section.

- **The ship's shade filling with animals by stages, and the console clearing it before launch (Shipfall
  Commons):** next version → 0.1.0. Built. It is the sheet's own central consequence applied to the one roof the
  crew always brings, and it gives the biome a relationship with the ship that no other biome has.
- **The lee-side heaps and searching them (middens):** next version → 0.1.0. Built; called *"the smallest build in
  the document and the most Jawa"* when it was proposed. The vrekka that builds and tends heaps stays 0.2.0.
- **The sun graves (Long Carry remains and supplies):** next version → 0.1.0. Built; loot guarded by geometry, so
  the crew learns the dash with its own bodies.
- **Wreck road, road salvage, crawler terminus; returning-clan tow and negotiation; vorrel cycle and dish;
  stampede; harrok; tollok; smoke haze; dung seeding and bound plants; dew-fringe rim; leachmoss and venomvine;
  mirrak hide; khorrak vacstone; sippra and skiralim behaviours; sound beds; arrival letter; the sarlacc's road:**
  next version → 0.2.0. Each can be met inside a stop.
- **Study discounts and the go-and-return overlay:** stayed out (cleanse next version → later). Planning tools for
  people who live here.
- **New in 0.1.0, by his card of 2026-11-18:** what the place does to the landed ship (a resting giant's warmth
  and bulk at the hull), with its answer (clear the shade). The warmth is new, small work; the dispersal is built.
- **New, not in any source:** the Empire's troopers taking the same sun as everyone; a strike team staging in an
  occupied shadow; tenants in the way of both sides at the ramp. All are readings of built behaviour and none adds
  a mechanic.
