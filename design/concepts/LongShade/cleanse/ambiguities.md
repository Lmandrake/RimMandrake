# Settled ambiguities — Designer decisions

All answers below are mine under this pass's delegated authority, except an explicitly attributed existing ruling. Evidence that changed the call is included. No question is sent; none requires guessing a live measurement. Numbers here are range/check choices, never owner taste claims.

1. **“Shade is currency / every crossing a gamble.”** Reading A adds currency, random failure or universal lethal gaps; B makes exposure and shelter decisions costly and legible. A would build unrelated systems. Take B, figurative, from the card-selected experience (biome_experiences_2026-10-20.md), ordinary Heatstroke and later Direct Sun ruling. Accept a safe well-equipped crossing; reject a random crossing debuff independent of geometry.
2. **“Nothing lives in the light / nothing survives leaving shelter.”** A makes open ground kill every creature; B permits tolerance-dependent crossings and specialised giants. A contradicts living Gloomcast/Harrok and Gennok endurance. Take B; qualify old About/description text. No instant despawn or mandatory thermal death to enact poetry.
3. **“Sun so low / shadow four times its height / overhead.”** A hardcodes LongShade angle, heat kind and shadow ratio; B derives them from tile geometry and uses low-sun land at its actual position. A is today's legacy XML, not authority to override 2026-10-19. Take B. Old `heatOffsetC=30`, sun min/max clamps and heat-kind fields must go through dependency compliance. Formula regression uses the standing shared zenith value, not a new biome number.
4. **“Latitude band.”** A normal Earth latitude band; B angular distance/bearing from the fixed substellar point. They give different suns and temperatures at equal latitude. Take B from ASHKARR_WORLD_DEFINITION.md and the_one_map.md. Never use a dated tile export to claim current placement or generate another planet.
5. **“Golden hour forever.”** A locks weather clear; B fixes dayside geometry while weather changes sun intensity and sky. A can hide ecological failure. Take B; the season bar unlocks the existing dry weather table, including sandstorm and GrayPall. Sunset styling must not override thermal geometry.
6. **“Shade cools.”** A refrigerates air and cures Heatstroke; B removes only Direct Sun exposure, retaining vanilla air and recovery. A makes a roof an unconditional cure in lethal air. Take B from the standing Direct Sun ruling. Recovery claims require air in which recovery is possible; matched hot-air controls deliberately retain injury.
7. **“Strict rest–dash–rest.”** A applies forced jumps and perfect route planning to every pawn/job; B is evolved native thermal movement, with humans reacting when they overheat. A conflicts with owner answer 1, ANSWERS_OWNER.md, 2026-11-10. Take B, including hungry animals and failed hunts, not just idle wander. No new human forecaster. Actual natives still need ordinary food and babies do not inherit adult endurance by assumption.
8. **“Nothing pursues.”** A any moment of directed melee/hunting is banned; B predators may make a bounded local attack and must retreat rather than sustain a chase across exposed gaps. A conflicts with the sheet's burst predator and current Dakkra, while endless vanilla hunting contradicts the sheet's positional ecology. Take B as the careful reading of desert.md §4: brief rush, recovery, no sustained pursuit. No source promise of an unavoidable fast attack is promoted into invincibility. The generator must test prey escaping, not only a successful kill.
9. **“Healthy natives endure a crossing.”** A grants immunity even with no refuge, lethal ambient air, player attack orders or fake shelter; B constrains autonomous choices toward genuine reachable shelter when the actor can respond. A erases both heat and Mirrak. Take B, with explicit exceptional control states and real Heatstroke signals. Never label an unreachable fixture an accepted crossing.
10. **“Every animal is shade-bound.”** A identically scripts every stock animal; B keeps individual physiology/roles while checking each native policy class under costly exposure. A would flatten the varied roster and fake endurance by private sun changes. Take B. Distinguish flight, metal feeding, sand feeding and ordinary grazing, and do not exempt stock hungry/predator jobs from the actual shelter constraints.
11. **“The rim / line / only grows there.”** A literal one-cell contour and instant plant death or movement after any shelter change; B recruitment within the existing shade-fraction transition band, followed by ordinary plant growth/death. A needs a new plant lifecycle and creates travelling dew rings. Take B from RM_Patch_DewfringeWildSpawnGate.cs and OnRim; pale sparse established boundaries, not an exact border guarantee or forced ring. Current thresholds live as checks. A lingering plant is not a ghost shelter.
12. **“Sparse / desert / thick shade.”** A arbitrary density or tint alone qualifies; B bare exposed gaps dominate the route decisions, plants occupy readable patches/rims, and shade means measured protection. A would accept a meadow or cosmetic rock ring. Take B from the root line, desert.md and dry-world lore. No pinned coverage percentage becomes a bar; M3 maps whole-map cover and food changes to distinguish the pictures.
13. **“Moving shade follows the giant.”** A sprite or path-cost aura plus follower label; B real exposure moves with actual body geometry, vacated cells clear, and live/dead/downed/despawn transitions invalidate sources. A leaves an invisible permanent road. Take B from the moving-shade enrichment and shared grid. Downing uses actual living-body geometry; death removes living-source protection while leaving a readable corpse.
14. **“Followers follow all the time.”** A permanent escort script; B intermittent FollowClose after ordinary needs and distance checks. A creates another AI override and can starve followers. Take B from current JobGiver_FollowShadowCaster and the historical multi-sample record in prove_gloomcast_follow.py. Pirrik/Chorn/Gennok/Tebbra are already settled choices; no new species or re-ask. Thermal survival of their actual routes is still unproven.
15. **“Dung is the scar / seeds young life.”** A feeding proof requires growth boosts and animal creation; B plain dung is the already-waived sufficient feeding trace and seeding is a separate extra. A retains a population loop by code coupling. Take B from closed GLOOMCAST_WAKE_RIDERS_1 and RM_CompDungSeeder.cs. Keep plain leavings even when the seeding path is gated; no separate feeding scar is requested.
16. **“A promising shadow can be a trap.”** A any unreadable hidden damage is fair; B looking raises suspicion and safe inspection confirms before full commitment, including aligned silhouettes. A would pass on a debug flag then ambush without warning. Take B from owner answer 2, ANSWERS_OWNER.md. Inspecting the visible pawn from outside strike range is sufficient confirmation; no new scan job or quest. Wrong-way orientation is an available clue, never the only clue.
17. **“Readable sign.”** A an internal kill flag or a debug log; B attributable ground evidence/remains or a named player message visible through ordinary play. A still looks like spontaneous disappearance. Take B from the owner loss-sign ruling. For proof, read at seizure, after death/consumption, after reload and on the next local return (sample through 2500 ticks); player cleaning and natural removal after the ordinary sign lifetime are excluded, not retroactive permission for a sign never present. Keep corpse ingestion in place.
18. **“Clean patch tells you danger.”** A live guarantee that a predator occupies it; B a static map-generation former-lair marker. A makes stale mapgen a safety promise. Current RM_GenStep_CleanPatches only implements B. Defer the marker; the kept Mirrak bar does not rely on it. Do not rebrand a stale marker as a necessary warning.
19. **“Native roster / five plants / missing tiny prey.”** A source-folder contents or stale About counts; B effective BiomeDef plus actual patches, separate exported defs and per-species diets. A duplicates moved species and invents new small prey. Take B from current XML; Tebbra/Sippra already exist. Qorrax is shared with Stillsand and canon patch species may be multi-homed. Preserve memberships; audit food and caravan pack eligibility, not evictions.
20. **“It lives on the rim / scarp / dung / hardpan / ash.”** A prose creates five bespoke mechanics; B ordinary grazing/flight/scavenging remains and unsupported specialist behaviour is a separate future promise. A expands code without evidence. Take B after searching current defs and sources. Exact Sippra rim travel, scarp attack/corpse carrying and substrate-exclusive plants move; recommend retiring Tazzok ash-only emergence because its ash act is already cut. Do not revive the calendar.
21. **“Life includes every unusual behaviour.”** A any mechanic on a plant/animal stays; B only ordinary feeding/locomotion/products and the selected signature need stay. A smuggles parasites, resource generators and gut reproduction back into land/life. Take B under the cleansing card. Metal eating/sand feeding stay for nutrition; vacstone output, Vorrel cycle/dish and growth/population seeding move. Visible Vorrel plant stays but raw plant ingestion and harvest fruit are unavailable by default, rather than secretly made safe.
22. **“Harrok off.”** A suppresses damage but keeps forced immobility; B releases the Wait override along with special strike, leaving the existing carnivore able to scavenge corpses normally and cast physical body shade. A strands a stationary hungry animal. Take B from the comp's joint idle/strike code. Its feeding must be traced; if ordinary scavenging is insufficient fix the decoupling, not the roster.
23. **“Widest gap / road exists.”** A longest geometric gap, or any wrecks pass; B current code samples the nearest rim pair between large shade islands and can skip blocked links. A and B require different proof and neither guarantees a working crossing today. Defer the entire road. A future spec must name the selected islands and prove post-placement reachability, reject a partly laid chain, and preserve the player's ability to repair it. No road is required by kept bars.
24. **“A wreck's shadow matters / shade GEAR required.”** A only bespoke cart/graves make this true; B ordinary physical casters, roofs and parasols alter exposure on actual routes. A retains curated salvage by convenience. Take B; road cart placement, terminus and Long Carry move. Shared Wreckage still places ordinary wrecks by its own contract; do not globally suppress it. No particular grave distance/count is a bar.
25. **“Ship shadow is a commons.”** A ordinary animals may use it; B staged attraction by species and pilot dispersal. B is an extra chain. Keep A through real shelter/native behaviour, defer B with its messages. Pirrik admitted by the first size rung is an old staging bug to settle before future enablement, not another first-release bar. Flight must remain real if used in that later scene.
26. **“The clan takes its hull.”** A a timer erases every unowned thing in a rectangle; B actual claimants negotiate and only the original hulk can be removed. A may destroy later colony roofs and contradicts owner answer 3. Take B for the deferred spec; T19 blocks unsafe resumed tow and T34 demands the bargain, identity and cancellation transitions. No first-release dependency on a hull.
27. **“Smoke lengthens shadows.”** A weather multiplies geometric length; B weather dims intensity while position controls length. A conflicts with the later pure-geometry ruling. Take B. Haze weather moves; recommend retiring its length multiplier, not rewriting the standing rule. The earlier ash-growth/sand-lock removal is already settled.
28. **“Shelter gear / ship gift.”** A awning and mast must remain because built and art-kept; B any ordinary roof and a parasol suffice. A contradicts the later keep-only gear ruling and makes redundant structures obligatory. Take B; recommend retiring awning and mast with code/defs/art preserved off. Protected art is not a mechanical waiver.
29. **“Shade lessons / must learn shelter.”** A gear is locked until study; B ordinary research always works, study merely discounts. Current EnvironmentalHazards research supports B. Defer discount hooks and study jobs without deleting access or clearing the crossing event used by other consumers. T04 checks the dependency graph.
30. **“Settings off / next version off.”** A skips future incident rolls but leaves jobs, build menus, comps, effects and saved runners active; B all player-visible effects of that extra are absent, including on reload, while dormant defs/code persist. A can still break whole-run bars. Take B under the cleansing card. Scope gates to LongShade consumers; do not switch off retained shared services or another biome to isolate an extra.
31. **“Arrival/scanner.”** A generic flavour is automatically needed; B welcome letter is extra, but the recorded all-biomes scanner decision is a standing integration. Take B. Current XML has scanner payload; the historical arrival-letter promise is unknown-built, not invented as present. Scanner may report actual targets only and its quiet line must not declare hidden dangers impossible. No phantom deferred heap/cart or compulsory special scanner UI.
32. **“Proved / whole season / old bars.”** A source/DLL/stale scene or kernel fuzz means experience passed; B build evidence, historical proof and current runtime proof differ. Take B. The concept has no locked bar leaves and predecessor root is null; old_bars stays empty. A season preserves needs/weather/AI/health/reproduction and serialisation, runs reload and another work order, and samples actual continuing population. No live result was collected on this pass.

## Remaining uncertainty and default

The geometric sun may reveal legacy tuning failures; native job priorities and food supply may fail together. Those are the five unknown shared meetings in bars.json, assigned to M1/M2/M3, not owner questions. Keep the global rule and the small scope while measuring them. If actual fixed-world climate makes the signature impossible without changing a ruling, present the concrete calibrated counterexample to the owner; do not guess a new sun or silently drop the required experience. There is no concrete current runtime evidence here requiring an owner question now.
