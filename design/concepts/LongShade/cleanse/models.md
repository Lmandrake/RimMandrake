# Instruments specified, not built

Build in the order below. Outputs are predictions until calibrated against the named game reads from the same source/def/settings fingerprint. No planetary generation or alternative-world sweep is authorised: fixtures vary local geometry/state only. Figures below are instrument tolerances and rough work estimates, not bars. Unknown groups are the exact shared touches in bars.json, not isolated example pairs.

## M1: Shelter geometry and exposure sweep

Question: Do rendered and mechanical physical shelter, including a moving/dead source and modified light, agree on exposure under the one tile-derived sun?

Informs B1/B2/B3/B5/B6/B7/B8 and R1/R2/R4/R7; settles the unknown shelter geometry meeting and supplies geometry to the heat argument. Build first because every later instrument consumes this mask and heat path; a wrong grid makes all their numbers convincing and wrong.

Assumptions: caster footprint, height, occlusion, roof, parasol coverage and light layers use the shared engine contract. Project caster geometry with an independent analytic ray/footprint oracle, not a second call to the same grid method. Heat flows into vanilla felt temperature/Heatstroke, not a separate exposure injury. Do not infer protection from a rendered dark pixel, plant shade fraction or path cost alone.

Inputs/range: resolve current defs and record shared global formula/tuning. Sweep elevations 0, near-zero, 2, 14, 30, 60 and 90 degrees plus threshold epsilons, bearings 0..360 with all grid octants and aligned/diagonal boundaries; supported caster footprints/heights/life stages, map corners/edges, overlapping roofs/static/moving/gear/light sources, source placement/removal/death/downing/reload. Use 150x150, 250x250 and 400x400 local grids. Heat samples span air 10..65 C and each pawn's comfort boundary, naked/apparel/parasol, baseline native life stages. Include actual fixed-world tile positions when supplied; do not derive today's placement from a dated CSV.

Leaves out: subjective art beauty, AI job priority, food-web equilibria, full shader reconstruction and alternative planets. A genuine renderer/recognition claim still requires live captures. Known unsupported renderer features are reported as a calibration failure, never waved through with a model.

Game calibration before belief: read per-cell real/static/moving/roof/gear/perceived masks, sun vector, felt temperature and matched Heatstroke traces in the actual runtime, plus screenshot masks with normal rendering and observer-only annotations. Sample boundary cells and interior/control cells, not random averages. Require exact protected/exposed classification on unambiguous interiors; boundary raster disagreement no wider than the rendered transition cell and documented as a band, not a hidden safe strip. Sun direction/elevation within 0.1 degree, felt temperature within 0.1 C and severity trajectory within 0.001 at synchronized ticks are proposed C7 tolerances; if normal engine polling prevents that, measure its lag and revise the instrument tolerance openly before trusting it. Never tune game heat to force model agreement. Check source-change and vacated-cell states across the documented cache update interval; a cache interval that causes a failed crossing is a B5/B7 defect even if documented.

Produces: heat/shade overlays, false-versus-real masks, max boundary and stale-cell error per transition, thermal trace pairs and a minimal reproducer for each worst case. Rough size: a shared-kernel adapter, an independent geometric oracle and a runtime export/overlay reader; about 2 builder days [guess, based on existing pure shade/heat kernels and missing synchronized export].

Trigger: shelter geometry unknown meeting, present legacy heat fields, first B1/B2/B5/B7 failure, or geometry/threshold constants changed twice to chase a check. Decision changed: fix shared dependency compliance/cache mapping, change consumer placement/routing, or report an actual climate conflict; never retain the mast/haze multiplier as a shortcut.

## M2: Situation and transition generator

Question: Across the states animals and workers can actually enter, do needs, retreat, shelter changes and reload preserve truthful and usable shelter decisions?

Informs B2/B3/B4/B5/B6/B7/B8, R2/R3/R5/R6/R8; settles unknown pawn response meeting. Also attacks the argued persistence and shelter perception groups. Enumerate shared state/priority classes rather than one test pawn per species label.

Assumptions: only the game's real job priority, reachability, path cost and health transitions count. Pure kernels can enumerate candidate states; a thin runtime fixture must supply vanilla think-tree decisions where a credible offline substitute is absent. There is no trustworthy offline proof of human suspicion or general vanilla AI from extension flags. Use T36's runtime adapter and T44's shared-response build/contract for those gaps; do not invent an all-AI simulator.

Inputs/range: loaded free-tier and effective campaign rosters; each distinct diet/hunter/follower/flight/life-stage policy; healthy/hungry/rested/hot/near-collapse/downed/dead, wild/tamed/drafted/roped/mental; idle/feeding/hunting/fleeing/mating/hauling/building/harvesting; reachable/blocked/occupied/removed refuge, real/false/moving/roof source; thermal safe/threshold/lethal air; natural and worn cover, lone/clustered followers, 1..40 colonists. Include native rest-dash-rest versus same-race ordinary-wander controls at actual default low-sun climates, including mild conditions. Worker predicates cover Direct Sun onset before Serious Heatstroke, equal-room-temperature outdoor roof/parasol refuge, nearest reachable recovery target, interruption and return to valid work; explicitly generate missing-response and Serious-only/room-temperature-only counterexamples from T44. Walk events add/remove caster, change gear, interrupt/resume job, kill/despawn host/prey, save/load and on/off/reset. Generate branch-boundary epsilons from actual configured predicates, with pair/triple products at job-priority meetings and explicit longest reachable transition walks. Cluster equivalent policy/branch outcomes, retain representatives and a coverage manifest; do not claim a sampled family covers all.

Leaves out: art perception, a fully faithful offline engine, long-term food balance and staged future extras beyond their isolation checks. Combat/player-order exceptional states are not guaranteed safe but still may not disappear silently or leave broken jobs.

Game calibration before belief: for every distinct think-tree/policy class, replay its boundary and interruption fixture in a disposable runtime map and compare selected job/target, ordered movement/feeding/retreat events, exposure source, health and serialization continuation. Require exact actor/target/state transitions and no missing/duplicated attack or sign across reload; movement/timer offsets may differ by at most the measured polling interval with explicit cause. A thermal crossing that fails B3 despite matching a timer model is still failure. Planted defects (unchanged vanilla wandering in mild sun, absent shared colonist interruption, Serious-only/room-temperature-only refuge selection, disable retreat, leave a stale host, admit a false cooling cell, resume a deferred runner, suppress a kill sign) must be distinguished from legitimate override states.

Produces: covered-predicate table, state/transition graph, counterexample path per bar and minimal saved fixture, including unsatisfiable initial-state reasons. Rough size: bounded finite-state enumerator plus runtime fixture adapter, about 2–3 builder days [guess, existing kernels/probes reduce setup but full job priority is not modelled].

Trigger: pawn response unknown meeting, first native/worker stuck or avoidable overheating case, new life-stage/priority branch, or repeated budget retuning. Decision changed: fix adapter/shared priority, consumer gate or job transition; defer a secondary behaviour before weakening a bar. No new colonist planning engine.

## M3: Whole-map season and food ledger

Question: With every retained consumer active and extras absent, can shelter, food, ordinary recruitment and colony work coexist over a season and through reload without a flood, starvation desert or stalled colony?

Informs B1..B8 and R1..R8; settles unknown food web, population and weather meetings. Uses M1 calibrated masks/heat and M2 calibrated transition classes, but may begin an empirical runtime trace immediately while those mature. It is the whole-mod model, not an extrapolation from a pretty scene.

Assumptions: finite real starting resources; actual diets, growth/recruitment, ordinary births, heat/weather and consumption; animal recruitment and exits follow actual game rules; no dummy full-needs pawns, infinite food, dung spawning or repeated staged cast injection. Use saved tile climate and actual weather transitions, not per-biome heat invention. If the offline population/AI abstraction fails calibration, use runtime season replay as the cheapest credible instrument; no claim of an offline balanced ecosystem survives that failure.

Inputs/range: full local maps 150x150..400x400; flat/hilly/mountainous, sparse/typical/crowded natural shade and settleable patches; saved spawn targets at 0.5x..2x density; free-tier and campaign cast; no colony, first settlement and 40 colonists; low-sun fixed-world tile climates and comfort-boundary diagnostics. Run a 15-day season with weather unlocked, a mid-way save/load at days 6..9, reversed build/harvest order, and day-100 continuations of worst regimes. Natural low-probability Gloomcast/Mirrak opportunities use a documented initial native sample and finite resource ledger, not new animals every time the proof needs them. Validate perturbations: ordinary hunting/harvest/taming, destroyed shelter, giant departure, stock depleted by play, sunlight changes from ordinary weather. No planetary seeds or alternative planets.

Leaves out: subjective enjoyment, planetary travel, unique narrative incidents, unbounded adversarial extermination and invalid starvation baselines. A climate where air is lethal even under a roof is reported separately, not silently pooled with ordinary recovery regimes.

Game calibration before belief: from the same initial runtime save export at initialization, each game hour, relevant event boundaries, reload and season end: species/ages/needs/jobs, edible plant stock, ground cover, ingestion/nutrition, births, recruits, kills, starvation/heat deaths, exits, shelter masks, worker progress and logs. Require exact resource accounting on deterministic microfixtures and event replay, and matching population/food regime on at least a season's independent held-out runtime trajectory. Proposed aggregate tolerance is 10 percent for stochastic populations/stock after matching RNG semantics; any sign reversal (growth versus collapse), missing food source, lost event, stale shelter, actual failed crossing, default native wandering with no rest-dash-rest, or error storm invalidates trust regardless of average agreement. State-read absence of extra-created things/jobs/letters is exact. Compression preserves all needs/growth/weather/pathing/job/health and serialization ticks and event order; batch exports, not gameplay.

Produces: map-time strips of exposed/covered/vegetated ground, food source-to-consumer table, actor-specific rest/crossing-gait/refuge-rest and worker-onset/interruption/recovery/death ledger, births/recruits/exit balances, job-progress traces and worst local case per bar, paired uninterrupted/reloaded/reordered results. Rough size: instrument the existing runtime, then add a calibrated food/heat event simulator only where it predicts usefully; about 3–5 builder days [guess, depends on existing exports and whether vanilla AI forces runtime replay].

Trigger: unknown food web/population/weather meetings (mandatory before acceptance), first seasonal depletion/flood/stall or temperature/growth/population tuning changed twice. A default climate trajectory with no native rest-dash-rest fails B3/B8 even when it produces no Heatstroke; thermal-boundary diagnostics cannot cure that failure. Decision changed: remove an accidental extra, correct diet/food/response coupling, retune shared permitted population/growth/crossing parameters without weakening the experiences, or report climate/roster conflict. Cannot change the fixed world, evict animals, restore private sun or fabricate food to hide a failure.
