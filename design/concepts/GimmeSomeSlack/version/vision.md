# Gimme Some Slack 0.1.0: the vision

Written only from the root paragraph he approved (`record.json` root, OWNER:1793895896496551063), `sheet.md` and his quoted words there. No sentence is inferred. Convergent and Divergent are measured against this text.

1. Gimme Some Slack hides the tidy conduit and draws what a scavenger base really looks like: too-long extension cords sprawling across the floor in loose slack loops and coils, curving slowly, avoiding sharp turns unless they must, and piling against walls.
2. At every three- or four-way junction the cords plug into a joining piece: the long power strip in the Modern look, a junction box in Industrial, connectors in the other two looks.
3. Sagging overhead lines are strung between masts and draw above pawns and buildings.
4. The mess is honest: it faithfully represents connectivity but not the precise conduit path, so every cord explains a real power connection and nothing is drawn that is not wired.
5. A cut line lies on the ground, sparking where it still reaches power and dead where it does not; a sparking end shocks anyone who crosses it on another job, while the colonist coming to fix it is safe, and one setting chooses None, Only downed lines or All.
6. A Wire down alert sends a colonist to re-string a cut span, and a clamp on a neighbour's line steals their power with a steady goodwill drain if they catch you.
7. Even a huge base with many wires and connectors shows no problematic delay when it is rewired.
8. It is judged first on one room of floor cords meeting at a strip, and one cut that leaves a sparking live end and a dead end, with the drawing true to the grid in both.
9. Four looks are told apart by eye, every feature has a working switch, and this mod draws no hoses (those are FlowWorks'); there is no worldgen and no multiplayer support.
