# Instruments specified, not built

Build in this order. Sizes, ranges and agreement criteria are Designer planning choices, not measurements of RimWorld or new passing bars. Use actual local-map snapshots on the supplied fixed world or disposable local test maps. No instrument generates a planet or sweeps world seeds. Record game/content/settings fingerprints with calibration. Predictions cannot close an unknown meeting until agreement with actual game observations is established. A missing faithful adapter is reported as blindness, never hidden behind a Python copy of an algorithm.

## M1 Situations and transitions

**Question:** Can reachable kept lifecycle, settings and work transitions preserve people, property and readable recovery through reload and interruption?

**Informs:** B2–B8; R5/R6/R8/R9/R10/R11; property and settings_lifecycle unknown meetings, medical/rescue edges in care_work. This is the required situation generator. Build first: a season is not credible if its restart or gates lose state.

**Inputs/range:** Enumerate authored partitions, not every float. Mire: safe/entering/escalating/stuck/freed/leaving; native/vulnerable/airborne; helper absent/available/reserved/dead; victim alive/downed/dead; safe destination reachable/unreachable. Burial: loose/held/excluded/dwell/buried/digging/partly placed/blocked/recovered, stockpile or not; distinct stuff, quality, HP, rot, biocode, style and component state. Illness: ambient/dose, completed/failed ingestion, onset/escalation/collapse/recovery/death/early removal, marked/unmarked, masters/subgates on/off. Lace: unknown/discovered/researched, self/doctor use, cuts/stumps, active/expired and independent study counters. Weather: Roil/clearing/return and overlapping hush reasons. Suppression: active/outage/expiry/restore/overlap. Add cancellation, reservation loss, spawn/death/map transfer and save/load at every relevant transition. Enumerate each mechanism's booleans fully, then use interaction coverage between mechanisms with explicit three-way death/reload/off and partial-merge/blocked-placement/cancellation traces. Each partition needs a natural-route witness in addition to boundary injection checks.

**Assumptions:** Production kernels govern scalar transitions; actual Verse adapters govern Thing ownership, Scribe, job prerequisites and effective gates. Reuse current Greentide C# fuzz for numeric boundaries; it is not a Scribe or job emulator. Failed preconditions yield UNMEASURED. The generator includes states only if a reachable trace or an explicitly labelled boundary injection establishes them.

**Leaves out:** Render/audio meaning, enjoyment, autonomous scheduling, balance and every arbitrary third-party component. The pure model cannot certify object identity or completed jobs: T34/T35 supply actual-engine witnesses.

**Real-game calibration before belief:** Execute successful and refused transitions on a disposable actual save, then replay every discovered failing trace. Compare next state, owner/identity/quantity, job target/destination/reservation, health stage, designation, effective gate and ordered warnings. Require exact discrete state and conservation agreement; timestamps may differ only by the production scheduler's documented scan cadence. A mismatch invalidates that partition until corrected. A model calling its own mirror is not calibration.

**Produces:** Reachable/unreachable coverage table with reasons, shortest failing action trace, full before/after fingerprints, per-bar counterexamples and optionally a state graph. Not a new every-field passing quota.

**Rough size:** Estimated 500–3,000 partitioned traces and 2–4 builder days including adapters. These are planning estimates.

**Trigger/decision changed:** Already triggered by property/settings unknowns, reduced-tuple burial, in-mud rescue finish and master-off cleanup. Output changes ownership/cleanup/job rules before tuning; T34/T35/T39 own repairs. It is not evidence that current game behaviour passed.

## M2 Season of an inhabited foothold

**Question:** Can growth, consumers, hazards, protection and competing colony work support a productive maintained home while neglect visibly costs access before making it untenable?

**Informs:** B2/B3/B4/B5/B7/B8; R2/R3/R4/R5/R6/R9/R10/R12/R13; vegetation, wildlife and care_work unknown meetings and feedback into ground_routes. This is the required whole-mod model.

**Inputs/range:** Export actual full-map terrain, eligible cells, species/stages, light/water, root/mud/constructed routes, path/sight grid, weather, suppression writers/expiry, hosts/wildlife, stock and worker state. Replay production growth/fruit/wither/seed-passage, suppression, actual grazing-consumption and tree-fall events with current constants. Use observed walking/work/food/illness durations in a discrete worker-capacity model, including hauling, burial, rescue and doctor downtime. Map sides 150/250/325; flat/large-hill/mountain relief; colonies 1/8/40; wet river, river edge, riverless-remnant negative contexts. Compare maintained, outage/repaired and neglected policies on each fixed geometry; repeat harvest-before-upkeep versus upkeep-before-harvest. Vary herd loss, blower outage 1/6/24 hours, approach width 1..5 and low/authored/high observed host/grazer/predator regimes. Growth/suppression/response constants at 0.5x/1x/2x are debug perturbations only: accepted behaviour uses defaults. Run a 15-day season, extend to 30 or longer until required natural growth and clearing/return completes, with saved-state restart mid-cycle.

**Assumptions:** Geometry is supplied, not generated by this model. Reuse trustworthy production kernels. Predator/gall/fall rates are calibrated event inputs or explicit bounded schedules, not pretend full ThinkTrees. Worker capacity is an estimate constrained by actual job/path samples. Keep all competing work, natural source attribution and flat danger; no biome escalation to cancel competence.

**Leaves out:** Full tactical AI, audio/visual crowding, arbitrary third-party behaviour and real-time FPS. A fluid worker-capacity estimate cannot close an autonomous AI stall: use actual game work traces. No Greatbole, ant-raid, vortex, extra-weapon or planetary hydrology economy is active. Shared Wreckage/Bones enter only as observed route obstacles and ordinary property flows.

**Real-game calibration before belief:** Capture naturally running all-kept-on default intervals on an initial, established and crowded map, including actual outage/recovery, grazing, gall disturbance/retreat and weather transition. Compare hourly eligible cells, births/deaths, harvest/food/wood flows, suppression footprints/expiry, path/reachability, queue age, stings/illness and recovery times. Require exact production-kernel decisions for replayed inputs and exact property conservation. Stochastic aggregate observations must lie within declared prediction intervals on held-out traces, with no systematic directional bias; an interval spanning both stable and runaway regimes is inconclusive. Maintained-versus-neglected direction and bottleneck identity must agree with the real game before choosing defaults from a prediction. T40 records actual fingerprints and intervals.

**Produces:** Hourly occupancy/suppression/access maps, resource/work tables, population envelopes, warning-to-loss/recovery windows and worst case per bar. Flood means continuing loss of usable home space to uncontrolled occupancy/spawning; barren means ordinary life/food/timber exhausted without renewal; stall means required work perpetually requeues or cannot reach targets; error storm means recurring exception signatures. Diagnostic thresholds are checks, not bars.

**Rough size:** Estimated 100–500 fixed-map policy traces, event-driven across tens of game days; 3–6 builder days. A small held-out set of real default runs calibrates them. No Cartesian explosion or second RimWorld.

**Trigger/decision changed:** Already triggered by unknown natural access pressure and growth/grazing/fall/light/medical-work feedback. Also trigger after first whole-mod flood/barren/stall or a constant changed twice to move a check. Output changes maintenance/growth/suppression rules or identifies missing natural activation, never rewrites the owner's experience to accept a broken loop.

## M3 Footing and doorway geometry

**Question:** Across geometry the local game can produce, can bounded defence and firm routes coexist with obstructive foliage and practical extraction/retrieval?

**Informs:** B1/B3/B4/B5/B8; R1/R2/R4/R5/R6/R13; ground_routes unknown and defence/rescue portions of vegetation/care_work.

**Inputs/range:** Use actual plant-eligibility, forward-arc, path-cost/affordance, LOS and placement predicates. Enumerate cardinal blower rotations, centre/boundary/outside cells, wall/door/open corners, narrow approaches, overlapping fields, 1..5-cell lanes, root/mud/sealed boundaries, actual fallback root-anchor outputs, map edges, water/cliff blockers, trunks/wrecks, reservations and full placement neighbourhoods. Cover actual footprint/body sizes and grounded/airborne state. Bearings cover all integer offsets within the configured field and every admitted rotation. Include no-safe-cell, occupied-only-safe-cell, only-safe-route-obstructed and blocked-dig-remainder cases. Full-map layouts come from real local outputs, never alternative worlds.

**Assumptions:** Frozen geometry can predict eligible/protected/blocked/reachable/placeable sets. Dynamic growth is M2 and actual AI is read in-game. Euclidean distance is not reachable footing. Reuse engine semantics; do not redesign a shared geometry service to fit an attractive diagram.

**Leaves out:** AI deciding to eat/flee, weather perception and season timing. Cell membership alone cannot pass effective protection: B3/B4 require actual natural plant/path outcomes.

**Real-game calibration before belief:** Feed identical exported geometry/candidate cells to the actual pathfinder, terrain/LOS, field state and placement readers. Require exact set agreement and match actual extraction destinations for each representative topology. Add any newly admitted shape to the partitions before claiming range coverage. A live low-steam/canopy look calibrates visual meaning separately, never proves the geometry.

**Produces:** Overlay maps of protected eligible growth, root/mud routes, reachable refuge and legal retrieval placement; failed-topology table with smallest counterexample per bar. Attack shared rules first when their group cannot hold.

**Rough size:** Thousands of small neighbourhood cases plus full-map reachability over recorded layouts; estimated 1–3 builder days using existing probes.

**Trigger/decision changed:** Already triggered by the route meeting and firm-ground rescue requirement versus condition reset. Re-run affected partitions after radius/arc/footprint/lane-width/path-eligibility changes or a stuck rescue. Output changes destination/route/placement logic or readable refusal, never introduces teleportation across an inaccessible map.

## M4 Clearing and heat timing

**Question:** Can clearing/return, canopy change and common heat rules leave reachable refuge for the required shade response and recovery work without inventing Greentide heat?

**Informs:** B1/B2/B3/B4/B6/B8; R4/R7/R8; weather_sun unknown and overlaps with vegetation/wildlife/care_work.

**Inputs/range:** Export condition priority/start/end, geometry-derived sun angle, roof/canopy shade, air/room temperatures, insulation, refuge path cost, native shade eligibility and ongoing work. Sweep geometrically possible dayside sun angles 5..90 degrees, ambient scenario envelope 28..65 C, available/destroyed/blocked shade, enclosed/open/unpowered room, canopy felled, clearing during rescue/haul/doctor collapse, reload before/during/after clearing and overlapping weather/hush expiry. Defaults are primary; scheduler-boundary values are debug checks. No fog discount, biome sun multiplier or private heat knob is an input.

**Assumptions:** Reuse common Sun/ Shade and vanilla temperature/Heatstroke state. Trace-based timing compares actual refuge travel/work and health change; it does not invent full physiology. Attribute radiation and hot air to their actual shared systems. If shared tree shade is not trustworthy, no credible local surrogate exists: report dependency unavailable until repaired.

**Leaves out:** Atmospheric fluid/humidity simulation, enjoyment, convincing rendering, mathematical judgement of sound meaning and unattended flyer visuals. The silence service's reason ownership can be read; listening still judges audible meaning.

**Real-game calibration before belief:** At identical real-game cells compare condition ordering, sun/roof/canopy inputs, felt temperature, vanilla Heatstroke change, natural job/destination and return ticks through actual clearing. Require exact discrete ordering/shade membership and numeric agreement within declared engine scheduler/rounding resolution. Matched exposed/shaded outdoor cells at equal air temperature plus an enclosed cooled room exclude legacy local offset and roof-as-air-cooler false explanations. Read warning before irreversible attributed loss.

**Produces:** Timeline of weather, refuge travel, shade availability, temperature/health, cooling, medical downtime and reload; worst refuge deficit and attributed source per scenario.

**Rough size:** Tens of calibration traces and hundreds of short replay/boundary cases, estimated 1–2 builder days after state export.

**Trigger/decision changed:** Already triggered by weather_sun unknown and legacy biome heat extension. Also a shared shade change, first exposed-recovery death or repeatedly changed weather/thermal constant. Output may require a shared dependency repair, route or warning; never a private sunlight exception. No live heat failure is claimed on this design pass.
