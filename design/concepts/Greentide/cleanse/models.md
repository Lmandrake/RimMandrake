# Instruments specified, not built

Build in this order. Sizes, ranges and agreement criteria are Designer planning choices, not measurements of RimWorld or new passing bars. Use actual local-map snapshots on the supplied fixed world or disposable local test maps. No instrument generates a planet or sweeps world seeds. Record game/content/settings fingerprints with calibration. Predictions cannot close an unknown meeting until agreement with actual game observations is established. A missing faithful adapter is reported as blindness, never hidden behind a Python copy of an algorithm.

## M1 Situations and transitions

**Question:** Can reachable kept lifecycle, settings and work transitions preserve people, property and readable recovery through reload and interruption?

**Informs:** B2–B8; R2/R4/R5/R6/R8/R9/R10/R11; property and settings_lifecycle unknown meetings, medical/rescue edges in care_work. This is the required situation generator. Build first: a season is not credible if its restart or gates lose state.

**Inputs/range:** Enumerate authored partitions, not every float. Mire: safe/entering/escalating/stuck/freed/leaving; native/vulnerable/airborne; helper absent/available/reserved/dead; victim alive/downed/dead; safe destination reachable/unreachable. Burial: loose/held/excluded/dwell/buried/digging/partly placed/blocked/recovered, stockpile or not; distinct stuff, quality, HP, rot, biocode, style and component state. Illness at defaults: ambient exposure, onset/escalation/collapse/recovery/death/early removal, untreated/treated, and legacy-marked/unmarked subjects with mark immunity off. Lace at defaults: ordinary visible research available/blocked by MedicineProduction/unfinished/finished, real crafting, self/doctor use, cuts/stumps and active/expired. Dose, survivor and branch discovery remain off throughout positive bar traces. Enumerate attempted dose activation, old marks, saved branches/counters and hidden-project cleanup only to prove off-state decoupling; separate explicitly enabled T51/T52/T53 regressions preserve their later ruled behaviour and never substitute for default evidence. Weather: Roil/clearing/return and overlapping hush reasons. Suppression: active/outage/expiry/restore/overlap. Add cancellation, reservation loss, spawn/death/map transfer and save/load at every relevant transition. Enumerate each mechanism's booleans fully, then use interaction coverage between mechanisms with explicit three-way death/reload/off and partial-merge/blocked-placement/cancellation traces. Each partition needs a natural-route witness in addition to boundary injection checks.

Add T54 ingestion/expiry traces for wild and harvested yearning fruit, fresh defaults and reload with an existing digestive effect. In separate enabled traces, exercise expiry within a working blower field and an enclosed dirt-floor room, with eligible outdoor ground as the positive germination control. Record candidate eligibility at expiry and whether each attempt produces a seedling. Use M3 for cell boundaries.

**Assumptions:** Production kernels govern scalar transitions; actual Verse adapters govern Thing ownership, Scribe, job prerequisites and effective gates. Reuse current Greentide C# fuzz for numeric boundaries; it is not a Scribe or job emulator. Failed preconditions yield UNMEASURED. The generator includes states only if a reachable trace or an explicitly labelled boundary injection establishes them.

**Leaves out:** Render/audio meaning, enjoyment, autonomous scheduling, balance and every arbitrary third-party component. The pure model cannot certify object identity or completed jobs: T34/T35 supply actual-engine witnesses.

**Real-game calibration before belief:** Execute successful and refused transitions on a disposable actual save, then replay every discovered failing trace. Compare next state, owner/identity/quantity, job target/destination/reservation, health stage, designation, effective gate and ordered warnings. Require exact discrete state and conservation agreement; timestamps may differ only by the production scheduler's documented scan cadence. A mismatch invalidates that partition until corrected. A model calling its own mirror is not calibration.

**Produces:** Reachable/unreachable coverage table with reasons, shortest failing action trace, full before/after fingerprints, per-bar counterexamples and optionally a state graph. Not a new every-field passing quota.

**Rough size:** Estimated 500–3,000 partitioned traces and 2–4 builder days including adapters. These are planning estimates.

**Trigger/decision changed:** Already triggered by property/settings unknowns, reduced-tuple burial, in-mud rescue finish and master-off cleanup. Output changes ownership/cleanup/job rules before tuning; T34/T35/T39 own repairs. It is not evidence that current game behaviour passed.

## M2 Season of an inhabited foothold

**Question:** Can growth, consumers, hazards, protection and competing colony work support a productive maintained home while neglect visibly costs access before making it untenable?

**Informs:** B2/B3/B4/B5/B7/B8; R2/R3/R4/R5/R6/R9/R10/R12/R13; vegetation, wildlife and care_work unknown meetings and feedback into ground_routes. This is the required whole-mod model.

**Inputs/range:** Export actual full-map terrain, eligible cells, species/stages, light/water, root/mud/constructed routes, path/sight grid, weather, suppression writers/expiry, hosts/wildlife, stock and worker state. Replay production growth, fruit, wither, shared sprouting, suppression, actual grazing-consumption and tree-fall events with current constants and seed passage off. Use observed walking/work/food/illness durations in a discrete worker-capacity model, including hauling, burial, rescue and doctor downtime. Map sides 150/250/325; flat/large-hill/mountain relief; colonies 1/8/40; wet river, river edge, riverless-remnant negative contexts. Compare maintained, outage/repaired and neglected policies on each fixed geometry, plus an explicit **pave the approaches early** policy: spend the same finite starting resources and normal construction work paving all home entrances, with used field-edge, harvest and river routes still unpaved. Continue growth, blower/cooling and grazing elsewhere at default gates. Add a fully paved used-route negative control wherever local affordances permit: covered cells owe no regrowth burden, and no compensating edge invasion or danger escalation may manufacture one. Repeat harvest-before-upkeep versus upkeep-before-harvest, including the order of paving versus harvesting. Vary herd loss, blower outage 1/6/24 hours, approach width 1..5 and low/authored/high observed host/grazer/predator regimes. Growth/suppression/response constants at 0.5x/1x/2x are debug perturbations only: accepted behaviour uses defaults. Run a 15-day season, extend to 30 or longer until required natural growth and clearing/return completes, with saved-state restart mid-cycle.

**Assumptions:** Geometry is supplied, not generated by this model. Reuse trustworthy production kernels. Predator/gall/fall rates are calibrated event inputs or explicit bounded schedules, not pretend full ThinkTrees. Worker capacity is an estimate constrained by actual job/path samples. Keep all competing work, natural source attribution and flat danger; no biome escalation to cancel competence. Treat constructed/sealed zero-fertility cells as permanently ineligible under the production predicate; bare dirt eligibility is read, never inferred merely from being outdoors. Charge paving actual material/work costs, not continuing invented growth upkeep.

**Leaves out:** Full tactical AI, audio/visual crowding, arbitrary third-party behaviour and real-time FPS. A fluid worker-capacity estimate cannot close an autonomous AI stall: use actual game work traces. No Greatbole, ant-raid, vortex, extra-weapon, deliberate-dose/survivor/discovery or planetary hydrology economy is active. Shared Wreckage/Bones enter only as observed route obstacles and ordinary property flows.

**Real-game calibration before belief:** Capture naturally running all-kept-on default intervals on an initial, established and crowded map, including actual outage/recovery, grazing, gall disturbance/retreat and weather transition. Include matched early-paved-entrance runs with suppression absent at those entrances, still-used unpaved resource routes and, where affordable/constructible, fully paved used-route controls; use the same baseline stock and record construction expenditure. Require exact game/model agreement that covered cells remain growth-ineligible; compare natural renewed obstruction on eligible resource routes separately. A model predicting recurring growth on intact paving is invalid. Compare hourly eligible cells, births/deaths, harvest/food/wood flows, suppression footprints/expiry, path/reachability, queue age, stings/illness and recovery times. Require exact production-kernel decisions for replayed inputs and exact property conservation. Stochastic aggregate observations must lie within declared prediction intervals on held-out traces, with no systematic directional bias; an interval spanning both stable and runaway regimes is inconclusive. Maintained-versus-neglected direction, early-paving versus unpaved maintenance burden, and bottleneck identity must agree with the real game before choosing defaults from a prediction. T40 records actual fingerprints and intervals.

**Produces:** Hourly maps separating paved/exempt and eligible unpaved occupancy/suppression/access, construction-versus-recurring-work and resource tables, population envelopes, warning-to-loss/recovery windows and worst case per bar. Flood means continuing loss of usable home space to uncontrolled occupancy/spawning; barren means ordinary life/food/timber exhausted without renewal; stall means required work perpetually requeues or cannot reach targets; error storm means recurring exception signatures. Diagnostic thresholds are checks, not bars.

**Rough size:** Estimated 100–500 fixed-map policy traces, event-driven across tens of game days; 3–6 builder days. A small held-out set of real default runs calibrates them. No Cartesian explosion or second RimWorld.

**Trigger/decision changed:** Already triggered by unknown natural access pressure and growth/grazing/fall/light/medical-work feedback. Also trigger after first whole-mod flood/barren/stall or a constant changed twice to move a check. Output changes maintenance/growth/suppression rules or identifies missing natural activation. If early entrance paving removes all player-relevant upkeep even on used eligible resource ground, the proposed narrowed signature is unsupported: repair the eligible-ground integration or revise its scope openly, never force growth through floors. A fully paved network ending regrowth upkeep is an earned outcome, not a failed loop.

## M3 Footing and doorway geometry

**Question:** Across geometry the local game can produce, can bounded defence and firm routes coexist with obstructive foliage and practical extraction/retrieval?

**Informs:** B1/B3/B4/B5/B8; R1/R2/R4/R5/R6/R13; ground_routes unknown and defence/rescue portions of vegetation/care_work.

**Inputs/range:** Use actual plant-eligibility, forward-arc, path-cost/affordance, LOS and placement predicates. Enumerate cardinal blower rotations, centre/boundary/outside cells, wall/door/open corners, narrow approaches, overlapping fields, 1..5-cell lanes, root/mud/sealed boundaries, early-paved home thresholds beside unpaved field/harvest/river routes, ordinary permitted floor affordances, fully paved used-route controls, actual fallback root-anchor outputs, map edges, water/cliff blockers, trunks/wrecks, reservations and full placement neighbourhoods. Cover actual footprint/body sizes and grounded/airborne state. Bearings cover all integer offsets within the configured field and every admitted rotation. Include no-safe-cell, occupied-only-safe-cell, only-safe-route-obstructed and blocked-dig-remainder cases. Full-map layouts come from real local outputs, never alternative worlds.

For T54 with seed passage enabled, check each candidate across an active blower boundary, recently grazed cells and suppression expiry, and across an enclosed dirt-floor room's doorway. Include roofs, floors and open outdoor dirt where the shared predicate distinguishes them. Compare candidate sets with the game's SproutRing eligibility read and confirm actual spawned seedlings obey it.

**Assumptions:** Frozen geometry can predict eligible/protected/blocked/reachable/placeable sets. Dynamic growth is M2 and actual AI is read in-game. Euclidean distance is not reachable footing. Reuse engine semantics; do not redesign a shared geometry service to fit an attractive diagram.

**Leaves out:** AI deciding to eat/flee, weather perception and season timing. Cell membership alone cannot pass effective protection: B3/B4 require actual natural plant/path outcomes.

**Real-game calibration before belief:** Feed identical exported geometry/candidate cells to the actual pathfinder, terrain/LOS, field state and placement readers. Require exact set agreement and match actual extraction destinations for each representative topology. Add any newly admitted shape to the partitions before claiming range coverage. A live low-steam/canopy look calibrates visual meaning separately, never proves the geometry.

**Produces:** Overlay maps of protected eligible growth, root/mud routes, reachable refuge and legal retrieval placement; failed-topology table with smallest counterexample per bar. Attack shared rules first when their group cannot hold.

**Rough size:** Thousands of small neighbourhood cases plus full-map reachability over recorded layouts; estimated 1–3 builder days using existing probes.

**Trigger/decision changed:** Already triggered by the route meeting and firm-ground rescue requirement versus condition reset. Re-run affected partitions after radius/arc/footprint/lane-width/path-eligibility changes or a stuck rescue. Output changes destination/route/placement logic or readable refusal, never introduces teleportation across an inaccessible map.

## M4 Clearing and heat timing

**Question:** Can clearing/return, canopy change and common heat rules leave reachable refuge for the required shade response and recovery work without inventing Greentide heat?

**Informs:** B1/B2/B3/B4/B6/B8; R4/R7/R8/R14; weather_sun unknown and overlaps with vegetation/wildlife/care_work.

**Inputs/range:** Export condition priority/start/end, geometry-derived sun angle, roof/canopy shade, air/room temperatures, insulation, refuge path cost, native shade eligibility and ongoing work. Sweep geometrically possible dayside sun angles 5..90 degrees, ambient scenario envelope 28..65 C, available/destroyed/blocked shade, enclosed/open/unpowered room, canopy felled, clearing during rescue/haul/doctor collapse, reload before/during/after clearing and overlapping weather/hush expiry. Defaults are primary; scheduler-boundary values are debug checks. No fog discount, biome sun multiplier or private heat knob is an input.

**Assumptions:** Reuse common Direct Sun and vanilla temperature/Heatstroke state. Trace actual refuge travel, work and health. Attribute radiation and Breaklight's ordinary air offset to their actual systems. Use the R7 default in the weather replay. For Direct Sun readiness, the proposed on branch requires qualified shared tree shade under the standing adopt-or-fall-back test. Report an unqualified dependency under C8/T38. The weather and roof-seeking trace under R14 can be evaluated independently.

**Leaves out:** Atmospheric fluid/humidity simulation, enjoyment, convincing rendering, mathematical judgement of sound meaning and unattended flyer visuals. The silence service's reason ownership can be read; listening still judges audible meaning.

**Real-game calibration before belief:** Read actual condition order, cell air temperature, roof status, natural job/destination and return ticks. Match B6's Roil and Breaklight windows by carrier, starting activity, cells, time of day and baseline air. Where Breaklight takes air to or above the carrier threshold, check increased roof-bound trips across repeated pairs and witness the visible clearing and natural steam return. Require exact agreement on condition order and roof membership and agreement on air temperature within engine scheduler/rounding resolution.

For R7/C8/T38, also compare felt temperature, radiation and vanilla Heatstroke at actual cells. At matched positive sun angle, nonzero weather sun factor, equal outdoor air and identical gear, verify positive exposed radiation exceeding both roof and canopy shade. Read the effective Direct Sun gate, source contribution and crown-grid qualification. Equal radiation or a gate-off mutation fails the radiation check. An enclosed cooled room distinguishes ordinary air cooling from radiation shading. Read warning before irreversible attributed loss.

**Produces:** Timeline of weather, refuge travel, shade availability, temperature/health, cooling, medical downtime and reload; worst refuge deficit and attributed source per scenario.

**Rough size:** Tens of calibration traces and hundreds of short replay/boundary cases, estimated 1–2 builder days after state export.

**Trigger/decision changed:** Already triggered by weather_sun unknown, unestablished shared-tree-shade qualification and legacy biome heat extension. Also a shared shade change, first exposed-recovery death or repeatedly changed weather/thermal constant. Output may require a shared dependency repair, route or warning; never a private sunlight exception. No live heat failure is claimed on this design pass.
