# Page one — what Greentide would be

Greentide is a steaming river jungle where food and timber are worth the dangerous trip outside. Wet growth repeatedly contests used unpaved harvest and river routes, where clearing, dry-air blowers and grazing help, while paving earns lasting access to the home. Neglect first costs work and access; warnings, rescue and retrieval give a prepared colony a way back before continued neglect can force it out.

**Land:** low steam below readable crowns, obstructing foliage, trapping mud and firm root lanes; ordinary hot air and shared sunlight, with steam lifting and returning.

**Life:** the existing alien cast, forage, exotic catches, timber, resin, grazers, predators, falling trees and defending gall insects; ambient Frenzy and crafted bleeding-control lace. No roster evictions.

**Signature:** wet growth feeds the colony and reclaims unpaved working ground, making clearing, dry-air protection and grazing useful there while paving earns lasting access to the home.

My runner-up is living Greatbole settlement and extraction: another colony-scale system. I defer it intact with its ruled consequences; ordinary fellable Greatbole trees remain. The explicitly first-playable shade response and predator silence cue stay.

Moved to next version, off by default:

- Ant raids and construction economy.
- False-bank disguise, illisk resistance and provoked thurrock wall-breaking; ordinary animals remain.

- Deliberate Frenzy dosing, survivor marks/resistance and branch-study discovery. Ambient illness and ordinarily researched/crafted lace remain; separate switches preserve the extras for later.

I recommend retiring unused sugar-famewort filler because food/sap already serves its role, and legacy alternative-planet scoring because worldgen is forbidden. These are recommendations, not deletions. The humming tree remains its Chill neighbour's content.

# Page two — what to check

Interpretations settled in this revision:

- Maintaining a home includes its working routes; it is not only an expedition destination. Neglect allows warned recovery. Rescue ends on reachable firm ground for both people.
- Paving permanently stops regrowth on covered cells, including home entrances. Clearing, blowers and grazing serve still-used eligible unpaved ground; no floor invasion, decay tax or escalation cancels earned safety. The season model now compares early entrance paving explicitly.
- Protection requires actual prevention and actual eating, not grid writes. Cooling is behind the blower; paving cannot prove its suppression worked.
- Digging preserves the same belongings and their condition. Stockpiles still sink; held items, corpses and minified objects remain excluded.
- Direct Sun is proposed **on**, conditional on qualified shared tree shade. The standing ruling permits off on tree-heavy maps, and the tree-shade design names Greentide; this is evidence against assuming activation. Qualification plus measured exposed radiation exceeding roof/canopy shade is required. An off/equal-radiation run cannot supply positive evidence; unqualified shade remains dependency work, not readiness.
- The weather bar now asks only for visible clearing, natural shade-seeking and returning steam. Common heat-law compliance stays a rule/check.
- Dose delivery, survivor progression and branch study are independent extras, despite earlier selection. Their future ruled contracts remain intact, like Greatbole's. Default lace uses ordinary visible medicine research without specimens.
- A debug-spawned gall cannot prove ordinary insect danger; natural host-associated placement is required.
