# Page one — what Greentide would be

Greentide is a steaming river jungle where food and timber are worth the dangerous trip outside. Wet growth repeatedly contests exposed paths and entrance approaches, so colonists hold their home with clearing, dry-air blowers, firm footing and grazing. Neglect first costs work and access; warnings, rescue and retrieval give a prepared colony a way back before continued neglect can force it out.

**Land:** low steam below readable crowns, obstructed foliage, trapping mud and firm root lanes; ordinary hot air and shared sunlight, with the steam occasionally lifting and returning.

**Life:** the existing alien cast, food, exotic catches, timber, resin, grazers, predators, falling trees and defending gall insects; the already-selected disease/resistance and discoverable bleeding-control medicine.

**Signature:** wet growth feeds the colony and reclaims exposed access, so holding an inhabited home requires local care.

My runner-up is living Greatbole settlement and extraction. It is a separate colony-scale system, so I defer it intact. Its healing, catastrophe, fruit, grubs and other ruled consequences are preserved for that version. Ordinary fellable Greatbole trees remain. The explicitly required first-playable shade response and predator silence cue stay now.

# Page two — what to check

The important interpretations I took:

- Daily struggle means maintaining an inhabited home, following your later answers; it is not just an expedition destination.
- Neglect permits warned recovery before abandonment. Rescue finishes with usable firm-ground escape for both people, not just removal of a health condition.
- “Block your doors” means actual obstruction of exposed entrance approaches. The later shared growth law protects floors, buildings and interiors; I do not break that law to fake pressure.
- Fast maturation alone does not prove encroachment. Ordinary natural cycles must change access on an occupied map.
- The blower protects a cleared directional approach, prevents new growth and cools behind it. It does not erase thickets. Grazing protection requires actual eating.
- Digging returns the same belongings, including their condition and component state. Stockpiles still sink; carried things, corpses and minified objects stay visibly excluded.
- Steam and clearing introduce no private heat species or sunlight multiplier. Shade answers direct radiation; enclosed cooling answers hot air.
- A functional gall that only appears when manually spawned does not establish ordinary insect danger. Natural host-associated placement is required.
- Keep the chosen disease/dose/survivor and branch-study/lace bargains; add no new reward progression. Lace still needs subsequent wound care.
- Deferring Greatbole preserves its ruled behaviour. Only its separate ordinary fellable tree stays now. Root lanes already have a fallback without living landmarks.
