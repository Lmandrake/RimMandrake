# Calls that change scope, construction or acceptance

All selections below are the Designer's delegated decisions; quoted/paraphrased rulings retain their cited provenance. No new question is sent. The absence of a current measurement is not a reason to ask the owner to predict it.

1. **What is the mod's daily struggle?** A dangerous excursion destination versus a home whose access must be maintained. The first permits a sterile safe base with interesting expeditions; the second requires natural upkeep pressure. Take the second: owner answers by card 2026-11-10 in `ANSWERS_OWNER.md`, answer 1, explicitly choose defending an inhabited foothold. This supersedes older agent claims of “destination, never home.”

2. **How severe is neglect?** Immediate irreversible enclosure versus visible deterioration, recoverable loss and possible abandonment after continued neglect. Immediate enclosure makes rescuers irrelevant. Take recovery first, with a usable rescue approach: same owner answers, answer 2. Timing is a model/check, not his guessed number. No current default season has established it.

3. **“Block your doors.”** Literal seedlings growing through a built door/interior versus naturally regrowing exposed access that obstructs use of the threshold. The literal reading requires breaking the shared ruled floor/edifice/interior exclusions. Take the exposed-approach reading: later ExplosiveGrowth `RM_SproutRing.CanSprout` and Wither route respect built ground. An ordinary pawn must actually experience impaired access, not just see foliage nearby. T33 is required if existing admissible growth never does that. The poem does not authorize changing the dependency's law.

4. **Fast growth equals encroachment?** A high immature growth rate versus a repeating natural source of route burden. A specimen plot can show the former forever without occupying any new cells. Require births/wither/seed passage and changed used-route occupancy/cost from natural cycles; inspect `RM_TopResolver.Wither`, not only a growth stat or forced top. This is the principal integration unknown, measured by M2 rather than guessed from the old sheet.

5. **“Darkness brings roots.”** A second nocturnal invasion engine versus obsolete imagery constrained by later light-gated tree-burst rules. The former adds another system and changes the generator. Take later light rules (`tree_burst_design_2026-10-17.md`, present ExplosiveGrowth source); correct active arrival/inspect prose rather than reintroducing an old subsystem.

6. **Greatbole means which thing?** The fellable plant versus mineable heartwood/core versus a playable transformation between them. Confusing them accidentally keeps a whole second base system and misattributes fruit to felling. Retain `RM_Greatbole` as ordinary life; defer landmark/core/ladder and harvest products together. `RM_Greatbole.xml` and `RM_Greatbole_Landmark.xml` explicitly distinguish them. No during-play maturation into the ancient landmark is promised.

7. **Does deferring Greatbole change its rulings?** Simplify or weaken healing/catastrophe/fruit costs versus move the intact system to a later Design Version. Take intact deferral under this cleanse's capability authority. Preserve `greatbole_harvest_spec.md`: permanent selective sealant, full violent healing, explosive crossover, catastrophe permanence, hostile grubs, song, thermal/pilgrim rules and seed adjacency remain that later system's constraints. Never re-ask his waived tradeoffs. By contrast the kit card explicitly requires silence and shade-seeking in the first playable, so those stay now.

8. **Causeways require living landmarks?** Delete roads with boles versus use existing fallback anchors. The former removes the floor's natural engineering contrast. `RM_GenStep_RootCauseways.Generate` explicitly falls back when `BoleCenters` is empty. Keep static lanes, test disconnected/blocked geometry in M3, and defer moving roads. Calling the source proves coupling, not need.

9. **“Ground-hugging,” “thick,” “dense.”** A flat fog recolour or merely high density number versus low ground occlusion with readable crown silhouettes and materially obstructed local routes/sight. Take the latter pictures (B1), with inspectable footing. No mandatory opacity percentage, tree count or art-variant quota. XML `movementDifficulty` is world travel, not proof of local footing; this is explicit in the density applier/source comments.

10. **Steam and heat.** Private ambient felt offset/wet-bulb clock/fog sun discount versus shared geometric Direct Sun plus vanilla air heat. The first contradicts the 2026-10-19 ruling. Take the latter. Present BiomeDef still carries a legacy `RM_SunHeatExtension`; its activity is not assumed harmless and T38 must remove any local exception. Correct arrival text saying roofs/shade categorically do nothing. A roof shades direct radiation without cooling outdoor air.

11. **Breaklight as a second hazard system?** Another local heat multiplier versus temporary clearing of the same standing weather with its ruled native shade response and return. The multiplier violates the shared sun law; keep clearing/AI, no private heat. Kit card 2026-09-11 explicitly rejects deferral of seek-shade. Actual weather priority and fallback, rather than a locked Clear screenshot, are required.

12. **Blower fuelled “or” powered.** Either resource suffices versus every installed power/fuel/flick prerequisite must be met. The first produces a different machine than `RM_CompDryFieldEmitter.IsActive`. Take existing conjunction; its costs and failed prerequisite are visible. It cools the enclosed back room per `BLOWER_ROOM_COOLER_1`, never a deliberate heater. A suppression write alone is not protection proof.

13. **What does protection erase?** An instant tree/animal vacuum versus preventing new growth within the existing forward arc and deterring affected wild animals. Vacuum behaviour adds deletion and defeats ordinary ecology. Take prevention/aversion, with normal clearing needed first. Outage retains only declared decaying hold; repair does not silently clear existing plants. M3 sweeps rotations and overlaps.

14. **Living defence.** A grazer-name/pen aura versus suppression written by actual plant ingestion. An empty pen would win forever under the first. Take actual ingestion and expiry (`RM_Patch_GrazingSuppressionHook.cs`), record who ate what and where. Losing a herd changes capacity, not the universe's growth law.

15. **Rescued means condition removed?** Reset Mired in place versus deliver usable escape onto nearby firm ground for victim and helper. The former remires them immediately. Take the latter: the owner expressly made this a general trapping-terrain rescue rule on 2026-11-10, answer 3. Current `RM_JobDriver_FreeMired` removes the condition only; T35 must extend it. No owner question remains.

16. **Native/flying mud immunity.** Every pawn in the map scan versus grounded vulnerable travellers only. The first can immobilize home fauna and airborne flyers. Take authored native eligibility and real flight state; `RM_MapComponent_TerrainMire` presently lacks the flying gate and `RM_MireExtension` alone does not prove roster coverage. Natives' use of their own environmental water also remains a standing requirement, not a new water system.

17. **“Dig belongings back out.”** Recreate matching def/stuff/count versus preserve the same object's complete state and quantity. Reconstruction refreshes rot/HP and loses quality/style/biocode. Take identity-preserving saved ownership. Current `Bury` destroys the Thing and `RM_BuriedCache` stores only the reduced tuple, so this is a real source gap. Freeze buried time under the existing minimal no-decay form; never refresh initial state. Partial merge/blocked placement must conserve a recoverable remainder.

18. **Stockpiled or carried property.** Stockpiles exempt versus loose stockpiles sink like any loose pile; all objects versus explicit excluded held/corpse/minified objects. Take no stockpile exemption (kit owner ruling 2026-09-11), keep exclusions visibly present. Never silently bury pawns/corpses, strip minified contents or duplicate inventory during a haul.

19. **Insects are “built.”** A functional spawnable gall def versus a gall naturally associated with its hosts. Manual spawn proves only the response. Natural host placement is required for ordinary insect life; a search finds the named hosts in current flora but no placement caller. Build minimal adjacent standalone gall placement using existing bounded reaction service, not a new propagation ecology. Host removal cannot silently despawn already living wasps.

20. **Ordinary life versus special abilities.** Every existing creature comp is sacred first-version life versus retain the cast and ordinary roles, defer redundant/independent behaviours. Take the latter under the latest scope card: visible vurraks remain while disguise/contact ambush moves; illisk shoals remain while non-blast armor moves; thurrock feeding/felling remains while provoked wall attack moves. No roster eviction and no new replacement creatures. Preserve their chosen rules for enabled future behaviour.

21. **Natural danger requires every promised plant attack?** Gorbeleth shooting, maddrick trapping, phorrik cloud, nemmer periodic drops and sap-tapping jobs versus existing damaging tree falls plus ordinary harvests. The first reinstates multiple independent mechanics. Keep falls for the binding plant-danger axis and defer the separate interactions. A decorative forest with no player-relevant falls still fails B2; “plants” cannot mean only a colour or move penalty.

22. **The disease reward is optional flavour?** Cosmetic survivor badge versus acquired protection after surviving the actual collapse. The first fails the selected 2026-09-25 reward. Keep earned resistance to Frenzy only; no general jungle immunity, no expedition-role/job lock. Disease master/mark master/subtoggle must govern old and new marks (`RM_IncidentWorker_FrenzyDisease` and dose route currently omit the mark master). Early cure and death earn nothing. The portable dose route is explicitly ruled, not an extra discarded for convenience.

23. **“Unique medicines” licenses a new reward tree?** Many custom medical systems versus the already-selected discoverable stellock chain. Take that single chain as needed by the binding reward side. The 2026-10-02 selection specifically includes branch study and use anywhere, so discovery cannot be cut while claiming that selected experience. Total bleeding stops temporarily, wounds still need care, and separate found-tech counters remain independent. No additional medicines invented.

24. **“Always steam/every tile river.”** Reject authored riverless tiles versus allow dying-river graves already accepted by the sheet's 2026-09-08 amendment. Take the amendment and fixed geography. Do not repair planet paint or create runtime salinity just to satisfy older absolutes. The corrected world definition also says the Scald is terminal rather than spilling; use peak-fed corridors, not a new basin connection.

25. **Caught fish must swim on another map?** Sea-floor twin rule everywhere versus the explicit land-biome catch-only waiver recorded in the inventory and `GREENTIDE_EXOTIC_JUNGLE_FISH_1`. Keep catch-only fish. This waiver is settled and is not re-raised. Living illisks and dhollollo are separate ordinary animals, not every catch's compulsory duplicate.

26. **Humming grove is still an open Greentide task?** Restore Thalquith because an item remains open versus preserve its current Chill assignment. Take the latter from the tree def and absent Greentide roster entry. No new Greentide hum bar or repeat request for an already-waived audio trade. Stored code/assets remain so the neighbour works.

27. **All-off means skip the next tick?** Return early while active penalties/caches/marks persist versus stop new effects and gracefully resolve existing state. The former strands pawns and leaves immunity effective. Take explicit cleanup/retrievability with effect-scope labels. Startup gates cannot claim immediate effect. No saved extra pawn/object vanishes just because default activation is now off.

28. **Default-off extras just hide their own checkbox?** Leave secondary entry points running versus gate every producer, job, letter, recipe and incident, and decouple retained callers. Take the latter. Vortices have both incident and Roil map-component routes; stench has thrown and damage-trigger routes; causeways need fallback after boles move; old aliases remain. Source caller evidence is in census/tickets. Shared engines and other biomes retain their existing uses.

29. **What counts as proof?** Historical “closed,” static regex, matching default values and quarantined runs versus a current causal reading with all retained mechanics active. Take the latter: no proof on this pass is current. Existing `_site` locks Clear and clears hostiles, and density equality can pass with a dead applier (`validation.py`). Keep these as checks; T34 supplies default-weather, default-ecology evidence. No bridge is used on this design pass.

30. **Whole season and action order.** A staged afternoon with hidden prerequisites versus a full-map season, mid-cycle save/reload and harvest/upkeep in the reverse order. Take the latter. Tick compression may accelerate wall time only, preserving weather, growth, jobs, hunger, medical and spawn ordering. M2 decides flood/barren/stall risks from calibrated traces; a model output is never called a game measurement.

31. **Missing external patches mean no multi-homing?** Declare a complete roster from local BiomeDef versus distinguish known local life from unavailable campaign additions. Take unknown external membership. Historical inventory paths under `src/RimUtinni/UtinniPatches/` do not exist in this checkout. T41 requests a post-patch cast read at implementation. Record overlaps then and move on, never evict. This is not an owner question or a reason to stop the proposal.

32. **Habitable band, underlight rain and bottled growth.** A river-distance cooking/desiccation meter, permanent canopy precipitation and portable growth invocation versus ordinary siting/ambience and natural growth. The former adds independent thermal/weather/tool systems; none was found active in the searched local engines. Defer these mechanics explicitly. Use supplied air heat, common shade, cooled shelter and actual water/route access for siting; condensation imagery does not create another precipitation simulation or private sun. Ordinary ambience must actually support the kept hush cue (current BiomeDef names Ambient_NightInsects_Standard), including daytime eligibility. The world rain doctrine remains unchanged.
