# Greentide — proposed Design Version 0.1.0

This is a Designer proposal, not an applied change, a passing verdict, or owner certification. Every scope choice and clarification below is delegated Designer judgment unless its source explicitly says otherwise. No game, bridge, build, deployment, art edit or live measurement ran on this pass. No standing ruling is rewritten. Named model outputs are predictions until calibrated against real game state.

Greentide is a steaming river jungle where food and timber are worth the dangerous trip outside. Wet growth repeatedly contests exposed paths and entrance approaches, so colonists hold their home with clearing, dry-air blowers, firm footing and grazing. Neglect first costs work and access; warnings, rescue and retrieval give a prepared colony a way back before continued neglect can force it out.

**Land:** low standing steam under a readable canopy; obstructing understory, slow trapping churnmud, static firm root lanes, vanilla hot air and ordinary shade geometry, with Breaklight temporarily lifting the steam.

**Life:** the present alien canopy/understory and wildlife cast; ordinary forage, exotic fishing, resin, timber, grazers and predators; tree turnover, gall defence, yearning fruit seed passage, the ruled Frenzy/resistance and selected stellock medicine chain. No animal roster evictions.

**Signature:** Wet growth feeds the colony and reclaims its exposed access, so keeping an inhabited foothold requires local clearing, dry-air protection and grazing.

**Runner-up:** living Greatbole settlement and extraction. It loses because mined rooms, healing, thresholds, catastrophe, fruit processing and visitors form another colony-scale cause-and-effect system, not a necessary part of holding an ordinary wet-jungle home. Defer that package intact, including its ruled consequences and warnings. Keep the separate fellable `RM_Greatbole` tree and ordinary hardwood. No species or owner ruling is retired by deferring a capability to another Design Version. The current brief authorizes this scope decision; the kit's explicit first-playable exceptions for shade-seeking and silence remain.

## Why a player reaches for it

Choose Greentide to live beside an abundant, dangerous resource supply and solve the practical problem of getting that supply home. It pays in edible alien plants, catches, meat, tameable grazers, ordinary wood, true hardwood, resin and a learned bleeding-control medicine. These are usable through ordinary eating, cooking, fishing, butchery, construction, study and care. There is no requirement for a new cooking/ingredient combinator, export quest or resource quota.

This experience is more demanding than vanilla tropical-swamp scenery because plants renew contested space, the floor can trap travellers and property, a concealed river predator hunts, and disturbing insect hosts has consequences. Vanilla work priorities, pathfinding, research and medical jobs do the ordinary work; no custom colony-management dashboard is added. Prepared footing and maintained routes earn safety. Biome danger does not rise to cancel learned competence (flat-danger decision recorded in `GREENTIDE_RISK_REWARD_EXCHANGE_1`). Enjoyment of daily maintenance is a prediction, not a bar.

## Using it: first hour, routine, colony scale

On arrival, crowns remain visible above moving low steam. Terrain inspection identifies mud, root lane or constructed footing even when ground detail is obscured. Native plants and animals use the existing roster, not donor substitutions or a hand-spawned showcase. The player chooses a firm approach to water and harvests and locates shelter. No special architecture template is required: ordinary enclosed roofed rooms work. If later campaign templates are built, windowless domes and blower doorways remain their design constraint.

Clear an exposed entrance approach before installing protection. The blower faces outward and protects its existing shared arc; the room behind is its cooling target. Installed power, fuel and flick requirements must all be met. The ordinary power/fuel/working readout must identify which prerequisite failed, and a visible working tell must cease on outage. The field prevents new growth; it is not a tree-clearing weapon, mud drainage device or guarantee against an animal already attacking. Repair restores prevention; colonists clear existing obstruction normally.

Grazing contributes only when a pawn actually consumes a plant. Pen labels and animal names grant no protection. Killing, starving or removing the herd ends new grazing suppression; earlier suppression expires under the shared engine. Blower and grazing overlap by the existing suppression service, never by multiplying two private growth rates.

Normal wet-native maturation and fruit/wither/sprout cycles run under the later light rules. Seed passage visibly links eating to renewal. The ordinary shared sprout path respects floors, buildings and enclosed rooms. Consequently the threatened entrance is its exposed, plant-eligible **approach**, including the access to its threshold; this is not permission to punch seedlings through walls or pave over the engine's law. Plants can make the approach unusable through actual occupancy and path burden. If the existing code cannot produce that naturally at defaults, the integration remains a required build gap; a debug top event is not a substitute. The builder must use the current shared API and admissible cells rather than redesign ExplosiveGrowth.

Daily ordinary work includes clearing used exposed paths, fuel/refill/repair, hauling before mud dwell, grazing care, extraction and digging when needed. At a colony of forty, the same work priorities and reservations serve many routes and patients; the player intervenes for exceptional outages, dangerous harvests and simultaneous medical/rescue failures. The colony must not need individual orders for routine rescues/digs or constant camera babysitting. The upkeep capacity and warning windows are not established yet: M2 is specified to test whether these demands coexist with food production and medical downtime.

## What changes in the colony's story

The jungle becomes a home that can be maintained, rather than a destination forbidden to settlement. Productive space is earned locally; there is no map-wide pacification or oasis truce. Grazers become useful neighbours to the entrance, a firm rescue lane becomes infrastructure, and an outage becomes a recoverable work emergency. A disease survivor can become a safer jungle hand through acquired Frenzy resistance. That qualification is the existing visible mark and resistance gate, not a newly invented caravan/job exclusion.

Stellock is the minimum selected unique medicine reward. A naturally felled eligible tree can yield a rotting self-sealed branch; study through shared found-tech counters reveals the existing medicine research. Completing research permits crafting lace from the authored resin/industrial-medicine/cloth chain. Self-use and doctor administration temporarily stop the recipient's total bleeding, including stumps. Wounds remain, do not become tended or healed, and can bleed again on expiry. Already-ruled portability remains: learned lace works elsewhere. This is a narrow item contract, not new cross-biome validation of the jungle.

The Frenzy replaces the already-ruled generic disease rows; no extra disease count is introduced. Ambient exposure and plant-derived portable doses both produce faster work followed by collapse. A coma entails meaningful lost work and a treatment/death fork, including the ruled multi-day downtime; its exact duration and multipliers are tuning/checks. A living pawn that actually reached collapse and recovered earns a persistent visible mark; death or pre-collapse removal does not. Resistance answers this illness, not every jungle hazard. The feature master must also govern old marks' protection. No moral/social reaction system is added for deliberate dosing.

## Costs, failure, and readable signs

Costs are time outside cover, heat-safe equipment and cooling, fuel/power, clearing/hauling labour, resin for engineered footing, illness downtime and medical materials. Yield, value and speed tuning are checks. The medicine reward does not erase wound care; fruit does not erase hunger costs or the need to retrieve it; tree falls produce timber while threatening workers.

Foliage visibly returning and changed hauling routes announce a neglected approach. A stopped blower advertises its failed prerequisite before suppression expires into new growth. There is a recovery window to re-fuel, clear and retrieve, rather than instant enclosure. Continued neglect can make routine access untenable and lead to evacuation with property left behind. It does not cause a scripted forced-move event. A maintained firm rescue route must still permit reaching stranded people; the owner settled recovery first and practical rescue in `ANSWERS_OWNER.md` on 2026-11-10. M2/M3 determine whether that window and geometry actually hold; no invented live result is asserted.

Mud applies visible slow/trap stages to vulnerable grounded travellers. Natives do not mire progressively at home; airborne pawns do not touch the mud while flying. The current extension can list immunity but authored native coverage and flying exclusion need verification/repair. Extraction is complete only when both victim and helper have usable escape onto nearby firm ground. If that ground is unavailable or a target/helper dies, the job fails readably, releases reservations and does not loop forever. No teleport across an inaccessible map or new custom rescue framework is implied.

Loose eligible property can sink even inside a stockpile. A visible persistent cell marker and inspectable recoverable contents replace it; the quantity and entire object state must survive burial, reload and digging. Transfer to a saved holder is the proposed implementation rule; reconstruction from def/stuff/count currently loses state. Quality, HP, rot, biocode, style and arbitrary supported components are preserved. Buried time is frozen as the existing minimal no-decay form specifies; digging does not refresh an already damaged or partly rotten thing. Blocked placement retains a saved remainder and its designation. Carried things, corpses and minified objects remain visibly excluded, never silently destroyed. Turning burial off stops new sinks and keeps old caches retrievable.

Falling trees use the existing shared crack/gnaw/shatter service, with its warning/fall/wood/trunk effects; the thurrock fells to feed and open canopy. Its separate provoked wall attack is deferred; no moult or new rut behaviour is added. A naturally placed gall near sarquin/nemmer can be disturbed into a bounded defensive wasp event, with readable stings and actual retreat. Natural placement is presently absent; the source def is functional but not a natural ecology by itself. Host removal prevents new host attachment, not arbitrary death or deletion of already spawned insects. Existing event budgets/population caps remain shared dependency rules.

Dhollollo hunts from water using shared concealment/reveal/lunge. A bridge protects footing, not the whole bank approach or every predator reach. The kept silence cue hushes biome ambience and supplies a visible warning, not machinery/weather/all audio. Overlapping reasons restore sound only after the last reason ends. Warning audio end/respawn is an accepted historical trade, not a fresh request for smooth fading. No pawn disappears without a readable sign: concealment has its inspect/hunt cue and reveal, flight has state/animation, death has ordinary corpse/sign, and retreat is not silent despawn.

## Edges and neighbours

One fixed desert world is supplied. Rivers are dayside and do not connect the basins; the world definition's corrected hydrology takes precedence over old prose saying the Scald itself spills. Peak-fed river corridors and dying-river remnants explain the setting. Do not generate a different planet or repair a zero-tile biome. Local map steps place existing terrain/plants/root routes; that is distinct from planet generation. No runtime cross-tile salinity, distance-to-river cooking/desiccation band, under-canopy precipitation or portable growth-extract tool is promised here. The habitable-band image means choosing reachable water, firm routes and ordinary protected/cooled shelter; it is not a new distance-based heat or humidity law. Ordinary biome ambience remains audible enough for the ruled predator hush to mean something; its actual sound source must be tested across the day, not assumed from an ambient def name. Riverless remnants keep their authored geography and receive negative/edge scenarios; never conjure a new river to manufacture a passing wet-jungle scene.

Air heat is ordinary ambient temperature, answered by insulation and enclosed cooling. Direct Sun is the same geometric function everywhere, including tree/roof shade. The legacy biome-specific felt-offset extension and explanatory arrival text are inconsistent with that later rule and must be removed from active effect/corrected by the builder. Roil and Breaklight are the visual/weather layer, not another sun value, wet-bulb hediff or humidity clock. Breaklight lifts low steam, shade-seeking carriers seek reachable shade and Roil returns. Shade does not reduce outdoor air temperature; cooling the room does. No fabricated fog heat discount makes the tests work.

CreatureBehaviors supplies scoped sight, aquatic hunting/reveal, bounded reactions and flight-compatible natives, seed passage, silence and shade-seeking. EnvironmentalHazards supplies falls, blower suppression/cooling, grazing writes, weather priority/return, found-tech study and static causeways. ExplosiveGrowth supplies the wet-native lifecycle and suppression grid. Wreckage supplies ordinary wreck placement/weathering/salvage; no local Greentide weathering row was located, so no new salvage reward is claimed. Bones appends ordinary butchering by-products without a second local bone economy. Their shared engines are not redesigned or globally switched off to implement local deferrals. A huge corpse/wreck interacting with paths or burial is a situation for the generator.

RimMandrake owns the invented free-tier experience; genuine canon/campaign additions remain the appropriate patch-layer responsibility. The historical campaign paths cited in the rehearsal inventory are missing here, so current external patch membership is unknown, not a complete fauna census. No roster placement is evicted. Available cast overlap must be recorded at application/review from post-patch state; this pass cannot truthfully certify the unseen membership set. Thalquith's Chill home is already explicit in its local def and absent Greentide roster entry; its stored assets/service remain neighbour dependencies. Do not re-open the catch-only waiver, hum-listen waiver/trade or old dryad off-default decision.

## Settings

Each retained major feature has a real on/off and necessary tuning under its actual shared owner. Defaults mean the version's behaviour, not the previous source defaults. Greentide's screen must name/link shared controls for growth/suppression, blower/cooler, tree falls, root routes, reactions, sight, silence, shade-seeking and weather; it must not create duplicate switches on the same wire. Local mire, burial, disease, marks/resistance, density, Roil and lace controls retain persistence, search/group/reset and effect-scope labels. A settings readback without the effect is not proof.

Changing a live feature off stops new harm and releases retained state cleanly: mired penalties cannot freeze, old caches remain diggable, old marks no longer protect when their master is off, concealment is revealed readably, active suppression is allowed its declared expiry/cleanup, and no saved pawn or object is deleted. Explain Now/new-map/next-start exactly. Roil's new-map application and crossover startup patches cannot pretend to be immediate.

Every built deferred extra gets a labelled **Next version — off by default** setting and a switch-off ticket. Keep code, defs, aliases and old-save resolution. Disable all local activation routes, UI offers and generated work/letters that belong solely to the extra; the definition's ordinary species and shared-neighbour services remain. Existing unsupported cross-biome mud controls stay off and labelled unsupported; no new cross-biome switch. Old saved extra instances remain readable and inert unless explicitly enabled. No migration deletion. The default fresh map and the default mid-save reload both must be free of active deferred behaviour.

## First hour

The colony follows a root lane past moving low steam and inspects the dark floor before committing a hauler. Fruit and timber make staying attractive. They choose a firm rescue approach and an ordinary enclosed home, clear its entrance and face the blower out into the green. No living-bole dungeon or ritual is necessary to begin.

## Ordinary week

Grazers eat near the maintained approach and the blower holds its cleared arc; fresh plants return outside it. Colonists haul catches, roots, resin and fallen timber, keep the firm route open, and respond to a gall they disturb rather than treating it as a generated quest. A wounded hunter gets temporary lace after the colony learns it, then real tending. Daily work runs through ordinary priorities.

## Worst day

The blower loses fuel while a fever patient collapses and a hauler mires on a neglected shortcut. A stopped working tell, returning foliage, visible Mired and a buried-cell marker identify separate problems. Available workers reach the hauler by firm footing, extract both pawns to safety, refuel, clear and dig. A colony that keeps ignoring the signs may leave property and evacuate, but an instant inaccessible trap is a failed design, not intended harshness.

## Season's turn

Breaklight lifts the low steam and the shade-seeking life moves under canopy; shared geometric sunlight and hot air still govern exposure. The steam returns. A mid-cycle save resumes with the same buried belongings, illness history, suppression expiry and work. The maintained colony remains productive beside threatening jungle; the neglected comparison shows the cost of lost access. If either becomes an empty larder, a vegetation/insect flood or a perpetual rescue loop, the whole bar refuses it and the models point to which rule to change first.

## Evidence boundary and second lazy-build attack

The real concept folder contains no record/sheet/tree/log or next-version file; `old_bars` is empty as the CASE states. The rehearsal at `design/RimMandrake/designer_codex_2026-11-09/Greentide/` supplies its inventory, uncertainty notes and later owner answers, not a locked acceptance tree. All local referenced implementation paths were checked for existence; historical campaign paths failed that check and are labelled unknown. Read inventory and present source together: old item prose calling plants, grazing or the base port unbuilt is stale.

The laziest apparently compliant build is an attractive swamp with fast growth, inactive gall defs, suppression counters, seed filth and a rescue that merely clears Mired. It fails because there is no natural contest of inhabited access, no naturally encountered insect hazard, no measured protection outcome, no conserved buried object and no firm-ground escape. A second lazy build removes the living Greatbole but leaves its incidents, ladder, seed recipes or scanner payload active; the switch-off tickets explicitly test those indirect routes. The remaining proposal is not claimed built or proven: unknown meetings are intentional first-build work, with calibrated instruments specified rather than guessed passing statements.
