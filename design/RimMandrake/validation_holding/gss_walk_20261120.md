## Where the black blots and the crater come from

**Neither is ours, and neither is vanilla. Both are flecks of the workshop mod Extra Explosion Effects** (Keshash,
packageId `keshash.extraexplosioneffects`, workshop folder 3066208882), which is in his 315-mod list [measured]:

- **The black blots:** FleckDef `Fleck_EEE_Soot` (`1.6/Vanilla/Defs/VanillaExplosionFleckDefs.xml:100-111` in that mod):
  vanilla's smoke puff texture `Things/Mote/Smoke`, tinted almost black `(0.1,0.1,0.1)`, at `MoteLow`, solid for 20 s and
  then faded over 2 s. Its C# (`Source/ExtraExplosionEffects/ExplosionEffects.cs:56,65`, `CreateBurntSoot`) throws it on
  every Bomb blast [measured def and code]. The curly edge in picture 10 is the smoke puff's edge [guess ~90% that the blots are
  this fleck: the texture and colour match, and no other mod in the list ships a scorch decal].
- **The crater:** FleckDef `Fleck_EEE_BombCrater_Dirt`, texture `Things/Flecks/Fleck_EEE_BombCrater_Dirt.png` (`:126-134`),
  thrown at `ExplosionEffects.cs:59` on soil (the grey `Fleck_EEE_BombCrater` on other ground, `:68`) [measured: the texture
  is the brown ring-in-ring crater of picture 10].
- **Both vanish after about 20 seconds**: they are flecks, not filth or terrain. Nothing is left on the map, and no save
  holds them [measured: `solidTime` 20, `fadeOutTime` 1 and 2].
- **This is KNOWN.** His card of 2026-11-12 (ledger note on RIMMANDRAKE_DECIDE_EXPLOSION_MODS_KEEP_ABSORB) was "Absorb the idea
  into Knockback": item `RIMMANDRAKE_ABSORB_EXTRA_EXPLOSION_EFFECTS_OWN_FLECKS` builds our own blast flecks (soot, scorch,
  debris, sparks) in Explosive Knockback through the art pipeline, then drops Extra Explosion Effects from the test lists.
  His words today ("Mark that art for significant improvement") go on that item as its art bar. No second item.
- Better Explosions (`nephlite.advexplosions`, kept by his card of 2026-11-12) draws only overhead gas and spark flecks
  (`Misc/Gas1-4`, `Smoke`, `Spark`, all `MoteOverhead`); it leaves nothing on the ground [measured: its 1.6 `Damages.xml`].
