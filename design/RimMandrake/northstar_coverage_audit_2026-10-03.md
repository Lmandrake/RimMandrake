# North-star coverage audit — 2026-10-03 (NORTHSTAR_COVERAGE_AUDIT_1)

Every `validation.py` under `src/` (**111**, `git ls-files 'src/**/validation.py'`; the item said 55 when filed) compared against its mod's intended function: About.xml description, defs, Mod Settings fields and cited design docs. Six Sonnet readers, one per batch of ~19, each row read from source; one row (WreckedMachines) spot-checked by hand and confirmed. Treat a row as evidence, not a finding: re-read the mod before building against it.

**Verdicts:** COVERED 16 · PARTIAL 67 · THIN 27 · STUB 1.
COVERED = every major mechanic and every settings toggle has an asserting component; PARTIAL = some uncovered; THIN = most behaviour unasserted; STUB = asserts almost nothing. *Spawn-only* means a thing is placed and counted but never shown to do its job.

## Bridge-call lint (`northstar_driver/lint_calls.py --summary src`)

1,898 literal-named calls, 22 problems. **Two were real and are fixed** (`b60666053`): Cuisine passed `jawa/list_things` an undeclared `fields=`; UnfinishedLine called `jawa/step_game_ticks`, which does not exist (`rimworld/step_game_ticks`). The other 20 are UNCHECKED (`**kwargs` wrappers the lint cannot see through), not known-wrong.

## Tickets

Each THIN or STUB script has its own child item, `<MOD>_COVERAGE_GAPS_1` (mod name upper-cased), whose spec is that mod's row below. The 67 PARTIAL rows are one standing child, `NORTHSTAR_PARTIAL_GAPS_FILL_1`: work them a mod at a time, next to that mod's own build or live run, adding the rows' cheap static bars first. Owner-validated `## north star` sections (FlowWorks, Graffiti, Pyrelands) are not touched by any of this.
