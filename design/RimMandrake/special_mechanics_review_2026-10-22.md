- *"We need to review all of these special mechanics."*

**Rites ruled, 2026-10-23 (owner):** every found rite of the Nine listed below (the Struck Glass, the Answering, the
Joining Water, the Ceded Room, the Open Boast, the Cold Ledger, the Stillsand, Blue Desert, Leaning Scrub, Long Shade and

rows here. The rows below are the 2026-10-22 snapshot.

**Nothing else here is decided.** Every disposition (keep / simplify / cut / merge / ask) is **FOUNDRY's opinion**, one
line of why, for him to rule on. No roster is touched (evictions are stopped); tile counts are never cited (the planet
is painted once at the end); every DLC is assumed.

**How it was measured.** One pass per biome over its mod folder (`D:\Luke\dev\RimMandrake\src\RimMandrake\<Mod>`, plus
named `src\RimUtinni\` companions), its validation walk (`design\validation_walks\RimMandrake\<Mod>.md`, whose
`## north star` holds the owner's experience line), `design\RimMandrake\biome_experiences_2026-10-20.md`, and the ledger
(`infrastructure\state\items\` live, `items\closed\`, `rimflow show`). Build state words: **built-validated** (a live
run is recorded) / **built-untested** (code and defs exist, no live run recorded) / **designed** (doc or item only) /
**tabled** (dropped, blocked, parked or v2) / **cut**. Code sizes are line counts of the files named (`wc -l`); mod
totals are all `.cs` / `.xml` lines in the folder. Spot-checked by FOUNDRY against the ledger and the files: the Struck
Glass rite state (`PYRELANDS_STRUCK_GLASS_RITE_BUILD_1`: validated to L1, owes L2 and L4) and the Stillsand water-ledger
line count (510 + 95). Other rows carry their own cited evidence; a row that says UNMEASURED was not measured.

Columns of every table: what it does for the player · build state (evidence) · what depends on it / what it depends on ·
cost to keep (code lines, live checks owed) · the fluff test (does it change a player decision? how often is it met?
is it in the north-star line, quoted?) · FOUNDRY's opinion.
