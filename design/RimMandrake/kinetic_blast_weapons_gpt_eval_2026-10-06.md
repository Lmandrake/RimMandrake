**Viable foundation; revise before commissioning the full range.** The lookup hierarchy is sensible, but direction, cones, non-lethality and control balance contain substantial defects. The map-component/flyer implementation is absent, so the existing “18/18 PASS” cannot certify these additions.

1. **High — Impact-centred cones can miss the direct target.** Engine `ExplosionCellsToHit` excludes the centre cell when `affectedAngle` exists. Consequently, a repulsor’s directly hit pawn—and a mine’s triggering pawn—can receive nothing. Angles also use signed `atan2(-z,x)` coordinates, not an automatically rotated ±45° range; wrapping needs explicit handling. **Fix:** use a shared cone-cell selector that includes the centre, handles angular wrapping and preserves LOS. Disable neighbour-cell expansion for strict cones. Radial falloff still applies within the cone; it does not produce a uniform shove. [Engine cell selection](https://github.com/Chillu1/RimWorldDecompiled/blob/master/Verse/DamageWorker.cs)

2. **High — `pushAlongShot` needs captured projectile geometry.** `Explosion` does not retain the launch vector. `DoExplosion(direction)` uses direction to establish LOS constraints; it does not store it as knockback direction. Reconstructing from the instigator’s current position fails after movement, death, despawning or map changes. Distance alone is harmless if the vector is normalized. **Fix:** capture horizontal `destination − origin` before projectile destruction, attach it to explosion context and snapshot it into requests; define a radial fallback for absent/zero vectors. Also give the **palm thumper** `pushAlongShot`: currently a direct hit gets the kernel’s arbitrary epicentre direction. [Explosion creation](https://github.com/Chillu1/RimWorldDecompiled/blob/master/Verse/GenExplosion.cs)

3. **High — `RM_Repulse` is not wound-free or non-lethal.** `DamageWorker_AddInjury` still injures pawns with positive damage despite `harmsHealth=false`; that flag protects ordinary thing HP, not this injury path. Separately, an eight-cell throw blocked immediately produces **32 blunt damage**, grav-ram **40**, before mass adjustment. **Fix:** use a no-injury worker or explicitly zero pawn damage, and set `impactFactor=0` for arrest equipment. Describe other repulse weapons as displacement weapons; pits remain lethal. “Safe in a friendly scrum” is unjustified. [Injury worker](https://github.com/Chillu1/RimWorldDecompiled/blob/master/Verse/DamageWorker_AddInjury.cs)

4. **High — The immunity window does not prevent control locking.** Ninety ticks after landing can expire before a 120-tick stun ends. Repeated “immune” hits that stagger can continue suppression. Flyers also temporarily remove targets from subsequent explosions, creating a protective interaction with friendly bombardment. **Fix:** prevent another launch until **stun ends + recovery window**, and prevent immune hits from refreshing kinetic stagger. Persist timestamps through saves. Test sustained multi-weapon fire and mixed kinetic/HE barrages.

5. **High — Shield handling needs an absorption contract.** Shield belts place `CompShield` on worn apparel, not normally on the pawn. Checking “active” only after damage misses a shield that absorbed the hit and broke. `force × 10` is also ambiguous: internal energy uses `energyLossPerDamage`; subtracting 28 energy directly is radically different from 28 damage-equivalent energy. **Fix:** capture actual absorption during damage processing, deduplicate one kinetic energy debit per blast/pawn, inspect apparel and built-in shields, and define break-through behavior. Test projectile interceptors separately. [Shield implementation](https://github.com/Chillu1/RimWorldDecompiled/blob/master/RimWorld/CompShield.cs)

6. **High — Trap API and rearming are underspecified.** The proposed signature has no cone parameter, and direct enqueueing lacks an explosion’s tick postfix to flush it. Reusing the trap’s ID for successive activations risks dedupe suppression. Vanilla trap “rearm” destroys and rebuilds; it is not a persistent ten-chemfuel reload. **Fix:** supply a cone/mask, unique activation ID and map-level flush route; share eligibility, LOS, budgets and shield handling. Implement saved armed/fuel state and a real reload job. [Trap lifecycle](https://github.com/Chillu1/RimWorldDecompiled/blob/master/RimWorld/Building_Trap.cs)

7. **Medium — Metadata lookup works, but “weapon” means different things.**

   - Thrown grenades: projectile def, grenade equipment def, throwing pawn.
   - Manned mortar: loaded shell’s **projectile**, mortar **building** as weapon, manning pawn as instigator.
   - Unmanned turret: projectile, internal `turretGunDef` weapon, turret instigator.
   - Vanilla explosive trap: projectile/weapon normally **null**; instigator depends on `CompExplosive`.

   **Fix:** tune projectiles primarily; do not expect shell-item or turret-building extensions to resolve universally. Replace both prefix and postfix `ForceOf` calls with one captured configuration; explicit force zero must win. Unique-weapon damage overrides also mean a `Thump` DamageDef patch does not guarantee every variant inherits it. [Launch metadata](https://github.com/Chillu1/RimWorldDecompiled/blob/master/Verse/Verb_LaunchProjectile.cs), [trap explosions](https://github.com/Chillu1/RimWorldDecompiled/blob/master/RimWorld/CompExplosive.cs)

8. **Medium — Per-request settings are sound but incomplete.** Snapshot settings before enqueue; never mutate shared/global settings. Palm cap five requires a **−1** bonus, contradicting purely additive “bonus” language. `immuneBodySizeOverride` is used but absent from the extension specification. **Fix:** specify signed cap offsets, clamping and master-off behavior; add a nullable immunity override that respects global disabling. Eligibility uses **≥**, so 3.5 itself remains immune. First-extension-wins must explicitly mean the whole configuration, otherwise default fields silently hide fallback values.

9. **Medium — Thump 2.5/+2 is reasonable for the narrow promise.** At 70 kg, distance one: `round(10 × (1−1/1.9)) = 5`, versus mortar three. Damage does not enter the kernel, so equal-damage comparison gives the same result. However, at distance two the thump is **outside its radius**, while mortar still throws; centre throws are directionally arbitrary. **Fix:** promise “stronger displacement beside impact,” retain these prototype values, and test actual equipped masses, cardinal/diagonal offsets and global settings.

10. **Medium — Damage sacrifice is coherent only with displacement throughput included.** Range, accuracy, warmup, bursts and repeat control are missing for several weapons. Eight-cell throws plus stun are powerful in open terrain too. Ammo-free turret arrays and cheap rearming pit gates can industrialize captures; enemy AI cannot exploit equivalent terrain. **Fix:** balance control per second and capture yield, specify complete firing stats, and give automated weapons charge/ammunition costs or substantial recovery. Document the player-favored terrain asymmetry.

11. **Medium — Compatibility and validation claims overreach.** Repulse’s non-harmful flag may exclude it from cover-breaking and liquid hooks; direct API blasts bypass explosion hooks entirely. Low building damage does not constrain separate blunt door impacts. **Fix:** specify explicit cover/cord/liquid policies. Replace “every scene uses a pawn-held weapon” with real mortar loading, turret firing and trap entry. Add centre-hit/wrapped-angle cones, destroyed shooters, all lookup fallbacks, shield break cases, immunity save/load, repeated mine activation and item conservation through merges/pits. Use deterministic mechanics fixtures plus separate real-verb accuracy trials; fifty faction rolls cannot guarantee a rare spawn, and immunity is not currently kernel-testable.

12. **Low — Reduce the launch range.** Grenade, directional rifle, mine and mortar shell provide distinct mechanics; the thump patch is independently worthwhile. **Fix:** merge palm thumper into the rifle as an early version; defer slam launcher, pulse cannon and grav-ram until control balance passes. Slam overlaps grenade/shell delivery, pulse automates the rifle, and grav-ram mostly escalates numbers plus one immunity exception. Commission retained silhouettes first.
