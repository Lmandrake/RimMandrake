**Convert finite lakes at map creation, once their existing activities survive conversion.** The premise is partly wrong: level water with a varied bed is coherent. Players notice contradictory rules, not competing representations.

**1. What players would notice, ranked by disruption**

1. **Activities disappear:** touching a lake with a canal suddenly invalidates fishing zones, swimming spots, aquatic farms, bridges, or drinking access.
2. **Identical ponds obey different rules:** one drains into a hollow; another remains unchanged. Lazy conversion avoids this particular seam only if every relevant interaction triggers it.
3. **Crossings change unexpectedly:** familiar deep-water art means impassable terrain in one place and swimmable water elsewhere. Recreational swimming does not automatically imply unrestricted deep-water travel.
4. **First-contact transformation:** pumping causes an immediate colour change, shoreline redraw, plant loss, ice change, or fish-stock reset before appreciable water moves.
5. **Edges look inconsistent:** canals meet lakes with mismatched shorelines, abrupt tint boundaries, or apparently disconnected water surfaces.
6. **Readouts change vocabulary:** “Shallow water” becomes exact metres and litres. Your example is useful, but clarify that 4,740 L is **this square**, and distinguish excavation depth from water depth.

Shared shallow/deep artwork can conceal much of the difference until interaction. Ocean replenishment is understandable; an unexplained inexhaustible inland pond is harder to accept.

**3. What should stay outside finite, level lake conversion**

- **Open oceans:** keep them replenishing. A coastal pump should not empty a planetary sea. Label them “Ocean — replenishing”; avoid implying a finite total volume.
- **Through-flowing rivers:** retain current and upstream supply. Flattening them into lakes erases their identity. An isolated backwater can become finite after separation.
- **Propane, hot liquids, and brines:** never convert them into ordinary water. A bounded propane lake can still have finite volume and a bed, but needs its own swimming, drinking, farming, freezing, and catch rules.

A dammed river reservoir needs explicit river behaviour before it can honestly promise lake-style storage. Also, draining a surface basin must not accidentally promise pedestrian access to the separate gravship-only sea-floor layer.
