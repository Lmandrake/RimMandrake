3. **Yes: one physical representation is possible.** Natural versus excavated can be provenance; finite versus limitless can be a boundary condition. Lazy versus eager initialization is independent of representation. Replace the pond’s float stock with integer decilitres if exact conservation is required.

The terrain adapter must preserve **depth × freshwater/saltwater × still/moving × toxicity**, plus ice/bridge/foundation layers. XML inheritance copies fields; it does not confer vanilla def identity.

`GridsUtility.GetWaterBodyType` reads base terrain when a foundation exists, ignores temporary terrain, and otherwise reads exposed terrain. It does **not** universally search beneath arbitrary fill terrain. Keep physical floors in FlowWorks data; do not assume an under-terrain bed supplies water semantics. [layer selection](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Chillu1/RimWorldDecompiled/master/Verse/GridsUtility.cs)

Same-type band swaps do **not** rebuild fishing bodies. New bodies initialize population/species; a normal saved-body reconstruction is not an unconditional reroll. Drying removes fishing-zone cells; refilling does not restore them. Incremental merging discards absorbed body objects, while deletion does not comprehensively split connectivity. Your flip fix addresses the demonstrated type-change bookkeeping failure, not these broader lifecycle issues. [tracker source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Chillu1/RimWorldDecompiled/master/RimWorld/WaterBodyTracker.cs)

Batch shoreline updates and reconcile topology, population and freeze/pollution counters without repeatedly reinitializing fish. Full scans/flood fills scale with map/body size; band changes still invoke terrain-change side effects.

**The biggest trap is treating terrain swaps as cosmetic.** They mutate habitat, construction, pathing and persistent fishing state. Particularly concrete: freezeable canal water with `waterBodyType=None` can leave `FreezeManager.DoWaterFreezing` dereferencing a missing body.

4. My recommendation:

- Adopt one volume/floor model for natural and dug water.
- Prefer B for new maps; migrate existing maps through A.
- Keep rivers/oceans limitless while preserving their vanilla metadata.
- Treat terrain selection as a gameplay adapter with persistent ecology state.
- Validate winter, drawdown/refill, bridges, fish, plants and external farming before broad conversion.
