## 1. The thing: `RM_Shovel` (FlowWorks, `Defs/Pits/ThingDefs/RM_Shovel.xml`)

- A stuffable **tool item, not a weapon** (self-review R1): `category` Item, `thingCategories` a new `RM_Tools` under
  Manufactured, no `equipmentType`, no `weaponTags`, so no pawnkind ever generates wielding it, Simple Sidearms and
  ideoligion weapon precepts ignore it, and a violence-incapable colonist can still dig. Mass ~2 kg, `techLevel`
  Neolithic, stuff `Metallic` + `Woody`, cost ~40 stuff, work ~1,600, **no research**, recipe at the crafting spot and
  both smithies (`recipeMaker.recipeUsers`; bench defNames read from the def dump in phase 2, never typed from memory).
  A swingable-spade weapon variant is a v2 option, not v1.
- A marker `RM_DiggingTool : DefModExtension` (empty in v1) on the def. The rule asks "does the pawn hold a thing with
  `RM_DiggingTool`", never "is it RM_Shovel", so another mod (or a v2 pick/mattock) opts in by patch.
- Trader stock: not in v1 (raiders drop them; the crafting spot makes them). Starting scenarios: every scenario we ship
  starts with one (owner Q3 "yes"): `RimUtinni/UtinniPatches/Defs/ScenarioDefs/Scenario_Utinni.xml` (our only
  ScenarioDef) gets a `ScenPart_StartingThing_Defined` RM_Shovel (steel, 1), `MayRequire="mandrake.rm.flowworks"`.
- Art: `Textures/Things/Item/Equipment/RM_Shovel.png` is owed. Filed as an art item (SHOVEL_SPRITE_SKETCH_BLADE_DOWN_HANDLE_UP);
  no generation in this item. Until it lands the def points at a vanilla item texture whose path is verified on disk
  in phase 2 (reading-rimworld-graphics), with a `<!-- PLACEHOLDER art -->` comment so the art audit finds it.

## 2. Who needs one: `RM_ShovelRule` (pure) + `RM_Shovels` (Verse)

Pure, selftested (`RM_ShovelMath.NeedsShovel(settingOn, isMachineLabour, raceDigsBareHanded)`):
- Setting **"Digging needs a shovel"** off -> nobody needs one (shipped behaviour before this item).
- Machine labour (`RM_DigLabor.IsMachineLabour`) -> no shovel: *"And droids. And machines."*
- A race / flesh type carrying a new `RM_DigsBareHanded : DefModExtension` -> no shovel. FlowWorks names no franchise
  def; this is the hook v2 utility creatures (digger animals) and burrowing xenotypes patch onto, guarded by
  PatchOperationFindMod. v1 ships no patch using it.
- Everyone else (colonists, slaves, prisoners on work, raiders, visitors) needs one.

Verse (`RM_Shovels`):
- `HasShovel(pawn)`: any thing in `pawn.inventory.innerContainer` has `RM_DiggingTool` (inventory is its only home: a colonist keeps a rifle equipped and the shovel in the pack, and carrying fill
  (`carryTracker`) does not collide with it).
- `FindShovelFor(pawn, forced, maxDist, raider)`: closest spawned thing with `RM_DiggingTool` that is reachable, not
  forbidden (colonists only), `CanReserve`, not burning; a coarse `AnyOnMap` listerThings check first, and memoised per
  pawn per tick so a scan over many designated squares pays one search.
- `Required` = setting on AND some def carries `RM_DiggingTool` (no def: nobody could comply, so nobody must).

## 3. Colonists: WorkGivers and JobDrivers (two jobs, not a C-target)

Built shape (replaces the phase-1 "targetC + prepended toils" plan after the Codex consult: a C-target needs its own
reservation, restart and stale-target handling in two drivers; two ordinary jobs need none):
- `HasJobOnCell` (dig: after the machine-cap refusal; fill: after the floor reservation test) calls
  `RM_Shovels.CanWorkOrFetch(pawn, forced)`: true if `Ready` (needs none, or has one) or a free shovel is findable;
  else `JobFailReason.Is("Needs a shovel (none free on the map).")`, false. A missing shovel never deletes a
  designation (it is temporary, like a pawn standing in the square).
- `JobOnCell`: not `Ready` -> `RM_Shovels.FetchJobFor(pawn, forced)` (vanilla `TakeInventory`, count 1, on the nearest
  free shovel) instead of the dig/fill job. The next scan, shovel in the pack, hands out the dig or fill as before.
- Drivers: `JobDriver_DigCanal` and `JobDriver_FillInCanal` `FailOn(() => !RM_Shovels.Ready(pawn))`: a shovel lost
  mid-job ends it; the cut/fill effect fires only at the end of the work toil, so nothing is paid twice.

Keeping it: an inventory shovel must survive vanilla's inventory clean-up. Engine names confirmed present in the
1.6 assembly (strings): `JobGiver_DropUnusedInventory`, `JobGiver_UnloadYourInventory`, `TakeCountToInventory`. Whether
DropUnusedInventory would drop a non-drug, non-food tool item is **an engine question this seat cannot decompile** (no
RimSage here); phase 2 settles it by the live row SH_shovel_stays_in_inventory, and if it drops, a Harmony prefix
exempts `RM_DiggingTool` things. Not reasoned from a doc.

Alert: `Alert_RM_DigNeedsShovel` — dig or fill designations exist, no free colonist holds a shovel and none lies on
the map: "Dig orders are waiting for a shovel. Make one at a crafting spot." Folded into the existing dig alert file
pattern (`RM_DeepDigAlert.cs`).

Other dig paths: "Repair breach" and dig-to orders go through the same designation/WorkGiver, so they inherit the rule.
God-mode / dev instant digs are untouched.

Sand and drift (owner Q2 "yes"): Pyrelands' `RM_ShovelTerrain` WorkGiver/driver (`Pyrelands/Source/RM_TerrainDig.cs`)
and vanilla `WorkGiver_ClearSnowOrSand` / `JobDriver_ClearSnowAndSand` on a cell with sand depth > 0 (Moving Dunes,
`MovingDunes/Source/Patch_ClearSandShovel.cs`, Harmony postfixes) follow the same two-job pattern. Neither mod depends
on FlowWorks: both source-link `_Shared/FlowWorksShovels/FlowWorksShovelRead.cs`, which reflects `RM_Shovels.Ready` /
`FetchJobFor` / `NoShovelReason`; FlowWorks absent -> nobody needs a shovel there. Snow-only clearing stays vanilla.

## 4. Raiders: shovels in the raid, shovels to fill, sappers' ladders

Owner Q1, 2026-10-25: *"Siege is mortar-based. Sappers however would bring shovels & ladders. And there's always a small
number of folks with ladders anytime, possibly. But ladders can just be made."* Siege digging (borrow pits) and siege
breach are OUT: not built, not behind a switch. `IsSiege` keeps `TryCross` null for a siege (Q7 2026-10-18 stands).

a) **Raids bring shovels.** `RM_Patch_Raid_Shovels`, a postfix beside the jump-pack one on
   `IncidentWorker_Raid.PostProcessSpawnedPawns` (label-only `PatchFeature` "Raiders bring shovels"; it does nothing
   when `shovelRequired` or the raider-trench feature is off): on a map with a dug trench (`ClimbCellCount >=
   MinClimbCellsForJumpPacks`, the jump-pack gate), for a faction Neolithic or better, a deterministic share of
   humanlike tool-users who need one (`RM_ShovelMath.GetsShovel(thingID, share)`, its own hash) get one shovel in
   inventory. Stuff: Steel from Industrial, else WoodLog. The share is `RM_ShovelMath.ShareFor(siege, sappers, assault,
   sapper)`: a siege (`RaidStrategyWorker_Siege`) 0; sappers (`RaidStrategyWorker_ImmediateAttackSappers`, defName
   `ImmediateAttackSappers`, read from Core `Storyteller/RaidStrategies_Sapper.xml`) "Sappers who bring a shovel"
   (default **50%**, PROVISIONAL); every other strategy "Raiders who bring a shovel" (default **25%**, PROVISIONAL).
   Dead raiders' shovels are loot.
b) **Only shovel-holders fill.** `RM_RaiderTrench.FillJob`, before `FetchJob`: a raider who needs a shovel and holds
   none takes any loose one within `FetchRadius` (forbidden binds only the colony) -> `TakeJob`; else null (no fill
   job: the raid jumps, ladders or waits exactly as with no fill material). The two raider fill drivers (`TrenchWork`
   fill branch, `FetchFill`) fail when the shovel is gone. Ladders and jumps need no shovel.
   `RM_RaiderTrenchMath.Choose` is unchanged: it already reads `fill != null`.
c) **Sappers bring ladders.** FlowWorks ladders are already carriable (minified `RM_LadderPortable`, handed out by
   `RM_Patch_Raid_LadderCarriers` in `Superdeep/RM_LadderParity.cs`). A sapper raid now always passes the
   "raid brings ladders" roll; the carrier share is unchanged. "A small number of folks with ladders anytime" is that
   existing roll on every other raid, and "ladders can just be made" is the existing build-on-spot path (raiders chop
   wood and build a ladder, `ChopForLadder` / `SpendLadderWood`). Nothing new owed for either.

## 11. Self-review (adopted into the sections above unless marked)

- R1 weapon vs tool: a weapon def brings pawnkind weapon generation, sidearm mods, pacifist equip bans and ideoligion
  precepts into a digging rule. Made it a plain tool item (§1).
- R2 scan cost: `FindShovelFor` per designated square would be a path search per square per scan; it is memoised
  per pawn per tick behind a coarse `AnyOnMap` check (§2).
- R3 one shovel, two diggers: the fetch job reserves the shovel, so the second pawn's search finds none; no deadlock. Known v1 limit (NOT fixed): a pawn keeps its shovel after its dig work is
  switched off, so it can hoard the colony's only one. The alert names who holds the shovels; a "drop the shovel when
  the work type is off" pass is filed for after the live run if it bites.
- R4 restarts: an interrupted fetch is simply re-offered by the next scan; a pawn holding a shovel is `Ready` and never
  fetches a second.
- R5 caravans: a returning caravan's `UnloadEverything` empties the pack; the pawn simply fetches again. Accepted.
- R6 all-off: `shovelRequired` off skips the raid postfix too (no shovels handed out for a rule that is off) and
  lifts the raider gate, so all-off is the pre-item game exactly (the scenario's one shovel aside).
- R8 raiders and forbidden: hostile pawns ignore forbidden, so a raid can walk off with the colony's shovels lying
  near the trench. Intended; store shovels indoors.
