# GPT top-ten review — design pass over Parts 2–4 (2026-10-08)

Parts 2 (likely future complications), 3 (unleveraged opportunities) and 4 (extensions beyond the
mod) of the ten GPT reports beside this file, read in full and checked against the source on disk.
Part 1 (debugging) is in `VERDICTS.md` and is not repeated here. Offline pass: no game run, no build.

**Rules of this pass.** A GPT idea is evidence, not a finding. An idea survives only if (a) the source
does not already do it, (b) no live or closed rimflow item already covers it, and (c) no owner ruling
rejects it (no worldgen feature, one home per animal, ship-only sea descent, one kind of heat, no
DLC-less fallback, no donor art, flyers fly). Every surviving idea owes Mod Settings toggles and
three-tier names (`RM_`/`RSW_`/`RUT_`).

**Columns.** Effort S = an afternoon, M = a few days, L = a week or more. Risk = what could go wrong
in play or in the code. "Checked" = what in the source was read to confirm it is not already there.

Ids are per mod (CB, DI, EH, FV, FL, GS, LP, SC, SS, TB), plus X for cross-mod ideas. Rows marked *(hygiene)* or *(robustness)* prevent a real future bug but add no play.

## Rejected, and why

About 120 GPT ideas were dropped. Most were dropped for one of four reasons:
- **Already built.** For example, pit falls from any arrival route, save/reload scenes in
  `playtest_runner.py`, the shared aerosol registry, the hose reuse of the cord kit, and the sunlight service.
- **Already filed.** Every "file →" item in `VERDICTS.md` exists in the ledger (81 of 82 ids
  re-measured; the 82nd, `DLL_SOURCE_STAMP_GUARD_1`, is a cited guard rather than a new item).
  Several helpers wrongly reported these as unfiled. The ideas they cover were not re-proposed.
- **Ruled out.** Ship-only sea access, one kind of heat, all DLCs present, a frozen hand-made world, and
  no donor art.
- **Speculative.** Thread safety in a single-threaded simulation, performance with no measured cost,
  versioned save migration before anything has shipped, and abstractions with no second consumer.
