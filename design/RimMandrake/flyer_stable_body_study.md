## 2. Sample judgements — eight body plans, looked at

Static E/S/N against flight frames 1–5 east. Metric, a quick cross-check (the Sketto pilot's numbers are the
authority): *core overlap* = pixels opaque in **every** frame ÷ mean frame area (a locked body stays opaque
across the beat, so this is high); *core drift* = mean RGB change on those pixels (repaint/relight = high).

| creature | plan | frames | core overlap E/S/N | core drift | verdict on the STATIC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RSW_Neebray (donor art) | membrane/ray | donor 4 | **0.61 / 0.78 / 0.87** | 14–19 | the construction exemplar: hull pixel-identical, only the wing shape swaps |
| RSW_Hawkbat (donor frames) | bat-wing | donor 4 | **0.72 / 0.55 / 0.61** | 10–21 | donor frames lock; **our painterly static is a DEAD END** — membranes spread across the torso in all three facings, there is no body under them to keep |
| RM_FireHawk | bird | ours 5 | 0.21 / 0.34 / 0.35 | 25–31 | frames 4–5 are a **different bird** (grey, orange lost); static N is a 1-px-wide sliver. Static E (perched, wings folded) IS convertible |
| RM_Krizzak | moth | ours 5 | 0.41 / 0.30 / 0.33 | 17–26 | wing count, body bulk and markings change per frame. Static (wings folded over back) convertible after a wings-off body pass |
| RUT_EmperorVulture | large bird | none | — | — | **best base in the sample**: perched, wings folded tight, torso/head/legs read clean in all facings |
| RM_Thozzik | insect | none | — | — | flies by stat, **no wings drawn in any facing** — the static cannot become a flyer; it needs wings designed first |
| RM_Blisterfloat | gas-bag floater | none | — | — | no wings by design; its flip-book is a bell pulse + tendril sway on a locked bell. Convertible, different recipe |

Donor exemplars score 0.55–0.87 core overlap with 10–21 drift; all three of our generated multi-frame sets score
0.21–0.49 with 17–45 drift. **That gap is the whole problem**, and it is a recipe problem, not a model problem: the
donor drew one body and swapped wings; we asked for N whole creatures.

**What makes a static convertible (from looking at these eight):**
1. **Wings folded or clear of the torso** in all three facings — the torso, head and legs must be visible as one
   unbroken shape (Vulture yes; Hawkbat no: membranes cover it).
2. **Facings are true facings** — east is a side view, north/south are back/front views at the same camera
   elevation as every vanilla flyer; never a dorsal top-down "east" (FireWasp).
3. **Wings are drawn at all** if the fiction flies (Thozzik fails).
4. **North and south read as a body**, not a sliver (FireHawk N fails).
5. **Wing roots are visible** where they leave the body, so a wing-edit has a seam to attach to.
6. **Flat even lighting, no cast shadow, no motion blur, no dramatic rim light** — any light baked from one side
   changes when wings move and is what shows up as core drift.
7. **Body centred with headroom** — the canvas must leave room for spread wings (≥ 30 % margin each side in
   south/north, above in east) so the frames do not have to shrink the body to fit.

## 3. Flyer-ready static gate, prompt templates, threshold, sheet column

### 3.1 The principle: lock the body by construction, not by asking

Every one of our drifting sets was made by prompting N whole creatures. The donor's sets hold because the artist drew
**one** body and changed only the wings. The recipes that do this, in the order to try them:

1. **Cut-out from a painted master** (`flyer_cutout.py`, one config per set in `flyer_cutout_sets/`, or
   `flier_layer_bake.py` for the hinge model): one master per facing, cut by reviewed polygons into a body plate and wing
   layers. No pixel is generated. This needs a master whose wings are readable, folded or spread.
2. **Generated wing layers** (`flyer_lock.py compose`, sections 3.3 and 3.4): for a static that draws no usable wing.
   A body master per facing, then wing-only transparent layers per pose, composited offline.
3. **Masked edit with the body re-pasted** (`flyer_lock.py lock`): when a wing must pass behind the body.

All three produce whole-body flip-book PNGs, the 1.6 mechanism. Nothing is a wing render-tree and nothing Spastic is
involved.

### 3.2 The gate, with measured values

**Flip-book acceptance is the exit code of `flyer_gate.py flipbook <Set>`,** which runs `flier_body_stabilize.py` on
every facing. All of these must hold, measured on the exported PNGs:

| test | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| exported body equality | byte-identical | replaces the study's guessed IoU and colour-drift bounds; the Sketto pilot's section 6 acceptance, plate 100 % identical |
| body localiser (ECC, features, parts) | LOCKED: at most 1 px and 0.5 deg; parts 1.5 px and 1 deg | `flier_frame_registration_audit_2026-10-20.md` |
| wing motion | FLAPS | Beldon lesson: identical copies pass a body-only gate |
| locked share (`flyer_lock.py check`, a recipe 3 frame set) | E 0.45, S 0.25, N 0.25 | Sketto pilot: donor Sketto 0.48 / 0.41 / 0.40; owner card 2026-10-09 set S/N at 0.25, back view measures 0.260 |

The identity check by eye stays: same individual in every frame, judged from stills.

**The static gate.** Calibrated 2026-11-01 on the 24 statics section 2 judged by eye (9 convertible, 3 partial, 12 not).
The numbers are alpha-only, measured per facing by `flyer_gate.static_metrics`.

| gate | measures | by-eye convertible | by-eye not | floor | what it can do |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 folded | body share of opaque area, S/N | 0.54–0.97 | 0.14–0.94 | at least 0.50 | catches Sketto 0.14, Vrisk 0.48 only. **It does not separate the classes**: a spread wing that is solid survives the opening as body |
| G2 true facings | N/S width over E width | 0.35–0.97 | 0.30–1.01 | at least 0.33 | catches slivers (Strill 0.30). Cannot see a dorsal "east" |
| G3 wings present | by hand, `NO_WINGS_DRAWN` | | | | Thozzik family, Strill |
| G4 headroom | body width over canvas, S/N | 0.40–0.80 | 0.16–0.82 | at most 0.85 | catches a body filling the canvas. The study's guessed 55 % width and 25 % top margin rejected 6 and 7 of the 9 convertibles and are gone |
| G5 flat light | by eye | | | | no automatic test |

So G1, G2 and G4 are **refuters**: a fail means a look is owed, a pass proves nothing. What settles a flyer is its
flip-book passing the gate.

### 3.3 Body-master prompt templates (static, per body plan)

Shared head, always: *"RimWorld creature sprite, painterly, the {label}: {canon or description look}. {FACING
view}. Even flat lighting, no cast shadow, no motion blur. Centred, the body uses at most half the canvas width,
generous empty transparent margin all round. Wings {PLAN clause}. Wing roots clearly visible where they join the
body. Torso, head and legs fully visible and unbroken."* The style source is **our canon brief / painterly house
style**; a donor frame is attached **only as a structure reference**, with the clause *"use the attached only for
pose and wing placement; ignore its flat cartoon style completely"* (owner: the donor's flyers are *"good to
study"* but *"extremely cartoonish, so we can't use them"*).

| plan | `{PLAN clause}` for the body master | wing layer poses |
|---|---|---|
| **bird** (FireHawk, Vulture, Skellick, Whisperbird, Convor…) | folded tight along the flanks, primaries crossed over the tail, not raised | up (tips above head), level (full span), down (tips below belly) |
| **bat / dragon membrane** (Hawkbat, Shyrack, Uvak, Soorrak…) | folded like a closed umbrella against the sides, finger-bones visible, membrane not covering the chest | up, level spread, down; membrane only, no body |
| **insect, 2 or 4 wings** (FireWasp, Krizzak, Sketto, Dustflutter, Thozzik…) | folded flat over the back along the abdomen (wasp/fly) or tented roof-like (moth); legs and segments fully visible | E: forewing up / level / down plus a translucent blur variant; N/S: wings as narrow strokes either side |
| **ray / kite membrane** (Neebray, Skinflap, Vrisk) | body = the spine and head drawn as a clear central ridge; membrane relaxed flat | the membrane's edges curl up / flat / curl down; spine pinned |
| **floater, no wings** (Blisterfloat, Dwommo) | bell relaxed, tendrils hanging | bell contracted / relaxed, tendrils swept (a body-pulse set: pin the central organs, not the bell rim) |
| **swarm** (FeverSwarm) | the cloud at rest | three churn states of the same cloud; no body to pin, so G1 is waived |
| **large** (Vulture, Uvak, Convor) | as bird or bat, and the body master ≤ 45 % canvas width, because wing span multiplies drawSize | same 3 poses; `flyingAnimationDrawSizeIsMultiplier` covers the span |

### 3.4 Wing-layer prompt template (one job per pose per facing)

*"Attached: the accepted body master of the {label}, {facing} view. Draw ONLY its wings, in the {up|level|down}
position of a wingbeat, attached exactly at the visible wing roots of the attached body, at the same scale,
palette, edge treatment and light. Output the wings alone on a fully transparent canvas the same size as the
attached image: do not redraw, move or include any part of the body. {PLAN wing clause}."* `flyer_lock.py compose`
then layers them.

### 3.5 Review-sheet column

Every flyer's row on a sheet shows a `flyer:` flag beside its other marks (`flyer_gate.cell_for`, read from the
committed plan): `flyer: convertible ✓`, `flyer: partial: <why>` or `flyer: ✗ <G why>`. A keep of a `✗` row leaves a
todo in `art.py enact`, so a good-looking dead end is never settled silently; `--mark-done <row>` says the keep stands.

## 4. Regeneration order and job counts

Unit cost per flyer per sex/life-stage variant: **3** body-master jobs (E + derived S, N; skip for a kept
convertible static) + **9** wing-layer jobs (3 poses × 3 facings) = **12 jobs**; compositing is free and offline.
A per-frame prompted flip-book costs 4 × 3 = 12 jobs with no lock, so the recipe costs nothing extra.

Tiers, as first planned, are the shape of `design/RimMandrake/flyer_regen_plan.json`, which `flyer_gate.py refresh` rebuilds:

| tier | what | item |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | latest ruling is redo, reject or replace-donor: gate the new static before he sees it | `FLYER_TIER_ONE_REBUILD_REDO_ROWS_FIRST` |
| 2 | kept but not convertible or partial: a cut-out or body pass that keeps his ruled look | `FLYER_TIER_TWO_REBUILD_KEPT_NEW_WINGS` |
| 3 | kept and convertible: wings only | `FLYER_TIER_THREE_REBUILD_EASY_WINGS_ONLY` |
| 4 | the rest, gated at their biome sitting | `FLYER_TIER_FOUR_REBUILD_UNRULED_AT_SITTING` |

Held sets (`FLIER_FRAMES_AUDIT_BODY_NOT_WING_EXTREMES`) stay held until the owner has seen the line overlays. Female
frames: *"Only if the females and males are very different looking."* Male frames serve both sexes by default.

### Tooling

- `flyer_census.py`: who flies, from defs and descriptions, with a sanity probe.
- `flyer_gate.py`: `static`, `flipbook`, `cell`, `refresh`, `audit`. `flipbook_compose.py` was not built: `flyer_cutout.py`
  and `flyer_lock.py compose` composite, and `flyer_gate.py flipbook` is the gate.
- `fill_queue.py`: a flight-frame row (`_Flying_<n>` in id, target or install path) is refused without `plan` and
  `derive_from`, or a `flipbook_override` naming why.
- `art.py enact` and the sheets: section 3.5.
