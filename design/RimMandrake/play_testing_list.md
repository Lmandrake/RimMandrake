This list is no part of any Design Version's passing. No script, no scene, no bar and no walk of the owner decides a line of
it. Nothing here is owed. No line has a ticket. A line is an idea of what to notice when somebody plays the mod for a long
time, after its version passed.

The rule: `design/RimMandrake/standing_rulings.md` §Season-long play is play testing.
