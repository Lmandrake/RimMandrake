# The experience, in the owner's words: Baroque Biomes (2026-10-20)

Collected at one sitting by question card, one card per biome. Each card showed the design note, the built mechanics and the life, plus three drafted two-sentence options; the owner typed his own or picked one. Each line is also written into its biome's `## north star` section in `design/validation_walks/RimMandrake/`. Abyss and the Lantern Deeps have no walk file yet; this file is their home until they do. Typo fixes only ("tain" to "rain", a doubled "twists").
