# Declined ship ideas in the biome designs: typed refusals and silent passes (recount, 2026-11-19)

Item `RIMMANDRAKE_APPLY_83_BIOME_ANSWERS_FOUR_CHANGE_A_DEFAULT`. This recount is for the declined-ship-ideas question
in the Conductor's sort of 2026-11-19. That sort counted *"about six"* ideas he refused in typed words and *"about
fourteen"* he passed over on a card with no words, and marked the split as a guess to re-count. The recount reads
the 20 `NEEDS HIM` entries in the 18 designs that the question settles, and the source each entry cites.

## The result

The draft split is wrong, by entry and by idea.

- **By entry (20):** 4 hold only typed refusals, 11 hold only silent passes, and 5 hold both.
- **By idea (41):** 11 he refused in his typed words. 1 he replaced in his own words. 29 were passed over with no
  words of his. Two of the 29 were cut by an agent under his card *"I cut, you veto"*, not by him. One (the Miasma's
  plate) sits inside an entry the Conductor answered.

So a second look at the silent passes covers 29 ideas, not about 14.

## Limits of this count

Each row rests on the design's own entry and on the source it cites. Where a design says "not chosen" and quotes no
words, the row reads silent. A typed refusal could exist in a source the design did not cite; that would move a row
from the silent table to the typed one. The counts are by idea as the designs name them; two designs group ideas
differently (the Slime's three, the Warscar's three), which moves the total but not the split.
