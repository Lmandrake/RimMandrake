# Contagion

Your recorded experience sentence: “A mutation engine under a red lid, where nothing is finished, including you. When the sky clears, run.”

Everything below is an agent proposal, including the guesses.

**Proposed root**

The Contagion keeps making unfinished life, and visiting it can leave your colonists changing too. During the Bloom, red cloud and fog cover the landscape. Sinking Gawpsacks and shaking Rattlegropes warn that the Burn is coming: the sky clears, dangerous sunlight reaches the ground, and creatures vulnerable to ultraviolet light retreat while armored predators remain outside. You must reach shelter or risk a brief opportunity in the clearer air. Exposure can cause lasting mutations. Sunbeam treatment stops further changes from the current exposure, but leaves existing mutations and gives no protection next time. There are reasons to venture back: a Bloody Mess consumes itself growing donor-matched natural organs and limbs; scanning short-lived creatures called Unfinished fulfills Helix contracts, jobs requesting those scans; and a growing mass called a Coalescence yields unusual tissue when the Burn collapses it. Chosen limb implants bring useful abilities with lasting costs, and removing one releases an Unfinished. Flesh-like plants, flying skin creatures and traces left by death make this a place of ongoing change. There is no forage; sterile mulch provides ordinary fertilizer.

**Claim:** Changing weather and lasting bodily costs make every expedition a bargain.

**Concern:** Those promises could still feel like unrelated hazards and rewards unless the journey home and its consequences matter.

**1. What the experience needs**

**Written picture:** A colonist returns through the red fog with useful tissue and a completed scan, then starts changing. Later, the sky clears and ordinary colony work becomes a rush for shelter.

**Question:** What feels wrong or missing in that story and the proposed root, given your experience sentence?

**Why this matters:** The reason to enter and the fear of staying need to belong to the same experience.

**Agent's guess:** Weather and bodily consequences should lead; valuable expeditions give the player reasons to accept them.

**2. What clearing skies let you attempt**

**Written picture:** A prepared traveller has a roofed refuge on each side of a clearing. Bloom fog and a predator obstruct the crossing. The Burn lifts the fog and drives some creatures into shelter, but armored Scaldhides remain and the traveller risks injury and mutation.

**Question:** What should that prepared traveller be able to accomplish between refuges before the sunlight becomes too costly?

**Why this matters:** A useful travel opportunity must still honor “When the sky clears, run.”

**Agent's guess:** One purposeful dash or brief rescue should be possible at a cost; prolonged outdoor work should remain dangerous.

**3. A worthwhile return trip**

**Written picture:** A colonist needs a natural leg. The team injects that colonist's genome into a Bloody Mess and returns four days later. The host dies producing a random batch of matched organs and limbs, but no leg.

**Question:** What would need to come home from that trip for it to feel like progress worth another expedition?

**Why this matters:** Waiting and returning must offer a worthwhile bargain even when the desired part does not grow.

**Agent's guess:** Other useful or sellable matched tissue can justify the trip if the uncertain outcome is clear before committing.

**Recommended demonstration order:** Weather and treatment first; then organ recovery and surgery, a Helix scan job, and the Coalescence's growing threat and collapse.
