# Abyss: owner answers (by question card, Tue 2026-11-10 ~08:34 PM PST)

1. **When shelter turns frightening:** Wonder draws you out: beauty offers something worth approaching, then leaves you exposed when darkness returns. Shelter remains worth choosing, while wonder tempts the crew beyond it.
2. **Keeping a hidden colony working:** Searches interrupt work: ordinary work is safe between searches, but exposed activity becomes dangerous while a search probe passes.
3. **Why staying becomes frightening:** Danger becomes more frequent: close threats and frightening reversals gradually leave fewer peaceful stretches, giving a practical reason to depart at the cost of breathing room.
