# TheForge: owner answers (by question card, Tue 2026-11-10 ~10:06 PM PST)

1. **Where the ruined works are:** Old works shape ordinary journeys: recognisable manufactured remains affect routes or resource trips on ordinary maps, with campaign sites deepening the experience.
2. **What warnings give time to do:** Recall before gas; orderly retreat before melting. Prompt action protects attentive crews, but loads may be abandoned and delayed rescues remain risky.
3. **What physically stops a steam front:** Walls stop the immediate front; roofs alone offer little protection from sideways steam. Enclosed rooms protect against direct contact while ordinary room temperature still matters. Asked and answered as a shared physical rule wherever this hazard occurs, not special colonist behaviour.
