# TheRot: owner answers (by question card, Tue 2026-11-10 ~10:11 PM PST)

1. **What connects generosity and hunger:** Both comfort and danger come from the same life, and generosity draws the crew toward dangerous places. Useful warmth and medicine make involvement worthwhile while rich resources tempt expeditions into defended groves.
2. **What remains to manage after preparation works:** Very little until an expedition. Understanding the Rot and arranging the base well earns peaceful ordinary days and dependable comfort between outings; its hungry character can concentrate in exceptional events.
3. **How late rescue remains possible:** A prompt, committed response can still succeed. Immediate action offers a real rescue opportunity, requiring diversion of people; delay worsens the outcome.
