PROPOSED ROOT

BlueDesert invites a colony into a beautiful, almost empty expanse whose ancient ice preserves things worth uncovering and things better left asleep. Its blue distance gives way to clear, fragile hydrocarbon life underfoot. Quarrying, following the animals to a slowly surfacing find, and reading the remains of earlier expeditions should make discovery feel tempting and dangerous: careful work and knowledge let the colony take something precious home, while careless warmth can turn the living field and its cargo into an explosive chain. The danger travels with harvested fuel and native produce, so keeping a hold cold matters as much as finding its riches. Storms change the routes and hide ambushers; the great walking cistern creates a different decision from an ordinary hunt. The common biome supplies the cold ecology, resources, weather and salvage; the campaign supplies its particular ancient peoples and weapon history. The reward is a discovery the player chose to risk, with enough warning and control to understand what went wrong.
