# Greentide: owner answers (by question card, Tue 2026-11-10 ~09:26 PM PST)

1. **What leads the daily struggle:** Defend an inhabited foothold: maintaining access to a rich home is the central bargain, with daily work clearing routes and protecting entrances.
2. **What neglect costs:** Recovery first, abandonment after prolonged neglect: warnings allow intervention, late response costs clearing and hauling, and continued neglect can force evacuation with supplies left behind. Reaching a stranded person should remain practical.
3. **When a rescue is complete:** On nearby firm ground: extraction delivers a usable escape for victim and helper. Asked and answered as a general rule for rescues from trapping terrain wherever it occurs, rather than special Greentide colonist behaviour.
