# Propane Lakes / the Chill shore: owner answers (by question card, Tue 2026-11-10 ~10:36 PM PST)

1. **What a successful refuelling stop feels like:** A dangerous place worth exploring: refuelling is one reason among several to visit, alongside wildlife, strange resources and discoveries. The shore's experience extends beyond its pumping operation.
2. **What chance remains when the lake creature approaches:** Stop pumping and withdraw. Noticing its approach offers a useful withdrawal window for the crew, while exposed equipment and pipe remain at risk.
3. **What makes a gas emergency recoverable:** People first, equipment uncertain. Evacuation remains practical; shutting the valve is dangerous work, and parts of the operation may have to be abandoned.
