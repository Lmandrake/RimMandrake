# WeepingStones

Your chosen experience sentence:
> Bright dry stone and secret weeping shade, where every pool is a gathering. Keep the oasis healthy, or it breeds something wrong.

Everything below is my proposal, including the pictures and guesses.

## Proposed root

WeepingStones is a place of bright, dry stone and hidden wet shade, where life gathers around pools. Predators leave drinkers alone at the water; attacking there turns nearby wildlife against the attacker's faction. Farmers catch breeders, stock and feed their pools, watch their health and decide what to harvest. Neglect and crowding can breed a dangerous vhorrin, making rescue and a later feast part of the same responsibility. Some delicious catches also trouble the people who eat them. Alien plants, wildlife and useful crops make the oasis worth living in and trading with. One old giant crab carries a working water condenser and a seasonal oasis. The colony can guide it, sell its next settling place, or kill it to take the machine onto a ship. Two optional quests put captivity and money against protecting its freedom. Ordinary pools and their keepers remain the heart of the place.

Pool health here means the condition of its living stock; this proposal does not add a water supply that grows and dries away.

## 1. Leaving the farm to its keepers

**Picture (written):** In a forty-person colony, handlers tend three pens while you build elsewhere. Current routines can stock the nearest pen and harvest any eligible resident, including a breeder you needed to keep.

**Question:** What decisions should you set once for each pen, and what trouble should require your return?

**Why this matters:** Routine work must sustain the farm without constant orders or accidentally eating its breeding stock.

**My guess:** Set the species mix and breeding reserve once; return for worsening health, escapes or a deliberate large harvest.

## 2. A welcome pool beside a garden

**Picture (written; mock needed before choosing placement controls):** The crab is ready to settle near your workshop, planted garden and open stone. Its current guidance chooses somewhere in the home area. Pool growth spares buildings and built floors but can change ordinary soil. No placement mock is supplied here.

**Question:** What land must a guided pool leave untouched, even if that means the crab cannot settle where you hoped?

**Why this matters:** Welcoming the oasis should not silently commit a working garden to becoming water.

**My guess:** Preserve planted ground as well as buildings and floors, and explain when no suitable settling place remains. The controls still need a labelled mock before a choice.

## 3. Shelter or captivity?

**Picture (written):** You protect the crab behind walls and defeat both hunter waves. The protection period ends with it alive, but still enclosed.

**Question:** When does temporary shelter become confinement, and what release must happen before the farmers count their promise fulfilled?

**Why this matters:** Protecting its freedom requires more than keeping it alive until time runs out.

**My guess:** Shelter is acceptable during danger; afterward it needs an open, usable way out and the farmers must receive their season at its water.

Recommended demonstration order: farm health, trouble and recovery; the shared-water truce; then the walking oasis and its bargains.
