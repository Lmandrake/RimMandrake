# TheSump: owner answers (by question card, Tue 2026-11-10 ~10:40 AM PST)

1. **A colony that knows the place:** Routine is automatic: workers clean and retrieve food from tar on their own; he plans routes, chooses dig sites and spends scarce solvent. The colony stays dirty.
2. **Time to react to a trap:** Nearby miner can make it: time to clear workers and to fetch a qualified miner from a nearby site; a distant one may not arrive in time.
3. **What evacuation saves:** People and chosen goods: everyone escapes and he picks which valuable stores to carry; buildings are lost.
