PROPOSED ROOT

FeverWood makes a home in the treetops depend on respecting the black water below. Living wood carries the colony's roads and growing ground; the marsh refuses heavy buildings unless the colony invests in stilts. The pools are openings onto one vast sekkulaath: its different tentacles threaten, watch and lay out treasure, and what happens at the water has lasting consequences. The colony learns when to approach, when to rescue someone, and when to fight for respite or a lasting victory over its own pools. Above them, alien animals make a living crown, and keeping the nectar herd calm matters to the colony's livelihood. Living bait can draw two enemies into a staggered fight; the kurreth hive offers a separate expedition into an enemy that notices intrusion and closes the way back. Imprisoning the deep's young buys useful products and risks an escape; returning them buys a gift. On hot days, doubled oil harvest tempts workers into haze that a spark can ignite. Ordinary play shows the creature through its limbs; the whole body's rising belongs to the campaign's separate plot.

Basis — the owner's chosen experience, reproduced from `design/RimMandrake/biome_experiences_2026-10-20.md:12` (picked by card, 2026-10-20): “A world in the treetops above a black mirror nobody touches. You live on the branches, and the water remembers.” That is the existing sourced quotation; the paragraph above is mine, not an extension of his words.

My claim: the water changes the decisions of a colony living above it, even on an ordinary working day.
The strongest objection: this could still become a collection of encounters beside an ordinary swamp base. In particular, terrain conveys height here; there are no separate floors or climbing gates. The root proposes an experience, not a claim that the present code has passed it.

Eight draft questions follow. **Ask 01 and 02 first.** They are a bank; serve at most three in a turn after independent review. A phrase, a different story, or “this doesn't matter to me” is enough. Silence changes nothing. Existing capabilities remain covered whichever scene leads.
