PROPOSED ROOT

LanternDeeps takes an expedition below the cold nightside into a persistent, wondrous cavern where mineral wealth turns out to be living company. Blue lanternstone and fungal pastures light galleries full of alien life, valuable stone and the excellent equipment of people who never came back. Your lamps make work possible and advertise your presence; living Lantern crystals offer safe light, at the cost of leaving their harvest standing. Crystal crust grows toward sleepers, fragments fight and multiply, and a roof gives dust and a grumble before it falls. Among the remains, dead machines stand near aware crystals and turn toward the light. Orun-Ghal, a skeleton still inside its walking mining suit, can be studied and befriended over repeated visits; it is an inhabitant, never a miner or a boss. The discovery should change how you regard what you came to extract: the riches and the haunted technology are alive and intelligent. The free mod supplies the cavern and its inhabitants; campaign companions add their particular rare minerals and mindstone uses. Returning means returning to the same place, carrying the consequences of what you did there.

The anchor is the owner's chosen experience, transcribed from `design/RimMandrake/biome_experiences_2026-10-20.md:16`: “A deep wondrous cavern of strange crystal life and haunted technology. Mineral riches for kings, until you realize they are both alive and intelligent.” That sentence is his; the proposed paragraph above is mine. It has no root approval.

My claim: the important turn is from finding valuable things to recognising inhabitants.
The strongest objection: the build can deliver attractive minerals, reactive animals and explanatory letters without making that recognition matter in play. The root proposes the experience; it does not certify that it already succeeds.

These nine questions are a bank. Start with **01 and 02**, then serve at most three at a time after independent review. The pictures are written thought experiments, not captures, visual mockups or staged acceptance scenes. A different picture or “this doesn't matter to me” is a useful answer. The guesses below are recommendations, never assumed replies. Existing capabilities are not being offered for removal.
