  audit note about the art installed then. It is not the judge's refusal. The round-3 refusals are in

  RM_Gembug, RM_Gloomcast, RM_IllothLarvae, RM_Wuum, RSW_FrilledGorg_j and others). Those sets are "leave".
- The batch's size matching shrank some refused back views to a fraction of the front view: the four Dragonsnake
  variants, RM_Iliss and RM_Irqit. The judge refused them for the size, not for the drawing.

- Two of our mods ship `Things/Pawn/Animal/SeaBeasts/CrimsonOpee/CrimsonOpee` with different front and back
  pictures (SWBestiary holds his kept ones, TerminalBiomes others).
- Set 42 (RSW_SmogMothLarvae) names a def that the live game does not have. It uses the texture of set 10, and has no
  redraw and no verdict.
