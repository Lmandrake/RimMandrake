## False leads, kept so nobody repeats them

- **345 `return`/`continue` inside a component** looked like silent passes. The idiom sets `upstream_failed` first, and
  84 unguarded ones were read: all exit after `_unmeasured`, in a dry walk (`session is None`), or on the passing path.
- **Field names against C#** (`.get("x")` with no matching identifier in any `.cs`): 163 keys, nearly all local dicts or
  fields reflected from vanilla. No yield; a per-tool reply schema would be needed.
- **GPT: a clock that truncates in `Trial.s_wait`** is real but untouched: only `northstar_driver/selftest.py` declares a
  bar this way; live rows run on the Suite, whose `wait_ticks` already checks.
- **Empty component (GPT)** cannot be refused at runtime, because a legitimate offline row records no bridge call; the
  lint gate of A11 holds it.
