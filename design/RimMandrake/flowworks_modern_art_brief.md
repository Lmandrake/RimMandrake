## What was wrong

The shipped Modern tanks, reels and pump (`Textures/RimMandrake/FlowWorks/{Tank,Hose/Styles,Pump}/Modern/`) are
the Scrapper/Industrial silhouettes — riveted steel drums on skids, a cast-iron crank reel, a four-flange pump —
repainted glossy red with chrome trim. Same object, new paint. A modern product differs in **form and material**,
not colour.

## Judge lines for this subject (M = modern)

- **M1. Product class, not palette.** Each Modern picture is recognisably one real product class listed below (a
  poly storage tank, an IBC tote, a resin hose-reel cart …). A picture whose silhouette is the Scrapper/Industrial
  object recoloured is REDO. — Owner 2026-10-22 (quoted above)
- **M2. Modern materials read at play zoom.** Rotomoulded polyethylene (soft rounded edges, satin sheen, moulded
  ribs/bands, no rivets, no bolted flanges), galvanised or powder-coated steel, stainless, injection-moulded resin
  housings. Rivets, weld seams, rust, hazard stripes and bolted end-caps belong to the other looks. — Owner
  2026-10-22; real products below
- **M3. Real product colours.** Real modern tanks and reels come in black, dark green, natural/translucent white,
  beige/taupe, slate grey, light blue, and (pressure tanks) a deep enamel blue. Primary red is not banned (B5) but
  a candidate whose only "modern" signal is a saturated primary is REDO under M1. — Owner 2026-10-22
- **M4. Small honest details.** Moulded-in level/gallon markings, a threaded screw lid, a brass or plastic bulkhead
  outlet, tie-down slots or strap hoops, a printed label patch (no readable brand, no real logo), a hose guide.
  Detail must survive 32 px/cell as shapes (a lid ellipse, a dark band, a label rectangle), not as noise. — B2
- Everything else is the standing brief: the measured building camera (C3; round tops an ellipse ≈0.56), the
  current def's canvas and footprint (D2/D3), real alpha (D4), no painted outline for the FlowWorks family
  (`building_camera_perspective_2026-10-14.md`, outline row), light overhead-front.

### RM_LiquidTank — Modern (2×2, drawSize 2×2, 512×512, Graphic_Single; today a horizontal drum)
A modern horizontal **leg tank** (refs 8, 9): one seamless rotomoulded polyethylene body, a rounded-rectangle /
elliptical cross-section lying lengthwise east–west, moulded-in feet along its base, two flat moulded hoop bands
where hold-down straps sit (galvanised straps), a large threaded screw lid on the top centre with a smaller vent, a
bulkhead outlet with a short plastic ball valve at one low end, a moulded level scale. Colour options: natural
translucent white with a darker water line showing through; heavy-duty light blue; black. Alternative class: a
**blue steel pressure tank** lying on a cradle (ref 10) — smooth enamel, domed ends with no rivets, a stainless
fitting cluster with a small gauge.

### RM_LiquidTankTall — Modern (2×2 footprint, drawSize 2×3 +0.5 north, 512×768)
A modern **vertical poly water storage tank** (refs 1–4): a tall cylinder of rotomoulded polyethylene, gently domed
top read as a 0.56 ellipse with a large threaded lid set off-centre, faint horizontal moulded bands, moulded gallon
graduation ticks down the front, tie-down slots near the top shoulder, a bulkhead outlet with a ball valve at the
base. Colour options: black, dark green, natural white (translucent, water line visible). Alternative class: a
**slimline rain tank** (refs 5, 6) — a tall, flat-fronted rounded box with vertical ribs, in a roof colour (slate
grey, beige, deep green), a mesh inlet strainer on top and a brass tap low on the front.

### RM_UniversalCargoTank — Modern (2×2, drawSize 2×2, 512×512)
An **IBC tote** (ref 7): a translucent white HDPE cube bottle inside a galvanised steel tube-grid cage, standing on a
black plastic or galvanised pallet base, a big screw cap on top, a grey butterfly-valve outlet low on the front, a
small white label plate on the cage. This is the most instantly recognisable modern liquid container behind any
store. Alternative: a squat vertical poly tank (as the tall, shorter) or a horizontal leg tank variant in black.

## Fire hose reels — owner, 2026-10-22, on the reel options card: *"Try looking up firehose reel"*

None of the three garden/commercial reels was picked; the Modern reel is to be a **fire hose reel**. Real modern classes
(sources below):
- **Swinging fire hose reel (EN 671-1)** — a red pressed-steel drum on a swing-arm wall/pedestal bracket that pivots
  out, red semi-rigid hose (EN 694), a brass or chrome manual stop valve (or automatic valve in the hub), a red/chrome
  jet-spray nozzle clipped to a holder, hose guide rollers. Always red (refs F1–F5).
- **Hose reel cabinet** — the same red reel housed in a galvanised, red-painted or brushed stainless cabinet with a
  solid or clear/wired-glass door, surface or recessed mounted (refs F6–F8).
- **Booster reel / fire cart reel** — fire-apparatus booster reel (Hannay F series: steel, graphite or red, bevel-gear
  manual rewind crank, 90° ball-bearing swivel inlet, red rubber booster hose) or a red powder-coated hose cart
  (refs F9–F12).
Judge: M1 applies (a fire reel, not a red-painted garden reel); red is the product colour here and passes M3.
