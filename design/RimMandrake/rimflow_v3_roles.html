RimMandrake Command Structure

Proposed roles around Foundry. Bench is folded into the Conductor. You talk to one agent, the Conductor. Foundry builds around the clock; the Conductor builds with you when you sit down. Everyone else judges, plans, watches or dispatches.

Three competing shapes

Five designs went in (Rounds, COO, Producer, Board, Inspector General). Both GPT teardowns killed the two that only watch (Rounds and Board): they show the problem and leave you as the one who makes things happen. The arbiter's three survivors differ in which role leads. All three share the Conductor (which absorbs Bench) and the Dispatcher. The full role map further down is all of them at once: the most capable and the most to build.

1 · Run it

Lead: Operations, including the economy pass. Light: Auditor, weekly. Later: Producer, Arbiter, Innovator.

Moves: your "go" messages (31/day → ≤26), test hours that catch nothing. Weak at: direction and design quality. Build: smallest.

2 · Build the right things

Lead: Producer plus Arbiter: the plan, and bars decided before building. Light: Operations. Later: Auditor, Innovator.

Moves: "how is it going?" answered at a glance, a real "done" for focus mods. Weak at: day-to-day running, learning from repeats. Build: medium.

3 · Stop repeating mistakes

Lead: Auditor (one recurring failure at a time) plus Innovator (experiments). Light: Operations. Later: Producer, Arbiter.

Moves: the 27.5% of your messages that signal a failure, repeat rulings. Weak at: planning delivery. Build: medium.

GPT arbiter's recommendation: shape 1 first, then add shape 2's planning discipline. Our recommendation: grow into the full map in that order: 1, then 2, then 3. Add each role only when its number shows the need, so no role exists as ceremony.

Who decides when each one runs

No AI decides when to wake itself. The Dispatcher runs every role from one schedule file, on three kinds of trigger, inside the token budget. Agents can request a run by filing a ticket; the Dispatcher queues it.

Producer

Owns what and when: turns the Conductor's goals into the plan and tickets: the critical path, the next player experiences to finish, each with its test scenarios written before building. When Operations' forecast misses, drafts the replan (a cut list) that the Conductor brings to you. Wants progress, so it never grades its own plan's work. Keeps the idea bank.

Who builds: Foundry, always; the Conductor, in session mode with you. Everyone else judges, plans, watches or dispatches.

Who has helpers: Foundry and the Conductor in session (builders), the Arbiter and the Auditor (independent checkers), the Innovator (experiments), Operations (small fixes). The Producer and Dispatcher have none.
