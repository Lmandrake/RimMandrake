**O1 is justified; “vanilla-equivalent” and the proposed trust proof are not.** The useful deliverable is a qualified fast runner with measured limits. I reviewed the inline design and public sources; exact 1.6 build-specific behavior still needs verification against the shipped assembly.

1. **[Critical] Delete “N ≤ 30 is vanilla-equivalent apart from F7.”** A maximum burst size does not establish equivalence. Update order, pause state, maintenance frequency, camera state, RNG consumption and asynchronous jobs also matter. All four equivalence arms are paused steppers: they can agree while sharing a defect relative to normal play. Add an unpaused native-loop reference.

2. **[High] §4 confuses vanilla’s tick target with its per-frame ceiling.** Given the quoted multipliers, Ultrafast targets **900 TPS**, boosted Ultrafast **9,000 TPS**; 30/300 ticks per frame are catch-up ceilings, not sustained targets. The accumulator constrains execution too. “At most 1,800 TPS at 60 FPS” is a loose bound that obscures the meaningful 900-TPS limit. [TickManager implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RimWorld-zh/RimWorld-Decompile/master/Assembly-CSharp/Verse/TickManager.cs), [DPA explanation](https://github.com/Dubwise56/Dubs-Performance-Analyzer/blob/develop/README.md).

3. **[High] M3 and M4 may accidentally be identical.** Current RimBridgeServer documentation says it enables `UltraSpeedBoost` at startup and exposes `set_time_speed(..., ultraSpeedBoost=...)`. Explicitly set, read back and restore the flag for each arm. Also record forced-normal-speed state and effective multiplier. This already answers the public bridge’s “is it settable?” question; verify the installed bridge version. [Bridge reference](https://github.com/pardeike/RimBridgeServer/blob/main/docs/tool-reference.md#rimworldset_time_speed).

4. **[Medium] The dev-tool comparison remains incomplete.** Tick-once already establishes paused direct ticking as a debugging primitive. Include the requested dev “tick N” actions in the census and inspect their **actual bodies**: looping `DoSingleTick`, scheduling future ticks and changing `ticksGameInt` have different semantics. I could not independently verify those actions’ exact 1.6 labels/bodies, so their counts should remain unclaimed. A debug action’s existence is precedent, not a correctness guarantee.

5. **[Medium] Separate the named mods by what they actually demonstrate.** [Smart Speed](https://github.com/juanosarg/Smart-Speed/releases) exposes/configures speed controls; [Time Control](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2109225757) changes relative simulation rates/day length; [RimThreaded](https://github.com/cseelhoff/RimThreaded) parallelizes work; [Performance Fish](https://github.com/bbradson/Performance-Fish) patches efficiency/caching; [DPA](https://github.com/Dubwise56/Dubs-Performance-Analyzer/blob/develop/README.md) profiles execution. None establishes arbitrary paused-burst equivalence. Pin compatible versions/forks rather than treating these as interchangeable benchmark configurations.

6. **[High — F4] Sky updates are simulation-relevant.** Multiplayer explicitly replaces cached sky glow with the current target before ticking. Therefore “sky colour interpolation only” is indefensible without tracing `CurSkyGlow` consumers in the target build. Test dawn/dusk, an eclipse and artificial lighting separately. Weather ambience may be cosmetic; that does not make the entire row cosmetic. [Multiplayer simulation context](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rwmt/Multiplayer/master/Source/Client/AsyncTime/AsyncTimeComp.cs).

7. **[High — F1–F3] These effects are broader than delayed activation.** Stale power topology can preserve an obsolete connection as well as delay a new one. Light changes can remain incorrectly bright or dark. Lazy region rebuilding does not prove every room/temperature consumer forces a rebuild. **`RegionGrid.UpdateClean` is missing** from both the table and O4; Multiplayer includes it alongside the other managers. Trace invalidation, consumption and job completion separately. [Manager handling](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rwmt/Multiplayer/master/Source/Client/AsyncTime/AsyncTimePatches.cs).

8. **[High — F5] There is a concrete world-simulation omission.** Multiplayer identifies `CompDissolutionEffect_Goodwill.WorldUpdate` and `CompDissolutionEffect_Pollution.WorldUpdate` as changing relations/retaliation and world pollution. F5 cannot remain a miscellaneous unread bucket underneath an equivalence claim. Audit all world/map/game component subclasses, including DLC and dependencies—not just your own source tree. [Determinism patches](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rwmt/Multiplayer/master/Source/Client/Patches/Determinism.cs).

9. **[High — F6/F7] The property grep does not support “vanilla sim unaffected.”** Searching `TickRateMultiplier` misses direct `Paused`, `RealTime`, frame-counter and cached-property readers. A historical concrete example is `Pawn_JobTracker.StartJob`: its job-loop accounting branches on `Paused` and `lastJobGivenAtFrame == RealTime.frameCount`. Verify whether that guard survives in your 1.6 assembly; test job churn/error recovery specifically. [Historical implementation](https://github.com/josh-m/RW-Decompile/blob/master/Verse.AI/Pawn_JobTracker.cs).

10. **[High — missing row] RimWorld 1.6’s variable tick rates need explicit coverage.** Multiplayer synchronizes `GenTicks.GetCameraUpdateRate`, projectile update rates and world-object update rates. Camera/view state therefore belongs in fixture provenance and the test matrix. “Full simulation” means invoking vanilla scheduling, which may itself batch or defer work; it does not mean every subsystem advances identically each tick. [VTR patches](https://github.com/rwmt/Multiplayer/blob/master/Source/Client/Patches/VTRSyncPatch.cs).

11. **[High — missing row] Background jobs are another clock domain.** Multiplayer explicitly completes scheduled pathfinder jobs before pawn ticking. Bursting changes worker scheduling opportunities and contention even when job boundaries nominally remain tick-based. Audit glow/path jobs, completion barriers and worker-visible mutable state. This is not merely a per-frame-hook problem. [Active completion barrier](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rwmt/Multiplayer/master/Source/Client/AsyncTime/AsyncTimeComp.cs).

12. **[High — F12/F13] “State reads are fine” and “cosmetic” are overclaims.** Multiplayer substitutes root positions during simulation instead of allowing tweened positions through. Audit `DrawPos`/tweener consumers, visual callbacks, sound callbacks and their RNG use. A cosmetic operation can perturb gameplay through shared RNG or mutable caches. Also, settling screenshots at Normal advances the simulation: use a paused presentation barrier or an explicitly recorded extra-tick phase. [Position patch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rwmt/Multiplayer/master/Source/Client/Patches/Determinism.cs).

13. **[High — F8/F9] Stop requests must be checked after each tick.** A 60-tick latch permits 59 further ticks after the triggering tick; frame-start-only long-event checks can permit much more across subsequent chunks. Distinguish the runner’s intentional pause from a newly requested pause—calling `Pause()` while already paused may leave no detectable transition. Define policies for letters, dialogs, queued events, saves and map changes. Autosaves also run serialization callbacks; their effects cannot be dismissed universally.

14. **[High — F10/O3] Adaptive chunks based on the previous mean do not bound overshoot to one tick.** A formerly cheap chunk can suddenly contain many expensive ticks. Check the deadline and cancellation between **every** `DoSingleTick`; then scheduling overshoot is at most the current non-preemptible tick plus checking overhead. The existing chunk scheme is approximately budget **plus an unchecked chunk**, not simply “60 × worst tick.”

15. **[Medium — F11] The GC measurements need runtime qualification.** Unity’s documented Boehm collector is non-generational; `GC.CollectionCount(0..2)` is not automatically three informative generation counters. Record allocation volume, heap growth and collection duration, and query `GarbageCollector.isIncremental` where supported. GC is not necessarily verdict-neutral when simulation code observes wall time. [Unity heap model](https://docs.unity3d.com/ru/2021.1/Manual/BestPracticeUnderstandingPerformanceInUnity4-1.html), [runtime flag](https://docs.unity3d.com/ru/current/ScriptReference/Scripting.GarbageCollector-isIncremental.html).

16. **[High — O4] Manual flushing creates a different execution model.** Multiplayer provides strong prior art: it relocates selected maintenance and suppresses the original frame calls. Simply adding extra calls changes ordering and can interact with Harmony patches or outstanding jobs. Treat this as a separate experimental backend requiring its own contract—not a transparent fix for F1–F3. [Relocation/suppression pattern](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rwmt/Multiplayer/master/Source/Client/AsyncTime/AsyncTimePatches.cs).

17. **[High] The benchmark needs matched starts and randomized blocks.** Reload the same fixture for every trial, establish a specified readiness/settling barrier, and separate warm-up from measurement. A/B/A does not cancel GC or thermal drift, particularly across eight configurations. Randomize balanced blocks; use three repeats as a pilot. A convenient full-list run supports a case study, not generalization to that list.

18. **[Medium] Measure throughput without assuming the bottleneck.** Report server execution time, complete-frame time and client end-to-end time separately. Control VSync/FPS cap, focus/background behavior, camera, profiler patches, logging and autosaves. Collect actual tick/frame samples; averages cannot produce p99. Six thousand ticks may provide too few burst frames for a useful tail estimate. Low speed could arise from simulation, rendering, maintenance, GC or guards—not necessarily Python polling.

19. **[High] Fix the RNG statement.** `Rand.PushState(seed)` across frames is not “impossible”; leaving a global scope open across callbacks is unsafe. A controlled runner can restore a private compressed RNG state at callback entry, capture the advanced state, and restore ambient state in `finally`. Include relevant maintenance in the chosen RNG contract; never reseed every tick. This still does not control Unity RNG, mod RNGs or asynchronous ordering. [Working state-management precedent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rwmt/Multiplayer/master/Source/Client/AsyncTime/AsyncTimeComp.cs).

20. **[High] Same save is not necessarily same complete start state.** Save loading can rebuild caches, consume RNG and leave process-static state behind. Record initial RNG state, exact game/DLL hashes, mod settings/load order, DLC, debug flags, IDs, camera state and pending work. Include some fresh-process reproducibility trials. Keep ordinary-play compatibility tests separate from tests using additional determinism controls.

21. **[Critical] “A–C ≤ A–A” is not an equivalence criterion.** A noisy baseline can excuse a large defect; two observations cannot characterize its noise. Predeclare field-specific practical bounds and require confidence intervals for paired differences to lie inside them. Use paired fixtures/seeds and enough independent trials. With zero mismatches in only three independent trials, the one-sided 95% upper bound on mismatch probability is still about **63%**.

22. **[High] Verdicts and endpoint totals are weak oracles.** Two FAILs can fail differently; two PASSes can conceal different behavior. Compare event timing, conservation invariants, topology membership, per-cell distributions, job targets/progress and exceptions at matched ticks. Use exact checks for discrete invariants and explicit absolute/relative bounds for continuous quantities. Require no NaNs. Preserve the first divergent tick; a final digest can miss divergence that later reconverges.

23. **[High] Replace three broad proofs with targeted adversarial fixtures first.** Include power connect/disconnect, dawn/dusk plus lamp toggling, room splitting, job churn, pollution dissolution and pause/long-event boundaries. Sweep N around chunk and scheduling boundaries—`1, 2, 29, 30, 31, 59, 60, 61`, plus large and randomized partitions. Vary rare/long-tick phase and insert zero-tick frames. An N-sensitive result is initially a scheduling incompatibility to investigate, not automatically a mod defect.

24. **[High] The harness needs an explicit execution contract before migration.** Specify pre/post-tick observation phase, initial-condition evaluation, exact target tick, partial completion and terminal reason. Prevent concurrent steppers and reentrant ticks. Cancellation/retries must not double-advance; game/map identity changes must invalidate the run. Capture logged ticking exceptions because a method can catch an exception and still increment the clock. Preserve frame-sensitive callers during migration.

25. **[Medium] Borrow stronger patterns from existing frameworks.** Factorio compares per-tick state CRCs and runs save/reload determinism tests: adopt checkpoint hashes, first-divergence localization and serialization round trips. Minecraft GameTest supplies small structure fixtures, setup ticks, tick-scheduled actions and bounded assertions: adopt those fixture semantics. Unity Test Framework distinguishes frame and physics yields; define separate RimWorld tick and maintenance barriers. Deterministic lockstep requires identical initial state, inputs **at identical ticks**, ordering and RNG—not merely equal seeds or matching verdicts. [Factorio](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-315), [GameTest](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/minecraft/creator/documents/gametestbuildyourfirstgametest?view=minecraft-bedrock-stable), [Unity tests](https://docs.unity3d.com/kr/Packages/com.unity.test-framework%402.0/api/UnityEngine.TestTools.html), [lockstep](https://gafferongames.com/post/deterministic_lockstep/).
