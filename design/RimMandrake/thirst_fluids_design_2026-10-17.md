(naive Opus review) and 3 (GPT review) and the merge are tracked in §11. Offline design only: nothing here was

## The owner's rulings this design serves (verbatim, 2026-10-17, card write-in)

> No non-potable water should be automatically gathered, that should be an order. Buckets can be drank from, even
> barrels if there are no other choices. There should be a small mood debuff if characters have no bottles to drink
> from and must drink from a tap on a tank or barrel (unless they are of the hardier traits that ignore such things,
> or they are wild and uncivilized). Drinking slime should only happen if there are no potable water supplies
> available. It provides a massive negative mood impact, a lingering laxative health issue for a day, and then the
> kind of slime may also affect you based on its own properties. Drinking salt water should be something characters
> do as a mental break type event due to extreme thirst: it briefly makes them feel better, but then induces
> medically dangerous states (design pass). [...] Include in that design pass there may be needs to supply crops
> with water, load fluids into machines, and other uses that cause a lot of traffic and business that we should
> think through in terms of bottles, buckets, barrels, pipes, and hoses.

Earlier rulings that still bind (from `design/RimMandrake/thirst_bottles_design_2026-10-16.md` §10, by card
2026-10-16): Dubs' thirst runs and ours steps aside (OQ-1); pawns drink from a pond or river (OQ-2); thirst empties
in ~1.25 days, up to 3x in heat, death ~3 days after empty (OQ-3); all animals get thirsty and drink from terrain
(OQ-5). Sprinkler/crop water rulings (`design/RimMandrake/sprinklers_design_2026-10-16.md` header): *"Nobody
"irrigates" a field on this world"*, *"It always counts as fresh as long as it's there"*, *"Nothing stays wet for
long without an obvious reason"*, watering only for adapted plants. Project rulings: one kind of heat (vanilla
temperature only); every DLC assumed; biome mechanics promised only inside our biomes; superb Mod Settings.

**This supersedes** the 2026-10-16 build's call that buckets and barrels cannot be drunk from
(`RM_ThirstUtility.cs:71`, `thirst_bottles_design_2026-10-16.md` §3 row "bucket / barrel").

## 1. Problem, constraints, success

**Problem.** The thirst build (commits `43266db9aa`, `77ef5bfcc8`, `e8ecd28f06`) gives pawns a thirst need and a
quality ladder (Distilled > Potable > Fouled > Toxic) and lets them drink anything with a `thirstQuality`, choosing
Fouled/Toxic when desperate. The owner's rulings replace that with a **behavioural** ladder: potable is drunk
normally; slime is a last resort with harsh, kind-specific consequences; salt water is never chosen, only drunk in
a mental break; non-potable liquid is never gathered without an order. And the whole liquid economy (drinking,
crops, machines) has to be shaped so pawn job load stays sane.

**Constraints.**
- Two thirst providers (Own `RM_Thirst`, Dubs `DBHThirst`), chosen once at startup (built, `RM_ThirstProvider.cs`).
- Everything is data on `LiquidDef` rows generated by `src/RimMandrake/FlowWorks/Tools/generate_liquid_suite.py`;
  a new liquid must not need new C# to be classified.
- Mod Settings: each new behaviour gets an on/off and its numbers are tunable; all-off degrades to "bottles are
  items".
- No new kind of heat; no DLC-absent path; animals drink terrain only (OQ-5).

**Success, measurable.**
1. No colonist ever starts an un-ordered job that fills a container with a non-potable liquid (a self-test row
   over `WorkGiver_FillBottle.HasJobOnThing` across every registry row; live: 1 in-game day on a map with brine and
   fresh water side by side, zero brine fills in the job log).
2. A pawn with a potable source reachable never drinks slime; with only slime reachable, does (live row).
3. Salt water is drunk only inside the mental state (no `JobGiver_RM_Drink` path reaches a Fouled-salt row).
4. Job load: on a 10-colonist desert colony, drink-related jobs (drink + fill + wash + haul) stay under a budget
   the owner sets (§8 proposes ≤ 4 jobs per colonist per day for water, PROPOSED).

## 3. The common core: every option below shares this drink model

The rulings are about **behaviour**, so the core change is to replace the quality ladder's "Fouled/Toxic when
desperate" with a **drink class** on every `LiquidDef` row, read by one decision function used by every drinker
(own think tree, administer-water, caravans; in Dbh mode only our bridge flags).

### 3.1 Drink class (new row field `drinkClass`, generated; `thirstQuality` stays as the hydrate/effect strength)

| drinkClass | meaning | voluntary drinking | auto-gathered | rows (PROPOSED) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Potable` | water you drink | yes | **yes** | FreshWater, IcyWater, five coloured waters, distilled outputs; BoilingWater once cooled (Q-F2) |
| `LastResort` | slime | only when **no potable source is reachable** (§3.3) | **never** (order only) | SlimeRed, SlimeGreen, SlimeWhite, SlimeYellow |
| `BreakOnly` | salt water | **never**; only inside `RM_BrineMadness` (§5) | **never** | SaltWater, Brine (Q-F1) |
| `Never` | not a drink | never, not even in the break | never (order only, for its real use) | AcidWater, ToxicWater (Q-F3), Propane, Chemfuel, Tar, ReactionLiquor, Lava, Astrofuel, Blood (Q-F4) |

"Auto-gathered" means any work giver a pawn takes on its own: fill bottle, fill from tank, empty into tank, haul to
a tank. **Non-potable liquids move only by an order** (§4). One row field means a future liquid is classified by its
row, never by new C#.

### 3.2 Vessel access tiers (who drinks from what, ruling 2)

| tier | source | mood | exemptions |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **a filled bottle** (inventory, then stockpile) | none (Distilled +2 stays) | — |
| B | **tap**: a tank tap (`RM_DrinkFromTank`), a bucket, or a barrel, *only when no bottle is reachable* | `RM_Thought_DrankFromTap` −3 for 0.5 day, stacking to 1 (PROPOSED "small") | hardy traits/genes and wild/uncivilized pawns (§3.4) |
| C | **potable terrain** edge (pond, river) | none today; Q-V2 asks whether a pond is a "tap" | — |
| D | **slime** (terrain or tank) when no tier A-C potable source is reachable | §6 | none: massive is massive (Q-S1) |

A bucket or barrel drink takes 1.5 L from the vessel, like a tank drink; a barrel is never consumed whole. A
bucket emptied by drinking becomes `RM_BucketEmpty` (or dirty), same as a bottle (the residue doer already does
this for bottles). The ruling says *"even barrels if there are no other choices"*: so the order inside tier B is
tank tap = bucket < barrel (barrel last), PROPOSED.

### 3.3 "No potable water available" — the definition the slime rule needs

The ruling turns on *available*, so the definition is load-bearing. PROPOSED: a potable source is available if any
tier A-C source is **reachable, not forbidden, not reserved, not in a cell the pawn's allowed area excludes, and
within the drink search radius** (vanilla food uses ~unlimited for colonists; PROPOSED: the same as the current
`JobGiver_RM_Drink` search). Not counted: potable water locked in a tank with no tap, frozen (§9.6), or behind a
hostile. Q-S2 asks whether "available" means *reachable right now* (pawn-centric) or *anywhere on the map*
(colony-centric; a pawn locked in a room with slime then drinks slime).

### 3.4 Hardy and wild exemptions (ruling 2: "hardier traits that ignore such things, or they are wild and uncivilized")

Real defs, MEASURED from the installed game (`Data/Core/Defs/TraitDefs/Traits_Singular.xml`,
`Data/Biotech/Defs/GeneDefs/GeneDefs_Misc.xml`, `GeneDefs_Spectrum.xml`, Biotech xenotype list):

| kind | candidates | why it fits | proposed |
|---|---|---|---|
| trait | `Ascetic` | already ignores bad rooms and plain food | **exempt** |
| trait | `Tough` | physical toughness, not fastidiousness | Q-T1 |
| trait | `Undergrounder` | indifferent to grim surroundings | Q-T1 |
| trait | `Cannibal` | vanilla already lets it ignore `AteRawFood` | Q-T1 |
| gene | `StrongStomach` (strong stomach), `RobustDigestion` (robust digestion) | "can eat anything" genes | **exempt** |
| xenotype | `Neanderthal`, `Dirtmole`, `Waster` (Biotech) | hardy-by-design xenotypes; exemption comes through their genes where they carry `StrongStomach`/`RobustDigestion` (gene lists UNMEASURED) | Q-T2: by gene only, or by xenotype too |
| wild | wild men (`Pawn.IsWildMan()`), animals | ruling text | **exempt** |
| uncivilized | tribal-tech factions (`Faction.def.techLevel <= Neolithic`), our Jawa xenotype (defName UNMEASURED) | "uncivilized" is the owner's word; tribal is the RimWorld meaning nearest it | Q-T3 |

Exemption is a data list (`RM_ThirstExemptionsDef`: traits, genes, xenotypes, a tech-level cap), so a race mod
patches in, and the same list feeds a `nullifyingTraits`/gene check on the thought. The slime and salt
consequences are **not** exempted by these (Q-S1).

## 4. Gathering: auto versus ordered (ruling 1)

**Rule.** Every auto work giver that moves liquid into a container or tank checks `drinkClass == Potable`. Today
`WorkGiver_FillBottle` fills any empty container at any fillable cell (§2 finding), so this is a **bug against the
ruling** in built code, fixable in one place (`RM_LiquidBottleUtility.TryFindFillCell` gets a `allowNonPotable`
flag, true only for orders).

**How an order is given** (three mechanisms; the options in §7 pick among them):
1. **Forced float-menu** ("Fill bucket with brine here"): vanilla-style, one container, one job. Exists in spirit
   (`forced` path of `HasJobOnThing`).
2. **Designator** "Gather <liquid>" on a liquid cell with a count (like a mining designation): the cell becomes a
   fill source for that liquid until the count is met.
3. **Bill** on a tank or a fill spot: "Fill N buckets of brine" (vanilla `RecipeDef` bill with a count/"until you
   have"): the most RimWorld-shaped way to say "keep 10 brine buckets in stock".

**Potable auto-gather also needs a stop.** Today the fill giver fills every empty bottle it can reach. PROPOSED: a
colony-wide target ("keep N filled potable bottles per colonist", default 3 = one day, Mod Setting), so filling is
demand-driven, like vanilla's meal bills. Without a target, a stack of 50 empties produces 50 fill jobs.

## 5. Salt water as a mental break (ruling 5)

**Trigger (PROPOSED).** A `MentalBreakDef` `RM_Break_DrinkBrine` (intensity Major) whose worker is only eligible
when the pawn's thirst is at `Parched` (<0.1) or `RM_Dehydration` ≥ 0.4 (moderate) **and** a SaltWater/Brine source
is reachable. Two trigger routes, Q-B1:
- **mood route**: it joins the normal mental-break pool (vanilla `MentalBreakWorker` with a `CommonalityFor`
  override), so it fires only when mood is also low; or
- **thirst route**: the need itself rolls a break when dehydration crosses a stage (like a hunger-driven break that
  vanilla does not have), so a happy but parched pawn can still crack.

**The state** `RM_MentalState_BrineMadness`: the pawn walks to the nearest salt source (terrain, bucket, barrel,
tank) and drinks until the need reads full (2-4 drinks), ignoring orders, then the state ends.

**Brief relief.** Each drink raises the need by the normal amount and adds `RM_Thought_SaltRelief` +8 for 3 hours
(PROPOSED). The need bar truly fills: *"it briefly makes them feel better"*.

**Then the danger.** `RM_SaltPoisoning` (hypernatremia), severity from litres drunk (0.25 per 1.5 L, PROPOSED):
- the need is drained: thirst falls ×3 while the hediff is above 0.2, so the relief reverses within hours;
- stages: 0.1 nausea (vanilla `Vomit` job chance, Consciousness −10%) · 0.3 confusion (−25%, Moving −15%,
  social fights possible) · 0.6 seizures (periodic `Downed` episodes via a hediff comp, −50%) · 0.85 coma ·
  1.0 death;
- recovery: severity falls 0.4/day while the pawn drinks potable water and is **tended** (Doctor, a tend
  increases fall rate by tend quality), and rises 0.1/day while thirst is 0 — so the cure is fresh water plus
  medicine, and a colony with no fresh water is in real trouble;
- the hediff names its cause, so nobody dies without a readable sign.

`RM_BrineShock` (`src/RimMandrake/Wasteland`) is a different thing (a brineleech's charge) and is not reused.

## 6. Slime as a last resort (ruling 4)

When §3.3 says no potable source is available, `JobGiver_RM_Drink` may pick a slime source. Consequences, all
on top of the hydrate (PROPOSED ×0.6, slime is thick):

| part | def | effect (PROPOSED numbers) |
|---|---|---|
| massive mood | `RM_Thought_DrankSlime` | −20 for 1 day, stacks ×2 (−30 max). Compare vanilla `AteHumanlikeMeatDirect` −20 |
| laxative, one day | `RM_SlimePurge` hediff | 1 day, then gone. Thirst falls ×1.5 (fluid loss), Consciousness −5%, and every ~2 h (mtb) a short `RM_SlimePurgeJob` that interrupts work and drops a filth; Dubs' bladder/hygiene need (if loaded) is hit too |
| per kind | see table | applied once per drink |

| slime | its row says | proposed effect when drunk | opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **red** | pH 4, AcidBurn on contact, corrodes apparel | a small chemical burn on the stomach (1 AcidBurn to `Stomach`), pain for a day | Q-S3 |
| **green** | pH 9, Rotting on immersion, "nothing immersed comes out the way it went in" | **if green slime is the Gelatinous body:** cures poison and starts `RM_Slimification` exactly like `RM_RawSlime` (reuse, do not rebuild); **if not:** a gut infection (vanilla `GutWorms`) chance 20% | Q-S3, the biggest: is liquid green slime the living body? |
| **white** | flammable, ignites at 150 °C | the most nutritious: +0.15 food (it is fat); no extra harm | Q-S3 |
| **yellow** | snot, the "disgusting" example row | extra −10 disgust mood (`RM_Thought_DrankSnot`), 10% `FoodPoisoning` | Q-S3 |

Per-kind effects are a list on the row (`ingestEffects`: hediff, severity, body part, chance, thought), so the
generator writes them and a new slime needs no C#. Slime has no bottled form (§2); Q-S4 asks whether to add one or
keep slime drinkable only from terrain and tanks (which keeps it a desperate act and avoids slime stockpiles).

## 7. Three solutions for the traffic: how liquid moves to mouths, crops and machines

The drink model (§3-§6) is shared. What differs is **who carries the liquid**, which decides the job load.

**Traffic arithmetic (from ruled numbers).** 3 L/day per pawn = 2 bottle drinks/day (ruled 1.5 L bottle, OQ-3
rate); ×3 in heat = up to 6. A bottle drink today costs **1 drink + 1 fill + 1 wash** (dirty stage on) + hauls.
10 colonists in desert heat: 60 drinks + 60 fills + 60 washes ≈ **180 water jobs/day**, before crops and
machines (estimated, arithmetic on ruled numbers). A tap drink costs 1 job; the tank is filled by pipe (0 jobs) or
by a barrel haul (1 job per 139 drinks).

### Solution A — "Bottles first, demand-capped" (smallest change to what is built)

Pawns drink bottles; taps are the fallback the ruling describes. The fill giver gets a stock target (§4: N filled
bottles per colonist) so filling is demand-driven, fills come from the nearest **tank** first (one short walk) and
terrain second, and wash is batched (a wash job takes up to 10 dirty bottles; PROPOSED). Crops and machines: pipes
and hoses only (§7.4); buckets for machines out of hose reach.
- **Buys:** the ruling's intent read literally (bottles normal, taps a mild hardship); least new code (a target,
  a batch wash, the drink-class filter).
- **Costs:** still ~2-3 jobs per drink. ~120-180 water jobs/day for 10 hot pawns (estimated).
- **Dominates when** colonies are small (≤ 6), the dirty stage is off, or the player wants water to be a
  visible chore.

### Solution B — "Drinking stations" (taps are the default, bottles are for travel)

A **water station** is a tank (or a barrel placed as a building) with a tap; pawns drink there in 1 job. The
"small debuff for drinking from a tap without bottles" is met by giving each station a **cup rack** (a cheap
attachment, or bottles stored adjacent): drinking at a station that has cups counts as "from a bottle" (no
debuff), so the debuff bites only colonies that never made bottles. Bottles exist for caravans, work far from a
station (`RM_ThirstPackBottles` already packs 2), and patients. Tanks fill by pipe, or by a barrel haul.
- **Buys:** ~1 job per drink; ~60-70 jobs/day for 10 hot pawns (estimated) and the bottle loop shrinks to
  caravans. Placing stations is a fun base-design decision (like placing a dining room).
- **Costs:** a new attachment/building and a new reading of the ruling ("cups count as bottles") that the owner
  must accept (Q-V1); pawns walk to stations, which costs time instead of jobs.
- **Dominates when** colonies are large or hot, or the owner reads "no bottles" as "no clean drinking vessel"
  rather than "no sealed bottle in hand".

### Solution C — "Network-first logistics tiers" (each vessel gets one job)

Each vessel is assigned **one kind of traffic**, by litres per trip:

| vessel | litres | job it owns | never used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| bottle 1.5 L | personal | drinking, caravans, patients | crops, machines |
| bucket 15 L | 10 bottles | a machine top-up out of hose reach; a small garden patch; firefighting (Q-M2) | bulk hauling |
| barrel 208 L | 139 bottles | bulk transport between bases/tanks, trade, tanker raids | drinking (except the ruled fallback) |
| hose | reach from a reel | temporary runs to a machine, a field, a new tank | permanent supply |
| pipe/canal | network | permanent supply to taps, sprinklers, machines | — |

Machines that consume a liquid read a **liquid input port** (pipe or hose end) first and accept a bucket/barrel
"refuel" only when unconnected (vanilla `CompRefuelable`-style job, one job per 15 or 208 L). Sprinklers are pipe
heads (already designed). Drinking uses B's stations on the network.
- **Buys:** the lowest steady-state job load (pipes do the carrying); a clear rule a player learns once.
- **Costs:** the most build: a liquid input port comp for machines, refuel-by-container jobs, bill-driven
  non-potable orders; mid-game before pipes is still A.
- **Dominates when** the colony is established and the map has pipes; it is also the shape the tanker-raid loop
  (`liquids_framework_design.md`, barrels and cargo tanks) already assumes.

### 7.4 Crops (shared)

Per the ruled sprinkler design: no field irrigation; only adapted plants want water; water comes from a sprinkler on
a pipe, or a pawn watering by hand. Hand watering is the traffic risk: PROPOSED one job per **bucket** (15 L waters
a 3×3 patch, number from `explosive_growth_ruled_spec_2026-10-16.md` UNMEASURED) and never per bottle. Watering
uses potable or "fresh" water only; does brine kill a crop (Q-C1)?

### 7.5 Machines (shared)

Fluids loaded into machines (chemfuel generators, propane, astrofuel for gravships, a still that distils brine,
slime compressors): one rule, PROPOSED: **a machine takes liquid from a port first, then from a bucket/barrel by a
refuel job; never from bottles.** Non-potable fuel loading is *its real use*, so ruling 1 is read as covering
**gathering from the world**, not moving an already-stored fuel into the machine that burns it (Q-G1).

### 7.6 Recommendation

**C as the target, reached through A then B**: ship A now (the drink-class filter and the stock target fix a
ruling violation in built code), add B's stations as the first logistics feature (it cuts jobs by about two
thirds on the arithmetic above), then C's machine ports when the first fluid-burning machine of ours needs one.
What would change it: if the owner reads "no bottles" strictly (Q-V1 = sealed bottles only), B's cup rack is out
and B degrades to "taps with a debuff", which is still fewer jobs but punishes good colonies; then A + C.

## 8. Edge cases

1. **Prisoners:** drink from a tap/bucket in their cell (tier B, debuff applies) or are given water by a Warden
   (built). Never slime or salt unless the cell holds only that (then §3.3 applies; Q-S2).
2. **Guests and traders:** drink the colony's potable taps and terrain; never a bottle from storage (vanilla
   guests do not eat your meals unless given). They never enter `RM_BrineMadness` on our map (they leave first;
   PROPOSED).
3. **Caravans:** bottles only (built); a caravan with no bottles but a barrel drinks the barrel (tier B, debuff).
   Slime/salt never on the world map (no source).
4. **Animals:** terrain only (OQ-5). Animals are "wild and uncivilized": no tap debuff, and they drink slime/salt
   terrain without the human consequences? Q-A1 (proposed: animals drink potable terrain only, and slime only when
   nothing else, with the per-kind effect but no mood).
5. **Downed pawns:** given water by a Doctor (built); a downed hostile gets none (built, `e8ecd28f06`). Never
   given slime or salt by a carer.
6. **Frozen fluids:** a bottle/bucket/barrel/tank below 0 °C holds ice: not drinkable until thawed (one kind of
   heat: vanilla ambient temperature; the freeze point per row, brine lower than fresh, PROPOSED). Frozen potable
   water does not count as available (§3.3). Melting = warm the room. Q-F5 whether pawns may eat ice/snow.
7. **Hostile visitors and raiders:** do not drink at all during a raid (vanilla raiders do not eat); siegers on a
   long siege drink their own packed bottles (PROPOSED).
8. **Children and babies (Biotech):** babies are fed water like milk by a carer (a bottle, never a tap); children
   are pawns.
9. **Slaves (Ideology):** as colonists; the tap debuff applies (Q-T3 whether slaves count as "uncivilized" – no,
   PROPOSED).
10. **Mechanoids, droids, entities:** no thirst (built exemption).
11. **Sanguophages, deathrest, cryptosleep:** frozen need (built). Blood as a drink for sanguophages: Q-F4.
12. **Boiling water:** never drunk while hot (it burns, `hot` row flag); cools in a container to fresh (Q-F2).
13. **Salt water sought by Dubs' AI:** in Dbh mode, salt/brine bottles carry `SeekForThirst false`
    (2026-10-17 card context), so Dubs never picks them; our break still fires off **our** reading of Dubs' need
    (`DBHThirst` level), Q-D1.

## 9. Dubs Bad Hygiene and Mod Settings

**Dbh mode.** Dubs owns the need and its drinking AI. Our rules survive as data on what Dubs sees:
`SeekForThirst true` only on `Potable` bottles; slime has no bottles; the tap debuff, slime last resort and salt
break are **ours** and run only where our code chooses the drink (Own mode) — except the salt break, which can read
