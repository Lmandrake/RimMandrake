# Why a ship stops at each biome: the one thing it can get, see or do only there (2026-11-19)

Item `RIMMANDRAKE_ENACT_FIVE_BIOME_ANSWERS_INTO_26_DESIGNS`. His note of 2026-11-18, about 4:05 PM PST: *"We should
force each biome to be worth it to uniquely visit it for SOMETHING."* His fuel answer of 2026-11-19 took the richer
fuel claim from six designs (the Blue Desert, the Cauldron, the Greentide, the Lantern Deeps, the Wastes and the
Webwork; `biome_rulings.md` §Five biome answers, 3), and the card said those six *"need another reason to visit"*.

How to read a row:
- **The draw** is the one thing, taken from the design's own "What the crew takes away" section (`design/concepts/
  <Biome>/design.md`, or `TerminalBiomes/<Sea>_design.md`). Where the design says it is unique on the planet, the
  design's words are quoted.
- **Source** is `design` when the design names it, and `UNDECIDED` when nothing left in 0.1.0 is unique to the
  biome. No row here is invented.
- **Version** is where the draw sits after his first-version answer of 2026-11-19 moved extras to 0.2.0. A draw in
  0.2.0 means a 0.1.0 stop has no unique reason to come yet.
- **Fuel** marks the six that lost a richer fuel claim (*lost*), the three richer sources (*richer*) and the replete
  one (*replete*).

## What this table shows

- **All six biomes that lost a richer fuel claim keep a draw of their own in 0.1.0**, named in their designs: blue
  ice, tree metal, hardwood, lanternstone and the dead's gear, unstripped war salvage, thrixweave.
- **Two biomes have no unique draw left in 0.1.0**: the Long Shade and the Rot. Their designs' draws moved to 0.2.0
  with the extras his first-version answer moved. Choosing a 0.1.0 draw for each is design work for the Conductor;
  this table does not choose one.
- **One depends on a signature choice still open**: the Gelatinous Slime (the gene journey or the contact film).
