## 3. Who decides when each runs

No AI wakes itself. The Dispatcher runs every role from **one schedule file**; agents may *request* a run by ticket.
Triggers: his message (Conductor) · clock (Operations, Producer, Auditor, Innovator) · events (feature claims done →
Arbiter; alarm → Operations; plan step fails → Producer). Token duties split by kind (owner, 2026-10-27): the Dispatcher (a program) enforces caps mechanically,
throttling in a fixed order - Innovator pauses first → Auditor weekly sample → Producer weekly → Arbiter batches daily →
Operations alarms only; Conductor never - and picks the account before releasing work. Operations is the only AI that
analyses burn (rates, bleeds, trends, forecast vs plan); when a forecast misses, the Producer drafts the replan (a cut
list) and the Conductor brings that trade-off to him to decide together. Every other role is token-blind: caps are
enforced around it and its instructions forbid token talk and token-saving judgment. Guards: quota unused at reset is
waste like running dry, and a no-AI check flags token talk in the blind roles' reports. He sets the starting file; Operations' economy pass may propose cadence
changes as experiments judged on their metric; reversible ones apply and the Conductor reports them; silencing a role
goes to him. **The Innovator asks first until it proves itself** (owner, 2026-10-27: *"Ask first until it proves
itself"*): every change it wants is a proposal through the Conductor to him. It has proved itself (a starting rule
he can change) when at least 3 innovations he accepted reach `adopted` on `design/RimMandrake/innovations.md` and no
trial of its own ended `revert-failed` or was reverted for a guard breach in the last 30 days; then the rule above
applies to its reversible agent and opportunity trials. A `revert-failed`, or his word, returns it to asking first;
game concepts, mods and merges are always his.

**The Dispatcher measures; Operations analyses.** The schedule's `measure` list holds the no-AI instruments the
Dispatcher runs; the first is `ops_watch` (`src/RimMandrake/Utils/ops_watch.py`, every 15 min from
`rm-ops-watch.timer`). It gathers every KPI family into append-only series, raises the Andon flags (band, run,
silent, target) and files one alarm item per NEW crossing; that filing is the "alarm → Operations" trigger above. His
words, 2026-10-27 (ledger OWNER note 05:59:41Z): *"Ops can analyze it anytime needed. ... It should just have those
things automatically gathered for it, so it's not constantly burning tokens gathering it all up for every question
considered."* The Operations agent reads the digest and never re-gathers. Detail: responsibilities doc §Operations.

## 4. Three competing shapes (from the design pass; GPT arbiter's survivors)

Five designs went in (Rounds, COO, Producer, Board, Inspector General —
`design/RimMandrake/csuite_design_2026-10-26.md` S2.5); both GPT teardowns killed the pure watchers (Rounds, Board):
they show the problem and leave him as dispatcher. Survivors differ in which role leads:
1. **Run it** — Operations leads. Moves his "go" messages and wasted test hours. Weak: direction.
2. **Build the right things** — Producer + Arbiter lead. Moves "how is it going?" and a real "done". Weak: running.
3. **Stop repeating mistakes** — Auditor + Innovator lead. Moves the 27.5% failure-signal share. Weak: delivery.
All share Conductor + Dispatcher. **Ruled 2026-10-26 (§6): grow 1 → 2 → 3**, adding each role only when its number
shows the need.

## 5. Grounding review (owner asked "is this a bubble fantasy?", 2026-10-26)

- **Grounded:** the diagnosis (§1 numbers); maker ≠ checker (classic separation of duties; measured today); delegation
  by class (five classes delegated, `design/RimMandrake/delegation_policy.md`); Anthropic guidance (state on disk,
  subagents for isolation, compaction acceptable) and dispatcher-fed worker pools; SRE-style budget-burn alerting.
- **Speculative:** an eight-role org chart is analogy to human firms — no evidence more roles → more progress (the
  49-agent community game-studio kit ranked its heaviest process last); every benefit is predicted; the Dispatcher
  and the channel that wakes an idle window are unbuilt/untested; supervisors cost tokens while the week already runs
  out early (~35 h before the Wed reset at current pace); our own census had arithmetic errors GPT caught.
- **Consequence for the build:** roles are names for jobs; each becomes an agent only when a metric shows its gap.
- Operations/Economist split trigger: if the daily economy pass is skipped or yields no action two days running,
  split the Economist out.

## 6. Build plan (v3.0 → v3.x) and the success test

1. **v3.0 Dispatcher** (no AI): schedule file, wake/handoff/verify/retry/record, lease-based "doing", caps; prove with a
   **48 h unattended run** (wake an idle window; recover a dead one; never take the game while he plays).
2. **v3.1 Conductor** quick mode on his phone (a standing window, fresh after each goodbye, metrics pack, fact-checked answers) + session mode =
   today's Bench.
3. **v3.2 Operations** (incl. economy pass) — shape 1, run it.
4. **v3.3 Producer + Arbiter** — shape 2, the right things; **v3.4 Auditor + Innovator** — shape 3, learning. Each
   only when its metric shows need.

Success test, measured weekly against the baseline tag (targets from the design pass; hypotheses, not promises):
his keep-going messages (31/day → ≤26 after v3.0); accepted items/day (accepted = pass on named scenarios, specific
build, non-maker check); test hours that catch nothing (≤18 h/wk); his failure-signal share (27.5% → ≤25%);
**process-commit share (70% → below 61%)**; "how is it going?" answerable at a glance. **Kill criterion:** after a week
of v3.0, if his keep-going messages have not dropped and accepted items have not risen, stop and reconsider; the
baseline tag restores v2.

**Measured by `ops_watch` from 2026-10-27** (series `D:\ClaudeWork\rimmandrake\ops_watch\series\`, family `v3`, and
the `testing` and `process` families): test hours that catch nothing (`testing.nothing_h_7d`, ESTIMATE while the
archmagi rig has blind days) and process-commit share (`process.share_7d`), each against its target above, every 15
min. **Owed** (the watch records them as UNMEASURED with the reason, never as 0): his keep-going messages and his
failure-signal share (only an AI tagging pass measures them; a deterministic classifier is owed), and accepted
items/day (no ledger event records the non-maker check yet). "How is it going?" at a glance is the watch's
`digest.md`. Item for the owed instruments: `OPS_KPI_MEASURE_OWED_VTHREE`.

**Proving it pays (owner order, 2026-10-27):** *"Please ensure that the RimFlow v3 will be able to actively determine
if it's actually helping vs. the baseline of the recently tagged Rimflow v2 (Bench + Foundry). We MUST prove that it's
superior and at what cost. After it's settled down and optimized of course. One of the v3 roles must own this regular
assessment."* Built under `V3_VERSUS_V2_MEASURE_PROVE_IT_PAYS`: `src/RimMandrake/Utils/v3_vs_v2.py` (no AI, weekly)
measures 22 metrics with the baseline doc's own definitions (7 primary, 4 guard, 5 cost, 6 report) against the tag
week; each is BETTER / SAME / WORSE outside a noise band bootstrapped from the tag week's 7 days. The rule
(`src/RimMandrake/Utils/v3_vs_v2_rules.json`, sha256 pinned in the program, registered before any v3 week) says:
SUPERIOR when at least 4 of the 7 primaries are BETTER and no guard and no veto primary (rework, owner waits over 3
days, already-ruled questions) is WORSE (owner by card 2026-10-27: "Majority of measures"); WORSE when at least 2
primaries are WORSE and BETTER does not outnumber them, or 1 is and none is BETTER; UNMEASURED under 4 measured primaries or with no token measure; the cost (HIGHER / SAME /
LOWER and the token ratio to v2) beside it. The standing is over the last 4 settled weeks (SUPERIOR needs 3 of 4 and
no WORSE); before 4 settled weeks it is PROVISIONAL. A simulator counterfactual (what v2 would have done with that
week's owner input) can only take a BETTER back, and counts only while its backtest on held-out v2 weeks passes; on
2026-10-27 it failed (`rimflow_v3_sim_findings_2026-10-27.md`), so it reports UNMEASURED. Operations owns the
assessment and sends it to him through its owner-only route; the Auditor re-checks the quality half token-blind.
