RimWorld 1.6 campaign (a Jawa scavenger clan on a hand-made desert world, with Star Wars flavour). The target mod list is 550 mods plus our own absent mods: **589 rows**. Generated by `src/RimMandrake/Utils/modaudit/gpt_package.py` from `MASTER_REGISTRY.jsonl` (merge of 16 assessor batches and 4 checker groups) and `facts/mod_facts.jsonl`. Glossary: PHASE = keep temporarily then phase out; ELIM = eliminate completely; sev 0-3/U; OURS = mandrake.rm/rsw/rut.

### 1. Identity

**What the game is.** *Flight of the Utinni*: RimWorld 1.6 with all DLC, a Jawa scavenger clan on
the desert world **Ash'karr, "The Sundered"**, who steal and wake an ancient Rakatan ship (born
*Kolyska*, painted *The Utinni*) out of a Hutt discard yard. "Getting it off the ground is the
campaign." Feel: *"Firefly / Battlestar Galactica / Oregon-Trail-in-space / a scientific expedition
crossing an unexplored world."* [`design/Jawa/reconciled_lore/01_campaign.md` §Premise, §Names of
record]. (`design/Jawa/concept.md` is marked SUPERSEDED by that file [its line 1].)

**The loop.** Land, camp, explore, gather, improve the ship, pressure rises, **decide what to leave
behind**, launch, repeat. The colony lives aboard; crew ~3–8; no tile is self-sufficient. Motion is
forced by the Empire (orbital pursuer), the Hutt ledger and Ta'Baa's theological clock; fuel has
many redundant paths [`01_campaign.md` §The loop, §The three pressures, §Fuel is life].

**Who the player is.** Six fixed `MandrakeJawa` founders; fixed pawns, ship and map; the start is
HARD; flight hardware ships unbuilt [`01_campaign.md` §v1 start]. The world is **one hand-made
frozen planet delivered as a savegame** [`CLAUDE.md` §"There is no worldgen feature"].

**Pillars.** Anti-exponential (the gravship and its industry are the only scalable trees); no arms
race (qualitative danger, never stat inflation); the 7-question test; "Mod count is not the
constraint — the capability ceiling is", with curation by Cherry Picker trimming, not uninstalling
[`01_campaign.md` §The pillars].

**Tone.** Star Wars *style*; invented exotic names are not IP and are franchise-free [`CLAUDE.md`
§"Star Wars style naming is NOT Star Wars IP"; `design/RimMandrake/biome_mod_architecture.md` §7 Q11a].

**The three tiers decide a mod's home** [`design/NAMING_SCHEME_PLAN.md` §1–2; owner 2026-08-30]:

| Tier | packageId / prefix | Means | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| RimMandrake | `mandrake.rm.*` / `RM_` | any RimWorld game | "Would a medieval-tribe player install this alone and understand it?" |
| RimStarWars | `mandrake.rsw.*` / `RSW_` | any Star Wars scenario | "Would a Hoth or Tatooine-smuggler campaign want this unchanged?" |
| RimUtinni | `mandrake.rut.*` / `RUT_` | this campaign only | "Does it name Ash'karr, the Kolyska, the Nine, this clan, or this story?" |

A functional fix takes the tier of the mod it fixes; an aesthetic reskin takes the tier of the
aesthetic [`NAMING_SCHEME_PLAN.md` §1 tie-break rules].

### 2. Hard rulings that bear on mod decisions

Each bullet: ruling ⇒ implied filter.

  planets"*) [`CLAUDE.md` §"There is no worldgen feature", owner 2026-08-15/18]. ⇒ World generators,
  seed/biome-scoring tools and mechanics that need world creation have no campaign job. But
  "dissonant" is not "safe to remove": the current save may reference them (§3). Precedent: a raid

  BIOME WITH ZERO TILES IS NOT A DEFECT"]. ⇒ "Our biome has no tiles, the donor's does" is never a

- **All biome content is one RM mod, `mandrake.rm.biomes`** (executed 2026-09-28), Utinni patches

  Helixien Gas Expanded) [`biome_mod_architecture.md` §7 Q16]. ⇒ A framework our mods lean on is KEEP, not dissonance.

### 4. Resonance tests

Ask of every mod, in this order. Each test names its source.

1. **What does it actually ship, and is any of it used?** Read its About.xml `<name>` and
   `<description>` and its Defs; a pure-C# mod can look like a framework
   [`outgrown_audit_2026-08-30.md` honesty note; `CLAUDE.md` §"Is there a mod that does X?"].
   Check Cherry Picker cuts and our own `MayRequire`/`loadAfter` references before calling it unused.
2. **Does anything of ours depend on it?** A framework or library one of our mods requires is KEEP
   until that dependency is removed [`biome_mod_architecture.md` §7 Q16].
3. **Fit: desert scavenger crew, Star-Wars-style frontier, ship-centred, keep moving?** Resonant:
   scarcity, salvage, water and fuel pressure, qualitative threats, alien-looking creatures.
   Dissonant: medieval/fantasy/Norse/orc identities, Earth-recognizable animals, modern-corporate
   brands [`01_campaign.md` §Premise, §The pillars; `creature_recognizability_rule.md`;
   `donor_proper_noun_backlog.md` categories].
4. **Does it raise the ceiling?** Run the 7 questions: parallel ladder, scales indefinitely, bypasses
   fuel/space/risk/time/mood/scarcity, makes the ship less necessary. Cosmetic, QoL, UI and sound mods
   "pass trivially" [`01_campaign.md` §The pillars; `forbidden_mods.md` §Excluded player-progression
   systems].
5. **Does it generate worlds, vary planets, or need world creation to work?** Dissonant
   [`CLAUDE.md` §no worldgen].
6. **Does it duplicate a system we own or have ruled on?** Biomes (`mandrake.rm.biomes`), liquids
   and depth (FlowWorks, `LiquidDef` registry), creatures (SWBestiary and the biome rosters), heat
   (vanilla only), flight (core), sea-floor access (the ship), in-game LLM (`claude -p`)
   [§2 above and sources there].
7. **Is it Star Wars IP?** Then its future is an `RSW_` port routed through Utinni patches, and the
   donor is on the sunset track [`biome_mod_architecture.md` §7 Q11; `STARWARS_DONOR_SUNSET_1`].
8. **Is it kept only for its art?** Donor and vanilla art are not kept [`memory/no-donor-art-kept.md`].
9. **What does retiring it cost?** Dependents, placed Things in the save, `ParentName` order,
   paired donors [§3 above].
10. **Performance.** On the 550 list a quicktest map ran **~8–10 TPS** (~120 ms/tick), main thread
    saturated, 30.6 GB private, cold load 20.7 min; the cause awaits "its own diagnosis", so per-mod
    cost is **UNMEASURED** [`Transient/foundry_fulllist_deploy_20261010.md` 14:00–14:20, §Owed].
    Noisy donors seen there: Worldbuilder (ConfigErrors NRE), Quarry (duplicate `Sandstone` key),
    Leo.RaidProtectionFee (a modal swallowed a raid) [same].

## 2. Rubric in brief (full text: RUBRIC.md)

- **Five questions per mod:** (1) what it adds, (2) forward plan, (3) likely problems, (4) is source available,
  (5) does it resonate with the scenario or clash with it.
- **Verdicts:**
  - `KEEP`: fit is neutral or resonant, and something of ours depends on it or it supplies something we will not build.
  - `KEEP-TEMPORARILY-THEN-PHASE-OUT` ("PHASE"): name what holds it in and the exit item.
  - `REPLACE-NOW-WITH-OUR-OWN`: concrete scope, a reason it cannot wait, and proof we have not already built it.
  - `ELIMINATE-COMPLETELY` ("ELIM"): it clashes with the scenario or duplicates ours, AND it passes the retirement-cost
    check (dependants, placed Things in the save, ParentName inheritance).
  - When evidence is missing, take the most conservative verdict.
- **Severity per kind** (0-3, or U = unmeasured):
  - `memory`: header-decoded texture estimate, <50 / 50-250 / >250 MB. This is a proxy, never a measurement.
  - `tps`: tick flags in the DLL, no per-mod measurement.
  - `log_noise`: attributed error blocks (attribution is a CANDIDATE).
  - `interaction`: patches into or from our mods.
  - `complexity`: XML-only / one DLL / Harmony + Prepatcher or more than 20 static ctors.
  - `size`: disk, <20 / 20-200 / >200 MB.
  - `maintenance`: source verified and recent / unverified / none or older than 2 years.
- **Confidence on every claim:** MEASURED (read off an instrument or a file), INFERRED, UNVERIFIED.
- **Forbidden shortcuts:**
  - judging by name;
  - "heavy" without a number;
  - treating a candidate log attribution as guilt;
  - reading def_total as "in game";
  - "unused" because `patched_by` is empty;
  - resolving a brief §5 contradiction inside a verdict;
  - inventing ledger items.

## 3. Programme, method and known weaknesses

**Pipeline (stage 0, offline):**
- `universe.py`: FULL.LATEST (the 550-mod target list) plus our absent mods = 589 ids, resolved to disk by About.xml parse.
- `defscan.py`: def and patch census over a synthetic ModsConfig of the universe, with load folders resolved.
- `diskfacts.py`: About.xml, folder walk, DLL string flags, PNG/DDS header texture estimate.
- `runtime.py`: one measured 550-mod session on 2026-10-10. It gives a Player.log error attribution (100 of 344 blocks
  attributed), the LoadTracer static ctors, and a stage-level TPS profile.
- `sources.py`: gh api verification of linked repos.
- `assemble.py` writes `facts/mod_facts.jsonl`.
- `batches.py` cuts 16 batches by category.

**Stage 1:** one assessor agent per batch (Claude Sonnet 5.5) writes `assessments/batch_NN.jsonl` and `records/batch_NN.md`.

**Stage 2:** four independent checker groups (Claude Opus 5.5) re-derive every MEASURED number from facts and open every
cited ruling, ELIMINATE row and card_worthy row. They write `checks/check_group_N.jsonl`, with corrections as old/new/evidence.

**Merge:** `merge_checks.py` applies amendments field by field. A checker's verdict change is taken **only** when the merge
author opened the primary source it cited (23 spot-checks in `merge_inputs.json`; 13 accepted, 1 not confirmed, the rest
non-verdict claims). Otherwise the row is DISPUTED and both verdicts are kept.

**Known weaknesses of the first pass:**
- Per-mod TPS and memory are UNMEASURED. Memory is a header proxy. It is blind to AssetBundles and resources.assets, and
  it counted images under Source/ (fixed in the facts regeneration, §9).
- Log attribution covers 29% of error blocks. "0 errors" often means "not attributed".
- Placed Things in the campaign save were never measured for any ELIMINATE.
- Live Mod Settings (the Config folder) were not read by the assessors. Checkers found three rows where the live
  setting contradicts a ruling or a doc.
- `dependants` held only About.xml `modDependencies`. Load-order, class-reference, patch, FindMod and by-version edges
  were missing, and this hid real consumers (§5).
- Assessors searched `design/Jawa/mods/` for rulings. Rulings that live in the ledger, in commit bodies and in our own
  patch headers were missed.
- Several "ADOPTED" entries in required_mods.md are Fetcher-era recommendations, not owner decisions. They were not separated.

## 7. Consolidated owner cards (OWNER_CARDS.md)


These cards come from the mod audit (589 mods, 2026-10-10/11). Every question raised by the 16 assessors and the 4
checkers was collected. Questions an existing ruling already answers were dropped; the table at the end names the
ruling for each. Duplicates were merged, and questions that need a measurement rather than a choice became tickets
(`ACTIONS_NOW.md` §E). That leaves **23 cards in 8 themes**.

Each card gives:

- the decision, in plain words;
- the mods involved;
- what each option buys and what it costs;
- a recommendation with one line of reasoning;
- where the evidence is.

"Phase-out" means "keep it now, remove it once our replacement exists". Per-mod detail is in `MASTER_REGISTRY.jsonl`
(column `owner_question_ids`) and in `records/batch_NN.md`.

### Theme 1. Leaving the big donor mods

#### C01. Do the "permanent resident" donors stay forever, or get ported like everything else?
**The decision.** On 2026-09-11 you named some big donor mods "permanent residents" (keep them for good). On 2026-09-20
you ruled "move everything to our own defs". The two rulings conflict for these mods, and today the audit marks two of
them keep-forever and the rest phase-out.
**Mods:** ReGrowth (`regrowth.botr.core`), Alpha Biomes (`sarg.alphabiomes`, the largest biome donor at 1,155 refs from our
mods), Biomes of Grimterra (`grimterra.biomesmod`), More Vanilla Biomes (`zylle.morevanillabiomes`), Alpha Animals
(`sarg.alphaanimals`), Alpha Memes (`sarg.alphamemes`), Vanilla Memes Expanded (`vanillaexpanded.vmemese`), VFE Tribals
(`oskarpotocki.vfe.tribals`).
- **A. Port everything (the 09-20 rule wins).** Each donor leaves once its biome or creatures are ours, which happens biome by biome at
  each biome's sitting, and finishes by the paint-once pass. *Buys:* no outside mod holds the campaign hostage, and the art
  can all be ours. *Costs:* the most porting work, and Alpha Biomes is a hard dependency of our biomes mod today.
- **B. The 09-11 residents stay for good.** *Buys:* no porting for the eight. *Costs:* the biggest memory and load share
  stays with outside authors, and donor art stays in the game (against "no donor art kept").
- **C. Mixed: frameworks and memes stay, biomes and creatures port.**
**Recommendation: A**, sequenced biome by biome as the biomes come up. Your later ruling and the no-donor-art rule both point
this way, and ending Alpha Biomes' hard dependency is mostly technical work (ticket `BIOME_DONOR_DEPENDENCY_UNTANGLE_HARD_LINK`).
**Evidence:** `design/Jawa/mods/mod_config_rulings.md:71`; `DONOR_DEFS_PORT_TO_OURS_1`; `checks/check_group_2.md` "Ruling
conflicts"; brief §5.9 (the 09-11 file says "Nothing here has been re-verified").

#### C02. Cut the donors we have already replaced now, or wait for the world remake?
**The decision.** Some donors' jobs are done by our own mods already, or will be at the remake. If they come out before the
remake, anything they placed in the current save (animals, furniture) vanishes from it.
**Mods:** Star Wars Animal Collection (`mlie.starwarsanimalcollection`, still 3,439 refs from our patches), Outer Rim Furniture
(`neronix17.outerrim.furnitureanddecor`), Chezhou Sandworm and its library (`chezhou.creature.sandworm`,
`chezhou.chezhoulib.lib`; our own Long Hunger replaces it).
- **A. Cut each one as soon as its references are gone.** *Buys:* a lighter list sooner. *Costs:* placed things in today's
  save may vanish, and the save is disposable until the remake anyway.
- **B. Cut them all at the world remake.** *Buys:* one clean break with no save surgery. *Costs:* they ride along
  until then.
**Recommendation: A for the sandworm** (it only duplicates our Long Hunger), **B for the other two** (the remake is
the planned break, so paying migration costs before it is waste).
**Evidence:** `records/batch_06.md`, `records/batch_08.md`, `records/batch_14.md`; ticket `SWAC_EDGES_CENSUS_PORTED_VS_LIVE`.

#### C03. GravTide and the sea: what survives once the ship flies to the sea floor?
**The decision.** You ruled that the ship is the only way down and back ("ship or nothing"). GravTide still provides
pressure hulls and boats. Vehicle Framework vehicles could reach the sea layer. The Warcasket was ruled "an alternative to
... ocean access".
**Mods:** GravTide (`gravtide.mod`), our Warcasket (`mandrake.rm.warcasket`), Vehicle Framework sea use (`smashphil.vehicleframework`).
- **A. GravTide goes once our sea-floor layer works alone. The Warcasket protects on shores and in toxic or vacuum areas,
  never as a way onto the sea floor. No vehicles on the sea layer.** *Buys:* one coherent sea rule. *Costs:* GravTide's
  boats are lost.
- **B. Keep selected GravTide systems (pressure hulls or boats) as ours.** *Costs:* porting work, and a second way to the
  sea that has to be fenced.
**Recommendation: A.** It is the only reading consistent with "ship or nothing".
**Evidence:** CLAUDE.md sea ruling 2026-09-26; `records/batch_08.md` gravtide.mod; `records/batch_15.md` mandrake.rm.warcasket.
