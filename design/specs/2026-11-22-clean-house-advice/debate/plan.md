`design/specs/2026-11-22-clean-house-advice/advice.md`. Each claim is tagged `[measured]` or `[guess ~N%]`;

## On one screen

**The plan in five lines.**

1. Until 12-06, Pyrelands and FlowWorks move through the pipeline that exists now, when their holds, stops and
   readiness allow. GimmeSomeSlack's walk stays offered to you.
2. For each of those mods, the Conductor finds the next legal act and starts it. A helper builds the record of what

4. Nothing else is built by default: no merger campaign, recorder, parking, SDK, new gate or new seat. A failure
   that has been reproduced and that crosses homes can earn one bounded test, outside the live pipeline.
5. On 11-29 and 12-06 you get a one-page report: what moved, what blocked it, what that cost, and what is still
   unknown. The report recommends the next step, and it may say the bigger question is still open.

**Where the advocates still disagree.** Whether to test crash recovery before a real incident. Whether to cap the

**Your decision: one** (the study had eight). Do new pipeline experiments wait outside the live pipeline until
12-06? The rest is the Conductor's to do under the rules that exist.

## 2. What the debate settled

After Round 2, the eight advocates' revised positions share these points (each advocate's own words are in its
viewpoint file, sections 3 and 4 of its rebuttal):

- Keep authorized mods moving through the current pipeline. Do not make them wait for a full look back
  (repair-current-runtime: "Neither mod should wait for a complete retrospective").
- Verify today's consolidations on the paths that active work uses (freeze-strangle-core, conceding: "Six reported
  consolidations are not six supplied completion proofs").
- Class a hand act before calling it a defect. Some are the Conductor's delegated judgement (freeze-strangle-core
  withdrew "record hand runs as core defects").
- Defer scheduled mergers, a whole-tree recorder, parking, an SDK move and any factory-wide cut-over.
- Never two live controllers for the same work, never a weaker bar, never a deletion that rests on a few quiet days.

What they did not settle is in §5. On cost: most advocates withdrew their forecasts as uncalibrated.
replace-durable-slice kept 25 to 43 M as a qualified allowance, and freeze-strangle-core kept a 60 M outer stop.
The one measured anchor is today's consolidation runs: 1.2 to 2.1 M each `[measured by the study]`.

## 4. The fortnight, 2026-11-23 to 12-06

### The rule for each mod

The Conductor starts each mod's next legal act as soon as holds, stops and readiness allow, with the tools that
exist. When that act cannot start or does not finish, the cause is found and repaired. A helper records each stop
beside the work, in one committed table, with these classes. A stop may have a chain of classes (an account stop
that then lost a result is both).

| class | what it looks like | what follows |
|---|---|---|
| mod work | a bar fails; a check or scene is wrong | Fixed in the sitting, as the rules say. Machinery waits |
| wrong fact | two readers give different answers for the same state at the same time | That caller reads its one home. The test compares against the intended rule; the old reader is deleted |
| slow dispatch | the state is right and nothing launched | The tick's timing is diagnosed and repaired. Meanwhile the Conductor launches by hand, with acts it may already do, each recorded |
| lost or doubled run | a run died, its result was lost, or it counted twice | Reproduced first. Its smallest repair, in its home, is tried first. Only a failure that crosses homes (several writers, or result and stage in different stores) earns the bounded test below |
| readiness block from machinery | a pipeline ticket or fault keeps the mod from being ready | Counted as blocked time and repaired as a pipeline defect |
| contention | other admitted work held the account, game, clone or Conductor that this mod needed, and that work is named | The Conductor admits less work or lowers concurrency |
| delegated act | a release, a clear or a decision that the rules give the Conductor | Recorded and classed, row by row, as necessary or as an avoidable rescue. An avoidable rescue is then a defect |
| your act or your hold | your walk, a hold, an OFF act | A wait, not a defect, and not blocked time |

### The one exception: a bounded test

A reproduced failure that crosses homes may earn one comparison: its smallest repair against one bounded
replacement (an outside runtime or a small core), on the real launcher and recording interfaces, in a scratch
path. Its conditions, from the advocates who argued it:

- the live pipeline stays the only controller during the test;
- the test includes an effect that succeeds while its acknowledgement is lost, a stale worker after its authority
  ends, and a duplicate result;
- a handover, if one is earned, first reconciles the runs in flight, and its rollback is rehearsed;
- the test stops if it needs a second recovery controller of its own.

### Proof and stopping, for each change

- Every repair: a test that is red before and green after, against the intended rule. If the same cause comes back,
  that repair is reopened. One recurrence does not by itself mean the plan failed.
- The bounded test: it stops at its first unresolved safety failure (a duplicate effect, a stale write, a false
  pass, a lost stage move).
- A checkpoint, not a cap: when recorded machinery work for this plan passes 30 M weighted tokens, the Conductor
  reads this plan again before it spends more. The danger it guards is the one the study measured: at least 517 M of
  machinery runs in nine days.
- Conductor-window spend is not in the run records, so every total is a lower bound.

## 5. Where the advocates still disagree

1. **Test crash recovery now, or after an incident.** rebuild-small-core wants one test this week whatever the
   records show: an effect that succeeds while its acknowledgement is lost, at the launch, result and stage
   boundary. The others want a reproduced, current failure first. This plan takes the incident-first side, and
   starts from the three launch failures already on record. The void count does not settle the question: it gives
   the reasons that runs stopped, not the safety of that boundary.
2. **A helper cap.** shrink-the-fleet holds one concurrent helper and two unfinished requests as the default.
   Several others accept fewer helpers only when the record shows contention (repair-current-runtime: "reduce
   concurrency when traces demonstrate contention"). The "contention" class in the stop table answers it for these
   two mods.
3. **The final destination.** freeze-strangle-core holds that the tick should in the end route all routine work.
   The others hold that this is not proved. Two mods in late stages cannot prove it either way, so the 12-06 report
   may leave it open.
4. **What earns a reader repair.** consolidate-facts-only: a wrong answer that ordinary use can reach. repair-current
   -runtime, in Round 1: a demonstrated obstruction. This plan takes the first, which is the rule that a concrete case
   in normal use is worth the fix.

## 6. Your decision

**Do new pipeline experiments wait outside the live pipeline until 12-06?**

- **(a) Yes.** New ideas for the pipeline go to a copy or a sibling repository; only a reproduced failure or a repair
  changes the live pipeline. For: the ground stops moving under the repairs; the study measured four pipeline
  designs in seven weeks, and four studies advised a system that was then retired. Against: `PURPOSE.md` makes the
  agent experiment an equal purpose of the project, and you work "a new way every week".
- **(b) No.** Experiments continue as now. For: full freedom. Against: each change of design can orphan the repairs
  in flight, which is what the study's table shows.

It is yours because it turns on the project's purpose, which is yours. The Conductor's obvious pick is (a), for this
fortnight only.

Everything else in this plan is the Conductor's to enact under the rules that exist: the priority of the two mods,
the repairs, the stop table, admission and concurrency, and the bounded test. None of it is one of your five acts.
Your walk of GimmeSomeSlack, and of any mod that reaches its walk, stays yours as always.
