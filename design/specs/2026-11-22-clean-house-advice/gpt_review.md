4. **The strongest alternative is to stop improving the home-built orchestrator and test replacing it. [guess]** Time-box an adoption trial against continuing repair, using identical workloads and fault scenarios.

   The engine dismissal is technically weak: durability addresses crashes and long waits even on one machine. [Temporal](https://docs.temporal.io/durable-execution) records execution history and resumes workflows. [DBOS](https://docs.dbos.dev/python/programming-guide) currently defaults to SQLite; Postgres is recommended for production, not mandatory for a trial. The document also misses [LangGraph and Deep Agents](https://www.langchain.com/oss-overview), respectively a durable workflow runtime and agent harness.

   **[guess]** A narrowly scoped rebuild around an existing engine/harness could remove more maintenance than consolidating bespoke machinery. Preserve mods, adapters, history and regression fixtures; replace orchestration only. Require idempotent effects, pinned toolchains and replay tests. Adoption earns continuation by recovery correctness and total operating cost—not brand names. This also respects “no archive-and-restart.”
