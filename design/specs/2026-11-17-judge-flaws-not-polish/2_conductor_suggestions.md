# The Conductor's position and suggestions (given to the owner before this review)

## Where the Conductor pushed back on the owner

- Two wind directions is not pedantry. With one direction, a fire that always drifts east passes "driven by the wind".
  Turning the wind once is the cheapest honest test of the owner's own phrase. Keep it. But it is a test-design choice,
  not a bar.
- "Almost nothing burns with the feature off" was a stale summary. The landed control is the reverse (wind off must
  flood), which proves the instrument can see.
- "Never most of the map" is the owner's own "clear before and after regions so scorchfruit have a bed" in other
  words. A fire that takes the whole map leaves no "before".
- The owner's proposed "significantly more abundant fire than vanilla" is weaker in one place: it would pass a map that
  burns hard once and is dead by day three, which is the failure the Designer recorded as this bar's known red. "Never
  out for long" should stay.

## Where the Conductor agreed with the owner

- The loop had no stopping rule that held: 11 looks on one stage. "Could a broken mod still pass?" always has another
  answer.
- Proof details are being treated as bars. Whether a test turns the wind once or twice should never send a mod back.
- Wind-driven fire belongs to all the Baroque Biomes; the owner has now ruled that is a next-version change.

## The Conductor's three suggested rules

1. The judge refuses only for three things: (a) a bar's experience has no scene at all, (b) the test cannot see (it has
   no control that shows the instrument can tell pass from fail), or (c) a player would plainly reject the result.
   Everything else is advice: recorded, never blocking.
2. The Designer writes at most ONE "must not" per bar: the failure actually seen or most likely, not a list of
   imaginable ones.
3. Two looks, then stop: one full look and one check of the fixes. Anything found later is advice.

Stated cost: a subtly wrong mod can pass coverage and only be caught when it is played. The Conductor thinks that is
the right trade under "use it, break it", but it is a real loosening.
