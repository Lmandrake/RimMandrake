# PART B. FINAL SPECIFIC RULINGS

[guess ~90%] These are proposed adoption text. Each numbered item contains one rule sentence and one sentence explaining why. The parenthetical identifies its effect on today’s behavior.

1. **Blocking flaws**  
   A finding may block only when it quotes an existing agreed player outcome or standing rule and shows an observed failure, a missing check of that outcome, missing necessary exposure, a wrong instrument or expectation, or false evidence that affects acceptance.  
   This catches incomplete work while requiring a concrete reason that the agreed outcome has failed or remains untested.  
   *(Tightens refusal quality and loosens acceptance.)*

2. **Non-blocking matters and small corrections**  
   Additional proof strength, unsupported tuning limits, internal implementation preferences, extra showcases, presentation polish and uncertainty about how well the mod will feel in play never block, while inaccurate text receives a local correction unless it affects a required decision or result.  
   This allows a potentially not-quite-right mod to pass because play is needed to discover what “not quite right” means.  
   *(Loosens.)*

3. **Looks and later discoveries**  
   Each release receives one full coverage look and one repair look, after which checks are confined to recorded unresolved flaws, defects demonstrably caused by repairs, and concrete missed flaws confirmed from the deciding evidence by an independent reader, with rewording, re-pinning and releases preserving that history.  
   This ends repeated searches while still catching a repair that breaks something else.  
   *(Loosens exploratory review and tightens review accounting.)*

4. **Designer leaves**  
   A leaf may contain the agreed player outcome, necessary conditions for encountering it, and any independently sourced failure exclusions, with each enforceable clause linked to its authority and all experimental settings and shipped tuning kept in the scene or implementation records.  
   This permits two genuine independent failures to be named while preventing the Designer from silently adding guarantees.  
   *(Loosens enumeration and tightens provenance.)*

5. **Checks and paths to pass**  
   A check must encounter the promised outcome and distinguish its concrete relevant false pass, and a refusal binds only the minimum closing result while offering particular controls, numbers and procedures as suggested methods unless their necessity follows from the requirement.  
   This catches fire immunity without fire and wind tests without wind response while allowing an equivalent inexpensive experiment.  
   *(Tightens relevance and loosens prescribed methods.)*

6. **Non-blocking records**  
   Record each non-blocking finding once in the verdict and the existing Polish record, and have the Conductor and relevant maintainer read consequential entries before the next relevant played session or feature change without automatically creating work, questions or another exam.  
   This keeps observations available without turning the advice list into another gate.  
   *(Loosens and adds a defined reading point.)*

7. **Ratchet alarm**  
   On later reviews, record the count and share of new mandatory demands whose deciding requirement, check and implementation material have no relevant change, and trip the alarm at the first such demand by suspending that demand’s blocking effect until independent evidence review confirms a concrete flaw.  
   This exposes moving conditions without stopping the mod merely because the reviewer may have missed a real defect earlier.  
   *(Tightens demand tracking and loosens automatic blocking.)*

8. **Passing into play**  
   A mod may pass coverage with known non-blocking findings and recorded live checks still unrun where the stage permits that, and its next applicable played session must execute the required live checks and observe the consequential findings through ordinary use, preserving failures and unresolved observations accurately.  
   This accepts imperfect early judgment while turning actual failures into focused fixes and repeatable regression cases.  
   *(Loosens predictive acceptance and tightens follow-through.)*

9. **Independent verification and calibration**  
   A refusal binds only after an independent reader checks its source, deciding evidence, consequence and attainable closing result, and the changed judging process must pass both acceptance and refusal calibration cases on its current brief before issuing binding verdicts.  
   This catches unsupported demands, stale premises and contradictory refusals before they cause another rewrite.  
   *(Tightens.)*
