## The answer

**You are right: mods keep their own state, and all other work goes in one list, with one place to write and one
place to close. The list must be worked by the pipeline's clock, not by a window that has to remember.**

- **The one list exists already: the rimflow ledger.** Of the 24 places the census counted, only four kinds are
  stores (the ledger, each mod's folder, your rulings, the machine readings). The rest are views, or ways round the
  list.
- **The list fails today for two measured reasons.** Work goes round it: handoff pointers, carry items, run results,
  notes on pages, promises typed to you, rulings left in prose. And only the Conductor window works it: 25 of its 48
  debts were written down and then left for over a day, while about 95 helper runs a day ended as a line for that
  one window.
- **Where the push-back stands.** A list is necessary and it is not enough. You gave the reason yourself: it is a
  focus-of-attention problem. A list that one busy window must remember to check is dropped the same way. So the
  list needs a worker that has no attention to lose: the pipeline's tick.

  them to four kinds of store plus views. The study did not say one list would not help: it said one list alone is

- **Ours, three points.** The study and GPT read the pipeline's changes as churn. Your motive is convergence: a
  pipeline that takes over more and needs you less. `plight.md` now says so. The study's wording made agreement sound
  like refusal: it rejected "everything" and a hard stop, and that read as a rejection of one list. And the study's
  "second pair of hands" was late Foundry and the Relentless tick under a new name, and no page said so.

## Your seven questions, answered for the plan

- **Everything, or a threshold?** All work outside a mod goes in the list. A remark that is not work does not. A
  creative wish goes into its mod's direction.
- **A hard stop, against "a fault of form never stops the work"?** No stop. The item's own check, run by the tick,
  says whether the work is done; an agent's note does not.
- **Completeness, proven?** In part, and the page says which part: each need a program can list has a home. A source
  no program reads says "not measured", never zero.
- **The 24 stores.** Four kinds stay. The rest become views, fold into the list, or are deleted (`plan.md`).
- **The Conductor as bottleneck.** The tick works every item that carries a check a program can run. The window
  keeps the calls that need judgement, and those are shown with their age.
- **Mods' records, or one record?** Both, by your split.
- **Creative work.** "The swamp should feel more dead" goes into the mod's direction at once. It becomes list work
  only if it lies outside every mod.
