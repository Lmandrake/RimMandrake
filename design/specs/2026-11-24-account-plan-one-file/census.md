## What the census says

- 21 rows. 9 were hand edits of JSON (rows 3, 9, 10, 13, 15 twice, 16, 18's numbers, 1). 6 needed a code change to say
  what he wanted (rows 2, 7, 9, 14, 18, 19). 4 lived in prose, a timer or a bare file (rows 4, 5, 17, 20).
- Three classes of fault happened more than once:
  - **a stale marker** kept an account out or in (rows 9, 19; and the hand-switch attribution of row 7);
  - **a rule with no field** was forced through another field or done by hand: "switch now when safe" (rows 11, 12, 16),
    a relative line (row 6), a time of day (row 10), an allowance that ends at a reset (row 5), Codex (row 20);
  - **a brake or an allowance with no end and no why** (rows 4, 5, 8).
- What never went wrong from the plan's side: the meter reader (`account_usage.py`) and the drain and account-end
  actuator of 11-20 and 11-24. They stay.
