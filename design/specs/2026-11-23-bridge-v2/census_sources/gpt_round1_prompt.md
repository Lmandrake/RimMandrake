- Verse.Rand: one global stream, state = (uint seed, uint iterations), private StateCompressed ulong, PushState/PopState; seeded from DateTime.Now at process start; we found no place where the state is written to a savegame.
- DebugSettings has: enableStoryteller (gates only Storyteller.MakeIncidentsForInterval; the IncidentQueue still ticks), enableRandomMentalStates (MentalBreaker and social fights), enableRandomDiseases, noAnimals (destroys every spawned animal on its tick), enableDamage, pauseOnError, fastEcology.
