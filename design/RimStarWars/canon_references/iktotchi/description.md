# Iktotchi

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeIktotchi` (verified present in
`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`). Matrix:
`Jawa_AscendantHelix: R` — **rare**, and placed in exactly one faction. Canonically apt: the
Ascendant Helix is the psychic/Force-leaning faction and the Iktotchi are the galaxy's
precognitive species.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Current canon** (`/wiki/Iktotchi`). Iktotchi were **near-humans** from the **moon Iktotch**,
**"distinguished by the two large, curved horns protruding downwards from their heads."**
🔑 **"They had a skin-tone often varying from peach to brown, battering-ram foreheads with
hardened, dense skin, and big, fleshy hands."** Their **blood was red**. Natural habitat
**rocky**. Language **Iktotchese**. Infobox: skin colour **Tan**; `distinctions` = **"Large
cranial horns"** (its only entry); habitat **Rocky**. The article carries `{{Expand|all
sections}}`.

🔴 **The canon infobox has no height, no mass and no lifespan** — all blank. **There is no
canonical Iktotchi height, mass or lifespan to cite.**

**Legends** (`/wiki/Iktotchi/Legends`) supplies all three numbers plus the biology. Infobox:
**height 1.8 meters**; **mass 80 kilograms**; **skin colour Pink**; **lifespan 90 standard
years**; `distinctions` = **Horns · Precognition · Telepathy · Piloting**; origin **Iktotch**
(a **harsh, windy** moon orbiting Iktotchon). Body text:
- 🔑 **"Iktotchi had a very resistant skin which protected them from the violent winds which
  crossed the satellite."** The thick skin has a *reason*.
- 🔑 **"Both males and females had down-curved cranial horns, which gave them an aggressive
  aspect. The males' horns were generally a little larger, a remnant from their
  mountain-dwelling, caprinaen ancestors. The horns were able to regenerate if damaged."**
  Goat-derived, sexually dimorphic, and regenerating.
- 🔑 **"They had limited manual dexterity because their broad hands were equipped with fatty
  digits; their hands were almost 1.5 times the size of a Human hand."**
- **"Iktotchi measured on average 1.80 meters and had a rather significant breadth. Their life
  expectancy was approximately 90 standard years."**

**Unusual abilities (Legends), with the caveat that matters most here.**
- **"Iktotchi had a gift of precognition (direct knowledge or perception of the future) which
  was relatively effective on their native world, but whose capacities became very limited as
  soon as they moved away from the moon."** 🔴 **An off-world Iktotchi is canonically a
  *diminished* precognitive.** Every Iktotchi in this campaign is off-world.
- **"In addition, Iktotchi had powerful telepathic abilities."** Precognition took the form of
  **dreams or visions**, and **"the effectiveness of Iktotchi precognition could be enhanced by
  way of using the Force."**
- **"Iktotchi also had great aptitudes for piloting"** — Saesee Tiin was one of the Order's
  best pilots — **"They were also regularly employed as mechanics or engineers."**

**Behaviour and culture (Legends).** *"In spite of their rather unfriendly appearance, Iktotchi
were sensitive beings who masked their deeper emotions under an appearance of stoicism."*
Respectful of other cultures and able to adapt to them, but **"they seldom interacted with
other races, and when they did, often experienced great difficulty."** Their precognition
**"generally proved a curse, because it often frightened other species and Iktotchi were made
the object of insults,"** and unscrupulous people tried to recruit the gift for crime. 🔑 **"As
a result, Iktotchi were barred from gambling at almost every casino in the galaxy."** Few
trusted them, suspecting them of telepathic spying or of carrying the **Omen Disease**; the
Iktotchi tried to **deny having extrasensory gifts at all.** Historically they were contacted
by the **Kwa**, who taught them rudiments of the Force, and they famously **carved the Republic
seal into a plateau weeks before the Republic's explorers arrived** — a precognition story that
is the species' defining anecdote.

## Visual brief

⚠️ **This is the one species in this batch where the reference images contradict the wiki's own
prose in the def's favour, and the honest answer is that the def's palette is closer to the
pictures than the infobox is.** The infobox says **Tan** (canon) / **Pink** (Legends); the prose
says **peach to brown**. The pictures run from warm tan all the way to **brick orange-red**.

**`wookieepedia_canon_infobox_saesee_tiin.jpg`** (canon infobox, film costume photograph of
Saesee Tiin, 740×1935 — the highest-fidelity reference here):
- **Skin is a warm tan/peach-brown with pink undertones**, and **extremely deeply furrowed** —
  heavy horizontal creases across the brow and long vertical folds down the cheeks. "Hardened,
  dense skin" reads as leather, not as armour plate.
- 🔑 **The cranium swells outward and backward into a broad, smooth, helmet-like dome** that is
  much wider than a human skull at the temples. This is the "battering-ram forehead."
- 🔑 **The horns emerge at the temples and curve forward and DOWN, past the jaw, to chest
  level** — long, thick, tapering, and **a distinctly different colour from the skin: pale
  cream-grey with reddish-brown streaking**, clearly lighter and cooler than the face. They
  frame the whole head and are the largest single element of the silhouette.
- **Completely hairless** — no hair, no eyebrows, no beard.
- **Hands are large, thick and blunt**, with long fleshy tapering digits — visibly bigger than
  human hands, exactly as "big, fleshy hands" / "1.5 times the size of a Human hand" say.
- **Ordinary human build and height** under the robe; the breadth is in the head and hands.

**`wookieepedia_saesee_tiin_fullbody.jpg`** (a second full-body of the same character) confirms
the horn colour independently: **pale grey-cream horns against reddish-tan skin.** The two are
not the same colour, and nothing in the def or its art can express that difference.

**`wookieepedia_legends_infobox.jpg`** (Legends infobox, painted) is at the **red** end:
**brick / reddish-mauve skin with darker rose shading**, snarling with **visible pointed
teeth**, same down-curved cream-tan horns, bald, deeply furrowed, and **long tapering
claw-like fingers**.

**`wookieepedia_ferren_barr.jpg`** (comic) is redder still: **strong orange-red brick skin**,
bald dome, pale horns curving down at the sides, pointed teeth, long clawed fingers.

🔑 **So the constants are: the broad domed cranium; long down-curved temple horns in a PALER,
different hue from the skin; total hairlessness; deep furrows; oversized blunt fleshy hands.
The variable is skin hue, running warm tan/peach → mauve → brick orange-red.**

**`donor_current_sprite.png` is the best of this batch's donor sprites, and it still misses one
specific thing.** It is
`RimMandrakeSW/OR/Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Heads/Iktotchi/Male_Normal_south.png` (512×512, with
male/female × 3 facings and `*m.png` masks):
- ✅ **The horns are there, and they are right in direction** — two down-curved horns springing
  from the temples and hooking forward past the jawline. This is a genuinely recognisable
  Iktotchi read, unlike the Cathar and Duros heads.
- ⚠️ **The cranium is a plain ovoid**, not the broad backswept battering-ram dome; the horns
  are merged into the head outline as one silhouette mass rather than reading as separate
  structures.
- ⚠️ No furrows, no wrinkles, no teeth; the eyes are two ordinary small dots.
- 🔴 **The horns cannot take their own colour.** The base texture is **pure greyscale**
  (measured: chroma spread 0 across all 45,810 opaque pixels) and the head type sets
  `useSkinShader: false` with `shaderType: CutoutComplex` plus the `*_southm.png` mask — but
  that mask is **red-channel only** (measured dominant values `(253,0,0)` and `(255,2,2)`, plus
  a small `(0,0,0)` region; the green channel is empty). With only one live mask channel there
  is **a single tintable colour**, so **the horns render the same hue as the skin.** Canon
  horns are consistently a paler, cooler cream against reddish-tan skin.

  the face.

## Def-versus-canon contradictions (report only — not fixed here)

1. 🔴 **There is no `nameMaker` at all.** Iktotchi is the only one of this batch's five species
   with no namer, and there is **no `RimMandrakeSWNames/…/Iktotchi` name directory** anywhere in
   `src/`. So Iktotchi pawns will be generated with **default RimWorld human names**, where
   canon names are Saesee Tiin, Ferren Barr, Talvee Chawin, Dagio Belcoze, Ax Tagrin. Compare
   Cathar, Duros and Echani, which all have `RSW_KoTOR_Namer<Species>` wired to real files.
2. 🔴 **`PsychicAbility_Enhanced` inverts the one canon caveat that applies to this campaign.**
   Precognition was **"relatively effective on their native world, but whose capacities became
   very limited as soon as they moved away from the moon."** Every Iktotchi the player meets is
   off Iktotch, so canon points at *diminished* ability, not enhanced. (The gene is right that
   the species is psychic — powerful telepathy is unqualified — so this is a direction error,
   not an invention.)
3. 🔴 **Horn colour cannot differ from skin colour.** The head texture is pure greyscale and its
   mask uses only the red channel (green empty), so one tint drives both. Canon horns are
   consistently a paler cream-grey than the reddish-tan face, in every image found.
4. 🔴 **Horn sexual dimorphism and regeneration are unrepresented.** Legends: males' horns
   generally a little larger, and **horns regenerate if damaged.** The male and female head
   textures would be the place for the first; nothing addresses the second.
5. 🔴 **Piloting and mechanical/engineering aptitude are absent — and the def has no Aptitude
   genes at all**, uniquely among this batch's five. Legends `distinctions` lists **Piloting**
   outright and the body text says they were "regularly employed as mechanics or engineers."
6. ⚠️ **`Immunity_Weak` is unsourced.** Nothing found gives Iktotchi weak immunity. The closest
   canon material is the *rumour* that they **carried** the "Omen Disease" — a slander about
   transmission, not a statement about their own immunity. Flag as invented.
7. ⚠️ **`MinTemp_SmallIncrease` is unsourced and arguably backwards.** Iktotch is described as
   a **harsh, windy** moon with **violent winds** and **rocky** habitat, and the species
   descends from **mountain-dwelling** ancestors — none of which suggests heat preference.
8. ⚠️ **`Outland_DeceleratedPregnancy` and `Turn_Gene_FastNeuralHeat` are unsourced.** The
   latter at least sits coherently beside a psychic gene; nothing in either article supports
   either.
9. ⚠️ **`Outland_Evasive` is unsourced as such**, though it is a defensible reading of
   precognition. Recording it as an inference rather than a cite.
10. ⚠️ **"Rather significant breadth" is unrepresented** (`Body_Standard`). Not an error —
    inventing a body-size gene where canon gives no height at all would be worse — but noted,
    since the def's own description calls them "tough in a fight."
11. ✅ **Skin genes are defensible, and this is where the images side with the def.** The wiki
    infoboxes say **Tan** (canon) and **Pink** (Legends) and the prose says "peach to brown,"
    which alone would make `Skin_DeepRed` and `Skin_PaleRed` look invented — but the Legends
    infobox painting and the Ferren Barr comic are plainly **brick and orange-red**, and
    `Outland_Skin_PaleBrown` covers the tan/peach end. **Do not "correct" the reds away on the
    strength of the infobox.**
12. ✅ **Other sourced and correct genes — do not "fix" these:** `Outland_ThickSkin` ("a very
    resistant skin which protected them from the violent winds"; "hardened, dense skin");
    `Hands_Pig` (**"broad hands … fatty digits … almost 1.5 times the size of a Human hand"**
    and **"limited manual dexterity"** — an unusually exact match); `Hair_BaldOnly` +
    `Beard_NoBeardOnly` (hairless in every image, including no eyebrows); `RSW_IktotchiHead`
    (horns present and correctly down-curved); **no lifespan gene** — Legends' 90 years is near
    enough to human that inventing one would be the error, and canon gives none.
