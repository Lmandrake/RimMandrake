# Mon Calamari

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeMonCalamari` (`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`).
⚠️ Its `<nameMaker>` is `RSW_KoTOR_NamerQuarren` — **the Quarren namer, not a Mon
Calamari one**. The two species share a homeworld but are canonically hostile and
have different naming; worth checking whether a Mon Calamari namer exists.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

The Mon Calamari (also Mon Calamarians) were a **humanoid, aquatic species** native to
**Mon Cala**, shared with the **Quarren**. Infobox: class **amphibious humanoid**;
distinctions **"large, goggle-like eyes, high-domed heads, webbed hands"**; habitat
**ocean**; language Mon Calamari. **Height, mass and lifespan are blank — do not
invent them.** Skin colour is a very wide individual menu: **blue, brown, cyan, gold,
green, grey, MOTTLED, orange, pink, red, reddish brown, salmon, white**; eye colour
**blue, gold, gray, green, orange, yellow**.

**Body plan.** Bipedal, amphibious, with **high-domed heads, webbed hands and large,
goggle-like eyes which stuck out from the SIDES of their head**. 🔑 **In addition to
being webbed, the Mon Calamari hand presented THREE SUCTION-CUP-LIKE HOLES on its
palm, and featured five claw-tipped fingers: one opposable thumb, two long middle
fingers, and two very short outer fingers.** **Although they were shaped like
flippers, their feet could nevertheless fit into boots designed for humanoid feet** —
which is the canon licence for putting a Mon Calamari in ordinary RimWorld footwear.
**Females were distinguished from the males by their more prominent chest areas.**

🔑 **Male and female Mon Calamari had BARBELS on their chin. Only having one barbel
was an indication of youth, and Mon Calamari gained more as they aged.** The barbels
**were also used to help detect pressure changes underwater** — so they are a sense
organ and an age marker, not decoration.

**Skin behaviour.** The Mon Calamari's skin **faded from vibrant in their youth to
more worn out as they got older**. **Individuals from the colder polar extremes of
their planet were noticeably less colourful than those from the tropical zones.**

**Unusual abilities — this is where the def is emptiest.**
- **Prolonged periods without exposure to water would result in a Mon Calamari's skin
  drying and peeling, creating a painful bleeding that could be exploited as a form of
  torture.** A real, canonical vulnerability.
- 🔑 **To survive through droughts, a Mon Calamari's body was able to release a
  HIBERNATION SHELL that covered their body, resulting in the Mon Calamari entering a
  state of BIOSTASIS through which they would wait out the drought.** Ecologist
  Elping Tsoo theorized the stasis could also be triggered by **stress or illness**.
  During droughts they would retreat to an exposed sea bed and form a wet muddy
  cocoon around themself and the shell; **the shell retracting over a Mon Calamari in
  a dry environment could be dangerous and result in suffocation.**
- **The Mon Calamari were vigorous swimmers and were capable of living underwater for
  long periods.**
- **They spawned many offspring** — Raddus's grandchildren numbered in the dozens —
  and **because not all spawns were expected to come of age, the Mon Calamari felt
  loss less keenly than humans**, who reproduced comparatively rarely and fewer. The
  early life of a Mon Calamari was known as their **"spawning days."**

## Visual brief

🔴 **The def's own description — "large high-domed heads with goggle-like eyes
sticking out either side" — is accurate as far as it goes, but the images show the
skin is PATTERNED, and the thirteen-entry flat colour list actively misleads.**

**`wookieepedia_the_mon_calamari.png` is the reference of record** (the canon infobox
image: three Mon Calamari, Ackbar centre, full body, transparent background):

- **The dome is TALL and SWEPT BACK**, rising well above the eye line and tapering
  rearward — the tallest part of the silhouette. It is **smooth and rounded, with no
  spike, crest or point.**
- 🔑 **The eyes are on the SIDES of the head, on distinct lateral bulges, and they
  protrude.** Large, round, goggle-like, **amber/orange iris with a big dark pupil and
  visible pale sclera**. They are not front-set like a human's and not on stalks like
  a Rakata's — they sit in bulging sockets on the cheeks/temples. **Front-set eyes are
  the most likely wrong answer from a text prompt.**
- 🔑 **Skin is MOTTLED AND STRIPED, not flat.** Base salmon/orange-red with **darker
  red-brown mottling over the dome and back of the skull**; the rightmost figure in
  this image carries near-**tiger striping** in brown over orange across the dome;
  the leftmost has a distinct **blue-grey patch ringing the eye**. "Mottled" is
  literally one of the canon skin-colour entries, and it is the honest description of
  all three faces here.
- **A wide, thin, DOWN-TURNED mouth** low on the face, under a **heavy pale
  cream/yellow pendulous chin-and-throat sac** that reads almost like a beard.
  **Barbels hang from the chin.**
- **Small paired nostril slits** on a short vertical ridge between the eyes.
- **Ordinary humanoid body, arms and legs**, in boots — consistent with the "feet fit
  humanoid boots" line. Hands are long-fingered and webbed.

**`wookieepedia_moncalamari_swe.jpg`** (a male and female underwater) is the second
reference of record and **adds the thing the infobox image hides**: this pair is
**deep reddish-brown with bold TEAL/CYAN countershading** — a teal mask around the
eyes and over the dome, teal bands down the arms, teal over the belly and inner
limbs. Also visible here: **heavily wrinkled, ridged, muscular skin texture**;
**broad flat webbed FLIPPER feet**; long webbed hands; and the **more prominent chest
on the female**, exactly as the text says. **Two contrasting colours in a defined
arrangement — this is the pattern a single flat skin gene cannot produce.**

**`wookieepedia_mon_cal_hand_holes.png`** is the detail shot and it settles the hand
completely: **five claw-tipped fingers with one opposable thumb, two long middle
fingers and two much shorter outer fingers, webbing between them, and THREE distinct
round suction-cup holes in the palm.** It also gives the best close view of the head
— the lateral protruding eye with its huge dark pupil and pale sclera, the nostril
slits, the down-turned mouth, and **a barbel projecting from the chin**.

**`wookieepedia_moncal_white_calamari.jpg`** is included as the far end of the colour
range (the canon "white" entry) — useful for proving hue really does vary this
widely, and for showing that the *arrangement* of dome, lateral eyes, chin sac and
barbels stays constant while hue does not.

**`donor_current_sprite.png` — the repo's current `RSW_MonCalamariHead` greyscale
mask — is closer than most in this library but has three specific defects.** Being
greyscale is correct and expected (the game tints it at runtime from the skin gene),
so no hue finding above applies to this file. What it gets right: **a tall dome and
two lateral eye bulges**, which is the core silhouette. What is wrong:
- 🔴 **The dome comes to a POINT with a small crest/spike at the crown.** The
  canonical dome is smooth and rounded, swept back with no point. This is a visible,
  fixable error.
- 🔴 **There is NO MOUTH, no chin sac and no barbels.** The barbels are canonically an
  age indicator and a sense organ, and the pale pendulous chin sac is one of the most
  recognisable features of the face. All absent.
- ⚠️ The eyes are rendered as **tiny black dots** in the bulges rather than large
  goggle-like eyes with a big pupil and pale sclera — the opposite of the species'
  headline distinction.

**Xenotype-versus-canon findings:**

- 🔴 **The iconic salmon/red-orange is barely represented.** The gene list has
  `Skin_Orange`, `Outland_Skin_Brown`, `Outland_Skin_PaleBrown`, `RSW_Skin_DarkGreen`,
  `Outland_Skin_PaleBlue`, `Outland_Skin_DeepAzure`. Canon lists **red, salmon and
  reddish brown** — the Ackbar colour, the one every player will recognise — and
  `Skin_Orange` is the only thing close. **`mottled` is also a canon skin-colour
  entry and nothing in a flat-colour gene system can express it.**
- 🔴 **Amphibiousness is entirely absent from the gene list.** The species' *class* is
  "amphibious humanoid"; they breathe water, are vigorous swimmers, and live
  underwater for long periods. No gene in the def touches this. (Same gap as the
  Nautolan def — see that entry.)
- 🔴 **The hibernation shell / biostasis ability is absent**, as is the **skin
  drying-and-peeling vulnerability out of water**. Those are the two most distinctive
  mechanical hooks canon offers for this species and neither is used.
- ⚠️ **`Outland_DeceleratedPregnancy` runs against the text.** Canon Mon Calamari
  **spawn many offspring** at once ("spawning days"; dozens of grandchildren).
  `Outland_EggLayer` and `Fertile` are well-sourced and correct; a *decelerated*
  pregnancy is the odd one out.
- ⚠️ **`KindInstinct` and `Turn_Gene_MotivationHigh` have no canon basis found.**
  Nothing in the article characterises the species as unusually kind. If anything
  the sourced psychological note is the *opposite* register: they **felt loss less
  keenly than humans** because most spawn did not reach adulthood.
- ⚠️ `MinTemp_SmallIncrease` / `MaxTemp_SmallDecrease` are only weakly supportable —
  the one temperature-adjacent line in canon is that individuals **from the colder
  polar extremes were less colourful**, which is a pigment claim, not a tolerance one.
- ✅ Correct and well-sourced: `RSW_MonCalamariHead`, `Hair_BaldOnly` and
  `Beard_NoBeardOnly` (no hair colour in the infobox at all; they have barbels, not
  hair), `Outland_EggLayer` + `Fertile` (spawning, many offspring), `Body_Standard`.
- **Not representable, and worth recording rather than hiding**: the three palm
  suction holes, the 1-thumb/2-long/2-short finger arrangement, flipper feet, the
  chin barbels as an age marker, and the two-tone teal countershading.
