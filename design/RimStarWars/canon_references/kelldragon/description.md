## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kell_dragon/Legends

Kell dragons, also known as kell drakes, were quadrupedal predators sometimes utilized in disposing of captives by crimelords and Dark Jedi.

**Biology and appearance** Kell dragons were hardy creatures related to the much larger krayt dragons indigenous to Tatooine, and like their larger cousins, were most comfortable in deserts. The squat, scaly reptilians walked on four three-clawed legs. Their roughly triangular heads held piercing yellow eyes, and jaws that were filled with sharp teeth that jutted out when their mouths were closed. Three rows of spikes ran down the beasts' backs to the ends of their tails.

The skin of Kell Dragons was extremely tough and highly resistant to most conventional weapons. Lightsabers, however, were extremely effective against them and could kill a Kell Dragon rather quickly with a few hits.

Designation: Non-sentient

Homeworld: Unknown, possibly Ruusan

Fandom: Star Wars

Appearances: Light of the Jedi, Dark Forces, Dark Forces II

Kell Dragons are squat reptiles with a deadly bite. Their backs are dotted with spines and they have sharp claws and their large jaws are filled with even sharper teeth. Their primary form of attack is to lunge forward and bite their prey. They are prized by crime lords and were even kept by the Dark Jedi Jerec as guard animals. They had thick hides and were impervious to nearly everything except being punched in the face by Kyle Katarn.
