# Tusken Raider

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeTusken` (`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`,
label `Tusken`). Assigned `TribeCivil: A` — abundant, and the ONLY race abundant
in that faction — so the Deep Desert Tribes (`04_factions.md` faction 4) read
visually as Tuskens almost exclusively. Art here is high-leverage.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
Tusken Raiders, less formally Sand People or Tuskens, are a nomadic sentient
species indigenous to Tatooine. **Height 1.9 meters (sourced to
*Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings*); mass 89 kilograms (sourced to
*The Force Awakens: Head-to-Head*).** Habitat desert; languages Tusken and
**Tusken Sign Language** (a trade-based sign language used when communicating
with outsiders). Tusken children are called **Uli-ah**. The name "Tusken Raiders"
was acquired due to a period of concerted attacks on the settlement Fort Tusken
around eight decades before 19 BBY.

🔴 **The canon article has NO "Biology and appearance" section at all** — only
Culture, History and Behind the scenes. Every appearance statement below comes
from the Culture section or the infobox, and there is **no sourced statement of
Tusken skin color, eye color, hair or anatomy anywhere in the canon article**
(those infobox fields are empty). Anything about what a Tusken looks like under
the wrappings is therefore unsourced by construction — see the taboo below,
which is why.

**Appearance is clothing, and clothing is sexed and aged.** All Sand People wore
mouth grilles and eye coverings to retain moisture and keep sand out. Males wore
rough wrappings and garments that provided protection and allowed ease of
movement. **In some tribes, females could be distinguished by their elaborate
jeweled masks with eye-slits and torso-covering sand-shrouds.** Tusken children
(Uli-ah) wore **unisex cowls and simple cloaks**, and could not dress like males
or females until they reached adulthood.

🔑 **The unmasking taboo is a hard canon constraint on art.** Their need to
protect their bodies from Tatooine's weather grew into a taboo against exposing
any part of their bodies in public. Tuskens almost never unmasked themselves,
**even in front of one another**; they were forbidden to remove their clothing in
front of others except at childbirth, on their wedding night, and at
coming-of-age ceremonies. **No outsider is known to have seen behind a Tusken's
mask and lived.** A Tusken sprite must never show face, skin or hair.

**Behavior.** Extremely xenophobic and territorial of native resources, often
attacking the outskirts of smaller settlements such as Anchorhead. They viewed
themselves as Tatooine's rightful inhabitants and all non-native species as
trespassers. **All water was sacred and promised to them**, so they raided
colonists' moisture farms; rare water wells such as Gafsa were also sacred, and
simply trespassing near one could provoke immediate violence. They harvested
**black melons** in the Jundland Wastes for a reliable source of milk, and
considered **worrts** a delicacy. Nomadic: clan groups of **20 to 30
individuals**, led by clan leaders, tribal chiefs and warlords. **They traveled
in single file to hide the number of individuals in their party.** Tribal culture
varied greatly — some tribes survived by killing outsiders, others used more
peaceful means; many were known to negotiate, Din Djarin could buy safe passage
with tribute, and **Tuskens were known to always keep their word.** Encampments
were scattered through a region of the Jundland Wastes called The Needles,
guarded by **massiffs**.

Males were the warriors, wielding **gaderffii sticks** (each warrior forges their
own, so **every gaderffii is unique**) and firing **cycler rifles** at vehicles.
Females held a number of roles. At age 15 a Tusken became an adult; boys had to
**slay a krayt dragon and cut out the pearl from its stomach** to gain their
adult role. The dead were burned on a funeral pyre with their weapons.

**The bantha bond is the species' other signature and is nearly mystical.**
During warrior initiation a young Tusken was given a bantha matching their own
gender and learned to care for it, the pair becoming extremely close as the youth
earned a place in the clan. When Sand People married, **their banthas also
mated**; should a rider die, **their bantha usually perished shortly after**. A
bantha that died first had its remains placed in a large graveyard treated with
great respect by Tuskens and other banthas.

**Unusual abilities / practices** (no superhuman physiology is claimed anywhere in
the canon article): Tusken Sign Language; the **gekko** kept by the tribe that
adopted Boba Fett, capable of crawling inside a host being's body and creating
hallucinations, used to guide individuals on spiritual journeys; superstitions
about specific landmarks (avoiding Mushroom Mesa entirely; always firing their
blasters before passing through the B'Thazoshe Bridge).

## Visual brief
🔴 **The single most valuable image here is `wookieepedia_family_male_female_child.jpg`,
and what it shows is that the prose massively undersells the difference between
male, female and child. It is not "a different mask" — it is three different
palettes and three different silhouettes.**

- **Male warrior** — warm **tan/ochre** coarse-woven robe. Head completely wrapped
  in bandage-like cloth strips, over which sits a mask with **two round dark
  lenses in raised metal rims** and a **snout-like mouth grille of stacked
  horizontal ribs** with a projecting central tusk-tube. Layered **tan leather
  plates strapped across the chest**, a wide leather belt with hanging pouches,
  wrapped cloth gloves, gaderffii carried vertically. Palette: tan, ochre,
  saddle-leather brown.
- **Female** — a **completely different figure**: a floor-length **pale
  cream/bone/ivory draped hooded sand-shroud** that covers the entire body head
  to floor in one fall of cloth, over which sits a **tall, rigid, metallic
  (brass/bronze) domed mask plate** with vertical rows of studs or rivets down its
  centre and a narrow eye slit — a vertical plate, NOT a face-shaped mask. A large
  brass/gold disc ornament at the waist. Gloves are yellow-green wrapped cloth.
  The cloth is **bleached pale, not tan** — a female Tusken and a male Tusken read
  as different colors at a glance.
- **Child (Uli-ah)** — small, **olive/sage-green** cowl and cloak, a plain pale
  khaki hood-mask with a single small dark eye slot and no grille, carrying a
  rust-orange leather satchel. **A third distinct palette.**
- 🔴 **The live-action tribe frame contradicts the reference render's palette
  sharply.** `wookieepedia_tribe_bobf_liveaction.jpg` (The Book of Boba Fett) shows
  a whole tribe in **cold, dusty grey-brown to near-black** robes, with masks
  reading as pale bone/grey against dark cloth — nothing like the warm tan/ochre
  of the reference render. Both are canon; the honest conclusion is that **Tusken
  cloth spans warm tan-ochre to cold dusty grey-black, and the on-screen
  live-action population sits at the COLD end.** A sprite pushed to warm tan alone
  will look like the reference book and not like the show.
- **The McQuarrie concept piece preserves details later art loses**, and they are
  worth keeping: **metal pegs/spikes protruding from the crown of the head
  wrappings**; the eye lenses as **two separate round glass discs side by side in a
  single metal frame** rather than goggles; a mouth grille with **downward-pointing
  tubes at each side plus a centre one**; a **silver metal canister at the throat**;
  and an olive-green cloak (a fourth palette datapoint, agreeing with the child's
  olive rather than the male's tan).
- **Consistent across every image**: not one square centimetre of skin, face, hair
  or eye is visible on any individual — exactly as the taboo requires. The mask
  and wrappings ARE the face. Anything below the mask is cloth, leather strap,
  pouch or metal.
- **The mouth grille is the identity feature**, more than the goggles: in all four
  images the ribbed/tubed snout projecting forward and down from the lower face is
  what makes the silhouette read "Tusken" at thumbnail size.

**donor_current_sprite.png is weak evidence and documents a serious gap.** The
only Tusken head art in this repo's `StarWarsRaces` mod is
`SWX/Pawn/HeadType/Sov_tusken/HeadSand{M,F}_{south,east,north}.png`; the south
male is copied here. It is **a plain pale-grey rounded head shape with two tiny
black dots for eyes** — no wrappings, no mask, no metal lens rims, no mouth
grille, no throat canister, nothing. Every feature that makes a Tusken
recognizable is absent, and the grey oval reads closer to a generic skull than to
a Sand Person. It cannot validate anything above. It also means the sexed
appearance (cream-shrouded female vs. tan-wrapped male) is not represented at
all, even though the mod does carry a separate `HeadSandF` file.

## Must show
- [ ] No skin, face, hair or eye visible on any individual — the mask and wrappings ARE the face
- [ ] Male: head fully wrapped in bandage-like cloth strips, mask with two round dark lenses in raised metal rims, and a snout-like mouth grille with a projecting central tusk-tube, over a tan/ochre coarse robe with leather chest plates
- [ ] Female: full-length pale cream/bone/ivory draped hooded shroud covering the entire body, topped by a tall, rigid, metallic domed mask PLATE (not a face-shaped mask) with a narrow eye slit
- [ ] A ribbed/tubed mouth grille projecting forward and down from the lower face is present on the mask
- [ ] Robe/cloth palette spans warm tan-ochre to cold dusty grey-black — not one fixed colour for every individual

## Engine limits
none known — the donor sprite's gap (a plain grey oval with no wrappings, mask, lens rims, grille or throat canister) is recorded as missing head/mask art, not a pipeline constraint; the mod does carry a separate `HeadSandF` file that could carry a distinct female silhouette.

## Candidate images
- `wookieepedia_family_male_female_child.jpg` — **the highest-value image in this
  entry.** The Wookieepedia infobox image (File:TuskenFamily-TSWB.png): an adult
  male, an adult female and an Uli-ah child standing side by side on a
  transparent background, costume-photography quality. It settles the male's
  wrapped head + lensed/grilled mask + leather chest plates, the female's
  cream sand-shroud + rigid studded brass mask plate + waist disc, and the
  child's olive cowl — all in one frame, at the same scale, so proportions are
  directly comparable.
- `wookieepedia_tribe_bobf_liveaction.jpg` — an in-show live-action frame of a
  full tribe outside their tents (The Book of Boba Fett, Chapter 1). **The
  palette-contradiction case**: cold dusty grey-brown/near-black robes against
  the reference render's warm tan. Also shows the tribe as a crowd, useful for
  the "single file / 20–30 individuals" clan scale.
- `wookieepedia_mcquarrie_concept.jpg` — Ralph McQuarrie's original Sand People
  concept painting (with banthas and a starship wreck). Retains the crown pegs,
  twin-disc lenses, side mouth-tubes and throat canister, and puts the cloak at
  olive-green. Concept art, so treat palette as one artist's call — but the
  hardware details are the origin of the design.
- `wookieepedia_warriors_illustration.jpg` — an illustrated piece of Tusken
  warriors mid-action with gaderffii raised; useful for pose/gaderffii silhouette
  rather than palette (it is stylized and heavily backlit).
