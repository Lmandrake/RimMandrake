## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neebray

Neebray mantas, or simply neebray, were a non-sentient species of flying creature originally from Tatooine and found throughout the galaxy, with the young growing up on planets and moons like Tatooine, Rugosa and Rishi Moon, and the gigantic adults living in the void of space like in the Kaliida Nebula, feeding on stellar gases.

**Biology and appearance** Neebray mantas, or simply neebray, were a non-sentient species of flying creature. They had flat limb-less bodies with two large wings, which were partially translucent, and two similar but significantly smaller wings as tail fins. Their head featured a large mouth with a red tongue and sharp teeth and six large eyes which helped them spot potential predators from the high distances. The neebray were distantly biologically related to tibidees&mdash; and as such the Tibidon sand whale&mdash; and mynocks, and originated on the Outer Rim Territories planet Tatooine. Neebray started their lives as tiny bird-sized infant versions of the adults, living in relatively safe nursery environments on Tatooine, and other worlds like Rishi Moon or the coral forests of the moon Rugosa. Neebrays fed on stellar gases. After reaching adulthood, the neebray would leave their homeworlds and begin their long migrations throughout the vacuum of space. Groups of baby neebray would also sometimes venture through space earlier. Adult neebray mantas would became gigantic, with the largest recorded wing span reaching 1,674 meters. Groups of neebray were called a pod. Although usually being peaceful creatures, they could become violent toward starships when they felt provoked or threatened.

Baby neebray had much bigger bodies proportionally to their wings than the adults. Adults also grew a long tendril from the underside of their jaws which was only a stub at the infants. Baby neebray were born with solid skin colors and developed spots on their wings and torsos when they aged, but could also already exhibit spots as infants. Neebray usually had slightly to much lighter-colored bellies. Possible colors included: red with translucent light-blue wings, gray with translucent beige wings, red with translucent red wings, light-orange with translucent light-blue wings, light-blue with translucent beige wings, and pink and purple with translucent pink wings. ### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neebray/Legends

Neebray were flying, limbless creatures which varied greatly in size and habitat. They could be found on many worlds, such as Rugosa, Rishi moon, and Tatooine (where they sometimes circled over Jabba's Palace), and even in space, notably in the Kaliida Nebula along the Balmorra Run.

**Characteristics** On the moon Rugosa, tiny baby neebray flew among the coral forests of the moon's long-dried-up ocean beds as a stop along their interstellar voyage. A moderately large variety had translucent wings, and could be found on a Rishi moon. The gigantic neebray manta lived in the vacuum of space, feeding on stellar gases. They used the Kaliida Nebula as nesting grounds. They also used Veil Nebula as feeding ground, feeding on its stellar gases.
