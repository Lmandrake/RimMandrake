# Greater Krayt Dragon

**defName**: `RSW_GreaterKraytDragon` (SWBestiary port of the donor `GreaterKraytDragon` from `mlie.starwarsanimalcollection`; live art in `src/RimStarWars/GreaterKraytDragonArtOverride`)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
The greater krayt dragon is a large species of krayt dragon, a giant carnivorous
reptile native to Tatooine, a desert planet in the Outer Rim. It is one of at
least two known krayt dragon species (alongside the smaller, more common canyon
krayt dragon); a rare subset of greater krayt dragons keep growing into the
"leviathan krayt dragon" form, a sixteen-limbed, venom-spitting giant.

**Body plan** (per Wookieepedia's "Biology and appearance" section, sourced to
the *Star Wars: Jedi Temple Challenge* Episode 1 (listed by the wiki as a non-canon appearance) and *Ultimate Star Wars*): a long
neck, a long whip-like tail, two horns, sharp teeth, and five pairs of legs
(ten legs total) — it literally "swims" through shifting sand dunes using
those ten legs. It is non-sentient, carnivorous, and hunts large prey
(banthas, dewbacks, rontos, even sarlaccs), ambushing from beneath the sand.
Its body produces a highly acidic venom used both to pre-digest food and,
when threatened, as a projectile spit weapon.

**Greater vs leviathan (do not mix)**: the greater krayt dragon page gives TEN legs (five pairs), two horns and a long whip-like tail. The Mandalorian animal is the LEVIATHAN form (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leviathan_krayt_dragon): sixteen limbs (eight pairs) ending in five clawed digits, a spiny tail, a large head protected by bony armor, length 184 m, height 28.68 m, width 45.6 m (infobox; *Mandalorian Visual Guide* / *Encyclopedia: Comprehensive Guide*); its spit is a greenish-yellow acidic fluid (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krayt_dragon_venom, *Visual Encyclopedia*) that also pre-digests its food. Only very few greater dragons grow into leviathans. The owner ruling below (7-8 pairs) matches the leviathan.

**Coloration/armor**: notably, neither the "Greater krayt dragon" nor the
general "Krayt dragon" Wookieepedia infobox fills in a skincolor field at all
— the wiki's prose gives no explicit color statement. This is exactly the
kind of gap the reference-image process exists to catch: text alone would
undersell or leave ambiguous what the design actually looks like, so the
candidate images below (not the prose) are the primary color/texture
authority for this creature.

**Canon role and appearances across media**: A dead juvenile greater krayt
dragon's skeleton is the famous landmark C-3PO stumbles across in the Dune
Sea in *Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope* (1977) — the species' first-ever
appearance, skeleton only. That prop skeleton was built from fiberglass
sauropod-replica bones originally made for a 1975 Disney film and left behind
in the Tunisian desert after filming. The skeleton/pearl-hunting angle recurs
across many Legends and canon sources (games, comics, visual guides).
The species' first live-action, on-screen appearance as a living creature is
*The Mandalorian* Season 2 premiere, "Chapter 9: The Marshal" (2020): a
massive leviathan-stage greater krayt dragon terrorizes the town of Mos Pelgo
and nearby Tusken Raiders; Din Djarin and Cobb Vanth team up (with Tusken
help) to kill it by having Djarin ride a live bantha rigged with
explosives, with Djarin holding the live bantha's reins as the dragon swallows both of them, then jetpacking out
through its mouth to detonate the explosives. Its cry/call is loud enough to be recognized and feared by
Tusken Raiders — this is also referenced earlier in Star Wars lore (Obi-Wan
Kenobi mimics a krayt dragon's cry to scare off Tuskens threatening Luke in
*A New Hope*, established in the general "Krayt dragon" article, not unique
to the greater subspecies but drawing on the same call). Krayt dragon pearls
found in its body are a valuable in-universe commodity. Other canon
appearances include *Star Wars: Commander*, *Star Wars Battlefront*/*Battlefront
II* (skeleton), *Star Wars Outlaws* (a live leviathan-stage individual),
*The Book of Boba Fett*, and *Star Wars: Hunters* (skeleton), among others.

## Visual brief
The candidate images (below) show a design consistent across its one live
on-screen canon incarnation (The Mandalorian) and matching the text's stated
distinctions (long neck, long tail, teeth, multiple stocky legs):

- **Color**: warm sandy tan / ochre-brown all over, close in hue to the
  Tatooine sand itself — clearly a desert-camouflage palette, not a
  "near-black" or saturated color. No green, blue, or other saturated hues
  appear anywhere on the living creature in either Mandalorian-sourced image.
- **Skin texture**: not smooth reptile scale — the images show a heavily
  weathered, cracked, almost rock-/bark-like hide, with small pebbly/nodular
  bumps along the back and a row of low blunt dorsal spikes/ridges running
  down the spine and tail (visible clearly in `wookieepedia_1.jpg`). This
  reads more like eroded sandstone or a crocodilian's osteoderm plating than
  classic "armor plates" — it is textured and ridged, but not a hard
  glossy-black chitin shell.
  Note: the mod montage's cartoon icon (`donor_workshop_screenshot.jpg`)
  simplifies this into a smooth flat olive-grey/khaki silhouette with a row of
  small triangular back spikes — a much lower-detail stylization than the
  Mandalorian design, but it agrees on the same broad claim (spiky dorsal
  ridge, no bright colors, horned head).
- **Head/mouth**: an enormous elongated jaw packed with many long, narrow,
  backward-curving conical teeth; visible nostril ridges; the film-still
  image (`mandalorian_still_1.jpg`) shows it mid-roar/spit with a bright
  yellow-white acid stream issuing from the mouth — the one strong saturated
  color on an otherwise monochrome-tan creature.
  The montage icon gives it two curved horns and a long serrated dorsal frill
  running from head to tail — consistent with the "two horns" the text notes,
  though the live-action design's horn/head silhouette is less exaggerated
  than the icon's.
  The Mandalorian model's mouth is confirmed to open extremely wide (large
  enough for a man to be swallowed and escape via jetpack, per the text) — the
  still image is consistent with that scale.
- **Body/limbs**: thick, heavily-muscled, short-clawed legs, more limbs than a
  typical quadruped (the extra pairs are somewhat obscured by dust/motion in
  the still but visible as multiple leg pairs in the clean render); a long
  tail that curls rather than dragging straight.
- **Skeleton (A New Hope landmark)**: `wookieepedia_skeleton_1.jpg` shows the
  bleached bone remains half-buried in a dune — a long sinuous articulated
  spine and a single visible skull, bones the same pale sand tone as the dune
  itself. This is the film-prop skeleton, not informative about living
  coloration, but establishes the "huge sinuous desert-dweller" silhouette and
  its status as an iconic Tatooine landmark.
- **No disagreement worth flagging between the two live-creature images** —
  both are the same Mandalorian VFX design (one a clean promotional render,
  one an in-scene film still), and they agree on palette and texture. The one
  real disagreement is stylization level between the photoreal Mandalorian
  design and the mod's own flat-cartoon icon substitute, which is expected
  and noted above rather than treated as a canon conflict.

**Bottom line for future art prompts**: sandy tan/ochre, not black or
saturated; a weathered, cracked, pebbly/ridged hide with a low spiked dorsal
ridge rather than glossy plate armor; enormous many-toothed jaw; ten legs
across five pairs; long curling whip-tail; huge (building/vehicle scale) as
established by the Mandalorian sequence and its ability to swallow a person.
