## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reek

Reeks were large, muscular quadrupeds native to the planet Ylesia that could also be found on Saleucami and the Codian Moon.

**Biology and appearance** Reeks were powerful and territorial mammals with a single central horn atop their head and a cheek horn on either side. They lived on grass plains and turf and had powerful hind limbs for charging. According to the Mandalorian Aran Tal, the creatures had an overwhelming smell.

Though naturally herbivores, a starved reek could become a ravenous meat-eater and change its skin color to a deep red, while on other worlds they could be brown, gray, or sometimes yellow. Those bred in the Petranaki Arena were purposely fed meat in order to increase their overall aggression, and were equipped with nose rings to be used by handlers. Their sprawling posture and immense weight made them relatively slow-moving. They sported two large cheek horns that could be used for head-locking in combat with another reek as a show of dominance within a herd; these fights could end in the death of one of the reeks.

Reeks from the Codian Moon lived in small herds.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reek/Legends

The reek was a large thick-skinned, horned, rhino-esque quadruped, native to Ylesia. Subspecies could be found on Iridonia, Kashyyyk and Ithor. They could also be found on Tatooine and in ranches on the Codian Moon and Saleucami.

**Biology and appearance** The massive bull-like Reeks were even-toed ungulates, herbivores by nature, but they were often fed meat for exhibition sport and executions. This turned their leathery skin crimson and made the reeks much more aggressive. Reeks were unable to survive on only meat, so they were given just enough plant matter to keep them healthy. This method was used by the Geonosians for their Execution Arena reeks.

These stout brutes had tough leathery skin which in all reeks, including subspecies, were normally brown in color and only changed depending on their diet. The mottling identified the subspecies. Their species could be affected by the Rakghoul plague.

Reeks were usually found in herds and resided in the mossy grasslands and were very protective over their chosen area of wood-moss turf. They were very protective of their herds, fighting violently with intruders using their horns. Used in displays of dominance in the wild, they could gore opponents, and few would be able to survive a trampling attack from a reek. They horn-teeth grew continuosly. Its strong jaws, used to chop tough wood-moss chunks into pieces, could rend flesh and snap bones easily. They used their front claws for digging up wood-moss and because their cheek horns grew continuously, reeks would rub their horns on trees and rocks to shorten and sharpen them. The horns of male reeks are usually larger than those of females, and they display them to attract a mate. During mating season, male reeks battled each other in ramming contests for the choice females among their herds.

While reeks were not terribly fast, they were intelligent. If given patient, gentle training, they could make excellent pack animals.
