## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Puffer_pig

Puffer pigs were a non-sentient, herbivorous, mammalian pig species native to the planet Kyryll's World. Puffer pigs were known to inflate their bodies to a large degree when they felt threatened.

**Biology and appearance** Puffer pigs were non-sentient mammalians. Part of the pig species, they were herbivorous. Their homeworld was the planet known as Kyryll's World. They lived in mines and amongst rocks and were capable of sniffing out valuable minerals with the efficiency of a dozen mining scanners, making them very valuable to smugglers and miners. The Mining Guild was known to pay very well for the animals.

When frightened, puffer pigs would inflate their bodies to massive proportions with the intent of deterring any predators  and were capable of multiple size increases if further threats occurred.  An uninflated puffer pig measured slightly less than one meter long and a little more than half a meter tall, but when inflated, they could grow up to nine meters in length and sixty centimeters in height.

Puffer pigs communicated using low squeals.
