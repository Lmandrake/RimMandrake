# Kel Dor

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeKelDor` (`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`,
verified present). Matrix placements: `Jawa_AscendantHelix: R`, `OutlanderCivil: R` — rare in
both, so this is a face seen occasionally, not a crowd species.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
Kel Dors were a sentient species native to the planet **Dorin**. The canon infobox gives
**no height, no mass and no lifespan** — all three are unsourced in canon and are recorded
here as absent rather than guessed. Skin colour is sourced three ways: **brown**, **orange**
and **yellow**. Hair colour and eye colour are **both blank in the canon infobox**. The one
listed distinction is *"rebreathers to survive in oxygen environments."* Diet is unsourced.
Language: Kel Dor.

Biology: the Kel Dor were **a tall, humanoid species that possessed three fingers and a thumb
on each hand** — four digits, not five. Their bodies were covered by a **thick, leathery
hide** that **allowed them to survive exposure to the vacuum of space for a time.** They wore
masks to filter the air when offworld **because oxygen was poisonous to them**, and they
**also wore goggles to protect their eye fluids from evaporating** — two separate functional
reasons, one per component. Genetically they were **distantly related to the Gand and the
Tognath**, who similarly wear breath masks offworld.

Society: clan-based. **The antiox breath masks were decorated with patterns unique to each
clan** — so mask ornament is a clan signal, not personal taste, and a squad of Kel Dor should
not all wear the same plate. They were represented in the Galactic Senate, whose Kel Dor
delegation rooms were equipped so antiox masks were unnecessary — i.e. a Kel Dor *does*
appear unmasked indoors on a sealed Kel-Dor-atmosphere world. Greeting: "Koh-to-yah!"

The canon article carries `{{Species-stub}}` and has no size figures, so **`Kel Dor/Legends`
was pulled as well** and is marked as Legends throughout: height **1.4–2 metres**, lifespan
**70+ standard years**, skin **peach to crimson red**, eyes **black**, with **some born with
silver irises, a mark often seen as an affinity for the Force.** ⚠️ That is the only
Force-related biology in either article, and it is (a) Legends and (b) a **minority** marker
tied to one iris colour — **not a species-wide psychic trait.** Legends also notes the species
became particularly adept at designing advanced environmental and life-support systems,
precisely because of its own physiological needs.

## Visual brief
**Owner ruling 2026-10-08 (verbatim): "That's realistic, not these cartoon versions you keep using. Replace the canon with something more realistic. The web is full of them."**
Added realistic references: `wookieepedia_plo_koon_prosthetic_unmasked.jpg` (behind-the-scenes photo, *Star Wars
Insider* 117, of actor Alan Ruscoe in the Plo Koon prosthetic head with the mask off), `wookieepedia_male_female_painted.png`
(realistic painting of a male and a female Kel Dor, *Force and Destiny Core Rulebook*) and
`wookieepedia_tarast_voon_painted.png` (realistic painted Kel Dor Jedi, *Force and Destiny Beginner Game*). Nothing was
deleted: **the comic `wookieepedia_unmasked_legends.jpg` is kept as the ONLY image of the true unmasked lower face
(tusks, mouth, eyes)** — no realistic one exists (searched: Kel Dor and Kel Dor/Legends page images, "Images of Kel
Dors", "Images of Plo Koon"). Render that anatomy realistically, never in its inked style.

🔴 **What the prosthetic photo does and does not show.** It is the real film makeup: it confirms the **deeply
furrowed, glossy orange-tan dome, the heavy brow, and the pebbled, cratered side-lobe clusters** in physical
material. But **below the brow it is the actor's own human face** — human eyes, nose and smiling teeth. Do NOT take
the eyes, nose or mouth from it; those come from the comic (small dark eyes, no nose, tusks).

🔴 **The single most important thing in this entry: the tusks are FACE, not equipment.**
`wookieepedia_unmasked_legends.jpg` (Plo Koon with the mask off) shows **two long, pale
ivory tusks curving downward and outward from the sides of the mouth** — they are part of the
head, they are the same material as teeth, and they are **completely hidden by the antiox
mask in every masked image.** Prose never mentions them. A sprite artist working from text
alone will not put them there.

What the **face** is, from the unmasked image:
- **A bald, bulbous, high-domed cranium** in warm orange-tan, with **deep engraved furrows and
  crevasses** running over the whole skull — not smooth skin, not wrinkles-around-the-eyes,
  but a full network of channels across the crown and cheeks.
- **A cluster of fleshy, pebbled lobes on each side of the head** where a human ear would be —
  irregular blobby masses, several per side, not a smooth pointed flap.
- **Small, dark, close-set eyes** with no visible sclera to speak of, set beneath heavy
  furrowed brows. The eyes are *small* relative to the head.
- **No projecting nose.** The mid-face is a fluted mass of vertical folds running down to the
  mouth; there is no nose shape to draw.
- **A narrow mouth with fine vertical striations** (reading almost gill-like) between the
  two tusks.

What the **equipment** is, from `wookieepedia_infobox_masked_plo_koon.jpg`:
- **A single tan/bone-coloured rigid plate assembly** strapped over the crown, combining
  (a) **two dark angular goggle lenses over the eyes** and (b) **a central snouted respirator
  plate over the mouth and nostril area**, with **decorative side flanges** flaring out over
  the temple lobes. The goggles and the respirator are one assembly in the render, but they
  are two functions — cite them separately, because the Senate-rooms detail means an unmasked
  Kel Dor is a legitimate thing to draw.
- **The mask hides the tusks, the mouth, the nose-fold mass and the eyes entirely.** Roughly
  the top third of the cranium and the side lobes stay visible above and around it.
- ⚠️ **The unmasked reference is Legends continuity** (`PloKoonUnmasked-STD.jpg`, a comic
  panel). The **canon** article contains **only masked images** — both `Plokoon_detail.png`
  and `Steadfast-WotF.png` are masked. So the face described above is the best available
  reference but is not canon-sourced, and it is drawn in a flat comic style; treat proportion
  as reliable and exact hue as the colourist's. The realistic prosthetic and the two paintings agree with it on the
  dome, furrows and lobes, and give the hue as **warm orange-tan to deep copper**, the skin glossy and leathery.
- The female in `wookieepedia_male_female_painted.png` is **redder (rust-copper)** and her side lobes hang lower,
  like braided masses; her mask is dark metal rather than bone — mask colour varies by individual.

**Hands.** The masked reference settles them: **long, slender, clawed digits — three fingers
and a thumb — with dark, sharply pointed claws**, held together in the render so the count is
readable. Matches the sourced "three fingers and a thumb."

**Body and dress.** `wookieepedia_vacuum_survival.jpg` (a card-art piece), the two paintings and the infobox all
put the Kel Dor in **long, heavy, full-length robes with wide sleeves**, tall and narrow in
silhouette. Nothing in either image contradicts "tall"; nothing in either image supports a
*heavy* build — the read is tall and lean, which is what the 1.4–2 m Legends range plus
"typically of the same weight as Humans" says.

### Def-versus-canon (flagged — not fixed here)
- 🔴 **`<description>` is the single character `.`** — no description ships for this xenotype.
  (Identical defect to `RSW_RimMandrakeGand`, found by the previous batch.)
- 🔴 **`nameMaker` is `RSW_KoTOR_NamerDuros`** — the **wrong species' namer.** Kel Dor naming
  is documented and distinctive (short, usually monosyllabic given names; surnames from
  ancient trades, e.g. Koon = "explorer"), and `RSW_KoTOR_NamerKubaz`/`…NamerMimbanese` show
  per-species namers do exist in this mod. A Kel Dor will be generated with Duros names.
- 🔴 **The species' defining biology — oxygen is poisonous to it — is not in the gene list at
  all.** The mask is present only as `RSW_HeadAttachment_keldormask`, a cosmetic head
  attachment. There is no gene expressing the atmospheric requirement, and none expressing
  the sourced **vacuum-survival** hide. Meanwhile `ToxicEnvironmentResistance_Partial` exists
  in this mod (the Kubaz def has it), so the vocabulary is available.
- 🔴 **No Kel Dor head type exists.** `Head_Gaunt` is the head gene, and there is **no
  `HeadType/keldor` directory on disk** (compare `HeadType/kubaz`, `HeadType/gand`). So a Kel
  Dor is rendered as a generic gaunt humanoid head with a mask laid over it — **the tusks, the
  furrowed cranium and the lobe clusters are not authored anywhere.**
- ⚠️ **`PsychicAbility_Enhanced` + `Turn_Gene_LatentPsychic` + `Turn_Gene_MeditationNeed`
  are unsourced as species-wide.** Canon says nothing about Kel Dor Force sensitivity as a
  species; the only support is a Legends line that *some* individuals born with silver irises
  were seen as having Force affinity. Three psychic genes on every pawn overstates it.
- ⚠️ **`Hands_Pig`** against a sourced **three fingers and a thumb** with long claws. Whatever
  `Hands_Pig` renders, the canon hand is a four-digit clawed hand and the masked reference
  shows it clearly.
- ⚠️ **`RSW_Skin_PaleOrange` is the only skin gene**, but canon sources **brown, orange and
  yellow** and Legends adds **peach to crimson red**. A single pale-orange gene collapses a
  genuinely wide sourced range.
- ⚠️ **`RSW_Ears_weirdflap`** renders (see `donor_current_sprite_headflaps.png`) as **two
  smooth, pointed, symmetrical flaps.** The canon side structures are **irregular clusters of
  pebbled lobes.** Closest available, but not right.
- ⚠️ Unsourced: `Turn_Gene_FrailDigestion`, `AptitudeStrong_Social`, `Outland_Evasive`,
  `RSW_lifespan_double` (Legends gives **70+ years**, i.e. roughly human, not double).
  `RSW_BodySizeGene_big` is the one size gene that **is** supported ("a tall race").
