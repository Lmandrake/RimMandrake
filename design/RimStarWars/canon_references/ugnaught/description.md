# Ugnaught

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeUgnaught` (verified in
`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml:2041`).
Assigned `Jawa_Junkers: S`, `Jawa_FreeDroidEnclaves: S`, `OutlanderCivil: R`,
`Jawa_HuttCartel: R` — four factions, more than any other species in this batch. That is
the right shape for canon: Ugnaughts are the galaxy's tradesmen and they turn up
everywhere someone needs machinery kept alive.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Canon article.** Ugnaughts were **porcine, sentient humanoids** native to **Gentes**,
*"considered by many to be among the (if not the) hardest-working species in the
galaxy."* Infobox: class **porcine humanoids**; **skin colour "varying shades of pink"**;
**hair colour gray, white**; **eye colour black, brown, green**; **lifespan
"centuries"**; origin Gentes; **habitat desert or wastelands**; language Ugnaught.
🔴 **No height and no mass in the canon infobox** — both fields are empty.

Biology, verbatim substance: **"Ugnaughts were diminutive, porcine humanoids who had
pink skin, upturned noses, white hair, and thick layers of jowls."** **"Some had tusks
that they used in blood duels."** **"They were strong and resilient, and their life-spans
reached up to more than 200 standard years."** **Ugnaught children were known as
"Ugletts."** Cloud City Ugnaughts ate **genteslugs and various molds and fungi** grown
in dark side tunnels. Their **natural habitat was desert or wastelands** — directly
relevant to a desert-world campaign.

Behaviour, and it is unusually specific and mechanical: **"The Ugnaughts divided
themselves into a tribal hierarchy."** 🔑 **"They favored the color red, the presence of
which tended to improve their work productivity."** **"Accusing their work of
malfunctioning was considered an insult among Ugnaughts."** **"It was common for
Ugnaughts to end a statement with the phrase 'I have spoken'"** when their mind was made
up. Din Djarin, in-universe: *"The stories of Ugnaughts' skill with smithing droids are
legendary. We know that Ugnaughts are considered the hardest working species in the
galaxy."* History: endemic to Gentes but **many were sold into slavery** and taken
offworld as indentured servants; the Cloud City population was hired to *build* the city
and was **gifted several of its large lower levels** in reward, which they held in
gratitude thereafter.

**Legends article** (marked Legends, not canon): height **1 to 1.6 meters**; mass
**43 kilograms**; skin **varying shades of pink**; hair **white or orange**;
distinctions **"dwarf-sized porcine humanoids"**; lifespan **approx. 200 years**. Body
text: **porcine humanoids with slightly upturned noses**, whose height "compared to
baseline Humans would be considered short or dwarfed"; **"quite hardy, able to withstand
long periods of discomfort."** Legends also details the culture the canon page only
gestures at: rule by elected **terend councils** led by elected **ufflor** officers;
life lived by one's **"blood profession,"** taught parent to child; infants called
**Ugletts**; and when too many Ugnaughts entered one profession, a **blood duel** was
called — **at the twentieth year of age, fought to the death**, the victor inheriting the
trade. *"Despite this outdated and violent custom, the Ugnaughts were generally a
peaceful people with a rich culture."* Etiquette: bow silently and wait for a **guttural
purring**; the common greeting "yaa-yaah" is **a personal insult**, after which one
should expect thrown tools. **Alza'ran** was a common Ugnaught name.

**Unusual abilities.** None supernatural. What canon actually gives is **very long life
(200+ years, "centuries")**, **strength and resilience out of proportion to size**, and
an extreme **aptitude for smithing, machinery and droid work.** The tusks are a
**duelling** feature, not a weapon system.

## Visual brief

⚠️ **The infobox's "pink skin" is Legends colour asserted over canon art, and the
live-action canon Ugnaught is not pink.**

- `wookieepedia_kuiil_infobox.jpg` — the canon infobox image, Kuiil from *The
  Mandalorian*, full figure, evenly lit, transparent background. His skin is a
  **weathered grey-tan / dun brown**, warmest and ruddiest across the snout and around
  the eye sockets, cooling to grey-brown over the leathery crown and jowls. There is a
  reddish undertone, but calling it "pink" produces the wrong colour.
- `wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg` — the Legends plate, and **this one IS pink**: a clear
  ruddy mauve-pink face. So the two continuities genuinely differ, and the infobox is
  quoting the Legends value while illustrating with the canon figure.
- `wookieepedia_cloud_city_group.jpg` — *Empire Strikes Back*, two Ugnaughts on Cloud
  City's furnace level. ⛔ **Do not sample skin from this one**: the whole plate is
  drenched in orange furnace light, and the faces read hot salmon-orange purely because
  of it.

**Proportions, measured off `wookieepedia_kuiil_infobox.jpg`** (500×1320): crown of the
leather cap ≈ y10, chin ≈ y250, sole ≈ y1290. Head ≈ 240 px against a standing height of
≈ 1280 px, i.e. **the head is about 19% of total height** where a human is ~13%. Combined
with Legends' 1–1.6 m, that is the classic **dwarf build: oversized head, barrel chest,
broad shoulders, thick short limbs, large hands, short legs.** Kuiil is *wide* — his
shoulder span is close to a third of his height. **Do not draw a scaled-down human.**

**The face, from Kuiil (live-action, the reference of record):**

- **A broad, flat, upturned pig snout occupying the middle third of the face**, with two
  large forward-facing nostrils, rising to merge into the brow with no bridge. This is
  the single identifying feature and it is **prominent, not small**.
- **Heavy overhanging brow ridge with a deep vertical furrow** between the eyes.
- **Small, deep-set eyes** — hazel-green on Kuiil, matching the infobox's green option.
- **A wide, downturned mouth with a protruding lower lip and visible lower teeth**
  reading as small blunt tusks; the canonical "tusks" are lower, and modest.
- **Thick jowl folds** running from the snout down past the corners of the mouth, plus
  heavy neck folds — the "thick layers of jowls" text is doing real work.
- **Skin is deeply wrinkled and leathery over the entire head**, crown included.
- 🔑 **Hair is white and it is LONG, in two specific places**: heavy **side-whiskers /
  mutton chops sweeping back from the cheeks past the jaw**, and a **chin beard**. The
  crown is bald. `wookieepedia_cloud_city_group.jpg` confirms this on two more
  individuals and adds a **long stringy fringe hanging from the sides and back of an
  otherwise bald pate**.
- 🔑 **Ears are large, pointed and stand out from the head** — clearly visible on the
  left figure in the Cloud City still. Kuiil's are hidden under his leather flight cap,
  so a Kuiil-only reference would miss them entirely.

**Dress, consistent across all three canon images:** heavy layered workwear — leather
apron or jerkin, tool belts, satchels, thick gloves, heavy boots. Ugnaughts are drawn as
**tradesmen in working gear**, never in robes. That is apparel rather than anatomy, but
it is what makes the silhouette legible at a glance.

🔴 **No `donor_current_sprite.png`: the repo has no Ugnaught art of any kind.** A search
of `src/` for any texture path matching *Ugnaught* returns **nothing** — no head, no
body, no hair, and not even a xenotype icon. The def's `iconPath` is
`UI/Icons/Xenotypes/Custom/CustomXenotypeIcon9`, a **generic vanilla RimWorld
custom-xenotype glyph**, where the other three species in this batch each have a real
`RimMandrakeSW/OR/OuterRim/XenotypeIcons/Xenotype_<Species>` icon. The species is
assembled entirely from generic genes, so every correction below lands on a **gene
choice or a missing gene**, and the snout/jowl/ear silhouette above has no art to land
on at all.

🔴 **Repo def versus canon — findings, in descending order of how wrong they are.**

1. **`nameMaker` is `RSW_KoTOR_NamerDevaronian` — Ugnaughts are being given Devaronian
   names.** This is a straightforward wrong-species wiring, not a judgement call. Canon
   names on record: **Kuiil**; Legends records **Alza'ran** as a common Ugnaught name.
2. **`<description>.</description>` — the def's player-facing description is a single
   period.** Every other def in this batch (Kaminoan, Umbaran, Zeltron) carries real
   prose. This is the text a player reads in the xenotype panel.
3. **`Hair_ShortOnly` contradicts the canon look.** The defining hair feature is **long**
   — sweeping mutton-chop side-whiskers, a chin beard, and a long stringy fringe around a
   bald crown. Short-only hair removes the species' most recognisable non-snout feature.
   (`RSW_Beard_Chops` + `Beard_Always` + `Hair_SnowWhite` are, by contrast, exactly
   right, and `Hair_SnowWhite` is well sourced — though Legends also allows **orange**.)
4. **No skin gene at all**, so Ugnaughts render in baseline human skin tones. Canon is
   either **dun grey-brown** (live-action Kuiil) or **pink** (infobox + Legends);
   neither is a human tone. This is the gap most responsible for how they read in game.
5. **`RSW_lifespan_double` undershoots canon.** Canon says **"centuries"** and **"more
   than 200 standard years"**; Legends says ~200. Doubling RimWorld's baseline lands near
   160.
6. **`RSW_Nose_SmallPig` names the right feature but the wrong size** — the canon snout
   is broad and dominant, occupying a third of the face.
7. ⚠️ **`AptitudeTerrible_Melee` sits awkwardly with a duelling culture.** Canon gives
   them **tusks used in blood duels**, and Legends has profession-inheritance duels
   fought **to the death**. `AptitudeTerrible_Shooting` is unobjectionable — nothing
   depicts an armed Ugnaught — but terrible melee is a claim the sources push back on.

Well founded and worth keeping on record so a later pass does not "fix" them:
`RSW_BodySizeGene_smaller` (Legends 1–1.6 m, "dwarf-sized"), `Body_Hulk` + `Jaw_Heavy` +
`RSW_Face_jowls` (barrel build, heavy jaw, "thick layers of jowls"),
`AptitudeRemarkable_Construction` (they built Cloud City), and
`RSW_statgene_compliant` + `Turn_Gene_Certain` + `Turn_Gene_MotivationHigh` (the
hardest-working species in the galaxy). **Not represented anywhere and the most
interesting hook in the article: red improves their work productivity.**

## Must show
- [ ] Skin is a weathered grey-tan/dun brown (the canon live-action Kuiil), not pink — pink is the Legends-only value
- [ ] Broad, flat, upturned pig snout occupying the middle third of the face, prominent rather than small, merging into the brow with no bridge
- [ ] Heavy overhanging brow ridge with a deep vertical furrow, and small, deep-set eyes
- [ ] Wide, downturned mouth with a protruding lower lip and small blunt lower tusks visible
- [ ] Long white hair worn only as mutton-chop side-whiskers and a chin beard, with a bald crown — not ordinary short hair
- [ ] Large, pointed ears standing out from the head, on an oversized-head dwarf build (thick short limbs, barrel chest, broad shoulders)

## Candidate images

- `wookieepedia_kuiil_infobox.jpg` — **the reference of record.** The canon infobox
  image: Kuiil, full figure, transparent background, even lighting, live-action
  fidelity. Settles the dun grey-brown skin, the broad upturned snout with forward
  nostrils, the heavy furrowed brow, the small deep-set green eyes, the protruding lower
  lip with blunt tusks, the jowl and neck folds, the leathery wrinkling, the white
  mutton chops and chin beard, and the dwarf proportions measured above. ⚠️ His flight
  cap **hides the ears**, so this image alone is not sufficient.
- `wookieepedia_cloud_city_group.jpg` — *Empire Strikes Back* still, two Ugnaughts.
  **The only source here for the large pointed ears**, and it adds the long stringy
  fringe around a bald crown and a snout seen in profile. ⛔ **Negative reference for
  colour** — the orange furnace lighting makes the skin read salmon, which is a lighting
  artefact and nothing else.
- `wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg` — the Legends plate. Kept because it is **where the
  "pink" comes from**: a ruddy mauve-pink face, long flowing white hair well past the
  shoulders, big snout and visible tusks, in working smock and apron. Legends design, so
  a **variant, not the target** — but it is the evidence that the pink/dun split is a
  real continuity difference rather than a lighting error.
