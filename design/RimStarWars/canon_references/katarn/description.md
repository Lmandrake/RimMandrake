## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Katarn

Katarns were dangerous and deadly predators that were native to the planet Kashyyyk, where they prowled the deeper, lightless levels of the world's complex vertical ecosystem. The imposing Katarn-class Commando Armor worn by clone commandos of the Grand Army of the Republic, was named for the deadly creatures, and the armor's capabilities lived up to the name.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Katarn/Legends

A katarn was a predatory quadruped reptomammal native to the planet Kashyyyk.
