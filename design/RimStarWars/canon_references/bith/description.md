**Homeworld naming trap:** in **canon** the Bith homeworld is the planet **Bith** (same name
as the species). In **Legends** it is **Clak'dor VII**. Both are correct in their own
continuity; the repo def uses the canon form.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Canon.** The Bith were a **peaceful and intelligent** sentient species native to the planet
Bith. They had **hairless, domed heads and black, glassy eyes that lacked eyelids and could
not shed tears.** They often worked as **musicians, scientists, and engineers**, and had
**very sensitive hearing, being able to separate and isolate sounds** around them. **Because
Bith had no noses, they smelled through special organs beneath the skin flaps on their
faces.** Canon infobox: subspecies **Y'bith**; **skin colour cream, green, orange, pink, or
white**; **eye colour black**; distinctions **large domed heads**; origin Bith. 🔴 **Height,
mass, and lifespan are all unsourced in canon** — those infobox fields are empty. There is
no hair colour field value, consistent with hairlessness.

Biology and appearance, canon body text: *"Generally considered to be 'weird' by other
species,"* the Bith's most distinguishing features were their **large eyes, high cranial
bone, and lack of a prominent nasal organ** — making them among the most recognizable species
in the galaxy — though they otherwise resembled other humanoids with a torso, two arms, two
legs and one head. **Their two large eyes contained large black pupils capable of picking up
a large range of wavelengths, giving remarkable eyesight.** Beneath the eyes were a series of
**olfactory facial folds**, which were **not used for respiration** — the species instead
**absorbed air through its skin.** Along with no nose, Bith **lacked any visually obvious
auditory organs**, yet had very sensitive hearing, with **loud noises causing them a great
deal of pain.** ⚠️ Popular fiction depicted sonic attacks making Bith heads explode; canon
states plainly that **this was inaccurate.**

Society and history (all from *Fully Operational*): Bith society was divided into
**city-states**. Around three hundred years before the Galactic Civil War a civil war broke
out between the city-states of **Nozho** and **Weogar**, in which both sides launched
**biological, chemical, nuclear and mutagenic weapons**, wrecking the ecosystem and killing
most of the population. The survivors imposed a **planet-wide quarantine**, permanently
splitting the species between those quarantined on Bith and those stranded off-world who
could never return. 🔑 **The species became pacifistic "not through any cultural tradition,
but instead due to historical exhaustion with conflict,"** and all Bith use their civil war
as an object lesson on the futility of violence. Those remaining on the homeworld live in
**domed cities** protecting them from **rapidly mutating flora and fauna**, mostly built
among mountain crags and expanding underground or upward; offworlders find being on Bith
**harsh and depressing**.

🔴 **The famous musical talent is a misconception, in-universe.** Stranded off-world Bith
struggled to make a living, and some became musicians; those who did became well known —
**"despite Bith possessing no greater innate talent for music than any other species."** The
reputation grew "through misconception and exaggeration by the galactic community." Rebel
Bith engineers also designed **digital flutes** that concealed recording equipment for
Alliance spies, and Bith rebels used the musical stereotype as cover. The **Bith language**
originally had a symbolic *and numerical* structure; after the civil war it became divorced
from the numerological aspects, though some Bith still use the numerical words as **coded
communication**. All Bith learn Galactic Basic young because the species depends on offworld
trade for basic necessities.

**Legends (`Bith/Legends`), where the numbers and the odd physiology live.**
- **Height 1.5–1.75 meters.** Mass unsourced. Class **craniopod**. Subspecies mainstream
  Bith, **Aalagar**, **Y'bith**. Skin **pale pink, red, yellow, or (rarely) green**. Eyes
  black. Origin Clak'dor VII.
- **Lifespan**: child 1–11, young adult 12–15, adult 16–50, middle age 51–70, old 71–84,
  **venerable 85+** — essentially human.
- Anatomy: **large heads, large lidless eyes, toeless feet, and long fingers with large
  finger pads and crosshatched fingerprints. Both the thumb AND the small finger were fully
  opposable.** 🔑 **Only ONE lung, and they exhale through their skin.** No proper nose;
  highly sensitive olfactory organs hidden in the **skin flaps of their cheeks**.
- **Senses**: Bith could sense the tonal qualities of sound *as other races sense colours*.
  Their eyes could focus to 0.07 on the Gandok Scale and **see microscopic detail of nearby
  objects, but were extremely nearsighted as a result.** High **manual dexterity** for fine
  tools; **only average gross motor skills**.
- 🔑 **Bith had evolved past the need for sleep.** Instead they slip into a light
  **meditative trance**, getting as much rest in **four hours** as others get in eight, and
  **remain almost fully aware of their surroundings** throughout.
- 🔑 **Reproduction is artificial.** A Bith's reproductive organs atrophy over time, as do
  the parts of the brain governing such impulses, so **Bith cannot reproduce naturally.**
  Mates submit genetic material to a **Computer Mating Service** for analysis against
  prospective mates; children are created from two parents' combined material, fertilized,
  and **incubated for a year.**

## Visual brief

**Every reference agrees closely on the head and disagrees on the body.** The head is the
species; the body is not fixed.

**The head, consistent across all four images:**
- **A huge, hairless, bulbous cranium that swells up and BACKWARD well past the face**,
  widest at the top-rear. It is not a sphere and not merely a tall forehead — it overhangs,
  giving the profile a heavy back-weighted teardrop.
- **A distinct vertical crease or furrow running down the centre of the crown**, with soft
  lumpy lobes to either side. This is a small detail that every artist includes and that a
  text-only prompt will omit.
- **Two very large, round, glossy, lidless black eyes** with a single bright specular
  highlight, set **low and wide on the face, beneath the cranial bulge**. No eyelids, no
  visible sclera, no brow.
- **No nose whatsoever.** In its place, **two vertical fleshy wrinkled folds/flaps** run down
  the midface from between the eyes to the mouth, plus creased folds beneath the eyes. These
  are the olfactory folds and they are a *soft, wrinkled, skin-coloured* feature — not
  nostrils, not a snout.
- **A small, low, thin-lipped mouth** with a wrinkled surround and a small receding chin.
  🔴 **There are no fangs, tusks or teeth on display in any reference.** The mouth is
  unremarkable and slightly downturned.
- **No visible ears at all** — confirming the "no visually obvious auditory organs" text.
- **Skin is pale**: cream, bone, pale pink, or a warm pale tan across the four images. Never
  dark, never brown, never saturated.

**`wookieepedia_infobox.jpg`** — the canon infobox illustration (*Star Wars: Alien Archive*
style painting), a Bith in a hooded work jacket with a bandolier of grenade-like canisters:
- **Warm pale tan-olive skin.** Large cranium, the centre crease, the two glossy black eyes,
  the vertical midface folds, small mouth — all present.
- 🔴 **The BODY is short, slight and short-limbed** — the figure reads as small and
  low-slung, with the head taking a disproportionate share of the silhouette. Legs are
  short; the stance is a little hunched.
- **Hands are visible and have long slender fingers with broad pale pads**, matching the
  Legends "long fingers with large finger pads."

**`wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg`** — the Legends *New Essential Guide to Alien Species*
infobox painting, a seated Bith musician playing a Kloo horn in a black band jacket:
- **The most extreme cranium of the set** — enormous, bone-cream, deeply lobed, with the
  centre crease very pronounced. The head is nearly as large as the torso.
- 🔑 **The best hand reference available.** Long, pale, **spidery elongated fingers with
  big flattened pads and prominent pale nails**, wrapped around the horn — visibly more
  digits' worth of length than a human hand. This is what `ElongatedFingers` should look
  like.
- **Body is slight and thin**, seated, human-proportioned limbs.
- Bone-cream skin, faint speckling on the crown.

**`wookieepedia_bith_brute.jpg` — the image that breaks the "thin Bith" assumption.** A
"Bith brute hired out to provide muscle," in stylised flat-colour illustration:
- 🔴 **The body is TALL, broad-shouldered and heavy** — a bouncer's build, not a musician's.
  Since this individual exists in canon, **a Bith is not necessarily thin**, and locking the
  species to a thin body denies a canonical role.
- **Bright pale pink skin** — the "Pink" cite, and the most saturated skin in the set.
- Same head in every respect: bulbous creased cranium, two black glossy eyes, vertical
  midface folds, small mouth, no ears.
- Fur-collared bomber jacket, fingerless glove, a baton over the shoulder, a horn in hand.
  Stylised, so treat line weight and palette as the artist's; the **proportions** are the
  finding.

**`wookieepedia_doikk_nats_musician.jpg`** — Doikk Na'ts, the canonical cantina-band Bith,
from *The Visual Dictionary*. Held as a fourth corroboration of the same head.

**`donor_current_sprite.png` — mostly right, with one clear defect.** It is
`OR/Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Heads/Bith/Normal_south.png`, the head drawn by the
`RSW_BithHead` gene. As a RimWorld humanlike head it is largely a **greyscale mask the game
tints at runtime**, so the absence of skin colour is correct and expected and the colour
findings above belong on the *skin gene*, not on this file. What it gets right: a **large
bulbous hairless cranium**, the **vertical centre crease**, **two round black eyes** in the
correct low-and-wide placement, and **pale midface folds** below the eyes. Three problems:
- 🔴 **The mouth is drawn as a dark red gash filled with jagged black spikes** — a fanged,
  toothy maw. **No reference shows any Bith with teeth or fangs;** the canonical mouth is
  small, thin-lipped and closed. This is baked-in red, not a tintable grey, so it will
  render as a red fanged mouth in game. This is the single thing most worth fixing.
- ⚠️ **The cranium is a wide heart/spade shape rather than the tall backward-swelling
  teardrop** — it broadens sideways where the references swell up and to the rear.
- ⚠️ **The eyes are flat black discs** with no specular highlight, so they lose the wet
  glassy read that the canon text specifically calls out ("black, **glassy** eyes").

## Repo def versus canon — contradictions to report

🔴 **`Skin_Melanin3` is a mid-brown human tone, and no Bith is brown.** Canon lists **cream,
green, orange, pink, white**; Legends lists **pale pink, red, yellow, rarely green**. Every
one of the four references is pale — cream, bone, pale tan, or bright pale pink. A single
mid-melanin human skin gene produces a colour that appears nowhere in either continuity, and
it also throws away the species' whole pale-and-varied palette. This is the most visible
error in the def.

🔴 **`Body_Thin` denies a canonical body type.** The Bith brute is a canon individual with a
tall heavy build, and the canon infobox figure is short and stocky rather than thin. Nothing
in either article gives Bith a slender build as a species trait; what *is* sourced is the
**large head** and **height 1.5–1.75 m** (Legends). The def carries **no body-size gene at
all**, so the modest height is unrepresented while an unsourced thinness is enforced.

🔴 **`Immunity_Weak` is unsourced, and the plausible-looking justification is backwards.**
Neither article says anything about Bith disease resistance. The quarantine of Bith was
imposed to contain a **mutagenic-weapon-devastated ecosystem**, and canon states explicitly
that other species merely **"assumed the Bith were plagued"** — the assumption is called out
as wrong. Encoding weak immunity ships that in-universe prejudice as fact.

- ⚠️ **`AptitudeRemarkable_Artistic` encodes the in-universe stereotype, not the fact.**
  Canon is explicit that Bith possess *"no greater innate talent for music than any other
  species"* and that the reputation arose "through misconception and exaggeration." The
  genuinely sourced aptitudes are **intelligence, engineering and science** — and
  `AptitudeStrong_Intellectual` is *not* in this def, though a "peaceful and **intelligent**"
  species is what both the canon lead sentence and the def's own description say.
- ⚠️ Unsourced: `AptitudePoor_Mining`, `AptitudePoor_Animals`. Neither appears in either
  article. (Poor gross motor skills *are* sourced in Legends — "prowess with gross motor
  skills was only average" — which would justify a mild manipulation-adjacent penalty, but
  not specifically mining or animals.)
- ✅ Defensible: `RSW_BithHead` (the domed cranium, correct feature choice),
  `ElongatedFingers` (Legends' long fingers with large pads and two opposable digits — well
  sourced), `Hair_BaldOnly` and `Beard_NoBeardOnly` (**correct**; hairlessness is stated in
  the canon lead sentence and there is no hair-colour cite at all).
- ⚠️ Missing, and these are the mechanically interesting ones:
  - **They have evolved past sleep** — four hours of aware meditative trance instead of
    eight of sleep. A `LowSleep`-class gene is directly sourced and absent.
  - **Loud noises cause them a great deal of pain**, and sensitive hearing is one of the two
    traits the def's own description bothers to mention. Nothing implements it.
  - **No nose; they absorb air through the skin and have only one lung** — a striking
    respiratory profile with nothing in the def.
  - **They cannot reproduce naturally** and depend on artificial conception with a
    year-long incubation. RimWorld can express sterility; nothing here does.
  - **Extreme nearsightedness with microscopic close vision** — a natural
    shooting-penalty/crafting-bonus pair, unimplemented.
  - **Pacifism from historical exhaustion.** The def sets
    `canGenerateAsCombatant: true` with no ideological or trait counterweight for a species
    canon calls peaceful, whose defining cultural fact is an object lesson on the futility
    of violence. Note this is a *tendency*, not an absolute — the Bith brute and the Rebel
    Bith show armed individuals exist — so it is a flag for the owner's judgement rather
    than a plain error.
