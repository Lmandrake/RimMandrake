# Races in this campaign — canon-reference roster

Purpose: this library exists so the owner can **correct how the races LOOK in game**. Each
entry records canon **appearance, behaviour, unusual abilities and size**, every fact carrying
its URL, and — critically — **trusts images over prose** and says so when they disagree.

## Provenance — where this roster comes from

The roster is a **repo fact**, not a judgement. Four sources were reconciled:

1. 🔑 **`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`** — the
   shipping def list, and the **primary authority for WHICH species exist and their real
   defNames**. Measured **69 `XenotypeDef`s** here, plus **1** in
   `MandrakeJawaXenotype.xml` (`RSW_MandrakeJawa`) = **70 shipped xenotypes**. This file is
   `GENERATED by src/RimMandrake/Utils/gen_races_mod.py. Do not hand-edit.`
2. 🔑 **`design/Jawa/worldbuilding/review/race_faction_assignment.prefill.json`** — the
   owner's race × faction matrix (`posture: matrix`, `generated: 2026-08-17`,
   `placedCount: 115`). Supplies **abundance** (A abundant / S some / R rare / N absent) and
   which factions each species fills. `04_factions.md` states the matrix **is the source and
   wins** for faction xenotype mixes, so **abundance here sets entry priority**.
3. **`design/Jawa/reconciled_lore/04_factions.md`** — the 13 factions. Names species only
   where the fiction turns on them (Geonosian ×6, Mon Calamari, Miraluka, Sith). It is a
   *faction* document, so its silence on a species is **not** evidence of absence.
4. **`design/Jawa/race_regen_architecture.md`** — the authorship architecture (species become
   authored YAML under `species/<Name>.yml`; the acceptance test is byte parity). Its §4.2
   list of "six deferred species" is the one place that **disagrees** with source 1 — see
   Disagreements.

Also consulted: **`design/Jawa/mods/required_mods.md`** on Humanoid Alien Races. 🔴 **The
shipped races are NOT HAR races.** `required_mods.md:610` calls HAR "needed for full alien-
species diversity … (SW Xenotypes can run HAR-less but full roster wants HAR)", and `:1161`
records the load-bearing distinction: *"our Jawa come from Outer Rim – Galactic Diversity,
which is a Biotech xenotype mod, not a HAR race."* Every row below is a **Biotech
`XenotypeDef` (a gene list)** — so appearance lives in `GeneDef renderNodeProperties`,
`forcedHeadTypes` and `HeadTypeDefs`, **not** in a HAR `AlienRace` body def. A correction to
how a race LOOKS lands on a gene, never on the XenotypeDef.

## Disagreements — recorded, not resolved

- ⚠️ **"Six deferred species" vs the shipped def list.** `race_regen_architecture.md` §4.2
  says *"The six deferred species (Herglic, Anzati, Muun, SithZ, Togorian — Ortolan already
  shipped) come back as authored files."* But **all five of those defNames are present in
  `RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml` today** (`RSW_RimMandrakeHerglic`, `…Anzati`, `…Muun`, `…SithZ`,
  `…Togorian`), and all five carry faction placements in the matrix. Either the deferral was
  executed and the doc is stale, or the defs shipped and the doc was never updated. **Not
  resolved here.** They are treated as IN, because the def list is the authority on existence.
- ⚠️ **Two Jawa defs.** `RSW_MandrakeJawa` (matrix: `Jawa_IndigenousTribes: A`,
  `Jawa_HuttCartel: R`) and `RSW_RimMandrakeJawa` (matrix entry present but **empty `{}`** =
  placed nowhere). One is presumably the live player race and the other a donor leftover.
  Both map to the single existing `jawa/` entry.
- ⚠️ **Three species are in the matrix with an empty placement** — `RimMandrakeDathomirian`,
  `RimMandrakeJawa`, `RimMandrakeYoderForceGremlin` — i.e. the def ships but no faction
  spawns it. Listed below at tier `—`. Whether that is deliberate (cut but not deleted) or an
  oversight is **not** recorded anywhere found.
- ⚠️ **`RSW_RimMandrakeKlatoonian` is a misspelling of the canon species name, which is
  Klatooinian** (two o's, `starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Klatooinian`). The def and the matrix key
  both carry the typo; the reference directory correctly uses `klatooinian/`. Renaming is
  governed by `NAMING_SCHEME_EXECUTION_1` — **do not rename ahead of it** — but the entry
  must not silently adopt the typo.
- ⚠️ **`RSW_RimMandrakeIridonian`** — "Iridonian" is the demonym for a Zabrak from Iridonia,
  not a separate species; canon's species article is **Zabrak**.
- ⛔ **Miraluka are OUT of every version** (`04_factions.md`, owner 2026-08-20: *"Miraluka are
  gone completely, every version"*). There is no Miraluka def and no entry is owed. Recorded
  so a later pass does not "restore" them.
- **`Baseliner`** appears in the matrix (and is the Empire's dominant xenotype at ~77%) but is
  vanilla-human, not a Star Wars species. No entry owed.
