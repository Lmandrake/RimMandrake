# Worrt

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Worrt

Worrts were a non-sentient species that hailed from the desert of the planet Tatooine. These squat, large-mouthed creatures caught prey by sitting stationary in the sand and using their purple prehensile tongues to wrap around passing rodents, insects or birds.

**Biology and appearance** Worrts were squat and spiky creatures whose appearance bore similarities to that of chubas. They had brown, warty skin and measured 1.50 meters in height. They were quadrupeds with dexterous forelimbs. A worrt's head featured a pair of sensitive cranial palps, two bulbous eyes that could be yellow in hue, two nostrils, and a large mouth. That mouth contained a purple prehensile tongue, and strong teeth laced with a venom potent enough to kill a bantha. When the creature became full, its belly would turn a bright yellow color. The eyelids of the worrts could keep sand out of their eyes, and their saliva was also capable of jamming a blaster. Worrts lay large amounts of small, soft eggs which were harvested for food and used as ingredients in drinks served at cantinas.

**Behavior and history (canon)** Worrts were predators that fed mostly on insects and rodents; a hungry worrt settled into the sand, indistinguishable from a rocky outcropping, then threw out its tongue, snapped the prey down whole and belched loudly (*Return of the Jedi* shows this outside Jabba's palace; the film puppet is canon appearance evidence). Tusken Raiders considered worrts a delicacy. Worrts were also kept as pets (Korrg in *Last Shot*, J'Rrosch in "All Creatures Great and Small"), and a worrt in a tank at Oga's Cantina on Batuu laid the eggs used in drinks (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Worrt). Height 1.5 m is the Databank figure; the Databank also gives burrowing/sitting ambush, tongue capture and carnivore diet.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Worrt/Legends

The worrt was an amphibious creature native to Tatooine, though they could also be found on Nal Hutta and Phaeda.

**Biology and appearance** Worrts were omnivorous, but was also known to attempt to eat rocks and metal objects, which led to the demise of many worrts. They used their long, prehensile tongue to catch smaller prey, such as gorgs, but were known to also attack creatures larger than itself. Sometimes, immediately upon eating prey, a worrt would let out a loud belch.

Worrts could, with great difficulty, be trained as housepets. Jabba Desilijic Tiure kept a number of worrts as pest control, exploiting the creature's lightning-quick tongue. The worrt counted the native ibian among its prey. Worrt venom tasted like tricopper and was strong enough to kill a bantha.

## Visual brief
Four images, very consistent: a big warty toad.
- **Silhouette:** a squat, fat, **toad- or frog-like body, hunched forward with the weight on long, thin, powerful forelimbs** (the text's "dexterous forelimbs"), with a huge round belly and the hind legs folded away. The arms are long and spidery with large, long-fingered, splayed hands (very long, tapering fingers with rounded tips in `canon_1`).
- **Head:** a broad, flat head, no visible neck, with **a very wide, thick-lipped, downturned toad mouth** (a heavy pouty upper lip in `canon_1`), two small nostrils, and **large bulging eyes on top, ringed with heavy lids**: orange-red in `canon_1`, amber/yellow-green in `canon_2` and `legends_1` and `legends_2`, vertical-slit pupils in the 3D renders. Between the eyes is a **ridged, grooved patch or brow plate** (banded folds, `canon_1` and `canon_2`).
- **Palps:** **two long, thin, curved stalk-like cranial palps (antennae) rise from the top of the head**, bending outward, with small knobbed tips in `canon_1`.
- **Spikes and skin:** a **crest of conical horn-like spikes around the top and sides of the head, over the shoulders and down the arms and back**: orange-brown in `canon_1`, tan to rose in `canon_2`, grey-brown in `legends_1` and `legends_2`. The skin is **warty, bumpy and rough everywhere**, with small raised tubercles.
- **Colour disagreement:** `canon_1` is **olive-green with a yellow-ochre belly and orange-brown spikes**; `canon_2` is **rosy brown to dusty pink-tan with tan warts**; `legends_1` (the film puppet) is **tan-brown**; `legends_2` is **grey-brown**. The text says "brown, warty skin", and the belly turns bright yellow when full. So brown is the baseline; olive-green and rose-tan are the render variants, and a yellow belly is a state change.
- **Size:** 1.5 m tall per the text; it looks bigger than a human torso in the puppet shot.
