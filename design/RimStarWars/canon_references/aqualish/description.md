## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Canon.** Aqualish were sentient humanoids from the planet **Ando**, *"identifiable by
round black eyes and fur."* Canon infobox: three subspecies — **Aquala, Quara, Ualaq**;
**skin colour black, blue, gray, green, light brown, or red**; **hair black, blond, or
brown**; **eye colour black, brown, or red**; distinctions **two or four eyes** and
**arachnid-like tusks**; language Aqualish; origin Ando. 🔴 **Height, mass and lifespan
are all unsourced in canon** — every one of those infobox fields is empty.

Biology and appearance, canon body text: Aqualish *"generally had bald heads and fur around
their cheeks and two large **chelicerae**."* Skin colour varies between individuals. They
tended to have **either two or four eyes**, and **different kinds of hands**: the criminal
**Ponda Baba** had **hairy hands with five digits**, while Aqualish in the Galactic Republic
Senate sported **hairless hands with three digits**. **Blood is red.**

Habits and history: *"The lives of Aqualish revolved around water, but they did not need it
to thrive and could settle far from it."* They served as Republic senators, then aligned
with the Separatists (the **Ando Commission**, three Aqualish, represented Ando in the
Separatist Senate). They maintained an **Aqualish mercenary army** hired out as corporate
security. During the Galactic Civil War some turned to **piracy and bounty hunting**,
others joined the Rebellion. Directly relevant to this campaign: **a collective of Aqualish
on Tatooine were members of the crime families controlling the Mos Espa workers' district**
that Boba Fett allied with and was betrayed by; their leader was assassinated by Fennec
Shand in 9 ABY. At least one Aqualish served in Jabba the Hutt's court. Others used the
abandoned Mandalorian covert on Nevarro as a hideout and were killed by Marshal Cara Dune.
An in-universe epigraph, from an explorer: *"The Aqualish people are not fond of questions."*

**Legends (`Aqualish/Legends`), where the numbers and subspecies mechanics live.**
- **Height 1.7–2.0 meters** — a *tall* species, taller than baseline human average.
  Mass unsourced. Class **amphibian**. Skin black, dark blue, deep russet, or green.
  Eyes black, dark blue, red, or dark brown. **Diet fish and shellfish.**
- **Lifespan**: child 1–11, young adult 12–16, adult 17–50, middle age 51–69, old 70–79,
  **venerable 80+** — i.e. roughly human, slightly shorter.
- Appearance *"combined aspects of arachnids and pinniped aquatic mammals"* — spider and
  seal, which is the read to aim for. **All Aqualish have two facial tusks which curve down
  over the mouth.** The tusks are **highly sensitive to heat and cold** and are used for
  **cracking open shellfish shells and burrowing through swampy loam** — they are tools,
  not just ornament. Aqualish carry **arachnid genetic material in their distant past**,
  shown by their relationship to the **Harch**.
- **Three subspecies, and the difference is in the HANDS**:
  - **Aquala** — the baseline breed. **Finned hands** and two bulbous black eyes.
    **Unable to manipulate most machinery or equipment** built for digited beings, so they
    stay on Ando in far greater numbers.
  - **Quara** — **clawed, five-fingered hands**, two eyes. Can use off-world equipment, so
    they are the subspecies usually found off-world.
  - **Ualaq** — **four eyes instead of two** (one pair for night vision, one for daylight)
    and fingered hands **varying from three to five digits**. Considered by some specialists
    to be the result of **genetic mutation**; the least common subspecies, often travelling
    far from Ando **to avoid discrimination by the Aquala**.
  - Quara and Ualaq together are only **ten percent** of the total population.
  - 🔑 **All three subspecies hate each other**, each believing its own race superior.
- **Behaviour**: notorious for **hair-trigger tempers that flare without reason**;
  "nasty, crude and aggressive"; careers as mercenaries, bounty hunters, pirates.
  Anthropologists trace the rage streak to the species' earliest evolution.

## Visual brief

🔴 **The canon infobox line "identifiable by round black eyes and fur" undersells one thing
and overstates another. The eyes are right and are the strongest single read. The "fur" is
NOT a cheek beard in the most recent and highest-fidelity canon depiction — it is a long
stringy mane from the sides of the head — and the four-eyed Ualaq are FURLESS entirely.**

**Common to every image, and therefore the species' load-bearing features:**
- **Two very large, round, glossy, domed black eyes**, set wide and low on a bald forehead,
  with a bright specular highlight and (at high fidelity) a faint dark purple-brown iris
  ring. These read as *insect/arachnid* eyes, not mammal eyes, and they are the feature that
  identifies the species at any size.
- **A completely bald, smooth, domed crown** above the eyes in every single reference —
  finely wrinkled at high fidelity. There is never hair on top.
- **Two thick tusks curving DOWN over the mouth**, joined at the top so they read as a
  single **two-lobed pale muzzle block** rather than two separate spikes. 🔴 **They are
  BLUNT-ended and pale — cream, pale pink, or ochre-tan — and they contrast strongly in
  value against the face.** They are not sharp fangs and not spider fangs in silhouette,
  despite the "arachnid tusks" wording. A text-only prompt reading "arachnid tusks" will
  produce sharp mandibles, and that is the wrong shape.
- **No visible nose**; nostril-like slits sit between and below the eyes.

**`wookieepedia_crime_family_bobf.jpg` — the reference of record for this campaign.** The
live-action Tatooine crime-family Aqualish from *The Book of Boba Fett* "Chapter 3," the
most recent, highest-fidelity, and most campaign-relevant depiction (Tatooine, criminal,
armed):
- **Skin is a pale, dusty, mottled grey-tan** — weathered, deeply wrinkled, and dry. 🔴
  **This is not on the canon infobox colour list at all** (black / blue / gray / green /
  light brown / red); "gray" is the nearest cite but the actual render is a warm dust-grey
  with pink undertones. **For a desert campaign this dusty grey-tan is almost certainly the
  right target, and it is NOT what the def's skin genes produce** (see below).
- **Hair is long, coarse, stringy and pale grey-blonde**, growing from the **sides and back
  of the head** and hanging **past the shoulders** — the crown is bald and **the cheeks and
  jaw are bare**. So the canonical "fur around their cheeks" is realised here as a
  **temple-and-back mane**, not a beard.
- **Tusks are thick, ridged, blunt, and pale ochre**, hanging down over the mouth like a
  pair of joined tubers.
- **Hands are five-fingered**, wrinkled grey, with visible nails — the **Quara** pattern.
- Rough woven brown-tan robes; background individuals in orange. Holds a blaster
  two-handed, competently.

**`wookieepedia_infobox.jpg`** — the canon infobox illustration (Ponda Baba archetype;
comic-style ink and colour):
- **Dark olive-grey to near-black skin.** This is the "Black"/"Gray" end of the colour list.
- **A dense, bushy, dark full beard covering the entire lower face, cheeks and jaw and
  spilling onto the chest** — this *is* the "fur around their cheeks" read, and it is
  substantial: a facial mane, not sideburns. **It disagrees with the live-action piece**,
  which has bare cheeks and a side mane. Both are canon; the *arrangement* of fur is
  therefore individual/variant, while the bald crown and the eyes are not.
- **Two thick pale-pink tusks** emerging out of the dark beard — maximum value contrast,
  which is what makes them read.
- Two eyes. Gloved hands (so digit count is not evidence here). Red jacket, dark trousers,
  boots. Stocky, upright, human-proportioned build.

**`wookieepedia_politicians.jpg` — the four-eyed Ualaq, and a strong negative on "fur."**
Live-action Separatist/Republic Aqualish senators:
- **Four eyes: a wide-set large upper pair plus a smaller lower pair**, clustered in the
  centre of the face. All four are the same glossy black.
- 🔴 **Completely hairless — no beard, no cheek fur, no mane at all.** Smooth, wrinkled,
  bare faces. If the def forces fur onto every Aqualish, the Ualaq cannot be built.
- **Skin is pinkish-red to mauve-tan** — the "Red" colour cite, and again not green.
- **Tusks are much shorter and lower-contrast**, integrated into the muzzle rather than
  hanging free.
- **Hands are visible and have three thick digits** — directly confirming the canon text's
  "hairless hands with three digits" for senatorial Aqualish.
- Ornate senatorial robes in red, orange and green.

**`wookieepedia_arms_dealer.jpg`** — a painted older Aqualish arms dealer over a table of
blasters. Greenish-tan skin (the "Green" cite), heavily wrinkled bald crown with a facial
scar, two large dark eyes, **short stubby pale tusks**, **sparse wispy whitish cheek fur**
reading as age rather than a full beard, and **five clearly separated fingers**. Useful as
the "old individual" data point: fur thins, tusks look shorter.

**`wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg`** — the Legends *New Essential Guide to Alien Species*
painting. **Bright olive-green skin**, bald bumpy crown, **four eyes** (large upper pair
plus small lower pair), **a full ginger-tan cheek-and-jaw mane**, pale cream tusks, and
**green five-fingered hands**. Stocky and broad-shouldered. Legends, and its saturated green
is the least corroborated colour across the canon set — treat the green as one book's take.

**`wookieepedia_quara_and_aquala_legends.jpg` — the one image that settles the subspecies
hands.** Legends line art, a male **Quara** (left) beside a female **Aquala** (right):
- **Quara**: stocky, jacketed and booted, bald crown, dark cheek fur, tusks, and hands that
  are **clawed digits fringed with fur**.
- **Aquala**: strikingly **gaunt and long-limbed**, a longer narrower face with hanging fur
  strands and small ornaments beside the tusks, and — the key detail — **arms ending in
  flat, fur-fringed FLIPPERS with no digits at all**, plus long splayed flat feet. This is
  what "finned hands, unable to manipulate machinery" looks like.
- Both have **two** eyes, consistent with the text.
- **The two subspecies differ in overall build, not just hands** — Quara stocky, Aquala
  emaciated and elongated. A single body type cannot represent both.

**`donor_current_sprite.png`** — `OR/Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Heads/Aqualish/Quad_south.png`,
the four-eyed head the `RSW_AqualishHead` gene draws. As a RimWorld humanlike head it is a
**greyscale mask that the game tints at runtime from the skin gene**, so its lack of colour
is correct and expected and the colour findings above belong on the *skin genes*, not on
this file. On shape it is **better than most donors in this library**: a bald smooth dome,
**four eyes in the correct large-upper/small-lower arrangement**, and **a pale two-lobed
muzzle block** that matches the joined-blunt-tusk read from the live-action reference well.
What is missing: **no cheek fur or side mane of any kind** (so the def's own description,
"round black eyes and **fur**," is not what the game draws), **no facial wrinkling**, and
the eyes are flat slots without the glossy dome highlight that makes the real thing read as
arachnid. A `Duo_south.png` two-eyed variant also exists on disk, so both eye counts are
buildable. ⚠️ **Note the sprite is the Ualaq (four-eyed, furless) pattern, while the def
text describes the furred two-eyed pattern** — the mod is internally inconsistent about
which Aqualish it is depicting.

## Repo def versus canon — contradictions to report

🔴 **`Hair_BaldOnly` + `Beard_NoBeardOnly` delete the feature the def's own description
calls identifying.** The def says Aqualish are *"easily identified by their round black eyes
and fur"* and then forbids both hair and beard. Canon gives hair colours (black, blond,
brown) and describes *"fur around their cheeks"*; the live-action reference has a
shoulder-length mane. As shipped, no Aqualish can have any. **The Ualaq are genuinely
furless, so bald-only is right for that subspecies and wrong as a species-wide rule.**

🔴 **`Outland_EggLayer` is unsourced.** Nothing in either the canon or Legends article says
anything about Aqualish reproduction — no eggs, no clutch, no gestation. Legends classes
them **amphibian**, which may be where the inference came from, but the inference is not in
the source. Under this library's standard, unsourced means absent.

🔴 **`Hands_Pig` collapses the one thing that distinguishes the three subspecies.** Canon
and Legends are specific and mutually exclusive on hands: **Aquala = digitless fins**
(cannot use machinery), **Quara = clawed five digits**, **Ualaq = three to five digits**,
and canon separately attests **five hairy digits** (Ponda Baba) and **three hairless
digits** (senators). A single trotter-style hand gene matches none of these, and it silently
removes the Aquala's defining limitation.

🔴 **Skin genes miss the colours the live-action canon actually shows.** The def carries
`Outland_Skin_DeepSage`, `Outland_Skin_DeepBrown`, `Outland_Skin_Brown`, `Skin_InkBlack`,
`Skin_SlateGray`. Canon's list is **black, blue, gray, green, light brown, red** — so
**blue and red are missing**, and red is exactly what the four-eyed senators are. More
importantly the *Book of Boba Fett* Tatooine Aqualish are a **dusty grey-tan**, which
`Skin_SlateGray` (a cold blue-grey) will not produce. For a desert campaign this is the
colour most worth getting right.

- ⚠️ `Outland_InsectBody` and `RSW_butchergene_insectmeat` overreach a real hook. Legends
  gives Aqualish *"some arachnid genetic material in their distant past"* and an appearance
  *combining arachnid and pinniped* traits — but they are classed **amphibian**, are
  described as **mammal-like (pinniped)** in the same sentence, and have **red blood**
  (canon, explicit). Rendering them as insects whose corpses butcher into insect meat
  contradicts the red-blooded amphibian/pinniped sourcing.
- ✅ Defensible: `Aggression_HyperAggressive` (the sourced hair-trigger temper is one of the
  species' two listed distinctions), `RSW_AqualishHead` with both Duo and Quad art (the
  two-or-four-eye rule), `DarkVision` (**properly sourced for once** — the Ualaq's second
  pair of eyes is explicitly *"one for night vision, one for daylight"*, though note this
  makes dark vision a **Ualaq-only** trait, not species-wide),
  `AptitudeStrong_Shooting` (mercenaries, bounty hunters, pirates, the Aqualish mercenary
  army).
- ⚠️ `Pain_Reduced` is unsourced — nothing about pain tolerance appears in either article.
- ⚠️ Missing and worth having: **height 1.7–2.0 m makes Aqualish a tall species**, and the
  def carries no body-size gene at all; the **heat/cold-sensitive tusks used as tools**;
  the **fish-and-shellfish diet**; and the fact that **the three subspecies hate each
  other**, which is a ready-made faction/relationship hook this campaign could use.
