# Kubaz

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeKubaz` (`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`,
verified present). Matrix placements: `Jawa_HuttCartel: R`, `Jawa_Junkers: R`,
`Jawa_AscendantHelix: R` — rare in three factions, so a recurring bit-part face.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
The Kubaz were a sentient species native to **Kubindi**. The canon infobox gives **no height,
no mass, no lifespan, no hair colour and no eye colour** — all unsourced in canon and recorded
here as absent, not guessed. Skin colour is sourced only as **"Dark"**. Distinctions: **long
snout** and **sensitive eyes**. Diet: **insectivorous**. Language: Kubazian.

🔴 **Class is sourced three incompatible ways and the wiki records all three**: **Insectoid**
("The Secrets of Long Snoot," *From a Certain Point of View*), **Elephantine** (Leland Chee,
Instagram, 2024-08-03), and **Mammalian** (*Cyphers and Masks*). Legends independently says
**Mammalian**. There is no reconciliation on the page; any def that picks one is picking one of
three sourced answers.

Biology and behaviour: they **lived on a diet of insects, using their long snout to snatch
their prey from inside hives** — the snout is a feeding organ, not decoration. This **did not
make them popular with sentient insectoid species such as the Geonosians**, despite the Kubaz
**themselves being descended from insects.** They had **dark skin, long snouts, and sensitive
eyesight causing them to require protective eyewear.** On the whole **their appearance
resembled that of the northern Mustafarians.** **Kubaz were unable to pronounce the phonemes
of Galactic Basic Standard, and their language was made of whirring syllables.**

History: the Galactic Empire **conquered Kubindi after claiming they could be allies**, then
**enslaved Kubaz and sought to brainwash others into spies** — which is why the species' most
famous member, the informant **Garindan ezz Zavor**, works for the highest bidder while
secretly trying to free his people. One in-universe line records open xenophobia toward them
("the Kubaz are a trash species"). Useful for a Junkers/Cartel bit-part: a Kubaz is read by
others as a snitch.

The canon article carries `{{Species-stub}}` and **has no "Biology and appearance" section at
all** — everything above is from the lead. So **`Kubaz/Legends` was pulled as well** and is
marked Legends throughout: height **1.7–2 metres**, lifespan **81 years**, skin
**greenish-black**, hair **black**, class **mammalian**, diet insects. Legends adds: **short,
prehensile facial snouts used for reaching insects within their hives**; **short bristly hair
growing from their heads**; they **stood upon two-toed feet**; **Kubindi orbited a blue star,
so the Kubaz were highly sensitive to red wavelengths** and **were forced to don protective
eyewear when travelling to planets with a red or yellow star**; and they were **able to see
into the infrared spectrum.** ⚠️ That is the mechanism behind the goggles, and it is Legends —
canon states only "sensitive eyesight."

## Visual brief
🔑 **The distinction the brief asks for: the SNOUT is face, the GOGGLES are equipment. There
is nothing else covering a Kubaz.** Unlike the Kel Dor, a Kubaz can breathe fine; the eyewear
is a light filter, not a rebreather. All three references agree.

**The snout (anatomy).** A long, tapering, tubular proboscis projecting forward and angling
**downward** from the middle of the face, roughly as long as the rest of the head is tall,
ending in a blunt open tip. In `wookieepedia_infobox.jpg` it is a **lighter mauve/grey-pink
than the rest of the head** and is covered along its underside and flanks with a **regular
pattern of dark pits or pores**. It is *not* an elephant trunk — it is stiff and conical, held
in one position, not coiling. Legends calls it prehensile; the images show no curl.

**The goggles (equipment), and they vary per individual.** Every image has the eyes behind a
separate strapped eyewear piece, and **the lens colour is not consistent**:
- `wookieepedia_infobox.jpg` — **two round RED lenses** in dark rims, joined by a bridge, with
  a visible strap running back around the skull.
- `wookieepedia_courier.jpg` — **orange/amber lenses**, worn under a hard helmet.
- `wookieepedia_alien_archive_legends.jpg` — **round black/dark lenses** (the *A New Hope*
  Garindan look).
So lens tint is a costume variable. **What is constant: the eyes are never visible.** No
canon image found in this pass shows a Kubaz eye.

🔴 **The crest of bristles — images and prose disagree, and the images win.** All three
references show a **crest of stiff, spiky bristles standing up from the brow and running back
over the crown**, and in all three it is **pale — near-white, silver, or a pale lavender-green
— not black.** Legends' infobox says `haircolor=Black`. Trust the images: the crest is pale
and it is the second-most recognisable feature after the snout.

**Skin.** Mottled dark **olive/greenish-grey** over the head and body, going nearly black in
shadow — which supports both canon's bare "Dark" and Legends' "greenish-black." The head is
**two-tone**: dark greenish cranium, **lighter mauve-grey snout.** That two-tone arrangement is
the thing to preserve, exactly as with the Hutt.

**Hands.** `wookieepedia_courier.jpg` has the only bare hand: **long, thin, pale sage-green
fingers with prominent knuckles** on a narrow palm — spindly, distinctly non-human proportion.
The infobox figure wears gloves, so it says nothing about hands.

**Build and dress.** Tall, narrow, upright biped in all three — no hunch, no bulk. Costuming is
consistently **concealing**: hooded cloak (Legends), layered jacket with a bandolier plus tall
boots (infobox), courier armour with helmet and satchels (courier). A Kubaz reads as *hiding*,
which fits the spy/informant fiction.

### Def-versus-canon (flagged — not fixed here)
- 🔴 **`<description>` is the single character `e`** — no description ships for this xenotype.
- 🔴 **No goggles / eyewear gene of any kind**, and 🔴 **the donor head sprite draws bare eyes
  with human eyebrows.** `RSW_Head_kubaz` gets the snout right and then puts two visible eyes
  and two eyebrow arcs above it — the one thing no canon Kubaz image shows. `UVSensitivity_Mild`
  captures the *mechanic* correctly; nothing captures the *look*, and the shipped art
  contradicts it. This mod already has head-attachment genes for eyewear-like parts
  (`RSW_HeadAttachment_keldormask`, `RSW_HeadAttachment_kaleeshmask`), so the vocabulary exists.
- 🔴 **`Hair_BaldOnly` forbids the pale spiky crest that every reference image shows.** Combined
  with the bald donor sprite, the shipped Kubaz has no crest at all.
- ⚠️ **`Eyes_Gray`** is unsourced — canon's eye-colour field is blank — and it makes the wrong
  thing (a bare eye) more prominent rather than less.
- ⚠️ **`Outland_InsectBody`** commits to one of three mutually exclusive sourced classes.
  Defensible (canon's lead does call them insectoid and descended from insects), but Leland
  Chee's "Elephantine" and *Cyphers and Masks*' "Mammalian" are equally cited, Legends says
  mammalian, and the images read elephantine. Worth an owner call rather than silent choice.
- ⚠️ Unsourced: `PsychicAbility_Dull`, `PollutionRush`, `MinTemp_SmallIncrease`,
  `Outland_FamiliarScent`. `ToxicEnvironmentResistance_Partial` has no basis found either —
  nothing in canon or Legends says a Kubaz tolerates toxins.
- Not represented, though sourced: **two-toed feet** (Legends), the **two-tone head**
  (dark cranium / pale snout), and the **long spindly hands.**
- ✅ **Correct, and worth recording as correct**: `nameMaker` is `RSW_KoTOR_NamerKubaz` — the
  right species' namer, unlike three of the other four defs in this batch.
  `RSW_Skin_DarkGreen` + `Skin_SlateGray` is a **good** match to "dark" / "greenish-black" and
  to what the images show. `RSW_butchergene_insectmeat` + `BS_Diet_Carnivore` matches the
  sourced insectivory. `DarkVision` is supported by Legends' infrared vision.
  `Outland_Voice_Insect` matches "whirring syllables" and the inability to speak Basic.
  `UVSensitivity_Mild` matches "sensitive eyesight."

## Candidate images
- `wookieepedia_infobox.jpg` — **the reference of record.** The canon infobox: a full-body
  Kubaz on white, painted. Settles the snout (length, downward angle, pale mauve tone, pore
  pattern), the pale spiky crest, the red-lensed strapped goggles, the mottled olive skin and
  the tall narrow build.
- `wookieepedia_courier.jpg` — a Kubaz courier at a run. **Its unique value is the one bare
  hand** — long thin pale-green fingers — plus a second goggle tint (amber) proving lens colour
  is a costume variable.
- `wookieepedia_alien_archive_legends.jpg` — Garindan in the black hooded cloak. **Legends**
  and very dark, so poor for colour; useful as a third independent confirmation of the crest,
  the snout and dark round goggles, and as the reference for the concealing-cloak silhouette.
- `donor_current_sprite.png` — the mod's current art,
  `SWX/Pawn/HeadType/kubaz/Male_Kubaz_south.png`. Greyscale, which is correct and expected
  (RimWorld tints heads at runtime from the skin gene), and **the snout is genuinely good** —
  right length, right downward taper, with the pore pattern present. **Negative reference for
  the face above the snout:** two bare eyes and two human eyebrow arcs, no goggles, no crest.
  That is the correction this entry exists to make.
