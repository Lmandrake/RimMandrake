## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Canon article.** Umbarans, **also known as the Shadow People**, were a **near-human**
species native to **Umbara**. Infobox: class near-human; **skin colour "Pale and
bluish"**; **hair colour black, silver, white**; **eye colour grey, pale blue, white**;
distinctions **ability to see in the ultraviolet spectrum**; origin Umbara; language
Umbarese. 🔴 **No height, no mass, no lifespan in the canon infobox** — all three fields
are empty.

Biology, verbatim substance: a **slender** near-human species with **pale, bluish
skin**. Four limbs, five-fingered hands, opposable thumbs. **Umbarans had gaunt,
emaciated faces with gray markings around the sockets of their colorless eyes.** A
**single heart**, the stabbing of which was fatal. **Although Umbarans did have hair,
some of them either were bald or shaved their heads.** Because of the species' skill at
manipulating others, **there were rumors that Umbarans had the ability to control
minds.** **With their colorless eyes, they were capable of seeing in the ultraviolet
spectrum.** 🔑 And a hard mechanical trait: **"Umbarans were exceptionally resistant to
sustained neural prompting. Because of this, for example, it was not possible to
permanently control an Umbaran through the implantation of an inhibitor chip."**
(cited to *Star Wars Zero Company*.)

Behaviour. On their **gloomy homeworld** the Shadow People formed **an organized society
divided into castes**, and **constantly schemed to improve their social rank.** Their
**technology was more advanced than that found in most of the galaxy.** They spoke
Basic but preferred Umbarese at home. A traditional garment was the voluminous
**Umbaran shadow cloak**. Politically: represented in the Senate by Mee Deechi, and
after his murder **they joined the Confederacy of Independent Systems, bringing their
extremely advanced technology to the enemies of the Republic.** The clone-trooper line
that names them is the single best behavioural summary: *"I can't even see the
enemy!" / "That's why they're called the Shadow People, Tup!"*

**Legends article** (marked Legends, not canon): height **1.7–2.0 meters** (stated for
males); skin **pale**; distinctions **ultraviolet vision** (specified as **below 300
nanometers**) and the **ability to influence others**; no mass or lifespan given.
Legends body text: **"gaunt, frighteningly pale near-Humans with achromatic sunken
eyes."** 🔑 And the counterpart to the night vision, which the canon page omits: **"Due
to Umbara's eternal twilight, their eyes were well adjusted to the dark, but they were
easily disoriented when exposed to sudden bright flashes of light."** Legends also
details the caste system as **almost one hundred levels**, with only the **top ten**
permitted to leave the homeworld — *"explaining their rarity outside the Ghost
Nebula"*, which independently supports the `S`/rare placement — and an assassination
culture using **microscopic poison darts, nanotechnology, undetectable poisons and
small stylish stiletto-like knives called vootkar**, with shadowcloaks worn to assist.
Ruled by the **Rootai**, the highest caste.

**Unusual abilities.** Three, and only one of them is a real power: **ultraviolet/dark
vision** (canon + Legends), **immunity to permanent neural control via inhibitor chip**
(canon), and **social manipulation** — which canon states as *skill* plus **rumour** of
mind control, and Legends states more strongly as an "ability to read, subtly influence
and sometimes control the will of others." Do not promote the rumour to a Force power:
the canon article frames mind control as a rumour *caused by* their social skill.

## Visual brief

**Owner ruling 2026-10-08 (verbatim): "That's realistic, not these cartoon versions you keep using. Replace the canon with something more realistic. The web is full of them."** Deleted, both *Clone Wars*: `wookieepedia_mee_deechi_face.jpg` (Mee Deechi close-up) and `wookieepedia_militia_soldier.jpg` (militia trooper). Added live-action: `wookieepedia_sly_moore_still.jpg` — Sly Moore, the Umbaran aide of Palpatine (*Attack of the Clones* / *Revenge of the Sith*), a costume-and-makeup portrait under even light. The canon infobox (`wookieepedia_senator_infobox.jpg`, an Umbaran senator from *Revenge of the Sith*) and the realistic Legends painting remain.

**Skin, from the evenly lit realistic images:** **very pale, desaturated, chalky white with a cool lavender-to-bluish cast** — near-white on Sly Moore with lilac-grey shading in the hollows, a little more violet on the infobox senator, blue-lavender in the Legends painting. Not grey-sickly, not green. (The green-teal face of the deleted militia trooper was Umbara's ambient light, not skin.)

**Face:**
- **Gaunt and hollow-cheeked**, high hard cheekbones, long narrow jaw, prominent brow, high bald dome on Sly Moore and the senator.
- **Eyes:** 🔴 live-action Sly Moore's eyes are **very pale, almost colourless grey-white irises with small dark pupils**, deep-set and ringed by **smoky lavender-grey shadow** around the sockets — so "colourless" is close to right in live action, but there is still a pupil. Never a blank solid-white eye.
- **Eye-socket shading:** soft smoky lavender-grey around the eyes (Sly Moore). ⚠️ The **hard-edged domino-mask grey band** the old brief made the species' identifying mark came only from the deleted animated Deechi and militia images — **live action shows soft shadowing, not a graphic band.**
- **Lips:** pale, slightly greyed, close to skin tone.
- **No eyebrows, no facial hair.**

**Hair.** Canon text: "Although Umbarans did have hair, some of them either were bald or shaved their heads." Every remaining image is **bald**; the silver-white slicked-back hair was the deleted animated Deechi — hair is now sourced in text only.

**Build and dress.** Tall, slender, long-limbed, narrow-shouldered near-human; long slim hands. Costume is a **voluminous high-collared robe or cloak** whose raised standing collar frames the head (Sly Moore's shaggy grey-white fur-textured shadow cloak with white inner collar; the senator's pale robes).

**`donor_current_sprite.png` is a UI icon, not pawn art, and is weak evidence** — the
Umbaran-specific texture on disk is only
`RimMandrakeSW/OR/OuterRim/XenotypeIcons/Xenotype_Umbaran.png` (512×512): a black
outline of a bald teardrop head with two flat grey discs for eyes, on white, with no
nose, no mouth and no markings. It is a menu glyph and tells us nothing about how a
pawn renders. 🔑 **There is no Umbaran-specific head, body or hair texture in the
repo at all** — the def builds the species entirely from generic genes (`Head_Gaunt`,
`Skin_LightGray`, `Outland_Eye_White`, `RSW_Body_gaunt`, `Hair_BaldOnly`), so every
correction below lands on a *gene choice*, not on new art.

🔴 **Repo def versus canon — three findings.**

1. **`Hair_BaldOnly` + `Beard_NoBeardOnly` forces every Umbaran bald, and canon says
   they have hair.** The article's sentence is explicit, and the animated Senate-era Umbaran Mee Deechi
   has a full head of silver-white hair (image deleted 2026-10-08; the text still stands). Baldness
   is *one* canonical option, not the species.
2. **`Skin_LightGray` is the wrong direction.** Canon says "pale and bluish"; the
   evenly-lit reference art reads **pale lavender-violet**. Grey is the one value that
   makes them look merely sickly rather than alien, and it is not supported by any
   source. (Contrast the Zeltron def, which does reach for `Outland_` skin genes — a
   pale-violet/blue option should be available here too.)
3. **`Outland_Eye_White` overshoots "colorless".** Every image shows a pale but
   structured eye with a visible pupil; the infobox itself lists **grey and pale blue**
   alongside white.

Also worth recording, as gaps rather than contradictions: the def carries `DarkVision`
(good — this is the canon UV/twilight vision) but **nothing corresponding to the
Legends counterpart weakness, "easily disoriented when exposed to sudden bright flashes
of light"**, which is the more interesting half of the trait for a RimWorld gene pair.
And the canon **inhibitor-chip immunity / resistance to sustained neural prompting**
has no gene at all; `Turn_Gene_SlowNeuralHeat` is a psycaster-heat gene and is not the
same thing. `AptitudeRemarkable_Social` and `Turn_Gene_TraitGrandeur` are well founded
by the caste-climbing and manipulation text. ⚠️ **Cross-def inconsistency:** the same
canon trait — ultraviolet vision — is given as `DarkVision` here and given **no gene at
all** on `RSW_RimMandrakeKaminoan`, though the Kaminoan canon infobox lists it as that
species' sole distinction.

## ruling

🔴 **RULED — owner, 2026-09-20. Verbatim: *"Umbaran too, greyish skin with a faint blue
tone."***

Shipped as `RSW_Skin_GreyBlue` (158,163,172) on `RSW_RimMandrakeUmbaran`, replacing
`Outland_Skin_PaleBlue` + `Outland_Skin_PaleAzure`.

🔑 **His ruling is narrower than the canon infobox, and it wins.** The infobox says
*"Pale and bluish"* and the 2026-09-17 pass fitted two blue genes to it. He is ruling
on how the species reads in OUR game: **grey dominant, blue as a faint tone.** The new
value carries 14 points of blue lift over its red channel, against `RSW_Skin_SlateBlue`'s
25, and sits one step off `RSW_Skin_MidGray` (162,162,162). ⛔ Do not drift it toward
either neighbour.

⛔ **Lavender-violet stays out, and both lines of evidence agree.** The words "lavender"
and "violet" appear **nowhere** in the canon article (independently re-verified against
the raw wikitext, 2026-09-20). It was only ever our own agent's reading of one painted
illustration.
