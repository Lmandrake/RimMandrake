# Gungan

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeGungan` (`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`;
`<label>` is lowercase `gungan` in the def). Assigned `Jawa_DeepwaterCompact: R` — rare,
and only in the water faction, which is a sensible placement for an amphibian.

The canon article is large and fully sourced; **no `/Legends` fallback was needed** and
none was fetched.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Infobox** (`/wiki/Gungan`):

- **Class: amphibian.** **Two races: Otolla and Ankura** (*Ultimate Star Wars*) — the
  infobox carries **two** images, one per race.
- **Height: 2 meters** (*Nexus of Power*). **Mass: 75 kg / 165 lbs** (*Absolutely
  Everything You Need to Know*). 🔑 2 m at 75 kg is **tall and markedly lanky**.
- 🔴 **Skin colour: brown, green, orange, pink, purple, white** — six, each with its own
  citation (*The Phantom Menace* for brown/green/orange/white, TCW "Blue Shadow Virus"
  for pink, TCW "Gungan Attack" for purple).
- 🔴 **Eye colour: blue, green, orange, purple, yellow** — five, each cited.
- **Distinctions: haillu (the earlobes); extendable tongues; EYE STALKS; three toes.**
- Origin **Naboo**. **Diet: omnivorous — molluscs and aquatic plants.** Languages
  **Gunganese** and Basic. ⚠️ **Lifespan: empty — unsourced.** Do not invent one.

**Biology and appearance** (canon, close to verbatim). Gungans were **tall humanoids
with a flexible structure and strong leg muscles which allowed them to live in the
waters of Naboo.** Their lives revolved around water but they **did not need it to
thrive and could settle far from it**, moving between land and water without
difficulty; native to Naboo's fresh water, they could nevertheless **survive salt
water** (the seas of Mon Cala). 🔑 **"Born as tadpoles, Gungan infants developed limbs
within months of being hatched"** — the species **hatches**, and younglings **learn to
swim before they learn to walk.** **Equipped with hardy lungs, they could hold their
breath for extended periods.** 🔑 **"Their body produced a mucus with waterproofing
properties… and they were capable of spitting it over a distance with great force, even
when underwater."** 🔑 **"Their skeletal structure was extremely flexible, as it was
made of cartilaginous material rather than hard bone."** **The long Gungan face had a
tough yet flexible bill for burrowing and rooting out food.** Some Gungans had
**whiskers on their bills, a sign of maturity.** They had **long muscular tongues** used
to scoop molluscs and lesser amphibians from the swamps, **eaten raw**.

🔑 **The two races, verbatim:** *"the lanky **Otolla**, who sported prominent bills and
eyes set on short stalks, and the heavier **Ankura**, whose eyes were hooded. The Gungan
earlobes called **haillu** were most prominent in the Otolla."*

**Society and culture** (canon). City-based clans and city governments answering to a
**High Council**. **Highly stratified; the society placed a high value on overall peace,
shunned violence, and condemned even minor crimes such as vandalism with corporal
punishment or exile.** Each city kept an army. 🔴 **"Though sometimes dismissed as
primitive, the Gungans had advanced technology, including bubble projectors, bongo
submarines and weaponry"** — they mined **energy plasma** from the planet's crust to
power cities and weapons, and preferred technology tied to their world's ecology. They
**domesticated kaadu, falumpaset and fambaa**; **taming a kaadu and bonding with it was
the mark of adulthood.** **"Unlike their human neighbors, the Gungans were a proud
warrior race,"** maintaining the standing **Gungan Grand Army**. Gungans **rarely left
Naboo**; *"the only Gungans seen abroad tended to be exiles, cast out… by the strict,
unforgiving laws that governed their people."* 🔑 **That is the canon hook for a Gungan
appearing off-world in this campaign at all: an off-world Gungan is an exile.**

**The Force.** *"The rate of Force-sensitives in Gungan society was low, and no native
traditions ever emerged. Few Gungans ever joined the Jedi Order, and none were ever
reported as becoming Sith"* — though **Roo-Roo Page** was Force-sensitive and targeted
by Sidious. **"Culturally, the Gungans were in tune with the Living Force and had a
working understanding of it."**

🔴 **On intelligence — the record is the opposite of the stereotype.** The article
explicitly rebuts "primitive," and cites the Gungan scientist **Thaddeus Wolk**, *"a
leader in the field of theoretical hyperspace physics"* who **taught at the Academy of
Carida**. **Dave Filoni, quoted on the same page:** *"There was some pretty big effort by
us and George to say that, outside of Jar Jar, the Gungans themselves aren't a goofy
group… They're actually quite efficient warriors."* **Jar Jar is canonically the
anomaly, not the type specimen.**

**Unusual abilities, summarised:** amphibious with **hardy lungs and long breath-hold**;
**cartilaginous skeleton** giving extreme flexibility; **forcefully spat waterproofing
mucus**, usable underwater; **extendable/long muscular tongue**; **bill** adapted to
burrow and root; hatched from eggs as **tadpoles**.

## Visual brief

The three references are all high-fidelity 3D/render sources, and they were chosen to
cover **both races and both sexes** — which matters more here than for any other species
in this batch, because the two races do not look alike.

🔴 **The single skin gene in the def cannot represent this species.** Across the three
images the skin is **mottled tan-olive with orange highlights** (Otolla male),
**saturated mid-green** (Ankura male), and **strong magenta-pink with pale belly
patches** (Otolla female) — and the infobox cites **six** colours with sources. The
Gungan palette is genuinely wide, and the def picks one narrow brown.

**Common to all three:**
- **A long, broad, flattened DUCKBILL muzzle** projecting well forward of the skull,
  with a wide flexible mouth line along it and a slightly bulbous tip. It is the
  species' primary silhouette feature.
- 🔑 **Eyes on short stalks — two separate mobile pods rising from the top of the
  skull**, each with a visible lid, well above and behind the bill. On the Otolla male
  they are amber-yellow; on the Otolla female **purple**. This is the second silhouette
  feature and the one a text prompt never gets right.
- 🔑 **Haillu: long pendulous ear-flaps hanging from the sides of the head**, leathery
  and strap-like, reaching to the shoulder or below. On the male they hang loose over a
  head-cloth; on the female they are **tied back into a long tail** — so they are
  handled like hair even though they are ears. Confirms "most prominent in the Otolla."
- **Skin is leathery and mottled**, matte, with darker blotching over knees, thighs and
  shoulders and a paler ventral surface. Not smooth and not scaly.
- **Three broad splayed toes per foot**, clearly countable in both Otolla images — the
  sourced "three toes." **Hands have four long digits** with small dark nails.
- **No hair anywhere.** No beard. (Bill whiskers are a separate sourced feature and are
  not visible on any of these three individuals.)

**Otolla vs Ankura, which the def does not distinguish at all:**
- **Otolla** (`wookieepedia_otolla_warrior.jpg`, `..._peppi_bow.jpg`): **extremely
  lanky** — long thin arms, very long bandy legs that bow outward and flare at the
  calf, narrow chest, no visible musculature. The 2 m / 75 kg figure clearly describes
  *this* race. Prominent bill, eyes on stalks, long haillu.
- **Ankura** (`wookieepedia_ankura_boss_nass.jpg`): **heavy, rotund, short-limbed**, a
  wide barrel torso, thick jowls, a much shorter and blunter bill, and — matching the
  text — **hooded eyes with NO stalks**, plus **short haillu** hidden under a crest.
  Skin a saturated green. An Ankura and an Otolla read as different creatures.
- **Dress and gear:** woven/leather tabards, sashes, wrist wraps and simple sandals;
  the warrior carries an **atlatl-style throwing stick with a glowing blue plasma
  energy ball** and a wicker holder of spares. **This is the visible face of the
  "advanced technology" line** — the weapons are energy weapons in organic-looking
  housings, and reading Gungan gear as stone-age primitive is a canon error.

**`donor_current_sprite.png`** is
`RimMandrakeSW/OR/Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Heads/Gungan/Male_Normal_south.png` — real
Gungan-specific art (512×512 RGBA), and shape-wise it is **the best donor sprite in this
batch**: it has the **broad duckbill snout with two nostril slits** and **two distinct
eye pods raised on stalks above the skull**, i.e. both diagnostic features. What it
lacks is the **haillu** (delegated to the separate `RSW_GunganEars` gene, whose art
exists at `OR/OuterRim/Genes/Ears/Gungan_*.png`), the mottling, and any Ankura variant.
🔴 **But it is a pure greyscale mask (measured: zero channel spread across 29,202 opaque
pixels) and `RSW_Gungan_FemaleNormal` / `RSW_Gungan_MaleNormal` both carry
`DefModExt_HeadTypeStuff` with `<useSkinShader>false</useSkinShader>`** — so the game
will **not** tint it from the skin gene. The head renders off-white/grey on a
pale-brown body.

## Must show
Two canon races (lanky Otolla, heavy Ankura) look genuinely different — items below note
where they diverge rather than averaging them.
- [ ] A long, broad, flattened duckbill muzzle projecting well forward of the skull
- [ ] Otolla: eyes on short mobile stalks rising above the skull. Ankura: hooded eyes with no stalks
- [ ] Haillu: long pendulous leathery ear-flaps hanging from the sides of the head — long and prominent on Otolla, short and covered on Ankura
- [ ] Leathery, mottled, matte skin with darker blotching over knees/thighs/shoulders and a paler ventral surface — not smooth or scaly
- [ ] Three broad, splayed toes per foot; hands with four long digits
- [ ] No hair anywhere on the head or face

## Engine limits
- **The shipped head cannot show any skin colour at all.** Both Gungan `HeadTypeDef`s set
  `useSkinShader: false` over a texture measured as pure greyscale (zero channel spread
  across 29,202 opaque pixels), so none of the six sourced skin colours (brown, green,
  orange, pink, purple, white) reach the face — the head renders untinted regardless of
  the skin gene chosen.

## Def-versus-canon (flagged — not fixed)

- 🔴 **`AptitudePoor_Intellectual`, and the def's own `<description>` line "but have a
  lower intelligence than humans", contradict the canon article directly.** The article
  goes out of its way to rebut exactly this: *"Though sometimes dismissed as primitive,
  the Gungans had advanced technology…"*, it names a **Gungan theoretical-hyperspace
  physicist who taught at the Academy of Carida**, and it quotes Dave Filoni saying the
  goofiness was **Jar Jar specifically, not the species**. The def has encoded the
  fandom stereotype the source material explicitly denies. This is the most serious
  finding in this entry.
- 🔴 **Nothing in the def makes a Gungan AMPHIBIOUS.** Canon class is literally
  *amphibian*: hardy lungs, extended breath-holding, freshwater and saltwater capable,
  underwater cities, swimming learned before walking. The gene list contains **no
  aquatic, swimming, breath, or water-related gene at all.** For the species' assignment
  to `Jawa_DeepwaterCompact` this is the gap that matters most.
- 🔴 **The skin gene range is far too narrow.** One `Outland_Skin_PaleBrown` against
  **six sourced skin colours** (brown, green, orange, pink, purple, white) — and the
  wiki's own two infobox images are a **tan-olive** and a **green**. Boss Nass being
  green and Peppi Bow being pink are both cited screen appearances. **There is likewise
  no eye-colour gene** for the five sourced eye colours.
- 🔴 **The brown skin will not reach the face anyway.** Both Gungan `HeadTypeDef`s carry
  `<useSkinShader>false</useSkinShader>` (`Defs/HeadTypeDefs/SW_HeadTypes.xml`), and the
  head texture is a **measured pure-greyscale mask** — so the head renders untinted.
  (Identical defect to `RSW_RimMandrakeNelvaanian`'s borrowed Bothan head.)
- 🔴 **The Otolla/Ankura split is not represented.** `Body_Thin` +
  `RSW_BodySizeGene_big` describes the **lanky Otolla** correctly and the **heavy,
  short-limbed, hooded-eyed Ankura** not at all. One xenotype cannot carry both, but the
  omission should be a decision, not an accident.
- ⚠️ **`Outland_LeaperLegs` overstates a mis-paraphrase.** Canon attributes the strong
  leg muscles to **living in the water** — the def's description instead says they let
  Gungans "jump around with ease." Jumping is not a sourced Gungan ability; swimming is.
- ⚠️ **No `nameMaker`**, so Gungan pawns get default human names, despite the species
  having a very distinctive naming style on screen (Nass, Tarpals, Lyonie, Peppi Bow,
  Rish Loo, Roo-Roo Page).
- ⚠️ **`Fertile` is unsourced.** Nothing in the article addresses Gungan fecundity; the
  tadpole life stage says how they are born, not how many.
- ⚠️ **`AptitudeStrong_Social` is unsourced** — and sits oddly beside a canon culture
  described as **strict, stratified, and quick to exile**.
- ⚠️ **Sourced features with no gene at all:** the **extendable/long muscular tongue**
  (an infobox distinction), the **cartilaginous skeleton** (extreme flexibility — a
  natural fit for a flexibility/injury gene), the **forcefully spat waterproofing
  mucus**, the **bill whiskers that mark maturity**, and the **three toes** (also an
  infobox distinction).
- ✅ **Correct and worth keeping:** `RSW_GunganHead` (its own head, and the art has both
  the bill and the eye stalks); `RSW_GunganEars` (the **haillu** — sourced, and one of
  only two prominent silhouette features); `Outland_EggLayer` (**well sourced** —
  Gungans hatch, and are born as tadpoles; the Feeorin def was flagged last batch for
  carrying this gene with *no* source, so this is the case where it is right);
  `Body_Thin` + `RSW_BodySizeGene_big` (sourced **2 m / 75 kg**, lanky — correct for
  Otolla); `Hair_BaldOnly` + `Beard_NoBeardOnly`; `Outland_UnusualSpeech` (sourced —
  Gunganese, and *"strong accents when speaking in Galactic Basic"*); **no psychic or
  Force genes** — correct, since canon says Force-sensitivity was **rare** with **no
  native tradition**; and **no lifespan gene**, correct, since lifespan is unsourced.
