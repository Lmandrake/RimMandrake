## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Legends (the wiki page `Uvak` carries the Legends template; no separate canon page exists) — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uvak

Uvak were a large, membranous-winged reptavian species with sharp beaks. They were native to the planet of Kesh, where they were used by both the indigenous Keshiri and the Human Lost Tribe of Sith for transportation. Uvak were known to become attached to their riders and could be temperamental when separated from them. On Kesh, in the time before the Omen crash-landed, uvak riders, called Neshtovar, were highly respected members of Keshiri society and were thought to descend directly from the Skyborn.

Adari Vaal had inherited an uvak named Nink from her Neshtovar husband after he died. This same uvak is the one she used to escape the Keshiri mob and later during the Keshiri revolt against the Sith on Kesh around 4975 BBY. Later, Vaal also rode Nink across the sea to Keshtah Major. Following the Second Galactic Civil War, the Sith Vestara Khai owned an uvak named Tikk, which she obtained in a competition at the age of ten years old.

**Biology and appearance** Uvak were large quadruped reptavians with large leathery wings, sharp beaks, and large tails. Like most predators, they had one set of emerald eyes at the front of their heads. A single uvak was larger than a full-grown Keshiri or Human being. Uvak also had four digits on their paws which ended in sharp claws. As a reptilian species, uvak were covered entirely in scales and were hatched from eggs. Due to their strength and stamina, uvak were used as steeds and beasts of transportation in Keshiri society.

Infobox skin colours are purple and orange (Lost Tribe of the Sith: Spiral 3), eyes emerald (Skyborn).
Uvak were temperamental and bonded strongly to riders (a rider's death could drive them insane); wild uvak without riders were reputed slothful and hostile; they were intelligent and could develop personalities. Each uvak was capable of carrying a maximum of two Human-sized individuals. The uvak were also capable of flying for long distances across landmasses and oceans by tapping into a jetstream current in Kesh's upper atmosphere. However, long-distance travel between Keshtah Minor and the other continents Alanciar and Eshkrene was considered by many Keshiri and Sith alike to be very dangerous if not impossible. While uvak were known to live on Keshtah Minor and Alanciar, they did not live on the polar continent of Eshkrene in the planet's South Pole due to its harsh and frozen environment.

  curved dark talons, a long mobile neck, and huge leathery bat-style wings attached at the shoulders, membrane
  stretched over long finger bones; wingspan far beyond the body.
- **Head**: a large, rounded, BIRD-LIKE head with a long deep beak and a huge gaping mouth; a pale bony crest/brow
  plate over a single round pale emerald/teal eye on each side of the head.
- **Skin**: dark charcoal, grainy, pebbled hide on the body and legs; grey-blue head and neck; the wing membrane a
  translucent pinkish-brown with darker bone ribs.
- **Size**: larger than a horse; one rider sits saddled at the base of the neck.
- **Disagreement (loud)**: the deleted comics showed near-black bodies with vivid red-orange wings and glossy
  cartoon highlights; the realistic painting is muted — charcoal, grey-blue and a dull pinkish-brown membrane. Use
  the muted painting palette.
- **Juveniles** (`canon_2`, comic, anatomy only): small, thin, long-tailed, big-eyed, pterosaur-like, with big wings
  relative to the body; the comic's purple/pink/lilac colours are NOT a target.
- **Prose vs images**: prose says sharp beaks and emerald eyes — both shown; prose places the eyes on the front of the
  head, the painting shows them at the side.
