# Chak-root (plant)

**defName**: `RSW_Plant_Chakroot_Wild` (label "wild chak-root plant")

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chak-root/Legends

Chak-root was a flavourful **red** plant that grew in the marshlands of Erysthes and could also be found on Naboo. Its sale carried high taxes, so it was a favourite of smugglers. It could be made into a liquor and a snuff. Infobox: origin Erysthes, habitat marshlands, description "reddish-coloured". The page does not describe foliage or root shape, nor farming on any particular world. (Source: A Guide to the Star Wars Universe.)

### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chak-root
The canon article treats chak-root only as a **substance** smuggled by criminals: Tregga in *Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide*; Alfris Sotin's claimed supply, the Spice Runners of Kijimi and the Guavian Death Gang in *Poe Dameron: Free Fall*; it also lists *Star Wars Outlaws* as an appearance, and its own infobox image is `Chak-root-Outlaws.png`. It says nothing of a plant's form; the plant description above is Legends.
