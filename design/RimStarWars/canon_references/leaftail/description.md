## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leaf-tail

The leaf-tail was a species of non-sentient winged rodent found on the planet Dagobah. Leaf-tails were about thirty centimeters in length with a wingspan of roughly the same width. They possessed a membrane on the end of their tail that resembled the leaf of a gnarltree, giving the leaf-tail its name and allowing the rodents to camouflage themselves and avoid predators.

Leaf-tails were flying omnivores, and although they preferred berries, they could sustain themselves on insects and other smaller rodents. Individual leaf-tails posed no threat to sentients, but should an individual come across a leaf-tail nest, dozens of the rodents could attack in a swarm using their teeth. During the Galactic Civil War, the tiny handful of smugglers who occasionally traveled to Dagobah in order to capture jubba birds told tales of leaf-tails causing near catastrophic damage to starships by chewing through cabling.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leaf-tail/Legends

Leaf-tails were a small rodent species native to the planet Dagobah.

**Description** A non-sentient species of winged rodent, leaf-tails had membranous leaf-like tails that aided in balance and camouflage. Omnivorous, leaf-tails ate both berries and rodents to sustain itself. They had expandable cheeks used to gather food&mdash;typically cyanoberries. They had great agility which helped them jump from tree to tree. Living in nests, leaf-wings attacked in swarms of dozens. The rodents were the main prey of creatures such as greater bogwings.
