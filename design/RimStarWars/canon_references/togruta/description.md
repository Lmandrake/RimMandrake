# Togruta

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeTogruta` (verified present in
`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`; matches the
defName recorded in `RACES_TODO.md`). Assigned `Jawa_WildsteamClan: S`,
`OutlanderCivil: S`.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

### Current canon (`Togruta`)
Infobox: **near-human**, sentient. **Skin colour: blue, orange, purple, red, white,
yellow.** Eye colour: black, blue, brown, green, orange, yellow. Distinctions: **lekku**
and **montrals**. Origin **Shili** (Expansion Region); habitat **mountains**; languages
Galactic Basic and **Togruti**. **Height, mass and lifespan are BLANK in the canon
infobox — unsourced, do not invent them.** A colony of ~50,000 Togruta lived on **Kiros**.

Verbatim on appearance: *"The Togruta were a **tall** near-human species, characterized by
their colorful skin tones, large montrals, head tails, and white facial pigments."* Skin
*"spanned a vast array of colors, including, but not limited to, orange, red, white, and
yellow"*, and — a functional, not decorative, explanation — *"These characteristics were a
result of evolution; Togruta coloration was helpful as a method of camouflage from
predators."*

🔑 **The head appendages, verbatim and exactly countable:** *"two large cone-like horns
called montrals atop their skull; and **three lekku**, also known as headtails, that
sprouted downward. Both a Togruta's lekku and montrals tended to grow until they reached
maturity, with **females generally having longer lekku than the males**. The **male's
montrals, however, were more branched** than the females."*

🔑 **Montrals are a sense organ, and canon says so plainly:** *"The Togruta's montrals
served as the **auditory system** and allowed them to sense the movement of objects around
them through **echolocation**. Their echolocation evolved to aid them in hunting and was
extremely powerful. They could sense up to 82 feet (25 meters)."* Separately: *"Togrutas
were also known to have **6 holes (3 on each side), right where the lekku met the
jawline**. The purpose of these external holes is unknown, but is implied to be ears based
on their location."*

Culture: mountainous/cliff habitats; the Kiros artisan colony *"built their society without
weapons, choosing instead to pursue art and beauty rather than war"*; **many Togruta go
barefoot** because they regard the ground as sacred and *"only wearing boots while
off-world"* — a directly usable apparel note. Shili's government could be led by a queen
(Regasa, *Supreme Huntress of the United Tribes*).

### The `Lekku` article (canon) — the precise geometry
This is current canon, not Legends, and it is more specific than the species page:

*"The name 'lekku' was also used to describe the striped head-tails of the Togruta. That
species, however, had three such appendages: **two lekku to the front, falling over the
chest, and one thicker lek that was centered at the rear base of their skull.** As seen on
Governor Gupat Roshti, **male Togruta's lekku were shorter than those of females**. The loss
of a lek, as seen on the bartender Selda, was not a crippling injury, but **the appendages
did not grow back** afterwards. **A Togruta's montrals were connected to their lekku.** The
**color of a Togruta's lekku was determined by their DNA.**"*

🔑 **Growth is staged, and canon gives the stages:** *"Lekku grew as their wearer matured. As
an infant, lekku were only found **around the Togruta's head**. In adolescence, the lekku
extended only **slightly past the collarbones**, but could reach **below the waist** by
adulthood."* The elder Gantika's reached only to her stomach — so length is not a
monotonic function of age.

Two notes from the same article worth carrying: lekku are shared by **Buktari, Twi'leks,
Togruta, Tosarvi, Walunas and male Ozrelanso** — *"The Togruta sported three of them, while
the other species had two"* — and Wookieepedia itself records that a licensed novel
(*The High Republic: Into the Dark*) **misidentified a Togruta's lekku as her montrals.**
Even professional Star Wars authors confuse these two organs, which is precisely why this
entry spells the count out.

### Legends (`Togruta/Legends`) — where the only numbers live
Legends infobox: **mammal**, **height 1.7 meters**, **lifespan up to 94 standard years**
(child 1–11, young adult 12–17, adult 18–54, middle age 55–74, old 75–94, venerable 95+),
**diet carnivorous**, distinctions *"**Sharp canine teeth**, Two Montrals, **Three or four
Head-tails**"*. Legends skin colours: red, blue, **green**, white, orange, purple.

Legends adds detail canon does not contradict and which matters for art:
- *"Unlike Twi'leks, a Togruta's lekku were **part of their montrals and ran parallel to
  the head, creating the appearance of wearing a headdress**."*
- *"Togruta had rusty skin tones ranging in hue from blue to red to green, with **white
  pigmentation on their faces** and **grayish lips**. While not all had this feature,
  **white stripes** also adorned various parts of their bodies, including their chests,
  legs, backs, arms and montrals. **The pattern of stripes were, like fingerprints,
  different in each individual.**"* The stripes are camouflage inherited from ancestors
  blending into Shili's **red-and-white turu-grass**.
- *"two montrals with three, and on rare occasions, four large lekku, whose **stripes were
  darker than those of the montrals**."*
- *"Young Togruta's montrals and **posterior lek** did not fully develop until adolescence,
  when their montrals grew curved and their **third lek grew to a similar length**."*
- *"**The stripes on a Togruta's lekku or montrals may become more radiantly colored after
  vigorous activity.** This would be the equivalent of getting red in the face."*
- *"As predators, Togruta had **sharp canine teeth**, which they used to painlessly kill
  thimiars… causing the creatures' post-mortem death spasms. This gave outsiders the false
  impression that Togruta were venomous."* 🔴 **Togruta are NOT venomous** — that is
  explicitly a misconception in-universe.
- Also capable of *"seeing objects immensely far away from them, too remote for any human
  to see."*

**What is fixed vs. what varies per individual** (a sprite must commit, so this is the
decision list):
| Feature | Fixed by canon | Varies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Montral count | **Exactly two**, cone-like, atop the skull | curvature; **branching (more in males)**; length grows to maturity |
| Lekku count | **Three** (canon); Legends allows a rare fourth | — |
| Lekku arrangement | two forward, **falling over the chest**; **one thicker lek centred at the rear base of the skull** | length (**females longer, males shorter**); the posterior lek only reaches full length in adolescence |
| Lekku length | grows with age: infant = **around the head only**; adolescent = **just past the collarbones**; adult = **can reach below the waist** | not monotonic — the elder Gantika's reached only her stomach |
| Stripe banding | present on montrals and lekku; **lekku colour is determined by DNA** | **the pattern itself is individual, like a fingerprint**; lekku stripes darker than montral stripes |
| Facial pigment | **white**, on the face | pattern is individual |
| Skin | saturated single hue | blue / orange / purple / red / white / yellow (canon); +green in Legends |
| Hair | **none** — the canon infobox haircolor field is empty and no Togruta has hair | — |

## Visual brief

All five references agree closely and are much more specific than the prose:

- **Montrals are two thick, hollow, upward-and-outward-sweeping cones**, rising well above
  the crown — in `wookieepedia_shaakti_montrals.jpg` and
  `wookieepedia_infobox_shaakti.jpg` they are roughly **as tall again as the head itself**.
  This is the proportion a text prompt gets wrong: they are not small horns, they are a
  head-sized structure.
- 🔴 **The banding is the single most load-bearing visual fact, and it is bold.** Every
  reference shows **dark transverse chevron/V bands on a pale cream-to-white base**, on
  both montrals *and* lekku. On Shaak Ti the bands are near-black on off-white and read as
  clean chevrons pointing up the montral; on Ahsoka
  (`wookieepedia_ahsoka_art.jpg`, `wookieepedia_two_kinds_of_lekku.jpg`) they are **blue**
  on white; on the Kiros pair they are grey-brown on cream. **The base is always pale and
  the bands always dark and transverse** — the hue of the bands varies, the arrangement
  does not. A plain unbanded montral is the failure mode.
- **The pale montral/lekku base is NOT the skin colour.** Shaak Ti is a saturated red with
  cream-and-black montrals; Ahsoka is orange with white-and-blue montrals; the Kiros male
  is ochre-yellow with cream-and-brown. In every case the appendages are far paler than
  the body. **Two colour regions are required.**
- **The two forward lekku descend a long way.** On Shaak Ti they pass the shoulders, run
  down the chest and **taper to points at about the waist**; the Kiros female's are longer
  still. Ahsoka's are shorter (mid-chest) — length is where individuals differ, but "hangs
  below the shoulder" is the floor. **The third, posterior lekku is not visible in any of
  these frontal references** — it is behind the head — so a south-facing sprite legitimately
  shows two, but a north-facing one should show the third.
- **Montrals and lekku read as one continuous structure**, which is exactly the Legends
  "appearance of wearing a headdress" line: the lekku emerge from the montral bases beside
  the jaw rather than from separate roots. `wookieepedia_two_kinds_of_lekku.jpg` is the
  decisive image here because it puts a Twi'lek and a Togruta side by side: **Hera
  Syndulla's two lekku are smooth, unmarked and the same green as her skin, and she has no
  montrals at all; Ahsoka's are pale, banded, and fused into a montral headdress.** If a
  sprite gets these two species confused, this is the image that settles it.
- **White facial pigment is a patterned mask, not a wash.** Shaak Ti: large white patches
  enclosing both eyes and the forehead against a red central face-mask, plus two small red
  brow bars. Ahsoka: white brow markings, white cheek chevrons, and a dark diamond on the
  forehead. `wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg` shows a third, swirlier pattern. **The pattern
  is individual; the presence of white is not.**
- **Male vs. female, from `wookieepedia_kiros_male_and_female.jpg`** (the wiki's own caption
  is "A male and female Togruta"): the male's montrals carry **extra forward-hooking
  points** — visibly more branched, confirming the prose — and his lekku are shorter; the
  female's lekku are very long. The male is **ochre-yellow**, the female **near-white with
  brown banding**. Both of those skin colours are canon and both are missing from the repo
  def (below).
- Faces are otherwise near-human: human eyes and mouth, **no visible nose bridge or
  external ear**, greyish lips (Legends). Shaak Ti's hands are ordinary five-fingered.

### The repo's art, and what it gets wrong
`donor_current_sprite.png` is `Female_Togruta0_south.png` — and it is worth knowing that
**`Female_Togruta{0,1,2,3}_south.png` are byte-identical to each other** (likewise all four
`_east`, all four `_north`, and separately the four male sets). The four "variants" the
`RSW_TogrutaHead` gene offers differ **only in the mask** (`_southm`), which carries the
white facial marking pattern. Mask 0 is blank (no markings at all); masks 1–3 carry three
distinct Ahsoka-like patterns. The `_southm` masks are also **shared between male and
female**, so the sexual dimorphism canon describes is not expressed in the face art.

The head base itself is a plain pale oval with two eyes — **no nose, no lips, no montrals,
no lekku.** The appendages come entirely from the separate `RSW_TogrutaMontrals` gene,
whose texture (`Montrals_south.png`) is a **single fused silhouette: two upward horns joined
by an arch, with two short tapering points at the sides.** Judged against the references:

- 🔴 **The texture has no banding whatsoever** — it is flat white with a soft grey gradient.
  This is the anooba-stripes failure repeated: the most recognisable feature of the species
  is simply absent from the art.
- 🔴 **The lekku do not descend.** They stop at roughly jaw level, inside the head's own
  footprint. Canon lekku reach the chest or waist.
- 🔴 **Only two lekku are represented.** The third lek — canon in both continuities, and
  specified by the `Lekku` article as **the thickest of the three, centred at the rear base
  of the skull** — is absent. It would be visible in the `_north` (rear-facing) texture,
  which is where the omission shows.
- ⚠️ The montrals are **coloured by `colorType: Hair`**, i.e. by the pawn's hair-colour gene
  rather than skin. That is a defensible design (it does give the appendages an independent
  colour region, which the references demand) but the xenotype's hair-colour pool makes it
  wrong in practice — see below.
- ⚠️ Male and female share one montral texture, so the "male montrals are more branched"
  distinction is not expressed.

## 🔴 Def-versus-canon contradictions found

Reported, not fixed. `RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml` is generated — do not edit it.

1. **Two canon skin colours are missing; two non-canon ones are present.** The def carries
   `Outland_Skin_Red`, `Skin_Orange`, `Skin_Green`, `Outland_Skin_Sage`, `Skin_Blue`,
   `Outland_Skin_Azure`, `Skin_Purple`. Canon skin colours are blue, orange, purple, red,
   **white**, **yellow**. **White and yellow are absent from the def**, and they are not
   obscure — they are the two colours worn by the male and female in the wiki's own
   "male and female Togruta" reference image, both cited to *TCW* "Kidnapped". Conversely
   **green and sage are not current-canon Togruta colours at all** (green appears only in
   the Legends infobox); green is a *Twi'lek* colour, and Skin_Green is also in the Twi'lek
   def. Net effect: the repo can roll a green Togruta but never a white or yellow one.
2. **Montral colour is driven by a hair-colour pool that contains no pale option.** The
   montral gene tints from hair colour, and the xenotype's six hair-colour genes are
   `Outland_HairColor_BrightRed`, `BrightLime`, `BrightSage`, `DarkBlue`, `BrightBlue`,
   `BrightPurple` — all saturated, none white/cream/bone. Plus `Hair_Grayless`, which
   removes the one route to a pale value. **Every Togruta in the game therefore has
   saturated montrals, and canon montrals are pale with dark bands in every single
   reference.** This is the highest-impact finding here.
3. **The montrals gene does not express the sensory function in any way.**
   `RSW_TogrutaMontrals` is `ParentName="RimMandrake_GeneHeadboneHornBase"`, and both the
   gene and that abstract base contain **only `renderNodeProperties`** — no `capMods`, no
   `statFactors`, no `statOffsets`. There is no hearing bonus, no darkness/sight benefit,
   nothing. Its own description — *"large hollow horns that cover the majority of the head,
   with soft fleshy tails extending below the head"* — describes the shape and **never
   mentions hearing or echolocation**, even as flavour text. Canon is unambiguous that
   montrals **are** the auditory system and give 25 m echolocation. The species' defining
   organ is modelled as decoration. (The xenotype carries no hearing-related gene either.)
4. **The xenotype description contains a factual error about lekku count.** It says
   *"large horns with fleshy tails coming down from them known as montrals"* — this calls
   the **tails** montrals. The montrals are the horns; the tails are lekku. It also never
   states there are **three** lekku. The rest of the description is accurate and unusually
   good: *"Their colorful pigments are a helpful camouflage from predators on their
   homeworld"* correctly reproduces the canon evolutionary explanation.
5. **The name-maker's last-name file is loaded but never used.** `RSW_KoTOR_NamerTogruta`
   is correctly Togruta-specific (it reads `RimMandrakeSWNames/SWX/Togruta/{First,Last,Nick}`
   — no wrong-species leakage, unlike the earlier batch's findings). But its `rulesStrings`
   define `lastGenerated->[NameTogruta]` and `[NameTogruta][maybeHyphen][NameTogruta]`,
   while the `LastNameTogruta` keyword bound to the `Togruta/Last` file **appears in no
   rule**. Surnames are therefore generated from the *first*-name list. Cosmetic, but it
   means the curated surname list is dead weight. (The Twi'lek namer has the identical
   defect.)
6. **No invented body-size or lifespan genes** — worth stating explicitly, since the
   earlier batch found those. The def uses `Body_Standard` and adds no age/lifespan gene.
   Canon gives **no** height or lifespan for Togruta, so `Body_Standard` is the correct
   conservative choice and there is nothing to fix. (Legends' 1.7 m and 94-year figures are
   near-human anyway.)
7. **Genes that are canon-supported and should NOT be "fixed":** `Outland_Teeth_Fangs` is
   correct — Legends lists *"Sharp canine teeth"* as a species distinction and describes
   Togruta as predators. `Hair_BaldOnly` / `Beard_NoBeardOnly` are correct — the canon
   infobox haircolor field is empty and no Togruta has hair. `StrongStomach` is a reasonable
   read of the carnivorous, raw-prey diet. ⚠️ `Outland_LowFertility` and
   `Outland_DeceleratedPregnancy` are **unsourced** — no canon or Legends text found in this
   pass says anything about Togruta fertility or gestation.

## Candidate images
- `wookieepedia_shaakti_montrals.jpg` — **the reference of record for the appendages.** A
  tight live-action head-and-shoulders of Shaak Ti: settles montral proportion (as tall
  again as the head), the near-black chevron banding on cream, the thick banded forward
  lekku, the white eye-and-forehead patches against a red face mask, and the greyish lips.
- `wookieepedia_infobox_shaakti.jpg` — the same individual full-body, which is what settles
  **lekku length**: the two forward lekku run the whole chest and taper to points near the
  waist. Also shows ordinary five-fingered human hands and red skin continuing down the
  neck and arms.
- `wookieepedia_two_kinds_of_lekku.jpg` — **the disambiguation image.** Hera Syndulla
  (Twi'lek) beside Ahsoka Tano (Togruta) in one frame: smooth unmarked skin-coloured lekku
  and no montrals, versus pale blue-banded lekku fused to montrals. The single most useful
  image in this directory for preventing the two species being drawn alike.
- `wookieepedia_kiros_male_and_female.jpg` — a *Clone Wars* render of a male carrying a
  female. The evidence for **male montrals being more branched**, for female lekku being
  longer, and for the **yellow and white skin colours the repo def omits**.
- `wookieepedia_ahsoka_art.jpg` — a painted Ahsoka. Stylised (treat line and palette as the
  artist's), but shows a third distinct white facial-marking pattern and blue-on-white
  banding, supporting "the pattern is individual, the presence of white is not."
- `wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg` — the Legends infobox plate, a painted Togruta woman in
  robes. **Legends, so weaker evidence for current canon**, but it independently shows the
  pale-base/dark-band montrals, long banded forward lekku, and a fourth marking pattern.
- `donor_current_sprite.png` — the repo's own `Female_Togruta0_south.png`. **Weak evidence
  about canon and strong evidence about the gap**: a bare pale oval head, no montrals, no
  lekku, no markings, and byte-identical to variants 1–3. See the visual brief for what the
  companion montral texture does and does not do.
