# Ollopom

**defName**: `Ollopom` (third-party, mlie.starwarsanimalcollection, not vendored — see Watch out)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
A Wookieepedia page exists (`https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ollopom`, pageid 623919), current canon.

Ollopoms are short, gentle, non-sentient herbivorous rodents native to the swamps of
Naboo (the Gungan swamps specifically). They are so plant-like that they are often
mistaken for pom petals, an aquatic plant that is also part of their own diet — the
in-universe naturalist Ardis San Tekka describes them as "flat, colorful herbivores...
[that] more closely resemble aquatic lifeforms or even plants."

Body plan and color, per the species infobox and body text:
- **Skin**: pink and yellow(-green) with purple spots.
- **Hair/fur**: green body fur, plus pink hair tendrils.
- **Eyes**: light brown, used to watch for predators.
- **Head**: large, flat, disc/leaf-shaped head (this flatness plus the fur and the
  bloom-like pink tendrils is exactly what makes them read as a plant/lily-pad rather
  than an animal at a glance).
- **Mouth**: small purple beak, with gnawing teeth on the underside used to graze
  floating flora (pom petals and other swamp plants).
- **Limbs**: 6 clawed legs in 3 pairs, used as paddles — they float atop the water
  rather than swimming submerged.
- **Tail**: long, low-hanging, fluked (fish-like) tail.
- Class: rodent (per D23 press material), non-sentient, herbivorous, aquatic/swamp
  habitat, origin world Naboo.

Canon role: mostly a background/prop species. The named individual "Ollie" is a pet
ollopom kept by the Ithorian collector Dok-Ondar in his shop on Batuu (Star Wars:
Galaxy's Edge, set 34 ABY), given to him by the pirate Hondo Ohnaka. Ollopoms later
got a full illustrated entry (traditional acrylic/gouache/watercolor art by Iris
Compiet) in the 2024 reference book *Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the
Galaxy*. The species originates in Legends continuity, first appearing in the 1999
video game *Star Wars Episode I: The Gungan Frontier*, and was carried into current
canon starting with a 2019 D23 article about Galaxy's Edge.

## Visual brief
All four candidate images — the 2024 Bestiary painting, the physical "Ollie" prop
photographed in its tank at Galaxy's Edge, and the two 2006-era Legends illustrations
from *Gungan Frontier* — agree closely and reinforce the same design, with only minor
variation from medium/lighting:

- A flat, oval/lily-pad-shaped body, held at the water's surface, olive-to-yellow-green
  overall with scattered purple/magenta round spots.
- A cluster of long, thin, pink (sometimes magenta-red) tendrils sprouting from the
  head/nape — the "bloom-like hair" — this is the single most distinctive and
  consistent silhouette feature across every image.
- A small dark purple beak and light brown/amber eyes on the flat head.
- Pale, semi-translucent green-gray paddle-like clawed legs visible underwater below
  the flat top, three pairs, tipped in purple/violet claws.
- A long fluked (fish-tail-shaped) tail trailing behind, same green with occasional
  purple spotting.

The one apparent disagreement is more about presentation than design: the "Ollie"
prop photo reads noticeably more pink/magenta overall than the paintings, but this is
consistent with the tank's dim purple/UV show lighting at Galaxy's Edge, not a
different color scheme — the same purple-spotted, green-yellow base and pink tendrils
are still legible under that light. **Treat the 2024 Bestiary painting
(`wookieepedia_bestiary_infobox.jpg`) as the anchor reference** — it's the newest,
highest-detail, official-book illustration, and it agrees with the older Legends art
on every structural point (flat spotted body, pink tendrils, purple beak, paddle legs,
fluked tail), so nothing about the physical-prop lighting should be read into a canon
color change.

A genuine donor mod sprite was also found (see Watch out) and it is a good match: the
RimWorld mod's flat-shaded south/east textures show the same olive-khaki body with
magenta/pink spots, pink head tendrils, and a fluked tail — i.e. this mod did NOT make
the earlier "generic brown" mistake that motivated this reference library. Use the
donor sprite only as a confirmation of palette/silhouette translation into game style,
not as the canon anchor — the Bestiary painting is.

## Watch out
No direct Steam Workshop screenshot specifically labeled for Ollopom could be
extracted — the Workshop page's screenshot gallery is JS-lazy-loaded and its real
image URLs are not present in the static page HTML. In its place, the mod's actual
shipped donor sprite textures (`Ollopom_south.png`, `Ollopom_east.png`) were pulled
from `emipa606/StarWarsAnimalCollection`, a GitHub mirror of the mod's source repo
that still holds loose PNGs even though the live Steam release packs this art into a
Unity AssetBundle. These are better than a screenshot would have been — they are the
actual shipped art — but note they come from a third-party mirror, not the Steam
Workshop page itself, and were not verified against a currently-running install of the
mod.
