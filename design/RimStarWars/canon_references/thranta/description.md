## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thranta

Thrantas were a large, flying species native to the planet Alderaan. As a species, they survived the destruction of their homeworld due to the relocation of some thrantas to Bespin.

**Biology and appearance** Thrantas were gray-skinned floating beasts with broad triangular wings and a long tail that served as a stabilizer during flight. They greatly resembled the aiwha, a species of winged cetaceans native to Kamino. They emitted mournful cries as they flew.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thranta/Legends

Thrantas were a group of flying animals originally found on Alderaan. While there were multiple species and breeds varying in size and shape, most of them were easily domesticated; with some used for personal travel, others - for massive air ferries, most thrantas served as air transport. A passenger compartment could be placed on creature's back or around its chest, ranging in size from a single seat to enormous public carriages.

Thrantas were buoyed by airsacks under their skin and fed upon small flying creatures and airborne zooplankton.
