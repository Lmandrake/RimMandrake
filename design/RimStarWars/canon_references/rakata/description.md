## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

### Canon (`Rakatan`) — a stub, and its infobox is empty
The **Rakatans**, also known as the **Rakata**, were an ancient **amphibious
humanoid** species native to the planet **Lehon**, who ruled the so-called
**Infinite Empire**. According to legend they were **the first to develop
hyperspace travel**. The species "was said to have eventually gone extinct as a
result of corruption from the **dark side of the Force**."

🔴 **The canon infobox supplies NO height, length, mass, skin colour, hair
colour, eye colour, distinctions, lifespan, habitat or diet — every one of those
fields is blank.** Do not supply any of them from this tier.

Canon deeds, all with dates: long before the Imperial Era the Rakatans **invaded
Kuat** and were **repelled by an uprising**, commemorated by a **Kuati Signet
embedded with a blue kyber crystal** ("Sky stone. The ancient world.", Luthen
Rael to Cassian Andor). Around **25,000 BBY** Rakatan invaders **sacked
Chandrila** and stole the twelve **Chandi Merle** statues from one of its
temples. They shared their name with antiquities called **Rakatan wraith boxes**.
The Infinite Empire's canon entry adds only that the **Aleena**, and possibly the
**Kindalo**, of Aleen were client species, "though there was little evidence of
any occupation," and that the empire dissolved prior to the Imperial Era.

First named in canon in the **German release only** of *Star Wars: Build the
Millennium Falcon* 45; mentioned in *Andor* ("Aldhani", "Harvest").

### Legends (`Rakata/Legends`) — the substance
Also called **the Builders**. Humanoid **with distinctive amphibian features**,
native to **Lehon** in the **Unknown Regions**. Infobox: skin colour **red,
orange, brown, green, turquoise, blue, or gray**; hair **typically none or
black**; eye colour **brown to gold, or blue**; distinctions **horizontal
eyestalks** and **tridactyl limbs**; diet **carnivore / cannibalistic**;
language **Rakata**; subspecies **Flesh Raiders, Bukesek, Droot, Ikalik**.

🔴 **UNMEASURED: no height, no mass, no lifespan is given on either page.** The
only size statement in the entire article is relative — as a species they were
"**uniformly lean and close to the same height**," outsiders "had some difficulty
in distinguishing between individual Rakata," and "**females tended to have a
more slender build**." A number for a Rakata does not exist to be cited. Do not
invent one.

**Appearance.** "Smooth skinned," "amphibian-like" humanoids that had **tall
craniums** along with **eyes that protruded from each side of their heads on
short stalks**. Bipedal, with **three digit hands that were tipped with claws for
digging through packed dirt**. Their **dentition consisted of a multitude of
sharp teeth**, reflecting carnivorous habits. Skin tones "tended to be **gray or
gray-brown**"; "**almost all Rakata had gray or reddish-gray skin**, though this
was a trait of the **dominant lineage of those that survived the ancient
plague**." The rare near-extinct breeds were the **blue-skinned Droot**, the
**red-striped Ikalik**, and the **black Bukesek**.

**Behaviour.** "By nature, the Rakata were **conquerors**," showing "outward
signs of malice," with "violent cores" that "erupted to the surface if pushed."
🔑 **"Enraged Rakata were incapable of being reasoned with and ignored their own
injuries in their desire to destroy those they believed to be their enemies."**
They were known to **eat and defile the bodies of slain enemies and even members
of their own species** — Predor Tul'kar "killed and ate his spies when they were
too obvious about their treachery." They **stripped entire planets** of metals,
ores, compressed gasses and radioactive plasma, **terraformed** worlds to fit
shifting needs, and **killed entire slave workforces**.

Later-era Rakata, "though often primitive, knew they were once much more powerful
and so were driven by a great desire to restore their species to its former
glory"; they were disadvantaged by primitive upbringing "though their
intelligence allowed them to learn to use advanced technology," and were
"intelligent enough to pick up new information, **including other languages,
quickly**" — especially the priest class.

**Two classes, and the split is visible in the costume.** The ruling **priest
class** "wore **long robes with electronic control devices on their forearms**"
and "were responsible for deciphering dead texts." The **warrior class**
"typically wore **angular plastoid armour and carried shocklances**." Those born
in more primitive tribes "tended to wear **armour made of wood and twine**, often
brandishing **fire-blackened spears**." Warriors were "darkly suspicious of the
priests, whom they saw as harnessing powers beyond comprehension," while priests
thought warriors "possessed great physical strength yet lacked brains." Society
was **tribal**, each tribe with its own goals. Priest-class scientists, trying to
recover the Force, "experiment[ed] on living beings… **dissection of animals and
grafting of tissue into prisoners' bodies**," giving them "a monstrous reputation
for brutality."

**Unusual abilities — and the one that matters is a LOSS.**
- The Rakata "were originally a **Force-sensitive species**," taught the Force by
  the **Kwa** but "**drawing only on the dark side**," ignoring the Kwa's
  teaching of balance.
- 🔴 **They "became blind to the Force as a result of a plague that afflicted
  their race," and that is permanent and species-wide.** "Rakata of later eras
  were **all Force-blind**." They "felt this loss on a spiritual level,
  describing it as **a black hole in their chests that consumed their life,
  contentment and happiness**." Every era in which a player could meet a Rakata
  is a post-plague era.
- They used enslaved **Force Hounds** — beings trained in the dark side — to
  locate Force-rich worlds, rather than sensing them personally.
- **No other biological ability is sourced anywhere in the article.** Their power
  was technological, not physical; the article gives them no strength, speed,
  toughness, longevity or regeneration claim.

### Technology — 🔑 this is the load-bearing section
The single most directly relevant sentence in the whole library, from
`Star_Forge`:

> "As was the case with **all apparent products of Rakatan dark side energy
> engineering**, the Star Forge managed to **survive several tens of thousands of
> years idling and without an operational crew by relying on its built-in
> automated repair systems**. This trait was replicated in certain artifacts,
> such as the **Star Map**s. The Rakatan Builders **destroyed** the Star Maps
> during the collapse of their Empire, but **these slowly repaired themselves**
> with the Star Forge's technology. While **the data in each one was incomplete**
> by the time the reformed Revan discovered them, **their combined information
> was enough** to reveal the location of the Star Forge."

Note what that passage does and does not say. It states self-repair as a property
of **all** Rakatan engineering, not a one-off. It gives a **destroyed** artifact
that **came back** — and came back **incomplete but still individually useful,
and sufficient in aggregate**. The `Rakata/Legends` technology section says the
same thing independently: "**Like many Rakatan technologies, the Star Maps were
self-sustaining and capable of regeneration.**"

Other durability evidence, each with its own source:
- **The Kashyyyk Builder Forge** (a terraforming computer) "**lost contact with
  the Rakata in 33,598 BBY**, and **over two hundred years later it
  malfunctioned**, causing a **hyper-acceleration in the growth of the
  wroshyrs**" — and was still running when Revan reached it, its own holocron
  interface reporting it "was last accessed by the Builders **29,642 standard
  years** before 3956 BBY." Its failure mode was **generative, not inert**: it
  kept doing its job, wrongly, for twenty-nine millennia.
- **Lehon's planetary disruptor field**, operated from the summit of the **Temple
  of the Ancients**, was still functioning ~20,000 years later and had been
  **repurposed** — "originally constructed to protect the Star Forge, but it was
  now used to protect the Rakata themselves."
- **Belsavis**: **stasis technology** that "left individuals **immobile though
  conscious** of events and preserved them for centuries," still holding Esh-kha
  prisoners after ~25,000 years; **teleportation technology**; and **power cores
  that drew energy from hyperspace** and, "if overloaded, were capable of
  **destroying an entire star system** with the effects spreading to nearby
  systems." The **Mother Machine**, an AI that "created entire species" trying to
  recreate Rakatan Force-sensitivity, was still operating.
- A **mind prison** — "a peculiar box that trapped the mind of its victim" —
  still worked in Revan's era; escape required taking over the body of the next
  being to open it.
- They "**did not see the Force and technology as being concepts at odds with one
  another**," were "one of a dozen ancient races that had developed **self-aware
  automata**," and "**built self-aware machines powered in part by Force
  energies**." They used "**mechano-organic technology elements** that were
  infused with the dark side."
- Other named devices: **planetary disruptor fields, builder droids, skipships,
  Star Maps, terraforming machines, temples**.
- The **Force-drive** was "the earliest form of the interstellar hyperdrive" but
  "**severely limited**… only capable of perceiving and travelling to worlds with
  a significant Force signature," which is why the empire had huge voids in it.
- 🔑 **A shape rule, if Rakatan machines are ever drawn:** "Many Rakatan devices
  of war were designed according to a common template: **a spherical core between
  wings, limbs or pylons**" — Star Forges, ships, fighters, and annihilators
  alike.
- **The Star Forge itself**: built **30,000 BBY** over the star **Abo**; drew
  matter from the star down fins; had "very little defensive weaponry" and
  "depended entirely on its massive ship production capability as a means to
  defend itself," plus internally manufactured battle droids and heavy blast
  doors. It was "**semi-living**," "gorged on dark-side energies," and "began to
  feed itself by **manipulating its own creators**," causing the Rakatan Civil
  War. Destroyed 3956 BBY by a Republic fleet.

⚠️ **Two counterweights the source insists on, and they are not optional.**
1. **The machines survived; the operators did not.** Losing the Force, "the
   Rakata **lost their ability to manipulate their own technology**. Soon, they
   were forced to rely on **inferior devices that were not dependent on the
   Force**." The priest class "could no longer fully control their own
   technology" because "all their ancient technology operated to its fullest
   potential by way of Force aptitude." Later tribes "had wiped themselves or
   each other out with **weapons they no longer understood**, leaving worlds
   wastelands."
2. **Self-maintaining is not self-operating.** "Without a dark-side user strong
   enough to use it without dying in the process, **the Star Forge went
   dormant**." It kept itself intact indefinitely; it did no work without an
   operator it could survive.

**Scale of the empire**, for context: at its zenith (~**25,793 BBY**) it linked
"**over five hundred** Force-rich worlds," with a population of "**ten billion
Rakatan warriors and over a trillion slaves**." It imploded around **25,200
BBY** — civil war caused by the Star Forge's corrupting influence, then a
**plague** that "**had only infected the Builders themselves**" (theorised to be
a slave-species bioweapon) whose "mysterious mutation… **caused it to strip the
Builders of their powers**." By **1000 BBY** the Rakata of Lehon were extinct,
leaving only refugees in the **Rakatan Archipelago**, where "two tribes had even
begun construction on small (**half-kilometre**) orbital factories similar in
function to the Star Forge." Conquered species included the Selkath, Wookiees,
Kumumgah of Tatooine, Noghri, Humans of Coruscant; by transporting slaves they
"were believed to have **inadvertently seeded swaths of the galaxy with sentient
life**."

## Visual brief

🔴 **START HERE: two of the three images this directory already held are ones the
wiki explicitly rejects as depictions of this species. They must not be used for
appearance, and they were the images most likely to be trusted, because they are
the only two in modern-canon comic art.**

- **`wookieepedia_comic_closeup.jpg`** (File:RakatanOnMonCala-2015StarWars48.jpg)
  sits under the article's own **"Inconsistencies"** heading: an "unidentified
  **Mon Calamari** from the script… was **mistakenly depicted** as a member of
  the Rakatan species." Looking at it, that is exactly what it is — the dome is
  **short, rounded and tapered forward**, the eyes are **big round yellow Mon Cal
  eyes on wide cheek bulges** rather than on stalks, the **mouth is wide and
  thick-lipped**, the skin is salmon-orange, and there are drawn forehead wrinkle
  lines. Every one of those reads is wrong for a Rakata. ⛔ Reference only as a
  negative example.
- **`wookieepedia_poe_dameron_comic.jpg`** (File:Rakata-PoeDameron7.png) is under
  the same heading. Lucasfilm Story Group's **Matt Martin**, quoted on the page:
  "I don't even remember that dude and I'm pretty sure I would have remembered
  approving a Rakata. He's '**generic alien extra #3457**' as far as I'm
  concerned." The page states their species "**remained unspecified**." ⛔ Not
  evidence about this species.
- **`wookieepedia_profile_game_render.jpg`** (File:Rakata_profile.png) is
  legitimate — it is the image the Legends article uses in its own *Biology and
  appearance* section — but it is only **290×290** and cropped to the bust, so it
  settles the head and nothing below it.

**What the five genuine images agree on**, and they agree far more specifically
than the prose:

- 🔑 **The cranium is a tall, BACKSWEPT, blade- or fin-like crest that comes to a
  point — not a rounded dome.** From the front it reads as a narrow vertical
  wedge; in profile it sweeps back and tapers to an edge. All five show it. This
  is the single biggest thing "**tall craniums**" undersells, and a dome is
  exactly what a text-only prompt produces from those two words.
- 🔑 **Eyes on short lateral stalks projecting horizontally out of each side of
  the head**, the eye itself forward-facing on the stalk tip with a visible iris
  and eyelid. **Both eyes are visible in a front view, well outside the width of
  the skull.** "Horizontal eyestalks" is literal, not a figure of speech.
- **The face is a narrow vertical wedge between the stalks, with a SMALL, low,
  downturned mouth** and two small nostril slits. The small mouth is the feature
  that most cleanly separates a real Rakata from the Mon Calamari mistake above.
- 🔑 **The legs are DIGITIGRADE** — a long shank, a backward-angled hock, the
  foot standing on the toes with claws and the heel well clear of the ground.
  Unmistakable in `wookieepedia_legends_infobox.jpg` and
  `wookieepedia_temple_group.png`, and visible on multiple figures in
  `wookieepedia_ancient_empire_art.jpg`. 🔴 **"Amphibious humanoid" will get a
  text-only prompt to draw ordinary plantigrade human legs. It is wrong.**
- **Three-digit hands with pale, bone-coloured claws**, confirming the
  "tridactyl limbs" distinction; feet likewise clawed.
- **Lean, gracile and long-limbed with a narrow torso**, consistent across all
  five and with "uniformly lean."
- **No hair on any individual, anywhere** — consistent with "typically none."

**Colour genuinely disagrees between images, and the prose tells you which to
trust.** `wookieepedia_legends_infobox.jpg` and `wookieepedia_temple_group.png`
(both KOTOR-era 3D renders) are **olive to yellow-green with a pale cream
ventral**; `wookieepedia_group_weapons.jpg` is **grey to grey-pink, mottled, with
darker speckling over the arms and shoulders**; `wookieepedia_ancient_empire_art.jpg`
is a **cool sage grey-green**; `wookieepedia_warrior_combat.jpg` is
**grey-lavender**. 🔑 **Default to grey / grey-brown / reddish-grey** — that is
the sourced skin of "the dominant lineage of those that survived the ancient
plague," i.e. of every Rakata anyone could actually meet — and treat the
olive-green of the two game renders as one game's palette. Eyes read **reddish to
amber-gold** in the painted images, agreeing with "brown to gold" over "blue."

🔑 **A costume finding the prose only half-states: their headgear is
CREST-SHAPED.** In `wookieepedia_ancient_empire_art.jpg` most figures wear
**hoods and cowls that sheathe the cranial crest and follow its taper** — in
orange, maroon, gold and deep blue — over **long robes with wide banded collars,
sashes, belts and capes**, and several wear **segmented gold vambraces on the
forearms**. That last detail is the priest class's "electronic control devices on
their forearms" made visible, and the whole image is the best available reference
for the Empire at its height rather than its ruin. The warrior read is separate:
**segmented dark-red/maroon plastoid plate with vambraces and a heavy boxy belt**
(`wookieepedia_group_weapons.jpg`, `wookieepedia_warrior_combat.jpg`), with
curved pale blades and a long slender polearm — and the primitive tribals in the
same painting wear only **leather strapping over a bare torso** and carry a
broad-bladed hafted **axe** and a bulbous mechanical **rifle**.

**Architecture, for whenever a Rakatan ruin gets built.** Not the species, but
the two best images carry it and nothing else in this library does.
`wookieepedia_ancient_empire_art.jpg` gives **pale blue-white monumental ribbed
and tubular arches** around a **giant vaulted portal**, over a warm orange tiled
floor. `wookieepedia_temple_group.png` gives a **stepped pale-stone ziggurat with
a tall ribbed spire and buttress fins**, half-fallen blocks at its foot, in a
jungle under a large moon. Both are **smooth, organic-industrial, with very few
right angles** — the opposite of blocky.

**`donor_current_sprite.png` is partial evidence.** The copied file is
`RimMandrakeSW/OR/Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Heads/Rakatan/Normal_south.png`; only
`Normal_{south,east,north}` exist, so there is **one head and no variants**. It
is a **greyscale mask**, which is correct and expected for a RimWorld humanlike
head — the game tints it at runtime from the skin-colour gene, so the absence of
colour is not a defect and the colour findings above belong on the *gene*. What
it gets right: the **tall cranium** and the **lateral eyestalks** are both there,
which is more than most donors in this library manage. What is wrong: the
cranium is a **symmetric vertical egg with no backsweep and no point**, the
eyestalks are **plain round balls with a dark slit** rather than stalks carrying a
forward-facing iris, and **there is no mouth at all**. Nothing on disk supplies
the digitigrade legs, the tridactyl clawed hands, or the crest-sheathing hood.

**🔴 Gene-list contradictions, verified against the def and not inferred.**
- **`PsychicAbility_Enhanced` and `Turn_Gene_LatentPsychic` contradict both canon
  and the mod's own description.** The def's description text says the survivors
  were "**left incapable of using the force**," and the article says later-era

- **No skin gene matches the canonical colour.** The wired genes are

- **`Hands_Pig`** is wired where the article's *stated distinction* is

- **`Outland_EggLayer` is unsourced.** Nothing in either page's wikitext says

- Correct and worth keeping: `RSW_Body_gaunt` + `Body_Thin` ("uniformly lean"),
  `BS_Diet_Carnivore` (carnivore/cannibalistic), `Aggression_HyperAggressive` +

  `Outland_WebbedFeet` is defensible from "amphibious," but the only *sourced*

## Candidate images
- `wookieepedia_legends_infobox.jpg` — **the reference of record.** File:Rakata_kotor.jpg, the Legends infobox: a full-body KOTOR game render at 448×720 on black. The only image that settles the whole body at once — backswept pointed cranial crest, lateral eyestalks, small downturned mouth, **digitigrade clawed legs**, three-digit clawed hands, lean long-limbed build, and the warrior kit (pale asymmetric shoulder-drape, segmented maroon chest harness, heavy boxy belt).
- `wookieepedia_group_weapons.jpg` — **the best colour and class reference.** File:Rakata-TUR.jpg, a painted illustration from *The Unknown Regions* showing four Rakata together in a red rocky landscape. Independent of the game renders, and the one that agrees with the sourced **grey / grey-pink mottled** skin of the surviving dominant lineage. Shows armoured warrior, uniformed mid-caste, and two lightly-clad tribals with a hafted axe and a bulbous rifle side by side — the class split made visual.
- `wookieepedia_ancient_empire_art.jpg` — **the highest-value image for anything other than the naked body.** File:Infinite_Empire_JMGD.jpg, 1412×820, from *The Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*: a dozen-plus Rakata in ceremonial dress inside a monumental hall at the Empire's height.
