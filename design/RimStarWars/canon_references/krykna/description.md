# Krykna

**defName**: `RSW_Krykna` — in-repo label "krykna"; no variants

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krykna

Krykna were predatory, non-sentient, spider-like creatures native to Atollon. They were resistant to blaster bolts and highly sensitive to negative emotions, making them extremely difficult to tame with the Force. They were repelled by the signal emitted by sensor beacons.

**Characteristics** Krykna were six-legged, spider-like creatures that had durable skin and cocooned their prey in underground hives. They also had beaked mouths surrounded by a pair of fanged pedipalps. Their skin was highly resistant to blaster bolts, including fire from starship cannons. However, their eyes were vulnerable, and they were also vulnerable to lightsabers and grenades. They had a strong aversion towards the radiation emitted by sensor beacons. They feasted primarily upon dokma. 

Although they could be connected with through the Force, it was extremely difficult, as they were highly sensitive to negative emotions.

The krykna exists only in *Star Wars Rebels*. Deleted: the second Rebels CG render (`Krykna-SWCT.png`) and the in-show frame of Sabine shooting one, both duplicates of what `canon_1` shows. Kept: `canon_1` (the cleanest Rebels CG render, animated, anatomy only) and `canon_4`, Ralph McQuarrie's realistic painting of the "knobby white spider" of Dagobah (*The Illustrated Star Wars Universe*) — the design ancestor, not a krykna and not a scale reference, but the best guide to how the creature should look as a REAL animal: knobby, rumpled, chalky skin under natural cave light.
- **Silhouette**: a pale spider-like creature with a small head-front slung low between the legs and a huge bulbous egg-shaped abdomen that rises tall and upright behind it, so the animal is tall.
- **Legs**: SIX legs per canon (infobox and text, *Rebels* "The Mystery of Chopper Base"; Garazeb: "More than two legs is just... excessive"). The pedipalps beside the beaked mouth are mouthparts, not a seventh and eighth leg. Legs are thin with knobby swollen joints, tapering to a sharp dark claw, and sprawl far wider than the body is long.
- **Head/face**: tiny relative to the abdomen. A flat face with a cluster of four round black bead eyes (two pairs, goggle-like), a beaked mouth with two short curved fanged pedipalps beside it. Ring-shaped joint collars at the leg roots.
- **Colour**: pale bone/ivory white to pinkish-beige (with green-grey moss at the joints in `canon_1`), to chalk white (`canon_4`); canon text says gray skin. Abdomen has faint vein lines and cracks.
- **Texture (realistic target)**: dry, wrinkled, finely pitted leathery-stony hide, bone-like segmented legs, not shiny chitin and not hairy. Render it as real weathered skin — the Rebels render's smooth, evenly lit plastic look is the animation, not the animal.
- **Size cues**: far bigger than a human; the infobox gives height 2 m (6 ft 7 in; *Dawn of Rebellion*, Comprehensive Guide). Lives in hives full of cocoons.
- **Other canon facts** (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krykna): skin gray, eyes black, blaster-resistant but eyes vulnerable, webs produced, repelled by sensor beacons, feeds on dokma; the Force can calm them (Kanan in "The Holocrons of Fate"), so they are hard to tame, not untamable.
