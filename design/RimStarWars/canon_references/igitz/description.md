Sources: *Star Wars Episode I: The Gungan Frontier* (first appearance), *Star Wars Math: Jabba's Game Galaxy*, *The Wildlife of Star Wars: A Field Guide* (the vivarium/diplomat detail), *The Art of Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace*, *The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia*. Infobox: non-sentient, amphibian, blue, Naboo swamps, omnivore; no height or mass given.

Igitz were a species of small amphibians hailing from the planet Naboo. Those blue-skinned, omnivorous critters dwelled in the planet's many dense swamps. Because of the environment in which they had evolved, the igitz were well-adapted to both land and water: while their front paws were clawed for treading on land, their hindquarters were finned, allowing it speed when in water. The igitz were a popular pet in most Gungan civilizations. A certain Gungan diplomat of the New Republic owned one. The diplomat often traveled with his pet igitz to the galactic capital of Coruscant, transporting it in a vivarium shaped like a Gungan hydrostatic bubble.
