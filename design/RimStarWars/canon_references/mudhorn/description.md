# Mudhorn

**defName**: `RSW_Mudhorn` (vendored in this repo's own `src/RimStarWars/SWBestiary` mod)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
Mudhorns were large, non-sentient, omnivorous horned creatures native to the
desert-and-cave planet Arvala-7. Wookieepedia infobox: gray skin, brown hair,
brownish-yellow eyes, a large frontal horn, a lipless mouth. Size ~2.57m tall,
~5.47m long. Biology text: "had a large horn, flat teeth, and long, wooly fur
... often coated in mud" — oviparous, laying a single large woolly-shelled egg
per clutch prized as a delicacy by Jawas, with yellow yolk inside. Canon role:
first appeared in *The Mandalorian* Chapter 2 ("The Child", 2019) — Din Djarin
fights and kills a mudhorn guarding its cave to retrieve its egg, with Grogu's
Force-assist turning the fight; the Armorer later declares the mudhorn Din's
personal signet (a stylized mudhorn skull/horn mark), which becomes the crest
of the new Clan Mudhorn founded with Din and Grogu, appearing throughout the
rest of the series and *The Book of Boba Fett* as his armor's chest insignia.
Also appeared as a zoo animal, in captivity at Lonisa City Zoo, in *The High
Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower* (231 BBY).

## Visual brief
The three candidate images are all real production stills/renders from *The
Mandalorian* and agree completely with each other: a mudhorn is a **heavily
built, low-slung, rhinoceros-like quadruped entirely covered in thick, shaggy,
matted wool/fur** — not bare "gray skin" as the infobox field literally
states; in practice the fur coat is the dominant visual feature and it reads
as **dark muddy brown** throughout (dirtier/lighter brown where mud has dried
and packed into the coat, darker toward the legs and underside). The single
frontal horn is large, thick, and gently curved, colored a contrasting **pale
ivory/bone-tan** against the dark fur — the single most visually distinctive
and identifying feature. The head/face is wide and lipless with a visible row
of blunt, slightly serrated teeth in an undershot jaw, small dark
close-set eyes, and a heavily creased, mud-caked hide visible on the bare
patches of the face around the eyes and mouth. Four short, thick, stump-like
legs support a heavy low body — genuinely rhino-proportioned rather than
bovine.

**The current donor sprite (`donor_current_sprite.png`) is a strong match**:
dark brown/near-black body, a pale grey-tan horn that reads clearly against
the dark coat, a low squat rhino-like silhouette, and a textured/ridged back
suggesting the shaggy coat, plus visible teeth in the jaw. The donor's body
color leans slightly more black/charcoal than the muddier medium-brown seen
in the film references, and the horn could read a touch more contrastingly
pale/ivory, but the overall silhouette, horn placement, and general "shaggy
dark rhino" read is already well aligned with canon — this is one of the
better-matching donors found in this pass.
