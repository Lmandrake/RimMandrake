# Taung

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeTaung` (`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`).
Assigned `Pirate: R`, `TribeCivil: R` — rare in both.

⚠️ **The canon (Disney-era) article is a four-sentence stub with a completely empty
infobox** — no height, mass, skin colour, eye colour, lifespan or distinctions, and
**no biology section at all.** Everything physical below comes from
**`Taung/Legends`**, which is a full article sourced chiefly to *Galaxy at War* and
*Star Wars Insider* 80. Facts are marked canon or Legends throughout.

🔑 **Lore note the owner may care about: the Taung are canonically the PROGENITORS of
the Mandalorians, and in Legends they are extinct** — believed to have died out by
the end of the Mandalorian Wars, surviving only as the Mandalorian culture they
founded. A living Taung population in this campaign is a deliberate choice, not an
accident of the setting.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Canon article** (`/wiki/Taung`) — the entire body: the Taung were an ancient
sentient species of the **Core Worlds planet Coruscant**. The day of **Taungsday** was
named for the species. The **beskad**, a traditional Mandalorian sword, was based on
an ancient Taung design. The **Taung & Zhell Society** was a student group at the
University of Coruscant named for them. Sources: *Star Wars: Build the Millennium
Falcon* 61 (2016), *Star Wars: The Rebel Files*, *Collapse of the Republic*. The only
canon appearance listed is a **mention** in *The Mandalorian* "Chapter 19: The
Convert." That is all the canon article contains.

**Legends article** (`/wiki/Taung/Legends`) — infobox, sourced to *Galaxy at War*
except where noted:

- **Class: humanoid, simian subgroup.** **Height: 2 meters. Mass: 95 kilograms.**
- **Skin colour: gray** (*Insider* 80). **Eye colour: yellow.**
- **Lifespan: 85 years.** Origin **Coruscant**. Language: **archaic Mando'a**.
- Distinctions field: **empty** — the diagnostic features come from the body text.

**Biology and appearance** (Legends, verbatim-close): a humanoid species, the Taungs
were a **bipedal race of sentient simians**. **Gray-skinned and yellow-eyed**, Taungs
were **typically tall and strong** — on average **two meters** and about **ninety-five
kilograms**. They were **known to be extremely resilient, able to endure physical
hardships that many other species could not.** 🔑 **Their five fingers were tipped
with sharp claws, and bony ridges adorned the top of their skulls.** Lifespans reached
and occasionally exceeded **eighty-five years**. **Life stages are unusually well
specified: children until 6, ADULTHOOD AT 13, middle age from 46 to 64, elderly beyond
65.** 🔑 **"Galactic historical records document no known instances of Taungs with a
sensitivity to the Force."**

**Society and culture** (Legends). Society was **nomadic and clan-based**; battle-tested
elder veterans became **chieftains**, with higher authority only in the **Mand'alor**
("Sole Ruler"). **Pragmatism and ruthlessness were common traits, matched only by
loyalty to clan kinsmen.** Naming practice: names corresponding to desirable traits —
**Atin ("persistent"), Kot ("strength"), Tor ("justice")**. Early Taungs waged war as
ritual worship of **Kad Ha'rangir**, a destroyer god personifying change and growth,
while denying **Arasuum**, the sloth-god of idleness. **Disputes were resolved through
blood duels: a challenge to single combat, to the death.** 🔑 **Despite the warring
nature the Taungs were "far from primitive," and maintained a high priority on
procuring the latest technological advancement whenever possible, often by plundering
a defeated foe** — this is a raider-with-good-gear species, not a stone-age one.
**Mandalorian armour began with them**, and their **helmets and war masks were forged
in the image of the Taung face, including slanted outer angles and a pointed chin.**
Their armour was **sparse in armament**, because they held that **unarmed combat
brought the highest honour**; children were **trained in martial arts from a young
age**, which made wandering Taungs **valuable to mercenary companies.**

**History, compressed** (Legends): evolved on prehistoric Coruscant alongside the
**Zhell** (thirteen Human tribes); centuries of war; a volcanic eruption blotted out
the sun for two years, after which the Taungs subjugated the Zhell and named
themselves ***Dha Werda Verda*, "Warriors of the Shadow."** The Zhell later drove them
off Coruscant; they fled to **Roon**, then under **Mandalore the First** conquered the
world they renamed **Mandalore**, recasting themselves as the **Mandalorians**
(*Mando'ade*). Under **Mandalore the Indomitable** they began taking in other species,
which changed Mandalorian identity permanently. Heavy losses in the **Mandalorian
Wars** (culminating in the devastation of **Malachor V**) left them believed extinct,
remembered as **the Progenitors**. An offshoot sect of **priestly female Taungs fled to
the Unknown Regions** to seek atonement through meditation (*The Unknown Regions*).

**Unusual abilities: none.** The only mechanical claims canon or Legends makes are
**exceptional physical resilience** and **explicit Force-blindness**.

## Visual brief

🔴 **This species is a helmet problem.** Almost every image of a Taung shows a
**helmet or war mask**, and the mask was deliberately **forged in the image of the
Taung face** (slanted outer angles, pointed chin) — so masked art looks like
anatomical evidence and is not. Anything drawn from these references must state
whether it is depicting the face or the mask.

- **Colour: the prose says GRAY; the images disagree with each other.**
  `wookieepedia_martial_arts_master.jpg` (the Legends infobox image, *Galaxy at War*)
  reads a clear **olive/yellow-green** over the whole head, neck, arms and legs, while
  `wookieepedia_last_stand.jpg` reads **flat cool grey** on the exposed arms. Trusting
  the images means the species' plate is **grey through olive-green**, not grey alone —
  which happens to be exactly what the def already does (see below).
- **Skull: the defining feature.** A hard, **plated, faceted cranium** with heavy
  **bony ridges over the brow and crown** sweeping back into pointed flanges at the
  temples — the "bony ridges adorned the top of their skulls" text is doing real work.
  The skull is broad and angular, not rounded.
- **Face: strongly wedge-shaped**, narrowing to a **pointed, downturned chin/jaw** that
  projects forward like a beak. **No visible external nose.** The upper lip / muzzle
  region is a hooked bony plate.
- **Eyes: SMALL, deep-set, recessed in dark sockets under the brow shelf, glowing
  yellow-amber.** The yellow of the infobox is confirmed; the *size* is not — these are
  narrow, hooded eyes, not large ones.
- **Hands: long, dark, distinctly clawed fingers** — clearly visible in the
  martial-arts image and matching the "five fingers tipped with sharp claws" text.
- **Build: tall, lean-muscled, long-limbed.** Consistent with 2 m at only 95 kg: this
  is a **rangy** frame, not a bulky one. Do not build a heavyweight.
- **Gear, which is most of what you see:** lamellar/segmented plate in **oxblood red
  and brass over bare limbs**, banded vambraces and greaves, wide belt with a domed
  boss, and **long spears** as the group weapon. The armour leaves the arms and legs
  largely bare — consistent with "sparse in armament" and unarmed-combat honour.
- ⚠️ **Hair is genuinely contested.** `wookieepedia_taung_helmet_comparison.jpg` shows
  **long, straight, dark hair to the shoulders with a topknot**, but that image's own
  wiki caption is *"Taung armored helmets resembled their facial structure"* — so the
  hair may belong to a helmet crest rather than a head. Both other images show no hair
  at all. The infobox `haircolor` field is **empty**. Unresolved; see the def note.

**`donor_current_sprite.png`** is `RimMandrakeSW/SWX/Pawn/HeadType/taung/taung_male_south.png`,
real Taung-specific art (512×512 RGBA), and it is a **greyscale mask that IS runtime-
tinted** — `RimMandrake_TaungHeadBase` carries no `useSkinShader: false`, unlike several
sibling head bases, so the skin genes will colour it. **It captures the right things:**
a broad angular cranium, hard slanted brows, and a pronounced **pointed downturned
chin** — the war-mask silhouette the text describes. **What it lacks:** the faceted
plate texture and the crown ridges (those are delegated to the separate
`RSW_Headbone_taung` / `RSW_FacialRidges_bumpy` / `RSW_Brow_pointy` attachment genes,
whose art exists at `SWX/Pawn/HeadAttachments/taung/`), and its eyes are plain dark
slits rather than recessed glowing amber. Reasonable art; the defects in this species
are in the gene list, not the sprite.

## Def-versus-canon (flagged — not fixed)

- 🔴 **`<description>` is the single character `.`** — the Taung ships with no
  description text at all. (Same defect as `RSW_RimMandrakeGand`, found in the previous
  batch.)
- 🔴 **`Outland_AcceleratedAgeing` contradicts the sourced lifespan.** Canon Taung live
  **85 years and occasionally longer** — *more* than a vanilla RimWorld human, not
  less. This gene shortens life for no sourced reason. By contrast
  **`Outland_AcceleratedMaturation` is correct and well sourced** — adulthood at **13**.
- 🔴 **No `nameMaker` at all**, so Taung pawns get default human names — despite canon
  giving both a naming *principle* (names meaning a desirable trait) and worked
  examples (**Atin, Kot, Tor**). A Taung called "Sarah" is the visible result.
- ⚠️ **`RSW_Ears_smallwebbed` is unsourced.** Neither article mentions Taung ears at
  all, and no image shows webbing; the art exists
  (`HeadAttachments/taung/webears_*.png`) but nothing in canon asks for it.
- ⚠️ **`KillThirst` encodes culture as biology.** Taung war-worship, blood duels and
  martial upbringing are **religious and cultural** in every source; nothing says a
  Taung *physiologically needs* to kill. `Aggression_HyperAggressive`, `Unstoppable`,
  `Outland_Evasive` and `Turn_Gene_FreeSpirit` are likewise unsourced as biology —
  and `Turn_Gene_FreeSpirit` sits oddly against a species defined by **clan loyalty
  and elaborate honour law**. `combatPowerFactor` is set to **1.8**, among the highest
  in the file; that is a balance choice, not a canon one.
- ⚠️ **`Hair_BaldOnly` is asserted where canon is silent** and one wiki image shows
  long hair (see the contested note above). Not a confirmed error — a confirmed
  *guess*.
- ⚠️ **`RSW_Eyes_BigYellow`: the yellow is correctly sourced; "big" is not.** Every
  image shows small, deep-set, hooded eyes.
- ⚠️ **`Body_Standard` alongside `RSW_BodySizeGene_big`.** 2 m at 95 kg is **tall and
  lean**; a "big" size gene reads as mass as well as height, and there is no gene
  expressing the leanness. Minor, but the species should not render as a bruiser.
- ✅ **Correct and worth keeping:** `RSW_Head_taung` (its own head, not a borrowed
  one), `RSW_Headbone_taung` + `RSW_FacialRidges_bumpy` + `RSW_Brow_pointy` (the
  sourced **bony skull ridges** — the best-served canon feature in this def),
  `Outland_Hands_Talons` (sourced **clawed five fingers**), `Robust` and
  `MeleeDamage_Strong` (sourced **extreme resilience** and warrior training),
  `Turn_Gene_Duelist` (sourced **single-combat blood duels**), **no psychic or Force
  genes whatsoever** — which matches the explicit canon statement that no
  Force-sensitive Taung is recorded, and is the thing the Rakata def got wrong.
- ✅ **`RSW_Skin_DarkGreen` + `RSW_Skin_MidGray` together are defensible**, and this is
  a case where the def is *better* than the prose: the article says gray, but the
  wiki's own infobox art is olive-green. Keeping both matches the images.

## Candidate images

- `wookieepedia_martial_arts_master.jpg` — **the reference of record for anatomy.** The
  Legends infobox plate from *Galaxy at War*: a Taung martial-arts master, head and
  torso, in red-and-brass lamellar armour with bare limbs. Best available read on the
  faceted plated skull, brow and crown ridges, wedge face with pointed downturned chin,
  absent nose, small recessed yellow eye, and long clawed fingers. **Reads olive-green,
  not grey** — the prose/image disagreement above. Painted illustration; treat the
  palette as one artist's, but it is the wiki's chosen infobox image.
- `wookieepedia_last_stand.jpg` — "The last stand of the Taungs": a group of Taung
  spear-warriors on a ruined Coruscant structure above a vast Zhell host. **Faces are
  masked**, so it is weak on anatomy — but it is the only reference for **group
  silhouette, exposed skin colour (flat cool grey), spear armament, and the scale of
  the Coruscant war.** Also the clearest demonstration that Taung war masks reproduce
  the slanted-angle, pointed-chin face shape.
- `wookieepedia_taung_helmet_comparison.jpg` — ⚠️ **partial negative reference.** The
  wiki captions this image as showing that *helmets resembled their facial structure*,
  so what it depicts is most likely **a mask, not a face**, and its striking
  shoulder-length dark hair and topknot may be helmet furniture. Kept because it is the
  single best view of that face/mask geometry — heavy brow plate, faceted crown,
  hooked lower jaw, glowing amber eye — but **do not cite it for hair, skin or eye
  anatomy.**
- `donor_current_sprite.png` — real Taung mod art
  (`.../HeadType/taung/taung_male_south.png`), the current in-game head. A tintable
  greyscale mask that gets the angular cranium and pointed chin right and omits the
  plate texture, crown ridges and recessed amber eyes.
