## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Canon.** Anzati were a long-lived humanoid species from the Mid Rim planet of **Anzat**.
They were feared and hated because **they fed on the life force of other sentient species**.
Canon infobox: designation sentient; class humanoid; **skin colour Pale**; hair **black** or
**white**; **eye colour blue**; origin Anzat. **Height, mass, and lifespan are all
unsourced in canon** — the infobox fields are empty and no figure is given anywhere in the
article body. In canon, Anzati agents trained **Aurra Sing** into a killing machine after
she left the Jedi Order; a robed Anzati once got hold of Jedi **Aayla Secura**, but she
survived; and during the Imperial era an Anzati named **Reyna Oskure** was associated with
the Acolytes of the Beyond and answered to Governor Ubrik Adelhard during the Iron Blockade.
Oskure is the source of the only in-universe canon line about the species: *"My people —
the Anzati — are long-lived compared to most. We have seen much."*

🔴 **Canon deliberately unpicked the most famous Anzat.** In Legends, **Dannik Jerriko**
(the pipe-smoker in the Mos Eisley cantina) was an Anzat. In canon, the 2016 reference book
*Star Wars: Complete Locations* identifies Jerriko as a **mutant human** instead. Do not use
Jerriko as an Anzati reference.

**Legends (`Anzat (species)`), where all the numbers and mechanics live.**
- **Height 1.5–1.7 meters. Mass 60 kilograms.** Skin **grayish-hued**. Hair black, brown,
  or white.
- **Lifespan is extraordinary and stepped**: child 1–12 years, young adult 13–99, adult
  100–649, middle age 650–799, old 800–950, **venerable 951+**. *"The youngest Anzati were
  ancient. The ages of the eldest could be measured in eons."*
- **The defining anatomy: two long, prehensile, tentacle-like proboscises kept coiled in
  twin cheek pouches on either side of the nose.** With them retracted into seams beside
  the nose, an Anzat is **practically indistinguishable from a Human**. Faces have
  **bulbous noses and flared nostrils**.
- **They feed by uncoiling the proboscises and inserting them into the victim's nostrils
  and up into the cranium**, drawing out what they call **"soup"** (also "luck," or "the
  Sea of Memory") — the gelid, mucoid medium of the victim's life essence. In most
  humanoids the soup lies in the brain cavity, but Anzati drain it from wherever it resides
  in a given species. Feeding **requires time and cannot be rushed**; they often grasp the
  victim's head to hold it in place. This earned them the nickname **"snot vampires."** The
  victim rarely survives, though an Anzat could keep one alive for several feedings.
- 🔑 **They do not drink blood.** The substance is life essence / brain fluid, not blood.
  This matters for the repo def (see below).
- **Unusual abilities.** Force-sensitive and **telepathic**, growing more adept with age;
  they mesmerise prey with a natural hypnosis Jedi described as akin to a **mind trick**,
  strengthening at close range. **Prodigious speed, agility and reflexes well beyond human
  capability**; strength ranging from ordinary-human to peak-athlete. Highly developed
  sensory organs, unknown in other species, for sensing a strong life essence at a
  distance. Per Jedi Master **Zao**, the soup *is* the future and therefore the Force, so an
  Anzat who drinks a Force-sensitive's soup **strengthens its own Force connection**.
- **No natural biorhythm: no pulse, and consequently no body heat.** How their circulatory
  system worked was a total mystery to xenobiologists. An Anzat that does not feed every
  few days merely grows hungry — starvation, not supernatural aging. Victims of an Anzat
  **do not become Anzati** (explicitly denied, despite the myth).

## Visual brief
**Owner ruling 2026-10-08 (verbatim): "That's realistic, not these cartoon versions you keep using. Replace the canon with something more realistic. The web is full of them."**
The two inked-comic images of Volfe Karkko feeding on Aayla Secura (*Star Wars* (1998) 34; `wookieepedia_volfe_karkko_legends.jpg` and its crop `wookieepedia_anzati_attacking_secura.jpg`) were deleted. No live-action Anzati with proboscises exists anywhere; the realistic sources are William O'Connor's painted plates from *The New Essential Guide to Alien Species* and one live-action extra from *A New Hope*.

**`wookieepedia_legends_negas_head.jpg` and `wookieepedia_legends_negas_fullbody.jpg` — the realistic reference of record** (realistic painted reference-book art, Legends):
- **An entirely human man's face**: gaunt, hollow-cheeked, heavy brow, lined forehead, flat broad nose, thin hard mouth, light eyes. Long, lank, straight **dark brown-to-black hair** worn loose past the shoulders.
- **Skin is a sallow, pale flesh tone** with warm tan shading — pale human skin, not grey and not bloodless white.
- **The two proboscises emerge from the CHEEKS, immediately beside the nostrils**, one per side, from a small fleshy swelling, and swing out and down as **thick, smooth, fleshy, pale pinkish-tan cords** with a blunt, slightly knobbed tip. In the head plate they curl outward to either side of the face; in the full-body plate they flick loose past the shoulders. Same colour as the skin.
- Full body: an ordinary lean human build in a hooded brown travelling cloak, dark trousers and tall boots, crouched mid-spring.

**`wookieepedia_infobox_jakku_comic.jpg` — kept, but it is COMIC ART** (Phil Noto, *Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising* 3). It is the only CANON depiction, so it stays for canon anatomy only: a woman with long black hair, pale ice-blue eyes, subtly pointed ears, and the same cheek-side proboscises hanging long and thin to near the waist. Do not copy its flat painted comic shading. 🔴 It disagrees with the realistic plates on the proboscis: the comic draws them **thin, string-like and very long (hanging to the waist)**; the realistic plates draw them **thicker, fleshier and shorter (reaching about the shoulders)**. Origin point beside the nose agrees in both. Build the realistic fleshy version.

**`wookieepedia_dannik_jerriko_anh.png`** — live-action *A New Hope* cantina extra later identified in Legends as the Anzati Dannik Jerriko: a dark-haired, completely ordinary human man smoking a long pipe, proboscises retracted and invisible. Its value is the point that **an Anzati at rest is indistinguishable from a human** — the proboscises are coiled away in cheek pouches until feeding.

Behaviour (from the sourced text, no longer illustrated here): an Anzati feeds by **grasping the victim's head in both hands and holding it still** while the proboscises enter the nostrils — a quiet, held, drawn-out act, not a lunge.

**donor_current_sprite.png is weak evidence, and it is not Anzati art at all.** There is
**no Anzati-specific texture anywhere in this repo** — the def carries `iconPath`
`UI/Icons/Xenotypes/Sanguophage` (vanilla RimWorld's vampire icon) and no head gene. The
only tendril art the def can reach is `RSW_Beard_chintendril`, whose texture is
`SWX/Pawn/HeadAttachments/feeorin/chintendril_south.png` — **the Feeorin's chin tendrils,
borrowed**. The file (copied here) is a greyscale mask showing **two very short stubby
tendrils at the bottom-centre of the face, i.e. on the chin**. That is wrong against every
reference: **wrong origin point** (chin, not beside the nose), **wrong length**, and **wrong
semantics** (a beard ornament rather than a retractable feeding organ). Being a greyscale
mask, its lack of colour is correct and expected — the game tints at runtime — so only the
*shape* finding stands. **The species' single defining feature is effectively unrepresented.**

## Repo def versus canon — contradictions to report

🔴 **The def builds a RimWorld blood-vampire, and Anzati are not blood-drinkers.**
`RSW_RimMandrakeAnzati` carries `Hemogenic`, `HemogenDrain`, `Bloodfeeder`, `Coagulate`,
`Deathrest` and `Superclotting`, and takes vanilla's **Sanguophage icon**. Canon and Legends
are unambiguous that Anzati drain **"soup" — life essence, from the brain cavity via
proboscises into the nostrils** — not blood, and that the act is a slow held feeding, not a
bite. `Deathrest` (a coffin sleep) has no canon basis at all. The genes that *would* be
right — a telepathic mesmerise and a proboscis body part — do not exist here.

- `RSW_Beard_chintendril` is the only nod to the proboscises and it is **the wrong feature
  in the wrong place** (see the sprite note above).
- ✅ Defensible: `PsychicAbility_Enhanced` (Legends telepathy/hypnosis that strengthens
  with age), `RSW_Skin_MidGray` + `Skin_LightGray` (Legends "grayish-hued"),
  `Hair_MidBlack`/`Hair_DarkBlack`/`Hair_SnowWhite` (canon black and white),
  `Hair_Grayless` (a species that lives 950 years and is described as ageless),
  `ArchiteMetabolism`/`TotalHealing`/`DiseaseFree`/`PerfectImmunity`/`Robust` and
  `LowSleep` (the extreme longevity), `AptitudeStrong_Melee` (speed/reflexes beyond human).
- ⚠️ The canon infobox says skin is **Pale**, and both the canon Noto art and the realistic NEGAS plates show pale, not grey.
  A grey-only skin gene set may be the *Legends* Anzat rather than the canon one; if the
  campaign wants the canon look, a pale option is needed.
- ⚠️ Unsourced: `Hair_DarkReddish` (no red-haired Anzat is cited anywhere),
  `LongjumpLegs`, `DarkVision`, `AptitudeStrong_Social`, `AptitudeStrong_Intellectual`
  (the sourced sense is a *special sensory organ for detecting life essence*, not night
  vision, and not charisma).
- ⚠️ Missing, and mechanically the most distinctive facts available: **no pulse and no body
  heat**; the need to feed **every few days or merely grow hungry** (a gentler drive than
  RimWorld's hemogen crash); and the rule that **victims do not become Anzati**.
