## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
Banthas were a species of large, hairy mammals with sharp, spiraling horns,
native to the desert planet Tatooine (though bred across the galaxy). Social
herd animals, they were domesticated prominently by Tusken Raiders as mounts
and companions — the Tusken/bantha bond is treated as near-mystical in canon
("almost impossible to discuss one species without bringing up the other").
Physical description per Wookieepedia: quadrupedal mammals averaging 2–2.5
meters tall, ~4,000 kg, with **extensive shaggy fur, brown or black in
color**. **Both sexes carry a pair of spiraling horns** that grow a new knob
each year (age is inferable from horn curl). Wide mouths, bright inquisitive
eyes, a large tail that drags on the ground, and wide flat four-digit feet.
Females produce blue milk. Diet: omnivorous, peaceful, herd-living; capable of
eating meat (Boba Fett fed his bantha cooked scurrier remains) despite usually
grazing desert vegetation. Canon role: the iconic Tatooine pack/riding animal,
overwhelmingly associated with Tusken Raiders but also used by moisture
farmers and settlers for bantha steak, butter, clothing, and furniture from
domesticated herds. Appears on-screen from *A New Hope* onward (Tusken Raiders
riding banthas above the Jundland Wastes) through *The Mandalorian*/*The Book
of Boba Fett*.

## Visual brief
Every candidate image agrees on the core silhouette: a heavyset, shaggy,
long-haired quadruped with a pair of large, tightly-curled, ram-like spiraling
horns and a hanging beard-like fringe of fur at the chin/chest. Color runs
**dark brown to near-black** across all images — the infobox close-up shows a
warm chestnut-brown face fur against pale ivory/tan horns; the herd screen
capture (dune backdrop) shows the herd reading as uniformly dark brown/black
en masse; the official Mandalorian-era concept art (Pablo Olivera) shows the
same dark brown coat with cream horns, ridden with a full rider saddle rig.
No image shows visible skin color (fully fur-covered) or any color variant
outside the brown/black-with-pale-horn range — text and images agree here.

**The current donor sprite (`donor_current_sprite.png`) is a reasonable but
simplified match**: it gets the dark reddish-brown body color and pale curled
horns right, but the horns are drawn smaller/less tightly-spiraled than every
reference image, the body reads as a flat solid mass with no shag/fur texture
or chin-beard fringe, and the overall proportions are boxier/more compact
than the real animal's heavy-set but clearly leonine-maned silhouette. A
regen should exaggerate the horn spiral and add visible shaggy fur texture,
especially the hanging beard under the chin.
