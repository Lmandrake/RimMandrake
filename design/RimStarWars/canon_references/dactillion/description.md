# Dactillion

**defName**: `RSW_Dactillion` (vendored in this repo's SWBestiary mod)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

From the canon Wookieepedia article (`starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dactillion`,
pulled via the MediaWiki API to bypass a Cloudflare wall on the rendered page):

- **Classification**: Reptavian — large, winged, carnivorous.
- **Origin/habitat**: Native to Utapau (sinkholes and plains); also present on
  Shantipole. Lives in the massive sinkholes, using its wings to catch thermal
  updrafts to fly up toward the surface, and its claws to scale the sinkhole walls.
- **Size**: Length 24 meters (per *Scarif and Other Planets in the Outer Rim*) —
  huge, far larger than a horse-scale mount.
- **Coloration**: Skin color given as **"Blue and purple"** (Star Wars Databank,
  cited directly in the infobox). Eye color "light-colored."
- **Distinguishing feature**: A **bicorn (two-pronged) beak** — the beak tip
  splits into two hooked/curved prongs, used in both defense and combat, plus
  claws for gripping.
- **Diet/behavior**: Carnivorous; the native sentient Utai were historically
  part of the dactillion's diet before taming them by feeding them meat.
- **Canon role**: Used as fast long-distance transport by Utapauns, and as
  battle mounts by the Utapaun Security Forces (grabbing enemies with claws,
  striking with the horn/beak prongs). First conceived as a Jedi mount for
  Obi-Wan/Ki-Adi-Mundi on Utapau during *Revenge of the Sith* development;
  built as a full creature model for a *Clone Wars* Season 6 arc ("A Death on
  Utapau," "In Search of the Crystal," "Crystal Crisis," "The Big Bang") that
  was cancelled before airing but released as canon story reels in 2014 (*The
  Clone Wars Legacy*) — Obi-Wan and Anakin ride dactillions off Pau City into
  the plains. Also appears in *Star Wars Rebels* ("Wings of the Master"),
  where wild dactillions on Shantipole fly alongside Hera's experimental
  B-wing prototype, and reportedly as leather goods in Episode IX.

Source: Star Wars Databank entry (via WebFetch) adds that dactillions are
"large winged creatures found on Utapau often used for transportation," notes
the Shantipole population specifically, and confirms the Rebels appearance.

## Visual brief

All three candidate images agree closely with each other and with the sourced
text, and disagree with nothing in it — this is a rare case where text and
art line up cleanly:

- **Body plan**: Unambiguously pterosaur-shaped — long slender neck, elongated
  toothless beak, digit-supported membrane wings (not feathered), long thin
  tail, clawed hands/feet used for perching and gripping. All three images and
  the sourced text agree this is a reptilian flyer, not a bird or mammal.
- **Coloration**: All three images show the same two-tone scheme the wiki's
  infobox states — a **blue-grey to slate-grey body** and **dark maroon /
  wine-red wing membranes**. The beak is consistently **pale tan/bone-colored**
  with the two-pronged ("bicorn") hooked tip rendered gold or pale yellow in
  every image. Eyes read red/orange in the closer shots (infobox render,
  donor sprite) — plausibly the "light-colored" eye the Databank cites, seen
  under different lighting, or an amber variant; no image shows anything
  resembling brown or green skin, so "blue and purple" in the text is
  confirmed by the art rather than undersold by it.
- **Riders/scale**: Both the concept-art landscape piece and the trading-card
  art show a rider in a hooded cloak with a spear/pike, seated at the base of
  the neck/shoulders with a visible harness/saddle strap — consistent with
  "mount" and "battle use, grabbing with claws" from the text. The card art's
  riders wear red robes suggestive of Utapaun security or Jedi-adjacent garb.
- **Disagreement between images**: The concept-art piece (`wookieepedia_concept.jpg`)
  gives the wings a warmer pink-magenta cast versus the more desaturated
  maroon/burgundy of the infobox render and the card art — read this as
  lighting/render-style variance, not a canon color split; treat maroon/dark
  red-purple as the safe center.
- **Donor sprite comparison**: `donor_current_sprite.png` (our current in-game
  south-facing sprite, head-on/foreshortened view) already lands close to
  canon — dark blue-grey head and body, maroon-red visible on the folded
  wings/ear-like flaps, tan-gold beak, red eyes. This is a solid starting
  point; nothing in it needs re-inventing. If anything, the donor's body
  reads a touch darker/more saturated-purple than the flatter slate-grey of
  the reference art, and the bicorn beak prongs are less pronounced/forked
  than in the references — worth sharpening on any redo, not a wrong hue.
