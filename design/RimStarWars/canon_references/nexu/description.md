## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nexu

Nexu were predatory felines native to the planet Cholganna. They were also found on Zhanox, Vodran, and Saleucami.

**Biology and appearance** Nexu were agile catlike animal predators with four red eyes and a defensive mane of sharp quills. The four eyes gave them excellent vision, with the second pair allowing them to track prey by its body heat using infrared vision. The nexu was 4.51 meters long and typically 0.94 meters tall. Nexu possessed sharp teeth and long claws, perfect for climbing trees. Their double tails were forked and whip-like. A nexu's light build allowed it to remain highly agile and difficult to hit, but also meant that it was easily hurt or even killed if struck with enough force; a single kick was enough to stun a nexu and cause significant pain. In contrast, a nexu's hide did seem durable enough to withstand blaster fire.

Despite their ferocity, it was possible to tame and train a nexu.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nexu/Legends

Nexu were feline creatures native to the chilly forests of the Indona continent on the planet of Cholganna.

**Biology and appearance** There were other breeds of nexu on Cholganna's other continents, but only the forest nexu had an additional set of eyes able to view in infrared wavelength, which aided in their hunting of bark rats and tree-climbing octopi. They were often exported and trained as security beasts, as used for patrolling on Malastare, or used in arena combat, as seen on Geonosis. Nexu were 4.5 meters long and generally 1 meter tall.

The nexu appeared to be feline in nature, with claws which could slice a Human in half. They had quills along their back, and a long tail that in the wild allowed the nexu to swing from tree branch to tree branch. The tail also split into two equal sections for a better grip on the tree branch they were swinging from. They had extremely sharp instincts and reflexes, although they were known to tire easily, and often used swift movements to finish an enemy quickly, biting down and thrashing their head about to break their victim's neck. Their tongue could be served as a delicacy. Cubs were born pure white. A particularly stealthy variety was the black nexu; this species was also one of the rarest.

While they were native to the planet Cholganna, they eventually became alpha predators throughout the galaxy, adapting to new environments through a natural hardiness and a quick reproductive cycle. Packs of nexu could be found roaming the tundra of Mygeeto and the jungles of Rodia.

Nexu were recognised in many cultures as an icon of viciousness, and several Mandalorian clans took the creature as their symbol. Some xenobiologists argued that nexu were far less violent than commonly believed and that it was the activities of sentient beings that agitated them. Left alone, they were smart, capable and strong but not cruel or aggressive.
