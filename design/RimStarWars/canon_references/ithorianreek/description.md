## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reek/Legends (section "Ithorian reek", wikitext pulled via the API 2026-10-14)

One subspecies, the Ithorian reek, was exported to Ithor. Their yellow skin color was attributed to their diet
consisting of yellow flora and bafforr trees. They were used as plow animals by the Ithorians and were larger and
stronger than those found on Ylesia. Their horns were also smaller because of the lack of hostility between reeks.
Males and females were separated to reduce competition and to isolate mating periods. The Ithorian reeks were wiped
out during the Yuuzhan Vong War. (Cited there to *The New Essential Guide to Alien Species*.)

From the same article's lead: the reek was a large, thick-skinned, horned, rhino-esque quadruped native to Ylesia.
