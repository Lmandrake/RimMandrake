## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bordok

Bordoks were a species of sure-footed equine herbivores native to the forest moon Endor. Since the bordoks could carry large loads relative to their small size, the sentient Ewoks used them as both pack animals and mounts.

**Biology and appearance** An equine species, the bordoks were related to other riding beasts from across the galaxy, including pulgas, fathiers, orbaks, and tris. They were sure-footed creatures capable of carrying large loads relative to their small size.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bordok/Legends

The bordoks were a vaguely equine herbivore native to the forest moon of Endor. They were used by Ewoks as pack animals and mounts when tamed. Bordoks were very sure-footed and, in spite of their size, capable of carrying very large loads.

**Biology and appearance** Forest dwelling Bordoks were medium-sized, pony-like animals best known as Ewoks employed them as beasts of burden, although similar creatures existed on many planets throughout the galaxy. Bordoks had long manes, gentle features, and strong backs.

Although somewhat similar in appearance to horses, bordoks were not true equines. They had upward-curving horns that were short on young females but could grow larger on an adult male. Bordoks also had split hooves that helped them climb through the trees of their arboreal home.

There were two species of bordoks: The lesser bordok grew to no more than 1.5 meters tall at the shoulder. The rarer greater bordok could reach twice this size. Both species were fairly intelligent, and were not startled easily. However, they did instinctively become more skittish at night due to the various nocturnal predators that stalked the moon's surface.

Some have been known to show a remarkable amount of intelligenge, enough to cause scientists to begin readdressing the level of their sentience.
