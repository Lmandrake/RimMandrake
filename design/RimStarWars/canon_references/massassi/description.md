# Sith Massassi (Pureblood)

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeSithMassassi`, label `Sith Massassi (Pureblood)`
(`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`).
Assigned `Jawa_AscendantHelix: R`.

🔑 **Read `../sith_species/description.md` first.** Massassi is a **caste of the
Sith species**, not a species of its own. The shared anatomy — red skin, bone
spurs, cheek tendrils, cranial horns, simian mouth, small nose and ears, three
clawed digits per hand and foot, left-handedness, species-wide Force-sensitivity —
lives in that entry. Only Massassi-specific material is below.

🔴 **There are TWO distinct Massassi appearances in canon, and they are not
cosmetic variants of each other.** Everything below is split accordingly, because
getting this wrong is the single easiest way to draw a Massassi that is not one.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

### The original Massassi
Infobox (`Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide` unless noted):
**height 1.9 meters**; **skin red or orange**; **hair red**; **eyes yellow or
black**; **lifespan up to 50 standard years**; distinctions **sharp predatory
features, cheek tendrils, tall stature**; origin **Korriban**, then **Ziost**
c. 27,700 BBY, then **Yavin 4** in 5000 BBY under exiled Naga Sadow.

Phenotype, verbatim where it matters: *"Phenotypically, Massassi were quite similar
to the rest of the Sith species."* Shared: red skin **ranging from florid crimsons
to lighter pinks**; fierce predatory profiles; **sharp bone spurs protruding over
their bodies, especially the elbows**; **a pair of tendrils hanging from their
cheekbones**; **cartilaginous eyebrow stalks that covered their eyes**; **simian
mouths, small noses and small ears**; **a slightly receding chin**; **three digits
on each hand and foot.**

🔑 **Exclusive to the Massassi caste — the only four things canon says are theirs
alone:**

1. **Large glowing yellow eyes with no pupils and no whites of any sort — the
   yellow covers the whole of each eye.** *"Unlike mainline Sith, the eyes of
   Massassi did not display details such as pupils or whites of any sort."*
2. **Taller, larger and more hulking humanoids than the species they hailed from,
   reaching between two and three meters in height, average weight ninety-five
   kilograms.** (Note the infobox's 1.9 m and the body text's 2–3 m disagree; both
   are sourced and both are recorded.)
3. **Extremely tough skin.** *"Powerful cauterizing agents such as cyanogen
   silicate were needed to provide Massassi with medical assistance. Nothing weaker
   was capable of corroding their resistant hides."*
4. **Very acute senses relative to Humans, particularly hearing, despite their small
   ears.**

**Life cycle, fully sourced and unusually fast:** childhood lasts **ten standard
years**; the next **three** are young adulthood; **adulthood begins at about
fourteen**; **middle age at twenty-five**; **old age at thirty-five**; any Massassi
**over fifty** was venerable. This is a species that is grown at 14 and old at 35.

**Behaviour**: *"fearsome, ruthless, and efficient soldiers"* — loyal, organized,
dedicated to their Sith masters, **bred to blindly follow orders and to live for
the glory of battle**. *"Even though they were not extremely intelligent, they could
be trained well enough to carry out complex battle plans and maintain their
equipment, starships, and weaponry."* They used **Massassi hieroglyphs** on artifacts
and structures, **alchemically hardened armor**, and had **priests and elders** within
their culture. On Yavin 4 they worshipped the gas giant **Yavin Prime as a deity**
and built the temples and monuments they are famous for. The Sith caste summary calls
them the caste whose *"loyalty had to be earned but once it was they were unswervingly
obedient."*

**Unusual abilities**: no Massassi-exclusive Force power. The species-level fact
carries it — the **entire Sith race, Massassi included, had so high a rate of
Force-sensitivity that they were considered a naturally Force-sensitive species**,
feeding on the dark side and empowering it in turn.

### The alchemically altered (Yavin 4 / Exar Kun era) Massassi
Verbatim: *"The Massassi under the command of Naga Sadow that relocated to Yavin 4
after the Great Hyperspace War underwent alchemical alterations under the
supervision of Sadow. These individuals and their descendants became more hunched
and monstrous than the original Massassi species, and were gradually considered
more like savage brutes than the warriors they once were. At this point, they became
**large creatures with sharp claws and grotesque fin-like growths on their head and
back.**"* And: *"During the reign of Exar Kun, the race had largely devolved into
mindless brutes."*

🔑 **This is a magically induced deformation of a subset of the population, not the
species' natural form.** Nothing sources a height, mass or lifespan for the altered
line.

## Visual brief
**Owner ruling 2026-10-08 (verbatim): "That's realistic, not these cartoon versions you keep using. Replace the canon with something more realistic. The web is full of them."**
No live-action or photoreal Massassi exists (searched: Massassi, Massassi/Legends, Sith (species) and Sith (species)/Legends
page images, "Images of Massassi" — comics, pen-and-ink RPG art, SWTOR game assets and painted SWTOR timeline art only).
Removed: the two *Tales of the Jedi* comic panels (`wookieepedia_massassi_architects.jpg`, `wookieepedia_massassi_warriors_funeral.jpg`),
the stylised SWTOR game asset (`wookieepedia_massassi_swtor_infobox.jpg`) and the *Essential Guide* pen-and-ink
(`wookieepedia_massassi_egtas_alchemical.jpg`). Added the three most realistic **painted** depictions:
`wookieepedia_massassi_kotorcg_painted.jpg` (head and shoulders, *Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide*),
`wookieepedia_massassi_and_kissai_painted.jpg` (Massassi warriors beside a human, SWTOR *Timeline 12: The Great Hyperspace War*)
and `wookieepedia_massassi_exar_kun_painted.jpg` (Exar Kun among the altered Yavin 4 Massassi, *Timeline 10*).
`wookieepedia_massassi_warrior.jpg` (pen-and-ink concept) is **kept only for the lean, long-haired original-form
silhouette**, which no painting shows. Render realistically: real skin, horn and muscle, natural light.

🔴 **The paintings disagree with the pen-and-ink "Massassi Warrior" concept, loudly.** The concept sheet shows a
tall, LEAN, upright warrior with long bound hair. Every realistic painting — including the pre-Yavin *Great Hyperspace
War* scene — shows a **massive, heavily muscled, broad-shouldered brute**, towering over the human beside it, with:
- **Saturated crimson-red skin**, glossy over the muscle, with darker red shading (all three paintings; hue settled —
  the deleted comics' blue-grey/violet was the colourist's).
- 🔑 **A crown of stiff fin-like spikes sweeping back from the skull** and **long hanging tendrils/dreadlock-like
  fronds** falling from the brow, cheeks and back of the head over the shoulders — fins AND tendrils together.
- **A heavy, deeply ridged brow over small, glowing, sunken eyes**; the KotOR painting shows a **fanged mouth
  framed by long hanging cheek/chin tendrils** and **folded, bat-like pointed ears**.
- **Bare torso and arms**, gold armour or a beaded collar worn over them.
- The **altered Yavin 4 Massassi** (Exar Kun painting) are the same red-orange creature pushed further: **hunched,
  crouching, crests and fronds thicker and more stringy, clawed limbs**, moving like beasts.

So the realistic consensus is a **red, fin-crowned, tendrilled giant**; the lean long-haired figure survives only in the
one pen-and-ink concept. The text's tridactyl Sith hand/foot (three clawed digits) is sourced but is not clearly visible
in the painted set.

⚠️ **No `donor_current_sprite.png`.** The Massassi xenotype has no species art of its
own; it renders from `RSW_Head_Bone` (forcing `RSW_Male_HeavyBoneNormal` /
`RSW_Female_HeavyBoneNormal`), `Outland_FaceTendrils`, `Body_Hulk` and
`RSW_BodySizeGene_big`. Nothing in this repo compares canon against the in-game
sprite.

## Def-versus-canon (flagged — not fixed)

`RSW_RimMandrakeSithMassassi`:

- 🔴 **No `<nameMaker>` at all.** Kissai and Dathomirian both carry
  `RSW_NamerPersonPureblood`; this xenotype carries none, so Massassi pawns fall back
  to whatever the faction supplies. A plain defect, independent of canon.
- 🔴 **No lifespan gene and no maturation gene.** Canon is precise and extreme:
  **adult at ~14, middle-aged at 25, old at 35, venerable past 50, maximum 50 years.**
  As shipped, a Massassi lives and ages like a human — roughly double. The mod has
  the machinery (`RSW_lifespan_nine`, `RSW_lifespan_half`,
  `Outland_DeceleratedMaturation`), so this is expressible and simply absent.
- 🔴 **No `Outland_ThickSkin`** — yet **the Kissai xenotype has it.** *"Extremely
  tough skin … nothing weaker than cyanogen silicate was capable of corroding their
  resistant hides"* is Massassi-exclusive canon. The trait is on the wrong caste in
  both directions.
- 🔴 **Eyes: `Outland_Eye_Yellow` only.** The Massassi's single most distinctive
  feature is **large glowing yellow eyes with NO pupil and NO sclera — solid yellow
  edge to edge.** The mod owns a large-eye gene (`RSW_Eyes_Big`, used by the Yoder
  xenotype), and nothing renders the missing pupil/sclera. The one caste-exclusive
  visual trait canon gives them is unrepresented.
- 🔴 **`Hair_BaldOnly`** against a sourced **hair colour: red**, and against the
  `Massassi Warrior` concept sheet's long bound hair. Directly contradicted by both
  the infobox field and the art.
- 🔴 **`RSW_Skin_SlateRed`** is `(125,95,100)` — a desaturated grey-mauve. Canon skin
  is **florid crimson to lighter pink, or orange**; every red-skinned Massassi image
  is saturated. `Skin_DeepRed`, `Outland_Skin_Red` and `Outland_Skin_DeepOrange` are
  also in the pool and are correct; the slate-red entry can roll a Massassi that is
  not red.
- ⚠️ **`RSW_BodySizeGene_big`** is `SM_Cosmetic_BodySizeOffset +0.2`. Canon is
  **2–3 m against a human ~1.7 m**, i.e. up to +75%. Directionally right, badly
  under-scaled. (`Body_Hulk` helps the silhouette but not the footprint.)
- ⚠️ **No acute-hearing / heightened-senses gene** — sourced and absent.
- ⚠️ **No psychic or Force gene of any kind.** Canon: *the entire Sith race, including
  the Massassi*, was considered naturally Force-sensitive. Kissai gets
  `PsychicAbility_Enhanced`; Massassi gets nothing.
- ⚠️ **`AptitudeTerrible_Medicine` and `AptitudeTerrible_Plants`** — unsourced.
  `AptitudeTerrible_Intellectual` **is** sourced (*"not extremely intelligent"*), but
  note the same passage says they *"could be trained well enough to carry out complex
  battle plans and maintain their equipment, starships, and weaponry"* and they built
  the Yavin 4 temples with **hieroglyphic writing** — so a *terrible* intellectual
  aptitude overshoots, and terrible-medicine is contradicted by the sourced presence
  of priests and elders.
- ⚠️ **`Outland_FamiliarScent`** — unsourced; canon's sensory claim is **hearing**.
- ⚠️ Sourced-but-absent shared-Sith anatomy, same as the other two castes: **cranial
  horns, bone spurs at the elbows, cartilaginous eyebrow-stalks, three clawed digits
  per hand and foot, simian mouth, small ears, left-handedness.**
- ⚠️ The `<description>` misspells **"formidible"** (should be *formidable*), and its
  claim that Massassi *"are known for having been responsible for many temples across
  the galaxy"* overstates canon, which places their temple-building on **Yavin 4**
  under Naga Sadow and Exar Kun.
- ⚠️ **Naming**: as with the other two castes, "(Pureblood)" is the wrong term — canon
  is explicit that **Sith Purebloods are hybrids and "were thought to be very
  different from the original Sith species as a whole."** A Massassi is a **Red Sith**.
  Owed under `XENOTYPE_CANON_CORRECTION_1` — flagged, not yet renamed (the `NAMING_SCHEME_EXECUTION_1` gate closed 2026-08-31).
- ✅ Well sourced and correctly present: `Aggression_HyperAggressive`,
  `MeleeDamage_Strong`, `AptitudeRemarkable_Melee`, `Robust`, `Pain_Reduced`,
  `WoundHealing_Fast`, `Body_Hulk`, `Outland_FaceTendrils`, `Outland_RidgedSkin`,
  `RSW_Head_Bone`.
