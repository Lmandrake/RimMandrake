# Vornskyr

**defName**: `Vornskyr` (third-party, mlie.starwarsanimalcollection, not
vendored — the mod's 1.6 release packs creature art inside Unity
AssetBundles rather than loose PNGs; see "donor art" note below)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
The mod's spelling ("Vornskyr") does not match any Wookieepedia title; the
canonical spelling on both current canon and Legends is **`Vornskr`** (no
second "y"). `Vornskyr` itself does not resolve — a plain `Vornskr` lookup
is the correct target creature and is what this file researches.

Wookieepedia's `Vornskr` page is tagged Legends-only (`{{Top|leg}}`) — there
is currently no distinct current-canon article, though vornskrs are the
Force-tracking predator native to **Myrkr**, the Force-nullifying planet the
task brief names, and that Myrkr/Force-hunting relationship is treated as
canon lore across all eras (it underpins Myrkr's in-universe role in both
Legends and current-canon Thrawn material).

- **Species**: canine beast, non-sentient, native to the forest planet
  Myrkr. Height ~0.8 meters.
- **Coloring**: infobox gives haircolor as **gray and black**, eyecolor
  **red**.
- **The Force-tracking ability is the defining trait**: vornskrs evolved to
  hunt ysalamiri (small lizard-like creatures on Myrkr that, in turn,
  evolved a Force-suppression "bubble" specifically to defend against
  vornskr predation). A side effect of the vornskr's Force-sense is that it
  also treats Force-sensitive beings (including Jedi) as favored prey.
- **Behavior**: primarily nocturnal, wild and vicious; hunts using a
  whip-like, mildly venomous tail to stun prey before finishing with its
  teeth. Docile individuals (e.g. smuggler Talon Karrde's pets Sturm and
  Drang) remain instinctively hostile toward Force-users even after
  domestication and tail-removal.
- **Cultural footprint**: the lightsaber form later refined into Vaapad
  (Form VII) was originally called "the Way of the Vornskr." Vornskrs were
  bred and trained as hunting/guard animals by figures including Tyber Zann
  and Talon Karrde.

## Visual brief
Three candidate images, and they diverge more than they agree — this is
another text-vs-image (and image-vs-image) mismatch case:

- `wookieepedia_alienarchive.jpg` (captioned "Two vornskrs on Myrkr" in the
  source article, credited to *The Essential Guide to Alien Species*) —
  shows a **hairless, smooth dark-gray/near-black skinned** quadruped:
  reptilian-textured hide rather than fur, tall pointed bat-like ears, a
  narrow snarling canine/reptile-hybrid muzzle with visible fangs, **red
  eyes**, and a long whip-thin tail ending in a bushy black tuft. Four
  clawed digits per paw. This is the closest single-creature match to the
  infobox's "gray, black, red eyes" and reads as the most credible baseline
  reference.
- `wookieepedia_essentialguide_pair.jpg` (captioned "A pack of vornskrs" in
  the source article) — shows two creatures that are **noticeably shaggier
  and furrier** than the AA image: pale cream/tan-white long fur, a mane-like
  ruff around the neck/shoulders, red eyes, a similar whip tail with dark
  tuft, and a more bulky/boar-like build than the lean AA individual. Still
  broadly consistent with "gray/black + red eyes" if the fur is read as a
  lighter variant, but the silhouette (heavier, maned, shaggy) disagrees
  with the sleek hairless AA depiction.
- `wookieepedia_swamp_pack.jpg` (a multi-creature pack scene in a dark
  swamp) — shows several **smooth black-skinned** vornskrs with **glowing
  yellow eyes** (not red), curved horn-like ears, and the same whip tail
  shape. The yellow eye color directly contradicts the infobox's "red" and
  the other two images; treat this as an outlier, likely stylized for a
  dramatic low-light comic/game scene rather than a color-accurate baseline.

**Net read**: body plan is consistent across all three — quadruped canine
predator, tall pointed ears, a long whip-like tail ending in a distinct
tuft, prominent fangs. Coloring is NOT consistent: two of three sources
agree on red eyes over gray/black skin (matching the infobox), but fur
density/length and the third source's yellow eyes are genuinely
contradictory. Treat `wookieepedia_alienarchive.jpg` as the primary color
anchor (matches infobox exactly) and the other two as showing the plausible
range of variation (or inconsistent non-canon art), not settled fact.
