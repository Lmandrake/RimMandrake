# Jerba

**defName**: `RSW_Jerba` (vendored in this repo's own `src/RimStarWars/SWBestiary` mod — note: this creature reuses the Bantha texture folder, `Textures/swanimals/Bantha/`, since in-game it ships as a smaller/younger reskin of the bantha art rather than unique art)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
A jerba was a shaggy, smooth-haired quadruped found in desert environments,
most notably on Tatooine. Strong, sturdy build with a horned head; considered
by some (Han Solo) to be "uglier than the back end of a shaved bantha."
Haircolor brown, eyecolor orange, with ridged horns per *Star Wars
Battlefront*. Their strong build made them good beasts of burden, and they
were also kept for milk and meat, with a subculture of jerba breeders across
the galaxy. Jerba leather was tanned for boots, jewelry, and rugs (Leia wore
jerba-leather boots as part of her dancing-girl costume in Jabba's Palace).
Tethers for jerbas and banthas stood outside Tosche Station in Anchorhead, and
wary jerbas gathered near the building — this is the creature's on-screen
debut in *A New Hope* (1977), visible tethered near the Millennium Falcon /
Docking Bay 94 area. Jabba the Hutt kept a jerba trophy head mounted in his
palace alongside other hunting trophies. Much more prominent in modern media
as the smaller mount ridden by younger Tusken Raiders in *The Book of Boba
Fett* — smaller and more agile than a full bantha, suited to a young rider.

## Visual brief
The two highest-fidelity sources — the genuine 1977 *A New Hope* movie still
and the Alien Archive illustration — agree closely: jerbas are covered in
**long, shaggy, unkempt brown fur** hanging in ragged locks (visibly longer
and stringier than a bantha's coat, almost goat-like), with a **pair of
short, forward-curving ridged horns** on the head and a blunt, slightly
undershot lower jaw with visible teeth. The movie still shows the real
practical creature as dark brown, dusty, and shaggy, photographed tethered
against a Tatooine adobe wall (with R2-D2 and a blaster rifle in the
foreground) — an authentic on-set reference for scale and texture. The
Alien Archive line-art gives a cleaner full-body profile: reddish-brown
shaggy coat, a collared neck (domesticated pack-animal framing), the same
short ridged horns, and a low-slung sturdy body silhouette. The small comic
panel (jerba tethered outside a cantina, next to a droid) is much lower
detail but is consistent — dark brown, laden with a saddle pack, docile.

**The current donor sprite (`donor_current_sprite.png`) is the shared Bantha
art (`Bantha_east`)**: a solid dark brown, smooth-shaded blob with a pair of
large curled ram-like horns, no fur texture at all, and a squared-off body.
This disagrees with canon jerba texture in one clear way — real jerbas are
explicitly **shaggy/long-haired**, not smooth-coated, and their horns are
short and gently ridge-curved rather than the large tight ram-spiral horns
this donor shares with the adult bantha. Since the mod intentionally reuses
bantha art for the jerba (as a "smaller relative" placeholder), any dedicated
jerba art should differentiate it with a visibly shaggier coat and smaller,
straighter/ridged horns rather than the bantha's heavy curled horns.
