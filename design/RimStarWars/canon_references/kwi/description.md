## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Legends only — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kwi
The page is tagged Legends (`{{Top|leg}}`); first appearance *Star Wars* (1998) #24, also *The Courtship of Princess Leia* (as the Blue Desert People), *Young Jedi Knights: Shadow Academy* and *Star Wars Galaxies*. No current-canon appearance is listed.

The Kwi were the degenerated descendants of the saurian Kwa&mdash;Dathomir's first sentient inhabitants.

**Biology and appearance** The Kwi were large, bipedal lizards with powerful hind legs and tiny forelimbs. They had light blue flesh covered in cobalt-blue scales. The scales located at the top of their skulls had an iridescence that tended to be almost lavender in hue. The face of a Kwi featured two bloodred eyes, a mouth full of black teeth shaped like spades and a long, black tongue. Thanks to their conformation, they could race at incredible speed. To put themselves into a running position, the Kwi would lower their heads and lift their tails out straight behind them to act as a counterbalance. An average member of the Kwi species was three meters tall and four meters long (*Dathomir* Fact File). Semi-sentient herbivores with no articulated language (they communicate by honking, snorting and grunting in herds); not described as predators. The carnivorous variant is the separate Rhoa Kwi tribe, and riding a Kwi was a rare honour among the light-side Witches (*Young Jedi Knights: Shadow Academy*).
