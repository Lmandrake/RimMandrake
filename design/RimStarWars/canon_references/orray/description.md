# Orray

**defName**: `Orray` (third-party, mlie.starwarsanimalcollection, not vendored — see Watch out)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
Orrays are large, stour (stout) quadruped reptiles native to the deserts of Geonosis.
Skin color is gray-brown (per the film infobox citation). Body plan: long snout, long
tail (on wild specimens), thick reptilian hide, four sturdy legs. Size: ~2 meters tall,
~3 meters long. They were bred and domesticated by the Geonosians as mounts and beasts
of burden, valued for strength and toughness. Domesticated/arena orrays working in the
Petranaki Arena had their tails amputated and the stump capped with metal, and were
fitted with saddles. Geonosian picadors rode them carrying stun-poles/spears, using
them to zap the crowd or haul execution carts. Canon role: seen ridden by picadors in
the Petranaki Arena in Star Wars: Episode II — Attack of the Clones (2002), including
the escape sequence where Padmé Amidala rides a loose orray during the First Battle of
Geonosis. Design notes (behind-the-scenes): built for AOTC, designed by Doug Chiang
with the final sculpt by Michael Murnane; the design has been described as a cross
between an alligator and a horse. Considered extinct as a species after the fall of the
New Republic. Diet/lifespan/habitat beyond "Geonosis desert" are not stated.

## Visual brief
All three candidate images agree closely — there is no text/image conflict here, and
no meaningful disagreement between the images themselves. The creature reads as a
heavy-bodied, low-slung quadruped with a long alligator-like snout, small eyes set
high on the head, thick wrinkled/tuberculate reptilian hide, and stubby powerful legs
with clawed toes. Coloration across all three images is consistent with the sourced
"gray-brown" description: sandy tan-brown to olive-grey, mottled/wrinkled skin, no
bright colors, no scales-as-plates (skin reads more leathery/pebbled than hard-scaled).
The film stills (`film_still_arena.jpg`, `film_still_padme_aotc.jpg`) show it in its
domesticated arena state — saddled, ridden, tail either capped or not prominently
visible in frame. The Wookieepedia infobox render (`wookieepedia_infobox.jpg`) shows a
Geonosian drone rider on the same creature type, confirming the long snout, low stance,
and tan-brown hide at a cleaner angle with the full body and legs visible; note this
image's lighting pushes the color slightly warmer/goldish than the cooler grey-brown of
the film stills — treat the film stills as the anchor reference for exact hue since they
are direct screen captures of the theatrical color grade, and use the infobox render
only for unobstructed body-plan/proportion detail (leg count, snout length, tail stub).
No image shows a metal tail cap in clear detail, so that detail is text-only, unverified
visually in this candidate set.
