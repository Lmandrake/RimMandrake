## 🔴 Read this first: canon has NO Bothan appearance, on purpose

**This is the single most important fact about this species and it changes what an appearance
reference can even mean.** Every other entry in this library reconciles art with prose. Here
there is no canon art to reconcile, because **Lucasfilm has deliberately refused to show a
Bothan.**

- The **canon article's infobox `|image=` field is empty**, and so are `height`, `mass`,
  `skincolor`, `haircolor`, `eyecolor`, `distinctions`, `lifespan`, `habitat`, `diet` and
  `language`. The article carries a `{{Species-stub}}` banner. Origin is given only as
  **"Bothan Space,"** not even a named homeworld.
- **Lucasfilm Story Group member Matt Martin, verbatim:** *"It's pretty easy to confirm: we
  are purposely trying not to show them to leave all possibilities for when that story gets
  told. The dude in Resistance is based on the EU design of a Bothan that the Animation art
  team used as reference for a background alien."*
- **Pablo Hidalgo** (Story Group), on why: *"A decade ago, we figured 'they're a production
  concept; production should define them.' No takers yet."* Because Bothans were first
  mentioned in *Return of the Jedi*, Lucasfilm decided a **live-action production** should be
  the first to establish their look. He has also joked that *"It's pretty much a recurring
  bit now. It's how you know they're good spies — you never see them."*
- 🔴 **The reference book *Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic
  Survival Skills* actively DISOWNS the Legends design**: it "pictures" Bothans by showing
  **silhouettes of Legends Bothans with red X's marked over them, implying those silhouettes
  are NOT what Bothans look like.** That book page is `wookieepedia_canon_bothans_xed_out.jpg`
  in this directory.
- Individuals *resembling* the Legends depiction have appeared in *Star Wars Resistance*,
  *Legends of the Alliance* (Mak Eshka'rey), *War of the Bounty Hunters*, *Sabine My Rebel
  Sketchbook* and the *Black Spire Outpost Cookbook* — but Wookieepedia states plainly that
  **"whether these creatures are in fact Bothans has yet to be confirmed."**

**Consequence for this library:** the furry canine/feline/equine Bothan is a **Legends**
design that canon has gone out of its way to leave open and has explicitly X'd out in print.
It is still the only visual reference that exists, and it is what this repo already ships.
That is a defensible choice for a Legends-flavoured campaign — but **it must be made
knowingly, because canon does not support it and one reference book contradicts it
outright.** This is the owner's call, not the reference's.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Canon, in full — it is short.** Bothans were *"a group known for operating a spy
network."* They were **known to communicate with each other in a series of growls.** They
originated from **Bothan Space**, and planets like **Torolis** were Bothan colony worlds.
Bothan spies were credited with obtaining the location of the **DS-2 Death Star II**, despite
heavy casualties, enabling the Rebel Alliance to plan its attack — the source of Mon Mothma's
line, *"Many Bothans died to bring us this information."* They also supplied the **door code
for the shield generator bunker** on Endor, and they **forged false chain codes that were
implanted into Aqualish by Nautolan doctors** (a nice cross-reference: this campaign carries
both Aqualish and Nautolan entries). First mentioned in *Return of the Jedi* (1983); first
canonical *appearance* in the 2016 novel **Bloodline**.

That is the entirety of canon. 🔴 **No canon height, mass, lifespan, skin colour, fur colour,
eye colour, or anatomical distinction exists. Do not invent one.** The only physical detail
canon gives at all is the **growled speech**.

**Legends (`Bothan/Legends`), which is the whole visual and mechanical basis.**
- **Height 1.4–1.5 meters** (*Behind the Magic*), with the body text giving **1.5–1.6 m**
  (*Ultimate Alien Anthology* / *Essential Guide to Alien Species*) and the lead calling them
  *"about 1.5 meters tall"* — so **a short species, well under human average**, with the
  sources disagreeing by about 10 cm. Mass unsourced. Class **mammal / humanoid**.
- **Furry mammalian anthropoids** who *"differed in facial appearance and body structure
  with **canine, feline, and equine** features."* **Hair colour brown or cream. Eye colour
  brown, green, or violet.** Homeworld **Bothawui**, plus several colonies.
- **Lifespan**: child 1–11, young adult 12–16, adult 17–45, middle age 46–65, old 66–84,
  **venerable 85+** — i.e. **roughly human maturation and a slightly shorter total span.**
- 🔑 **The one distinction the infobox lists: MOOD-SENSITIVE FUR.** *"Bothans were covered
  in fur which shifted in response to their emotional state by way of gentle ripplings. It
  was this trait, named **Wrendui**, that betrayed them when members of their kind intended
  to be duplicitous in their dealings with others."* **Wrendui is also their body language —
  a third named language alongside spoken Bothese and written Botha.** This is the species'
  signature feature and it is deliciously ironic: a race of master spies whose own fur gives
  away their lies.
- **They possessed tapered pointed ears, and both males and females were known to sport
  beards.**
- **They were master politicians and spies, craving intrigue and subterfuge.** Officially
  neutral through most conflicts, including the Galactic Civil War, while the **Bothan
  Spynet** played every side. Members of the Galactic Republic from at least 4000 BBY. Large
  role in founding the New Republic.
- ⚠️ **Tails belong to HYBRIDS, not to Bothans.** Bothans could interbreed with at least one
  other species — the Jedi Lord **Valenthyne Farfalla** was a **half-Bothan** — and *"such
  hybrids somewhat resembled baseline humans with **haunches, hooves, fur, pointed ears and a
  long tail**."* The tail, haunches and hooves are described **only** for the hybrid. Nothing
  in the full-Bothan description mentions a tail at all.

## Visual brief

⚠️ **Everything in this section describes Legends art, because that is all that exists. None
of it is canon-supported, and the first image is canon actively rejecting it.**

**`wookieepedia_canon_bothans_xed_out.jpg` — the negative reference, and the most
canon-authoritative image in this directory.** From *Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by
Ewoks*: a 3×2 grid of six pale grey circles, each containing a **dark grey Bothan silhouette
bust** — visibly showing tall pointed ears, a muzzle, and a shaggy mane in varying
arrangements — and **each struck through with a thick red X.** The book's own point is that
**these are not what Bothans look like.** Kept, and labelled loudly, exactly as this library
handles the Rakata's disowned images. Low resolution (316×253 px) and silhouette-only, so it
carries no colour or detail evidence — its value is entirely the ruling it represents.

**`wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg` — the Legends reference of record** (*The New Essential
Guide to Alien Species* infobox painting, full body, transparent background). This is the
design the repo has effectively adopted:
- **Head is strongly CANINE/EQUINE**: a **long tapering muzzle** projecting well forward from
  the face, ending in a **dark wet nose**, with a horse-like straight bridge rather than a
  dog's stop. **This forward muzzle projection is the single most defining silhouette
  feature.**
- 🔑 **Enormous ears** — tall, narrow, sharply pointed, standing **up and outward** from the
  top of the skull, roughly a third of the head's height. Not small elf points: closer to a
  fennec fox or a bat in proportion. Pale pink-tan interior skin, furred exterior.
- **Fur covers the entire head, neck and hands** in **warm tan-to-mid-brown**, lighter on the
  muzzle and throat, darker over the crown — a naturalistic mammal coat with visible
  direction and length variation, not a flat colour.
- **A mane of longer, straighter blond-tan hair** falling from the crown down past the jaw on
  both sides, distinctly longer than the body coat.
- **Full facial fur reading as a beard/whiskers** around the jaw and under the chin — which
  matches the sourced "both males and females were known to sport beards."
- **Dark eyes**, small relative to the head, set forward under a modest brow.
- **Hands are furred with distinct digits and small claws.**
- 🔴 **No tail is visible**, consistent with the text giving tails to hybrids only.
- **Build is stocky and short-legged**, torso long relative to the legs — reads short and
  broad, agreeing with the 1.4–1.6 m figures.
- Wears an elaborate merchant/spy outfit: red-brown coat, gold-yellow tabard with hanging
  ornaments, bracers, belt pouches, cuffed boots. Costume is individual, not species.

**`wookieepedia_bothan_legends_um.jpg`** — a Legends comic panel of a Bothan running from an
explosion. **Disagrees with the NEGAS painting on the felid/canid balance:**
- **Much more FELINE/LEONINE** — a **short broad muzzle** rather than the long tapering one,
  a flatter face, and a **spiky upright mane** framing the whole head like a lion's.
- **Fur is a bright orange-tan** with cream on the muzzle and throat — considerably more
  saturated than the naturalistic NEGAS coat.
- **Ears are smaller and more cat-like**, set higher and less dramatically tall.
- **Dark eyes, dark nose, furred hands** with digits.
- **No tail visible** here either.
- Green tunic, bracers, belt. Comic-stylised, so treat line and palette as the artist's.
- 🔑 **Taken together, the two Legends images differ on muzzle length, ear size, mane shape
  and coat saturation while agreeing on: full-body fur, pointed ears, a projecting muzzle
  with a dark nose, a mane, dark eyes, furred digited hands, no tail, and a short stocky
  build.** That intersection is the most defensible target — and it is precisely the
  "canine, feline, and equine mixture" the text describes, realised differently by each
  artist.

**`donor_current_sprite.png` — right on fur, wrong on the silhouette.** It is
`OR/Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Heads/Bothan/Male_Bothan_south.png`, the head drawn by
`RSW_BothanHead`. As a RimWorld humanlike head it is a **greyscale mask the game tints at
runtime**, so the absence of fur colour is correct and expected and the colour findings above
belong on the *skin/fur genes*, not on this file. What it gets right: a **jagged fur ruff
framing the cheeks and jaw**, and a **small dark triangular nose**. Three problems:
- 🔴 **There is no muzzle.** The head is a smooth round dome with the nose sitting flat on
  its front surface. **The forward-projecting muzzle is the feature both Legends references
  agree on and the one that makes a Bothan read as a Bothan in silhouette**, and it is
  absent. Notably, **the repo already contains the art to fix this**: `RSW_Nose_Snout`
  ("Carriers of this gene have an elongated, canine-like snout") draws
  `SWX/Pawn/HeadAttachments/bothan/snout` — art authored *for* the Bothan — and **the Bothan
  xenotype does not include that gene.**
- 🔴 **There are no ears in the sprite at all.** Ears come only from the separate
  `Outland_ElvenEars` gene the def carries, which is a *human elf point* — nowhere near the
  tall outward fennec-scale ears of the reference.
- ⚠️ **Eyes are plain human slots** rather than the small forward-set dark mammal eyes, and
  the fur ruff is a flat outline rather than the layered directional coat of the paintings.

## Repo def versus canon — contradictions to report

🔴 **The def's description is verbatim the Legends design that canon has X'd out.** It reads:
*"Bothans are a species of furry mammalian anthropoids from the planet Bothawui, they have a
mixture of features resembling a mix of canine, feline and equine."* Every element of that —
Bothawui as the homeworld, the canine/feline/equine mixture — comes from **`Bothan/Legends`**,
not from canon. Canon gives only "Bothan Space" and no appearance at all, and *How Not to Get
Eaten by Ewoks* prints the Legends silhouettes **crossed out**. Flagging, not fixing: for a
Legends-flavoured campaign this may well be the right choice, but it should be a decision
rather than an accident, and it is the reason this species cannot be validated the way the
others can.

🔴 **`Tail_Furry` is a HYBRID trait applied to the full species.** Legends gives haunches,
hooves, fur, pointed ears **and a long tail** specifically to **half-Bothans** like Valenthyne
Farfalla. The full-Bothan description has no tail, and **neither Legends reference image shows
one.** As shipped, every Bothan in the game has a tail that no Bothan is sourced to have.

🔴 **The species' one listed distinction is missing entirely: mood-sensitive fur (Wrendui).**
The *Ultimate Alien Anthology* infobox lists exactly one distinction for Bothans, and it is
this — fur that ripples with emotional state and **betrays them when they intend to be
duplicitous.** For a race of spies it is the defining trait, it is mechanically expressible
(a social/deception effect visible to others), and nothing in the def touches it.

🔴 **`Beard_NoBeardOnly` contradicts the source directly.** Legends: *"both males and females
were known to sport beards."* The NEGAS reference shows full jaw and chin fur. The def forbids
beards outright — on the one species whose sources go out of their way to say **both sexes**
have them.

- ⚠️ **`Outland_ElvenEars` is the wrong ear.** Sourced as *"tapered pointed ears"*, and the
  reference shows **tall, narrow, outward-standing ears about a third of head height**. A
  human elf-point is far too small and sits in the wrong place.
- ⚠️ **`Outland_AcceleratedMaturation` and `Outland_AcceleratedPregnancy` are unsourced, and
  the lifespan table argues the other way.** Legends gives child 1–11, young adult 12–16,
  adult 17–45 — **human-paced maturation** — with a **shorter** total span (venerable 85+).
  Nothing anywhere describes fast growth or short pregnancy.
- ⚠️ Unsourced: `WoundHealing_Slow` and `RSW_statgene_metabolismcheat`. Neither healing rate
  nor metabolism appears in either article.
- ⚠️ `Hair_Grayless` sits oddly against a species whose sourced hair colours are **brown and
  cream** and which reaches "venerable" at 85 — a normal mammalian lifespan with no
  agelessness cited.
- ✅ Defensible: `RSW_BodySizeGene_small` (**well sourced** — 1.4–1.6 m is genuinely short),
  `Outland_Furbody` (full-body fur is the core sourced trait), `VoiceRoar` (**properly
  sourced from canon for once** — Bothans *"were known to communicate with each other in a
  series of growls"*, one of only two physical facts canon gives),
  `AptitudeStrong_Social` and `AptitudeStrong_Intellectual` (master politicians and spies
  craving intrigue and subterfuge — squarely sourced), and the fur colour set
  `Hair_SandyBlonde` / `Hair_ReddishBrown` / `Hair_DarkBrown` / `Hair_DarkReddish` /
  `Hair_MidBlack`, which brackets the sourced brown-and-cream range and both references'
  tan-to-orange coats reasonably well.
- ⚠️ Missing besides Wrendui: **the canine/equine muzzle** (art already on disk via
  `RSW_Nose_Snout`, gene simply not included), and **eye colours brown / green / violet** —
  the def sets no eye gene, and **violet** is a distinctive sourced option worth having.
