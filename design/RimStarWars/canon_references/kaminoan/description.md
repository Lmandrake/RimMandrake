# Kaminoan

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeKaminoan` (verified in
`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml:1003`; label
in the def is lowercase `kaminoan`). Assigned `Jawa_AscendantHelix: S` — some, in the
gene-cult faction, which is apt: Kaminoans *are* the galaxy's geneticists.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Canon article.** Kaminoans were a tall, thin **amphibian** sentient species from the
aquatic planet **Kamino** in Wild Space. Infobox: designation sentient; class
**amphibian**; **skin colour White**; **eye colour "Black sclerae, white pupils"**;
distinctions **ultraviolet vision**; origin Kamino; habitat "on water"; language
Kaminoan. 🔴 **The canon infobox gives NO height, NO mass and NO lifespan** — those
fields are empty in the wikitext, so any figure quoted as canon is invented.

Biology, verbatim substance: Kaminoans were **slender, towering, pale-skinned
humanoids whose oblong heads sat atop elongated neck bones that allowed them limited
flexibility.** They had **two large, almond-shaped black eyes with white pupils** that
enabled them to **see into the ultraviolet spectrum**. **Male Kaminoans had fin-like
crests atop their heads, while females were completely bald and wore unique head
crescents.** Their **long and frail limbs gave them a graceful, gliding walk** and
featured **small feet** adapted to Kamino's firm seabeds and later hard flooring.
**Kaminoans reached adulthood at 11 years old.**

Behaviour. Kaminoans **generally displayed few signs of outward exuberance or
personality**, considered the clone troopers their finest achievement, and were
**isolationists** who concerned themselves with offworlders only when profitable — a
residual distrust traced to the great flood, after which they judged it less risky to
**adapt their own genetic code and their planet** than to accept Republic aid. They
were **generally scientifically rational**, but made pilgrimages to the sunken ruins of
their ancient cities. They did not think themselves superior to other species; they
simply saw other worlds as a distraction. Nala Se to the clone Fives: *"You were
created in our laboratories. You are Kaminoan property."* By the Imperial era the
species was **all but destroyed** — the Empire levelled every known Kaminoan facility
and city, deported or killed the population, and **only a small number of survivors
were scattered across the stars.** A scattered-remnant species is exactly the right
shape for a rare xenotype in a scavenger campaign.

**Legends article** (marked as Legends, not canon, and *not* interchangeable with the
above): height **2.2 meters**; mass **65 kg female / 70 kg male**; lifespan **up to 80
standard years**; distinctions **very tall, long limbs, long necks**. Legends adds
detail the canon page omits and that reads directly as art direction:
**semi-luminescent, porcelain-like skin, somewhat like mother-of-pearl**; **glassy,
glazed, large eyes**; a **digitigrade stance** on slender legs; almond eyes that
**see colour in the ultraviolet**, so what look like white Kaminoan buildings are
actually painted in colours humans cannot see. Legends life cycle: **full adulthood at
11 years**, physical prime to roughly **40**, ageing signs around **80** — with one
genetically engineered outlier (Kina Ha) living **over 3,000 years**. Legends also
confirms **females bald, males with headcrests and fin ridges along the rear of their
small, bulbous skulls**, and that many high-ranking Kaminoans **wore a large pale
collar around the neck.**

**Unusual abilities.** Ultraviolet vision is the only sensory ability sourced, and it is
in both the canon infobox and the Legends body. There is no Force ability, no
psychic trait, and no combat capability anywhere in either article — the species'
"power" is entirely **cloning and genetic engineering**, and canon is explicit that
their limbs are *frail*.

## Visual brief

🔑 **The neck and head shape are the whole read, and the repo art has neither.** Four
images, two of them live-action reference plates, agree closely.

**Silhouette proportions, measured off `wookieepedia_kaminoans_group.jpg`** (the
Clone Wars two-figure plate, 505×1180, transparent background). On the right-hand
female: crown of head ≈ y45, chin ≈ y115, shoulder line ≈ y250, feet ≈ y1100. So
against a standing height of ~1055 px the **head is ~7% and the bare neck ~13%** —
**the neck is close to twice the length of the head**, and head-plus-neck is roughly
**a fifth of total standing height.** That is the number to hit. A human head-on-neck
is nothing like it.

**Skin is NOT white.** The canon infobox says "White" and every image disagrees in the
same direction:

- `wookieepedia_lama_su_male.jpg` (live-action, AotC): **pale cool grey with a
  lavender/blue cast**, brightest across the crown, with visible fine wrinkle texture
  over the whole face and neck.
- `wookieepedia_taun_we_female.jpg` (live-action, AotC): distinctly **grey with a
  green-blue cast** — noticeably darker and cooler than Lama Su, so hue varies between
  individuals.
- `wookieepedia_kaminoans_group.jpg`: **grey-mauve**, consistent with the above.
- ⚠️ **Lips are the one saturated colour on the whole body: a clear pink/mauve**, on
  both live-action heads. A flat monochrome Kaminoan is wrong; the mouth is a colour
  accent.

**The face, from the two live-action plates.** These settle several things a text
summary gets wrong:

- **Eyes: enormous, almond, upswept at the outer corner, sclera solid glossy BLACK,
  with a large pale grey-white iris/pupil disc** taking up roughly a third of the eye
  width and set centrally. "White pupils" undersells it — it is a big soft disc, not a
  dot. A fleshy lid-ridge overhangs each eye.
- **There IS a nose** — small, narrow, keeled, with two downward-facing nostrils, set
  low on the face. Kaminoans are frequently described as featureless greys; they are
  not.
- **Mouth is small, closed, with full pink lips** and no visible teeth.
- **The head is oblong and tapers downward-forward to a narrow rounded chin**, giving a
  short muzzle; the cranium is bulbous behind and above, and the top is *keeled* (a
  ridge front-to-back) rather than domed.
- **No external ears.**
- **Male (Lama Su): a raised fin crest on the crown running fore-and-aft**, plus small
  fin ridges toward the rear of the skull. **Female (Taun We): smooth cranium with no
  crest**, wearing a beaded head-band with a hanging beaded strand and a disc ornament
  at the jaw — i.e. the female "head crescent" is **worn jewellery, not anatomy**, and
  should not be drawn as a body part.
- **The neck attaches at the rear/underside of the skull, bird-like**, not under the
  centre of the jaw, and carries visible ringed/wrinkled skin.

**Body, from the group plate.** Arms **very long and thin**, hanging past the hip;
hands large relative to the forearm with **long slender fingers** (four visible digits
plus thumb, all elongated) — the `ElongatedFingers` gene is well founded. Torso
narrow and flat. Robes hide the legs in every image found, so the **digitigrade
stance** is text-only evidence (Legends) and was not confirmed visually.

`wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg` is the Legends design and **differs**: same long neck
and tapering skull, but with **thin whisker-like filaments trailing from the sides of
the head** and a warmer grey-tan skin. Treat it as a **variant reference, not the
target** — the live-action AotC heads are the reference of record.

🔴 **`donor_current_sprite.png` — the repo's Kaminoan art misses the species' defining
feature entirely.** It is
`RimMandrakeSW/OR/Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Heads/Kaminoan/Male_Normal_south.png`
(512×512), the head forced by `RSW_KaminoanHead` via `RSW_Kaminoan_MaleNormal` /
`RSW_Kaminoan_FemaleNormal`. It does capture three things: the pointed crown crest,
the almond black eyes, and a tapering lower face. What is wrong:

- **There is no neck at all.** It is a head plate that sits on a standard RimWorld
  humanoid body, so at sprite scale a Kaminoan reads as a slightly odd human. The one
  measurement above — neck ≈ 2× head length, head+neck ≈ 20% of height — is not
  representable in a head texture. **This needs body/neck art or a head graphic that
  includes the neck column, not a recolour.**
- The eyes are **plain black almonds with no pale iris disc**, dropping the single most
  striking facial feature.
- **No nose, no lips, no wrinkle texture** — the face is a smooth mask, where both
  live-action plates show a keeled nose, pink lips and fine wrinkling.
- The crest reads as a single sharp spike; canon is a **low fore-aft fin** plus rear
  ridges.
- The head occupies only a small central fraction of the 512×512 canvas, so effective
  resolution for the detail above is low.

## Candidate images

- `wookieepedia_lama_su_male.jpg` — **the reference of record for the male head.**
  Live-action Attack of the Clones close-up: settles the pale-grey-with-lavender skin,
  the black sclera with large pale iris disc, the keeled nose with downward nostrils,
  the pink lips, the fore-aft crown crest, the fine wrinkle texture, and the way the
  long neck attaches behind the jaw.
- `wookieepedia_taun_we_female.jpg` — **the reference of record for the female head.**
  Same source and fidelity: smooth crestless cranium, cooler green-grey skin (proving
  hue varies between individuals), pink lips, and the beaded head-band that is
  *jewellery* rather than anatomy.
- `wookieepedia_kaminoans_group.jpg` — the canon infobox plate, two full-length figures
  on a transparent background, one crested and one not. **The measurement source for
  the neck-to-head-to-height proportions**, and for the long thin arms and elongated
  fingers. Clone Wars animated style, so treat surface finish as the show's, not the
  species'.
- `wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg` — Legends design, full body. Kept as a **variant,
  not a target**: it adds whisker-like head filaments and a warmer grey-tan skin that
  the live-action version does not have. Useful only to show what NOT to copy if
  live-action fidelity is wanted.
- `donor_current_sprite.png` — the repo's own head texture, kept as the **negative
  reference**: it is what the game currently shows, and the visual brief above is
  largely a list of what it omits.
