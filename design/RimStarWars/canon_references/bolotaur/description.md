# Bolotaur

**defName**: `RSW_Bolotaur` (SWBestiary, texture folder `Bolotaur`)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
The Bolotaur is a **Legends** creature (Star Wars Galaxies era, not current
"real" canon film/TV continuity, but part of our project's accepted roster
per `star_wars_canon_names.md`'s master 160-species list): a large,
long-necked reptilian creature native to **Kashyyyk**. Non-sentient,
class "Reptile."

"The Bolotaur was a large, long-necked reptilian creature native to
Kashyyyk. They lived short lives, but reproduced at a high enough rate to
survive in great numbers. The bolotaur's short lives were spent eating,
which was necessary due to their very fast metabolisms."

History: sometimes tamed and used as mounts — Trandoshan slavers used them
on Kashyyyk during the Clone Wars, and Imperial scout troopers rode them to
navigate Kashyyyk's dense forest terrain during the Empire's occupation.
Faced extinction from poachers during the Galactic Civil War. First
appeared in *Star Wars Galaxies: Rage of the Wookiees* (2005).

No infobox skincolor/size fields are populated on Wookieepedia — color and
precise size come only from illustrations, not sourced text.

## Visual brief
The two candidate images broadly agree on body plan but differ somewhat on
color:
- The in-game 3D render (bounty hunter rider) shows a **tan/light-brown to
  golden-brown** body with darker brown scaled/segmented back plating, a
  long sinuous neck, a blunt horned/spiky-crested head, thick short legs
  ending in large clawed feet, and a long tail.
  Environment (Kachirho-style Wookiee village) confirms the Kashyyyk
  setting.
- The painted illustration (stormtrooper rider, jungle/river setting) shows
  a **darker olive/moss-green** body with a similar low, thick-necked,
  spike-crested turtle/lizard head, dark mottled scale pattern, and large
  clawed webbed-looking feet, wading through water.

Both agree: squat, low-slung reptilian mount with a long neck, spiky/horned
head crest, heavy clawed feet, and a long tail — ridden by a single ground
trooper with a simple saddle. Color varies from tan/brown to olive-green
between the two images, which may simply reflect natural individual
variation (as with real reptiles) rather than a hard disagreement.

Our own donor sprite (`donor_current_sprite.png`) shows a **dark olive-green**
body with a lighter tan/gold head crest and long tail — closer to the
painted illustration's green than the in-game render's tan/brown, and a
reasonable match to the shared body plan (squat, long neck, long tail,
crested head) from both images.
