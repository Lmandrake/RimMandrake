## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
The Bolotaur is a **Legends** creature (Star Wars Galaxies era, not current
"real" canon film/TV continuity, but part of our project's accepted roster
per `star_wars_canon_names.md`'s master 160-species list): a large,
long-necked reptilian creature native to **Kashyyyk**. Non-sentient,
class "Reptile."

"The Bolotaur was a large, long-necked reptilian creature native to
Kashyyyk. They lived short lives, but reproduced at a high enough rate to
survive in great numbers. The bolotaur's short lives were spent eating,
which was necessary due to their very fast metabolisms."

History: sometimes tamed and used as mounts — Trandoshan slavers used them
on Kashyyyk during the Clone Wars, and Imperial scout troopers rode them to
navigate Kashyyyk's dense forest terrain during the Empire's occupation.
Faced extinction from poachers during the Galactic Civil War. First
appeared in *Star Wars Galaxies: Rage of the Wookiees* (2005).

Diet is unspecified (Wookieepedia category "Creatures of unspecified diet"); fast metabolism and constant eating do not establish herbivory. Other Legends appearances: *Republic* 81-83. No infobox skincolor/size fields are populated on Wookieepedia — color and
precise size come only from illustrations, not sourced text.

## Visual brief
**Owner ruling 2026-10-08 (verbatim): "That's realistic, not these cartoon versions you keep using. Replace the canon with something more realistic. The web is full of them."** The stylised Star Wars Galaxies game render (`bolotaur_wookieepedia_2.jpg`, tan rider-in-village scene) was deleted. No live-action or photoreal bolotaur exists (searched: `Images of bolotaurs` — only SWG game captures and this painting); the reference is the realistic painted illustration `bolotaur_wookieepedia_1.jpg` (Galaxy at War), and the donor sprite.

What the painting shows: a squat, low-slung, heavy turtle/lizard-like reptile wading through jungle water with a scout trooper in a simple saddle on its back.
- **Head:** broad, blunt, turtle-like head with a wide flat mouth, reddish lips/gums, heavy brow, and a crest of short pale spikes running back over the neck.
- **Neck:** thick and moderately long, folded skin under the throat.
- **Hide:** dark olive/moss-green on the back and limbs, covered in small rounded pebbly scales; paler cream-green throat and belly.
- **Limbs:** thick short legs with large splayed feet ending in long curved pale-tan claws.
- **Tail:** long, trailing behind.

The colour question is settled toward **olive-green** (the tan/golden-brown version came only from the deleted game render).
