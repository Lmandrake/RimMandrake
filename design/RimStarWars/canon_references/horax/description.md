# Horax

**defName**: `Horax` (third-party, mlie.starwarsanimalcollection, not
vendored — see Watch out)

**Watch out**: this is a genuine Wookieepedia creature, confirmed distinct
from RimWorld's own Anomaly DLC "Horax Cult" faction. `starwars.fandom.com`
has a real `Horax` article (pageid 515533, current canon) and a linked
`Horax/Legends` article (pageid 36520) — nothing here is about the Anomaly
faction.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
Two tiers, same core creature:

- **Current canon** (`Horax`, sourced to *Star Wars: Absolutely Everything
  You Need to Know*, 2015): Horaxes were a type of giant **blue** reptile
  native to the frozen Outer Rim world of **Nelvaan**. Distinctions:
  **saber-sharp fangs, enormous horns, and scales**. Nelvaanian villagers
  had to acquire horax tail scales as part of a warrior initiation ritual;
  the scales could be brewed into an elixir that repelled the beasts for a
  year. The canon infobox carries no `image=` value — Wookieepedia has no
  canon-tagged image for this creature.
- **Legends** (`Horax/Legends`, sourced to the *Star Wars: Clone Wars*
  Databank and the 2003 Genndy Tartakovsky *Clone Wars* microseries,
  "Chapter 22", first aired 2005-03-22): large, non-sentient, **quadrupedal**
  reptile, able to grow **over 15 meters tall** (attributed in-universe to
  Nelvaan's low gravity). Blue skin, **orange eyes**, a **large spiked and
  clubbed tail**, large fangs outside the mouth, and horns on the end of the
  snout. Solitary and territorial; pairs only for mating or territorial
  fights. Carnivorous: the snout horns are shovel-like, used to uproot trees
  and split open siltcrawler burrows to eat the hibernating colonies; four
  enormous saber-like fangs protrude from the maw. The Legends infobox gives
  habitat "Not cold" and the text says horaxes did not fare well in extreme
  cold (the Techno Union siphon generator's ice age thinned the population),
  so the snowy setting is not an adaptation to cold
  (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Horax/Legends). In 19 BBY, Anakin Skywalker killed one after it attacked him,
  Obi-Wan Kenobi, and a clone squad on Nelvaan, disrupting a young
  Nelvaanian's initiation ritual in the process (the "Battle of Nelvaan").
  The Behind-the-Scenes note on the canon page confirms directly: the
  creature "was originally created for Chapter 22" of the Legends
  microseries. The canon page is text only and carries no image; the blue,
  horned, fanged description matches the Legends design.

**Both tiers agree on the load-bearing facts: this is a huge, blue-skinned,
horned-and-fanged reptile, not a small or drab creature.**

## Visual brief
**Owner ruling 2026-10-08 (verbatim): "That's realistic, not these cartoon versions you keep using. Replace the canon with something more realistic. The web is full of them."**

**Animation-only canon — no realistic source found (searched: Wookieepedia `Horax`, `Horax/Legends`, `Battle of Nelvaan` page images — the creature exists only in the 2003 *Clone Wars* microseries and the *Clone Wars Campaign Guide* painting).** The images below are animated/stylised; render this creature realistically anyway — real-world anatomy, materials and lighting, not the cartoon's flat shading or exaggerated proportions. The flat-animation Battle of Nelvaan still (`wookieepedia_battle_horax.jpg`, which showed clone troopers for scale) was deleted; the *Campaign Guide* painting (`wookieepedia_infobox_cw_horax.jpg`) is the most naturalistic depiction and the target — thick, heavily wrinkled blue hide like a giant mammal's, horn and tooth with real keratin/ivory texture. `wookieepedia_tail_attack.jpg` is flat animation kept ONLY because it is the sole image of the spiked, clubbed tail.

Unlike the wyyyschokk/pekopeko cases, there is **no text/image conflict**
here — the two remaining images line up cleanly with both the canon and Legends text:

- `wookieepedia_infobox_cw_horax.jpg` — painted/rendered concept-style
  close-up of the head and forepaws in a snowy forest. Confirms: deep
  **blue**, heavily wrinkled hide; **large tan/beige horns** sweeping up and
  out from the snout in a wide V; **orange/yellow eyes**; multiple long
  saber-like fangs both upper and lower jaw, visible even with the mouth
  mostly closed; thick clawed forepaws with pale tan claws. A tiny robed
  figure with a green lightsaber stands at the creature's foot for scale —
  confirms the "over 15 meters" claim is not an exaggeration; the animal
  dwarfs a standing human many times over.
- `wookieepedia_tail_attack.jpg` — a darker action still: a segmented blue
  tail with pale spiked/clubbed knobs at each joint sweeping through the
  frame, with a trooper or creature silhouette flung through the air.
  Confirms the Legends text's "large spiked and clubbed tail" as an actual
  weapon used on-screen, not just a design note.

**One image was checked and discarded**: Wookieepedia's `AnakinKills.jpg`
(also linked from the Battle of Nelvaan page) was downloaded and inspected,
but it shows a *different* scene entirely — green pig-like creatures in the
foreground, a machine tower, and a robed spirit figure, with no horax visible
at all. It is not included as a candidate; the filename is misleading for
this creature.

**Net read: render blue**, a massive wrinkled reptilian quadruped, tan
sweeping horns on the snout, orange eyes, multiple exposed saber fangs, thick
clawed limbs, and a spiked/clubbed tail as a visible weapon. All three kept
images agree with each other and with both text tiers — there is nothing to
flag as a text/image mismatch this time.
