## Sourced text (Wookieepedia, Legends)
The silooth was a large **Sithspawn beetle** bred by the Sith Empire as a warbeast: ferocious,
relentless, **spraying acid** and **crushing victims with powerful mandibles**. Non-sentient
(Dark Side Sourcebook). Mutated by Sith alchemy from a precursor no bigger than a womp rat to
the **size of a bantha** (the quote calls them "tank-sized scavengers"). **Six legs**; body
covered in **brown segmented chitin plates**, artificially thickened, with **chitinous points
across the carapace**; head at the front of the carapace with **two yellow eyes** and a large
jaw of **yellowish jagged teeth**. Infobox: skin colour brown carapace, distinctions
chitin-plated armour and large mandibles. Only mass deployment: the Battle of Kalsunor;
descendants still live on Kalsunor. Height, mass and lifespan are unsourced and absent.
Page: Silooth/Legends. Fully read.

Legends extras (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silooth/Legends): the natural precursor was a docile swamp beetle; the Sith made it predatory and difficult to control, and it clacked its mandibles loudly before attacking. Acid sprays up to ten metres from sacs in the maw; excellent low-light vision but cannot see past ten metres. After Kalsunor the Sith struggled to control the marauding silooth and abandoned the world, leaving them behind. **Source conflict on limb count:** *The Dark Side Sourcebook* says eight legs; the *Jedi Academy Training Manual* and *Book of Sith* illustrations show six, and Wookieepedia assumes six because those are the later sources.

### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silooth

Canon only establishes the silooth as a type of Sith warbeast named in *The Bestiary of Darth Caldoth* (*Dooku: Jedi Lost*, 2019, audio drama; spelling confirmed by the script). Canon gives no anatomy, size or acid attack; all the detail above is Legends.

## Visual brief
Two Wookieepedia images agree with each other; the donor does not read as the same animal.
- `wookieepedia_silooth_bos.png` (Book of Sith art, 2384x1632): **orange-tan to sandy brown**
  armoured beetle on **six long, thin, jointed legs** with big **scythe-like claw tips**, a
  tall **spined carapace shaped like a swept-up shell** (rows of dark spikes), a
  **horned, reptilian-looking head** with one orange-yellow eye, a **wide toothy maw**, and
  **two large forward mandible-pincers** with pale frilled blades. Legs are lanky relative to
  the body: tall, spidery stance.
- `wookieepedia_silooth.jpg` (513x470, painted): a **deep red-brown to maroon** beast rearing
  on hind legs, huge **curved horn-like mandibles** sweeping up from the head, **open maw of
  yellow teeth**, **large spiked crab-like claws**, spiked shell. Colour differs (maroon) but
  anatomy agrees.
- `donor_current_sprite.png` (128x128): a small **orange-brown plated dome with a dark tip and
  a pale face-mask**, legs tucked under. It carries the brown-orange palette and a plated
  shell but is **squat, short-legged and has no visible horns or mandibles**. Treat as a
  palette match, shape too weak.
- Prose says brown; images show orange-tan (Book of Sith) and maroon (painting): brown to
  rusty orange is safe.
