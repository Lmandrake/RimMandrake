# Granite slug

**defName**: `RSW_GraniteSlug` — in-repo label "granite slug"; no variants


## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Granite_slug

Granite slugs were a type of small slug found on the ecumenopolis Coruscant. The slugs were silica parasites capable of consuming mineral matter, excreting acidic saliva as they moved across the surface of buildings and then consuming the resulting slime using their two mouths. This left behind trails of acid on the buildings that created rune-like scars in the duracrete.

During the Clone Wars, the Senate Rotunda Preservation Committee, a committee within the Galactic Republic's Galactic Senate, was responsible for exterminating granite slugs and keeping the Senate Rotunda in the Senate Building architecturally sound.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Granite_slug/Legends

The granite slug was a silica parasite native to the planet Coruscant that left a corrosive acidic slime wherever it travelled. They were preyed upon by Hawk-bats.

## Visual brief
Both images are LEGENDS (Legends page only; the canon page has no image), and they disagree strongly with each other. The prose gives almost nothing visual (a small slug, two mouths, acid trails).
- **`legends_1` (colour illustration, infobox):** a long, low slug, roughly caterpillar/sea-slug shaped. Body is ochre-orange banded with large blue-teal ringed blotches (concentric spots) running in rows down the flanks. The back carries a dense crest of short fleshy, teal-tipped papillae/fronds, tallest over the hump of the midsection. A pale grey-white foot/belly runs underneath. The head end has three or four thin, tall stalks (eye stalks and tentacle stalks, with trumpet-like flared tips; one points forward and up, others curl) and a small spiky-ringed mouth at the front. The tail end curls upward and finishes in a second spiky starburst-shaped ring of tentacles around an opening, which reads as the "second mouth" the canon prose mentions. A trail of yellow-green slime sits behind.
- **`legends_2` (black-and-white ink drawing):** a plain, smooth, fat, tapered slug with two short eye stalks tipped with dark knobs, a faint mottled/plated skin pattern and no frond crest, shown over a spreading black slime trail. Far simpler than `legends_1`; no second mouth visible.
- Neither image carries a size cue; text says only "small". Both agree on: slug body, eye stalks, slime trail.
- **Trust order:** `legends_1` is the only colour reference and matches the "two mouths" prose; `legends_2` supports a plainer baseline silhouette.
