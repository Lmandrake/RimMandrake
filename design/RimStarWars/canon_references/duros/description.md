# Duros

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeDuros` (verified present in
`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`). Matrix:
`OutlanderCivil: R`, `Pirate: R`, `Jawa_Junkers: R`, `Jawa_DeepwaterCompact: R` — **rare, but
placed in four factions**, the widest spread of the five species in this batch. A Duros is the
one most likely to actually be met, and it fits the traders/pilots role canon gives them.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Current canon** (`/wiki/Duros`). The Duros were a **humanoid species** native to **Duro**, a
Core Worlds planet. 🔑 **"They had smooth blue-green skin, red eyes, lipless mouths, long thin
noseless faces, and green blood."** Infobox `distinctions`: **No nose · Large eyes · Green
blood**. Language **Durese**. A ritual phrase of the Duros people: ***"Let us become spears."***
They were **related to the similar-looking Neimoidian species**, having diverged
distinguishably by **15,000 BBY**.

🔑 **The unusual ability, and it is a good one:** *"When the Duros experienced high amounts of
strain, a kind of oil — which had a highly irritating smell and bad taste — gathered in the
sacs along their ribs. This was an evolutionary throwback and defense mechanism, remaining
from when the Duros were liable to be eaten by a number of entities roaming Duro."*
(*The High Republic: Light of the Jedi*.) A stress-triggered chemical repellent in **rib
sacs** is the single most distinctive Duros trait in current canon and it is purely
behavioural/mechanical, not visual.

🔴 **The canon infobox has no height, no length, no mass and no lifespan** — all four fields
are blank. **There is no canonical Duros height or lifespan to cite.** What it *does* give is
a **wide colour menu**: skin **Blue, Brown, Gray, Green, Purple, Turquoise**; eyes **Gold,
Red, Yellow**.

**Legends** (`/wiki/Duros/Legends`) fills in the numbers and the biology. Infobox: class
**Reptile**; races include **Neimoidian** and **Near-Duros**; **height 1.7 – 2.2 meters**;
skin **Blue, Green**; distinctions **Noseless · Large red eyes · Affinity for space travel ·
Green blood**; lifespan bands — child 1–9, young adult 10–14, adult 15–35, middle age 36–49,
old 50–69, **venerable 70+**; language **Durese**; mass **empty**. Body text:
- **"humanoids with smooth blue-green skin, red eyes, lipless mouths, long thin noseless faces
  and green blood."**
- 🔑 **"Olfactory organs beneath their eyes were responsible for their sense of smell."** The
  noselessness is not anosmia — the organ moved.
- 🔑 **"Both females and males were bald, though both sexes were easily distinguished."**
- 🔑 **"Their large red goggle-like eyes also had slit pupils."**
- 🔑 **"Duros females laid eggs as they were descended from ancient reptiles, and like the
  Neimoidians, they were born in a larval grub stage, but unlike their cousins who left the
  raising of their young mainly to the state, the Duros took care of their offspring from
  birth."**

**Behaviour / culture (Legends).** **Usually taciturn**, but they **enjoyed telling stories
about their travels**, and were said to have **a photographic memory when it came to telling
tales**. **Many were pilots and explorers — adventurous, though sometimes rash.** Among the
**first cultures to develop hyperdrive** (possibly the first); Duros-charted routes were still
in use in the Imperial era, and they remained known for **superior astro-navigational skills**.
They preferred the honorific **"Traveler"** regardless of actual occupation, and using it was
good etiquette. Durese was a **lingua franca among spacers**.

## Visual brief

🔴 **The single biggest disagreement in this directory: the prose says "blue-green," the def
says blue only, and every species-level reference image is GREEN or GREY-GREEN. The one
unambiguously blue Duros here is a single famous individual.**

**`wookieepedia_cantina_pair.jpg`** (film photograph, the two Duros in Chalmun's Cantina —
practical makeup, the origin of the design) is the highest-fidelity reference:
- **Skin is a desaturated grey-green / slate blue-green**, not blue. It reads closer to wet
  clay than to any azure.
- 🔑 **The cranium is the silhouette**: a tall, smooth dome that swells **upward and
  backward** well past a human skull, then the face narrows sharply to a **small pinched
  chin** — an inverted-teardrop head. This is what "long thin face" actually means and it is
  the species' defining shape.
- **Large almond eyes with a glowing orange-red iris and a horizontal slit pupil**, set wide
  and angled, under heavy hooded lids. They read as lit from within.
- **No nose whatsoever** — just a slight vertical ridge and a shallow depression beneath each
  eye (which is where the Legends text puts the olfactory organs).
- **Lipless mouth**: a narrow downturned slit with no lip roll at all.
- ⚠️ **The skin is NOT smooth here.** Deep horizontal creases across the brow and vertical
  furrows down the cheeks. The prose in both continuities says "smooth"; the film makeup is
  heavily wrinkled. Treat wrinkling as legitimate variation, not as a rule.
- **Hands have long, thin, tapering fingers** — clearly longer than human, supporting the
  def's `ElongatedFingers`.
- **Completely bald**, both individuals, matching the Legends text.

**`wookieepedia_canon_infobox.jpg`** (the current-canon infobox render, a Duros fighter in dust
fatigues): **plainly green — a mid olive/jade green with no blue in it at all**, mottled
lightly. Same tall backswept dome, same red-orange slit eyes, same noseless lipless face,
bald, brow creased. Human proportions, ordinary build, and **short** relative to the frame.

**`wookieepedia_legends_infobox.jpg`** (Legends infobox, a Duros pilot holding a helmet):
**pale grey-green, almost celadon** — the palest of the four — with the same dome skull, wide
orange-red eyes and long green fingers. Note the pilot dress: the space-travel affinity is
baked into how the species is illustrated.

**`wookieepedia_cad_bane.jpg`** (Cad Bane, full body): **the blue one.** Pale ice/powder blue,
faintly greenish in shadow, with **red eyes**, a lipless mouth showing **small visible teeth**,
the same elongated backswept cranium (mostly under the hat), and **long thin blue fingers with
what read as three main digits**. Human height and lean build. It confirms blue is real — as
**one point in a range that also contains green, grey-green and celadon.**

🔑 **Constants across all four: the tall backswept dome cranium narrowing to a pinched chin;
large glowing red-orange slit-pupil eyes; no nose; a lipless slit mouth; total baldness; long
thin fingers.** The variable is hue, which runs **blue → grey-green → jade → celadon.**

**`donor_current_sprite.png` is the second big finding.** It is
`RimMandrakeSW/OR/Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Heads/Duros/Male_Normal_south.png` (512×512):
- **Shape is wrong in the one way that matters**: a **rounded human ovoid**, very slightly
  tapered at the chin. **There is no tall backswept dome.** Nothing about this silhouette
  reads Duros.
- ✅ Correctly **noseless** and **mouthless**, which is right — but the eyes are two ordinary
  small dots with human eyebrows above them, nowhere near "large goggle-like."
- 🔴 **Colour: measured pure greyscale (chroma spread 0 across all 31,425 opaque pixels), and
  `SW_HeadTypes.xml` sets `useSkinShader: false` on both Duros head types with NO
  `shaderType: CutoutComplex` and NO `*m.png` colour mask in the folder.** By contrast the
  Cathar and Iktotchi head types set the same flag but *do* add `CutoutComplex` and *do* ship
  `*_southm.png` red-channel masks (measured R=253/R=127). **On disk, nothing supplies the
  Duros head a colour** — so the blue-green skin most likely does not reach the face at all.
  ⚠️ This is an inference from def + texture bytes, not an in-game observation; confirm in
  game before treating it as settled.
- Note the *other* Duros art on disk, `SWX/Pawn/HeadType/duros/`, has **`Fivehead`
  Normal/Pointy/Wide variants** — a tall-forehead head, i.e. much closer to the canonical
  dome — but the head type the def actually points at is the plain `OR/…/Heads/Duros` one.

## Engine limits
- **The shipped head cannot show any skin colour at all.** `SW_HeadTypes.xml` sets
  `useSkinShader: false` on both Duros head types with no `CutoutComplex` shader and no
  `*m.png` colour mask file in the folder — measured pure greyscale (chroma spread 0) on
  the current donor sprite. So none of the skin-colour genes (blue, green, or otherwise)
  plausibly reach the face as things stand. ⚠️ This is an inference from def + texture
  bytes, not an in-game observation — confirm in game before treating it as settled.

## Def-versus-canon contradictions (report only — not fixed here)

1. 🔴 **Green is missing from the skin genes entirely.** Def carries
   `Outland_Skin_DeepBlue`, `Skin_Blue`, `Outland_Skin_PaleAzure` — three blues. Canon prose in
   *both* continuities is **"smooth blue-**green** skin"**; the canon infobox lists
   **Green, Turquoise, Brown, Gray, Purple** alongside Blue; the Legends infobox lists
   **Blue, Green**; and the canon infobox *image*, the Legends infobox image and the film
   photograph are all green or grey-green. A Duros that cannot be green is wrong.
2. 🔴 **The head cannot show its skin colour.** `RSW_Duros_MaleNormal` /
   `RSW_Duros_FemaleNormal` set `useSkinShader: false` over a **pure-greyscale** texture with
   **no `CutoutComplex` shader and no colour mask** — unlike Cathar and Iktotchi, which pair
   the same flag with `CutoutComplex` plus an `*m.png` mask. So even the blues in the gene list
   plausibly never reach the face. (Inference from disk; verify in game.)
3. 🔴 **The head shape is a plain human ovoid.** The tall backswept dome narrowing to a pinched
   chin is the species' entire silhouette and it is absent from the shipped head, even though a
   closer `Fivehead` head exists elsewhere in the same mod's textures.
4. 🔴 **`Outland_Scalebody` and `RSW_butchergene_lizardskin` contradict "smooth."** Both
   continuities say **smooth** skin. Reptilian *ancestry* is sourced (Legends class
   **Reptile**, egg-laying, "descended from ancient reptiles"), so the genes are not baseless —
   but a **scaled** body surface is not what any source or image shows.
5. 🔴 **The rib-sac irritant oil is absent.** The one unusual ability current canon gives the
   species — a stress-triggered, foul-smelling, foul-tasting chemical defence in sacs along the
   ribs — has no gene of any kind.
6. ⚠️ **Nothing represents the species' actual reputation.** Legends distinctions include
   **"affinity for space travel"**, "superior astro-navigational skills", and a
   **photographic memory for tales**; the def instead gives `AptitudeStrong_Shooting` /
   `AptitudePoor_Melee`, neither of which any source supports at species level (Cad Bane is one
   gunslinger, not the species).
7. ⚠️ **Eye size and eye art.** `RSW_Eyes_HugeRed` is the right idea — canon `distinctions`
   include **"Large eyes"** and Legends specifies **"large red goggle-like eyes … with slit
   pupils"** — but the gene reuses the **Jawa** eye texture
   (`SWX/Pawn/HeadAttachments/bigeyes/jawaeyes_red`) at `drawSize 0.2`, and no source gives a
   Duros a Jawa eye. Slit pupils are not represented.
8. ⚠️ **`MinTemp_SmallIncrease` and `Outland_DeceleratedPregnancy` are unsourced.** Nothing
   found gives Duros heat preference or an altered gestation length. `Outland_DeceleratedPregnancy`
   also sits oddly beside `Outland_EggLayer`.
9. **The larval grub stage is unrepresented** — Legends states Duros are "born in a larval grub
   stage." RimWorld arguably cannot express this; recording it as a known, accepted omission.
10. ✅ **Sourced and correct — do not "fix" these:** `Outland_EggLayer` (Legends: females laid
    eggs), `Outland_Blood_Green` (canon distinction), `Hair_BaldOnly` + `Beard_NoBeardOnly`
    (Legends: both sexes bald), `ElongatedFingers` (visible in every image), **no body-size
    gene** and **no lifespan gene** — canon gives neither, and Legends' 1.7–2.2 m / venerable
    70+ are close enough to human that inventing one would be the error.
11. ✅ **Namer is correct** — `RSW_KoTOR_NamerDuros` reads
    `RimMandrakeSWNames/SWX/Duros/{First,Last,Nick}`, all present, contents Duros-appropriate
    (`Hol`, `Nar`, `Nod`). No wrong-species namer here.
