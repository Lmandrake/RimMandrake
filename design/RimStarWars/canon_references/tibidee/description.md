**defName**: `Tibidee` (donor: Star Wars Animal Collection (Continued), `mlie.starwarsanimalcollection`; the donor's own def, no owned port). Cast in The Forge via `src/RimUtinni/UtinniPatches/Patches/WildAnimals_TheForge.xml` (`MayRequire` on the donor). Donor art: `swanimals/Tibidee/Tibidee`.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia, canon)
Tibidees were **giant silicon-based** creatures native to Stygeon Prime (Databank) and found on
other mountainous Outer Rim planets such as Oosalon. Non-sentient. They **fly gracefully on
giant flat wings plus internal gasbags**; **green eyes**, **long tails**; keen vision; a mating
call that resembles communication frequencies and attracts them to starships (a pest, remotely
related to neebrays and mynocks). Length 16.17 m (Rebels: Head to Head) or 53 ft 2 in
(Encyclopedia); mass 750 kg; habitat cold, rugged terrain. Lifespan and diet unsourced.
Normally gentle but attack anything they feel is threatening (Databank, stormtroopers in
Rebels); known to form mated pairs. In "Rise of the Old Masters" the *Phantom*'s jamming
frequency was mistaken for a mating call: they collided with it and Hera lured them onto the
Imperial forces (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tibidee; Canon, Rebels, first appearance).
Page: https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tibidee, fully read.

## Visual brief
- `wookieepedia_tibidee_sw_card_trader.png` (1940x1520, 3D render): a **manta-ray-like flier**
  seen from the front-below: **very long narrow swept wings** tapering to points, mottled
  **pale grey-white** upper surface with a pale stripe, a dark under-edge with a **red-brown
  underside to the wing**, a small broad head with **yellow eyes**, small side fins and a
  tail behind.
- `wookieepedia_tibideemate_homeworlds.png` (1374x830, Homeworlds art): two tibidees over
  mountains. **Bulbous, rounded white-grey body** with a **wide circular open mouth**, **round
  yellow eyes on the sides of the head**, **big red wings with ribbed veins** edged in a pale
  grey spar, short blue-grey claws under the belly and a **segmented tail** with a red fin.
  Plump and gasbag-like, not sleek.
- `donor_current_sprite.png` (128x128): a **pale grey head-on shape with two upswept wing tips
  and a small face**. Palette (white-grey) agrees; there are no red wings and no gasbag body.
- The images differ on body (sleek ray vs plump) and on wing colour (grey-green top with red
  underside vs fully red); both agree on **huge flat wings, white-grey body, yellow eyes (prose
  says green: images show yellow, trust the images on appearance) and a long tail**.
