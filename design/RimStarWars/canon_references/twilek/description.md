# Twi'lek

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeTwilek` (verified present in
`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`; matches the
defName recorded in `RACES_TODO.md`). Assigned `Jawa_HuttCartel: S`, `Jawa_Junkers: R`,
`Pirate: R`, `OutlanderCivil: R` — the widest faction spread of any race in this batch, so
its art will be seen constantly.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

### Current canon (`Twi'lek`)
Infobox: **near-human**, sentient. **Races: Lethan and Rutian.** **Skin colour: black,
blue, gray, green, olive, orange, pale, pink, purple, red, teal, white, yellow** — thirteen,
each separately cited. Eye colour: black, blue, brown, green, hazel, orange, pink, purple,
red, yellow. Distinction: **lekku** (that is the whole list — one entry). Origin **Ryloth**
(Outer Rim); habitat **desert or wastelands**; language **Twi'leki**. **Height, mass and
lifespan are BLANK — unsourced, do not invent them.** Also called *tail-heads* or *Twi*
(xenophobic) and *Rylothians*.

Opening, verbatim: *"Twi'leks … were a **tall** near-human species whose most striking
feature was a pair of long appendages protruding from their skulls, called lekku. They
hailed from the planet of Ryloth … and **female Twi'leks were often exported off world as
slaves.** Twi'leks were considered **one of the most common species in the galaxy, with its
members easily able to blend into a crowd without drawing attention**, as opposed to rarer,
more prominent species, like Togrutas."*

🔑 **The head appendages, verbatim:** *"Their most distinctive feature was the pair of
**prehensile** appendages that sprouted from the **crown or base of their skulls**, known as
lekku, or head-tails. Although Twi'leks normally had **two** lek, **some individuals had
four**. **Super sensitive lek could grab and hold objects and react to the slightest
touch.** A damaged lekku could cause serious harm to a Twi'lek's health."*

🔑 **Ears — the trait the repo's own description gets wrong:** *"While most males typically
had **ears with lobes resembling those of humans**, some males had **cone-shaped hearing
organs** located in the same place on the head, a trait **customarily** found in the females
of the same species."*

🔑 **Teeth are naturally blunt; sharp teeth are cultural, not biological:** *"Although
Twi'leks naturally had **blunt teeth**, **males typically sharpened them into points**."*

Body: *"Twi'leks were usually **thin**, although **corpulent** individuals were known to
exist"* (Orn Free Taa). *"Under normal circumstances, they generally had a **higher body
temperature** than other humanoid species. They had even higher body temperatures **when in
fear**."* They **interbreed with humans** — Hera Syndulla and Kanan Jarrus' son Jacen
*"appeared mostly human but inherited his mother's green pigment in his hair and ears."*

Society: Twi'leks were used as **undercover agents** precisely because they are common and
unremarkable — *"Due to their frequent use as slaves as well as their commonality in the
galaxy, Twi'leks could move surreptitiously through places an army could not penetrate."*

### The `Lekku` article (canon) — the best single source on the appendages
*"In Twi'leks, lekku were **shapely prehensile tentacles that grew from the crown of their
skulls**. While Twi'leks normally had two lekku, certain individuals like **Orn Free Taa**
had four of them. Super-sensitive lekku could be used for grabbing and holding objects.
Those organs were very sensitive to touch, and **a damaged lek could cause severe harm** …
[**Squoxx** died within an hour after one of his lekku was bitten off.]"* Loss is not always
fatal — **Loden Greatstorm** survived having both cut off and would have received
**prosthetic replacements**.

**Lekku are a language organ, with specific documented gestures:** *"the right lek tip
raised meant 'hello,' the left lek tip dipped meant 'goodbye,' both lekku tips crossed over
twice meant 'love you,' and both lekku tips jabbed into the speaker's back meant 'may spice
salt your wounds!'"* One Twi'lek Jedi's *"lekku twitched whenever she lied."*

**How they are worn varies, and this is explicitly individual choice:** *"**Some Twi'leks
like Cham Syndulla tattooed their lekku.** Some wore them simply **hanging down their back**
or **falling over the chest**, while others like **Bib Fortuna bent them around the neck or
shoulders** for aesthetic purposes."*

Same article, on the neighbouring species — useful because the two are constantly confused:
lekku belong to *"all Buktari, Twi'leks, Togruta, Tosarvi, Walunas, and male Ozrelanso.
**The Togruta sported three of them, while the other species had two (occasionally four, in
the case of Twi'leks).**"*

### Legends (`Twi'lek/Legends`) — the only source of numbers
Legends infobox: **mammal**, **height 1.6–2 meters**, **diet omnivorous**, races **Lethan**
and **Rutian**, distinctions *"Lekku"* and *"**Cone-shaped ears** (feminine trait)"*.
Legends skin colours: black, blue, green, orange, red, pink, purple, **tan**, yellow, white.
**Lifespan is blank in Legends too — there is no sourced Twi'lek lifespan in either
continuity.**

- *"mostly **hairless** … mammalian humanoids, with two legs and two arms."*
- 🔑 *"The Twi'leks were **omnivores**. This diet was essential given that food was scarce on
  their homeworld, Ryloth. **Twi'leks possessed multiple stomachs, an unusual trait that
  enabled them to eat almost any food.** When traveling off their homeworld, Twi'leks rarely
  refused food, which frequently led to obesity among starfarers. Twi'lek society also
  considered **obesity a sign of affluence and success**. In obese Twi'leks, **ear flaps
  enlarged to store fat**."*
- *"The brain-tails … **contained part of a Twi'lek's brain**. The very word 'Twi'lek' was
  believed to be derived from semi-Basic '**twin lekku**.'"* They also **store fat** and are
  **erogenous zones**. Twi'leki (Ryl) *"was spoken using a combination of words and subtle
  movements of the lekku"*, and Twi'leks *"could even communicate in complete secrecy"* that
  way.
- 🔑 *"**Newborn Twi'leks had no lekku.**"*
- *"Twi'lek females were often used as slaves or dancers **because of their beauty**."*
- **Lethan (red) rarity:** *"the red-tinted Lethan Twi'leks were one of the rarest
  subgroups, perhaps even the most rare, with **only one in a million** having red skin."*

**What is fixed vs. what varies per individual** — the decision list, since a sprite must
commit:
| Feature | Fixed by canon | Varies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lekku count | **Two** is the species norm | **Four** occurs in rare individuals (Orn Free Taa) |
| Lekku origin | **the crown / base of the skull** — never the sides, never the jaw | — |
| Lekku shape | thick at the base, smooth, **tapering to a point**, fleshy not horny | length; thickness with body mass (they store fat) |
| Lekku surface | **no banding** (this is the hard line against Togruta) | **spots/mottling** (Aayla Secura) and **tattoos** (Cham Syndulla) do occur |
| Lekku carriage | — | **down the back**, **over the chest**, **swept up over the head**, or **coiled round the neck/shoulders** — all four are canon and are personal style |
| Ears | present, in the human position | **large pale cones** (customarily female) **or** small human/pointed lobes; *some* males have the cones |
| Teeth | **naturally blunt** | males often **file** them to points — grooming, not genetics |
| Hair | **none** — infobox haircolor blank, Legends "mostly hairless" | — |
| Skin | one saturated hue, no markings | 13 canon colours; **red = Lethan (1 in a million)**, **blue = Rutian** |
| Build | usually **thin** | corpulent individuals exist and are socially *admired* |

## Visual brief

- 🔑 **Two lekku, from the crown, smooth and completely unbanded — this is the whole species
  read, and it is the thing most often drawn wrong.** Every reference agrees: the lekku
  emerge from the top/back of the skull, are as thick as a forearm at the base, taper
  steadily to a blunt point, and have **no transverse stripes**.
  `wookieepedia_two_kinds_of_lekku.jpg` is the decisive image, because it puts Hera Syndulla
  (Twi'lek) and Ahsoka Tano (Togruta) in one frame: **Hera's lekku are smooth and exactly
  the same green as her face; Ahsoka's are pale with bold blue bands and fused to montrals.**
  🔴 **A banded Twi'lek lek is a Togruta lek. A Twi'lek has no montrals and no horns at all.**
- **The lekku are the same colour as the skin, or very slightly darker.** Not a contrasting
  colour. `wookieepedia_numa_youngling.jpg` shows the maximum: a teal child whose lekku are a
  slightly deeper blue-teal than her face, with a soft darker line along the upper edge.
- **Markings, where they exist, are soft blotches or applied tattoos — never bands.** Aayla
  Secura (`wookieepedia_aayla_secura.jpg`) carries **irregular pale blue-grey mottled
  spots** scattered along both lekku. That is the one canon "patterned lekku" look in this
  directory, and it is nothing like Togruta banding.
- **Carriage genuinely varies and a sprite has to pick one.** Aayla: over the shoulders and
  down past the waist. Numa: straight down the back. The Legends plate
  (`wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg`): swept **up and over** the crown, then curling out.
  Bib Fortuna in `wookieepedia_infobox_twileks.jpg`: **draped around the neck and across the
  torso.** The Ryloth crowd shows several at once. *For a top-down RimWorld sprite,
  "down the back or over the chest" is the only carriage that reads at that scale.*
- ⚠️ **The ears are where the prose, the def and the images all diverge, so trust the
  images.** `wookieepedia_oola.jpg` (live-action Oola, green) shows the female cone plainly:
  a **large, pale, trumpet-shaped cone protruding sideways from each side of the head, pale
  pink at the mouth of the cone and clearly a different colour from the green skin** — a big,
  unmissable feature. But `wookieepedia_aayla_secura.jpg` and
  `wookieepedia_numa_youngling.jpg` are **both female and both have small swept-back
  *pointed* ears, no cones at all**, and so does the female in the Legends plate. So the
  cone is **not** a reliable female marker — it is one of two female ear forms. The orange
  female in `wookieepedia_infobox_twileks.jpg` (seen in profile) has a large flat **disc**
  over the ear which may be a headpiece rather than anatomy.
- **Skin: saturated, unmarked, and the pale/cream end of the range is heavily used.**
  `wookieepedia_innocents_of_ryloth.jpg` is the best population sample in this directory — a
  crowd of ordinary Ryloth villagers — and it runs **teal, mid-blue, pale powder-blue,
  grey-blue, and a large number of cream/tan/ochre individuals**. The infobox trio is
  **orange, pale cream (Bib Fortuna), and blue (Aayla)**. 🔴 **Cream/pale/tan is not an edge
  case; it is roughly a third of every crowd shot here** — which matters because the repo def
  has no pale or grey option (below).
- **Faces are otherwise near-human**: human eyes with visible whites, human nose, human
  mouth and lips, no muzzle. Oola and Aayla both have ordinary five-fingered hands. Build is
  slim and long-limbed. Bib Fortuna's is the one exception — a heavy body **and a tall domed
  cranium** that the lekku emerge from, which fits the Legends "obesity is affluence" and
  "ear flaps enlarge to store fat" notes.

### The repo's art
`donor_current_sprite.png` is `Female_Normal_south.png`. It is much better than the Togruta
equivalent: a pale head with **two lekku descending on either side of the face**, past the
chin, with a crown/hairline suggestion where they emerge. Species-correct in count, origin
and shape, and the "over the chest" carriage is a canon one. Two head types × two patterns
exist (`Normal` and `Striped`, male and female), all twelve textures distinct. The
**`Striped` variant is soft horizontal pale bands baked into the lekku** — a reasonable
stand-in for Aayla's mottling *if* it reads as soft blotching in game, but ⚠️ it is drawn as
regular transverse banding, which is the Togruta cue. It is the one thing in the Twi'lek art
that risks blurring the two species.

⚠️ **A colour question worth an in-game check.** All four Twi'lek `HeadTypeDef`s carry
`useSkinShader: false` (Tabula Rasa mod extension) and **there are no `*m.png` mask files in
the Twi'lek head folder at all** — measured, zero. The Togruta heads set the same flag *and*
`shaderType: CutoutComplex` *and* ship a full set of `_m` masks. If the Twi'lek heads render
untinted for want of a mask, an eighteen-gene skin-colour palette would land on a
grey-white head above a coloured body. **I could not settle this from disk — it needs one
look in game.** Flagging the asymmetry, not asserting the bug.

## 🔴 Def-versus-canon contradictions found

Reported, not fixed. `RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml` is generated — do not edit it.

1. **🔴 There is no red skin gene, so the def cannot produce a Lethan Twi'lek.** Canon lists
   only **two** Twi'lek races, `Lethan` (red) and `Rutian` (blue), and the def covers Rutian
   several times over (`Skin_Blue`, `Outland_Skin_DeepBlue`, `Outland_Skin_Azure`,
   `Outland_Skin_PaleAzure`) while omitting red entirely. Red is separately cited to
   *Smuggler's Run* in the canon infobox. Also missing: **black**, **gray**, and
   **white/pale** — leaving the def with 18 skin genes covering only 6 of the 13 canon
   colour families (yellow, green, blue, purple, pink/magenta, orange). Pale/cream is the
   visually costly omission: it is Bib Fortuna's colour, it is in the article's own infobox
   image, and it is about a third of the Ryloth crowd shot.
2. **🔴 `Turn_Gene_FrailDigestion` is the exact inverse of the species' documented
   adaptation.** Legends: *"Twi'leks possessed **multiple stomachs**, an unusual trait that
   **enabled them to eat almost any food**"* — an evolved response to famine on Ryloth.
   Nothing in either continuity suggests weak digestion. Note the pairing is backwards
   across this batch: the *Togruta* def gets `StrongStomach` and the Twi'lek — the species
   with the extra stomachs — gets frail digestion.
3. **⚠️ `Turn_Gene_AgressionSubmissive`, `Libido_High` and `Beauty_Beautiful` encode an
   in-universe atrocity as species biology.** The sourced texts say female Twi'leks *"were
   often exported off world as slaves"* and *"were often used as slaves or dancers because of
   their beauty"* — facts about **how others treated them**, and about a **subset of one
   sex**. No canon or Legends text found in this pass says Twi'leks are innately submissive
   or high-libido. `Beauty_Beautiful` and `Libido_High` also apply to every Twi'lek including
   males and children. This is a design call for the owner rather than a typo, but it is
   currently stated as genetics.
4. **The xenotype description's sexual-dimorphism claim is wrong on both halves.** It says
   *"the males have pointed human ears and females have cone shaped hearing organs
   instead."* Canon: male ears have **lobes resembling those of humans** (not "pointed"),
   and the cone is *"customarily"* female — **some males have cones too**, so "instead" is
   too strong. And the references break it further: Aayla Secura and Numa are both female
   with small pointed ears and no cones. The description also omits that some individuals
   have **four** lekku, and its phrase *"smooth, patterned lekku"* is self-contradictory —
   though "smooth" is the correct and important half.
5. **`Outland_Blood_Magenta` is unsourced.** No blood colour for Twi'leks was found in the
   canon article, the Legends article or the Lekku article. Unsourced means absent.
6. **Nothing in the def expresses what the lekku actually do**, although canon is
   unusually specific: **prehensile, able to grab and hold objects**, hypersensitive to
   touch, load-bearing for the Twi'leki language, and **damage to one can be lethal**
   (Squoxx died within the hour). `RSW_TwilekHead` is a bare `forcedHeadTypes` gene — no
   `capMods`, no `statFactors`, no manipulation or talking offset, and no body-part
   definition that could be injured. Likewise the canon **higher body temperature** (raised
   further by fear) maps very naturally onto a RimWorld heat/cold-tolerance gene and is not
   represented at all.
7. **No four-lekku variant exists**, and no juvenile lekku-less form (Legends: *"Newborn
   Twi'leks had no lekku"*). Both are minor/optional; recorded so the omission is a choice.
8. **The name-maker has the same unused-surname defect as the Togruta one.**
   `RSW_KoTOR_NamerTwilek` is correctly Twi'lek-specific — it reads
   `RimMandrakeSWNames/SWX/Twilek/{First,Last,Nick}`, **no wrong-species leakage** — but its
   `rulesStrings` derive `lastGenerated` from `[NameTwilek]` (the *first*-name file) and the
   `LastNameTwilek` keyword bound to `Twilek/Last` **appears in no rule**. The curated
   surname list is never read.
9. **Things the def gets right and that should NOT be "fixed":**
   - **No fangs/sharp-teeth gene.** Correct, and subtly so: canon teeth are *naturally
     blunt* and males *sharpen* them — culture, not genetics. (Contrast the Togruta def,
     which correctly *does* carry fangs.)
   - `Hair_BaldOnly` / `Beard_NoBeardOnly` — correct; canon haircolor is blank and Legends
     says *"mostly hairless."*
   - `Body_Standard` with **no invented body-size or lifespan gene** — correct. Canon gives
     no height or lifespan, and Legends' 1.6–2 m is the human range. There is no sourced
     lifespan in *either* continuity, so any longevity gene would have been an invention.
   - `Outland_Evasive` is arguably supported — *"easily able to blend into a crowd without
     drawing attention"* and employment as undercover agents — but that is social
     invisibility, not agility, so treat the mapping as loose rather than sourced.
   - ⚠️ `Outland_LowFertility` and `Outland_CalmingPheromones` are **unsourced**; nothing
     found this pass speaks to Twi'lek fertility or pheromones.

## Candidate images
- `wookieepedia_two_kinds_of_lekku.jpg` — **the reference of record, and the reason this
  directory matters.** Hera Syndulla (Twi'lek) and Ahsoka Tano (Togruta) in one frame:
  smooth unmarked skin-coloured lekku and no montrals, versus pale blue-banded lekku fused
  to montrals. Settles the one confusion that would ruin either sprite. (The same file is in
  `../togruta/`, deliberately.)
- `wookieepedia_infobox_twileks.jpg` — the canon infobox: three Twi'leks side by side —
  **orange** female in profile, **pale cream** heavy-set male (Bib Fortuna, lekku coiled
  round the neck and torso, tall domed cranium), **blue** female (Aayla, Rutian). One image
  that establishes the colour spread, both build extremes, and two carriage styles.
- `wookieepedia_innocents_of_ryloth.jpg` — **the population sample.** A *Clone Wars* crowd of
  ordinary Ryloth villagers, male and female, kneeling. Teal, mid-blue, powder-blue,
  grey-blue and many **cream/tan** individuals; all with two smooth unbanded lekku worn down
  the back. The best evidence that the repo def's missing pale/grey range is a common look,
  not an edge case.
- `wookieepedia_oola.jpg` — live-action Oola, green. **The clearest look at the cone-shaped
  ear**: large pale trumpet cones protruding sideways, distinctly paler than the skin. Also
  shows lekku emerging from the crown and running down the back.
- `wookieepedia_aayla_secura.jpg` — a CGI Aayla mid-lightsaber-swing. The evidence for
  **mottled/spotted lekku markings** (irregular pale blotches, *not* bands) and for full
  adult lekku length past the waist. Note she is female with **small pointed ears and no
  cones**, which is what breaks the tidy dimorphism claim.
- `wookieepedia_numa_youngling.jpg` — a teal Twi'lek child. Shows that a juvenile already
  has proportionally long lekku, worn down the back, slightly deeper in hue than the face,
  with a soft dark upper edge — and, again, small pointed ears on a female.
- `wookieepedia_hera_syndulla.jpg` — a green Twi'lek in her own right; complements the
  comparison image with a full look at the best-known modern Twi'lek.
- `wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg` — the Legends infobox plate, a blue Rutian female with a
  blaster. **Legends, so weaker evidence for current canon**, but it is the clearest image of
  the fourth carriage style (lekku swept **up over the crown** and curling outward), of lekku
  thickness at the base, and of leather wraps banded around the lekku as *worn ornament*
  rather than pigment.
- `donor_current_sprite.png` — the repo's own `Female_Normal_south.png`. Unusually good as a
  starting point: correct count, correct crown origin, correct taper, canon carriage. See the
  visual brief for the `Striped` variant concern and the unresolved mask/tint question.
