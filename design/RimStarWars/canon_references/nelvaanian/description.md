# Nelvaanian

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeNelvaanian` (`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`).
Assigned `TribeCivil: R` — rare, and only in the tribal faction.

⚠️ **The canon (Disney-era) article is a stub with a completely empty infobox** — no
height, mass, skin colour, eye colour, lifespan or distinctions. Every physical
number and colour below comes from **`Nelvaanian/Legends`**, whose infobox is fully
populated and sourced to *The Clone Wars Campaign Guide*. Where a fact is
Legends-only it is marked. The species' entire screen life is **four episodes of the
2003–05 *Star Wars: Clone Wars* micro-series (Chapters 22–25)**, so the appearance
record is small but consistent.

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Canon article** (`/wiki/Nelvaanian`). Nelvaanians were a sentient species native to
the **frozen** Outer Rim world of **Nelvaan**. They practiced a perilous initiation
ritual in which young males had to obtain a scale from the tail of a **horax** —
giant blue-skinned reptiles with saber-sharp fangs and enormous horns — in order to
join the ranks of their tribes' greatest warriors; the scales were then used to make
an **elixir capable of repelling horaxes for up to a year with its smell**. The
Nelvaanians were **a peaceful, low-tech race**; however, during the Clone Wars the
Separatist-aligned **Techno Union experimented on members of the species, turning
them into cyborg monstrosities**. Sourced to *Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You
Need to Know* (2015) and *Rise of the Separatists* (2019). That is the **whole** canon
article body — there is no biology section at all.

**Legends article** (`/wiki/Nelvaanian/Legends`) — infobox, sourced to *The Clone Wars
Campaign Guide*:

- **Height: 1.5 m (females), 2 m (males), 3 m (males, mutant)** — the 3 m figure is
  the Techno Union cyborg mutant, not a natural Nelvaanian.
- **Mass: 50 kg (females), 130 kg (males).** 🔑 This is a strongly **sexually
  dimorphic** species: the male is a third taller and **2.6× the mass** of the female.
- **Skin colour: blue.** **Hair colour: blue-green with a black headcrest.**
  **Eye colour: black.**
- **Distinctions: long snouts; sharp teeth; wolf-like appearance.**
- Origin Nelvaan; language **Nelvaanese**. **Lifespan: not given** — unsourced, do not
  invent one.

Legends body text: a race of **blue-furred lupine humanoids**. They called their
planet the "Great Mother" and worshipped it. **Males became warriors and scouts;
females were builders, gatherers, and raised the children.** A tribe was governed by
**one shaman** holding spiritual rites and **one chieftain**, elected through
religious tests and mutual admiration. During the Clone Wars, General Grievous lured
warriors from the **Rokrul** village by posing as a prophesied figure from Nelvaan
mythology foretold by the village shaman **Orvos**; Techno Union Skakoan combat
engineers then captured and rebuilt them as cyborgs using "unique microelectronics"
and the **3L41UH7 serum**, producing "a small army of hulking mutants." Anakin
Skywalker freed them at the **Battle of Nelvaan**; the Nelvaanians called him **Holt
Kezed — "Ghost Hand"** in their language, for his mechanical right arm. Grievous was
struck by the similarity between the spiritual Nelvaanian warriors and his own
**Kaleesh** people.

**Behind the scenes** (Legends): the Nelvaan language is partially **Hungarian**
(Genndy Tartakovsky, DVD commentary), and their appearance was inspired by the
**"dog-headed people" from the 1983 Nelvana film *Rock & Rule***.

**Unusual abilities: none sourced.** ⚠️ The claim in the repo def's own
`<description>` — "a keen sense of smell and acute hearing that is purported to be
able to detect a creature's movement from a mile away" — **does not appear in either
the canon or the Legends article wikitext**. Treat it as unsourced until a primary
citation is produced. No Force-sensitivity, psionics or special ability is attested
anywhere in either article.

## Visual brief

All three references are painted RPG-sourcebook illustrations (the species has no
photographic or high-res 3D reference in the wiki), and they agree closely.

🔴 **"Blue-furred" badly misdescribes what the images show. A Nelvaanian is
predominantly BARE BLUE-GREY SKIN with fur only in patches** — the Legends prose
word "blue-furred" is the single most misleading phrase in the record, and a
text-only prompt fed that phrase will produce a wolf in a full pelt, which is wrong.
What the images actually show:

- **Body: smooth slate blue-grey skin over clearly readable human musculature** —
  pectorals, abdominals, deltoids and quadriceps are all defined and visible, not
  buried under a coat. Fur appears only as **short scruff on the forearms, outer
  thighs, calves and jaw-line**, plus the mane.
- **Colour is a cool blue-grey, mid-value, slightly lighter on the chest and belly**;
  the third image's head reads paler, almost lavender-grey. Not saturated blue.
- 🔑 **A long straight BLACK mane** springing from the crown and running down the nape
  onto the shoulders — this is the "black headcrest" of the infobox, and it is the
  species' most recognisable feature after the snout. It hangs loose and long, human
  hair-like, not a ruff.
- **Head: a long, narrow, distinctly canine/equine muzzle** with a **black nose pad**
  at the tip and a wide mouth showing **prominent sharp teeth, the lower canines
  visible even at rest.**
- **Eyes: SMALL, dark, close-set and deeply recessed under a heavy overhanging brow
  ridge.** ⚠️ This contradicts the def's `RSW_Eyes_Big` (below). The infobox's "black"
  eye colour matches the images.
- **Ears: large, upright, pointed, set high and angled outward from the skull** —
  clearly visible past the mane in all three images, with a visible inner ear
  surface. They are a major silhouette element.
- **Hands: five fingers with black claws.** **Feet: broad, plantigrade, five toes,
  black claws** — bare, with only leather strap/sandal binding at the ankle.
- **Build: tall, broad-shouldered, long-armed, forward-leaning hunter's stance.**
- **Body markings: bold BLACK geometric spiral / meander bands** painted or tattooed
  around the biceps, shoulders and sternum, repeated on their painted shields. These
  read as cultural decoration, not pigment, but they appear on **every** individual in
  all three images, so a Nelvaanian without them will not look like one.
- **Dress: minimal — a woven sash and fringed loincloth in tan/ochre/red, leather
  ankle bindings. Weapons: a long wooden spear with a chipped stone head and a large
  painted oval shield.** Consistent with "peaceful, low-tech."

`wookieepedia_hunter_over_droid.jpg` adds the environment: the Nelvaanian stands
bare-chested and barefoot on rock in a **snowfield under grey cloud**, which is the
one image that makes the canon "frozen" habitat concrete — this species goes
essentially naked in snow.

🔴 **`donor_current_sprite.png` is not Nelvaanian art at all.** There is **no
Nelvaanian texture anywhere in `src/`** (only a name-list directory,
`.../RimMandrakeSWNames/SWX/Nelvaanian`). The sprite shown here is
`RimMandrakeSW/OR/Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Heads/Bothan/Male_Bothan_south.png`, which is
what the def's `RSW_BothanHead` gene actually forces the game to render. It is a
**rounded, muzzle-less, ear-less furry face with a tiny nose and no visible teeth** —
it has none of the three features that define a Nelvaanian head (long snout, large
pointed ears, visible canines). Treat it as a **negative reference**: it documents
the bug, not the species.

## Engine limits
As things stand, the xenotype's `RSW_BothanHead` carries `useSkinShader: false` over its greyscale mask (`Defs/HeadTypeDefs/SW_HeadTypes.xml`), so no skin-colour gene reaches the face — a Nelvaanian pawn renders a blue body with a grey-white, untinted face. This is separate from the head shape being wrong (the Bothan head also lacks the snout and ears); even a correctly-tinted head asset would need this shader flag off to show blue skin on the face.

## Def-versus-canon (flagged — not fixed)

- 🔴 **`RSW_BothanHead` is the wrong species' head.** A Nelvaanian's diagnostic
  features are a **long lupine snout** and **large upright pointed ears**; the Bothan
  head sprite has neither. This is the single biggest defect.
- 🔴 **The blue skin will not reach the face.** `RSW_Bothan_MaleNormal` /
  `RSW_Bothan_FemaleNormal` (`Defs/HeadTypeDefs/SW_HeadTypes.xml`) both carry
  `DefModExt_HeadTypeStuff` with **`<useSkinShader>false</useSkinShader>`**, so the
  head art is drawn at its own baked colour and is **not tinted by
  `RSW_Skin_SlateBlue`**. The pawn will have a blue body and a grey-white face.
- ⚠️ **`RSW_Eyes_Big` contradicts every reference image**, which show small, dark,
  deep-set eyes under a heavy brow. There is also **no eye-colour gene** for the
  sourced **black** eyes.
- ⚠️ **`AptitudePoor_Medicine` contradicts the one piece of canon technical skill the
  species has**: Nelvaanian shamans brew the **horax-scale elixir**, a year-long
  chemical repellent. `AptitudePoor_Plants` sits badly beside "builders and
  gatherers" as the female role. `AptitudePoor_Intellectual` and `RSW_GS_Primitive`
  are defensible from "peaceful, low-tech race."
- ⚠️ **`MinTemp_SmallDecrease` understates a species from a world whose canon habitat
  is literally "Frozen"**, and which the reference art shows bare-chested and barefoot
  in a blizzard. Compare `RSW_RimMandrakeOrtolan`, which gets
  `MinTemp_LargeDecrease` with no cold-world sourcing at all.
- ⚠️ **`RSW_BodySizeGene_big` flattens a strongly dimorphic species.** Canon is 2 m /
  130 kg male vs **1.5 m / 50 kg female**; one uniform size gene cannot express that.
  (The 3 m figure belongs to the Techno Union cyborg mutants and should not be used
  for a baseline Nelvaanian.)
- ⚠️ **`RSW_statgene_predator`, `Turn_Gene_Duelist`, `NakedSpeed`** are unsourced as
  species traits — the warrior/scout role is **male-only cultural**, not biological,
  and the species is described as *peaceful*. `Aggression`-adjacent genes are absent,
  which is correct.
- ⚠️ **The def's `<description>` asserts scent and hearing acuity ("a mile away") that
  neither Wookieepedia article contains** — and no gene implements it either, so the
  text promises a sense the pawn does not have.
- ✅ Correct and worth keeping: `RSW_Skin_SlateBlue` (sourced blue), `Hair_InkBlack` +
  `Hair_LongOnly` (the sourced **black headcrest**, long), `Beard_NoBeardOnly`,
  `Outland_Ears_FlatPointed` (pointed ears are sourced and prominent),
  `nameMaker = RSW_KoTOR_NamerNelvaanian` — **this one is the right species' namer**,
  and a matching name-list directory exists on disk.
- Not represented at all: the **black geometric spiral body markings**, present on
  every reference individual.
