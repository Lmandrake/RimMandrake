## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boar-wolf

Boar-wolves, also known as borras, were non-sentient, bristle-backed creatures that lived on Endor, a forested moon that orbited the planet of the same name. Boar-wolves had thick hair, humped backs, and and two tusks.

They were killed for their meat by the Ewoks, a species of diminutive sentients native to Endor. Ewok hunting parties who managed to bring back boar-wolf meat were considered to have been lucky. Some Ewoks also crafted knives from boar-wolf teeth. Although boar-wolves could be dangerous, they were domesticated by the semi-sentient Gorax, who would care for the young after killing their mother.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boar-wolf/Legends

The boar-wolves, or borra, were a predatory animal that lived on the forest moon of Endor.

**Characteristics** Boar-wolves had been known to be three meters tall. They had hair, sharp teeth, a humped back, two tusks and large, hooked claws that could tear holes in trees. They had pungent and stringy meat.

They had a penchant for howling at the gas giant Endor when it appeared in the night sky. With a keen sense of smell, they usually hunted in pairs for prey such as blurrgs and Ewoks, preying anything that came across their path. These truly impressive predators possessed an incredible patience to hide and wait out of sight once they have cornered their prey, having fed on many Ewoks this way.

- **Overall shape:** a massive, heavy quadruped carried low at the front, with a pronounced HUMP over the shoulders
  and back; bristly, shaggy hair along the spine (Legends film creature, and the canon text: "bristle-backed", "humped
  backs"). Legends text gives up to three metres tall.
- **Head:** long, wolf-like snout with a mouth full of large uneven fangs; two TUSKS (both texts). The canon comic
  draws it as a pig-pink hog with a gaping red mouth; the Legends film creature is grey-blue and wolf-headed. The two
  images disagree on colour and on how pig-like the face is; both agree on bulk, hump, fangs and tusks.
- **Colour:** Legends film creature dull blue-grey hide with darker shaggy hair and reddish flank patches; canon comic
  pinkish-brown with dark legs. No single colour is attested twice.
- **Limbs:** thick legs with large hooked claws (Legends text: claws "that could tear holes in trees").
- **Donor sprite:** a dark grey-brown wild boar with tusks and a short dark crest: it reads as an ordinary boar and
  misses the hump, the size and the wolf head. Its juvenile is a striped piglet; canon says nothing of young.
- **Size:** big predator, hunts Ewoks and blurrgs in pairs (Legends text). Contradiction to flag: the donor def
  is sized and labelled like a farm boar, while canon is a three-metre predator.
