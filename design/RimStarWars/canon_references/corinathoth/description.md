# Corinathoth

**defName**: `RSW_Corinathoth` (vendored in this repo's own `src/RimStarWars/SWBestiary` mod)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
Corinathoths are a species native to the planet Maridun. The entire canon
description is one sentence: they were hunted by the Amanin, a sentient
species native to the same planet. No coloration, size, diet, or body-plan
detail is given anywhere in the canon (post-2014) continuity text.

Corinathoths originated in *Star Wars* Legends continuity, first appearing in
issue #16 of the Dark Horse comic series *Star Wars: Empire* (published
2004-01-28). They were pulled into the new canon only in 2022, via a single
mention in the reference booklet *"Mustafar and Other Planets in the Outer
Rim"* (part of the *Star Wars Encyclopedia* booklet series) — that mention is
purely textual (native to Maridun, hunted by the Amanin) and carries no new
canon artwork of its own.

## Visual brief
**Important caveat: there is no canon-continuity artwork of a Corinathoth.**
The only image found — and the only one Wookieepedia itself illustrates the
species with — is explicitly captioned "A Star Wars Legends depiction of a
group of Corinathoths," sourced from the 2004 *Empire* comic. Wookieepedia's
own "Behind the scenes" section makes clear this is Legends-only art bolted
onto a name that was later reused in canon with no accompanying visual.
Treat this image as the best available reference, not as confirmed canon
appearance.

That Legends image shows a herd of stocky, quadrupedal, ceratopsian-like
dinosaurs (triceratops-style bony head frills and multiple horns), with
warm orange/tan hides mottled with darker rust-brown patches, including
visibly smaller young in the herd — a large plant-grazer / prey-animal
reading consistent with "hunted by the Amanin."

**The current donor sprite (`donor_current_sprite.png`) is in the same
general family as the Legends art**: a tan/brown mottled quadruped with two
prominent horns/tusks on the head. It is far simpler (no bony frill, no
herd/scale cues, a lower and more compact body) but the horn motif and warm
tan-with-brown-patch coloring are consistent with the only reference image
available. Given the total absence of canon-text detail, this is a
reasonable design to keep or lightly extend (bigger frill, more visible
mottling) rather than a confirmed mismatch to fix.
