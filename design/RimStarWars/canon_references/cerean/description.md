## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

**Canon.** The Cereans were a species of sentient **mammals** hailing from the planet
**Cerea**, *"distinguished by their enlarged conical cranium that contained a binary brain."*
Canon infobox: class **mammal**; **skin colour light tones**; **hair colour blond, brown, or
white** *(usually with age)*; 🔑 **eye colour yellow**; distinctions **cone-like skull, binary
brain**; habitat **garden**; origin Cerea. 🔴 **Height, mass and lifespan are all unsourced in
canon** — those infobox fields are empty.

Biology and appearance, canon body text: **humanoid mammals distinguished by their massive
coned craniums**, keeping **complex binary brains within their towering skulls**, which
*"allowed the garden species to see both sides of any issue."* 🔑 **"These organs relied on
blood pumped from their two hearts."** *"Cereans could grow hair and some sported eyebrows,
white beards, dark stubble, and even topknots and braids."*

Named individuals and culture: **Ki-Adi-Mundi**, the Cerean male who sat on the **Jedi High
Council** during the waning years of the Republic — he served as a Jedi **for over a century**
(part of a council investigating the assassin Mae-ho Aniseya in 132 BBY, then on the High
Council a century later during the Invasion of Naboo), fought through the Clone Wars, and was
killed by the 21st Nova Corps at Order 66. **O-Mer**, a Cerean Padawan hunted for sport by
Trandoshans on Wasskah. **Soo-Tath**, a debt collector during the Galactic Civil War, killed
by the droid 0-0-0's neurotoxin. **Kidi Aleri**, a female communications specialist on Leia
Organa's Operation Yellow Moon team. Cerea itself *"remained largely unspoiled by
technology."*

Two in-universe lines worth having, both about the head: the Yarkora Varralis to Ki-Adi-Mundi
— *"Such a profile you cut. That **rugged frame**. That beard. That **head**!"* — and a First
Order stormtrooper: *"Ki-Adi-Mundi? Was that the one with the **pointy head**?"* The head is
the species, in-universe as well as visually.

**Legends (`Cerean/Legends`), where the numbers and the trade-offs live.**
- 🔑 **Height 2 meters** — a **tall** species. Mass unsourced. Class **mammalian**. Skin
  **tan**. Hair **red, blonde, brown, or black**. Origin Cerea, in the **Mid Rim**.
- **Lifespan**: child 0–10, young adult 11–14, adult 15–49, middle age 50–60, old 61–69,
  **venerable 70+** — i.e. **maturing slightly faster than a human and living somewhat
  shorter.** Not a long-lived species, despite Ki-Adi-Mundi's century of service.
- **The cranium extends up to TWENTY CENTIMETRES above the forehead**, housing complex binary
  brains supplied *"with sufficient blood by an extra heart in their heads."* (Note the
  canon text says two hearts; Legends specifies one of them is **in the head**.)
- **The binary structure let them ponder two sides of an issue at once**, process information
  and **solve problems rapidly**, and gave a **highly advanced capacity for concentration and
  meditation.** Because of this thoughtful nature they tended to be **calm, rational and
  analytical**, preferring peaceful philosophies and a life in harmony with nature.
- 🔴 **The costs, which are as well sourced as the benefits.** *"Though the quick-thinking
  Cereans had equally quick reflexes, they were commonly **not as well coordinated as
  Humans**."* And: *"Because of the weight of their enlarged skulls, Cereans often had **back
  problems** and were **less physically coordinated than the average Human**."*
- 🔑 **Strongly sexually dimorphic.** **Male Cereans aged more rapidly than females**, and
  males were outnumbered by females at a **birth-rate ratio of twenty females to every one
  male.** Because of the species' **low birth rates, Cerean Jedi were exempt from the Jedi
  Order's vow of non-attachment** — a ready-made campaign hook.
- ⚠️ **Force-sensitivity among Cereans was "similar to that of baseline Humans."** They
  trained as both Jedi and Sith. It was *suggested* that their binary brains let some Cerean
  Force-users explore **both the light and dark aspects** of the Force at once — which is a
  cognitive consequence, **not elevated Force power.** This matters for the repo def.

## Visual brief

**All four references agree on the cranium and on almost nothing being alien below it.** The
head is the entire species read; the body is essentially human.

**The cranium, consistent across every image:**
- **A tall, narrow, bald, skin-covered CONE rising vertically from the top of the skull**,
  tapering to a **blunt rounded apex** — not a point, and not a horn. In the references it
  rises **roughly as tall again as the face beneath it**, which is far more dramatic than
  "enlarged forehead" suggests. Legends' "up to twenty centimetres above the forehead" is a
  good sanity check on the proportion.
- 🔑 **It is laterally compressed — a tall wedge, not a circular cone.** Viewed front-on it
  is a narrow tapering blade; the skull visibly narrows inward from the temples before rising.
- **Soft horizontal creases and wrinkles run across it**, and it is **the same flesh colour
  as the face** — pale tan/pinkish. It is skin and bone, not chitin, not hair-covered.
- 🔴 **The cone is completely hairless in every reference.** Where a Cerean has hair, it grows
  from **the sides and back of the lower skull, below the cone**, and hangs down.
- **Below the cone the face is essentially HUMAN** — human eyes, human nose, human mouth,
  human ears, normal proportions. There is no other alien feature. **This is the trap for a
  text-only prompt**: "sentient alien mammal with a binary brain" invites invented alien
  facial features, and there are none. Get the cone right and everything else is a human face.

**`wookieepedia_infobox_mundi_bodyshot.jpg` — the canon reference of record** (Ki-Adi-Mundi,
full-body live-action reference shot on a transparent background):
- **Pale tan/flesh skin**, matching canon "light tones" and Legends "tan".
- **Bald wrinkled cone**, tallest and narrowest of the set, with the lateral compression very
  clear and deep transverse creases.
- **Long grey-white beard and moustache**, sunken eyes, heavy brow, aged face — this is the
  *old male* data point, and it confirms the canon "white beards … usually with age."
- **Body is human, upright, unremarkable, and reads TALL** (agreeing with the Legends 2 m).
  Slight forward carriage of the head. Brown-and-tan Jedi robes, wide dark belt, lightsaber,
  tan suede boots.
- No visible tail, claws, fur, or non-human limb structure of any kind.

**`wookieepedia_omer_youth.jpg` — the youth data point, and the eye-colour proof.** O-Mer, a
Cerean Padawan, CGI render from *The Clone Wars*:
- 🔑 **Eyes are unmistakably YELLOW-GOLD**, bright against the face — directly corroborating
  the canon "eye colour yellow" cite (from "Padawan Lost"). Since the def sets no eye gene at
  all, this is a concrete thing to add.
- **Completely hairless** — bald cone, bald lower skull, no beard. So Cereans are not
  obligately haired.
- **The body is very thin and lanky with long limbs**, gaunt in the face. A juvenile Cerean is
  a beanpole, not a scaled-down Ki-Adi-Mundi.
- Skin is a warmer tan-pink with visible dirt and wear. Sleeveless top, worn trousers, wrapped
  boots, coloured cords at the throat and shins.

**`wookieepedia_negas_legends.jpg` — the best hair-arrangement reference** (a Legends *New
Essential Guide to Alien Species* painting of a female Cerean with a blaster):
- 🔑 **Long, straight, mid-brown hair growing from the SIDES and BACK of the head, below the
  bald cone**, falling well past the shoulders and blowing back. **This is how a haired Cerean
  works: bald cone above, long hair below.** A text-only prompt will put hair on the cone,
  and that is wrong.
- **The cone here is slightly shorter and broader than Ki-Adi-Mundi's**, and the individual is
  clearly female — consistent with a sexually dimorphic species, though the sources do not
  describe a cranial sex difference, so treat this as artist variation rather than a rule.
- **Amber/yellow eyes**, agreeing with O-Mer and the canon cite.
- **Body is slim and athletic, human-proportioned, and long-limbed.** Sleeveless leather
  jerkin, bracer of cartridges, belt pouches, thigh holster, tall black boots — a scoundrel,
  not a Jedi, which is a useful non-Jedi Cerean.
- Legends, so treat the palette as one book's take; the hair arrangement is the finding.

**`wookieepedia_kiadimundi_2025.jpg`** — a recent (2025) Ki-Adi-Mundi illustration, held as a
fourth corroboration of the cone, beard and pale tan skin.

**`wookieepedia_female_force_adept_legends.jpg`** — a Legends female Cerean Force Adept.
Fourth/fifth corroboration; also a second female reference.

**`donor_current_sprite.png` — the cone is present but badly undersold.** It is
`OR/Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Heads/Cerean/Male_Normal_south.png`, the head from
`RSW_CereanHead`. As a RimWorld humanlike head it is a **greyscale mask the game tints at
runtime**, so the absence of skin colour is correct and expected and the colour findings above
belong on the *skin and eye genes*, not on this file. What it gets right: **a distinct
narrower cone rising from the top of the skull** — the correct feature, correctly placed, and
better than a mere tall forehead. Three problems:
- 🔴 **The cone is far too short and the head far too wide.** The sprite is a broad egg with a
  modest bump on top — perhaps a quarter of the head's height. Every reference shows the cone
  rising **as tall again as the whole face**, on a skull that **narrows inward** below it. As
  drawn, the silhouette reads as a slightly conical human head rather than the species'
  defining tower, and "was that the one with the pointy head?" would not land.
- ⚠️ **No lateral compression and no transverse creases** — the references' tall flattened
  wedge with horizontal wrinkling is the texture that makes it read as skin over an enlarged
  braincase.
- ⚠️ **Eyes are plain dark dots**, and the def sets no eye-colour gene, so the **canonical
  yellow eyes** — corroborated by two independent references — will not appear.
  There are also small tapering tufts at the temples whose purpose is unclear and which match
  nothing in the references.

## Repo def versus canon — contradictions to report

🔴 **`PsychicAbility_Enhanced` inverts the sourcing.** Legends states plainly that
**"Force-sensitivity among Cereans was similar to that of baseline Humans."** The Cereans'
fame is **intellect** — a binary brain that ponders both sides of an issue and solves problems
rapidly — not innate Force power. The frequency of Cerean Jedi is **vocational and cultural**
(and the Order granted them an attachment exemption because of low birth rates), not genetic
aptitude. The one Force-adjacent claim in the sources is *suggested*, cognitive, and
different in kind: that a binary brain might let a Cerean explore **light and dark aspects
simultaneously**. Shipping an enhanced-psychic gene makes every Cerean colonist a natural
psycaster on no evidence, and it does so instead of the aptitude that *is* sourced.

🔴 **The sourced physical DRAWBACKS are entirely missing, and they are unusually specific.**
Legends gives Cereans, twice over: **back problems from the weight of the enlarged skull**,
and being **less physically coordinated than the average Human** (*"commonly not as well
coordinated as Humans"*). RimWorld expresses both easily — a manipulation or movement
modifier, or a spine-related condition. The def carries **no penalty of any kind**, so the
species is shipped as pure upside. For a library meant to make races read correctly, a
big-headed thinker who is *clumsy and has a bad back* is far more characterful than one who
is not.

🔴 **The two hearts are unrepresented** — canon states the binary brain *"relied on blood
pumped from their two hearts,"* and Legends specifies **an extra heart inside the head.**
This is one of only two anatomical facts canon gives about the species and nothing in the def
touches it.

- 🔴 **No eye gene, on a species whose canon infobox names a specific eye colour — yellow —
  corroborated by two independent references** (O-Mer's CGI render and the NEGAS painting).
  Likewise **no skin gene** (canon "light tones", Legends "tan") and **no hair gene** (canon
  blond/brown/white; Legends red/blonde/brown/black). All three are left to inherit whatever
  the parent stock supplies, so the species has no colour identity at all in game.
- ⚠️ **`Body_Thin` is only half-right and cuts against a canon description.** O-Mer and the
  NEGAS female are genuinely slim, so thin is defensible for those — but the one in-universe
  description of a Cerean body is Varralis admiring Ki-Adi-Mundi's **"rugged frame,"** and the
  full-body reference is unremarkably built rather than gaunt. Nothing in either article makes
  slenderness a species trait.
- ⚠️ **The sexual dimorphism is missing, and it is dramatic**: **twenty females born per male**,
  with **males aging faster**. RimWorld can express a gender ratio, and a species the player
  meets as "some" would visibly skew female if it were implemented.
- ✅ Defensible: `RSW_BodySizeGene_big` (**well sourced** — Legends puts Cereans at 2 metres),
  `RSW_CereanHead` (the correct defining feature, even if the sprite undersells it),
  `AptitudeStrong_Intellectual` (**the best-sourced gene in the def** — binary brains, rapid
  problem-solving, seeing both sides of any issue), and `Turn_Gene_MeditationNeed` +
  `Turn_Gene_SlowNeuralHeat`, which map neatly onto the sourced *"highly advanced capacity for
  concentration and meditation"* and the calm, rational, analytical temperament.
- ⚠️ Missing lore hooks worth noting for the campaign rather than the art: **Cerea was
  largely unspoiled by technology** (a low-tech, nature-harmonious homeworld), the **garden**
  habitat cite, and the **attachment exemption** granted to Cerean Jedi because of low birth
  rates.
