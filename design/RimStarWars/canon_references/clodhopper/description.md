# Clodhopper

**defName**: `RSW_Clodhopper` (vendored in SWBestiary, texture folder `Clodhopper`)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
Legends-continuity species (page tagged `{{Top|leg}}`). Omnivorous, dim-witted,
**flightless avians** native to Naboo, living in the Gungan Swamp. Skin
color: **brown and red**. Eye color: **blue**. Rarely more than three feet
tall, but they feed in swarms and become a real menace — a notorious pest
species that can spawn 200 young in only two days, all of which spend most of
their time eating. Protected by tough, durable skin; use powerful forelegs to
forage and hop. A favorite food is the Tooke-trap plant. Natural predators on
Naboo include narglatches (any age) and Gungans, who hunt clodhoppers both
for meat and for their thick skin (used to make musical instruments). Export
off-world is strictly forbidden by law after a colony at Corva was wiped out
by clodhoppers accidentally shipped with Naboo grain — an ecological-disaster
cautionary tale. (The behind-the-scenes note flags that the *Secrets of
Naboo* sourcebook itself describes them as "insects" despite the infobox
class listing them as avians — a minor internal canon inconsistency, not a
render decision to worry about.)

## Visual brief
The one candidate image (a *Wildlife of Star Wars* field-guide-style
illustration) matches the sourced text closely and is a clear, information-
dense reference: **adult clodhoppers have vivid red/crimson heads and necks**
with a **pale grey-blue patch of skin around the eye** (this is very likely
what the text's "blue" eyecolor field is describing — the eye itself reads
dark within a blue-toned surrounding patch, not a glowing blue iris), a
**pale grey beak**, and **brown, ridged, feathered-looking bodies** with
long necks and sturdy clawed forelimbs used for foraging — shown here
foraging on a spiny red-and-cream leafed plant (consistent with the
Tooke-trap-plant diet note). Juveniles in the same image are smaller, more
uniformly brown/olive, and lack the vivid red head coloring of adults —
useful if a juvenile/adult variant is ever wanted. The overall silhouette
reads as a stocky flightless ground bird (ostrich/cassowary-adjacent), which
matches the "flightless avian" classification even though the field guide's
own sourcebook oddly also calls them insects in prose.

**The current donor sprite** (`donor_current_sprite.png`) is a solid partial
match: reddish-brown body coloring is right, and there's a pale cream tuft/
plumage cluster near the head/shoulder consistent with the reference art's
head plumage. Two differences worth flagging: the donor's **eye reads plain
dark/black**, not the blue-toned eye patch the reference art and the sourced
"blue" eyecolor both call for, and the donor's head is not as strongly
saturated red as the adult reference — closer to the reference's more muted
juveniles. A regen should push the head toward a more vivid red and add the
blue-toned eye patch.
