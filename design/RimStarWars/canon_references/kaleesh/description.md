# Kaleesh

**defName**: `RSW_RimMandrakeKaleesh` (`src/RimStarWars/StarWarsRaces/Defs/XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml`,
verified present). Matrix placement: `TribeCivil: R` — rare, and in the *tribal* faction, which

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
The Kaleesh, also **Kaleeshi**, were a **reptilian** sentient species from the Wild Space world
of **Kalee**. The canon infobox gives **no height, no mass and no lifespan** — unsourced in
canon, recorded as absent. Skin colour: **red**. Eye colour: **yellow**. Hair colour: **blank
in the canon infobox.** Distinctions: **snake-like eyes, flat nose, elongated ears, large
tusks, four-fingered hands, five-toed feet, digitigrade stance.**

Biology, at length: **usually red-skinned humanoid reptilians.** They possessed **distinctive,
snake-like, beady yellow eyes with slit pupils set beneath a pronounced brow ridge.** Their
**nostrils appeared as two long slits that extended toward the eyes**, while their **mouth
featured fangs, which, when closed, are visible**, and their **lower jaws featured large
tusks, accompanied by bony protrusions extending from the chin.** 🔑 **Although all of their
facial features were reminiscent of bats** — the **elongated, pointed ears** and **flat
noses** — **they usually wore masks.** They had **long, thin arms** ending in **four-fingered,
claw-like hands, two of which are opposable thumbs**, and **four-jointed legs, back bent,
with five-toed scute feet: two in front, two on each side, and one in the back.** They were
**digitigrades, walking on their toes rather than their entire feet.** Claws ranged from
**white to black.** They possessed a brain, a heart, lungs, a liver and nerve endings, and
🔑 **their internal organs were extremely flammable.** As reptiles they could **recognise
cold-bloodedness in others** and **perceive colour differently.** **Their scales quivered
menacingly**, almost as if warning of an impending attack.

Society and the masks: **though primitive**, a fierce, renowned warrior culture **known for
their lack of mercy**, not likely to display empathy, kindness or vulnerability, and spiritual
— they worshipped a pantheon including the trickster god **Ithano**. They hunted the native
beasts **mumuu, erkush and karabbac**, generally **killing to demonstrate bravery and to make
masks and clothing** rather than for meat alone; clothing from **animal hides**, and the
**traditional masks carved from the skulls, bones and teeth of the deadliest predatory
beasts.** 🔑 **A Kaleesh's features were normally and traditionally hidden behind the mask,
with only their slitted golden eyes and tusks visible.** **Before battle, Kaleesh smeared
karabbac-blood war paint above their eyes on their skull masks.** Rarely seen without the
masks, which **also made it difficult to read their expressions**; and **if it were discovered
that someone wore a similar mask just for effect, the Kaleesh would not tolerate it.** They
dressed in traditional, **light-coloured, distinctive campaign cloaks with mumuu markings and
partial hoods**, and used **swords, shields, spears, slugthrowers** and the **Czerka Arms
Outland rifle.** They practised **Kaleesh martial arts** (the unarmed style **Na Jang** was
taken from them and taught across the galaxy) and **favoured roggwarts as pets.**

⚠️ **Grievous is the only well-documented individual, and canon explicitly warns off him**:
*"His mostly cybernetic body eliminated most resemblance to other Kaleesh."* His **pre-cyborg**
face is legitimate reference; his faceplate is not.

Canon has no size figures, so **`Kaleesh/Legends` was pulled**: height **1.6–1.8 metres**,
mass **80 kilograms (males)**, lifespan **up to 80 standard years**, skin red, eyes yellow,
hair **black and brown**, distinctions **four digits, tusks**. Legends adds **heavily arched
brows** and **large, triangular ear flaps**, with **pointed tusks protruding outward from
their jawbones, framing their faces on either side**, and **bony protrusions jutting from
their chins.**

## Visual brief
**Owner ruling 2026-10-08 (verbatim): "That's realistic, not these cartoon versions you keep using. Replace the canon with something more realistic. The web is full of them."**
Removed: the flat-coloured Alien Archive infobox illustration (`wookieepedia_infobox_masked.jpg`), the animated
*Path of Fear* eye close-up (`wookieepedia_eyes_closeup.jpg`) and the Grievous concept-sketch sheet
(`wookieepedia_grievous_concept_alien_stage.jpg`). Added: `wookieepedia_legends_negas_painted.jpg` (realistic
painted full figure, *The New Essential Guide to Alien Species*) for mask, skin and dress, and
`wookieepedia_eyes_rots_liveaction.png` (live-action *Revenge of the Sith* close-up of Grievous's eyes).
⚠️ **No realistic UNMASKED Kaleesh exists anywhere** (searched: Kaleesh and Kaleesh/Legends page images, "Images of
Kaleesh" — SWTOR, comics and RPG art only). The comic face `wookieepedia_unmasked_face.jpg` is **kept as the only
anatomy reference for the face** — render that anatomy realistically, not in its inked comic style.

🔴 **Where the realistic painting disagrees with the deleted illustration:** the removed infobox showed a
rust-red cloth-wrapped figure with **no skin visible**. The realistic painting shows **bare rust-red skin on the
arms, hands, legs and clawed bare feet**, and a **light sand/cream campaign cloak and wrappings** — matching the
sourced *"light-coloured campaign cloaks"*. Rust-red cloth is therefore a variant, not the norm.

🔑 **The mask/face split, and it is the whole entry.** Canon says the mask is worn *nearly
always* — but unlike the Kel Dor's, it is **cultural, not medical**. A Kaleesh can be unmasked
without dying; a mask worn "just for effect" by an outsider is an offence. So both states are
legitimate to draw, and the *anatomy* below must exist under the mask regardless.

**The face (anatomy), from `wookieepedia_unmasked_face.jpg`** — Grievous before his cybernetics:
- **Deep brick-red / rust-red skin**, matte, crossed by **darker striations and scar-like
  channels** over the brow, temples and cheeks. This is the sourced "red" — it is a dark,
  earthy brick red, not a bright scarlet.
- **Two large, wide-set yellow eyes with vertical slit pupils**, ringed by dark orbital rims,
  under **a heavy arched brow ridge.** The eyes are the brightest thing in the face.
- **No projecting nose.** Between and below the eyes sits **a broad, flat, faceted plate**
  reading almost hexagonal — the "flat or snub nose," with the two nostril slits running from
  it up toward the eyes.
- **A long, forward-tapering lower muzzle**, giving the head an elongated wedge in profile.
- **A narrow mouth showing small teeth**, and 🔑 **two long, pale ivory tusks curving up and
  outward from the lower jaw**, framing the mouth. **The tusks are FACE.** Together with the
  eyes they are the only things the mask leaves visible, which is precisely why prose readers
  mistake them for part of the mask.
- Ears are not visible in this image (hooded). Take the ears from the sourced text and Legends:
  **elongated, pointed, large and triangular — bat-like flaps.**

**The mask (equipment), from `wookieepedia_legends_negas_painted.jpg`:**
- 🔴 **The mask is BONE-WHITE / cream, not red** — an animal skull with **two dark angular eye
  sockets**, a projecting jaw structure and **pale fangs hanging from its lower edge**; the painted
  mask also carries **pale backswept horn spikes at the crown** and frayed fibre ruffs at the sides.
- **Bare skin shows at the limbs**: **deep rust-red, matte**, on the arms, hands and legs; feet are
  **bare, broad and clawed** (three forward claws).
- **Dress:** a long **sand/cream campaign cloak**, cloth wrappings binding the forearms, shins and
  torso, a wrapped tabard-skirt, a bandolier of pouches across the chest, and a **long spear**.
  The Legends / SWTOR images also show rust-red cloth and knit caps with trailing cords — variants.

**Build.** The painted figure reads **tall-ish, lean and long-limbed**, upright, with the
**long thin arms** the text describes. It does **not** read as heavy or oversized — consistent
with Legends' 1.6–1.8 m / 80 kg.

### Def-versus-canon (flagged — not fixed here)
- 🔴 **`nameMaker` is `RSW_KoTOR_NamerNagai`** — the **wrong species' namer**, and Nagai is a
  separate species with its own def (`RSW_RimMandrakeNagai`) elsewhere in the same file. Kaleesh
  names are documented and distinctive (Qymaen jai Sheelal, Dumas Mar'Ti, Izvoshra). Same class
  of defect the previous batch found on `RSW_RimMandrakeIthorian`.
- 🔴 **Six hair genes on a scaled reptilian: `Hair_LongOnly`, `Hair_DarkReddish`, `Hair_MidBlack`,
  `Hair_DarkBlack`, `Hair_InkBlack`, plus `Outland_BaldMale`.** Canon's hair-colour field is
  **blank**, the unmasked face image shows a **bare, striated, hairless head**, and the only
  hair-like elements anywhere are costume cords. Because `Outland_BaldMale` bald-caps only
  males, the shipped result is **long-haired female Kaleesh** — invented. (Legends does source
  hair as *black and brown*; `Hair_DarkReddish` is sourced nowhere, and *brown* is the one
  Legends colour the def omits.)
- 🔴 **`RSW_BodySizeGene_big` is contradicted by the only sourced numbers**: Legends gives
  **1.6–1.8 m and 80 kg for males** — human-normal to slightly short. Canon gives no height at
  all. Nothing supports a big body size.
- 🔴 **Nothing represents the flammable internal organs**, which is the species' one sourced
  hard mechanical vulnerability (and the reason Grievous burns in *Revenge of the Sith*). It is
  exactly the sort of trait a RimWorld gene can express, and it is absent.
- ⚠️ **No Kaleesh head type exists.** There is **no `HeadType/kaleesh` directory on disk**
  (compare `HeadType/trandoshan`, `HeadType/taung`). The face is assembled from modular
  attachments over a standard head. The modular choices are mostly *good* — see below — but the
  **elongated wedge muzzle, the faceted flat nose plate and the striated skin texture** are not
  authored anywhere.
- ⚠️ **`Outland_Ears_Akhkharu`** is a borrowed ear gene from another mod's vocabulary. Canon
  needs **large, elongated, pointed, triangular bat-like ear flaps.** Whether Akhkharu ears read
  that way should be settled by **looking**, not by the name.
- ⚠️ Not represented, though sourced: **four-fingered hands with two opposable thumbs**;
  **digitigrade stance**; **five-toed scute feet (two front, two per side, one rear)**;
  **four-jointed legs and bent back**; **fangs visible when the mouth is closed**.
- ⚠️ Unsourced: `MinTemp_SmallIncrease` + `MaxTemp_SmallIncrease`, `Outland_Evasive`.
- ✅ **Correct, and worth recording as correct**: `Skin_DeepRed` (sourced red, and the right
  *dark brick* end of it); `Outland_Eye_Yellow` (sourced, though **slit pupils are not
  represented**); `Outland_Nose_NoseSlits` (sourced — two long slits); `Outland_ChinHorns`
  (sourced — bony protrusions from the chin); `Outland_JawTusks` (sourced — lower-jaw tusks);
  `Outland_EyeVisual_Recessed` (matches "beady… beneath a pronounced brow ridge");
  `Outland_Scalebody` and `RSW_butchergene_lizardskin` (reptilian, scaly); `Body_Thin` (long
  thin arms); `BS_Diet_Carnivore` (they hunt and eat meat); `Aggression_Aggressive`,
  `RSW_statgene_predator`, `MeleeDamage_Strong`-adjacent warrior traits (sourced: formidable
  warriors known for lack of mercy). `RSW_HeadAttachment_kaleeshmask` is the right *shape* of
  solution — the mask should be a visible layer, not baked into a head.
