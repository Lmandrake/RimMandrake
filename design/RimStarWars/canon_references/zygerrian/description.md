## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

⚠️ **The canon article has NO "Biology and appearance" section** — it opens straight
into *History*. **All the biology lives on `/Legends`**, and the repo's own
`<description>` is a near-verbatim lift from that Legends section.

**Canon.** The Zygerrians were a **humanoid feline species** from **Zygerria**. 🔑
*"Having **evolved from a horned sentient canine species**, they built a powerful
empire by capturing and selling **slaves** until the Jedi of the Galactic Republic went
to war with them and outlawed it."* They openly revived the slave trade when they
allied with the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars.

Canon infobox: class **feline**; 🔑 **skin colour "Light" — a SINGLE entry**; **hair
colour blue, brown, gray, pink, red**; **eye colour blue, gold, green, purple,
silver**; distinctions **humanoid**; origin **Zygerria**; **habitat rocky**; languages
Galactic Basic Standard and Zygerrian. **Height, mass and lifespan are blank in the
canon infobox.**

**Legends** — where the substance is. Infobox: class **humanoid / feline**;
**height 1.7 meters**; **mass 60 kilograms**; **skin colour Light**; hair **black,
brown, gray, red**; eyes **blue, yellow**; **lifespan 81 years**; distinctions
**pointed ears**, **clawed hands**, **faces covered in fur (some individuals)**,
**bony spurs on chins of males**.

Biology and appearance: *"The Zygerrians were a bipedal sentient humanoid species.
**Feline in appearance**, the species possessed **strong, angular features and large,
pointed ears**. Other distinctive features included **long fangs that jutted from their
jaws** and **claws that extended from their hands**. Some Zygerrians had **faces and
ears that were entirely covered with fur** — ranging in colour from **brown to gray,
red, or black**. Other individuals displayed a certain amount of **bare skin on their
faces, which was light in tone**. The Zygerrians' eye colour varied between **yellow
and blue**."*

🔑 **Sexual dimorphism, and it is explicit:** *"**Males traditionally had more hair
than females, with bands of fur growing on their cheeks.** Male Zygerrians also
displayed a number of **bony spurs protruding from the chin, which females lacked**."*

**Life cycle and build** — the one place real numbers exist: *"With an average lifespan
of **eighty-one years**, the Zygerrians **reached adolescence at ten years; they were
considered adult at seventeen, middle-aged at forty, and old at sixty-one**.
Medium-sized, they usually grew up to **1.7 meters** and weighed around **sixty
kilograms** on average. They generally had **sallow complexions** and were **physically
strong**, but **some individuals suffered from obesity**."* They spoke Galactic Basic
**with a pronounced accent**.

Culture: Queen Miraj Scintel, verbatim — *"Slavery is the natural order of things. The
weak deserve nothing more than to kneel before the strong, bound to our service."*
They **favoured cruel methods of control, like electro-whips**.

## Visual brief

🔴 **The def gives Zygerrians RED skin. Canon gives exactly one skin colour — "Light" —
and not one of the four references shows red skin. This is the loudest error in this
batch.**

**`wookieepedia_zygerrian_atai_molec.jpg` is the single most informative reference**
(Prime Minister Atai Molec, captioned on the wiki as *"displaying his species'
traditional features"* — *Clone Wars* CGI, bust):

- 🔑 **The EARS are enormous** — broad, tall, upright, cupped forward, with **visible
  internal cartilage ridging**, rising well above the crown and roughly as tall as the
  face itself. They dominate the silhouette. **These are not small triangular cat
  ears.**
- 🔑 **Each ear carries a dark grey-black HORN on its leading edge** — a short curved
  spur. This is the visual residue of *"evolved from a horned sentient canine
  species"* and it is the most distinctive thing about the head. **Nothing in the def
  represents it.**
- 🔑 **THREE bony grey SPURS project downward from the chin**, like a short stiff
  beard of tusks. This is the canonical male feature.
- **Skin is a pale pink-mauve flesh tone and largely hairless on the face** — the
  "bare skin on their faces, which was light in tone" case. **Nothing red.**
- **Pale blue eyes**, deep-set under a heavy, permanently scowling brow ridge.
  Flat feline nose, thin down-turned mouth.

**`wookieepedia_zygerrians_swe.png`** (the canon infobox image: three Zygerrians, full
body) confirms the constants and shows the variation:

- **All three have the huge upright pointed ears**, swept up and out, with darker
  interiors.
- **Skin/fur runs pale tan through grey-brown** — again, **no red**.
- **The male on the right has clear downward chin spurs and cheek fur bands**; the
  **female in the centre has smooth cheeks and no chin spurs**, exactly as the
  dimorphism text says. **This is the reference that proves the male/female
  difference is real and visible.**
- **Bodies are lean and wiry with ordinary humanoid proportions** — consistent with
  1.7 m / 60 kg. Hands are long-fingered.
- One carries a large curved blade; all wear layered leather-and-cloth rig with tall
  boots.

**`wookieepedia_dartsdnar_tcwcejtb.png`** (Darts D'Nar) is the **furred** end of the
range: **warm brown fur over the face and skull with a darker brown mane**, dark ear
tips, **visible long fangs**, prominent chin spurs, **amber/orange eyes**, and a
notably heavier, more muscular build. He carries the **electro-whip** the text names.
Together with Atai Molec this brackets the species: **fully furred brown at one end,
bare pale pink at the other, ears-plus-brow-plus-chin-spurs constant across both.**

**`wookieepedia_zygerriansareback_tbb.png`** is a wide *Bad Batch* environment shot —
several Zygerrian slavers with lit electro-whips on a **rocky, dusty landscape**
(matching the canon `rocky` habitat). **Low appearance detail**; its value is the
**ear silhouette read at distance** (still unmistakable) and the electro-whips in use.
Do not draw face detail from it.

**`donor_current_sprite.png` is the weakest donor art in this batch.** It is the
`RSW_Ears_BigCat` attachment and it is a **tiny 2.4 KB asset** consisting of two small
black-outlined triangular cat ears. Against the references:
- 🔴 **Far too small.** Canonical Zygerrian ears are as tall as the face and dominate
  the head; these are modest triangles perched on top.
- 🔴 **No horns** — the ear-horn is the species' signature feature and is absent.
- 🔴 **No internal ear ridging, no cupped shape** — flat triangles, where the
  references show a broad, ridged, forward-cupped shell.
- Note this is an *ears* attachment only, so it is not expected to carry the face; the
  face comes from the borrowed `RSW_CatharHead`. But **nothing in the def carries the
  fangs, the claws, or the ear-horns**, and the ears themselves are the one part that
  is specified — and they are wrong.

**Xenotype-versus-canon findings:**

- 🔴 **`Skin_DeepRed` and `Outland_Skin_Red` contradict canon skin colour.** Canon and
  Legends both list skin as **"Light"**, one entry, and Legends adds **"sallow
  complexions"**. **Red** appears in canon as a **hair/FUR colour**, not skin — so a
  red *fur* option is defensible, but red as the species' primary skin is not, and no
  reference shows it. `Outland_Skin_Brown`, `Outland_Skin_PaleBrown`,
  `RSW_Skin_MidGray`, `Skin_SlateGray` and `Skin_LightGray` do map correctly onto the
  canonical brown/gray/light range — the two reds are the outliers.
- 🔴 **The ear-horns are absent**, and they are the visible trace of the single most
  interesting canon fact about this species (*evolved from a horned sentient canine
  species*). Highest-value addition.
- 🔴 **`RSW_Ears_BigCat` is far too small and the wrong shape** — see above.
- 🔴 **Fangs and claws are in the def's OWN description and in no gene.** *"long fangs
  that jutted from their jaws and claws that extended from their hands"* is text the
  def already ships; nothing implements it.
- ⚠️ **`Hair_BaldOnly` + `Beard_NoBeardOnly` fight the canon fur.** Canon lists **five
  hair colours** (blue, brown, gray, pink, red) and Legends states **males grow bands
  of fur on their cheeks**; Darts D'Nar is fully furred with a mane. Forcing every
  Zygerrian bald and beardless loses the furred half of the species' range. (The chin
  spurs are correctly handled separately by `RSW_Beard_chinspines` — see below.)
- ⚠️ **`Outland_AcceleratedMaturation` and `Outland_AcceleratedPregnancy` are weakly
  supported at best.** The Legends numbers are **essentially human**: adolescence at
  10, adult at 17, middle-aged at 40, old at 61, **lifespan 81 years**. That is not an
  accelerated life cycle, and it also means **no longevity gene is warranted** either
  way. Nothing sourced about gestation.
- ⚠️ **Eye set is incomplete**: `Outland_Eye_LightBlue` ✅ (canon blue; Atai Molec's
  pale blue) and `Outland_Eye_Yellow` ✅ (canon gold; Darts D'Nar's amber) are both
  right, but canon also lists **green, purple and silver**.
- ⚠️ **`Turn_Gene_Certain`, `Turn_Gene_Duelist` and `Turn_Gene_HighBeautyStandard` are
  unsourced.** Nothing in either article supports them.
- ✅ **Correct and well-sourced**: `RSW_Beard_chinspines` — an exact, specific match for
  the canonical **bony chin spurs**, and the best-aimed gene on this def.
  `Aggression_Aggressive` and `RSW_statgene_predator` are fair readings of a feline
  slaver empire whose queen says the weak deserve to kneel. `Body_Thin` fits
  1.7 m / 60 kg and the lean builds in the infobox image.
- ⚠️ **Note the dimorphism is not expressed**: chin spurs are **male-only** in canon and
  cheek fur is **male-heavier**, but `RSW_Beard_chinspines` sits in a flat gene list.
  Worth checking whether it is gated by sex in the gene def itself.
- **Not representable, and worth recording rather than hiding**: the pronounced accent,
  the obesity variation, and "sallow complexion" as distinct from a flat light skin.
