# Kinrath

**defName**: `Kinrath` (third-party, mlie.starwarsanimalcollection, not vendored — see Watch out)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
**Two separate creatures share the name "Kinrath" across Star Wars canon
tiers, and they are NOT visually the same design** — this is a bigger split
than the usual current-canon/Legends text variance seen elsewhere in this
library:

- **Legends (`Kinrath/Legends`) — the KOTOR-era creature**, sourced to
  *Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic* and *KOTOR II*. Large,
  non-sentient arachnoids found on **Dantooine** and **Kashyyyk**
  (habitat: grasslands, caves, forests). Average coloring **light
  yellow-brownish**, with variant colorations in orange and green. Named
  subspecies: **viper kinrath** (more poisonous, tougher) and **hive
  kinrath** (lives subsurface on Dantooine, hive-and-matriarch social
  structure). Four legs, plus a distinctive **leg-like poisonous appendage
  extending from the face** used as an attack weapon (not simple fangs).
  Completely **blind** — navigates and hunts purely by heat-sensing. Red
  lightsaber crystals could naturally form in unhatched kinrath eggs.
- **Current canon (`Kinrath`, main page) — retconned onto "Netcasters"**,
  from *Star Wars: The Bad Batch* Season 2 episode "Tribe" (2023). These
  are **yellow/golden** spider-like creatures native to the forests of
  **Kashyyyk** (not Dantooine in this tier's prose, though caves on
  Dantooine are mentioned as a secondary habitat). Six eyes in two groups
  of three, sitting above a distinctive **flower-shaped organ of six
  petals** on the head/upper body. Four long segmented legs ending in
  claws surrounded by petal-shaped flaps. Spoon-shaped body, wider at the
  bottom. Darker off-color horizontal stripes circle the body. Spin webs
  from a rear orifice; venomous; capable of distinguishing hostile from
  peaceable intruders. **The "Behind the scenes" section states this
  Bad Batch creature design was explicitly derived from — and later
  in-universe unified with — the KOTOR kinrath**: Disney+ subtitles call
  the netcaster a "kinrath," and *Star Wars: The Secrets of the Wookiees*
  confirms the two are the same species. An earlier, unfinished *Clone
  Wars* episode (only a leaked clip survives, shown at Celebration
  Anaheim 2015) used a more upright, mantis-postured precursor design
  before the concept was reworked into the "Tribe" episode's spoon-bodied
  version.

**Net read: both tiers agree the creature is warm yellow/gold/tan-brown in
base color — there is no text/image color disagreement here, unlike the
Wyyyschokk and PekoPeko cases.** The disagreement is instead in BODY PLAN:
the KOTOR original reads as a long-necked mantis/scorpion-like biped-stanced
quadruped, while the "official" retcon (Bad Batch) reads as a low, rounded,
spoon-bodied spider with a signature flower organ and a tight eye cluster
neither prior design had.

## Visual brief
Four real candidate images were pulled and they split cleanly along the two
design lineages above — read them as two related but distinct visual
targets, not one:

- `kotor_screenshot_hivekinrath_dantooine.jpg` — actual in-game KOTOR
  screenshot: a kinrath rearing up at the player in dim grassland/forest
  lighting. Confirms the body plan from text: four very long, thin,
  multi-jointed legs, a long extended neck/head reaching forward (this is
  the poisonous face-appendage attacking, not a separate head), overall
  olive-brown coloring under the scene's warm dark lighting. Matches the
  "long-necked mantis-like" read.
- `wookieepedia_legends_infobox_viperkinrath.png` — the Legends species
  infobox image (a rendered 3D model, likely from a later KOTOR-related
  game asset). Shows the same body plan cleanly and in neutral lighting:
  **yellow-tan base with rust/reddish-brown mottling**, an upward-curving
  segmented neck ending in a small mantis-like head, four extremely long
  jointed legs with clawed/pointed tips, NO visible eyes-cluster or flower
  organ. This is the strongest clean color+shape reference for the
  Legends/KOTOR design and confirms "light yellow-brownish" from the text.
- `wookieepedia_currentcanon_netcaster_infobox.png` — Bad Batch "Tribe"
  render, front-on: confirms the flower organ (orange/red, six petals,
  positioned below a head bearing **six black eyes in a tight cluster**),
  golden-tan chitin, dense mossy Kashyyyk forest setting with webbing
  visible. This is the strongest reference for the current-canon "netcaster"
  design and its two features absent from the KOTOR model (flower organ,
  eye cluster).
- `wookieepedia_currentcanon_netcaster_rear.png` — same creature from
  behind: confirms the spoon-shaped body silhouette (narrow at top, wider
  toward the rear) and dark horizontal banding/stripes across the
  abdomen, matching the text description exactly.
- `unfinished_tcw_netcasters_conceptclip.png` — leaked clip from the
  cancelled *Clone Wars* episode, three creatures shown: bright
  **yellow-gold**, more upright/standing posture than the final Bad Batch
  version, small mandibles visible on the nearest one, the six-eye cluster
  already present on the two further back. Useful as a third data point
  confirming yellow/gold as the stable base color across every design
  iteration, but its posture is closer to upright-standing than either the
  KOTOR original or the final low-slung "Tribe" version — a third,
  intermediate body plan, included for completeness rather than as a
  primary reference.

**Distinguishing from Wyyyschokk (the sibling Kashyyyk Shadowlands
spider in this library):** Wyyyschokk is ruled as **blue-grey with a
yellow-orange abdomen cross, blue-black legs, and spiky bristle tufts**
(see `wyyyschokk/description.md`). Kinrath, in every candidate image across
both canon tiers, is **warm yellow/gold/tan-brown with no blue anywhere** —
the two should read as opposite ends of a color wheel, not near-duplicates.
Wyyyschokk's signature mark is the abdomen cross; Kinrath's signature marks
are the six-eye cluster and flower organ (current canon) or the long
mantis-like neck-appendage (KOTOR/Legends) — neither has a cross marking.
