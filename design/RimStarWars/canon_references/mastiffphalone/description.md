## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mastiff_phalone

Mastiff phalones were a non-sentient species of large avian quadrupeds that were native to the savannas of Maridun. As carnivores, they were dangerous predators.

**Biology and appearance** Mastiff phalones were a non-sentient species of avian with a long body and four legs. They had a coat of gray feathers with dark-blue markings. While they had the remnants of wings visible at their front legs, their powerful limbs ended in long claws. They also had manes of dark feathers and a long feather-like tail and resembled a griffin. The mastiff phalones were fast runners and their strong limbs with claws and their toothy beaks made them dangerous carnivorous predators on the grass plains of their native planet of Maridun.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mastiff_phalone/Legends

The mastiff phalone, also known as mastif phalone, was a large, aggressive, carnivorous bird native to the savannas of Maridun.

**Biology and appearance** The top predators of Maridun, mastiff phalones were quadrupedal pack hunters about two meters long. As flightless avians mastiff phalones had turkey-like heads with a sharp beak, and a mane of large brown feathers. Males used the feathers in courtship displays. They had a muscular build with sharp talons large enough to wrap around a man's waist. They were nearly as large as a landspeeder and impossible to domesticate.

They tended to hunt in groups of two or more and used the tall grasses of Maridun's savannas as cover. They were fierce but tended to retreat from an attack if the prey was too powerful, only to later attack the same prey again. When sufficiently agitated, a mastif phalone could be more dangerous than a rancor and just as hard to bring down. The immigrated Lurmen had found a peaceful way of dealing with them by a group effort of having several Lurmen distract the mastiff phalone, while one of the group took a rope and tied it around the its legs, immobilizing the creature. The Lurmen later released the captive mastiff phalone back into the wild.
