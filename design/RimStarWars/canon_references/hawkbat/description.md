# Hawkbat

**defName**: `Hawkbat` (third-party, mlie.starwarsanimalcollection, not vendored — see Watch out)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
Two separate Wookieepedia pages exist for "Hawk-bat" and they diverge sharply
— more sharply than usual for this library:

- **Current canon** (`Hawk-bat`, pageid 506226): a thin stub. A hawk-bat is
  "a type of bat which had a beak with sharp contours." The only concrete
  detail given is a simile: Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin's bony nose "looked
  like a hawk-bat's beak" (*Battle to the End*). The species' name was also
  a derogatory insult for an unsavory person (*Servants of the Empire: Rebel
  in the Ranks*), and the Jedi youngling clan Dooku belonged to was named
  after the creature (*Dooku: Jedi Lost*). No coloration, size, habitat, or
  behavior is given anywhere on the current-canon page. It also appears
  (via its infobox image) in *Star Wars: Hyperspace Lounge* material.
- **Legends** (`Hawk-bat/Legends`, pageid 11574): far richer. Hawk-bats were
  predatory **reptavians** (part-reptile, part-bird/bat) native to urbanized
  worlds, especially **Coruscant** — one of the few native Coruscanti species
  to survive the planet's urbanization, alongside the thrantcill. About 1
  meter tall with an average **1.5-meter wingspan**; leathery wings on a
  thin membrane stretched over wing-bones, the membrane **studded with
  spiky growths**. A hooked beak lined with tiny teeth shreds prey; **ruby
  eyes** with an extremely wide vision spectrum (UV, infrared, and beyond)
  plus echolocation for spotting camouflaged prey. Skin color is explicitly
  two-staged: hatchlings are **green**, and on reaching maturity a hawk-bat
  sheds its green skin (and eats the shed skin) to reveal **purplish
  yellow-gray** adult coloring. They hunt in coordinated flocks — a lone
  hawk-bat is vulnerable, but a disturbed flock retaliates as one — favoring
  granite slugs and shadowmoth larvae, scooping prey off vertical walls.
  Eggs are camouflaged brown-and-green to look like rocks; mothers guard
  nests fiercely. Hawk-bats were also eaten as a delicacy (Emperor Palpatine
  kept a private aviary of them for that purpose) and gave their name to the
  lightsaber form Ataru and to the Hawkbat Battalion of the 101st Regiment.

**Important correction to this creature's brief:** the "predatory bat-like
flier that prominently appears on Lothal in *Star Wars Rebels*" is a
**different, separate Wookieepedia species: `Loth-bat`** (from the junior
novel *Battle to the End* and the *Rebels* episodes "Flight of the Defender"
and "Jedi Night"), not the hawk-bat. Loth-bats are giant gliding bats native
to Lothal that roost under building eaves and make unnerving high-pitched
squeals at night; they have no confirmed relation to hawk-bats beyond the
shared "bat" word in the name and a passing narrative comparison (Agent
Kallus mentally likened the Grand Inquisitor to a loth-bat). **This pass did
not find or verify the "hawk-bats in the belfry" idiom** cited in the task
brief — it may exist in some other source, but no citation for it turned up
in the pages pulled here, so it is not asserted as fact.

## Visual brief
This is a stronger case of text/image (and current-canon/Legends) disagreement
than usual, and three of the four candidate images line up with each other
against the fourth:

- `wookieepedia_current_canon_infobox.jpg` (current canon, a display prop
  photographed for *Star Wars: Hyperspace Lounge* material) shows something
  that barely reads as "hawk-bat" at all: a squat, **slug/larva-bodied**
  creature in deep violet-to-near-black skin, a bulky snouted head with
  small forward-curling horn-like growths, visible curved fangs, and large
  clawed grasping forelimbs. No batlike membrane wings are visible in this
  pose. This is a legitimate current-canon depiction but is visually
  disconnected from everything else found for this creature — treat it as
  the minority/outlier reference, not the target.
- `wookieepedia_legends_infobox.jpg` (*The Wildlife of Star Wars: A Field
  Guide* illustration) — the clearest single reference: a pterosaur-like
  silhouette with **violet-purple dorsal wing membrane fading to
  cream/pale-gold on the ventral side**, ribbed wing structure, a short
  beaked head with a small forward horn and a green eye (not the ruby the
  text names — see below), clawed wingtips, long tail. Matches the "purplish
  yellow-gray adult" text closely for the wing coloring.
- `wookieepedia_legends_juvenile.jpg` (same Field Guide source, two
  individuals) directly illustrates the text's green-juvenile → purple/gold
  adult transformation: a green-and-purple individual perched atop a
  building, still gripping a shed skin below it, next to a separate
  **pale gray-white** flying individual with a **red/orange eye** diving
  past. Confirms the molt described in the sourced text and shows the eye
  color is inconsistently rendered across sources (green in one image, red
  in another) even though the text says ruby.
- `donor_mod_workshop_icon.jpg` — cropped from the donor mod's own Steam
  Workshop screenshot (full grid screenshot showing "HAWK-BAT" labeled next
  to Gornt/Gorak/Iriaz — see Source URLs). **This is the strongest
  confirmation available**: the mod's own stylized icon uses the same
  **violet-purple-topped, cream/pale-yellow-bottomed wing membrane**,
  small head with a slight brow horn, and long tail as the Legends Field
  Guide art — i.e. whoever built this RimWorld mod referenced the classic
  Legends hawk-bat, not the Hyperspace Lounge current-canon redesign.

**Net read: render the Legends version** — a purple-topped, cream-to-pale-gold-
bellied, membrane-winged reptavian bat with a small forehead horn, hooked
beak, and long tail, consistent across three independent sources (Field
Guide art ×2 and the donor mod's own icon). The current-canon Hyperspace
Lounge image is real but is the outlier and should not be used as the color
target — flagging it here rather than quietly dropping it, per this
library's own rule about disagreement.
