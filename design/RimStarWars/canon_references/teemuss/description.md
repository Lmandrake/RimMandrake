## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tee-muss

Tee-muss were a domesticated species native to the planet Onderon and exported across the galaxy to planets such as Florrum, where they were used as battle mounts, or Felucia, where they were used by nysillin farmers. Tee-musses fed on the purple kings' crown shrub found in the highlands of Onderon.

During the Galactic Civil War, Orson Krennic wore gloves made from the hide of tee-muss calves.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tee-muss/Legends

Tee-muss were a species of domesticated farm animals native to the jungle planet Onderon. They were located on various worlds around the galaxy&mdash;such as Balnab, Felucia, and Florrum&mdash;because of domestication purposes or to serve as mounts.

**Description** Tee-muss were four legged odd-toed ungulate. They had a short proboscis and two tusks on their lower jaw. Their ears were long and normally bent and pointed outward, when the tee-muss was attacked or threatened, they pointed downward. The call of a tee-muss was an excellent transmitter for warnings.
