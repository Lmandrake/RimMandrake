## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blixus

Blixus were rare, half-shelled semi-aquatic creatures native to Sljee.

**Biology and appearance** They possessed five tentacles and six sharp legs. They shared a common ancestor with the rathtar, vixus, and sarlacc, all possessing a large maw, tentacles, and a voracious appetite. Each was native to different worlds, and the different species had adapted separately to become great hunters in their specific environments.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blixus/Legends

Blixus were large non-sentient carnivorous cephalopods from an unknown planet of origin.

**Biology and appearance** A horrific monstrosity coated with a half-shelled, articulated carapace, with hard chitin covering its six pick-like legs. Its well-muscled underbelly concealed a ghastly gash of a mouth filled with sharp teeth, and a pair of pincer-tipped graspers. Its most terrifying feature, though, were the five sinewy tentacles that terminated in sucker-lined grasping pads.
