## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycrit

Rycrits were a species in the galaxy whose skins were used in the making of clothing and items. Their meat was also edible, being used to make spiced rycrit strips.

During the Clone Wars, Jedi General Aayla Secura wore a belt made of rycrit hide.

During the reign of the Galactic Empire, White Worms scrumrat Rebolt possessed the Commander of Staves, a staff of his own making which he wrapped with black rycrit leather for insulation.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycrit/Legends

Rycrits were herd animals native to Ryloth. They were quadrupeds with short fur, long faces, four nostrils, pointed teeth, and sharp cloven hooves. Twi'leks raised them for their hides, and as a source of meat (often served as rycrit stew).

Planets with a substantial Twi'lek population often imported rycrit herds. One such planet was Talasea, though the rycrits there went feral when the last of the Talasean colonists were killed by Imperial forces under Darth Vader.

The Twi'lek Kur ate on one for a month when banished to the nightlands by Nolaa Tarkona. He used the bones for tools and hide for a blanket.
