"The grazer was a docile, four legged, slow-moving herbivore native to the planet Alderaan. Bred for their rich source of nutritious and tasty meat and agricultural purposes, grazers were easy to manage and grew to tremendous sizes. [...] Grazers were also related to the Nerf. Wild grazers were significantly leaner than the fatter and more well-fed domestic grazer."

## Visual brief
**Owner ruling 2026-10-08 (verbatim, made on the wild grazer and applying to every animal): "That's realistic, not these cartoon versions you keep using."** The only depiction is a Legends watercolour, so render it realistically: real-world anatomy, wrinkled hide, natural pigment and light, no ink outlines or wash.

Viewed `wookieepedia_legends_1.webp` (*The Wildlife of Star Wars* watercolour; the domestic grazer is the LEFT animal) and `donor_current_sprite.png` (donor male, our stand-in).
- **Overall shape:** enormous, nearly spherical barrel body, far bulkier than the wild form, on short thick pillar legs with blunt hooves.
- **Colour:** mostly white/cream hide, with large irregular tan-to-gold patches across the back and upper flanks, the patches holding darker leopard-style rosettes; white dominates the underside and legs.
- **Head:** small, carried low at the front; floppy flat ears; a wrinkled, trunk-like snout; a few small horn knobs; open mouth in the image.
- **Tail:** short and thin.
- **Donor stand-in:** a white egg-shaped blob with a dark dorsal ridge and one hooked horn — the colour mass agrees, but no gold rosette patches, no legs read, no ears, and a cartoon outline. It is the domestic shape only in bulk.
