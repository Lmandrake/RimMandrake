# Cannok

**defName**: `RSW_Cannok` (vendored in SWBestiary, texture folder `Cannok`)

## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
Legends-continuity species (page tagged `{{Top|fga|leg}}` — Legends, not
current Disney canon; still bona fide Star Wars content, just older EU).
Small, aggressive, non-sentient predators native to Onderon and its jungle
moon Dxun, first appearing in *Tales of the Jedi* 1 (comics) and later
detailed in *Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords*. Skin color:
**mottled yellow-green**, characteristically bumpy and wrinkled. Distinctions
per the infobox: **bulging eyestalks** (eyes on short stalks that move
independently of one another), a **large, toothy mouth** with an
upward-facing jaw connected low on the skull and thin needle-like teeth
jutting from the lower jaw over the upper lip, and **4 tridactyl (three-toed)
feet** ending in tough spatulate claws. Squat, bloated body; short stubby
tail; a thick bony ridge down the back from which thin fleshy spines
protrude. Habitat: grasslands and jungles. Diet: omnivorous — notoriously
eats anything that fits in its mouth. Hunts in packs of three or more,
unafraid of fire, emits only a husky grunt. Several were later relocated to
Telos IV for ecosystem restoration, where their population exploded and
caused ecological damage.

## Visual brief
Three images agree closely and reinforce each other — this is a
well-corroborated design across very different art styles (game concept art,
in-game 3D render, and a comic panel):
- **Squat, bloated, toad-like body** posture confirmed in both the KOTOR2
  concept sketch and the in-game render.
- **Color**: dull tan/khaki/olive-yellow-green mottled hide on the back and
  flanks, consistently **paler/whitish on the belly/underside** — visible in
  both the concept art and the in-game render. Matches "mottled yellow-green"
  closely.
- **Spiked dorsal ridge**: a fan/row of thin pointed spines running from the
  crown of the head down the back — present in all three images, most
  dramatic in the concept art (long thin quills radiating off the skull) and
  the in-game render (shorter spikes continuing down a segmented back plus
  a spiked tail tip).
- **Eyes**: the concept art shows a single large deep-socketed eye (not
  obviously stalked); the in-game render clearly shows **two eyes on
  short stalks that can be read independently** — matching the "bulging
  eyestalks" text description more precisely than the concept art does.
- **Mouth**: both the concept art and in-game render show a wide,
  low-slung jaw lined with many small sharp teeth, matching the toothy-mouth
  description.
- **Legs/feet**: thick, stocky legs ending in clawed toes are visible in the
  in-game render; the "4 tridactyl feet" detail is a bit harder to confirm
  precisely at this resolution but is consistent with what's visible.
- The comic panel (*Tales of the Jedi*, first appearance) only shows a
  cropped close-up of a toothy tan head with the same spike-fringe and
  needle teeth — consistent with, not contradicting, the other two.

Overall this is a strongly consistent design across sources: squat toad/frog-
like quadruped, mottled tan-olive hide, pale belly, fringe of dorsal spines,
wide toothy mouth, stalked eyes.

**The current donor sprite** (`donor_current_sprite.png`) is a strong match:
olive-tan mottled body, spiked dorsal ridge/fringe, wide fanged mouth, and
pebbled belly texture. One difference worth noting: the donor sprite draws a
single large amber eye rather than the two independently-moving stalked eyes
the in-game KOTOR2 render and the source text both call for — a regen should
add the stalked-eye detail rather than a single flat eye.
