### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mynock

Mynocks (IPA: /ˈmaɪ.nɒk/, Merriam–Webster: \mīnäk\) were a bat-like parasitic species that hailed from the world of Ord Mynock. A group of mynocks is called a battery. Even so, they also thrived in asteroids and could survive in realspace, with some even living within exogorths. For sustenance, they could eat the energy supply of starships.

**Biology and appearance** A species of silicon-based bat-like parasites native to the planet Ord Mynock, Mynocks chewed on the power cables and energy conductors of starships, and could drain a ship's entire power supply. Some said that mynocks could feed on so much energy that they became "spark-drunk." They reproduced by splitting in two and growing new creatures from each half. Mynocks were capable of surviving in the atmospheres of planets and the vacuum of space, though only a few varieties lived on planets. They were also known to inhabit asteroid fields, thriving on asteroids, and the gullets of exogorths, where they shared in its meals and were part of its internal ecosystem. Nevertheless, most exogorths hated having mynocks inside them. Mynocks were remotely related to grallocs and tibidees, large flying creatures native to Stygeon Prime who were also a nuisance to starpilots.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mynock/Legends

Mynocks were a bat-like silicon-based space-dwelling parasitic species that could be found across the galaxy. They were often seen leeching power from spacecraft and could absorb matter from a ship's hull causing it to breach, and if not caught in time, cause catastrophic failure.

**Biology and appearance** The mynock was a leathery, black-skinned flying creature resembling a manta ray. They were vacuumbreathers, capable of surviving in extremely cold extraplanetary environments. Mynocks had bat-like wings with clawed tips, which they could use to fly in atmospheres, however, they were mostly limited to the vacuum of space due to an allergy to helium found in the atmosphere of many planets. This allergic reaction would cause them to inflate and nearly explode before dying. Many spacewalking sport hunters would kill mynocks by attaching helium-based grenades to them in what was called "Mynock Puffing." 

They fed on electrical, stellar, and electromagnetic energy, and would thus attach themselves to power cables or ion ports on ships and structures. The energy was absorbed through a specialized, suction-cup-like organ resembling a big sucker-like mouth located between their eye stalks. In space, mynocks traveled by riding stellar winds, to capture energy emitted by stars, absorbing radiation with their skin. After gathering enough energy, creatures would find an asteroid to land, and attached themselves to various surfaces, consuming silicon and other materials, to acquire enough extra mass for reproduction.

Mynocks had very few internal organs, and their biological makeup resembled that of single-celled organisms. In place of bones, mynocks had cartilage beneath the flesh. Similarly to most single-celled micro-organisms, mynocks reproduced by mitosis, however they were also known to lay eggs. When reproducing, mynocks spawned larvae on planetary surfaces.

Some mynocks had yellow eyes, and had shiny spore sacs that could distend. They had stingray-like tails and poison teeth. Some Mynocks had legs, while others were legless, some dragging themselves along by their ventral sucker-disk. 

Mynocks were distantly related to xuvvas. A few (sub)species of mynocks existed, including one that had a normal mouth, rather than a sucker-like one, and another that had blue skin and gave birth to live young.
