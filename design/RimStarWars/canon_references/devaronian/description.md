## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)

Class **mammal**. Homeworld **Devaron**. Infobox: skin colour **brown**; hair colour
**brown**; eye colour **black**. ⚠️ **Height, mass, lifespan and diet are all blank** —
recorded as absent, not guessed. Note the infobox lists a *single* colour for each of
skin and hair, while the article body and the images both give much wider ranges (see
the visual brief).

**Body plan.** Sentient humanoids with two legs, a torso, a single head, two arms with
hands, fingers and an opposable thumb. The head features **a nose, a mouth filled with
pointed teeth, and two forward-facing eyes**, which "notably came in shades of **black,
blue, brown, dark brown, green, pink, red, and yellow**" — eight cited colours, each to
a different work. **Both sides of the face are flanked by two pointed ears that stick
out slightly.**

🔑 **The dimorphism is the species' defining visual fact, and it is not clean.** "The
species had two sexes, male and female, which were **primarily dimorphic**."

- **Many males had short horns growing from the top of their heads.**
- **Most females were mostly hornless, with small vestigial bumps on their foreheads,
  along with a full head of hair.**
- ⚠️ **"However, some females — such as the crime lord Isa Durand, and others —
  possessed full sets of horns as well."** So horns are **not** strictly male.
- **Male Devaronians were typically bald** — "however, **some of them grew sideburns as
  well as beards**."
- **Some female Devaronians possessed a thin coat of gray fur on their body.** Note this
  is attributed to **females**.

**Biology — two hard mechanical traits, both well sourced.** "Devaronians had **black
blood**, and were **silver-based** due to the unique blood filtration and cleansing
system of the species. Processed through **two livers**, their bodies were constantly
cleansed of toxins and carcinogens which allowed them to be **highly resistant to
poison and consume foods toxic to other species**." On Devaron **sulfur was used as a
stimulant** to enhance speed and strength, because inhaling it entered the bloodstream
rapidly; their livers struggled to eliminate sulfur, so **long-term use could be
dangerous**. And: "**Their skin was also immune to fire and extremely high
temperatures.**"

**Behaviour, and it is unusually specific.** "Devaronians lived under a **matriarchal
government** on their homeworld; though males were not prohibited from holding positions
of power, **few desired to do so**. **The biological dimorphism of the species carried
over into their culture**, wherein **females tended to be more settled and serious
minded, while males longed to wander the stars**. Devaronian males were **extremely
dedicated to their families despite their wanderlust**, and were known to **send the
earnings they accumulated abroad back to their families** to support them."

🔑 **"Force-sensitivity was found in higher frequencies among Devaronians than in other
species.** Despite this, **no strong Force-based tradition emerged** on Devaron or among
their colonies." The Jedi established the **Temple of Eedit** on Devaron to train
Force-sensitive children there, "with **females making up the larger percentage of
students as males desired to travel** more than the Jedi culture permitted."

**In the galaxy.** Devaronians **developed hyperdrive technology independently**, without
extraterrestrial assistance, and had explored and interacted with the galaxy **before
the Galactic Republic ever made contact with their region of space** — they were "a
common sight across the galaxy for **tens of millennia** before the Clone Wars." They
have become increasingly prominent in the less civilized parts of the **Outer Rim** in
recent decades, partly because of the Empire's distrust of aliens. Named individuals
span exactly the roles the Cartel placement wants: **Cikatro Vizago** (led the Broken
Horn Syndicate on Lothal), **Roland Durand** (criminal, Ord Mantell), **Burg** (a
mercenary, *The Mandalorian* "Chapter 6: The Prisoner"), **Yelkin** (a thorilide miner),
**Torro Sbazzle** (Executive Chef of Cloud City), and **a red-skinned male Devaronian
named Kardue'sai'Malloc** in the Mos Eisley cantina — that last is a direct canon
citation for **red** skin.

## Visual brief

🔴 **The infobox says skin colour "brown." All four references disagree, and they
disagree in two different directions.** This is the anooba case again. What the images
actually show:

- **Bright, saturated ORANGE-RED**, in `wookieepedia_infobox_male_female_pair.jpg` —
  both figures, male and female. Not brown, not tan: a strong vermilion, the most
  striking thing about the image. This is independently corroborated in the article body
  by "**a red-skinned male Devaronian named Kardue'sai'Malloc**."
- **Pale peach / human flesh tone**, in `wookieepedia_male_pale.jpg` (Burg) and
  `wookieepedia_female_tress_hacnua.jpg` (Tress Hacnua) — genuinely pale, with no red
  cast at all.

So the real range is **pale flesh → vermilion**, and "brown" is a poor description of
either end. Trusting images on appearance: **a Devaronian is most recognisably
red-skinned**, and pale is a legitimate second morph. Do not paint a brown one.

**The horns differ sharply between the two male references, and both are horns.**
- The infobox male has **two large horns rising from the top of the skull and sweeping
  up and OUTWARD** into fine points, roughly matching skin hue at the base and going
  paler/cream toward the tips.
- Burg has **two large horns rising and sweeping up and BACK**, and they are **dark
  grey-brown — a completely different value from his pale skin**, reading as hard keratin
  rather than flesh.

⚠️ Note both are **large**, while the prose says males have "**short** horns." The images
outvote the adjective; the horns are a major silhouette element, not a nub. Both figures
are **bald**, confirming the prose and the def's `Outland_BaldMale`.

**The female vestigial bumps are the clearest single find in this entry, and they are
completely unlike horns.** `wookieepedia_female_tress_hacnua.jpg` shows them
unmistakably: **two (arguably three) small, smooth, ROUND, dark reddish-brown raised
discs set flat on the forehead** — like polished beads pressed into the skin, sitting
above and between the brows where a male's horns would erupt. They are **domed, not
pointed; dark against pale skin; and lie flush with the forehead plane** rather than
projecting. The kneeling female in the infobox image has the same feature rendered as a
**dark red marking on the forehead**. 🔑 A text-only prompt reading "small vestigial
bumps" will produce short horns or a smooth brow; the canonical feature is
**contrasting round dark discs on the forehead**, and that is what a female Devaronian
head sprite needs.

**Female hair is a real and vivid feature.** Both female references have **a full head of
hair, worn at shoulder length or shorter**, and the colours are strong: **purple/violet**
on the infobox female, **white/silver** on Tress Hacnua. Neither is the infobox's
"brown." Tress Hacnua also has **prominent freckling** across the cheeks and nose, and
the infobox female has **darker red mottling/freckling** on cheeks and forehead — so
**freckled/mottled skin is attested on both female references** and is worth having.

**Face.** The male infobox face is the most alien of the four: **deep-set eyes with a
green glint, a broad mouth drawn back over visible pointed teeth, heavy angular brows,
and pointed ears standing well out from the skull**. Burg's face is the same architecture
but heavily lined and jowly with a flatter nose. Both female faces are **markedly more
human** — ordinary lips, small noses, human eyes — with the pointed ears and forehead
discs doing all the species work. Some of that is the reference type (a painted
adventure-game plate versus a painted portrait), so treat the *degree* as uncertain; the
**ears on all four** and the **pointed teeth on the males** are the consistent reads.

⚠️ **The gray body fur is not visible in any reference.** Canon attributes "a thin coat
of gray fur" to **some females**, and none of the four images shows it. Text-only.

**`donor_current_sprite.png` is on-target for the male and says nothing about the
female.** It is `SWX/Pawn/HeadAttachments/devaronian/bighorns_south.png` — a greyscale
render-node mask (absence of colour is correct and expected; the hue findings above
belong on the skin and hair *genes*). It renders **a pair of large horns sweeping up and
outward**, which is a good match for the infobox male and, being named `bighorns`,
correctly follows the images rather than the prose's "short." It exists in
`_south`/`_east`/`_north`, so the coverage is complete. 🔴 **What is missing entirely is
the female:** there is **no forehead-bump asset** anywhere in the repo for this species,
so a hornless female Devaronian currently has an unmarked human forehead and is
identifiable only by ears. Given that the round forehead discs are the species' clearest
female signature, that is the art gap to close.

## Repo def versus canon

🔴 **`Beard_NoBeardOnly` directly contradicts a cited canon sentence.** Canon: male
Devaronians "were typically bald — however, **some of them grew sideburns as well as
beards**," cited to *Droids in Distress* and *Sisters*. The def forbids beards outright.
This is a flat contradiction, not a simplification.

🔴 **Nothing represents the elevated Force-sensitivity.** "Force-sensitivity was found in
**higher frequencies among Devaronians than in other species**" is a distinctive,
explicitly comparative species trait with an obvious Biotech analogue (a psychic gene at
low incidence), and the def has nothing for it. Worth reading alongside the inverse
finding in `rakata/description.md`, where psychic genes were given to a species canon
says is **Force-blind**: this library has now found the same error in both directions.
⚠️ Note also that canon pairs the sensitivity with "**no strong Force-based tradition
emerged**" — the frequency is biological, the culture is absent, so this should not
become a species of Force-users.

⚠️ **The skin palette covers only the red half of canon.** `Skin_DeepRed`,
`Outland_Skin_Red`, `Outland_Skin_DeepOrange` and `Skin_Orange` are **all red or
orange** — an excellent match for the infobox image pair and for Kardue'sai'Malloc, and
the def deserves credit for following the images rather than the infobox's "brown." But
**two of four references show a pale flesh-toned Devaronian** (Burg, Tress Hacnua) and
the infobox's own cited colour is **brown**, and the def admits neither. The palette is
right but too narrow.

⚠️ **`Outland_Chest_Fur` puts the fur on the wrong sex.** Canon: "**Some female**
Devaronians possessed a thin coat of **gray fur on their body**." A generic chest-fur
gene in RimWorld reads as a male secondary characteristic, and canon assigns the trait
to females — and to the *body*, not specifically the chest. Inverted rather than absent.

⚠️ **Hair colours: one invented, and the only canon-cited colour missing.**
`Hair_SnowWhite` is confirmed by Tress Hacnua and `Outland_HairColor_DarkMagenta` by the
infobox female — both good, image-led choices. But **`Outland_HairColor_BrightSage`
(green) is attested nowhere** in either the article or any reference, and **brown — the
sole colour the infobox actually cites** — has no gene at all. `Hair_DarkReddish` is
unattested but unobjectionable.

⚠️ **`Mood_Pessimist` is unsourced.** Nothing in the article describes Devaronians as
pessimistic. What the article *does* give is a strong, specific and currently
unrepresented behavioural profile: **male wanderlust versus female settledness**, males
**extremely dedicated to their families** and remitting earnings home, and a
**matriarchal society**. If a mood or trait gene is wanted here, wanderlust is the
sourced one.

⚠️ **No body-size gene and no sourced size to check it against.** Height, mass and
lifespan are blank in the infobox and absent from the article body — genuinely
**unsourceable**, recorded rather than guessed. The def carries no size gene, which is at
least not a contradiction.

⚠️ **`BS_Diet_Carnivore` is weakly supported.** Diet is blank in the infobox and unstated
in the article. "A mouth filled with pointed teeth" is suggestive but not a diet
citation, and canon gives a Devaronian **Executive Chef of Cloud City** and a species
that can **eat foods toxic to others** — i.e. the sourced dietary trait is *tolerance*,
not obligate carnivory.

**Excellently matched, recorded so a later pass does not undo them.** This def has the
best-sourced physiology in the batch:
- `ToxResist_Total` — canon: two livers, constantly cleansed of toxins and carcinogens,
  **highly resistant to poison**, can consume foods toxic to other species. A precise hit.
- `FireResistant` — canon: "**Their skin was also immune to fire and extremely high
  temperatures.**" A precise hit.
- `Outland_Blood_Black` — canon: "Devaronians had **black blood**." Correct.
- `RSW_DevaronianHorns` and `Outland_BaldMale` — correct, and the horn dimorphism is the
  species' headline feature. ⚠️ Just note canon allows **some females full sets of
  horns**, so a strictly sex-gated horn gene is slightly narrower than canon.
- `MinTemp_SmallIncrease` and `Outland_ThickSkin` — consistent with fire/heat immunity.
- `Outland_DeceleratedPregnancy` — unsourced either way; harmless.
