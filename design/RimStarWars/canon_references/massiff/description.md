## Sourced text (Wookieepedia)
### Canon — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Massiff

The massiff was a reptilian and canine species of dangerous predatory hunters from the desert planets Tatooine and Geonosis. Massiffs were often domesticated for sentry and guard tasks, most well known the Tusken Raiders of Tatooine.

**Biology and appearance** A massiff was a non-sentient squat quadruped reptilian and canine species with a row of many spiky spines along their humped backs. An average massiff stood at roughly one meter high. They had brown and grey or grey-green, bony plate armor, and thick and leathery skin which greyed with age. The size of the spines, which the reptiles used for defense, also indicated a massiff's age as they continued to grow over their lives. The creature's large black or yellow eyes allowed it to see well at night and in the dark in general, allowing it to easier spot approaching danger and hunting prey at night, and extremely precise smell senses. Massifs were snarling hunters and fiercely loyal pack animals, stopping at nothing to eliminate perceived threats, who lived in dens. Their mouths could open very wide and were filled with long sharp teeth and they had powerful and dangerous bites. Massiffs were desert creatures native to the planets Tatooine and Geonosis and also residing on the planet Florrum.

Massiffs could be roasted and eaten by humans.
### Legends — https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Massiff/Legends

Massiffs were reptilian creatures found on Geonosis and Tatooine. The species' original world was unknown; however, the Geonosis and Tatooine populations were by far the most numerous. Canine in appearance with an armored hide, the beasts could be domesticated and used for sentry and guard tasks. On Geonosis, possession of a massiff was a sign of authority and aristocracy. The Geonosians and massiffs had a somewhat symbiotic relationship; in exchange for shelter, the creatures would rid the hives of pests. It was easy to tell the age of a massiff, for they grew a new pair of antlers every standard year.

**Biology and appearance** Massiffs were reptiles that had coarse skin, large jaws, toothy mouths, big dark eyes and a line of hard spikes along their backs.
