# JawaRules — validation walk
subject: src/RimStarWars/JawaRules  (packageId `mandrake.rsw.jawarules`, from About/About.xml)
deps: brrainz.harmony (Harmony); Ludeon.RimWorld is base game, not third-party
list: minimal   # Harmony is on the minimal list already; no other third-party mod is required for 4 of 5 rules
status-hint: one Harmony assembly, five independently-armed postfix/transpiler rules — hand-patched (not PatchAll) so one missing target never disables the others (src/RimStarWars/JawaRules/Source/JawaRules.cs)

## must be true
- A pawn whose `genes.Xenotype.defName == "RSW_MandrakeJawa"` (the constant `JawaRulesMod.JawaXenotype`, JawaRules.cs:20) can never be given a Sow job on a growing zone or a hydroponics basin (`Patch_GrowerSow_ExtraRequirements` postfixes `WorkGiver_GrowerSow.ExtraRequirements`, forcing `__result = false`), but the SAME pawn can still Harvest, cut plants and chop trees — those are separate WorkGiver classes this patch never touches.
- The ban is keyed on the pawn, not the colony: a non-Jawa pawn in the same colony can sow normally.
- Any `Humanlike`-intelligence pawn generated with `pawn.relations == null` gets a `Pawn_RelationsTracker` attached immediately (`Patch_CreateInitialComponents` postfixes `PawnComponentsUtility.CreateInitialComponents`) — this is the fix for OuterRim/KotOR droid races whose fleshType has `isOrganic == true` in the engine's eyes; vanilla Animal-intelligence mechanoids are untouched by the `Humanlike` guard and correctly keep `relations == null`.
- A tamed or newborn player-faction animal with no player-chosen name (`Name == null` or still `Numerical`) draws its name from its own race's `nameGenerator` instead of vanilla's numeric "Species N" fallback (`Patch_GenerateNecessaryName` postfixes `Pawn.GenerateNecessaryName`, guarded to `RaceProps.Animal` and `Faction == Faction.OfPlayer` only); an already-named or player-renamed animal is never overwritten.
- World feature labels (the italic region/sea/range names on the planet view) render at 0.60 peak alpha instead of vanilla's 0.30 (`Patch_WorldFeatures_UpdateAlpha` transpiles the one `0.3f` constant in `WorldFeatures.UpdateAlpha` to `0.6f`).
- World feature label glyphs sit ~1.5 units above the planet surface instead of vanilla's 0.4 (`Patch_WorldFeatureText_Lift` transpiles all four `0.4f` constants in `WorldFeatureTextMesh_TextMeshPro.WrapAroundPlanetSurface` to `1.5f`), so they no longer intersect the terrain mesh.
- Each of the five rules is armed (or fails) independently and logs a rule-named line either way — a renamed/removed target method in one rule never silently kills the other four, and a transpiler whose expected-hit-count doesn't match logs a named error even though the game keeps running on the unmodified IL.

## the walk
1. [L] Player.log after load contains, once each, all five armed lines with their exact prefixes: `[RimMandrake.StarWars.JawaRules] no-sow: armed for xenotype RSW_MandrakeJawa`, `[RimMandrake.StarWars.JawaRules] droid-relations: armed for humanlike pawns with no relations tracker`, `[RimMandrake.StarWars.JawaRules] pet-names: armed; tamed and newborn animals will draw from their race namer`, `[RimMandrake.StarWars.JawaRules] world-labels: armed; world feature names peak at 0.60 alpha instead of 0.30`, `[RimMandrake.StarWars.JawaRules] world-label-lift: armed; world feature names sit 1.50 above the surface instead of 0.40` — OR, per rule, its named `TARGET METHOD NOT FOUND` error (never neither).
2. [L] Player.log contains no `[RimMandrake.StarWars.JawaRules] world-labels: expected exactly ONE 0.30 constant in WorldFeatures.UpdateAlpha and found` line — the transpiler's own self-check that it hit exactly one `ldc.r4 0.3` in the IL.
3. [L] Player.log contains no `[RimMandrake.StarWars.JawaRules] world-label-lift: expected exactly 4 0.40 constant` line — the transpiler's own self-check that it hit exactly four `ldc.r4 0.4` instructions.
4. [B] `jawa/spawn_pawn` a player-faction pawn with xenotype `RSW_MandrakeJawa` on a quicktest map → `jawa/pawn_genes` confirms `Xenotype.defName == "RSW_MandrakeJawa"`.
5. [B] `jawa/order_pawn` the Jawa pawn (from step 4) onto a Sow job on a growing zone or hydroponics basin plant → expect the job is refused (WorkGiver returns no requirements met); then `jawa/order_pawn` the same pawn onto a Harvest or PlantCut job on plant material → expect it succeeds. Confirms the ban is per-WorkGiver, not a blanket PlantWork-tag removal.
6. [B] `jawa/spawn_pawn` a second player-faction pawn of any non-Jawa xenotype → `jawa/order_pawn` it onto the same Sow job → expect it succeeds. Control case proving the gate is keyed on xenotype, not on colony membership.
7. [B] `jawa/pawn_relations` on a Humanlike-intelligence pawn already on the map → confirm the relations tracker is non-null. (Weak on its own — vanilla Humanlike/organic pawns get a tracker with or without this patch. The behavior this patch actually fixes only shows on an OuterRim/KotOR droid pawnkind, whose exact defName is NOT grounded in JawaRules' own files — this mod carries no dependency on that mod and doesn't name one. **Gap, not a guess:** running this check for real needs `list=full` with Outer Rim - Droid Depot or an ABF Synstruct mod active, and the concrete Humanlike-droid pawnkind defName pulled from THAT mod's own Defs at test time.)
8. [S] (human pass) look at the planet view before/after: world feature labels visibly denser and lifted clear of the terrain.
