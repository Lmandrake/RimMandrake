## must be true
- Exactly 69 XenotypeDefs in XenotypeDefs/RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml (one per absorbed species) and exactly 69 matching `_Kind` PawnKindDefs in PawnKindDefs/RimMandrakePawnKinds.xml, each pointing race back at PlayerColony via ParentName="BasePlayerPawnKind".
- RSW_MandrakeJawa (XenotypeDefs/MandrakeJawaXenotype.xml, 38 genes, hand-authored not copied) is distinct from RSW_RimMandrakeJawa (the 24-gene generator-output donor copy inside RimMandrakeXenotypes.xml) — same label "Jawa", different defName, and RSW_RimMandrakeJawa_Kind's xenotypeSet must point at RSW_MandrakeJawa, not the donor copy (a documented past defect: both shared a label so the wrong one was invisible in play; fixed at source, with a PatchOperationConditional safety net in StarWarsPatches/Patches/JawaXenotype_Repoint.xml).
- RSW_Jawa_Skittish (GeneDefs/Jawa_Skittish.xml) and RSW_Jawa_MiningDisabled (GeneDefs/Jawa_MiningDisabled.xml) are Jawa-only replacements for donor genes that had gameplay-breaking donor behavior (fainting mid-firefight; a hard mining WorkTag disable) — neither carries a geneClass or mentalBreakDef, both are on RSW_MandrakeJawa's gene list.
- Outer Rim's 16 rescued species pawn kinds (PawnKindDefs/SW_RescuedKinds.xml: Aqualish, Arkanian(+Tribal), Geonosian(+Tribal), Herglic, Jawa(+Tribal), Kaminoan, MonCalamari, Nikto(+Tribal), Quarren(+Tribal), Wookiee(+Tribal)) are copied WITH both of their abstract parents (RimMandrake_ColonyPawnKind, RimMandrake_TribalPawnKind Abstract="True") so a faction fielding them directly does not lose raids/caravans when the donor leaves.
- 48 RulePackDefs in RulePackDefs/SW_NameMakers.xml carry species naming, including RSW_KoTOR_NamerJawa (RSW_MandrakeJawa's nameMaker) — a rule pack without its Languages/English/Strings word lists resolves and produces nothing, so this is a real risk, not just a def-exists check.
- ⚠️ UNDECLARED SOFT DEPENDENCY: HeadTypeDefs/SW_HeadTypes.xml uses `<li Class="TabulaRasa.DefModExt_HeadTypeStuff">` modExtensions (36 occurrences, no MayRequire) shipped by `neronix17.toolbox` ([Neronix17] Tabula Rasa) — that packageId appears only in About.xml's `loadAfter`, never in `modDependencies`. Per the modExtension-missing-type trap (a missing modExtension class discards the WHOLE containing def, silently, load succeeds), every HeadTypeDef in this file is at risk if Tabula Rasa is ever inactive. Confirmed ACTIVE in the current live ModsConfig.xml, so this does not currently misfire, but it is unenforced.
