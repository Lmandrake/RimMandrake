# World rename proposals — four Helix settlements, two feature near-dups

_Drafted 2026-09-12 after the owner rejected "Specimen Hall", "The Revision",
"The Fair Copy" and "Cold Archive" as "actually ridiculous" — verbatim: "Think
about what scientists would actually name their sites. Better still what do
those races in Star Wars call things?" Nothing here is applied; every line is
an option for his card._

Register sources: `design/Jawa/bridge/INHABITED_CAST_HELIX.md` (the Helix are
"competent, polite, slow, and keep excellent records" — their sites should be
named the way working scientists name field stations: after the place, the
sequence, the founder, or the program, never a metaphor);
`design/RimStarWars/star_wars_canon_names.md`; `canon.yml > helix_lineage`;
the frozen sheets' own plain-noun register, which already holds `Aquifer
Station`, `Bonepick Station`, `Cell Seven`, `Vent Nine/Twelve/Forty` — numbered
and functional names are the established voice of this map. The owner's own
precedent is **Ashfall Research Base** in the Ashfall Range
(`worldbuilding/ashfall_research_base.md`): location + function, nothing
theatrical. Live tile data from `world/ASHKARR_WORLDMAP_tiles.csv`.

SW precedent styles used (each grounded in named canon, not invented lore):
- **Polis Massa / Research Station Shantipole** — a medical or research
  facility simply carries the name of the place it sits on.
- **Mount Tantiss** — an Imperial cloning/storage facility named for the
  landform that holds it.
- **Aurebesh site codes** — KOTOR-era military and corporate designations run
  Aurek, Besh, Cresh, Dorn (the SW alphabet used exactly like Alpha/Bravo);
  Czerka's installations in the same era are numbered depots and sites.
- **Czerka depot** — corporate stores named site-plus-function (KOTOR-era
  Czerka depots and outposts; SWTOR's facility code CZ-198 is the same habit).
- **Kaminoan register** — Kamino's facilities are called what they are
  (hatchery, military complex); the cities (Tipoca, Derem) are place names.
- **Tarkin Initiative** — an installation/program carrying its principal's
  name.

Founder names below come only from the shipped cast
(`INHABITED_CAST_HELIX.md`): Director Ynnu **Sorne**-Ehr and Prime Geneticist
**Se-Taun**. No new lore.

---

## 1. Specimen Hall → (tile 1707, mycotic jungle, Twilight Crags region)

1. **Crag Station** — location: named for the Twilight Crags it sits in — Polis Massa / Shantipole style.
2. **Site Aurek** — sequence: the first-designated field site — Aurebesh site-code style (KOTOR-era designations).
3. **Se-Taun Station** — founder: the Prime Geneticist who signs every improvement — Tarkin Initiative style (facility named for its principal).

## 2. The Revision → (tile 17595, nightside ice, Rimewall region)

1. **Farside Station** — location: the far side of the terminator, said plainly — Research Station Shantipole style.
2. **Site Besh** — sequence: second site in the designation series — Aurebesh site-code style.
3. **Sorne Annex** — founder: the Director's house lends the outstation its name — Tarkin Initiative style.

## 3. The Fair Copy → (tile 2741, nightside ice, Sootreach region)

1. **Sootreach Station** — location: carries its region's name — Polis Massa / Shantipole style.
2. **Site Cresh** — sequence: third in the designation series — Aurebesh site-code style.
3. **Germline Annex** — program: named baldly for the germline work done there — Arkanian Microtechnologies style (the outfit named for the work).

## 4. Cold Archive → (nightside ice, Rimewall region — the ice IS the refrigeration, so location-functional names are honest here)

1. **Rimewall Depot** — location + function: the stores facility in the Rimewall — Czerka depot style.
2. **Cold Stores** — function: cold storage sited where the planet does the freezing for free — Kaminoan register (a facility called what it is).
3. **Site Dorn** — sequence: fourth in the designation series — Aurebesh site-code style.

> If both The Revision and Cold Archive draw from this list, pick **Rimewall**
> for at most one of them — both tiles sit in the Rimewall region, and putting
> the root on two settlements re-creates the near-dup this pass exists to fix.

---

## 5. Fall Line Barrens → (feature; collides with the "Fall Line" range root)

Meaning to keep: the barren, broken desert country at the foot of the Fall
Line escarpment. Sheet register: plain compound nouns (Sootreach, Rimewall,
Deadstone, Ashteeth).

1. **Scarpfoot Barrens** — the land at the escarpment's foot, meaning kept exactly.
2. **Talus Barrens** — talus is the rock waste an escarpment sheds; technical-plain, same register as Oxalate Watch.
3. **The Breaks** — real-geography term (as in the Missouri Breaks) for broken land falling away below a rim.

## 6. Scald Spine → (feature; collides with "The Scald" sea)

Meaning to keep: the notched ring range that walls the Scald crater (the
Scald Gate is its one breach).

1. **Cinder Spine** — keeps the ridge word; swaps the sea's root for the volcanic province's character.
2. **The Cauldron Ring** — the ring shape said outright; keeps the boiling-crater sense without the "Scald" string.
3. **Cratercrown** — one-word compound in the sheet register (Rimewall, Sootreach); the crown of rock around the crater.
