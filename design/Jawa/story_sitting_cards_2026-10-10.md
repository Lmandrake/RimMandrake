# Campaign story sitting — card deck for the morning (2026-10-10)

## Header

- **22 cards in 6 rounds** (4 per round, last round 2). Rounds 1–2 are the two clusters the owner named: the war-lab interior, and the order of the Cathedral, the Spire and the crater.
- **Covered:** every live GAP / TONE:GAP row in the gather except the v2 and flag-only rows listed under "Left for a later sitting" (spine-order gaps, war-lab interior, Spire/crater presentation, the tone gaps, and the purpose gaps for the Sump, the Tenant, the Deeps, the Hutt sites and the asteroid belt), plus 2 contradictions the gather did NOT list, found while preparing (cards 5 and 6). The gather's own contradictions are all already resolved or flag-only (see below), so no card repeats them. Counts are mine, not a re-measured census of the gather.
- **Hard rule for every round (say it on the first card and repeat per round):** NO NEW PLACES, NO NEW FACTIONS, NO NEW MECHANISMS. Every option below is built from an existing fragment; a "stitch" is a labelled join of two existing fragments, never new lore. Free text always overrides.
- **Dependencies:** marked `DEPENDS ON card N`. Cards 2, 3 need card 1; 11 needs card 5; 8 is easier after 7; 12 after 8. Ask in round order.
- **Contradictions in the gather that need NO card (housekeeping, owner already ruled):** G80 (the old Spire plot-prep doc still on disk: delete it, the Key ruling stands), G82/G83 (open-ended victory stands; cite it on any endings card), G84 (structural), G85/G87 (resolved), G86 (write the missing item file). Doing these does not need him.
- **Left for a later sitting, and why:** G40 sky ladder / repulsor spires, G43 moons / Force powers / water bottles, G45 Sarlacc and ant nests, G44 faction bases, G46 ship footprint. All are ruled v2 or are flags with no spine role; carding them would invite exactly the "new game" the owner fears. Also left: datafile and cut-scene TEXT (that is the Phase C knit, after the spine is ruled), and the Geonosian hive and droid enclaves (purposes already stated, no gap).
- **Tone cards** (3, 10, 14, 15, 16, 17) offer 2-3 short register lines drawn from existing registers, because a reveal with no tone is a fact, not a moment.
- **Two things the owner may want to know before round 1:** the war lab and the Scald's dark tower are already ruled as two different dungeons (`design/Jawa/worldbuilding/biomes/the_scald.md`, amendment 2026-09-07); the Spire is ruled as the Key (`design/Jawa/worldbuilding/ashfall_research_base.md` section 6).

## ROUND 1 — Inside the war lab (the densest cluster)

**Round-1 intro for the owner:** the lake ends, the lab is ripped open, the Spire's codes unlock the door. That much is ruled. Nothing says what the player finds inside or why it matters. These four cards fill the inside using only what is already written about it.

### Card 1 — What is the player there to get from the war lab?

**Cold context.** The lab sits under the propane lake "directly over the Impact Site where the first infection occurred … where the Assailants were first contained and studied … the study subjects here are live, trapped, and being studied" (`design/Jawa/worldbuilding/biomes/the_propane_lakes.md` section 8). The Spire note calls it "the campaign's antipodal endgame" (`ashfall_research_base.md` section 6). No source says what is inside or what the prize is.
**Options**
- **A. The records of how the Assailant was first contained and studied.** Buys: ties straight to the Cathedral's reveal "Where the infection started… and remains to this day" and to the datafile ladder, so reading the lab is the Antiquities ladder paying off. Costs: the endgame becomes a reading job unless card 3 gives it danger.
- **B. The living specimens themselves (they are the dungeon).** Buys: a dungeon with real stakes, uses the "live, trapped" wording as written. Costs: leaves the reason to go in at the endgame as "survive it" with no story prize.
- **C. Stitch: the lab holds what the Cathedral's "pyrrhic surgical strike" needs, so the Cradle's weapon-ask is answered here.** Joins the Cathedral wanting the Cradle as a strike against the Assailant (`design/Jawa/worldbuilding/dungeons_arc_spec.md` section 2.6) with the lab being the containment site. Buys: the Cathedral arc and the Spire arc finally converge on one place. Costs: makes the war lab carry two arcs, so it must not slip into a rails ending (open-ended victory still stands).
- **D. Leave the prize unstated on purpose; the lab pays out as loot and sights only.** Buys: no new commitment. Costs: the endgame beat stays a shell. 
**(Recommended): C** — it is the only option that connects three existing threads without inventing anything.

### Card 2 — What does the player feel and learn on first stepping into the lab?   `DEPENDS ON card 1`

**Cold context.** The gather marks entry to the lab "TONE: GAP". Existing registers to borrow: the Rakata register "victims and tyrants" with the tyranny kept for the flesh dungeon only (`ashfall_research_base.md` section 5); the catastrophe register for the Cathedral being found (`cathedral_concealment_arc_spec.md` section 6.1); and the Rakata commander's cold line "Rakata do not thank tools" (`dungeons_arc_spec.md` section 3.10).
**Options (pick the register line)**
- **A. "Preserved and still working": a clean, silent, powered place that was never meant to be opened.** Buys: matches "shielding intact, amazingly". Costs: low menace.
- **B. "Not empty": something in here notices you.** Buys: fits live, trapped subjects. Costs: collides with the register guard if it reads as Rakata cruelty, since tyranny must not surface before the flesh dungeon.
- **C. "Evidence": it is a laboratory, and the first thing shown is how carefully they studied.** Buys: keeps the tyranny guard and feeds the Assailant story. Costs: quieter than a climax.
**(Recommended): C if card 1 is A or C, B if card 1 is B** — tone should follow the prize.

### Card 3 — Do the trapped specimens stay a threat, or does the player decide their fate?   `DEPENDS ON card 1`

**Cold context.** Ruled: the specimens are alive and trapped (`the_propane_lakes.md` section 8), shielding came through the crater intact (same section), and a hard ban keeps live and dead Assailants distinct. The Scald's dark tower already holds a separate infestation, so this lab is not that fight.
**Options**
- **A. They are the dungeon's enemies, breaking free as the player moves through.** Buys: clear combat shape. Costs: "trapped" becomes background.
- **B. The player chooses whether the containment holds (stitch: intact shielding + live trapped subjects).** Buys: a real decision using only ruled facts. Costs: needs a mechanism for sealing, which the owner has not asked for; only take this if it can be done with the existing shield.
- **C. They stay behind glass and are never a fight.** Buys: cheap, safe. Costs: the lab loses danger.
**(Recommended): A** — no new mechanism, and B can be added later if he wants it.

### Card 4 — What does the crater cut-scene actually show?

**Cold context.** Ruled: "a 'cut scene.' It's big enough that everyone on the planet will notice in some way" (`the_propane_lakes.md` section 8 amendment). What it shows is a gap (G59, G71).
**Options**
- **A. The lake itself: ignition, the surface torn open, the lab revealed with shielding gleaming.** Buys: the most literal reading. Costs: requires the player to be near it, which "everyone on the planet notices" does not guarantee.
- **B. The planet reacting: every region's sky or ground notices, then a cut to the crater.** Buys: honours "everyone will notice". Costs: needs per-biome beats.
- **C. Stitch: the Empire in orbit noticing (they "watch from orbit"), then the crater.** Joins the Empire pressure (G4) with the cut-scene. Buys: raises the stakes of the pressure clock. Costs: ties the event to an Empire reaction that may start the detection timer.
**(Recommended): B** — it is the only one that satisfies the ruling for a player who is elsewhere.

## ROUND 2 — Cathedral, Spire and crater: what happens in what order

**Round-2 intro:** three beats with no stated order, plus two clashes the gather did not list. Do cards 5 and 6 first; they change what the others mean.

### Card 5 — Which place is "where the Assailant first struck"?

**Cold context.** Two ruled sources both claim it. The flesh complex deep in Umbra is "The Assailant's first-impact point … the one place the whole war traces back to" (`dungeons_arc_spec.md` section 2.1). The war lab is "directly over the Impact Site where the first infection occurred" (`the_propane_lakes.md` section 8). The Cathedral's reveal line "Where the infection started… and remains to this day" (`canon.yml` assailant_reveal_arc) could mean either.
**Options**
- **A. The flesh complex is the landing; the lab is where they later studied what came out.** Buys: both stay as written, "first infection" becomes "first contained". Costs: the lake's "Impact Site" wording needs a one-word amendment.
- **B. The lab is the landing; the flesh complex is where the infection spread and grew.** Buys: puts the endgame on the true origin. Costs: contradicts the flesh complex's "the one place the whole war traces back to" wording.
- **C. Both are parts of one impact zone, antipodal halves.** Stitch of the two texts. Buys: nothing needs deleting. Costs: they are on opposite sides of the planet, so it is a stretch.
**(Recommended): A** — least rewriting and keeps the register guard (flesh complex = the visceral reveal).

### Card 6 — Are the Spire and the Scald's dark tower the same building?

**Cold context.** The Spire "was the command center for the Rakatan surface level machine" and holds the codes (`ashfall_research_base.md` section 6). The dark tower in the Scald's crater lake is "the Rakatan's ground-based high command, carrying the control systems for the Rust Cathedral … a different dungeon from the propane-lakes war lab" (`the_scald.md`, amendment 2026-09-07). Two ruled "command" sites in the same region, never compared.
**Options**
- **A. Two sites, as written: the Spire holds the codes, the tower holds the Cathedral's control links.** Buys: no deletions, two dungeons. Costs: players will ask why the Rakata had two commands; needs one sentence.
- **B. One site: the tower is the Spire seen from the lake.** Buys: simpler map and plot. Costs: the Spire is "visible only occasionally over the mountains" and the tower sits in a lake, so one text must give way.
- **C. Two sites, with a one-line division: the Spire commanded the surface machine, the tower commanded the Cathedral.** A clarifying stitch. Buys: keeps both and explains them. Costs: assumes the Cathedral is a separate system from the surface machine, which the Cathedral doc supports (terramanufacture works).
**(Recommended): C** — A with the explanation players will need.

### Card 7 — Does the Spire come before, after, or independent of the Cathedral thaw?

**Cold context.** The gather finds "no stated dependency" (G16). Ruled only that the Cathedral cannot grant the codes (`ashfall_research_base.md` section 6).
**Options**
- **A. Independent: the player can do either first.** Buys: the wander-through stays primary. Costs: two timelines to test.
- **B. Soft order: the Cathedral's trust tells you the Spire exists (stitch: Cathedral stage VOUCHED + the Spire as a hinted place).** Buys: gives the Cathedral arc a payoff beyond the reveal. Costs: the Cathedral cannot give the codes but can point; a player who skips it must find the Spire another way.
- **C. Spire first, always (nothing else shows it).** Buys: a clear spine. Costs: punishes wanderers; violates the optional-digging rule.
**(Recommended): A** — keeps the surface the star; B can be layered on later.

### Card 8 — Does the crater event wait for the Spire, or can the lake end first?   `Easier after card 7`

**Cold context.** Two-key ruling: "the lake's ending opens the ground; the Spire's codes open the door" (`the_propane_lakes.md` section 8 amendment). The lake ends by player routes OR an automated fallback. So the lab can open with no way in.
**Options**
- **A. Independent: the lake can end any time; the door waits for the codes.** Buys: exactly as ruled. Costs: a player may stand at an open crater with no way in for a long time.
- **B. The automated fallback only fires once the player has reached the Spire.** Buys: removes the stranded-at-the-door case. Costs: changes the "if they fail to, it may happen anyway" ruling.
- **C. Independent, and the crater cut-scene points toward the Spire.** Stitch: cut-scene (card 4) + the Spire's landmark. Buys: no wasted wait; pairs with card 9. Costs: needs a line in the cut-scene.
**(Recommended): C** — honours the ruling and solves the stranded case.

## ROUND 3 — Reveals, Cradle ask and the clock before the Reclamation

### Card 9 — How does the player learn the lake has opened?

**Cold context.** The cut-scene covers a nearby player; "presentation beyond the cut-scene" is a gap (G62). The narrator "is free to reference history the player has not yet learned" (`canon.yml` narrator). The Utinni already receives the Rakatan band (`cathedral_concealment_arc_spec.md`, CARD A1).
**Options**
- **A. The narrator says so, once.** Buys: cheap, reliable. Costs: tells, doesn't show.
- **B. Stitch: the Utinni "feels" it on the Rakatan band (A1) and the ship reacts.** Buys: in-fiction, uses the mind the player already has. Costs: relies on the player having the transponder moment first.
- **C. A map change only (new feature on the planet map).** Buys: the quietest. Costs: easy to miss.
**(Recommended): B with A as fallback** (if the band has not been heard, the narrator speaks).

### Card 10 — What is the moment of taking the Spire's codes like?   `DEPENDS ON card 6 only if the answer is "one site"`

**Cold context.** Fact ruled (the codes live on an isolated system in the Spire core); the moment is a gap (G62). Registers on hand: the Reclamation's "a price the player should see coming"; the Testament's "gratitude without warmth"; the Rakata commander's "Rakata do not thank tools".
**Options (register lines)**
- **A. "Cold access": a machine that does not care who holds it.** Buys: matches the Rakata register. Costs: low emotion.
- **B. "Keys to something sleeping": the codes read like a warning.** Buys: sets up the lab. Costs: telegraphs too much if the lake is not open yet.
- **C. "Nothing happens": the codes just transfer.** Buys: restraint. Costs: anticlimax.
**(Recommended): A.**

### Card 11 — What exactly does the Cathedral want the Cradle to strike?   `DEPENDS ON card 5`

**Cold context.** The Cathedral wants the Cradle "as its pyrrhic surgical strike against the Assailant", and releasing that knowledge to the Hutts needs a deal protecting the droids (`dungeons_arc_spec.md` section 2.6). The target is not named.
**Options**
- **A. The flesh complex (the first-impact point).** Buys: matches the Cathedral's "where it started". Costs: the strike lands in Umbra, far from the Spire/lab line.
- **B. The war lab (the place the specimens still live).** Buys: ends the two arcs at one door. Costs: only if card 5 says the lab is the landing.
- **C. Left to the player's choice (they pick which one the Cradle hits).** Buys: wanderer-friendly. Costs: doubles the aftermath to write.
**(Recommended): follows card 5** — A if flesh complex, B if lab.

### Card 12 — Does the Reclamation come before or after the crater?   `DEPENDS ON card 8`

**Cold context.** No stated order (G16). The Reclamation is "late game, one concentrated event" (`09_arcs_dungeons_quests.md` section 2b); the crater is the lab's door.
**Options**
- **A. Independent: either can come first.** Buys: open play. Costs: two late-game orderings to support.
- **B. The Reclamation comes last in the main line; the crater is before it.** Buys: a legible spine. Costs: the crater may never fire if the player delays and the fallback timer outruns the Reclamation.
- **C. The crater can fire during the Reclamation, as a second crisis.** Stitch of two ruled events. Buys: the climax. Costs: stacks two planet-wide crises.
**(Recommended): A.**
