# Canon drain, 2026-11-26 — the record (CANON_DRAIN_1)

A dated record. Do not edit it after the drain; git holds the deleted sources.

## What this drain does

- Scope of group 1: the 14 undated `design/Jawa` docs whose first line was a SUPERSEDED banner that named
  `design/Jawa/reconciled_lore/NN` as the live version of their content. `reconciled_lore` became the authority at THE LORE SWITCH (841fe1b085,
  2026-08-30). Delete-don't-supersede (owner, 2026-09-09) says the dead source goes.
- Method (the owner's two blind arms): arm 1 (the bundle worker) writes, per source, the live facts that are absent
  from `reconciled_lore` and from the live design docs, and the stale lines it finds in `reconciled_lore`. Arm 1
  writes its list here BEFORE arm 2 answers. Arm 2 (GPT through `src/RimMandrake/Utils/gpt_consult.py`) gets the same
  sources and the same question, and never sees arm 1's list. The owner's rule is "cut only where both arms
  agree", so no fact that either arm calls live is lost. A fact that both arms name is folded into its live home. A
  fact that only one arm names is copied verbatim into "Disputed" below, with the arm that named it: this record keeps
  it, and canon does not take it without a second arm. Then the source is deleted, and the references in undated live
  docs are repaired. Dated records keep their citations.
- A stale line in `reconciled_lore` that an arm names is fixed only after the worker reads the cited live text.
- "Live" means: still true under the rulings of today. A proposal that was never ruled is not a live fact, and git
  keeps it.

## Arm 2 answer, batch 1 (verbatim)

#### design/Jawa/concept.md

A. ABSENT LIVE FACTS

- Weapons and loot must offer “qualitative interest, not power creep.”
- Enemy escalation must avoid “raid-point bloat,” as well as stat inflation.
- Player automation that “trivializes hauling” is forbidden.
- Tile-water forms and collection routes: “surface” water is pumpable; “groundwater” requires a well and hauling to a tub; deep desert/salt flats can have “none.”
- The specified onboard reservoir is a “minifiable Water Butt (100-cap),” supplemented by bottled water in cargo.
- Android water denial includes deliberately poisoning/polluting holdings; fouled water gives “tox buildup instead of relief.”
- Captured android holdings may be temporarily cleansed through “Biotech cleanup”; pursuit remains the limiting timer.
- Live-map enrichment tooling is “an authoring/GM tool only, never in-fiction player power.”

B. STALE LINES IN THE SUCCESSOR

- none

#### design/Jawa/droid_system_spec.md

A. ABSENT LIVE FACTS

- Transient stun “may not even fall over” and “resumes context.”
- Mindless droids perform “low-level work only.”
- Programmable-tier capabilities include “machines, repair, plants, self-defense.”
- Sapient droids can be “savants” and form bonds “including with non-sapient droids.”
- Sapients may rebel when denied “maintenance and recreation.”
- Maintenance includes “oil baths and cleaning.”
- Charging visuals include “minor lightning” and a “pulsing luminous nimbus.”
- Droids are “hardened against outside signals”; a “large dish-blast area disable” is the sole ranged electronic-warfare concession.
- Most factions regard deformatting as “lobotomy-in-illness”: unethical, but sometimes necessary.
