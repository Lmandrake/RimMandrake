## Framing

The biome's final invented fauna set is **16 names** (11 roster + 5 ratified). Current
distribution: **2 one-syllable / 12 two-syllable / 2 three-syllable** (one-syll: skreth,
grolth; three-syll: ollareth, gorrameth; everything else two). Rule 7's target — roughly a
fifth / half / a quarter — puts 16 names at about **3 / 8–9 / 4**. The gap is therefore
small: the two-syllable wall is 10 roster names, but only **3 renames** are needed to
reach 3 / 9 / 4, and renaming more would churn names that already serve. I propose exactly
three: one 2→1 and two 2→3, chosen where the new length also fits the creature — the
sealed bead takes the single held syllable, the slung grazer and the ribbon-plume bird
take the trailing three. `thornbug` is the owner's own word and is not a candidate.
Slime-law check (rule 8): **no roster creature is slime/goo/gel-bodied** — glavoth is a
sap-sucker that sets in hard lacquer (amber, not goo), drommath a taut inflating sac,
mulleth soft-bodied but a limbed grazer — so the law binds none of the 11; the one
proposed monosyllable is long-vowel anyway, so even a goo reading of glavoth is satisfied.
