## TIER 1 — founding-doc contradictions (owner ruling required; nothing rewritten)

1. ⭐ **THE SCALD OUTFLOW** — `the_one_map.md` (owner, 2026-08-18): the Scald
   is a perched lake that SPILLS **the planet's largest river**. `the_scald.md`
   (owner, 2026-09-07, via the world-def sea table): terminal pan, eight rivers
   in, none leave. Two owner rulings, thirteen months of sheets built on the
   second. **The single most consequential card in this review** — the answer
   re-plumbs the dayside.
2. **RIVER-END ABSOLUTES** [x3]: world-def/wasteland "EVERY river dies in a
   sealed salt plain" vs the Miasma's rivers-reach-the-Grey-Sea, the Scald's
   eight, the Greentide's graves. One hierarchy ruling amends three docs.
3. **SHOKKWEAVE SOLE-SOURCE VIOLATED**: `the_propane_lakes.md` §4/§7 — the
   Blizzarisk spins "hyperweave webs… hyperweave silk." Predates the Webwork
   ruling. Card: rename its silk or cut it.
4. **VAPOR FATE** [x2]: `terminator_sea.md` "vapour carried dayward… never
   condenses" vs the entire dayside dew/fog economy (Weeping Stones,
   shrubland) that depends on it condensing. Fix is one word: "never condenses
   *as rain*."
5. **GROWTH-LAW EXEMPTIONS**: R-H3 rules ALL plants grow freakishly; R-H2b
   names the terminator seam as the ONE exemption — but six sheets
   (terminator_sea, nightside_ice, wasteland, arid_shrubland, dune_sea,
   deep_desert) run slow growth, mostly without naming an exemption. Card:
   rewrite R-H2b as an explicit exemption list.
6. **WEEPING STONES RAIN** [x2]: §10b's "rare gentle rain" vs R-H1
   (rain only at altitude) and its own §6 rain ban. Card: fog-drizzle
   carve-out (like slime rain) or strike the sentence.
7. **R-H4 UNAMENDED**: "here it never rains" predates the BlackRain ruling;
   the carve-out was ruled but never written into the rule.
8. **R-H9's FLOODS**: the ash-churn tar mechanism needs frequent violent
   floods; the measured Pyrelands has rain 0 on 207/226 and 9 river tiles.
   Card: site the churn-water (flood pulses? the Cracked Lands' walls?) or
   move the making upstream.
9. **ASSAILANT-AUTHORSHIP UNGATED**: the Rot, the Slime and the Contagion
   state bioweapon origin in unpartitioned prose with no player-facing ban;
   the Scarlands has the ban. Card: copy the ban to all three.
10. **ONE_MAP DECAY**: its Scald-rivers-carry-the-jungles paragraph predates
    the ratified Greentide/Webwork/Fever Wood assignments; its own
    water-elevation rule conflicts with its own perched-Scald. Supersession
    lines owed either way rules #1 goes.
11. **DESERT.MD STALE BAN**: "explosive growth belongs to the Pyrelands" vs
    the world-wide EXPLOSIVE_PLANT_GROWTH ruling.

## TIER 2 — sheet-vs-sheet (highest first)

12. **Sickest air ×3** [x3]: Greentide (50d), Webwork (35d), Miasma (15d) all
    claim it. Miasma's is true; Webwork's has no in-sheet mechanism at all.
13. **The Rot's false superlative**: §0 "the mod's fastest disease clock (35)"
    — the Miasma's 15 is the same donor mod.
14. **FUEL OVERSUPPLY** [x2]: ~12 distinct fuel economies (propane, tar,
    sap-chemfuel, seep-oils, tree liquors, blastpod, tholins, venomvine,
    Glower, plant-vault ore, hydrocarbon flora, wick-plants). Fuel scarcity
    survives nowhere trade reaches; the Propane premium and deep-desert
    fire-as-treasure both collapse. Card: a fuel-niche doctrine — accept
    cheap, tier by grade/transportability, or cut sources.
15. **Wood monopoly stale** [x2]: blade-sheet "only large workable non-metal
    material on the planet" vs greenwood/hardwood, silverbole, mushroom-wood,
    bog-timber, crystal wood, kelp lattice.

    deep_desert/fall_line's "superrotating wind" over overlapping arcs — dune
    grain and yardang grain cannot both hold; superrotation was never ruled.
