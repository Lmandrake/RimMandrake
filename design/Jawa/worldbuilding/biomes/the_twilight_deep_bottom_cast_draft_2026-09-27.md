## 1. The society in one paragraph

Three lamplit holdings stand on one long bank where a channel's eddy meets a cluster of
wells — each holding a per-tile Inhabited place with a **chart name**, because on a sea
whose light walks weekly, the only fixed thing about an address is what the well-keeper
wrote it down as. **Nine-Well Bank** is the seat: the chart house, the lamp-wright's
workshop, the child underfoot. **Low Lantern** is the working eddy downstream: the
Compact's tended weir, the stake-line, the silt farm. **Farwake** is one dome apart,
upslope on high dry ground, where the two who watch the sky live. Between them they are
not a town — they are a *practice*: everything the Compact knows about living under a
shedding sky and beside a river that looks dry is visible in what these seven do every
day, which is exactly how the player is meant to learn it. Nobody lectures. The
well-keeper refuses a sale, the widow drives a stake, the child will not chase her light
past a certain lamp — and each refusal, each chore, each swerve is a mechanism wearing a
face. Ban 4 is absolute and engine-enforced (`raidsForbidden true`): no resident fights
the player, sanctions anyone with force, or guards anything with a gun. The Compact's
teeth are prices, stock and silence, never violence.

All seven names below are **invented exotic words, franchise-free** (Q11a — "Star Wars
style" naming is not Star Wars IP), collision-checked against `src/` and `design/`
2026-09-27, all clean. The *races* are campaign content (`RSW_`, Utinni layer, patched on
by `MayRequire` exactly as the Hold's cast is today — content doc §9.4); the houses,
holdings and every mechanism taught are `RM_` and stand alone in the free tier.

## 2. The cast — seven residents

Each entry: who they are (in the biome's voice) · **teaches** (mechanism + how it lands
in play) · **trades or asks** · **hook**. The roles are §9.3's, given names, houses and
their teaching duty; where §9.3's prose survives, it survives on purpose.

### 2.1 Vhessune, the well-keeper — Nine-Well Bank — teaches LEASE PRACTICE

Nautolan, f, 50s. She reads the skylights and keeps the chart of which wells are opening
and which closing, in lamp-black on vaal-green vellum; she has been wrong once in thirty
years and it cost a family its plot. She prices rights, and she will not sell one on a
well she thinks the gardener is closing — and will not say why.

- **Teaches: lease practice** (light-economy pass §4), by **trade with a refusal built
  in**. She sells `RM_SkylightRight` (priced by the pallu count × her read of the well's
  remaining days) and `RM_WellChart` (her forecast, accurate ~a week, then a picture of a
  sea that no longer exists). The whole tenancy grammar lands through her counter: a
  young well is dear, a mid-life well is fair, **a waning well she will not sell at all**
  — the refusal IS the free hint the player didn't pay for, and the first time they buy
  anyway from ignorance and watch the well close, they understand every rule at once. On
  a leased well's death she offers **first refusal on the next to open** — the renewal
  loop that makes tenancy a weekly visit, which is how the rest of the cast gets met.
- **Trades / asks:** rights and charts for silver; asks nothing — her ledger does the
  asking.
- **Hook:** Sellowen quietly holds one skylight right she does not know he holds — the
  one crack in a ledger that has been right for thirty years but once.

### 2.2 Tharukel, the gardener-warden — Farwake — teaches THE SKY (drift, panes, lid-dark)

Selkath, m, 60s. The one whose work is the roof: he watches the lanternwhale and logs its
passes; he has never touched it; the Compact's law about the gardener is his to keep and
he has decided it is the only law that matters. He knows the shadow before it falls and
stands outside to watch it every time.

- **Teaches: the gardener event family and its dangers** (danger pass D1/D9, light pass
  §5) — not one of the four mandatory mechanisms, but the coherence the other six stand
  on — by **warning made visible**. His behaviour is a forecast with a face: hours before
  a pass he is outside, still, facing up; before a lid-dark he lights every lamp at
  Farwake and the child is kept in at Nine-Well Bank. A player who reads him gets the
  sky-read for free; a letter names him the first time (*"Tharukel is watching the
  roof"*), and after that he IS the letter.
- **Trades / asks:** nothing for sale. He asks one thing, once, of any diver who stays: a
  count of panes on their deck after a heavy shed — his log wants the number, and the
  asking teaches D8 (clear your deck) without a tutorial line.
- **Hook:** on a fraction of the passes he watches, a well closes in front of everyone —
  which is Vhessune's ledger moving. The two of them have not needed to speak about it in
  twenty years.

### 2.3 Maruvenn, the net-widow — Low Lantern — teaches STAKE-LINES

Quarren, f, 40s. She fishes the shaft alone since her partner walked into the channel to
save a net. She keeps the ford stones repaired and adds one every year, and she will tell
a diver exactly where the edge is, once, and never again.

- **Teaches: the stake-line** (river pass §4.3) — by **one warning, then shown example
  forever**. Her single warning is verbatim design: *the stakes stand where the surge
  reaches, not where the calm ends.* After that the teaching is her chores, visible
  daily: she re-drives stakes along Low Lantern's line (the maintenance job the player
  will owe on their own line), and the ground itself backs her — **thessmoss stops dead
  at her stakes**, so the turf draws the same boundary the lamps do. Before an
  undersurge she is the first mover: crews off the inner bank, and a player who copies
  her keeps their stock.
- **Trades / asks:** asks for lattice-timber for stakes now and then, paid in
  hand-picked murrol. Will not sell her ford stones' positions; she shows them, once.
- **Hook:** her partner ended past the LAST weir — the one place the river still reaches
  the sink (river pass Q2). Dorrhul's weir downstream has caught three people since. It
  did not exist yet, the year it mattered.

### 2.4 Dorrhul, the weir-keeper — Low Lantern — teaches THE WEIR FORM

Herglic, m, 60s. Broad, slow, patient as the eddy he works. The bank-farmer of the old
chart, grown into the works around his plot: he hates tikkarr and will not kill one, and
he measures a good year in ghallowyn rings and hopper-loads.

- **Teaches: the weir form** (river pass §4) — by **shown example plus one small quest**.
  The Compact's weir at Low Lantern pre-exists, tended, on the eddy — the player learns
  the form by looking, never by being told: lattice-timber and net-cord across the
  eddy-margin, a hopper he empties daily (veil-fall, panes, drifted goods, murrol slow
  enough to pick up by hand), a silt-trap behind it and the richest sown bank on the
  seabed around that. The quest is the Compact's own practice handed over as a job:
  *"drop the bundle in upstream; collect it at my weir"* — one errand that teaches
  drift, arrest and the whole freight economy in five minutes. **His weir never
  breaches, because it is tended** — and that sentence, in his inspect text, is the
  breach mechanic taught before it ever fires on the player's own works.
- **Trades / asks:** sells weir-catch (pane material, returned drift, bank produce) and
  buys net-cord; asks for a dredge hand when the trap clogs — doing the job once
  teaches it.
- **Hook:** everything his weir arrests is wealth the drifting sky and river taxed off
  someone — including, sometimes, the player's own lost stock, sold back at a fair
  price with no apology. The river is a deed; he is its clerk. Maruvenn's stakes are
  what keep his farm out of the river's ledger.

### 2.5 Bimmelo, the child — Nine-Well Bank — teaches THE VAULISK TELL

Gungan, 9. The waelune-chaser; the one who knows where the piip are thickest. The
player's first sight of a Compact person on the floor should be a child with a light in
each hand, because *"It's a pretty ocean."*

- **Teaches: the vaulisk tell** (danger pass D4) — by **shown example the player must
  notice**. Every Compact child is taught the tell before they are taught to swim a net:
  *a lamp with no piip around it is not a lamp.* Bimmelo chases her waelune anywhere on
  the bank — and swerves, every time, wide around one steady gold light out in the dark,
  without breaking stride and without explaining. The full tell is readable off the thing
  she avoids: no piip sparks, no breathing pulse (every living lamp in this sea
  breathes; the counterfeit is steady), thessmoss dead in a ring. A player who follows
  the child learns it free; a player who sends a pawn to harvest "that hoolimbre we
  didn't plant" learns it the other way. Optional micro-quest: her waelune rolls into
  the dark and she will not go after it — the retrieval route passes the false lamp,
  and she says the tell out loud, once, the way it was said to her.
- **Trades / asks:** trades piip-sightings and the best waelune drifts for any small
  bright thing; asks the player's children (if any) to come chase lights.
- **Hook:** Sellowen will not buy a vaulisk lure-organ while she is in the shop — the
  Compact will not have the lamp that never breathes in their houses, and the
  lamp-wright honours it hardest where the child can see.

### 2.6 Sellowen, the lamp-wright — Nine-Well Bank — teaches THE LIGHT TRADE (and the sanction ladder)

Mon Calamari, m, 30s. Makes aluun-pane lanterns and lamp-bladder strings; the bottom's
dealer. Sells light, pigment and, quietly, one skylight right the well-keeper does not
know he holds.

- **Teaches: the light economy's hard edges** (light pass §2.2, §4.3) — by **trade whose
  stock IS the lesson**. He is where `RM_TetherChain` comes from, in ones, slowly — the
  constellation is exactly as big as his ledger likes you, so the hard cap arrives as a
  relationship, not a rule. The **sanction ladder lands through him**: poach a claimed
  well and his prices rise; persist and the chains, charts and techprints stop — the
  lamps you own keep burning, the constellation just stops growing. And outside his door
  stands the Compact's own answer to the suulk, labelled in its inspect string: **a
  cheap lit decoy stake, alone at the edge of the holding** — shown example, free to
  copy, the night the grazer first comes for the player's gold.
- **Trades / asks:** chains, lamps, panes, vaal-green and lamp-black, and — at good
  standing — the sun-sphere's techprints, the dearest thing the Compact sells and the
  end of his own lease business, priced like he knows.
- **Hook:** his quiet skylight right is keyed to a well Vhessune's chart says is
  mid-life. If her once-in-thirty-years wrongness has a second coming, it is this well
  — a story the sitting can arm or disarm (§For the sitting, decision 4).

### 2.7 Aldra Sohm, the diver who stayed — Farwake — teaches WHAT THE COMPACT IS (optional)

Human, f, 40s. An outlander who came down on a ship years ago and never went back up; the
bridge between the player and the cast, and the one who says out loud what the others
will not: that the Compact keeps the last ordinary sea, and the surface has no idea.

- **Teaches: the frame** — by **being the quest-giver voice the Compact refuses to be**.
  The others teach by chore and refusal; she translates. She is the one who can say
  "watch the child", "buy the chart before the plot", "his weir has caught three
  people" — hint text with a house. If any bottom quest needs a giver who explains
  stakes plainly, it is hers.
- **Trades / asks:** nothing; she asks the player, early, what year it is on the
  surface, and does not much care about the answer.
- **Hook:** the picked wreck out in the dark is her ship. The Compact stripped it over
  years, with her blessing — *"cleaned up most of the scavenge"* with a face and a
  consent attached. She kept one hull-lamp; it burns in Farwake's window, the one
  electric light on the sea floor.

## 3. Mechanism coverage table

The four mechanisms the owner's constraint requires, each with a primary teacher and a
reinforcer — no mechanism hangs on one scene:

| mechanism | primary teacher | delivery | reinforced by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **the weir form** (river pass §4) | Dorrhul | shown example (the tended pre-built weir) + the upstream-drop errand quest | Maruvenn — weirs catch people too; her loss predates his weir |
| **stake-lines** (river pass §4.3, content §2.2) | Maruvenn | one warning, then daily re-driving shown; thessmoss draws her line | Dorrhul — a lapsed stake-line is the breach's first domino, said in his inspect text |
| **the vaulisk tell** (danger pass D4) | Bimmelo | shown example (the swerve) + optional retrieval micro-quest; the tell said aloud once | Sellowen — will not buy the never-breathing lamp; the tell as household law |
| **lease practice** (light pass §4) | Vhessune | trade with the refusal built in; first-refusal renewal; aging charts | Sellowen — the sanction ladder felt as stock and prices; the chain trickle |

Also carried, beyond the required four: the sky economy and D1/D8/D9 (Tharukel, warning
behaviour), the suulk decoy (Sellowen, shown example), the freight economy (Dorrhul's
errand), the undersurge tells (Maruvenn first mover), and the ark reveal (Aldra Sohm).
Every delivery is a chore, a refusal, a price or an errand — **no resident is a combat
encounter, no teaching requires one, and nothing above strains ban 4.**
