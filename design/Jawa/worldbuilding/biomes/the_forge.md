## 1. What it is

One mountain, three zones, and the only vertical skyline in the Dune Sea: **tall,
dark foundry towers rising defiantly against the sun**, streaming vent-steam
like banners, visible for a hundred kilometers. Below them the red seams and the
flows; around them the ash skirts; above them, improbably, **herds** — vast
placid gas-grazers drifting in the vapor columns. The mountain rains on itself
in scalding bursts, mosses drink in the seconds between fall and flash, and at
the skirts a ring of dead red creep marks where the Contagion tested the heat
again, and died again, as it always does.

## 2. Planetary position

Deep-to-mid dayside (sun +51..+64) × **the planet's one volcanic anomaly** — and
a theological singularity: on a tidally locked world every joule belongs to the
star, **except here. Vulcanism is the one heat that owes nothing to the sun.**

## 3. Driving forces

- **Native fire.** The melt rises on its own account; the Forge outshines the
  sky from below — the only place on Ash'karr where it can.
- **The closed rain.** The massif is its own weather engine: vent-steam climbs
  two kilometers, condenses at the cold summit, and falls as **sudden violent
  boiling rain that flashes back to steam on the rock within seconds** (owner)
  — the same water, cycling forever, the only rain in the Dune Sea. It scalds
  (the scald damage type, reused from the Greentide kit).
- **The vapor columns** — complex chemical updrafts off the vents and melt:
  the pasture of the sky herds (§4).
- 🔴 **Nothing lives in the lava** (owner, contrary to the rumors) — **but
  there is movement there**: old mechanoid-type machines, glowing with the
  same heat as the liquid around them, doing something unrevealed (§6 ban).

## 4. How the biology adapted

**The admission test: lives in the flash-interval, or in the sky.** Ground
fauna stays heat-adapted-and-few — the Forge is the biome nearest pure
geology; its life is overhead or between raindrops.

- **The flash-interval flora** (owner): beautiful heat-resistant mosses, and
  shrubs and even trees of shocking tolerance, all drinking in the seconds
  the boiling rain exists as liquid. Signature: **fireweed** — whose
  heat-proof fiber is the source material of the planet's heat-survival gear
  economy: the desert kit, the Greentide kit, and the only clothing that
  lets you walk the Forge itself. The hottest place on Ash'karr grows the
  thing that lets you endure everywhere else.
- **The sky herds** (owner): aerial creatures floating in the turbulent air
  above vents and lava, absorbing and metabolizing the complex chemical
  vapors —
  - **Fumeriders** (the Alpha Animals Aerofleet, renamed — recorded for the
    roster): drifting jelly-fleets in the columns.
  - ⭐ **The Beldons** — actual Star Wars canon (Bespin's colossal
    gas-grazers; icon carve-out applies in full): vast, placid, drifting
    over the melt — **and they produce tibanna gas** (§7, §8 — the ruling
    that reshapes the campaign).
  - **The fleet fliers** — small darting things that race in, eat fireweed,
    and retreat before they perish.
- **The Contagion's corpse-ring** (owner): the heat is too terrible even for
  the Contagion — **though it will often push into this place and die,
  always testing.** The skirts carry a ring of dead red creep, always
  fresh: the one ground the weapon cannot take, marked by its own failed
  invasions. Ambient die-off event; the ring is the lore.

## 5. Always true

- The towers stand whole; the machines in the melt still move; the quotas
  are still being run for no one.
- The rain boils, falls, and flashes; the flora lives between.
- The herds drift the columns; the tibanna is in the beldons.
- The geysers are densest here and thin with distance (the vapor law's
  epicenter).
- The Contagion's dead ring is always fresh at the skirts.
- Two faiths hold the mountain and do not correct each other (§8).
- The Empire holds the gas (§8), and everyone's ammunition knows it.

## 6. Never true — 🔴 HARD BANS (linter-checkable)

1. 🔴 **Nothing lives in the lava** — no lava-native creature def, ever; the
   moving machines are machines.
2. 🔴 **What the lava-machines are doing is never revealed** — no def, text,
   or quest resolves it (the Cathedral's deep-drill law, applied).
3. 🔴 **No furred, chilled, or lush-register fauna** — heat-adapted and few.
4. 🔴 **No taking of Forge fire in Tribes canon** — Pyrelands fire is
   collected; Forge fire is only witnessed (§8); a Tribes text that carries
   it away violates the faith as ruled.
5. 🔴 **No tibanna source but the beldons** — no mineral or synthetic route;
   the herds are the monopoly's whole basis.
6. 🔴 **No ordinary rain** — the closed boiling cycle only.
7. 🔴 **No vanilla-Earth flora or fauna** (standard eviction).

## 7. Uniquely available

- ⭐ **Tibanna gas** — the blaster gas, harvested from the beldon herds
  (canon trait, ratified): the planet's weapons-gas source, full stop —
  and currently not yours (§8).
- ⭐ **Fireweed fiber** — the heat-gear economy's raw material.
- **Free heat** — geothermal industry: smelting, kilns, power with no fuel
  bill; the one place fire costs nothing.
- **Obsidian and volcanic glass** — the §B3 terrain family as materials.
- **The vents** — the vapor law's richest taps.
- **The towers** — forge-tech salvage for whoever survives a vertical
  dungeon over an open melt (§8).

## 8. Inhabited objects

- ⭐ **The foundry towers** (owner): the ancients FORGED with all this power
  and heat and mineral wealth — automated foundries **sunk deep into the
  magma below and yet remaining whole**, drawn up into tall dark towers
  against the sun. **Another piece of the Rust Cathedral's legacy** — §GM:
  the smeltery of the god-project (the Cathedral the mind, the Propane
  Lakes' works the far end, the Forge the metals): asteroid mass came down
  here to be worked and fed to the assembly. The glowing tenders in the
  melt still run it. Tower interiors are vertical dungeons: floor after
  floor of forge-works over open melt, heat as the wall, the tenders as the
  garrison.
- ⭐ **The Imperial gas operation** (owner, ratified): **the Empire holds
  the tibanna harvest over the heads of all** — a garrisoned gas-mining
  station working the herds, metering the one thing every blaster needs,
  **watching their ammunition slowly run out with a smart grin. The
  situation must resolve soon, or those who resist will have no guns to
  speak back.** A campaign clock, not just a location: the embargo is the
  Empire's quietest weapon and the resistance's loudest deadline
  (`TIBANNA_EMBARGO_PLOT_1`). Doctrine-consistent: Allow-supplied, vertical
  power — they don't need the Forge; they need everyone else not to have
  it.
- ⭐ **Two faiths, one mountain, no correction**: the Jawa sacred geography
  sees **Sh'kaar's forge** — the war-sun's anvil, his LavaLake/LavaCrater
  sites, the AncientHeatVent's "right kind of joke." The Deep Desert Tribes
  see **the unstolen fire** — the one flame older than the theft, owing
  nothing to the thief. Their precept, the flame harvest's inverse:
  **Pyrelands fire is collected; Forge fire is only witnessed. The fire
  that owes nothing may not be taken.** Pilgrims climb to see it once. Two
  churches, each certain, quietly disagreeing forever.
- **The corpse-ring** at the skirts; **geyser fields** thinning with
  distance; obsidian quarries; the Tribes' witness-trail up the ash.

## 9. Artistic theme

**"Dark towers against the sun, herds grazing the smoke."**

- **Light:** the doubled furnace — hard white sun above, red seams below;
  tower silhouettes black against both; steam banners catching the glare.
- **Palette:** basalt black, obsidian gloss, seam-red, ash grey, fireweed's
  defiant green in the flash-zones, beldon amber drifting over everything.
- **Silhouette language:** the planet's only verticals — cones, towers,
  steam columns; the herds' soft enormous rounds against hard geometry.
- **Motion:** drift above (herds, steam), pulse below (rain-flash, seam
  glow), and in the melt, slow deliberate movement that is not alive.
- **Sound:** vent-roar and geyser-cough, the boiling rain's hiss-and-flash,
  tower harmonics in the updrafts — and beneath it, felt more than heard,
  the working of machines under the melt.
