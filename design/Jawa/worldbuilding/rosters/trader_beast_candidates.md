# Trader-beast candidates — Star Wars herd/pack/beast-of-burden fauna

Owner ruling, 2026-09-10 sitting: "There are MANY Star Wars herd animals that
you can easily search the web for to put as Trader-beasts." This is a
**PROPOSAL** for the owner's pick — nothing here is built, no defs, no art.
Every fact below is web-sourced (link given per row); nothing is invented.

**Already in a cast or the homeless roster — do NOT re-propose these**
(checked against `design/Jawa/worldbuilding/review/round2/decisions_propagated.json`):
Bantha, Ronto, Eopie, Falumpaset, Dalgo, Dewback, FeralNerf/Nerf, FrilledGorg,
Gorg, LongtailGorg, Kybuck, Shaak, Orray, Kaadu, Veermok, Blurrg,
`homeless:Behemoth` (the existing "huuuge trader pack animal," already IN),
`homeless:AA_Behemoth` (unrelated 16-square Forsaken Crags creature —
name-collision warned about in `biome_findings.md` line 258; do not confuse
the two), JRWGorgonops, RSW_ShaleGorger, AA_EngorgedTentacularAberration.

Columns: **size vs Bantha** is qualitative from the cited source, not a stat
conversion. **Desert-plausible** judges the species' native biome against
this campaign's desert-world admission rules, not whether it *could* be
reskinned. **Recognizability** is icon (clearly on-screen/named in a film or
show) vs obscure (game/RPG/comic-only or background-only).

## Recommendation

If the owner wants exactly one or two adds without touching the biome-ban
rules: **Happabore** and **Luggabeast** are the strongest picks — both are
canon, both are on-screen desert-scavenger-world pack beasts from a Jakku
setting that already reads like a cousin of Ash'karr, and neither collides
with anything already cast. **Jerba** and **Voraxx** are the next-best desert
herd/mount variety without duplicating the existing Bantha/Ronto/Eopie/Dewback
set. Everything in Tier 3 is here because the owner asked for the full "MANY"
search, not because it clears the desert-world admission rules as-is.
