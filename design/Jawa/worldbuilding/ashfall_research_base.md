## 1. What it is

The Ascendant Helix's live study of the weapon at open throttle — a compound on a
Contagion peak in the Ashfall Range, upslope of the observation posts at the valley
mouths (`the_contagion.md` §8). Not a lab improvised in a panic like the war lab
under the propane lake: this one is deliberate, funded, polite, and meticulously
recorded, which is the Helix horror register (`INHABITED_CAST_HELIX.md` — "competent,
polite, slow, and keep excellent records"). The cruellest ground on the dayside,
kept tidy.

**The program is the Overdrive** (owner, `OCULAR_OVERDRIVE_SITE_1`, verbatim in
intent): the base's datafiles show the Helix **actively attempting to modify the
Contagion to make it more robust and productive** — to focus its creativity where
they want it — **at the risk of unleashing it across the planet.** The UV cage
(`the_contagion.md` §3) is the planet's only quarantine, and the Overdrive program
is an attempt to breed something the cage cannot hold.

## 2. The plot the base carries (owner, verbatim in intent — RULED)

One line of plot only, by the owner's instruction (`the_contagion.md` §8):

**The reveal of the real relationship between the Helix and the Assailants**: how
the Helix "adopted" Rakatan genetics to adopt *themselves* into that family line, in
an attempt to control the old technology in the Cathedral — a failure that gave them
intelligence and cruelty in equal parts.

How it braids with standing canon:

- `helix_lineage` (canon.yml, ruled): forged ancestry ADOPTED and the tragedy kept —
  a diluted real thread, a splice claimed as descent, kinship carried in literacy
  not base pairs. The item's intent ADDS the instrumental motive: the family line
  was also a **key** — Rakatan descent as command authority over the
  Cathedral's Rakatan-bound machines — and the splice's failure is written on the
  Helix themselves (intelligence and cruelty in equal parts). The terramanufacture
  amendment already carries the control-of-old-technology thread
  (`INHABITED_CAST_HELIX.md`); this doc gives it its address.
- `rust_cathedral` (canon.yml, ruled): the Cathedral "HATES the Helix from the
  moment they side with the Assailants." ⭐ **The Ashfall Research Base IS that
  siding, made concrete and geographic** — a mechanoid cathedral watching a
  gene-cult court the biological weapon in frenzy. The enmity is **ideological and
  strategic, not adjacency** (`assailant_weapon_remnants.md` ruling 5): MEASURED
  2026-09-06, 45.5° of arc from the nearest Rust Cathedral tile to the original
  site; a tile move is the owner's call if he wants neighbors.
- The datafiles are the evidence chain: sequence logs that show the splice being
  designed rather than inherited; procurement ledgers for antiquities bought
  unread; Overdrive trial records — each generation of the weapon a little more
  robust, a little less caged — and the risk assessments, filed, initialed, and
  overridden.

## 4. Dungeon sketch (DRAFTED this pass — layout concept only)

The environment does the heavy lifting: the Bloom's red fog (ranged accuracy ×0.4),
the Burn's sterilizing lance, forage nil, water red — all inherited from
`the_contagion.md` §§3–6, none of it restated or modified here. The base is the one
place on the peak that pretends none of that is happening.

- **Concentric grammar** (dungeons doctrine, `dungeons_arc_spec.md`): outer
  **containment ring** — fences, tesla + railgun emplacements (turret register:
  Helix), burn shelters for staff; middle **sample galleries** — the Unfinished in
  cells, goo cultures, the failed decants that make Ossik Bram's argument for him;
  inner **the datafile core** — the archive, the reveal, the prize.
- **Guardians**: Helix containment response is a standing pawn group
  (`04_factions.md` §9); escaped Assets are hunted alive. The base does not raid;
  it **retrieves** — hostility here reads as recovery of specimens, the player
  included.
- **The Burn is the way in** (the biome's own clock, `the_contagion.md` §3): the fog
  lifts, the natives hide, and the Helix's outdoor lines are exposed to the same
  sky. Move in the Burn; hide in the Bloom — the base's assault window is the
  biome's survival lesson, taught twice.
- **Authoring route**: KCSG via the bridge, template-then-hand-finish, LARGE-map
  and `RUT_` defName rulings inherited from the dungeons arc (owner, 2026-09-01);
  the scene-composition skill applies. No defNames are minted in this doc.

## 5. Hard bans

1. 🔴 The Contagion's §6 bans all apply on this tile — above all **no green squares,
   no net-positive mutation, the war-legacy split holds**.
2. 🔴 **Register guard** (canon.yml `rakata.victims_and_tyrants`,
   `dungeons_arc_spec.md` §1): the Rakata tyranny surfaces at the Assailant flesh
   dungeon, nowhere earlier — the Ashfall datafiles speak about **the Helix**, and
   the Assailant stays unnamed and unsympathetic in every line of them.
3. 🔴 **No worldgen**: the base is a fixed site on the one frozen map, authored in
   place via the bridge.
4. 🔴 The base never functions as a Contagion-sample vendor that upgrades the
   player — the genetic lottery pays in trade goods (`the_contagion.md` §7), never
   in boons.
