
Back in the day, variant monsters were classic GM dick moves.

"Oh, you thought you were going to fight a
red
dragon, so you stocked up on potions of fire resistance. You were deceived! This is a
pthumeriarian blue
dragon, which appears identical to red dragon but breathes ice instead. Hah! Got you now! Looks like you should have read the Monster Manual VI and anticipated such a possibility!"

Etc.

Fred Augis

These days, that kind of behavior isn't well respected, and for good reason. Instead, I've tried to make 1d100 Variant Skeletons that are compact
OSR-type challenges
. Each type should be:

-interesting to fight

-exploitable

-memorable

-potentially telegraphed

They are low on detail but, hopefully, provide some interesting ideas for a room, a dungeon, a random encounter, or a summonable undead helper.
If you want more skeletons and more stats, Paper Elemental has a PDF
here
.
