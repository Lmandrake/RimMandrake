# Canon experiences — puzzles, events, encounter concepts, experiential beats
(DUNGEON_DESIGN_RESEARCH_1)

Each entry: the canonical experience, its provenance, and a one-line fit note for
this campaign (Rakatan vaults / Assailant flesh dungeon on RimWorld tile maps).
[S#] refers to SOURCES.md. "Fit:" lines are candidates for the owner, nothing ruled.

## Structural / navigation experiences

1. **Topple-the-pillars: player restructures the dungeon.** Eagle's Tower, Link's
   Awakening [S10] — knock down four pillars with a carried ball; the top floor
   collapses onto the third, changing the whole map. Fit: a vault where destroying
   four grav-anchors drops the core's suspended shield ring — the map physically
   changes state once, mid-visit, on the player's action.

2. **Flip the map: one layout, two states.** Stone Tower Temple, Majora's Mask
   [S10] — the entire temple inverts. Fit (flesh dungeon): peristalsis — a section
   that floods/contracts on a cycle or trigger, so one corridor is two different
   rooms depending on state. Engine-cheap version: blast doors + flooding
   fleshmass regrowth alternating routes.

3. **Shortcut back to the entrance, unlocked from inside.** Dark Souls / Undead
   Burg [S8]; Skyrim's standard dungeon exit convention [S6]. Fit: near-perfect for
   RimWorld because of HAULING — breaching an inner wall to open a straight haul
   route to the entrance is both the exploration reward and the anti-tedium fix
   (metrics C1/C3).

4. **Multiple entrances as strategy select.** Caverns of Thracia [S1] — three ways
   into level 1. Fit: vault outer works breached in 2–3 places (it's already
   canon that the outer ring is breached); each entrance prices differently
   (turret coverage vs collapsed crawl vs flooded duct).

5. **Midpoint entry / bilateral exploration.** Xandering technique [S1] — drop in
   at the center, explore outward both ways. Fit: the pit-gate pattern RimWorld
   Anomaly already ships (undercave entry [S29]) — a flesh dungeon entered through
   a sinkhole into its middle, with the reveal one way and survival the other.

6. **Foreshadow the payoff through glass.** Undead Burg's visible bridge [S8];
   cyclic "foreshadow" cycle [S2]. Fit: the vault core visible from the garrison
   ring through an impassable barrier — RimWorld supports line-of-sight past
   embrasure/fence-type barriers; let the player SEE the caskets an hour before
   reaching them.

## Encounter concepts

7. **Lock-in arena with role-composed opposition.** DOOM 2016 [S19] — encounters
   composed from enemy ROLES (pressure, sniper, swarm, tank), fought in a sealed
   arena that pulls the player forward. Fit: garrison-ring set-piece — doors seal
   behind the crew (undercave rockfall pattern is engine-precedent), mechanoid
   roles already exist (lancer=sniper, scyther=pressure, centipede=tank).

8. **Crescendo event: the alarm you choose to pull.** Left 4 Dead [S15] — an
   obstacle whose activation summons a timed horde wave; player prepares first.
   Fit: THE vault-door beat — powering the breach cutter wakes the defense grid
   for N hours; the player picks when. Converts a static turret line into a
   prepared-defense drama the RimWorld player already knows how to think about.

9. **Hold-out finale, then run for extraction.** L4D finales [S15]; undercave
   collapse timer [S29]. Fit: waking a sleeper casket (type-③) triggers the
   vault's death-throes: survive the wave, then carry the casket out under a
   collapse clock. Shipped-engine precedent for every piece.

10. **Faction triangle: let them fight.** DCSS/DOOM monster infighting; type-②
    vault canon says the Forsaken Arsenal is STILL FIGHTING the flesh. Fit: rooms
    where mechanoid garrison and fleshbeasts are mid-war when the player arrives —
    the player's real choices are when to intervene and who to let win. This is
    RimWorld's best trick (multi-faction map events) aimed at a dungeon.

11. **The patrol you can watch and time.** Stealth canon (Commandos/Invisible Inc
    lineage); structured unpredictability [S15]. Fit: mechanoid patrol loops
    visible from cover; real-time-with-pause makes timing-a-crossing genuinely
    playable. Randomize phase, not route (metrics F1).

12. **The guardian vault: risk-priced loot room.** Brogue's lever-gated guardian
    machines [S12]; five-room Room 1 [S5]. Fit: self-contained side rooms — pull
    the lever, the cage opens both ways. Cheap to author many; each one is a
    raid-in-and-leave target for a low-commitment visit.

## Puzzle experiences (steel-proof problems, five-room Room 2 [S5])

13. **Power routing as puzzle.** System Shock/Prey lineage, but RimWorld-native:
    conduits, switches, breakers already exist. Fit: the vault runs on one reactor
    — routing power opens THIS wing's blast doors but also wakes THIS wing's
    turrets; the puzzle is sequencing, and it's readable in the game's own UI.

14. **Environment as lock: heat, cold, darkness.** RimWorld-native systems;
    Anomaly darkness mechanics [S29]. Fit: a cryo-wing at −80°C (gear gate, not
    key gate), a furnace corridor the player must vent first (burn-box inverted —
    the dungeon uses the players' own killbox physics against them [S30]), deep
    darkness where only lit paths are safe.

15. **The false vault / red herring.** Tomb of Horrors' false crypt [S9]; five-room
    Room 3 [S5]. Fit: a decoy core — convincing, looted-light, wrong — whose
    tell is readable in the environmental storytelling for players who LOOK
    (rewards attention rather than punishing its absence; fairness rule B3).

16. **Recursive unlocking: emblem keys deepen old wings.** Spencer Mansion [S7].
    Fit: Rakatan sigil-cores carried between wings; each found core re-opens an
    earlier wing one layer deeper. Gives re-traversal new content (metric C1) and
    makes the map itself the progression ladder.

## Narrative / experiential beats

17. **What-happened-here vignettes.** Worch & Smith [S21]; skeletons mid-act.
    Fit: the vault's frozen instants — a breach team dead in a doorway facing the
    wrong way; sleeper caskets with one lid open from the inside. The tyranny
    reveal (ASSAILANT_FLESH_DUNGEON_1) should be STAGED in scenes before any
    letter names it — scenes are pre-reveal-safe if they're ambiguous until
    recontextualized.

18. **The reversal at the bottom.** Ravenloft's villain-first design [S9]; the
    specced digestion scene (Rakatan structure being EATEN — tyrant and victim in
    one image). Fit: already the flesh dungeon's ruled centerpiece; the corpus
    adds: put ONE image at the bottom, not an exposition dump — the strongest
    dungeon reveals are single readable tableaus.

19. **Loot as narrative fuel.** BioShock audio-log lineage [S21], improved: the
    item file already proposes artifacts that trigger the ship's cradle-memory
    surfacings. Fit: confirmed by corpus — delivery of story through carried-out
    objects beats on-site text for a game where the player's attention is on
    pawns, not prose. Reliability is a metric (F3).

20. **Sanctuary rhythm: the camp inside the horror.** Darkest Dungeon's camping
    [S20]; Dark Souls bonfires [S8]. Fit: one defensible, warm, powered room per
    dungeon act — authored rest trough (metric A2) where the crew treats wounds
    and the player exhales. In the flesh dungeon, its safety should feel
    conditional (the walls are still alive).

21. **The thing you choose not to wake.** Type-③ vault canon (frozen Rakata);
    Ravenloft's tone [S9]. Fit: the campaign's rare emotional scene — corpus
    guidance: make LEAVING a fully supported, acknowledged outcome (a letter, a
    mood, a map mark), not a null result — walk-away must be an authored beat,
    or players won't believe it was a choice.

22. **Teach-test-twist a dungeon mechanic.** Nintendo-derived pacing pattern
    [S18]. Fit: each dungeon family introduces ONE new mechanic (grav-anchor,
    peristalsis valve) — show it safe, require it once, then combine it with
    threat at the climax. Keeps hand-authored one-shot dungeons learnable on the
    first and only visit.

## Anti-canon (celebrated, and wrong for us)

23. **Tomb of Horrors as an experience** [S9] — uncued instant death rewards
    out-of-game knowledge; violates fairness (B3) in a permadeath-adjacent game.
    Steal its SET-PIECES, never its cruelty.
24. **Wind Waker's zero-friction loops** [S4] — beautiful anti-backtracking, but
    at RimWorld pacing an over-smoothed dungeon reads as a corridor of chores;
    keep SOME re-traversal where state changed (C1's target is low, not zero).
25. **Grindable dungeons** (Diablo-lineage respawn farms) — collides with DCSS's
    anti-scum constitution [S13] and with fixed-world campaign fiction: our
    dungeons deplete, permanently, and that permanence is the drama.
