# ARTIST_BIOME_INSPIRATION_MOD_1 — artists draw inspiration from a biome

The owner floated this during the Long Shade bedazzle sitting on 2026-09-29. It was typed in the
session; the quote guard could not see the turn, so it is recorded here under BENCH:

> "Here's a wild idea: a new mod for artists. Each artistic character (above say 10 skill)
> they are assigned a particular inspirational biome they find beautiful and that gives them
> a bonus to art in that biome. Maybe they obtain a hated biome at the same time. Or maybe
> their inspiration is unpredictable: use it once and it changes to keep you moving. That
> would be more Jawa."

## spec

It is a design idea for later, not scheduled. A design pass should weigh three variants:

1. A fixed **inspirational biome**: a bonus to art quality or inspiration when the pawn creates in it.
2. The fixed biome paired with a **hated biome**, which gives a penalty or mood hit.
3. A **restless inspiration** that is spent on use and then rerolls to a new biome. The owner
   flagged this as the most Jawa, since it pushes the caravan to keep moving.

The idea spans every biome, so it belongs in the RimMandrake tier, with Mod Settings per the
standing rule. Each biome's golden hour, sky or palette could be what an artist finds beautiful.

## criteria

- A design doc with the variants weighed.
- The owner rules it.
- A build item is filed.
