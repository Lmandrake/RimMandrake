Root cause: `BIOME_OWNERSHIP_WAVE_1` (2026-09-09, see e.g.
`src/RimUtinni/UtinniPatches/Defs/BiomeDefs/RUT_Webwork.xml`,
`RUT_TheRot.xml`) **replaced** every donor/vanilla biome this cast patch targets with a
brand-new, hand-authored `RUT_`-prefixed `BiomeDef` that carries **its own hardcoded
`<wildAnimals>` block directly in the def** (confirmed: 24 of 26 `RUT_*.xml` BiomeDef
files under `UtinniPatches/Defs/BiomeDefs/` have their own `<wildAnimals>`; the 2
without — `RUT_BlueDesert`, `RUT_PropaneLake` — are deliberately lifeless by design). A
`PatchOperationConditional` whose xpath test never matches (because the target biome
defName is gone) silently does nothing — no error, no effect — which is exactly why
this has apparently gone unnoticed: it fails safe, not loud.

**Not touched this pass**: reverted my own regenerate (`git checkout --` on both the
