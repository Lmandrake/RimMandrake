# CHILL_AURORA_SURGE_1 — aurora surge storms: floor weather you can harvest

## what

Card pick, 2026-09-27 sitting: **aurora surge storms.** When the electrojet
spikes, the spike reaches the floor as weather: every Iliss lights up, the
Skyharps sing loud, and arcs walk across the conductive frost.

## deliverables

1. A surge weather/event on the Chill seabed map, coupled to the same
   surface-aurora state that drives the floor light (CHILL_FLOOR_LIGHT_1
   owns the coupling; this item consumes it).
2. **Harvest**: a collector building that turns a surge into a real power
   income — free watts against the brutal cooling load
   (CHILL_THERMAL_ENGINE_1), if you built for it.
3. **Risk**: suited pawns caught outside during a surge take shock
   hits/stuns — the storm is harvest or hide, never neutral.
4. Creature dressing during a surge: Iliss glow behavior, Skyharp audio —
   cheap flags on existing defs, not new systems.

## scope guard

thaw temptation — were NOT adopted at the 2026-09-27 sitting. Do not build
them without a new ruling.
