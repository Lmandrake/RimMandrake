# SCALD_DARK_TOWER_1 — the dark tower, Rakatan high command, in the Scald's crater lake

Owner ruling, 2026-09-07 (verbatim in intent, given while reconciling `ANCIENT_WAR_LAB_1`
against `wasteland.md` §10): *"the nearby lab concept has been reborn as the dark tower
that rises from the volcanic lake near the Scald. That was the ground-based high command
of the Rakatan and has control systems for the Rust Cathedral in it as well as fierce
defenses... as well as ocular warped assailants that have pressed their way inside. A
different dungeon with similar theme."*

## spec
- **What it is**: a tower rising from `RUT_TheScald`'s crater lake — the Rakatan's
  ground-based high command post. Carries the control systems for the Rust Cathedral
  (`the_rust_cathedral.md`, `RUST_CATHEDRAL_MECHANICS_1`) — a command-and-control tie
  between the two sites, not a shared biome or roster.
- **Defenses**: fierce — Rakatan/Forsaken-era, in the same military register as the
  Forgotten Arsenal (`the_forgotten_war.md`): garrisons that never got the order to
  stand down.
- **Threat inside**: *ocular warped* Assailants — infiltrators that pressed their way
  in from outside, not native to the tower and not the same as the propane-lakes war
  lab's contained study specimens (`ANCIENT_WAR_LAB_1`). "Ocular" ties to the Contagion
  lineage (`OCULAR_OVERDRIVE_SITE_1`/`the_contagion.md`) — confirm with the owner
  whether this is meant as the same Ocular strain reaching the tower, or a separate
  warped variant, before authoring the fauna.
- **Where**: in `RUT_TheScald`'s crater lake (312 tiles, region Scald,
  `LIQUID_BIOMES_MAP_1_RECONCILIATION.md`). Additive to the frozen `the_scald.md`
  sheet (`BIOME_FREEZE_FABLE_REVIEW_1`) — does not touch the boil, roster, or
  two-faith shore rulings there.
- ⛔ **Not the same item as** `ANCIENT_WAR_LAB_1` — similar theme (a Rakatan-era
  facility now hosting warped Assailant intruders), different site, different
  content. Do not merge the two dungeons or their fauna.
- Naming per the tier grammar (`RUT_`/`RSW_`).
