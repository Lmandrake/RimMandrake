## spec

Ruled by question card, turn 2 (decision taken by question card; strength slider requested). Unroofed natural stone and steel structures slowly erode under the Dark's falling grain, leaving sweepable tholin dust that refines into fuel. Roofs stop it. Mod Settings: a strength slider including off. The etchcap fungus grows where grain eats rock (`ABYSS_ETCHCAP_BUILD_1`, whose etch-hollow cells are the natural home of this erosion). Grain is supplied by `ABYSS_DARK_BUILD_1`; build on that.

## criteria

- Erosion on unroofed stone and steel only; roofed cells immune.
- Tholin dust item, sweepable, refinable to fuel.
- Strength slider in Mod Settings, 0 = off, default = shipped behaviour.
- Etch-hollow terrain link to the etchcap.
