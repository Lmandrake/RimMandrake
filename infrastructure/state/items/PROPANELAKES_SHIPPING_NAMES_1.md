# PROPANELAKES_SHIPPING_NAMES_1 — owner card: propane lakes working names

## what

Two working names shipped this pass (`COMMISSION_LEDGER_CLEANUP_1`,
the_propane_lakes sheet), both taken straight from the sheet's own prose
rather than invented fresh:

- **`RUT_BurnerAscendant`** ("burner, ascendant") — the_propane_lakes.md §4's
  own term, "The Burners, ascendant." A form of this mod's own Krissek
  lineage (`RM_Krissek`, the_blue_desert's "the Burner"), bigger, paler,
  colder-tolerant, halo always lit while moving.
- **`RUT_VWake`** ("v-wake") — the sheet's own term, "the V-wake creature and
  its kin." A submerged serpentine predator in the propane lake proper.

Neither name is owner-ratified prose (unlike e.g. `RUT_Glower`, which the
sheet capitalizes as a proper noun) — both are working defNames/labels taken
from descriptive phrases in the sheet, same class as `RSW_VentStalker`/
`RUT_DarkCrust` from the poison_forest wave.

## decision needed
