# FAUNA_LORE_DIVERSIFICATION_1 — huge things terrify, tiny things are harmless

Owner, 2026-09-11 (verbatim, FAUNA_TOLERANCE_NORMALIZATION_1 ruling 4): *"We
need to look at the art and eventual description to deduce whether a beast is
particularly scary or harmless. Diversity is good. I am less interested in
forcing everyone to a scaling and more that we allow huge things to terrify
and tiny things to be harmless. So let's scale by size first. Then we can make
a later round of diversification decision when we make the beast graphics and
lore."*

## spec
- Runs AFTER the full-roster size scaling (Law 3 at 12–15×bodySize) lands and
  AFTER/WITH the beast graphics + lore pass — the art and description are the
  judging evidence, so this round rides that work, not the stat manifest.
- Per beast (or per family), an owner card: scary or harmless relative to its
  size band? Deliberate divergences from the band recorded as a manifest
  (defName, band value, ruled value, one-line reason) — the decision record.
- Former above-band outliers (JOE_Nautilant, the bs 32–40 giants) are natural
  first candidates to re-terrify, but nothing is grandfathered — they were
  brought into the band by the size pass like everyone else.

## verify
- [ ] Every divergence traces to an owner card; the manifest is committed and
      the graphs regenerate showing deliberate, labeled off-band points.

## criteria
The roster reads diverse on the damage graph for reasons a player can see in
the art and read in the description — never as leftover donor numbers.
