# GREENTIDE_RISK_REWARD_EXCHANGE_1 — the jungle is terrifying, and pays accordingly

## the ruling

**Owner's direction, 2026-09-22.** ⚠️ **Provenance note:** his message arrived mid-tool-call, so the
`block_forged_owner_said.py` transcript check could not see it and **this is recorded under BENCH's

> *"The jungle should be filled with really intimidating dangers in terms of plants, beasts, diseases,
> and insects. That should in turn be countered with tremendous food abundance, unique medicines, a
> seemingly endless possibility for Star Wars cuisine ingredients, and... (get creative! come up with
> more rewards for braving this terrible place!)"*

## what it establishes

**The Greentide is an EXCHANGE, and both sides must be extreme.** Not a hazard biome you avoid, and
not a larder you farm — a place whose danger is the price of a payoff nothing else on the planet
offers.

**The danger side, four named axes** — all four, not a pick:

1. **Plants** — already in motion: `HOSTILE_MOBILE_PLANTS_1` (rooted ambushers that wake each other),
   and the roster's own strangler and acid-pod rows.
2. **Beasts** — the biome already carries a large cast; `MIASMA_FEVERWOOD_GREENTIDE_BMT_1` and the
   fauna roster are where that lives.
3. **Diseases** — ✅ MEASURED 2026-09-22: `RM_Greentide` carries six (`Disease_Flu`,
   `Disease_Plague`, `Disease_Malaria`, `Disease_GutWorms`, `Disease_AnimalFlu`,
   `Disease_AnimalPlague`) and a seventh, organ decay, was ruled in this session. All seven are
   **vanilla** — under this direction the biome plausibly wants diseases of its own.
4. **Insects** — ⚠️ UNMEASURED as a designed axis. The campaign twin's roster includes a megaspider
   row, but whether this biome has any *authored* insect threat of ours is not established. Check
   before assuming it is a gap.

**The reward side, his three named plus more to invent:**

1. **Tremendous food abundance** — the counterweight to a place that kills you. Note the tension to
   resolve deliberately: he also ruled `allowFarmingCamps` ON this session, and the biome's identity
   is hazard. Abundance should come from *braving* the jungle, not from settling comfortably in it.
2. **Unique medicines** — nothing on this planet else offers them. Ties to the roster's sap-bleeders
   and to canon-inspired roles already captured (bark resins, acid pods, glowing understory flora).
3. **A large space of cuisine ingredients** — *"seemingly endless"*. This is the one that wants a
   SYSTEM rather than a list: many ingredients combining, not twenty separate recipes.
4. 🔑 **"Get creative! come up with more rewards"** — an explicit invitation. A design pass owes him
   *new* reward categories, not just these three filled in.

## ⛔ Do not design this in isolation — four items already carry pieces of it

🔴 **This item is the FRAME, not the content.** Its job is to make the exchange coherent; the pieces
live elsewhere and must not be duplicated:

- `GREENTIDE_BIOME_DENSITY_1` — already carries the economy half of his earlier ruling (*"most are
  useful, many are dangerous"*) and the choked-foliage movement cost. **That item is where reward and
  hazard being the same object is recorded.**
- `GREENTIDE_JUNGLE_TREE_ROSTER_1` — 21 invented plants, 19 useful and 9 dangerous by design, with
  the sap tap, the export timber, the hardwood and the forageable roots already assigned.
- `GREENTIDE_EXOTIC_JUNGLE_FISH_1` — the catch, and a food/ingredient source.
- `HOSTILE_MOBILE_PLANTS_1` — the plant-danger axis.
- `GREENTIDE_MECHANICS_2` — the eight scoped-out mechanics (wet-bulb, Roil weather, tree-fall, the
  Lunger, root causeways) that supply much of the *intimidation*.

⇒ **What is genuinely NEW here:** the disease axis as ours rather than vanilla, the insect axis, the
cuisine-ingredient *system*, and additional invented reward categories.

## spec

1. **Audit both sides first**, against the items above, and say what each axis already has. ⛔ Do not
   open with invention — four of the six axes are partly built, and `BMT_FLORA_ABSORPTION_1`'s lesson
   is that finished work sits unnoticed.
2. **Design the exchange as a curve, not two lists.** What does the first hour in the jungle cost and
   give, versus the tenth? An exchange that is uniformly lethal-and-generous has no arc.
3. **Bring him card sets** on: our own diseases vs. vanilla; whether insects are a distinct threat or
   part of the beast cast; the shape of the cuisine system; and a slate of **new** reward categories
   for him to choose among — that last one is his explicit ask and should be the widest card.
4. Feature-gate the extremes in Mod Settings per the standing rule — a biome tuned this far in both
   directions needs the sliders.

## verify

Each of the four danger axes and each reward category has a named home item or a recorded decision
that it is out of scope. The exchange is written as a progression, not a pair of lists. No content is
authored here that duplicates the five items above. ⛔ No live-proven claim from the Mac.

## criteria

A player who has been to the Greentide twice goes back a third time, knowing exactly what it costs.

## Watch out

- 🔴 **The two halves must be designed together or they will not balance.** Danger and reward are
  currently spread across five items with no single place that checks they add up. That is precisely
  what this item is for — ⛔ so do not let it become a sixth content item.
- ⚠️ **"Tremendous food abundance" plus farming camps plus a rich forage yield can make a hazard
  biome the best place to live.** Say how that is prevented; the anti-exponential concern that
  flattened the gizka's market value is the same class of problem.
- ⚠️ **Seven vanilla diseases is already a lot.** Adding ours on top risks a biome that is simply
  tedious rather than frightening — consider replacing rather than stacking, and say which.
- ⚠️ *"Seemingly endless"* cuisine must not become hundreds of hand-authored recipes. A combinatorial
  system with few ingredients reads as endless; a long flat list reads as filler.
