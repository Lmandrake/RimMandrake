## The rite (card text, BENCH's)
A found disposal order, struck out in the rite, calls an old salvage operation that strips loose metal and then your powered
buildings, the ship included, until driven off.

## spec (numbers `// INVENTED`)
1. **Found:** at a river-mouth disposal depot on a Miasma map, an inscription under condemnation stamps (the disposal order).
   Learned through the Rites tab's found-rites row; performable anywhere after.
2. **Asks:** the order is read aloud and struck out by the participants.
3. **The event (the point):** an old reclamation operation answers: machines arrive that collect loose metal first, then dismantle
   powered structures (the gravship included) until the colony drives them off. Needs reclaimer machine defs and a force budget.
4. **Outcomes:** cohesion (shared memories by quality); Rekko's favour told by the Narrator, shown only in his odds. Any salvage the
   driven-off machines drop is the risk's reward (FOUNDRY to size; flag to BENCH if it reads as a power).
5. **Collision check:** the Mud Claim (Rekko, B7) owns post-recede salvage; the Inherited Wreck claims a wreck. This one **calls**
   the salvager on yourself.

## Cap
Rekko's fifth found rite (the Unfinished Laid Down, the Inherited Wreck, the Mud Claim, B7; the Mending Weld, B12): **at the cap**.
