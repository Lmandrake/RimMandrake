# JAWA_MESS_IMMUNITY_1 — Jawa are immune from messes

Owner ruling 2026-09-24, typed at the Sump sitting, verbatim (answering a card
proposing a mess-lover trait for all four aligned factions):

> "Jawa should be immune from messes. The others just live with it and suffer."

⇒ The broader mess-lover trait is **declined, not deferred** — no faction gains
mood FROM mess. Exactly one carve-out exists: the Jawa xenotype takes no mood
penalty from filth/squalor. Junkers, deep tribes and Black Star endure the
penalties like everyone else — their alignment with nasty biomes is presence
(`SUMP_INHABITED_NOTES_1`), not comfort.

## spec

A gene (or trait) on the Jawa xenotype suppressing filth/squalor/mess-derived
negative thoughts — dirty environment thoughts nulled, nothing added. Campaign
tier (the Jawa are Utinni-layer), planet-wide — not Sump-specific; filed off the
Sump sitting because its tuning law surfaced it.

## verify

Quicktest: a Jawa pawn in a filthy room registers no filth/squalor mood
penalties; a non-Jawa pawn in the same room does; no pawn anywhere gains mood
from mess.

## criteria

Mess costs everyone except the Jawa, and rewards no one.
