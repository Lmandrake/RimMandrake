## spec

- **Found:** at the Slime (site to be drafted at build: a ring of hand-prints pressed into hardened slime is
  BENCH's suggestion; owner may rule).
- **Asks:** the participants stand in a ring, each holding a handful of raw slime, and join hands for a short while.
- **Effect (the owner's own explicit ask):** one participant's **permanent hediffs** (scars, chronic conditions,
  missing-part-class injuries as FOUNDRY scopes it) are reduced to **weak hediffs spread over several**
  participants instead. Severity is shared, not erased.
- **Risk:** everyone holding slime is being read (slimification ticks; the antidote stock matters).
- ⚠ **Tension, flagged not altered:** the register's binding ruling is that *a rite gives cohesion, never a
  power*; the hediff-spreading is a mechanical power and is the owner's explicit ask, recorded as his.
- Plus the standard cohesion outcomes (shared memory by quality, favour told by the Narrator).
