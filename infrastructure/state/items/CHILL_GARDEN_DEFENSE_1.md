# CHILL_GARDEN_DEFENSE_1 — the garden's tiered immune system

## what

Owner's brief, typed this session (2026-09-27): the bottom should feel
*"delicate, entrancing, precious, shocking when it can actually defend itself
but quite overcomable."*

Card ruling: **tiered — Iliss first, then Tarnn.**

1. **Iliss arcs (the warning).** First offenses against the garden —
   harvesting past a threshold, killing floor life, directed heat damage —
   draw the current-eels: literal electric arc-discharges through the
   conductive frost around the offender. Stinging, eerie, survivable.
2. **Tarnn wake (the shock).** Sustained destruction wakes the Tarnn
   colonies: things catalogued as scenery stand up and fight, several at
   once. Sized like a hard skirmish, NOT a raid — one genuinely frightening
   fight, then the stillness returns.

## calibration rulings

- **Overcomable is a requirement**, not a hope — a prepared expedition beats
  either tier without a wipe.
- **The drill route triggers only the Iliss tier** (card ruling on
  CHILL_WARLAB_ROUTES_1): drilling agitates — arcs harass the work site, a
  thin scar remains, but no Tarnn wake and nothing permanent at scale.
- Both species already exist (`RM_Iliss`, `RM_Tarnn`, built 2026-09-26 with
  art); this item adds the sleeper/threshold behavior, not the creatures.
