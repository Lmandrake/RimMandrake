## the principle, decomposed

Every biome owes four answers:
1. **Its annoying material** — a nuisance that makes the biome itself (tar in the
   Sump: stinky, sticky, messy, little way to stay clean).
2. **The nuisance's own reward** — annoying materials are never just annoying.
3. **A weak solution makable locally** — the biome lets you cope, not win.
4. **A strong solution made in ANOTHER biome** — e.g. the Poison Forest as the maker
   of acidic solvents that clean anything. The gap between weak-local and
   strong-foreign is the trade economy.
