## what

Build the flora roster from
`design/Jawa/worldbuilding/biomes/the_propane_lake_flora_pass_2026-09-27.md`
(Slackwax, Ghostpane, Eldspar, Tarspool, Keelgrass, Stonewater, Fuselight,

sitting rulings below — **the doc is being revised to match them under
PROPANELAKE_FLORA_PASS_1; build from the revised doc, not the original.**

## rulings that bind this build (sittings of 2026-09-27)

- **plantDensity 0.18** — accepted (earlier sitting, by card). Set the exact
  fields the doc's §"plantDensity and the unset-field trap" names.
- **Fuel harvest is ALLOWED, very late game.** Owner typed: *"it's ok to
  harvest them for fuel. This is very late gme"* and, on shape: *"Just have
  it result at the harvest bench (hydrocarbon flesh) that can then be
  converted at the refinery."* ⇒ harvesting the fatty flora yields a raw item
  (**hydrocarbon flesh**) that a refinery recipe converts to chemfuel. The
  doc's "never a free fuel farm" hard-law section is overruled and comes out.
- **Stonewater**: owner typed *"Cooking + real water source w/ power"* — the
  ice is a real cooking/hydration input AND a powered extractor on stonewater
  brakes acts as a genuine water source. Melting costs watts: even drinking
  feeds the thermal economy.
- **Fuselight teeth: REAL explosion**, small radius, one comp (card, twice
  confirmed). Peroxide is self-oxidizing — the sole exception to
  CHILL_FIRE_BAN_1, and the ban must not suppress it.
- **Eldspar growDays 60** (card — owner chose the regrowing stand-in over
  never-regrows). Living crystal heals over a long game; only the bomb scars
  are forever.
- **Warm bloom, both edges** (card): Stillbloom and kin flower in a heat
  plume's outer ring while the innermost cells boil bare — park anywhere and
  you create a halo of bloom around a ring of harm.
- **AuroraGlass moves into the garden** (card): Eldspar hearts yield it;
  remove it from the RM-tier rare-catch set. (It also appears as a rare vein
  yield on the war-lab drill shafts — CHILL_WARLAB_ROUTES_1.)
- **Tarspool crown scrape = the SAME raw material as the rime-nodule
  euphoric** (earlier sitting, by card).
- **The tarnn/eldspar boundary dispute stays UNSETTLED on purpose** (earlier
  sitting, by card) — the fauna def and flora def disagree about where the
  line is. Do not "fix" it.
