# NIGHTSIDEICE_BREACH_CRACKS_1 — the breach loop: staging cracks at the base edge

Split from `NIGHTSIDEICE_HEAT_DIAL_BUILD_1` item 2. Design:
`design/Jawa/worldbuilding/biomes/nightsideice_bedazzle_review_2026-10-01.md` §4 row 1. Needs
`NIGHTSIDEICE_SHIVVEN_BUILD_1` first (the cracks are where the shivven come up).

## spec

1. The `RM_HeatDial` (built, `NIGHTSIDEICE_HEAT_DIAL_1`) drives breach frequency: higher dial, sooner and
   larger breaches. Zero dial, no breaches.
2. Breach staging cracks: an attackable building (VFE siege-burrow shape) that opens **only in open ice at
   the base's edge** (the hull rule: never through a floor or a hull, Anomaly's open-ground placement rule),
   then releases shivven if not destroyed in time.
3. The **first** breach is timed and taught (a letter that names the dial and what raised it, with a
   countdown, Anomaly pit-gate warning tiers); later ones are only vaguely warned.
4. Mod Settings: breaches on/off, frequency scale, first-breach countdown, warning on/off.

## criteria

- With the dial above the threshold a crack opens on open ice beside the base, never on a floor.
- The first one carries the teaching letter and a countdown; the second does not.
- Destroying a crack in time stops its breach.
