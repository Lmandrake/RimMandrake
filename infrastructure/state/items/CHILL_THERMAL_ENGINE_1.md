# CHILL_THERMAL_ENGINE_1 — brutal cooling load + the boil shroud

## what

Owner typed this session (2026-09-27): *"There should be bubbling animations
surrounding the hot ship boiling the propane simply by being normal
temperature. There should be a MASSIVE cooling load on the ship down there
requiring extensive ship heating."*

Two mechanisms on the Chill seabed map:

1. **Cooling load — brutal, uniform** (card ruling; the zoned option was
   declined). Cryogenic ambient; the liquid wicks heat out of the hull so hard
   that heating is THE logistics of every visit. An underpowered ship freezes
   room by room — a real, plannable fail state. One severity everywhere on the
   floor; no gentle shallows.
2. **The boil shroud.** Liquid-terrain cells adjacent to the warm hull carry a
   permanent bubble/boil fleck effect — the ship boils the lake by existing.
   Any warm thing outside (working pawn, powered lamp, heater) carries its own
   smaller shimmer. The player's presence is visible as violence against the
   medium before they touch anything.

## constraints

- Fire ban (CHILL_FIRE_BAN_1) makes all heating **electric only** — no stove,
  campfire or chemfuel fallback below. The power problem is the point.
- Pairs with CHILL_HEATED_SUIT_1 (pawn-side of the same cold).
- Physics framing (owner typed): *"So there's no oxygen down in the sea floor
  so it's not explosive."* The floor's danger engine is cold, not fire.
