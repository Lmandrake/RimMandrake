> knit it all together beautifully, sensibly, and without generating a whole new game
> out of it (as it my normal behavior. :) )"

## The ask, in the owner's frame

- **The wander-through scenario is primary and vital.** Nothing here may turn
  Ash'karr into a rails game. The campaign is what a player finds if they dig; the
  digging must stay optional and the surface must stay fascinating without it.
- **The campaign already exists — in pieces.** It grew organically across ~six weeks
  of notes, sheets, rulings and item files. Nobody has ever read it end to end.
- **The formal pass is two things**: (A) a GATHER that puts every fragment in one
  place with its source and status, and (B) a SITTING where the owner rules the
  stitches. Then (C) the knitting content gets generated — as separate items.
- **The owner's stated failure mode is generating a whole new game.** The sitting's
  method must make new *connective* content easy and new *worlds* hard.

## Phase A — the GATHER (BENCH orchestrates; a Fable subagent, backgrounded)

Design work is never done in-window (`Agent_Policy.md`). One artifact:

**`design/Jawa/campaign/CAMPAIGN_ARC_GATHER.md`** — an INDEX + EXTRACT, never a
rewrite. Every fragment is quoted (or tightly paraphrased, marked as such) with its
source path and one status tag:

`RULED (date, where)` · `DRAFTED (by whom, agreed?)` · `MENTIONED-UNUSED` ·
`CONTRADICTED-BY <path>` · `SUPERSEDED-BY <ruling>`

Grouped under exactly these six headings:

1. **The spine** — what the campaign IS, in the order a player could live it, as far
   as anything states it: the yard → the theft → getting it off the ground → the
   pressures → the Antiquities ladder → the Cathedral thaw → the Spire/the Key → the
   crater event → the war lab → the Reclamation → the open end. Where a beat is only
   implied by two fragments, say which two and label it INFERRED, not stated.
2. **Places with purposes** — every dungeon, site and landmark and what it is FOR in
   the story: the Ashfall Research Base / the Spire (= the Key, RULED); the war lab
   under the propane lake; the Rust Cathedral / Archon; the six Forsaken vaults and
   the Assailant complex; the Ocular / Contagion sites; the Forge and the tibanna
   clock; the Fever Wood's Tenant; the Sump; the Miasma's warden-mother; the Scald's
   dark tower; the Fall Line; Zeddo's Yard and the Hutt sites; the Helix sites (Site
   Aurek, Farside Station, Site Cresh, Cold Stores); the Geonosian hive; the droid
   enclaves; the Deeps; anything else found. A place with no stated purpose is listed
   with `PURPOSE: GAP`.
3. **Mentioned-but-unused threads** — the floating station(s) / orbital towers / the
   sky ladder / skyhook; the asteroid belt and the asteroid-fed factories; orbital
   platforms; anything V2_DREAMS.md or a proposal parked. Each with: where it was
   mentioned, what it was going to do, whether anything on the map or in a mod already
   points at it.
4. **Reveal moments** — in the order the player could hit them, each as SPECIFIED
   (quote it) or GAP (say what is missing: the fact revealed, the place, the trigger,
   the tone). Include the literacy-ladder gating that orders them (`antiquities_design`
   + the Ashfall "encrypted, the ladder unlocks" ruling) and the register guard
   (Rakata tyranny surfaces at the flesh dungeon, nowhere earlier).
5. **Transitions** — for every transition between beats: the tone/experience it is
   SUPPOSED to have where stated (the flood is "a cut-scene everyone on the planet
   notices"; the Reclamation is "a price the player should see coming"; the Testament
   is "gratitude without warmth"), and `TONE: GAP` where nothing says.
6. **Contradictions** — listed, sourced, NOT resolved. Start with the known ones: the
   Spire prep's four candidate shapes vs the Key ruling; any doc still saying
   "campaign function TBD"; the concealment spec's old asymptotic-v1 assumption vs
   A3; victory OPEN-ENDED vs the god-mapped win paths; anything that names a place
   the map no longer has (the 2026-09-12 renames: Site Aurek, Farside Station, Site
   Cresh, Cold Stores, The Breaks, Cratercrown, Zeddo's Salvage Yard).

Rules for the gather:
- **Nothing invented.** Every line has a source path. A gap is written `GAP`, never
  filled with a plausible sentence. The brief to the subagent says this in its first
  line and its last, because a Fable subagent handed a story will write the story.
- **Dead or live?** Where a fragment's status is in doubt, use the two-blind-arms
  discipline (memory `two-blind-arms-doc-audit`): one arm reads the fragment list
  only, one reads the rulings only; a fragment is marked SUPERSEDED only where both
  agree. Never on one arm.
- **Read the git log, not just the file.** Several docs were ruled today
  (`919d5c155`, `8c4731b5e`) and older files still carry the pre-ruling text.
- Size target: the gather is long by nature; keep each entry to the quote + tags +
  one line of context. No narrative between entries.

## Phase B — the SITTING (owner + BENCH, live, cards)

The interaction the owner asked for — the method that makes him generate a TON of
connective content and no new game:

- **Cards, not prose.** One card per GAP and one per contradiction from the gather.
  Each card offers 2–4 concrete options, and **every option must cite which existing
  fragments it stitches together** (by gather entry id). An option that needs a
  fragment the gather does not have is not allowed on the card. Free text always
  overrides (memory `ask-with-question-cards`, `cards-simple-language-clear-trades`):
  simple language, and each option states its trade.
- **The hard rule, written on the first card and repeated on every batch header:**
  NO NEW PLACES, NO NEW FACTIONS, NO NEW MECHANISMS — unless a card explicitly asks
  for one, and then the card says why the existing pieces cannot carry the stitch.
  The owner's own words are the reason: "without generating a whole new game."
- **Batch by act.** Cards go out in the order the player lives the story, in batches
  of 4–6, so each ruling builds momentum for the next. After each batch BENCH shows a
  running one-page **"the arc so far"** — the spine as ruled to that point — before
  the next batch goes out. The owner sees the story assembling.
- **Reveal cards get one extra field**: the tone/experience of the moment, offered as
  2–3 short register lines to pick from (the way the biome sheets' §9 artistic-theme
  lines work), because a reveal without a tone is a fact, not a moment.
- **Every ruling is propagated in the same sitting** (deciding-and-superseding):
  the contradicted sentence is DELETED from its doc, not bannered; frozen sheets get
  an amendment block (add, never change); canon.yml gets the key.
- **Output**: `design/Jawa/campaign/CAMPAIGN_ARC.md` — the canonical spine, in acts,
  each beat with its places, its reveal, its transition tone, and its source rulings;
  no `GAP` left unlabelled (an explicitly deferred gap reads `DEFERRED (owner,
  date)`). Stored per `CANON_STORAGE_ARCHITECTURE_1` Phase 0+1 (prose canonical,
  certainty tiers, derived claim index).
- **Do not reopen ruled cards.** The 2026-09-12 sittings (MECHANICS_CARDS_SITTING_1,
  the Ashfall/Archon/Mechanoid-pass/crater rulings, WORLD_NAME_FIXES_1) are inputs,
  not cards.

## Phase C — the KNIT (after the sitting; NOT this item)

Per ruled transition, the content owed — quest text, letters, dungeon set-piece prose,
cut-scene beats, datafile text, the reveal usher lines — filed as separate items
(`DUNGEON_SETPIECE_TEXT_1` is the existing pattern). This item closes when
CAMPAIGN_ARC.md exists and is propagated; the generation wave is its successor.

## traps
- **The gather is an extract, not an authoring pass.** A Fable subagent handed "the
  campaign story" will write a beautiful one. Forbid it in the first line of the
  brief; grade the return by sampling quotes against their sources.
- **Frozen sheets** (`BIOME_FREEZE_FABLE_REVIEW_1`): amendments add detail and never
  change a ruling. A stitch that needs a frozen ruling changed is an owner card, not
  an edit.
- **Inaccurate material is DELETED at propagation, not bannered** (owner, 2026-09-09).
  The Spire prep's four shapes are the first thing this rule will meet.
- **Some docs say "campaign function TBD" for things ruled today.** The file is not
  the truth; the file plus the git log is.
- **The seed document is 1,531 lines of 2026-08-02 conversation.** Much of it was
  superseded by later rulings. Extract from it, but every extract must be checked
  against canon.yml's `ruled:` before it gets a RULED tag.
- **The owner's generation is the resource being protected.** If a batch produces
  more than one "let's add a new X" answer, stop the batch and re-card with the
  stitch options made more concrete — that is the signal the cards are too open.
