# THIRST_DESIGN_SLIME_IS_THE_LAST_RESORT

Owner, 2026-10-17, by card write-in (verbatim), answering whether to keep the thirst build's calls beyond the design (salt/brine/toxic bottles "don't seek out" under Dubs; plain rivers/lakes = fresh; buckets can't be drunk from):

> No non-potable water should be automatically gathered, that should be an order. Buckets can be drank from, even barrels if there are no other choices. There should be a small mood debuff if characters have no bottles to drink from and must drink from a tap on a tank or barrel (unless they are of the hardier traits that ignore such things, or they are wild and uncivilized). Drinking slime should only happen if there are no potable water supplies available. It provides a massive negative mood impact, a lingering laxative health issue for a day, and then the kind of slime may also affect you based on its own properties. Drinking salt water should be something characters do as a mental break type event due to extreme thirst: it briefly makes them feel better, but then induces medically dangerous states (design pass). Throw these rulings together and do a 3x3 deep design pass on all the different rulings and interactions needed for all our fluid types, the concept of thirst in Rimworld, and the edge cases and interactions/rules needed with the health, gathering of fluids and storage, access, and character/pawn jobs. Include in that design pass there may be needs to supply crops with water, load fluids into machines, and other uses that cause a lot of traffic and business that we should think through in terms of bottles, buckets, barrels, pipes, and hoses.

Supersedes the build's "buckets can no longer be drunk from" call. Deliverable: a 3x3 deep design doc (deep-design-3x3 skill) at design/RimMandrake/thirst_fluids_design_2026-10-17.md.
