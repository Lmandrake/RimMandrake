# CHILL_THERMAL_FOOTPRINTS_1 — warmth writes on the ice

## what

Card pick, 2026-09-27 sitting: **thermal footprints.** Everything warm marks
the Chill's ice floor — a walking pawn's melt-prints refreeze glossy, a
parked warm machine leaves a polished shadow. The expedition's history is
written on the floor, permanent-ish and legible.

## deliverables

1. A floor-print mechanism: warm movers over ice terrain leave a refrozen
   glossy print (terrain overlay / fleck-with-persistence / cheap tile flag —
   builder's choice; it must survive save/load and stay cheap at map scale).
2. Legibility both ways: the player sees their own beautiful trail — and the
   garden's defense (CHILL_GARDEN_DEFENSE_1) can weight its convergence along
   old trails, so a second visit finds the first visit waiting.
3. Prints on rime terraces (CHILL_RIME_TERRACES_1) may read differently from
   prints on bare ice — nice-to-have, not a bar.

## scope guard

The works menu's unpicked siblings — melt-settle, fogging-the-Windows,
supercool pools — were NOT adopted at the 2026-09-27 sitting. Do not build
them without a new ruling.
