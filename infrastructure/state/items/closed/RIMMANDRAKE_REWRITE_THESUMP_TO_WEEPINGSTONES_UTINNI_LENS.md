**Question:** How long should a biome design assume the ship stays? The pursuit documents give a probe droid 5 to 8 days after landing, then scouts, a strike team, a cordon, an assault and a bombardment; a loud crew halves each wait and a hidden one doubles it. No document states a stop's length. The pilot assumed about a week free, a first fight in week two or three, and nothing past a month. What should the other twenty be designed for?
**His words:** The numbers you're generating are likely the outer edge allowable for each biome, and that's just fine. Go with that.
**Other options:** Follow the pursuit clock per biome (Recommended) · About two weeks everywhere · About a month everywhere

**Question:** The same question came up in all three pilot biomes: does natural cover hide the landed ship from the Empire's search from orbit? Your 2026-08-05 ruling pauses the orbital clock on dark or covered tiles, but only the Abyss (permanent dark) is on the built list. Jungle canopy, steam and fog do not count today. What should count as cover?
**Picked:** Partial cover, by biome (Recommended)
**Its description:** Why: makes where you land a real choice without making any biome a free hideout. Canopy, steam and fog slow the search by one step; full dark still pauses it. Costs a small build per covered biome and one more thing each design must state. Recommended: it answers your 'what helps them stay and hide' question in every biome.
**Other options:** Only true dark hides · Any thick cover fully hides

You can also buy it (the intended motivation to have to trade constantly, very Jawa). (by the way you should always look for solutions that make you say Very Jawa). One or two are REPLETE with fuel (the frozen Chill beach for example)
**Other options:** A few named fuel stops, in 0.2.0 (Recommended) · No biome fuel in these versions · Every biome has some fuel source
