Second half of the owner's 2026-09-30 ruling (see `CHILL_CRYOPONICS_GROWER_1` for his words): a
normal growing bed for the Chill's six bed plants, whose placement is restricted to the floor of the
Chill (a PlaceWorker gating on the Chill's floor terrain/biome), so the dive crew can farm them
standing on walkable ground.
