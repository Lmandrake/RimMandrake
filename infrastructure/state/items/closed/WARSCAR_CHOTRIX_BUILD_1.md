# WARSCAR_CHOTRIX_BUILD_1 — the chotrix, the Warscar's invisible hunter

Owner ruled by question card on 2026-09-30 at 21:10 PDT: admit as pitched, name **chotrix**, and cloak
lacquer is **PERMANENT**. The pitch is §5 of
`design/Jawa/worldbuilding/biomes/warscar_bedazzle_cast_2026-09-30.md`; it grew from his typed request
for invisible hunters ("tricky in this genre").

## spec

- `RM_Chotrix`: RM tier, Warscar only, bodySize ~0.9, one or two per map. A lean, low scavenger that
  ate the old line's light-bending stealth lacquer and carries it in its hide.
- Invisible while standing or stalking. Use the stock invisibility hediff with a strike-reveal; reuse
  the reveal from `RM_AquaticAmbushInvisibility`. It shows when it strikes and for a few seconds after.
- It hunts lone small animals, tetchik and chatrak calves, and lone pawns at night. It never attacks a
  group of 2 or more, and flees after one bite if hurt.
- Readable signs (no animal vanishes unseen):
  - prints on `FOOTPRINT_TRACK_GRID_1` (invisible pawns are recorded);
  - a moving ring of tetchik silence on the Geiger choir;
  - heat shimmer when it runs;
  - dragged-kill marks on the grid.
- Butchering yields **cloak lacquer**. Applied to a cloak at a tailoring bench, it makes the wearer
  invisible while still and unseen, **permanently** (owner ruling). The owner's standing principle is
  powerful tech balanced by other means, so balance comes from rarity (1–2 per map), the bench cost and
  the still-and-unseen condition, never by making it timed.
- A Mod Settings gate.

## criteria

- The creature spawns on a Warscar quicktest map, stays invisible, reveals on its strike, and leaves
  prints on the track grid.
- A lacquered cloak grants still-invisibility that persists across save and load.
- Art is commissioned and wired.
