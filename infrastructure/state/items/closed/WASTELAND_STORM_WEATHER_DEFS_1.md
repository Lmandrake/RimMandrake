## why this is a card, not a build

wasteland.md's own "Owed" section (§ "Engine feasibility pass") says it
outright: "the storm map-reshuffle (mutator churn on a frozen world needs
its own tooling and its own ruling on scope)." This is not a def-authoring
task — three WeatherDefs are cheap — it is a scope question about whether
and how a storm event may reshuffle map mutators/tiles on a world design
that is otherwise supposed to be FROZEN once authored (see this repo's own
standing "the planet is painted once, at the end" rule). Authoring the
WeatherDefs without that ruling would either commit to a mutator-churn
mechanism nobody has scoped, or ship three inert weather flavor events with
none of the "dose + exhumation" mechanic the sheet actually wants.

## options for the owner

1. **Flavor-only storms now**: three WeatherDefs (ash / radiation-halo /
   plasma-terminator) with visual/temperature effects only, no map-mutator
   churn — ships something today, defers the exhumation mechanic
   indefinitely.
2. **Full scope now**: rule on what "exhumation reshuffle" is allowed to
   touch (new mutators appearing under an ash storm? existing terrain
   changing?) and file the real engine-feasibility item.
3. **Drop the mutator-churn half entirely**, ship flavor storms only,
   permanently — simplest, but loses the sheet's own "storm as excavator"
   idea.
