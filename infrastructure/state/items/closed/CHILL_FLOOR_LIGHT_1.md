# CHILL_FLOOR_LIGHT_1 — drowned aurora over bioluminescent points

## what

Card ruling (2026-09-27): **layered light** on the Chill seabed map.

1. **Drowned aurora.** Liquid propane is water-clear; the black mirror is only
   black from above. From the floor, the brightest sky on the planet arrives
   as dim shifting curtains — a map-light level that rises and falls with
   aurora weather on the surface above. The floor breathes light.
2. **Bioluminescent constants.** A steady scatter of point light from the
   glowing flora — Fuselight (the peroxide lamp) and Ghostpane membranes —
   so the bottom is never absolutely black even when the aurora sleeps.

## intent to preserve

The floor must read **totally unlike the dayside** (owner brief, typed): the
surface is white glare on black glass; the bottom is violet-teal dusk lit by
a sky that ripples. Eerily beautiful, alien, mildly wonderful — this item is
the single biggest carrier of that feeling.

Sound note for whoever builds ambience: Skyharp's filaments carry the
electrojet — the floor should sing faintly in aurora weather.

## dependencies

Flora glow comes from CHILL_FLORA_BUILD_1; this item owns the map-level
aurora light layer and its weather coupling.
