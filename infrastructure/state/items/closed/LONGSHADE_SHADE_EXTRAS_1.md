# LONGSHADE_SHADE_EXTRAS_1 — Long Shade small ideas, all seven admitted

The owner admitted all seven by question card 2026-10-08 (earlier deferral 2026-09-29; the mirror field also became `SOLAR_MIRRORS_MOD_DESIGN_1`). Source:
`design/Jawa/worldbuilding/biomes/longshade_shade_ideation_2026-09-29.md` §2 and §4.

- **The empty patch**: a perfect shadow with no animals in it is a warning.
- **Tollok**: ticks living in wild shade, which makes built shade worth more.
- **Stampede for your roof**: an overheated herd finds its patch full and bolts for the base.
- **Lure awnings**: shade put up to bait game.
- **Mirror field**: turning a mirror reshapes the shade map.
- **Harrok**: a stilt predator that is its own shade.
- **Jawa return**: a clan comes back and tows the dead sandcrawler away, taking the biggest
  shadow on the map with it.

## criteria

Each of the seven is filed as its own build item.
