# CHILL_CRYOPONICS_GROWER_1 — a fully enclosed cryoponics grower for the Chill's bed plants

The Chill's six bed plants grow only on liquid propane (`RM_TheChillBed`'s only terrain), which the
dive crew cannot stand in. Owner ruling 2026-09-30 (typed on the card): *"Two items. A cryoponics
growing objects for these plants that's fully enclosed. And a normal growing bed that can only be
placed on the floor of the Chill."* This is the first of the two; the second is
`CHILL_FLOOR_GROWING_BED_1`.

## spec

- A buildable, fully enclosed grower (hydroponics-style `Building_PlantGrower`) that can grow the six
  Chill bed plants anywhere, carrying its own cryogenic propane environment.
- Pawns sow and harvest from it normally.

## criteria

- All six bed plants sow, grow and harvest in it on a quicktest map; it has art and a Mod Settings gate.
