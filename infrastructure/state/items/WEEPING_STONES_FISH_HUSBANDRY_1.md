## scope

Fishing as *husbandry*, unique on the planet: the player stocks, feeds, reads
and can overdraw pools; murrin surface-rings are the pool's readable health.
Distinct by construction from the Scald (edge angling) and the Rust Cathedral
(eel lines).

The ruling adds four requirements over the portfolio draft:

1. **Moisture farmers specializing in fish** — the colonist fantasy is the
   fish-farmer; work/economy identity, not a side verb.
2. **Rimcuisine hooks** — recipes/ingredients worth cooking; the catch feeds a
   cuisine line, not just raw nutrition.
3. **Fish are nasty and way too active** — hostile handling, jumpy, biting;
   tending a pool is not serene.
4. **Some catches are NOT fish** — alien beasts you really wonder if we
   should be eating. Catchable pool fauna that reads as a creature, not a
   fillet.
