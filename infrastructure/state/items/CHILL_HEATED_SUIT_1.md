# CHILL_HEATED_SUIT_1 — the heated suit, one charge clock

## what

Owner typed this session (2026-09-27): *"Definitely hypothermia risk with
wearable heaters to keep you alive (that's the appropriate suit type)."*

A heated-suit apparel line for EVA on the Chill floor, with **one clock**
(card ruling — the two-clock heat+air option and the no-clock option were
declined): a battery charge gauge. Fiction says the suit handles air too;
mechanically the only gauge is warmth.

- Full charge: pawn works outside safely.
- Empty: hypothermia sets in FAST — the walk back to the airlock is the
  horror beat, not instant death.
- Recharge aboard the ship (powered charger or hull proximity — builder's
  choice, but it must consume the ship's power budget, feeding
  CHILL_THERMAL_ENGINE_1's logistics).

## constraints

- Electric charge, never fueled — nothing burns down there (CHILL_FIRE_BAN_1).
- Every DLC assumed present; if Odyssey vacuum-suit machinery gives this
  near-free, use it — but the player-facing gauge stays singular.
- Unsuited pawns outside are in immediate, severe hypothermia territory.
