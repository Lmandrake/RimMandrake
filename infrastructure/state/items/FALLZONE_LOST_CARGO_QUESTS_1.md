# FALLZONE_LOST_CARGO_QUESTS_1 — lost cargo quests from the fall zone

Filed by BENCH, 2026-09-20. A lead the owner asked to be recorded, not a
commitment. Verbatim: *"Make a note that the injected wreckage in the fall zone
can lead to quests about lost cargo of interest."*

## spec

The wreckage already injected into the fall zone is a quest surface. A manifest
is a list of things that landed somewhere and were never recovered — so reading
one can point a player at cargo still out there.

This connects to `BAZAAR_STOLEN_GOODS_PROPERTY_1`'s scanner modules, which read
provenance and manifests, but it is **not** part of The Bazaar epic and must not
be built into it. The trade window reads goods in front of you; this is what
happens when a manifest describes goods that are not.

## verify

Nothing is built from this item until it is designed and ruled. A design pass
would owe: what makes a manifest yield a lead, how a lead becomes a quest, and
which existing wreckage injection it reads.

## Watch out

- 🔴 **The player did not fall from space** (owner, 2026-09-20). The fall's
  wrecks belong to someone else; a lost-cargo quest is a claim on another
  party's property, not recovery of the player's own.
