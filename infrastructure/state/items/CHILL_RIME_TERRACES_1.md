## what

sparkling with strange interaction with hydrocarbon life"*. At the Chill's
temperatures water ice is harder than most rock — the floor's base terrain is

and have for ten thousand years, so whole districts of the floor are stepped
pale dunes of shed rime. Soft going (movement penalty), glittering loose;
snowdrifts made of molted armor.
