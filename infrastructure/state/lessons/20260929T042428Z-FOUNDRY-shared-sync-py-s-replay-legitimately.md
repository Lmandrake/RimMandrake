2026-09-29 FOUNDRY: `shared_sync.py`'s replay legitimately REWRITES the SHA of local-only commits when origin/main has moved (a rebase, not a drop) — verifying "did my commit survive?" with `git merge-base --is-ancestor <old-sha> origin/main` gives a FALSE "DROPPED" reading in exactly this case, distinct from `SHARED_SYNC_DROPS_PEER_COMMITS_1`'s real race. After a replay, if the old sha reads as not-an-ancestor, diff its tree/patch content against the new HEAD commit at the same position (`diff <(git show <old-sha> --format="") <(git show <new-sha> --format="")`) before concluding anything was lost — an empty diff means the content survived under a new hash, which is the expected, harmless outcome of a rebase-style replay.
