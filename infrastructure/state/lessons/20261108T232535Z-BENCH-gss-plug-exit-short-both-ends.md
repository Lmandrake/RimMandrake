GSS plug exits: a short cord plugged at both ends gave each end only L/2 to re-lay, and CordPlugExit.Lay skips any join closer than 0.3 to the end of what it is handed, so with L/2 < 0.9 no join (min 0.6) was tried and the raw lay kept a 160-deg hairpin 0.19 cell off the strip plug (lab4 Modern 210,102, socket 0->1 when PlugTip moved 0.05 nearer the strip). Fix: hand Lay L/2+JoinRest (joins still at most the middle, so the two ends never overlap). A small geometry move flipping a count means the rule had a cliff, not that the move was wrong.
