The live mod list is often left on a test tier (filed 3x: 2026-09-25, 09-29, 10-07). A bridge release does not restore ModsConfig; a modset_builder swap that never restored left an 11- or 13-mod tier, so the next 'full-list' restart loaded it and looked healthy; and a hand-edit of the live ModsConfig not mirrored into infrastructure/state/modlists/ModsConfig.FULL.LATEST.xml is silently reverted by the next restore (SeaShores, Bacta dropped). Before any launch meant for the full list, PARSE activeMods (never grep) and compare with FULL.LATEST; run modset_builder.py --restore when it differs; anyone enabling a mod live updates FULL.LATEST in the same step. A 'Game is loading' relay may be a subagent's quicktest on his screen: stop bridge work, verify the list, kill any RimWorld process started before the restore.
