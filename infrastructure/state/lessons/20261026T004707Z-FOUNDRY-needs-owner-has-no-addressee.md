rimflow needs=owner says nothing about WHO must answer, and --for names the seat that works the item, not the addressee: any seat sets needs=owner freely, so RimPulse once listed agent-to-agent asks as his. Since 2026-10-25 RimPulse shows a ledger ask only when it is addressed to him (filed/asked by OWNER or with --owner-said/--by-card/--per-ruling, --for OWNER, only L4 owed, or words like 'owner decides' / a reason starting 'owner:') AND it is a thing to view or a fresh ruling/fact (not answered, not built/validated, not 'deploy owed'/'awaiting live check', not FYI, not asked 3+ days ago without a re-ask). Everything else is one dim 'N not shown: ...' line. When you set needs --to owner, start the reason with 'owner:' (or 'agent:' for a question to a seat/judge) and re-ask with needs --to owner to bring a stale one back.
