/tmp (tmpfs, 18G) sat at 100% on 2026-11-03 from other workers' files: a cp backup to /tmp wrote a 64 KiB truncated copy and a later cp-restore truncated the source. Never keep a backup copy in /tmp; take it with git show HEAD:<path> or put it on ext4 (~/.seat-tmp) and cmp it before overwriting.
