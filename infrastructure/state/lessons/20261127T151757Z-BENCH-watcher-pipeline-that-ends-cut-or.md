A watcher pipeline that ends in cut (or head) prints nothing while it is alive: cut holds its output until the stream ends. The Conductor's log watcher was armed three times on 2026-11-27 as 'tail -n 0 -F log | grep --line-buffered ... | cut -c1-600' and delivered no event in 90 minutes; one NEEDS CONDUCTOR line (6:50 AM PST, UTINNI_WORLDMAP_FLIGHT_ICON_1) was missed and found by a hand read. Proof on a scratch file: the old pipeline wrote 0 bytes after a planted matching line; a polling loop (wc -c, then tail -c of the new bytes through grep and cut, so each pipeline ends) printed the planted line and left out two lines that must not match. The working command is in src/RimMandrake/rimflow/roles/conductor.md, Start of turn. Three expiries with no events were read as a quiet log; read the half hour by hand at each expiry.
