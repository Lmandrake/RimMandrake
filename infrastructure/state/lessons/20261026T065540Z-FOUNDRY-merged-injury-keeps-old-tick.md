RimWorld 1.6: a hediff's tickAdded does not identify the killing wound. Hediff_Injury.TryMergeWith folds a new Burn/Crush/Shredded/Frostbite (injuryProps.canMerge) into the existing wound of its def on the same part, and the survivor keeps its OLD tickAdded. To know what killed a pawn, record DamageInfo in ThingComp.Notify_Killed (ThingWithComps.Kill calls it inside Pawn.Kill before DeathActionWorker.PawnDied, which is not given the dinfo). Also: vanilla Building_Heater has no CompHeatPusher (it heats in its own TickRare via CompTempControl), so code that finds 'heaters' by CompHeatPusher misses the colony's main heater.
