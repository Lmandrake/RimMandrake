Context-strategy sim (src/RimMandrake/Utils/sim/ctx_strategy_sim.py, D:\ClaudeWork\rimmandrake\perpetual_ctx\sim_results.md): measured over 49 BENCH sessions, 11,025 API calls. Under the 1-h TTL, gaps up to 60 min are 99.6-100% cache-warm and gaps over 1 h are 98-100% full re-writes, which are 7.5% of spend. A 5-min TTL would cost 1.69x on the same calls, since 33% of round-opening calls come after a gap over 5 min. BENCH's real practice (1M window, handoff near 350k) costs ~164k input-token-equivalents per round at a 251k mean context. A fixed handoff at ~125k, or a stateless restart every round, simulates ~36% cheaper. The interactive wake only shares 27k of its 77k prefix with the previous session's cache, so ~50k is written fresh on every restart, and that wake cost is the biggest lever: with a fully cached wake, handoff at 75k saves 35% vs 200k auto-compaction. Clearing tool results in place does not pay, because tool results are only 17% of context growth.
