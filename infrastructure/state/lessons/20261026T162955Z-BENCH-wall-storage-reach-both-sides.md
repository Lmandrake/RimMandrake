Two RimWorld engine traps for wall-mounted storage (cold ladder review 2026-10-26). (1) An item on a wall cell is listed in the regions on BOTH sides (RegionListersUpdater.CanRegisterInAdjacentRegions is true), so an OnCell->Touch reach patch lets pawns take it from the back of the wall too; vanilla and RimFridge behave the same, so this is accepted, not a bug. (2) def.blockLight is read live by the darkness/lighting section layers, but GlowGrid.lightBlockers is written only at Building.SpawnSetup/DeSpawn: a setting that flips blockLight in play must call map.glowGrid.LightBlockerAdded/Removed on built things, or a phantom blocker outlives the building (fixed in ColdStorage bac56b6b50).
