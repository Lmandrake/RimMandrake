A network call or a landing (git fetch, land.sh) inside an ops_watch collector runs under the metronome's 15 s sense kill. A kill blinds the whole family and aborts the call, so the work never finishes and every beat repeats it. Never put a fetch or a land in a collector: give it its own unit and let the collector only read (seat_dirt.tidy moved to rm-seat-tidy.timer, 9708495bcd, after the disk family went blind for 4 h and then for 5 h on 2026-11-22; a fetch deadline under the phase limit did not stop it, because the land after the fetch still ran past the kill).
