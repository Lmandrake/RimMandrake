./publish refuses (exit 2, 'not mine to overwrite') when the DLL needing a rebuild is dirty in the working tree from a peer's in-progress build, and every unpushed commit then waits on that peer. Route that worked (2026-10-26, LeaningScrub): src-only scratch clone at HEAD, winbuild there, hash-object the dll+srchash, commit via a private GIT_INDEX_FILE + commit-tree + update-ref, then update-index --cacheinfo the real index to HEAD for those two paths (else status shows MM, a staged revert). The peer's working-tree DLL is untouched. publish.rebuild_dlls could do this itself instead of refusing.
