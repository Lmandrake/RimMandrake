A biome sheet's 'MEASURED there' position can be a misquote: the_scald.md said 'arc 0-30' while quoting centre (35,185) in (arc,bearing) in the same sentence. Before moving tiles to match a sheet, trace the position to the founding ruling (worldgen_interactive_def.md axis rulings, ASHKARR_WORLD_DEFINITION gazetteer, paint_ashkarr constants in git) - the sheet can be the drifted side. Also: a lake's temperature painted at one elevation is not re-derived when its elevation is later re-ruled (Scald -7.8 C = 1,411 m lapse).
