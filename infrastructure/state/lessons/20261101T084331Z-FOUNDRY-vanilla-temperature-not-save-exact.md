Any deterministic sim that must continue identically across save/load reads TileTemperaturesComp.OutdoorTemperatureAt(tile, TicksAbs) + GameConditionManager.AggregateTemperatureOffset (internal in 1.6: AccessTools.MethodDelegate) outdoors, and quantises indoor reads to 1/16 C.
