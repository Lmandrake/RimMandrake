jawa/settlement_remove mode=settlement on a PLAYER Settlement whose map was already dropped (mode=map force=true) fails with 'Abandon threw: NullReferenceException': SettlementAbandonUtility expects a map. The mapless player Settlement then persists in the world until a save reload. Order matters: remove the settlement (or abandon it) while the map still exists, or do not create a player Settlement in a save you will keep; a reload is the only clean-up. Workaround context: a player-faction Settlement precreated with world_objects_add avoids the TILEMAP_GENERATE NRE loop. 2026-10-26 live wave.
