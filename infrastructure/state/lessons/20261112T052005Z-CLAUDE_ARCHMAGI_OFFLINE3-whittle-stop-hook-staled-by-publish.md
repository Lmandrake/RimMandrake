The Whittle Stop hook (.claude/hooks/whittle_stop.py, last_change) calls evidence stale when ANY project file outside the Whittle folder is newer, so ./publish itself makes finished evidence stale: landing pulls other lanes' commits (9, then 7 within ~5 min on 2026-11-11 with ~7 loops running), their files get fresh mtimes, and re-run evidence + re-judge + publish never converges. Measured on CATHEDRAL_MISSION_BOON_OFFERS_1: the re-run at 4de5e82144 was byte-identical apart from stamps, yet the next publish (6d21a97ac8) re-staled it. A fix belongs to the hook's owner (judge staleness by the item's own touched paths or by git content rather than tree mtimes); until then a round whose work, evidence and judge are already published should stop and let the 3-refusal STALLED valve end the turn, never touch mtimes to pass.
