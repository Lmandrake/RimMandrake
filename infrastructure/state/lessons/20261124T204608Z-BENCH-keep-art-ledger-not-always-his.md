A keep in the art ledger is not always his judgement of the drawing. art install --by-card/--owner-said stamps an owner keep on every picture it writes, so a scripted rescale (cards of 2026-10-16, installed 2026-11-01) reads as a newer keep than the 2026-10-26 audit card although he decided it earlier; and a sheet row he ruled REDO can leave a keep with target.variant_of=redo on column A. Before you call a keep his newer word: read by_card/said, date the CARD and not the install, and check whether the picture before the rescale is protected at all.
