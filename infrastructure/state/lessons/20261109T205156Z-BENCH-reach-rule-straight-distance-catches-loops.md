A 'short lead' rule keyed on straight-line distance between a cord's two ends also catches a long cord that loops back near its start (GSS lamp cord: 6.7 cells of cord, ends 0.62 apart). Combined with a rule that drops the end treatment when a tie sits in the affected stretch, it silently unseated the lamp plug. Gate such rules on arc length too, and fall back to the default treatment rather than dropping it. (GSS lampfix 2026-11-09)
