A whole-repo export into tmpfs /tmp can be OOM-killed mid-stream by the 6 GiB tool cap (tracked tree 3.58 GB; tar plus extract is about 7 GB). The truncated tree then gives a confident wrong proof result: DONOR_OFF worker read flyers 5 and FAIL offline where the truth was 24 and PASS. Treat 'tar: Unexpected EOF' or exit 137 in an export step as no result. Measured: infrastructure/state/facts/oom_kills_2026-11-03.md
