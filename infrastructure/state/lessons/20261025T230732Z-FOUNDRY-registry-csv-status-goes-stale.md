The mineral registry CSV listed three rows as BUILT (seep-salt, delta salt, fulgurite) whose defs were long gone: the two salts had been merged into RM_RawSalt (EnvironmentalHazards RM_MaterialMerges_Aliases.xml) and fulgurite excised by the owner (b9c028109a). It also listed RUT_TibannaGas, which never existed. A free-text 'defs' column cannot be checked; that is why the CSV now has a machine-read 'bindings' column that gen_mineral_registry.py resolves against the def dump plus the repo XML. Trust a sheet's status only after resolving its defNames.
