A world-map pattern meant to span many tiles (Twilight Sea stained glass) must not be painted into a biome FACE texture: a face repeats every ~3 tiles, so at planet zoom the pattern becomes a camouflage speckle (round-2 in-face try, Transient/world_water_review_r2_2026-10-24.html, dropped). Use RM_WorldLook's swirl layer instead: world-planar UVs, its own texture per biome, settable repeat. Also: a dark sea face is LIFTED by the global sea films (deep film navy at 0.5 over a value-22 face reads 39 and rings each island), so 'darker than every other sea' needs a per-sea film exemption, not just a darker face. And a dark still water face (The Chill) breaks the biome_worldfaces water rule (val 60-140, sd 3-9) on the owner's order, so record the exception before the verdict refuses it.
