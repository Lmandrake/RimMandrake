The art-eval fauna loop had no way to give a creature fresh regen rounds: rounds were derived (REGEN rows in runs/fauna_*/decisions.jsonl, regen_rounds.json max-merged) and job ids were arteval_<DEF>_rN, so a hand reset would both be overwritten by the max-merge and collide with old job ids in fill_queue. Fix (3a69bd5110): fauna_drive.py reset appends round_resets.jsonl and job ids carry the series (s1r1). Also: fauna_drive.latest() picked the 'newest' candidate decision by run-dir NAME sort, so a run named fauna_contagion_* would lose to an older fauna_leaningscrub_*; it now compares the decision 'at' times.
