A hose-to-hose mate that keeps both lengths (R6) cannot hold the coupling at the point where the hoses were laid. Any bend toward the partner lengthens the run to that point, so the coupling must slide back toward the inner corner (MatePairG2). Near-taut short hoses, such as the hose_hard_fittings mateA/mateB station (4.72 of material against a 4.59 chord, with a 37-degree turn), still have no local mate. They need a lay-level re-route or mate (step 4). The old S5 bar 'curvature jump under 0.2 kmax over half a cell' is stricter than the clothoid dk/ds cap, so a G2 mate is judged by the ruler instead.
