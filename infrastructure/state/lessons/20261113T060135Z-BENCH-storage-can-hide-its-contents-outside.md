Storage can hide its contents outside its own ThingDef: Adaptive Storage puts showContainedItems=false on a separate AdaptiveStorage.GraphicsDef naming the def in targetDefs. A closed/open rule that reads only the def's comps and modExtensions reads such storage as open (the Armoury's hidden smuggling panels, found by selftest_explosiveknockback_storage_census.py). Join the GraphicsDefs when classifying storage.
