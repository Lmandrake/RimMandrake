A tile's temperature is not re-derived when its elevation changes: the Scald's 312 tiles went from a perched 1,411 m lake to -350 m (2026-08-21 ruling) but kept the 1,411 m temperature (7.8 C of lapse) for two months, in every record and the dev save. When a ruling or edit moves elevation, re-stamp temp_c from world_physics_audit.t_intent (arc curve minus 5.5 C/km above sea level) in the same change; jawa/world_tile_import takes a partial tile,temp_c CSV.
