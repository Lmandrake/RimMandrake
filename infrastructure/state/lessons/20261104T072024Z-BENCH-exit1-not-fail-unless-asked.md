A live proof's exit 1 is a FAIL only if the proof asked the game and got an answer. Of 73 historical exit-1 runs that Relentless scored as mod FAILs, only 22 were real: 24 were proofs that could not read (a traceback after the last verdict, read None, NOT REPRODUCED), 22 were harness faults (IndeterminateError, WSL bridge errors, ENOSPC) and 5 ran on the wrong tier or a stale deploy. Read the log tail before you score a strike (relentless_live.could_not_ask). A k=None beside real values on the same line is a partial read, not a blind one. But never erase an observed violation: a FAIL line or failed assert that reports None keeps its FAIL (the None may be the defect), and the worker classifies it; that moved 8 of the 24 dud readings back to FAIL.
