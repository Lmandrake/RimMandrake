Vanilla PawnTweener (1.6) springs DrawPos toward the logical position at 0.09*deltaTime*60*TickRateMultiplier per frame: a walking pawn draws ~0.85 cell behind Position at any speed, and while PAUSED the tween freezes (a pawn drawn every frame never resets), so bridge step_game_ticks + capture shows pawns at stale spots. Any draw math keyed on pawn.Position but applied to DrawPos (pit sink clamp, PIT_BOAR_FIX_SUNBATHES_ON_THE_WALL) must key on the drawn position instead.
