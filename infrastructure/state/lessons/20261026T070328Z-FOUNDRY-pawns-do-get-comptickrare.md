A PAWN does get CompTickRare in 1.6: Pawn.Tick runs base.Tick() then "if (this.IsHashIntervalTick(250)) TickRare();", and ThingWithComps.TickRare calls every comp's CompTickRare (decompiled rw16 Verse/Pawn.cs). Only NON-pawn Normal-ticker things (buildings) miss it. Never add a CompTick that calls CompTickRare at IsHashIntervalTick(250) on a pawn comp: it runs the rare logic twice on the same tick (DivingInteraction orruhmu swelled and squirted in one tick; Tarnn zapped double). A RimSage reading said the opposite and was recorded in gpt_reviews/VERDICTS.md B1; lint_ticker_compat.py already had it right. Read Pawn.Tick, not ThingWithComps alone.
