A GPT shrink to 256 px cannot be emitted directly, because gpt-image's minimum is about 655k px. What worked for 9 facings (Bloodlurk C, Scaldhide B, Ribbonwhip B, 2026-10-24, commit 72c734649f): first a Codex edit at 1024 asking for a redraw 'for 256 display', with thin parts thickened to 8 px or more, a dark outline about 4 px, and the palette locked to the original (name the palette and say it stays exactly as it is). Then a premultiplied BOX reduction to 256 (PIL RGBa resize BOX). Without the palette lock, colour drifted 13.7 (reds brightened). With it, drift fell to about 7. GPT also drifted the subject size 9-11% on one facing; conform_sprite.py against the original fixed that. Expect validate_sprite to WARN area +16-35%: that is the intended thickening, not a defect. Earlier pure-BOX shrinks of chunky creatures (TunnelSnake, Vurra, Durrok, Surrik) were not mutilated. The GPT redraw matters for thin limbs, tails and whips.
