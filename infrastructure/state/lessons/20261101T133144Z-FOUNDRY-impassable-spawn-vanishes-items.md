GenSpawn.Spawn defaults to WipeMode.Vanish, and GenSpawn.SpawningWipes (1.6) returns true for an Item under a new Impassable building, so a mid-game spawn of a wall/plug/blocker silently DELETES loot, weapons and corpses on its cells. Use WipeMode.VanishOrMoveAside (precedent RM_Settling.cs:293). Found in RM_HiveSealing resin plugs (fixed 1af8f6aafc); other mid-game impassable spawns with the default are worth one grep.
