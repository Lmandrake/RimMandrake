A GenStep that lays a long line of Impassable, undeconstructible things (a rounded radial trunk, a wall run) can cut a small pocket map in two: on the 50x50 Chill floor the Return Comb horseshoe plus two 22-cell busbar trunks sealed a wedge off in about half of all maps. A rounded diagonal line is a staircase, and RimWorld regions are 4-connected and pathing will not cut a blocked corner, so even a 'thin' diagonal line is a wall. A 1-cell gap is not enough at a staircase corner; leave at least 2 consecutive line steps open, matched by cell. Check any such GenStep with an offline flood-fill over many random sites before shipping.
