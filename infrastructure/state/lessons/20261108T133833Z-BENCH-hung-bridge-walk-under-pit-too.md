PAWN_WALKS_UNDER_DECK staging: a pit only 3 squares north-south cannot show a pawn crossing under a deck over its middle row. The pit sink draws a floor pawn D x 0.3 cells south (cap 2.4 at 4 m) and ClampSinkToLip holds its drawn centre at or north of the south lip, so at 4 m the south-row pawn is drawn at the lip (bz) and the north-row pawn at bz+0.1: both frames show it south of the deck (drawn bz+0.82..bz+2), never on the other side. ralph/live/ladders5.py's bridge part (4x3 pit, 8 levels) stages exactly that. For a visible crossing the AFTER row must be drawn wholly north of the deck: z0 - sink - 0.75 > deck north edge, i.e. at 2 m (sink 1.2) walk to 3 rows north of the deck row (pit at least 5 rows), at 4 m (sink 2.4) 4 rows north (pit at least 6 rows).
