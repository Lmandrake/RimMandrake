A pawn's death DOES reach Destroy(KillFinalize): Pawn.Kill despawns the pawn into its corpse, then calls base.Kill (ThingWithComps.Kill -> Thing.Kill -> Destroy(KillFinalize)) unless already destroyed, so a comp's PostDestroy(KillFinalize) fires and the dead pawn inside a corpse is Destroyed (decompiled 1.6 Pawn.cs ~2260, Thing.cs 1047). The catch: PostDestroy's map is base.MapHeld at that moment, which resolves through the corpse and is null when no corpse was placed (no corpseDef, placement failed, died in a container). For death logic prefer ThingComp.Notify_Killed(prevMap, dinfo), which ThingWithComps.Kill calls right after, with pawn.prevMap. (Corrects a wrong 2026-10-26 lesson claiming PostDestroy never fires on pawn death.) Separately: Thing.Destroy never drops or destroys an IThingHolder's contents; a holder Thing must spill or ClearAndDestroyContents in its own Destroy override (RM_Thing_CargoFloat).
