RimWorld 1.6 has a study mechanism outside Anomaly. A thing with CompProperties_CompAnalyzableUnlockResearch (fields canStudyInPlace, analysisRequiredRange, showProgress, progress and completed letters, analysisID) is studied by JobDriver_StudyItem, in place when canStudyInPlace is true; a ResearchProjectDef that lists the thing in requiredAnalyzed stays locked until the count is met, and the research screen names what is not studied. Progress is kept by analysisID in Find.AnalysisManager, so it outlives the thing. Biotech's mechanitor chips use it on items; no vanilla building uses it, so prove it once live on a building before a bar leans on it. Anomaly's CompStudiable (gated on monolith level) is a different comp: RAKATAN_ARCHOTECH_MACHINES_1 ruling 9 ruled out 'study' on the CompStudiable reading only. Read from RimSage 2026-11-26.
