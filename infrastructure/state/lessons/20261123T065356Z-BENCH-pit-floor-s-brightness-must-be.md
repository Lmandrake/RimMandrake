A pit floor's brightness must be read from its CENTRE square: a square on a pit's south edge carries a light band (45.6 vs 27.4 red at 8 m), and a whole-square mean there read one colour at every depth while the shade drew correctly (FlowWorks, ArchBig, 2026-11-22). A darkening shade must also be pure black: a (10,8,7) tint over a 34/255 floor drew 16 m at 0.535 of 2 m where the law said 0.34.
