A picture cut into pieces that join across squares (a strip, an atlas edge, a lane piece) can be seamless as pixels and still draw a dark hairline at every join in game: RimWorld sets no wrap mode on a mod texture, so Unity's default Repeat makes the bilinear sample at a quad's edge blend in the piece's opposite edge (often a transparent end). Clamp the piece texture where its material is made (RM_DeckArt.PieceMat). An offline seam check on the composed PNGs cannot see this; model the GPU sampling (bilinear, mipmaps, sub-pixel phase, Cutout alpha) with the shipped wrap mode, as selftest_flowworks_jetty_seams.py check 6 does. Measured 2026-11-26 on ArchSmall: a 21% dip at the join before, at most 2% after.
