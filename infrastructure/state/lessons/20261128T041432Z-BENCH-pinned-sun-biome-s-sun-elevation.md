A pinned-sun biome's sun elevation depends on the map's tile, not on the biome: the same RM_LongShade regenerate read E 83 on one quicktest tile (crown shadow under the tree) and E 15.2 on another. To stage a low sun, start quicktests until map_info latitude/longitude gives 90 - arc(lat, lon to the substellar 0,0) in range, then regenerate. Never expect an offset from a previous world's E.
