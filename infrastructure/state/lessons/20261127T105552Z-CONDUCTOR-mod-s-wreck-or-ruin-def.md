A mod's wreck or ruin def that must stand on any ground AND on a donor mod's special floor names no terrainAffordanceNeeded. Read from the 1.6 source (GenConstruct.CanPlaceBlueprintOver) and Vanilla Factory Expanded's Terrain_Floors.xml on 2026-11-27, not yet seen in play: the game accepts a floor blueprint under a standing building only when the building names no ground need or the new floor's affordances hold that need, and VFEF_FactoryFloor's only affordance is FactoryFloor. A wreck that names Heavy can neither get factory floor laid under it nor be installed on factory floor. Found when a fresh reader caught a design bar (repair a found wreck where it stands) that the design's own ground rule refused (WreckedMachines stage 3).
