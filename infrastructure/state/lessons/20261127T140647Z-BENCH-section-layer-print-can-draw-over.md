A section-layer print can draw over or under a pawn only by its vertex height (the depth test), never by its own sub-mesh or material: MapDrawLayer.RefreshSubMeshBounds sets every section sub-mesh's bounds to the section rect at y 0, so the renderer draws every section mesh before every pawn (all Custom/Cutout, queue 2900). Measured on SteveDesktop 2026-11-27: moving a cable material's queue after the pawn changed 41 edge pixels. To put a rope behind a pawn, print its vertices below the pawn's lowest drawn node (AltitudeLayer.PawnRope); a separate material or section layer changes nothing. Read both with jawa/cell_draw_census (vertY, squareY) and jawa/pawn_render_nodes.
