A vanilla comp that does its work in BOTH CompTick and CompTickRare (CompHeatPusher: PushHeat every 60 in CompTick and x4.1666 in CompTickRare) assumes its thing runs exactly one ticker. A Pawn runs both (Pawn.Tick -> base.Tick comps CompTick, plus TickRare at IsHashIntervalTick(250)), so such a comp on an animal does its work twice: a heat pusher on a pawn heats ~2x. Before putting a vanilla building comp on a pawn, grep it for both overrides; override CompTickRare to return when parent is Pawn. Found: Wastes Smolderback (fixed), Pyrelands furnace beast (HEATPUSHER_FIX_PAWN_DOUBLE_PUSH).
