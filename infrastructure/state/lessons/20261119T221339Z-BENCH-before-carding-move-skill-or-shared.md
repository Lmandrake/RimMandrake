Before carding a move of a skill or shared file, count its readers with a repo-wide search and put the count on the card. On 2026-11-19 the Conductor carded the move of generating-images and editing-images as one dependency (gpt_consult.py); the helper found about 60 readers including the live art daemon rm-artpiped, and a plain move would have broken the art pipeline at its next restart (item RIMMANDRAKE_MOVE_IMAGE_SKILLS_TO_WORKFLOWS_GPT_TOOL_BORROWS, 97fd9a31ed).
