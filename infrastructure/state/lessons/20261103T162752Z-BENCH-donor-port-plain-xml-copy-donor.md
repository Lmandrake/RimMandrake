A donor port (plain-XML copy of a donor def, e.g. 2026-10-18 AB_* -> RM_* retirement) copies the donor's XML, NOT what our patches did to it: RM_AlienGrass/RM_HardyGrass/RM_ToxiGrass silently lost the FloorEmplacement layer a RUT patch gave AB_AlienGrass/AB_HardyGrass/AB_ToxiGrass (ROT_MOSS_VERIFY_UNDER_THE_MUSHROOMS, 2026-11-03). When porting, grep src/**/Patches for the donor defName and fold every patch into the port; re-run the census that motivated the patch (plant_draw_layers.py).
