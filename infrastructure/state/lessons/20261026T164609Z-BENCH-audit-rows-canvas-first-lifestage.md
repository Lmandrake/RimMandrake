Facing-audit redraw rows (mkrows_audit.py, 2026-10-26) sized the canvas from the FIRST life stage's drawSize, but runner.py judges the canvas against the card's size.drawSize = the race's adult max, so a shared-texture race whose baby is small gets a too-small canvas and the judged render is HOLD_CANVAS before any model (RM_Thermadon row 256 for drawsize 1.2, adult 3.0 wants 512; RSW_Blixus 256 vs adult 6.0 wants 1024). Conversely a life-stage variant texture (RSW_Silooth__Silooth_j, RSW_Fambaa__Fambaa_j) must be judged at ITS stage's drawSize, not the adult's, or a correct 256 juvenile is held. And view-only redraws (new north/south beside an installed east whose canvas predates the rule) were held on every facing because canvas_mismatch checked the installed east too. Fix: policy.canvas_mismatch skips card.installed_facings and accepts a new facing on the installed facings' canvas (selftest_art_eval covers it); batch drv.py writes installed_facings for view specs and the stage drawSize for variant specs; Thermadon/Blixus re-filed at 512/1024.
