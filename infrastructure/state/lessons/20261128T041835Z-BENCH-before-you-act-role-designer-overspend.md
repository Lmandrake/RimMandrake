Before you act on a role:Designer:overspend_day alarm, subtract the phantom charges. A Designer run that never started, or that closed with no tokens, is charged its whole budget (role_launch.run_spend, designer_stage 'unmeasured'). A Fable-pinned stage budget is 7.94M, so one failed launch adds 7.94M to the day. On 2026-11-27, 4 such runs gave 31.8M of the 58.0M that WreckedMachines stage.json reads as spent, and one raised the 09:15Z repeat alarm (9.98M = 2.04M real + 7.94M). Read the measured day from state.json roles.Designer.runs (tokens). Also: the mod_model pin scales the run caps by 2x but not daily_token_cap. So a Fable-pinned Designer day passes the 8M cap at its normal amount of work.
