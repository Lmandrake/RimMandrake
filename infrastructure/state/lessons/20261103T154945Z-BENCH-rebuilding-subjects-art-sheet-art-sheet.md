Rebuilding a --subjects art sheet (art_sheet.py --subjects ... --name X --date D) rewrites its COMMITTED snapshot infrastructure/state/art/sheets/X_sheet_D.snapshot.json and can re-letter its columns (sluice_panning_warden 2026-11-03: A/B singles gone, new E set) under an existing decisions file whose picks are column letters. Do not commit a rebuilt snapshot for a sheet that has owner picks unless art.py remap has run; restore it with git show HEAD:<path> > <path>.
