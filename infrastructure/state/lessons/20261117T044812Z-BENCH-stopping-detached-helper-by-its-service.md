Stopping a detached helper by its .service name hangs for the full 10-minute stop timeout while the agent keeps working: the stop signal reaches the claude_bounded.sh wrapper, not claude, which lives in the claude-seat-<SEAT>-<unit>.scope. Stop the SCOPE (systemctl --user stop claude-seat-BENCH-<unit>.scope); the service then ends and its return step runs. Snapshot the clone's uncommitted work first (git diff --binary HEAD and a tar of untracked files): the return step bundles and resets the clone, and a --resume relaunch starts in a fresh one. Measured 2026-11-16 on the Gimme Some Slack Fable run.
