Pawn_JobTracker.TryTakeOrderedJob sets job.playerForced=true for ANY pawn, and 1.6 never interrupts a playerForced job on damage (Notify_DamageTaken: !curJob.playerForced). A comp that steers a wild/AI animal with TryTakeOrderedJob makes it ignore being shot and cuts through mental states, flees and sleep; use pawn.jobs.StartJob(job, JobCondition.InterruptForced, ...) and only from idle jobs (null/Wait/Wait_Wander/GotoWander). JobDef.isIdle is no filter for this: Flee and FleeAndCower are isIdle. Also: Goto (PathEndMode.OnCell) to an impassable cell fails CanReach silently every time; target a standable reachable cell beside the thing. Found in CompKrizzak (KRIZZAK_LIGHTCHASE_FIX_IDLE_REACHABLE).
