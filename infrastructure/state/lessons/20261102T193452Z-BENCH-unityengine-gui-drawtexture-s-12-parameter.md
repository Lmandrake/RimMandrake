UnityEngine.GUI.DrawTexture's 12-parameter leaf (the only overload holding the 'null texture passed to GUI.DrawTexture' warning) is INTERNAL in RimWorld 1.6's IMGUIModule, as are the 9- and 11-param forwarders: a Harmony target lookup with BindingFlags.Public silently lands on a forwarder the JIT may inline. Use Public|NonPublic and check method flags with dnfile (WORLD_NULLTEX_FLOOD_HUNT_NEEDS_A_STACK).
