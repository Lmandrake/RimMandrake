Then regenerate the derived queue views with `rimflow render -- --overwrite-queues` (that flag belongs to `render.py`, must come after `--`, and `reindex` does not accept it) rather than hand-merging them. ⛔ Never resolve it with `checkout --ours/--theirs` — that silently discards a concurrent seat's events.
