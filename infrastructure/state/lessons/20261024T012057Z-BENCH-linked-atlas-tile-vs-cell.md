Compose and mock against the SHOWN cell: pipefit/compose.py drew on the whole tile and its mock rendered the whole tile, so couplings hidden in the padding, an elbow kink and detached caps all looked fine offline. Also: Graphic_Linked repeats one period every cell, so any broad feature in a strip (a repair band, a blotch, a mirrored palindrome period) reads as per-cell dashes; cut a seamless non-mirrored period and damp broad along-pipe colour.
