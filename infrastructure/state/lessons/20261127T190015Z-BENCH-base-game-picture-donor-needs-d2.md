A picture that replaces a base-game building picture must be made at drawSize x 128 px per cell, not on the base game's canvas. The base game ships many building pictures at 64 px per cell (the psychic emanator: 128 x 128 for a 2 x 2 drawSize), and the art judge refuses a picture of ours at that size under rule D2 of design/RimMandrake/art_judge_brief.md (it refused the Wrecked Machines emanator on 2026-11-27; a second judge kept the power cell at the same fault, so two judges disagreed). The image model's drawing is 1408 px, so nothing is lost by fitting onto a reference enlarged to the D2 size; only the reference is enlarged (grab_source_art.py SMALL_CANVAS_SCALE). A thin small silhouette also needs a fresh drawing more often: the outline limit is a share of the body, which is a few dozen pixels on a 10 000 px body.
