FilthMaker.TryMakeFilth(c, map, def) defaults shouldPropagate=true: when c already holds that filth at the hard cap (Filth thickness 5, !CanBeThickened) or is not WalkableByAny, the deposit spills onto a random 8-way neighbour checked only for WalkableByAny, ignoring any Roofed/Standable gate the caller applied to c. A repeated 'saturate this cell' deposit therefore creeps outward and under roofs, and every call on a saturated cell pays 8 neighbour searches. For a deliberate per-cell mark pass shouldPropagate:false and early-out on !CanBeThickened (CHILLPRINTS_FIX_SATURATED_DEPOSIT_PROPAGATES; decomp rw16 RimWorld/FilthMaker.cs).
