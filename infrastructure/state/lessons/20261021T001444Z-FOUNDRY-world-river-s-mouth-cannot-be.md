A world river's mouth cannot be inferred from geometry: on Ash'karr 3 of 11 canonical mouths are neither the lowest tile nor the biggest-river leaf (7 do not border a sea), and 2 links are level. To re-stamp canonical riverDist, carry the target (tile,river_dist) as data and derive the lay order from it (river_lay.order_links_mouth_first); never trust a save-exported links CSV's row order (parity U4, 2026-10-20).
