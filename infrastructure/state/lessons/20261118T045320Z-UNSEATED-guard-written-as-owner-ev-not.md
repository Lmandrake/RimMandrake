A guard written as `owner_ev is not None` does not mean 'we have a reading': CT.owner_events returns a plain {} for an UNREADABLE ledger shard, which passes that guard, so every tsn is 'not in' it. Measured by probe in designer_stage.source_errors: owner_ev={} reports 'OWNER:<real tsn> finds no event in OWNER.jsonl' — a true event of his called a forgery, in the same words a genuine forgery gets — while owner_ev=None reports nothing at all, the opposite behaviour for an almost identical cause. Key the could-not-look case on FALSINESS and give it its own UNMEASURED message. The landed fix in inventory_errors (895f0834c356) is the worked example; the twin at l.347 is filed as RIMMANDRAKE_SPLIT_UNREADABLE_LEDGER_FROM_FORGED_SOURCE_CITATION. Check what a helper actually RETURNS for the unreadable case before trusting any is-None guard over it.
