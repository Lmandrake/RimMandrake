The tick reason 'game_only' on a mod at an offline stage does not mean 'waiting for a free game'. It means its open road-back items are game-only faults and the offline stage gives none of them a run, so the mod is stuck until its record moves. On 2026-11-20 the Conductor told the owner all day that FlowWorks was waiting for the game; it had sat 497 minutes. Read the whole reason line (fold it; the line is cut at 200 characters) before repeating it.
