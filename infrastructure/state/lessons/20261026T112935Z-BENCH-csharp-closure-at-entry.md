C# allocates a lambda's closure (display class) at the ENTRY of the scope that owns the captured variable, on every call, even when the lambda never runs. A ThreadPool lambda in HoseMateCache.Get cost 32 B per pair per frame on pure cache hits; EnsureLay and DrawAll's FindAll had the same hidden per-frame allocation. Fix: move the lambda into its own helper method (Queue, LayAgain, JointsBefore). Measure with GC.GetAllocatedBytesForCurrentThread after a warm-up call (SelfTest HoseFrameMemoChecks F2-F4). Also: foreach over an IList<T> boxes the enumerator; use an indexed loop on hot paths.
