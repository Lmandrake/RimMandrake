The steward drops a lane-unstick item as 'the lane recovered by itself' as soon as the lane shows RUNNING. In this case (STEVE_LANE_UNSTICK_BRAKED_UNOWNED_8EB8, 2026-11-04T08:09Z) the recovery came from a helper's published fix (849cbd013f, 7ff9de03cd), and the helper was still working the item. The drop is terminal, so the fixer cannot close the item with its sha. The ledger now credits the repair to nobody. Before it drops, the steward should check for a live claim or lease on the item, or for a commit since the brake that touches testq or the worker, and leave the item to its owner.
