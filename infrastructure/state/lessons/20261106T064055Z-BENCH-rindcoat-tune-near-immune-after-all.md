RINDCOAT_TUNE_NEAR_IMMUNE_AFTER_ALL: an apparel stat that a hazard clock reads (HazardTargeting.SumApparelStat -> GetStatValue) is scaled by Core's StatPart_Quality for every ArmorRating_* stat (awful x0.60 .. legendary x1.80, ArmorRatingBase in Stats_Apparel.xml).
