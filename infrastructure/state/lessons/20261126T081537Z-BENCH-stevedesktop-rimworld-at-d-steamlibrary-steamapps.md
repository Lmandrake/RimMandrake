On SteveDesktop, RimWorld is at D:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\RimWorld, not Archmagi's C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam path. An offline harness that pins the Archmagi Managed path (csproj HintPath, or an AssemblyResolve probe) builds with MSB3245 and fails there. Take the Managed folder from an override (the pilot reads RIMWORLD_MANAGED).
