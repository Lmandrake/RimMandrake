Word overlap between a trap and a later commit subject is no evidence the trap was overtaken: on the real GSS/EK/FW ledgers (2026-11-16) a >=3-shared-word, >=40% rule dropped 10 traps and all 10 were false (other mods' commits, restatements, the very build the trap described). The miner now drops deterministically only on hard signals (the quoted words landed in the mod's files, a ticket the trap names closed later, a ticket filed about the limit closed later and its close speaks to it) and sends word-overlap candidates to the claude -p currency reader (modledger_reader.CURRENT_SYSTEM).
