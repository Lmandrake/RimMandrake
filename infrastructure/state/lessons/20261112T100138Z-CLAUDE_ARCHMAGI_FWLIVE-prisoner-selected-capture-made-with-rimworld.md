A 'prisoner selected' capture made with rimworld/select_pawn shows the PREVIOUS selection: select_pawn refuses any non-colonist (prisoner, wild, hostile) and the screenshot still succeeds. Select with jawa/select_things ids=<ThingID> action=select, then rimworld/open_inspect_tab inspectTabId=Health|Prisoner|Needs (retry ~0.5 s: no tabs exist until the pane opens), then take_screenshot. Also: jawa/clear_ui defaults hideHud=true for 20 s, which hides the inspect pane, so pass hideHudSeconds=1 before a player-view capture. Found in pit_exposure_live.py, 2026-11-12.
