The bridge call log's times (bridge_calls.jsonl, field t) have 1-second resolution: an interval union of calls merges every short call ending in the same second and undercounts busy time threefold (80 s against 241 s summed call ms on GSS run 4d0e9a2ae543, 2026-11-18). The client is serial, so busy time is the sum of call ms; treat a sum above the wall as overlap and report waits unknown.
