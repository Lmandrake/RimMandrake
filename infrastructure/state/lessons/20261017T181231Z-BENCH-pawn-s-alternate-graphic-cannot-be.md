A pawn's alternate graphic cannot be forced through Thing.overrideGraphicIndex: PawnGraphicUtils.TryGetAlternate rolls kindDef.alternateGraphicChance seeded by thingIDNumber and reads nothing else. To switch an animal's look on a state (e.g. the brine-whitened taozin grub), set alternateGraphicChance 0, put the look in alternateGraphics, and postfix TryGetAlternate (RSW_TaozinPalePatch); vanilla then draws body, rotting and dessicated from it.
