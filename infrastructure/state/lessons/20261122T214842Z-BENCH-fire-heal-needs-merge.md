A cell-automaton fire that lets a carried fire light a second cell beside it (to close the gaps between one-cell lanes) floods unless a carry that meets a fire already burning lights nothing: each sideways cell takes a neighbour lane's forward cell, that lane steps diagonally into the next lane's cell, and the shove runs out to the flank, which then creeps 45 degrees a row (Pyrelands model, 2026-11-22: 10,600 cells burned against 1,440 in a day). Merge on collision first, then heal; and anchor the heal only on another lane or a recent burn so the outermost lane never heals outward. A sideways wobble of the carry, tried for raggedness, diffused the flanks and put fires in bar fire_walks_with_wind's outer lanes on 1-2 of 40 seeds; a wind-driven burn scar with straight flanks is the natural look, so no wobble.
