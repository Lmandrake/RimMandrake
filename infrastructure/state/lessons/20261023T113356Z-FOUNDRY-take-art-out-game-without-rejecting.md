To take art OUT of the game without rejecting it (a v2 deferral, a cut feature that may return), use artledger.retire(path, owner_said=...) — it archives the bytes in the art store, deletes the PNG and writes a live sha=None event art_guard accepts; art.py purge is reject-forever and refuses live pictures. Restore later with art.py install <mod> <rel> <sha>. (v2 Nine rites, 2026-10-23)
