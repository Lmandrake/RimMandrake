campaign before `rimworld/list_letters` showed both letters targeting the scratch map's own
`mapId`, not his home map. **Next time: check `jawa/world_tile_get`'s faction/feature fields
before generating on a tile, or accept the raid as free flavor if the tile is already scratch.**
