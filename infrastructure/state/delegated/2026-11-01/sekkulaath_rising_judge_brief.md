## Hard reads: where the maker went beyond his words

1. **The threat factor is global and permanent at ×1.15.** "Elsewhere only threat rises" is read as
   points only, with no raid-frequency change.
2. **Sporefall's young is taken by the deep** with no goodwill break and no gift, and freeing it early
   buys no mercy.
3. **Pools savage for good.** "Damage confined to its region" is stretched to the living biome.
4. **Steamreach counts as local.** It sits off the label, and no doc places it in the Fever Wood.
5. **Herd-groves, wood-roads and mirrors are left out** as having no world object.
6. **Sites close by a new impassable biome, keeping the landmark.** Is `RM_SunkenGround` needed, or
   does a site-level object do it better?
7. **The letter and Narrator line** show the shadow without naming the Assailants. Is that the shadow
   he asked for, or does it say too much about what frightened the deep?
8. **The whole body in one frame.** "Only tentacles are ever drawn" was the ordinary-play rule. Does
   the frame break it, or the size mystery?
