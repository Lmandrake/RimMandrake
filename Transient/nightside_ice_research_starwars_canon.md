# Nightside Ice Biome — Star Wars Canon Research

Research for a tidally-locked nightside ice plateau biome (colder than Hoth, no sun ever,
aurora-lit, native life under the ice or disguised as terrain). Sourced via Wookieepedia API.

## 1. Hoth in depth

**Planet facts** (`Hoth`): terrestrial, frigid, one sun but 23-hour days / 549 local days a year; terrain
is frozen ice plains, glaciers, oceans (33% water), mountains; population "fewer than 10" sentients
before the Rebellion arrived. Named surface features: Clabburn Range, Icefall Plains, Moorsh Moraine,
Wampa Mountain, Shyloah's Crest. Full fauna list per the planet infobox: Dragon Slug, Fork-nosed ice
worm, Hoth hog, Hoth spider, Ice scrabbler, Rayboo, Sapphire ice worm, Tauntaun, Wampa; flora: algae,
lichen, ice fungus.

- **Wampa**: semi-sentient carnivorous mammal, 2.5–3m, white fur, black eyes, small cranial horns, lives
  in caves. Ambush predator — uses white fur as camouflage, closes in, stuns/kills with claws and fangs,
  then **drags the catch back to its cave and hangs it upside-down from the ceiling to eat later** (this
  is exactly what happened to Luke). Cousin species: the Mogu (visually similar, warmer planet Koboh).
  Wampas themselves have a predator: the **Dragon Slug**. Displaced wampa clans (Echo Base was built over
  their caves) return to raid the base at night, forcing corridor lockdowns — "something living under/in
  the built environment, displaced, that comes back" is a strong beat.
- **Tauntaun**: semi-sentient reptomammal, 15 subspecies, scaly skin under thick fur, fatty blubber,
  secretes pungent oils for mating/scent communication, gives live birth ("taunlets"), herds of ~25 led by
  a female matriarch, migratory. **Cannot survive Hoth's night — temperatures below −60°C** (Han's line to
  Luke: "it'll be dead before dawn" is the emotional beat; the tauntaun literally cannot survive what our
  biome is by default). Omnivore: eats lichen/algae/ice-plants AND ice scrabblers AND Hoth hogs. Lives in
  glacial caves/grottos warmed by the planet's core, where sunlight through the ice lets small plants grow
  — **geothermal micro-pockets are the only green in an otherwise dead landscape**. Can burrow into snow
  for warmth.
- **Ice scrabbler**: rodent-like, found on Hoth and on Gigor; tauntaun prey.
- **Hoth hog**: small omnivorous relative of the ice scrabbler; **heat-sensitive tongues** — flicks tongue
  to "taste" the air for traces of heat from thermal vents/heated caves, then digs down with powerful
  clawed feet to reach buried geothermal vegetation grown from ancient volcanic warmth. Scavenges in
  family units. (A land-animal built entirely around detecting warmth in a cold-dead world — directly
  useful for "thermal-sensing tunneler" design.)
- **Dragon Slug**: fire-breathing creature living in ice-caves, preys on wampas. Turquoise/green or
  blue/black colouring. (A literal fire-in-ice predator — heat as the rare, valuable, dangerous thing.)
- **Fork-nosed ice worm**: rare worm species, known only from a **fossil** recovered by Doctor Aphra —
  i.e. Hoth's own worms are already extinct/relict by the modern era. Forked nose.
- **Sapphire ice worm**: burrowing worm; digs winding burrows that **the wind whistles through at night,
  producing haunting melodies** — sound-as-atmosphere from a creature never seen.
- **Glacier fly**: tiny flying insect resembling ice itself (near-invisible camouflage), lived in
  symbiotic relationship with the fork-nosed ice worm; both species are implied to now be extinct
  ("used to live symbiotically... before they all went extinct").
- **Hoth spider**: named in Hoth's own fauna list (via "In the Lair of the Dragon Slug"); a cave-dwelling
  arachnid alongside the Dragon Slug.
- **Rayboo**: prey animal hunted by wampas (per Battlefront sound-design notes) — otherwise sparsely
  documented, but establishes "wampas hunt more than tauntauns."
- **Echo Base**: Rebel headquarters carved into Hoth's ice by Alliance engineers — literally hollowed
  tunnels in a glacier, built directly over/into displaced wampa dens. The Empire located it via a probe
  droid that crash-landed and was initially mistaken for a meteorite — "the sky drops something that
  looks natural and isn't" is a strong Echo Base beat, plus the general shape of "buried, camouflaged
  outpost in a hostile ice waste, discovered by something falling from orbit."
- Legends-era material (not canon but flavour-adjacent): Hoth asteroid field with its own ecology
  (see space-slug entry below), 15 Hoth star systems (Shron, Biosh, Nushk, Jhas, Ordaj) sharing the same
  frozen-world profile.

## 2. Other canon/legends ice worlds

- **Ilum** (canon): Unknown Regions, frigid, terrain of **crystallized glaciers**, ice plains, mountains;
  the Jedi Temple's Crystal Cave is where younglings "Gather" their kyber crystal — **the crystals
  themselves are alive/Force-sensitive and call to a specific individual**, a "the ice itself contains
  something precious and semi-sentient" beat. Fauna: Asharl panther, Blismal, Gorgodon, Razhak, Snowfeather
  bird. Ilum was later hollowed out and weaponized as **Starkiller Base** — a whole ice world converted
  into a star-devouring superweapon, i.e. "the ice conceals something ancient and world-ending built
  into the planet's own crust."
- **Csilla** (canon): ice-locked homeworld of the Chiss, Unknown Regions. Most population (60–70 million)
  lives in **illuminated, heated underground caves that contain forests** — full ecosystems sealed away
  from a surface too cold to use. The above-ground capital (Csaplar) is kept **deliberately staged to look
  busy and populated to outsiders while actually being mostly empty** — a "hollow, performing civilization"
  beat: a facade of life over a dead/near-dead surface.
- **Orto Plutonia** (canon): frigid ice-plains/tors/canyons world, homeworld of the **Talz**, discovered as
  inhabited only during the Clone Wars despite centuries of contact with its moon Pantora — "an entire
  native civilization hidden in plain sight because nobody thought anything could live there." Fauna:
  narglatch (used as mounts). A sovereignty/native-rights story: the Pantorans nearly went to war
  believing the Talz were a hostile invading force.
- **Talz** (canon species): four-eyed biped — **small eyes for day vision, large eyes for night vision**,
  shaggy white fur, sharp talons, small feeding/communication proboscis, primitive tribal culture,
  own language (Talzzi) that most others can't parse. Directly useful precedent for "a sentient/near-
  sentient species built to see in permanent dark or near-dark."
- **Mygeeto** (canon): frozen-class world, crystalline terrain, frigid climate; native fauna **Whiteworm**;
  home to the Lurmen. A crystalline-terrain ice world distinct from Ilum's kyber angle — worth checking
  Whiteworm further if a crystal-terrain tunneler concept is wanted.
- **Vandor** (canon, *Solo*): frigid mountain/plains world, fast-flowing (unfrozen) rivers. Fauna:
  **Kod'yok** — peaceful, non-sentient, shaggy horned quadruped with thick brown wool insulation and two
  pairs of nostril tusks, farmed by locals for meat/milk/hide/fur, herds thinned by overhunting; and
  **Luftgriff** (name only surfaced, not fetched in depth — a flavour name worth a second pass if wanted).
- **Polus** (Legends, KOTOR): frigid arctic world, **massive frozen ocean**, blizzards *and* recurring
  **ice storms that scour the surface**, average day temperature **−50°C** (i.e. canon already treats
  Hoth-or-colder as survivable-by-near-Humans in Legends) — homeworld of the Pyn'gani, who live in
  villages built around **shield generators** to survive the storms, alongside native **Dral'k ice
  worms**. Strong precedent for "settlement survives only inside a powered shield/heat bubble."
- **Space slugs / Exogorths** (canon, Hoth asteroid field — the "this is no cave" scene): a
  **silicon-based, sentient species living inside asteroids in the vacuum of space**, colder and more
  hostile than any planetary surface. They eat their first meal (often a black dwarf star fragment) to
  create internal digestive gases that pressurize into a breathable internal atmosphere housing a whole
  gut ecosystem; they burrow into an asteroid until fully hidden, or slowly accrete an asteroid-like shell
  around themselves, and their gaping mouths are disguised as craters/tunnels to trap prey that wanders
  in. Named individual: Sy-O (the one from Empire Strikes Back). This is the strongest canon precedent for
  "life form that IS the terrain, and traps things that walk into it."
- **Mynocks** (canon): bat-like, silicon-based parasites that survive unprotected **in hard vacuum**, live
  in asteroid fields and inside exogorths' stomachs, feed on ship energy systems; "a group is a battery."
  Cold/vacuum-surviving flying parasite precedent.
- **Unidentified ice planet** (canon, *Darth Maul – Black, White & Red*): frigid, snow/ice mountains and
  plains, breathable atmosphere — used purely as an isolated dumping ground/prison-ship graveyard;
  Maul calls it "this cold and haunted tomb." Reinforces "ice world = the place you abandon the dead and
  the disgraced."
- Ziost, Rhen Var, Anteevy, Ando Prime and Toola did not surface strong canon hits in this pass (Ziost's
  canon material centers on ash/dust devastation rather than ice — do not cite it as an ice world without
  re-checking; the other four returned thin or Legends-only results and were not pursued further given
  time budget — flag as a gap if the owner wants them chased specifically).

## 3. Specific hunts: tunnelers, heat-sensors, frozen-in-ice, scavengers, colder-than-Hoth, cold-dwellers

- **Ice wyrm** (canon, *The Bad Batch*, planet Barton IV): huge worm-like creature, **34.8 meters**,
  hardened bumpy shell, gaping many-toothed mouth. **Travels entirely beneath the snow, drilling large
  grooved tunnels, breathing through a series of blowholes it surfaces to use.** Much less active during
  the planet's coldest seasons (i.e. goes dormant/torpid in true deep cold rather than being active in
  it), and can be **deterred by sensor beacons** in slightly warmer conditions — implying a
  sound/vibration or heat-signature sensitivity. This is the single best direct precedent for a
  blind ice-tunneling megafauna and is almost exactly the shape of our under-the-ice tunnelers.
- **Sapphire ice worm** (canon, Hoth): burrowing worm whose winding burrows let the wind **whistle into
  haunting melodies at night** — a creature that is felt/heard, never seen, its presence marked only by
  sound moving through the ice itself. Strong "ambient horror without a visible monster" hook.
- **Fork-nosed ice worm** (canon, Hoth): known only as a **fossil** — canon already treats Hoth's own
  native ice-worms as an extinct, vanished megafauna by the modern era, once symbiotic with the (also
  extinct) glacier fly. A world colder/deader than Hoth could plausibly still have living versions of
  something Hoth only has fossils of.
- **Hoth hog** (canon): the clearest canon **heat-sensing** creature — heat-sensitive tongue "tastes" the
  air for warmth from thermal vents/buried geothermal pockets, then digs down with clawed feet. Family
  scavenging groups.
- **Wampa** (canon): thermal/scent tracking implied (hunts by smell — Han's tauntaun "smells something"
  right before an attack) plus white-fur camouflage; drags living prey back to a larder-cave to consume
  later — the closest canon gets to "a lair full of the frozen/preserved dead," though it's fresh-kill
  storage rather than true freezing.
- **Carbon-freezing / carbonite** (canon): not a native-ice-world creature, but the galaxy's standard
  method of **suspending a living being in a solid frozen block indefinitely, alive but inert**, is
  extremely well documented (Han Solo frozen in carbonite is the iconic case, "Hansicle" is even an
  in-universe/fan nickname with its own page). If the biome wants a "thing frozen in the ice, still alive,
  eventually released" beat, this is the load-bearing canon mechanism to reference/parallel rather than
  invent from scratch — a body sealed in clear hard ice that is not dead, just stopped.
- **Space slug / Exogorth** (see section 2): the strongest "creature that traps you by looking like inert
  terrain" precedent in canon — mouth disguised as a crater/tunnel, whole body accreted to look like a
  natural asteroid.
- **Scavengers of the frozen dead**: no canon species was found whose specific niche is "eats things found
  frozen in ice/snow" (Hoth hog and ice scrabbler scavenge geothermal plant matter, not carcasses); the
  closest real-world-adjacent canon beat is **Echo Base's aftermath** — after the Battle of Hoth,
  humanoid scavengers moved in specifically to scrap the wrecked AT-ATs left buried in the snow, i.e.
  Hoth's own "post-disaster carrion economy" is sapient salvage, not a native species. This looks like a
  genuine gap in canon worth inventing into rather than porting.
- **Colder-than-Hoth canon precedent**: **Polus** (Legends) is explicitly colder in its stated numbers
  (avg. day temp −50°C, ice storms that scour the surface, permanent shield-generator settlements) than
  Hoth's night-only −60°C extreme; the **Hoth night itself (−60°C and worse) is already stated in-canon
  to be lethal to Hoth's own cold-adapted megafauna** (tauntauns), so "colder than Hoth" as a design
  brief is really "as cold as Hoth's own night, but that is now the DAYTIME baseline" — nothing above the
  ice survives permanently, full stop, which matches no canon world we found (all canon ice worlds we
  checked have breathable, non-lethal average conditions with a *worse* night or storm season; ours would
  be canon's worst Hoth-night made into the permanent state). No true "deep space rogue planet" or "the
  Maw" ice-world creature turned up in this pass — flag as unresolved if the owner wants it chased
  further.
- **Deliberate extreme-cold dwellers**: the Chiss (Csilla) and Pyn'gani (Polus) are the two canon/legends
  precedents for **sapients who chose to keep living on/in a world this cold** rather than abandon it —
  both do it by going underground/under shield rather than adapting to the surface, which matches our
  "native life is under the ice" framing better than any surface-dwelling species does.

## 4. Ice-world weather and phenomena

- **Aurora** (canon): treated in canon as a genuine recurring astronomical/atmospheric phenomenon —
  "colourful rainbows of radiation visible in the night sky" — documented on Tiikae, Piu and Kavith III
  (none of them ice worlds in the hits returned, but the phenomenon itself is established canon physics,
  freely portable to any world with the right magnetosphere/atmosphere, which an aurora-lit nightside
  plateau already assumes).
- **Polus ice storms** (Legends): "blizzards as well as ice storms that would frequently scour the
  planet's surface" — a stronger, more abrasive weather event than a snowstorm, explicitly separate from
  ordinary blizzards in the planet's own description.
- **Hoth's own storms**: Echo Base sensor/weather beats (per the planet article's referenced sources)
  center on **blizzards** severe enough to force airspeeders out of use in favor of tauntauns, and cold
  severe enough to be a standing lethal hazard independent of any creature — the storm itself is a
  recurring antagonist in the Echo Base story, not just scenery.
- **No canon "ice quake" terminology surfaced** in this pass (searches returned Blizzard-class Star
  Destroyers and Valley of Blizzards, not a seismic ice phenomenon) — if the owner wants ice-quakes/
  glacier-calving as a mechanic, treat it as original invention bent from real glaciology rather than a
  canon-sourced beat.
- **Avalanche**: no dedicated canon avalanche-on-an-ice-world event surfaced either; "Avalanche Software"
  (a real-world dev studio credit) was the only strong hit. Same verdict — invent, don't port.

## Best material for the Nightside Ice

1. **Ice wyrm (Barton IV)** — the direct template for our under-ice tunneler: a huge, blind, shell-armored
   worm that drills tunnels beneath the snow and breathes through blowholes. Bend colder: ours never
   surfaces at all (no air pocket needed above −60°C ambient), and its "less active in the coldest
   season" becomes permanently torpid except when it senses a heat source — i.e. it hunts warmth, not
   prey it can see.
2. **Hoth hog's heat-sensing tongue** — the cleanest canon mechanism for "detects prey/food by residual
   warmth alone." Bend colder: make the ENTIRE native fauna set thermal-sensing rather than one small
   scavenger, because on a world with zero ambient light or surface warmth, heat sensing isn't a niche
   adaptation, it's the only adaptation that works.
3. **Sapphire ice worm's wind-song burrows** — a creature that is never seen, only heard, as wind moves
   through its tunnel network. Bend colder: let the whole plateau "sing" at night from a labyrinth of
   worm-bored tunnels underfoot, so the player hears the biome's life before ever proving it exists.
4. **Exogorth / space slug** — terrain that is actually a mouth. Bend colder: instead of an asteroid,
   make a "dead ice hummock" or "drift" on the plateau surface the thing that isn't dead — it looks like
   every other windswept snow ridge until something walks across it.
5. **Wampa's larder-cave behaviour** — drag living prey home, hang it, eat later. Bend colder: combine
   with carbon-freezing's "alive but stopped" logic — our tunneler doesn't need to keep prey fresh by
   metabolism, the ambient cold itself freezes what's dragged below solid, so a "larder" reads as a wall
   of the frozen dead, some perhaps not actually dead yet.
6. **Talz's day/night eye pairs** — a species built with two separate visual systems for two separate
   light regimes. Bend colder: on a nightside with NO day cycle at all, strip the day-eyes entirely — our
   native life (if any is even eyed) is permanently one-mode, aurora/thermal-vision only, because there
   has never been a sun to see by.
7. **Csilla's staged-empty capital** — a civilization that fakes surface liveliness while actually living
   underground almost entirely. Bend colder: repurpose as a ruin rather than a going concern — a
   long-abandoned surface installation on our plateau that still LOOKS staffed (lights, standing
   structures) purely because nothing has degraded it, with whatever built it long retreated under the
   ice or dead.
8. **Ilum's living kyber crystals becoming Starkiller Base** — precious, semi-sentient (or at least
   Force-active) crystal embedded in glacier ice, later revealed to have a terrible dormant capability.
   Bend colder: something valuable and strange is frozen into OUR ice too, inert and beautiful until
   disturbed — gives a design reason for salvagers/scavengers to brave the plateau despite everything in
   this document above arguing they shouldn't.
