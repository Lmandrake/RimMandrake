# The biome: the Lantern Deeps

Owner-ratified sheet, frozen. Do NOT contradict it and do NOT re-propose what is ruled or already proposed (listed below): build on it.

- NOT a surface biome: an injected underground POCKET MAP (persistent, never regenerated) reached from the coldest surface maps (<= -40 C) through one of two mouths: a natural lanternstone geode breaking the surface, or an old mineshaft in a ruined high-tech mining facility. Enclosed, roofed, a steady 17 C, still air, no sky, no weather, no ship can land or enter.
- Thematic handle: LIGHT THAT IS ALIVE. Image: "a blue lantern burning under the mountain, and a dead miner's suit walking toward it."
- Lanternstone: the blue native crystal that carries light; volatile (it explodes when broken badly); it grows (it can be sown). Kyber (campaign only, crafting material ONLY, no Force anything) is quiet where lanternstone glows. The mindstone: the rarest crystal, AWARE; a droid mind made from it is a new race, the Kindled, never yet made by anyone (the first making is an in-play event).
- Crystalline life from physics: piezoelectric nerves (stress makes voltage) wired to the aurora's ground currents overhead ("a reconnection storm is a feast day"); accretion from seeping solutions; cleaving is birth and, done fast, violence; PHOTONIC METABOLISM: they eat light, "your lamps are a feast"; resonance: the caverns hum; death is a shatter.
- The well-provisioned dead: nobody gets here without excellent technology, so every corpse and ruin is superbly equipped (exosuits, droids, power cells, cutting gear), and the Deeps' minds, which cannot move, WEAR what came down: dead droid chassis stand up and work of their own accord in the mindstone galleries ("the Working Dead"). Living droids act strangely or stop near mindstone and avoid the Deeps. Droids that leave come out mind-wiped and drift to neutral factions.
- Lore (campaign): the mechanoids are a crystal-minded form of life, not truly artificial; the Deeps' crystal minds are their "wild cousins" and could talk to the player; the Rust Cathedral is "the insane enslaved one". Late-game knowledge only. Selling kyber raises Imperial heat and Hutt interest.
- Hydrocarbon fauna (owner's own remakes): pale glowing blue skin, yellow hydrocarbon (oil, wax) internal fluids, unlike any dayside life.

Already RULED or BUILT (do not re-pitch; you may build ON these):
