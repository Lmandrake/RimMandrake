## Recommendations

### 1. The Warnings, Read Backward

**Pitch:** Every quarry contains one layer of a five-epoch translation; reading from recent graffiti down to Rakatan text unlocks practical knowledge—and reveals that the “gods” were warning miners, not blessing them.

- **Experience:** Pawns brush frost from inscriptions, hear a low recognition tone, and add imperfect translations to a biome-local journal. The final text reads: *What the sky returns is not a gift.*
- **Marks:** Discoverable technology, relationship to the gods.
- **Build:** **Small C#** — `CompWarningTableau`, study job, and persistent `GameComponent` tracking fragments; XML tableau/fragment defs. No precepts or rituals.
- **Only here:** The Blue Desert physically preserves every civilization’s failed interpretation in the same quarry walls.

### 2. Cold-Cutting Protocol

**Pitch:** Completing the middle Warning tier grants a slower mining mode that extracts blue ice without warming the face or rolling the thaw table.

- **Experience:** A new designation outlines the cut in pale cyan; tools make muted glassy taps instead of ordinary mining impacts. Safe faces retain visible frost, while careless cuts sweat, crack, and hiss.
- **Marks:** Discoverable technology, unique mechanic, unique resources.
- **Build:** **Small C#** — cold-cut designation and work giver; patch blue-ice yield resolution or route it through `CompColdCuttable`. Trade speed and yield for zero thaw chance.
- **Only here:** It is knowledge created by quarrying a material that becomes dangerous precisely when worked conventionally.

### 3. The Blue-Ice Heat Sink

**Pitch:** Blue ice becomes functional thermal mass: load blocks into a rack to hold a room cold through power failure, eventually producing distilled-water canisters.

- **Experience:** Blocks slowly cloud from deep blue to white while the rack groans and drips into sealed cans. During a blackout, the Cold Hold warms by degrees rather than instantly becoming a bomb.
- **Marks:** Unique resources, gravship touch.
- **Build:** **Small C#** — `CompRefuelable` cold-sink rack, room-temperature offset, spent-block conversion, and inspection-string heat capacity; XML items/building/recipe.
- **Only here:** This is the only biome where “water reserve,” explosive pantry safety, and treasure-grade mineral extraction are the same economy.

### 4. The Line Gives Back Slowly

**Pitch:** Ablation salvage incidents unfold over several hours instead of spawning loot instantly.

- **Experience:** First comes a distant crack, then a dark silhouette under thinning ice, then exposed wreckage, meteoritic metal, or a named freeze-dried corpse. Vekkit tracks and circling vrisk make the find visible before the letter arrives.
- **Marks:** Unique resources, relationship to the gods, surprising creatures.
- **Build:** **Small C#** — staged `IncidentWorker`, emergence markers, edge-biased placement, and corpse-history generation; XML incident variants and sounds.
- **Only here:** The Blue Desert does not receive meteorites—it returns things swallowed by the sky in earlier ages.

### 5. The Carbon Garden

**Pitch:** Add three unmistakably plant-like natives: creeping **milelace**, the many-leaved **Ninefold Censer**, and branching **longglass**.

- **Experience:** Milelace stitches fresh drifts into silver mats; censers open after Haze and exhale visible hydrocarbon breath; longglass turns toward wind and accumulates frost on new growth. All remain small, transparent, unsowable, and dangerously warm-reactive.
- **Marks:** Unique mechanic, unique resources, soundscape, interesting weather.
- **Build:** **XML only** for baseline plants using `RM_CompPlantCharge`; **small C#** if Haze flowering and post-drift succession are included. Milelace can yield insulating frost-fibre.
- **Only here:** These are an anhydrous plant succession powered by atmospheric hydrocarbons and moving ice-sand—not transplanted desert vegetation.

### 6. The Three-Sky Sequence

**Pitch:** Connect the ruled weathers into a learnable cycle: ice fog gathers, Haze loads the air, then the ice-sand drift releases it.

- **Experience:** Visibility contracts, virr pitch rises, static crawls over blue ice, and finally the storm rearranges paths and buries the lee side of structures. Forecasts describe signs rather than percentages.
- **Marks:** Interesting weather, soundscape, unique mechanic.
- **Build:** **Small C#** — map phase component adjusting WeatherDef transition weights and notifying fauna/flora; XML weather ambients and forecast text.
- **Only here:** Its weather is a metabolic cycle shared by the atmosphere, charged flora, buried predators, and frozen ground.

### 7. Silence Has Teeth

**Pitch:** Make the ossivel choir a trustworthy diegetic alarm rather than decorative ambience.

- **Experience:** Packs hold shifting organ chords until a large moving creature enters range; every throat stops within a beat, leaving only wind and echo. Tamed packs can guard a perimeter without producing conventional alerts.
- **Marks:** Soundscape, surprising creatures.
- **Build:** **Small C#** — one map-level sustainer controller, pack clustering, body-size/movement checks, and optional visual “choir fell silent” mote; XML `SoundDef`s.
- **Only here:** Elsewhere danger announces itself. Here the memorable sound is the sudden removal of the biome’s only living music.

### 8. After the Storm, the Drifts Hunt

**Pitch:** Murrek do not simply spawn after storms; existing animals crawl into new drifts, leaving readable occupied mounds.

- **Experience:** A suspect drift subtly pulses, sheds grains against the wind, and produces an occasional hooked scrape. Probing or digging flushes the murrek; abandoned burrows remain as collapsed bowls.
- **Marks:** Surprising creatures, interesting weather, unique mechanic.
- **Build:** **Large C#** — burrow/unburrow jobs, persistent pawn state, drift-cell selection, flushing interaction, and weather-end map logic.
- **Only here:** The terrain genuinely becomes the predator without making pawns disappear or teleport invisibly.

### 9. The Vhaulk Road

**Pitch:** A vhaulk’s passage should remain legible long after the giant leaves the map.

- **Experience:** Pillar feet compress blue ice into a broad rime road; nearby flora are cropped flat, cistern seams boom at long intervals, and frost hangs in its wake. Departure leaves a map-edge trail and letter rather than silent despawning.
- **Marks:** GIANT beast, soundscape, relationship to the gods.
- **Build:** **Small C#** — movement comp placing temporary terrain/filth and grazing scars, entrance/departure incident handling, plus the ruled heat/ion/lightning detonation gates.
- **Only here:** The “mountain” writes temporary geography into a landscape otherwise erased by wind.

### 10. Dovvik Sweep

**Pitch:** A trained dovvik can be ordered along a drawn path, draining charged plants into a visibly safe corridor for one day.

- **Experience:** Its proboscis frosts each plant from base to tip; drained specimens turn matte and stop singing. The safe colour slowly retreats before the charge returns.
- **Marks:** Surprising creatures, unique mechanic.
- **Build:** **Large C#** — line designation, animal job driver, trainable command, and timed safe state in `RM_CompPlantCharge`.
- **Only here:** It turns husbandry into bomb-disposal inside a living hydrocarbon minefield.

### 11. Gravship Rime-Lock

**Pitch:** Drift against a landed gravship adds departure mass until crews dig out marked hull cells.

- **Experience:** Windward hull sections vanish beneath sculpted banks; the ship interface reports exact buried cells and estimated clearing work. Engines can force departure, but hot wash may ignite nearby charged flora.
- **Marks:** Gravship touch, weather, unique mechanic.
- **Build:** **Large C#** — Odyssey gravship integration, hull-edge drift accounting, clearing jobs, launch warning, and optional forced-launch consequence.
- **Only here:** The ship is caught between cold burial outside and a temperature-sensitive cargo hold inside.

### 12. Star Wars Layer — Jawa Salvage Claim

**Pitch:** Fresh ablation finds sometimes draw a Jawa scavenger crew that offers a bargain, contests the wreck, or helps translate a later quarry inscription.

- **Experience:** Droids probe the ice while Jawas erect heaters at an alarming distance; their chatter mixes with cracking ice as both parties decide whether to trade or race.
- **Marks:** Unique resources, discoverable technology, soundscape.
- **Build:** **Large C#** — separate Star Wars patch with incident, Jawa `LordJob`, negotiation outcomes, and compatibility-gated defs.
- **Only here:** The biome’s returning wrecks make it the natural pilgrimage ground for an old scavenger clan, while canon content remains outside the native layer.

Every system should have real settings for frequency, danger, audio volume, accessibility tells, cold-cut balance, drift depth, and gravship consequences.

## Top 3

1. **The Warnings, Read Backward** — closes the two weakest marks with one coherent discovery spine.
2. **The Carbon Garden** — answers the owner’s flora request while making weather, sound, resources, and explosive ecology feel alive.
3. **The Blue-Ice Heat Sink** — turns the signature resource and Cold Hold from lore into an immediately useful gravship decision.
