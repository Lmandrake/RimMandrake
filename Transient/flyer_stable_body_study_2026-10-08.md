Donor exemplars score 0.55–0.87 core overlap with 10–21 drift; all three of our generated multi-frame sets score
0.21–0.49 with 17–45 drift. **That gap is the whole problem**, and it is a recipe problem, not a model problem: the
donor drew one body and swapped wings; we asked for N whole creatures.

**What makes a static convertible (from looking at these eight):**
1. **Wings folded or clear of the torso** in all three facings — the torso, head and legs must be visible as one
   unbroken shape (Vulture yes; Hawkbat no: membranes cover it).
2. **Facings are true facings** — east is a side view, north/south are back/front views at the same camera
   elevation as every vanilla flyer; never a dorsal top-down "east" (FireWasp).
3. **Wings are drawn at all** if the fiction flies (Thozzik fails).
4. **North and south read as a body**, not a sliver (FireHawk N fails).
5. **Wing roots are visible** where they leave the body, so a wing-edit has a seam to attach to.
6. **Flat even lighting, no cast shadow, no motion blur, no dramatic rim light** — any light baked from one side
   changes when wings move and is what shows up as core drift.
7. **Body centred with headroom** — the canvas must leave room for spread wings (≥ 30 % margin each side in
   south/north, above in east) so the frames do not have to shrink the body to fit.

## 3. Flyer-ready static gate, prompt templates, threshold, sheet column

> ⚠️ **RECONCILE with the Sketto pilot.** `Transient/sketto_design_2026-10-08.md` was still a skeleton when this
> section was written (all 7 sections `(pending)`). The numbers and recipe below are my **assumptions**, marked
> `[R]`, built from my own three donor and three in-house measurements in §2. Where the pilot's measured
> exemplars or its accepted recipe differ, **the pilot wins**: replace each `[R]` value with its figure.

### 3.1 The principle — lock the body by construction, not by asking

Every one of our drifting sets was made by prompting N whole creatures. The donor's sets hold because the
artist drew **one** body and changed only the wings. We can do the same thing mechanically:

1. **Body master per facing** (E generated; S and N derived from E with the existing `--derive-facings`):
   wings **folded or removed**, wing roots visible. This is also the grounded static, the art the owner rules on.
2. **Wing poses as separate transparent layers**, generated per facing against the body master as a reference
   (*"only the wings, nothing else, transparent everywhere else"*), 3 poses: up, level, down.
3. **Composite offline** (PIL) into whole-body frames `<prefix>N_<facing>.png`: beat = up, level, down, level
   (4 frames, the donor's count; vanilla birds use 8, which costs no more generations, only more compositing).
   The body pixels are **identical in every frame by construction**, so the diff gate cannot fail on the body.
   ⚠️ This is a **build step producing whole-body flip-book PNGs**, which is the 1.6 mechanism. It is **not** a
   wing render-tree, and nothing Spastic is involved (the `FIREHAWK_FLIGHT_BEHAVIOR_1` reversal still holds).
4. **Fallback when a layer will not composite** (a wing that must pass *behind* the body in N/S): use a masked
   edit of the body master, then **re-paste the body-mask pixels from the master** after the edit. Again the
   body is pinned by construction, and only the seam is the model's.

### 3.2 The gate — what the owner checks before approving ANY flyer's static (a sheet can show 1–4 automatically)

- **G1 Folded:** the torso, head and legs read as one unbroken shape in E, S and N. No wing membrane, feather
  fan or elytra crosses the torso. *(auto: body-mask area ≥ `[R]` 60 % of the alpha area in S/N)*
- **G2 True facings:** east is a side view; north and south are back and front views at vanilla camera
  elevation. Never a dorsal top-down view, and N/S never a sliver. *(auto: N/S alpha width ≥ `[R]` 35 % of E's)*
- **G3 Wings present and rooted:** if the creature flies, its wings (or its flight organ: bell, membrane,
  gas-sac) are drawn, and the roots are visible where they join the body.
- **G4 Headroom:** body bbox ≤ `[R]` 55 % of canvas width in S/N and its top ≥ `[R]` 25 % below the top in E.
- **G5 Flat light:** even, shadowless light, no motion blur, no rim light. *(owner's eye)*
- **Flip-book acceptance (after compositing or edit):** body-mask IoU across frames ≥ `[R]` **0.97**, and mean
  ΔRGB on the body mask ≤ `[R]` **6/255** (composited frames score 1.00 and 0). For reference, the donor sets
  score **0.55–0.87** core overlap and 10–21 drift on my cruder whole-silhouette metric, and ours score
  **0.21–0.49** and 17–45. Then the identity check by eye: same individual in every frame (FireHawk frames 4–5
  fail this). And a 2-tick loop GIF beside each facing on the sheet.

### 3.3 Body-master prompt templates (static, per body plan)

Shared head, always: *"RimWorld creature sprite, painterly, the {label}: {canon or description look}. {FACING
view}. Even flat lighting, no cast shadow, no motion blur. Centred, the body uses at most half the canvas width,
generous empty transparent margin all round. Wings {PLAN clause}. Wing roots clearly visible where they join the
body. Torso, head and legs fully visible and unbroken."* The style source is **our canon brief / painterly house
style**; a donor frame is attached **only as a structure reference**, with the clause *"use the attached only for
pose and wing placement; ignore its flat cartoon style completely"* (owner: the donor's flyers are *"good to
study"* but *"extremely cartoonish, so we can't use them"*).

| plan | `{PLAN clause}` for the body master | wing layer poses |
|---|---|---|
| **bird** (FireHawk, Vulture, Skellick, Whisperbird, Convor…) | folded tight along the flanks, primaries crossed over the tail, not raised | up (tips above head), level (full span), down (tips below belly) |
| **bat / dragon membrane** (Hawkbat, Shyrack, Uvak, Soorrak…) | folded like a closed umbrella against the sides, finger-bones visible, membrane not covering the chest | up, level spread, down; membrane only, no body |
| **insect, 2 or 4 wings** (FireWasp, Krizzak, Sketto, Dustflutter, Thozzik…) | folded flat over the back along the abdomen (wasp/fly) or tented roof-like (moth); legs and segments fully visible | E: forewing up / level / down plus a translucent blur variant; N/S: wings as narrow strokes either side |
| **ray / kite membrane** (Neebray, Skinflap, Vrisk) | body = the spine and head drawn as a clear central ridge; membrane relaxed flat | the membrane's edges curl up / flat / curl down; spine pinned |
| **floater, no wings** (Blisterfloat, Dwommo) | bell relaxed, tendrils hanging | bell contracted / relaxed, tendrils swept (a body-pulse set: pin the central organs, not the bell rim) |
| **swarm** (FeverSwarm) | the cloud at rest | three churn states of the same cloud; no body to pin, so G1 is waived |
| **large** (Vulture, Uvak, Convor) | as bird or bat, and the body master ≤ 45 % canvas width, because wing span multiplies drawSize | same 3 poses; `flyingAnimationDrawSizeIsMultiplier` covers the span |

### 3.4 Wing-layer prompt template (one job per pose per facing)

*"Attached: the accepted body master of the {label}, {facing} view. Draw ONLY its wings, in the {up|level|down}
position of a wingbeat, attached exactly at the visible wing roots of the attached body, at the same scale,
palette, edge treatment and light. Output the wings alone on a fully transparent canvas the same size as the
attached image: do not redraw, move or include any part of the body. {PLAN wing clause}."* The compositor
then pastes body over wings (S/E) or wings over body (N, where the wings are nearer the camera), using the
plan's layer order.

### 3.5 Review-sheet column

Every row whose def has `MaxFlightTime > 0` (or that the census adjudicated `should-fly`) gets a **`flyer:`
cell** beside the art: `convertible ✓` / `partial: <G-number why>` / `✗ <G-number why>`, with G1, G2 and G4
computed and printed automatically and G3/G5 left as checkboxes for him. A `keep` click on a `✗` row asks
*"keep the look, and file a body pass?"* rather than recording a plain keep, so a good-looking dead end can
never be settled silently. The flip-book (once it exists) shows as a looping GIF in the same row.

### Recommended tooling changes (recommendation only, nothing built)

- **`fill_queue.py`: a `flyer` row flag** (or `plan: bird|bat|insect|ray|floater|swarm`). It files the body
  master as now (E, then derived S/N), and **files the 9 wing-layer jobs only with `derive_from` = the accepted
  body master and a hard dependency on its verdict**, the way `--derive-facings` already waits on an E master.
  It refuses a flip-book row that has no `plan`, and it refuses per-frame whole-creature frame prompts outright
  (the `regen_gt_*_flying_N` shape).
- **A `flipbook_compose.py`** (new, offline): body master + wing layers + plan layer order → `<prefix>N_<facing>.png`
  for N = 1..4, then runs the §3.2 body-lock metric and writes a loop GIF for the sheet. Its exit code is the gate.
- **`art.py enact`:** on a `keep` of a row the sheet marks `flyer ✗`, file the body-pass job instead of closing the
  row, unless his note says otherwise; on a `keep` of a `flyer ✓` row, file the wing jobs automatically.
- **Review sheets** (`art_sheet.py` / `refresh_sheets.py`): the `flyer:` column of §3.5, the auto G1/G2/G4 numbers,
  and the loop GIF.
- **Census as a selftest-able tool:** promote `Transient/flyer_census_2026-10-08.py` into `src/RimMandrake/Utils/art/`
  so each sitting can re-derive its flyer rows (it reads descriptions as well as `MaxFlightTime`, with its sanity probe).
