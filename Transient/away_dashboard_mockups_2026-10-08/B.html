B · The Return Ledger

Not a live window: a page that is written for you when you come back. Type "I'm back" (or open the link on your phone) and it is regenerated from the ledger, the session files, memwatch and artpipe for the hours you were gone.

It reads like a morning paper. First a two-sentence lede, then what broke, then what is waiting for you with the oldest first, then what finished, each with a link to its report, sheet or save.

It is published as a private claude.ai Artifact at one fixed URL, so the same page works on the phone and on the desktop. Each item has a checkbox; ticking it records that you have seen it, and the next edition leaves seen items out.

There is nothing that has to keep running. If nobody asks for the page it is simply not written, so there is no stale view to go out of date.
