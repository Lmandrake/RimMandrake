## Defects
FIXED (all found by the fuzz / lint, each with its mutation):
- **Arson-justice raids unreachable above 1500.** `PyrelandsMechanicsMod.cs:70` offered the "Arson debt before a raid" slider up to 2000, but `MapComponent_BurnLine` caps the debt at `ArsonDebtCap` = 1500 (`PyrelandsTuning.cs`), so any threshold above 1500 (the top quarter of the slider, and any saved value there) could never raid. The slider now ends at `ArsonDebtCap` and `RM_BurnKernel.EffectiveRaidThreshold` treats a larger saved value as the cap (`IncidentWorker_FireRaid.cs:53`). Proven live by the liveness probe at every slider value in steps of 25.
- **Fire clock fired at once when the master switch came back on.** `MapComponent_BurnLine.cs:188`: with `pyrelandsEnabled` off the component returned before `fireFront.Tick()`, so the clock never reached its "disabled -> re-arm to -1" branch and kept a stale deadline; switching the biome back on fired a front (and sent its letter) immediately - exactly what the comment in `PyrelandsFireFront.Tick` says it prevents. The master-off path now still ticks the clock. Fuzz: 5 of the first 5 failing seeds were this.
- **Zero cross-biome coverage still dropped ash.** `FireEcologyHook.cs:393`: `attempts < 1 -> 1` also applied to a rate of 0 (`crossBiomeCoverage` slider's left end), so an opted-in biome with coverage 0 still got one ash deposit per 250 ticks. A rate of 0 now deposits nothing; any positive rate still deposits at least one.
- **Fire fronts lit far fewer cells than their width.** `PyrelandsFireFront.cs:182`: the line was `(unit Euclidean step x i)` truncated to cells, which folds a diagonal line onto itself (centre cells repeat). Measured against the old construction: it lit only 85.9% of requested cells on average over random bearings and widths 1..31 (a 9-wide front at 45 degrees lit 5). `RM_BurnKernel.FrontLine` steps one cell per step along the dominant axis (exact `width` distinct, 8-adjacent cells at every angle; odd widths and axis-aligned fronts are unchanged). BEHAVIOUR CHANGE to flag: a 9-wide front now lights 9 cells at every bearing (old: 9 at 0 and 90 degrees, 7 at 15 / 30 / 60, 5 at 45 and 135), so diagonal fronts span up to 41% further and light up to 1.8x as many cells; no arson debt is affected (the biome and the rite light with no player instigator).
REPORTED, not changed:
- `WorldObject_RM_FurnaceHerd` / `PyrelandsTuning.cs:223,237,337`: charge only moves outside the dead band 5..35 C. Empty-to-full takes 8.9 days at 40 C and 1.8 days at 60 C (measured by the fuzz), but a leg whose tile sits in the dead band neither charges nor discharges, so the Pyrelands / terminator legs end only at `WorldHerdMaxLegDwellTicks` = 3,600,000 ticks (60 days). The fuzz herd cases in 20-degree climates confirm the herd still advances (safety valve), just slowly.
- `PyrelandsTuning.cs` `NearTerminatorBiomeDefNames`: `RUT_PoisonForest` and `RM_PoisonForest` are not BiomeDefs (lint WARN).
- `CompFurnaceBedIgnition` / `RM_FurnaceKernel.BedCells`: the cell range is `round(1 x ratio) .. round(2 x ratio)` (banker's rounding, ratio floored at 0.25): a beast of base body size <= 0.8 (ratio at the floor) smoulders NO bed cells, and below ratio 0.5 the low end is 0. Probably intended (smaller = cooler) but not stated anywhere; 70-127 of the fuzz's random beasts hit it.
- `FireEcologyHook.cs` `rolledFires` is static: it survives across maps and, on a long session, is cleared wholesale at 100,000 ids, after which already-burning fires roll their scorch-fruit chance again (bounded, harmless in practice).
- The raid's two refusal exits cannot be reached through the gate (`RaidCanFire` already requires a Tribes faction that is hostile or has goodwill); `RaidPlan.Refuse` is defensive only (0 hits in 10.7B burn steps, as expected).
