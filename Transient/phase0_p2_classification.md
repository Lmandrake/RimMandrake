## Names that P3 needs from this pass (shared defs, do not duplicate)

`RM_Bulloo` (the small aerofleet; the Colossal Aerofleet's fission turns into it), `RM_BlueGel` (the gel the
aerofleets produce), `RM_Dustveil`. Support bodies are mod-local copies named `RM_<Part><Mod>` when a second mod needs the
same donor body (e.g. `RM_SlugBodyTheForge`), so each biome mod loads alone.

## Method notes

- Ports are generated by `src/RimMandrake/Utils/aa_port.py` (new): donor def + folded rename/tolerance patches,
  `wildBiomes` and the donor stat extension dropped, VEF.* comps kept behind the VEF `MayRequire`, donor SoundDefs
  swapped for a vanilla family, leather/meat decoration items swapped for vanilla (PROVISIONAL), every texPath moved to
  OUR final path (art owed, nothing installed), donor-only bodies/body parts/damage/hediff/ability/item defs ported as `RM_`.
- Rows: 22 roster elements repointed to ours with `MayRequire` on the defining mod when it is a different mod.
