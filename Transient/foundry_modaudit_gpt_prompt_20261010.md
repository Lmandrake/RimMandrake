# Task: final analysis and enrichment of a 589-mod audit for a RimWorld 1.6 scenario mod stack

## Context
RimWorld 1.6 modpack for a single hand-made, frozen scenario ("Jawa scavenger clan on a desert world", Star Wars-style naming, six fixed founders, a gravship as the only way between layers). Players receive a savegame; there is no worldgen feature. Our own mods (102 of 589, packageIds mandrake.rm.* / mandrake.rsw.* / mandrake.rut.*) sit on top of ~480 third-party donor mods, frameworks and libraries plus 6 Core/DLC. The full list currently loads 550 mods in ~21 min, uses ~30 GB private memory, and runs ~8-10 TPS on one map before a (now fixed) 70 ms/tick HugeThings cost; owner's goal: decide, once and for all, keep / keep-then-phase-out / replace-with-our-own / eliminate for every mod, with evidence, and keep the record for the long term.

The audit was done by 16 first-pass assessors (cheaper model) and re-checked by 4 independent Opus checkers, then merged. Known first-pass weaknesses are documented in the package (wrong dependants field, missed rulings, rejected evidence feeding scores, etc.), and the facts pipeline was regenerated afterwards (FACTS_REGEN_DELTA.md lists rows whose verdicts may change).

## Attached
GPT_PACKAGE.md (the whole picture compactly: scenario brief, rubric summary, method and weaknesses, the master table of all 589 mods, systematic errors, consolidated owner cards, actions-now list, ruling conflicts, unmeasured-memory top 30, donor dependency maps), FACTS_REGEN_DELTA.md, OWNER_CARDS.md.
