# Rainbow pigment concept research

Context: "rainbow pigment" harvested from glowing bacterial film in a boiling
alien sea (Ash'karr). Owner's favourite directions so far: glow paint (luminous
dye toggle -> free weak light source), +1 art quality bump, permanent sparkle
effect, unlock line of extreme-high-art producibles for master artists only.
Shifting-colour lighting is DISLIKED (clashes with existing oscillating
shipboard lighting).

Goal: 25+ concrete mechanics to steal from (1) RimWorld mods and (2) other
games, concept only — no code copying.

## 1. RimWorld mods (finery, dyes/paints, glow, prestige, masterwork)

- **Glowmoss** — https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2441383010 — bioluminescent mycorrhiza grown in pots as a heatless light source that also adds beauty.
- **Glowing resources** — https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3546074606 — per-ore "glow switch" toggle so mined rock/resource stacks can be made to shine.
- **[SYR] Glowing Healroot** — https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1517921835 — reskins a plant to give off a glow texture, no gameplay system, purely cosmetic precedent for "glowing organic resource."
- **AutoDye** — https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2563707717 — pawns auto-dye outfits to a configured colour per outfit policy; relevant as a UX pattern for a "toggle a colour/effect on this apparel" interaction (glow toggle could ride the same UI).
- **Beautiful Coloured Lights** — https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1118285675 — coloured lamps grant extra beauty over white light, incentivising colour choice mechanically, not just cosmetically.
- **Jewelry** — https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3203280763 — adds necklace/ring/bracelet/earring apparel slots made from gold + stuffable gemstones, plus a dedicated "laser sculpting table" production bench faster than the vanilla sculpting table — precedent for a pigment-only production chain/bench.
- **Tribal Jewelry** — https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2411055916 — jewelry crafted from tusks/horns (organic trophy materials) rather than metal/gems — precedent for jewelry/finery sourced from a harvested creature/biological material, same shape as a bacterial pigment.
- **Quality Expanded** — https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2634653569 — makes item quality tiers matter more broadly (stat scaling beyond just art/market value) — relevant if pigment-treated items should scale a stat, not just value.
- **Crafting Quality Rebalanced** — https://www.nexusmods.com/rimworld/mods/257 — reworks the quality-roll curve per skill/work type including Art, with configurable thresholds for Excellent/Masterwork — precedent for a craft material that shifts the quality-roll curve upward (which is basically what "+1 art quality tier" is).
- **RimWorld base game — Royalty title apparel/quality requirements** — https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Titles — a noble's title demands apparel of a MINIMUM quality tier and full-body coverage, with a stacking mood penalty ("Want <TITLE>-quality apparel") if unmet — vanilla precedent for "a material/treatment that exists specifically to clear a quality gate," which a rainbow-pigment art-quality bump slots directly into.
- **Alpha Biomes — bioluminescent trees** — https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=1841354677 — a biome-native glowing flora that functions as ambient/area lighting tied to a specific biome's ecology, same shape as sourcing a glow effect from a specific alien sea's bacterial mat rather than a universal recipe.




## 2. Other games (dyes/pigments/lacquers/luminous paint as progression/prestige)

- **Terraria — Glowing Mushroom Dye** — Hardmode equipment dye that recolours ALL your gear to a glowing blue-black palette that literally emits light in the dark, and shifts to magenta during a Blood Moon (a conditional/event-reactive glow). https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Glowing_Mushroom_Dye
- **Terraria — Glowing Mushroom paint** — painting a glowing-mushroom plant/block changes the COLOUR of the light it emits (not just the object's colour) — decorative paint that is simultaneously a light-colour tool. https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Paints
- **Minecraft — Glow Ink Sac on signs** — applied to a sign, makes text glow/outline for legibility in darkness without being an actual light source — a "readability without illumination" tier of luminous effect, weaker than a real light. https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glow_Ink_Sac
- **Minecraft — Glow Item Frame** — combining a rare mob drop (glow squid ink) with an ordinary frame makes displayed items visible in the dark without casting real light — a cheap "always-visible/always-legible" display effect distinct from lighting.
- **Subnautica — Creepvine bioluminescence retained when domesticated** — one plant keeps its glow (and functions as a real light source) specifically when replanted in a player-built grow bed, i.e. domestication preserves a wild organism's natural bioluminescence as a utility. https://subnautica.fandom.com/wiki/Bioluminescence
- **Subnautica — Rockgrub** — the one creature whose bioluminescence actually lights the surrounding terrain (most "glowing" creatures/plants are cosmetic-only) — precedent for reserving true area-light behaviour to a rare biological source rather than making it universal.
- **Subnautica 2 — Bioluminescence biomod** — glow becomes a wearable player upgrade (passive "biomod") that grants darkvision-like utility rather than a placed light — an alternate framing: pigment as a body/apparel buff, not just paint.
- **Oxygen Not Included — Artifacts** — decorative pedestal-mounted objects where SOME (not all) artifacts additionally cast light in a small radius alongside their decor bonus — precedent for "decor item, and a subset of them are also light sources" as two overlapping but separable properties on one item class.
- **Oxygen Not Included — Neutronium-encrusted artifacts need cleaning** — artifacts must be processed at an Artifact Analysis Station before their real (higher) decor/light values apply — precedent for a two-stage material: raw harvested substance vs. a processed/refined version that unlocks the good stats.
- **Kenshi — colour-scheme dyeing via faction NPCs** — armor/clothing takes on the colour of whichever character equips it (tied to their assigned faction colour scheme), and an item must be "cleared" (sold) before it can take a new colour — precedent for dye as a STATEFUL, overwritable property of the item rather than a one-shot permanent stamp. https://kenshi.fandom.com/wiki/Colour_Scheme
- **Crusader Kings 3 — artifact quality tiers (Ordinary/Masterpiece/Eminence/Glory)** — an artifact's grade is set by its creator's skill tier (Ordinary→Legendary) and can be further improved after creation by "sponsoring" it — precedent for a crafted object's prestige tier being raised POST-creation by a secondary investment, not only rolled once at craft time. https://en.namu.wiki/w/크루세이더%20킹즈%203/유물
- **Monster Hunter — Decorations (gem slots)** — small slotted gems grant discrete skill bonuses to gear independent of the base item's own stats, with slot LEVEL gating which decoration tier fits — precedent for a separable "socket a pigment/gem into a finished item after the fact" upgrade layer, distinct from a crafting-time material choice. https://monsterhunterrise.wiki.fextralife.com/Decorations
- **Monster Hunter Rise: Sunbreak — Rampage Decorations** — a special decoration category that exists ONLY on Master Rank gear and grants an entirely different (more powerful) tier of skill than normal decorations — precedent for "a high-tier version of the same slot mechanic gated behind master-rank/master-crafter status," parallel to a master-artist-only producible line.
- **Stardew Valley — Dye Pots (HSV sliders)** — full player-authored colour control (hue/saturation/value) applied simultaneously to a whole worn outfit from one set of dye-ingredient items, with "weak/medium/strong" dye-strength items requiring repeat applications to reach full saturation — precedent for treating "dye strength" as its own tunable resource property (a weak vs. strong rainbow-pigment extraction). https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Dyeing
- **Don't Starve Together — Glow Berries as a consumable light-on-a-creature/inventory effect** — eating a Glow Berry makes the player's own body glow for a fixed duration; feeding one to a carried creature or worn mask makes THAT item/pet glow while stowed in inventory (light with no external light-source object) — precedent for glow as a temporary buff on a pawn/held item, distinct from a placed light source. https://dontstarve.wiki.gg/wiki/Glow_Berry
- **World of Warcraft — Weapon Illusions** — a fully COSMETIC-ONLY enchant-appearance system layered on top of the mechanical enchant, collected separately and applied at will, so the visual glow/particle effect and the stat effect are decoupled — precedent directly on point for "sparkle effect is a separate, freely toggled property from any stat/quality change." https://www.wowhead.com/guide/transmogrification/weapons/illusions
- **Animal Crossing: New Horizons — Sable's fabric patterns** — unlocked incrementally through a relationship/friendship gate (visiting Sable repeatedly) rather than bought outright, and usable across furniture, clothing AND walls/floors once a paid unlock (Pro Decorating License) is purchased — precedent for gating a decorative-material unlock behind a relationship/trust progression rather than a pure tech-tree research, and for one material working across multiple item categories. https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Item:Custom_Designs_Patterns+_(New_Horizons)



## Top 8 to steal

1. **WoW Weapon Illusions (cosmetic-only glow, decoupled from stats)** — proves "permanent sparkle effect" should be its own toggle-able layer, independent of whatever else the pigment does. Solves the "shifting-colour lighting clashes with oscillating shipboard lights" objection: a STATIC sparkle/particle skin, no colour animation, applied like an illusion rather than baked into the dye colour.
2. **Oxygen Not Included artifacts — decor bonus and light-source bonus are two separable properties, only some artifacts have both** — gives the owner a clean design lever: rainbow-pigmented items don't have to ALWAYS glow; "glow paint" can be an explicit toggle applied at dye time (matches his own favourite), keeping plain-dye and glow-dye as two SKUs from one pigment.
3. **CK3 artifact quality tiers improvable after creation via sponsorship** — gives a second progression axis beyond "the craft roll." A finished item could be brought UP a quality/prestige tier later by a rainbow-pigment "re-lacquering" pass, not only at the original crafting quality roll — nice fit for "bump an item's art quality up one level" as a post-hoc action rather than only a recipe-time input.
4. **Monster Hunter Rampage Decorations — a whole extra decoration tier that exists ONLY on master-rank gear** — the cleanest precedent for "a line of extreme high-art producibles only master artists can make": gate the pigment's best recipes behind crafter skill level, not just item quality, so it reads as a master's tool, not a universal upgrade.
5. **Subnautica Creepvine — bioluminescence is retained (and becomes a real light source) only when domesticated/replanted by the player** — strong flavour match for a bacterial film harvested and then CULTURED/stabilized outside its boiling sea rather than a static "reagent." Suggests a farming/culturing production chain, not a one-shot loot harvest, and justifies rarity narratively.
6. **RimWorld vanilla Royalty apparel-quality gate** — the game already has a hard mechanical slot for "apparel must clear a quality threshold or the pawn is unhappy." A rainbow-pigment +1 quality bump has an IMMEDIATE, already-built payoff: it's the tool that gets a marginal item over a title's quality bar, giving the material obvious utility without inventing a new system.
7. **Don't Starve Glow Berries fed to a carried pet/mask — glow as an inventory/worn buff, not a placed object** — offers a light third option beside "paint the wall" and "glow furniture": a glow-treated WORN item (a jawa's robe, a pack animal's tack) that lights the wearer's immediate vicinity while walking, useful for scavenger/caravan flavour distinct from static base lighting.
8. **Terraria Glowing Mushroom Dye's Blood-Moon colour shift** — the one piece of "reactive" colour-changing worth keeping despite the shifting-light veto: a discrete, RARE, EVENT-triggered recolour (e.g. pigment glows differently only during a sandstorm or at a specific hour) reads as a special occurrence rather than the disliked continuous oscillation — a way to keep some "living colour" flavour without touching the vetoed mechanic.
