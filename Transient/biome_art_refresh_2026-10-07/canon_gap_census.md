# Canon art gap census — every biome sheet (2026-10-07)

Generated by `canon_gap_census.py` (provenance, mechanical) + `canon_gap_census_md.py` (merges a sonnet visual Must-show check of every row whose in-game art is OURS). Not queued — census only.

**Method.** Newest sheet snapshot per biome (`infrastructure/state/art/sheets/`), each row resolved to a canon-library entry with `art/subject.py resolve_canon`. The IN-GAME column's sha256 is looked up in the art ledger's `artpipe` events (`infrastructure/state/art/events/*.jsonl`) to find the job that made it, and that job's done JSON says whether it carried a canon brief (`canon`/`target_canon`/`canon_reference`). The canon library dates from 2026-09-15; override art committed before then cannot have been canon-briefed.

Classes: `NEVER_REGENERATED` = never regenerated (in-game bytes are donor art; no artpipe render installed); `OVERRIDE_UNTRACED` = our *ArtOverride art, bytes trace to no ledger render (halo pass 09-20 rewrote them); `REGEN_NOT_CANON_BRIEFED` = regenerated, but the installed render's job carried no canon brief; `CANON_REGEN` = canon-briefed regen installed
