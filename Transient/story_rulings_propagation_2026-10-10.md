## Rulings applied

- Card 5: the flesh complex IS the war lab, the single impact site (the lab plus the excavated crater, beneath the Chill).
- Card 6: the Scald's dark tower IS the Spire: in the mountains, visible from the lake.
- Card 7: Cathedral trust grants ENTRY to the Spire, never the codes. Card 10: cold access.
- Card 8: an early lake blast reveals the lab, Empire heat rises, an Imperial garrison must be beaten.
- Card 9 and card 4: how the player learns the lake opened, and the cut-scene register.
- Cards 1, 2, 3 and 11: the lab's contents and register, the specimen choice, the Cradle strike target.
- Cards 12 and 13: the Reclamation is independent of the crater event and fires on a woken count.
- Card 19 plus digest: the creature is the sekkulaath ("Tenant" stays a class-name word); it rises whole in the crater event.
- Cards 16 and 17: tone recorded in the gather (G77).
