1. [HIGH] Pre-flight is scheduled before site creation but requires a valid site, weather, fog, temperature, latitude, and three colonists. It must fail on every clean run. Split it into deployment/session pre-flight before phase 3, site pre-flight after each reload, and post-test restoration checks.

2. [HIGH] The mod set is not exact. Checking required packages present and selected exclusions absent permits arbitrary extra mods, duplicate package IDs, wrong versions, and wrong load order. These can patch the biome while still passing. Require an ordered `(packageId, version/content hash)` manifest, reject every unexpected active mod, and record DLC/build versions.

3. [HIGH] Deployment freshness is unverifiable. `Player.log` creation time does not prove process start, `.srchash` does not prove which DLL is loaded, and filesystem mtimes are clock-sensitive. Record process start time and game version; retrieve loaded assembly MVID/hash and content-pack path from runtime; compare composed XML/assets as well as assemblies.

4. [HIGH] Persistent settings are modified in the user’s real Config and restoration assumes shipped defaults. A crash can poison later runs, while some components may snapshot settings only at construction. Use an isolated RimWorld config/profile, snapshot raw settings files, restore them in an outermost `finally`, and reload/recreate the map after any setting whose runtime behavior is not proven dynamic.

5. [HIGH] The climate setup is internally weak. Reading candidate tiles may populate the caches the plan says never invalidate; “mean 50°C” does not guarantee 50°C at midsummer; three cell temperatures are affected by roofs, fires, and weather. Set climate before any tile read or explicitly invalidate caches, then verify tile seasonal curves and outdoor temperature on many unroofed cells. Freeze/record longitude, absolute date, local hour, latitude, and temperature throughout long tests.

6. [HIGH] The sites are not independent. Three maps in one quicktest world consume one RNG stream, retain earlier maps/components, and may be bundled into later saves. Seed string plus tile IDs cannot reproduce mapgen RNG state. Use one fresh world/process per site with preregistered distinct seeds, one map per fixture, and record RNG state or the exact save immediately before generation.

7. [HIGH] Bar 1 can false-pass because its expected allowlist comes from the live patched def: an unwanted injector simply becomes “allowed.” Total plant count can also satisfy coverage through plants outside eligible cells or spatial clumping. It can false-fail on ruins, deliberately spawned probes, crops, or non-wild plants. Compare against an immutable expected manifest, count naturally generated plants by cell intersection with the denominator, remove probe/setup cells, and measure largest bare region or quadrant coverage.

8. [HIGH] Bar 2 is likewise self-referential: a corrupted `wildAnimals` table defines its own acceptable result. Factionless filtering includes unrelated spawned animals, and seven kinds plus weak correlation does not prove the claimed distribution. It can false-fail from low animal density, heat, predation, or the invalid `spawnedTick > 0` rule when world time is already nonzero. Snapshot immediately after generation, identify mapgen provenance, use a fixed roster manifest, and sample far more animals.

9. [HIGH] Bar 3 can false-pass if Deep ash or other rungs existed before burning, or if burn-line/fire-hawk mechanics alter the patch. It can false-fail because floor burning is probabilistic and “grass present” does not prove each cell burned. Clear all ash and fire first, disable every unrelated fire mechanic, track a fixed cell cohort and its before/after terrain transitions, and repeat controlled burns across multiple patches.

10. [MED] Bar 4 divides whole-map plant count by a filtered plantable-cell count, so plants outside the denominator can hide bare expanses. Conversely buildings, patchmakers, temporary blockers, and unsuitable cells can inflate the denominator. Compute occupancy cell-for-cell, require the plant to occupy that eligible cell, and add spatial metrics such as maximum connected bare area. Keep visual judgment secondary.

11. [HIGH] Bar 5 counts unspecified plants, so boundary spread, Quickgrass, Scorchfruit, or untouched survivors can masquerade as EmberGrass regrowth. It can fail correctly functioning regrowth because the burn converts soil to low/non-growable ash, the seven-day run changes season, or temperature suppresses growth. Count only newly spawned EmberGrass in a sealed, fully cleared cohort; record terrain/light/growth factor; run replicated 50°C and 30°C controls. Budget the control site explicitly.

12. [HIGH] Bar 6 can pass on pre-existing yield or food gained from another meal without proving harvest or ingestion. Mature spawned plants also do not validate natural production. It can fail from pathing, reservation, harvesting skill/yield RNG, heatstroke, or pawn AI. Empty inventories and the area, issue a forced harvest/ingest job, verify job completion and consumed thing ID, and assert yield and food deltas attributable to that item.

13. [HIGH] Bar 7 treats disappearance as rot: hauling, eating, fire, cleanup, stack merging, or destruction can all pass. At 53°C it tests extreme-temperature acceleration rather than the advertised intrinsic spoil time. It can fail if temperature changes or rot ticking is delayed. Isolate forbidden pawns/fire, inspect `CompRottable` stage/progress and destruction cause, hold temperature constant, and compare against a reference food in the same chamber.

14. [HIGH] Bar 8 can pass on existing or fire-generated ash and on luminance differences caused by camera, UI, fire glow, clouds, or exposure. It can fail because cleaners remove filth, eligible cells are scarce, stochastic deposition is low, or whole-map drifts are invisible in the chosen frame. Start from zero loose ash, disable cleaners and all fire sources, track newly created IDs/count deltas, fix camera/render settings, and use replicated deposition-rate confidence bounds.

15. [MED] Bar 9’s def predicate can pass on a technically different but visually inert field; the judge can pass due to unrelated frame differences. It can fail when sparse random particles are absent in one still. Verify effective runtime overlay/sky/particle values, capture synchronized multi-frame sequences with identical camera and lighting, and require a quantitative pixel/particle distinction plus human review.

16. [MED] Bar 10 has the same defects, and comparing BlackRain with vanilla Rain may be invalid if Rain cannot naturally run under Pyrelands conditions. Daylight or particle RNG can create false failure. Force both weathers in an otherwise identical controlled map, collect multiple settled frames, and quantify rain/sky color rather than relying on one subjective screenshot.

17. [HIGH] K=3 is inadequate for fauna rank and Spearman tests; map-level spatial correlation makes raw pawn/plant counts pseudo-replicates. “Top three,” seven observed kinds, and ρ≥0.4 have unknown power and false-failure rates. Use at least 10–20 independent maps or continue until roughly 150–300 wild animals are observed, preregister a multinomial/Poisson goodness-of-fit test and confidence intervals, and keep deterministic purity checks separate.

18. [HIGH] Isolation is incomplete. Keeping `fireClockEnabled` on while merely voiding a census wastes the fixture; disabling burn line/fire-hawk only for four bars permits contamination of bars 3, 5–7, 9, and 10. Disable all unrelated Pyrelands ignition, migration, cleaning, pawn work, predators, and incidents for every controlled bar; test those mechanics separately.

19. [HIGH] Loading a minimal-tier save under the 614-mod list does not test full-list map generation, patchmakers, flora/fauna injection, or world-component initialization. It may also silently discard/add serialized content. Generate fresh full-list sites for GREEN-full; if UI generation requires an owner, make that a hard prerequisite rather than accepting a tier fixture.

20. [MED] Closing every modal can accept or dismiss consequential dialogs and conceal failures; “first failed precondition” forces repeated expensive boots. Unexpected dialogs should cause refusal with a screenshot and title. Evaluate and report all safe preconditions together, mark the log immediately before each phase, and guarantee settings/weather/storyteller/tier restoration and bridge release after every exception.
