Draw it SOUTH facing: front view, the RimWorld top-down three-quarter camera, the face toward the viewer. The fleet flier is a small alien flying creature from another world that darts into volcanic vapor columns to graze on fireweed. It is NOT a bird: it has very long, narrow, elegant wings, each wing about twice the length of its body, a sulphur-yellow body banded with magenta, wings of translucent smoky magenta membrane stretched between long thin finger-bones with sulphur-yellow edges, eyes black and glossy; its face is an elongated curled feeding proboscis in front of a long narrow skull, like a moth or butterfly tongue, four small black eyes in a row along each side of the head, no beak. It has a slim body and a long thin tail. Pose at rest: it stands on two thin legs with both long wings held half-open and drooping at its sides so the full length of each wing reads clearly. Same flat painterly RimWorld cartoon style, bold dark outline and line weight as the reference images. Square 1024x1024 canvas, fully transparent background, the whole creature and both wingtips INSIDE the canvas with a clear empty margin on every side, no shadow.
