Sluice box (river bank, 1x2) RM_SluiceBox / FLOWWORKS_QUARRY_DIGGING_1

CURRENT in game

NEW render(s)

facts PASS ; validator skipped; canon_check skipped; 256x400

prompt and notes

Footprint 1x2 cells, drawSize (1.4, 2.2): the structure fills the canvas height and about 85% of its width, centred. It is ONE texture drawn rotated for all four facings (Graphic_Single, rotatable), so it must read as plan view from above and stay believable when turned 90 degrees: no strong side-on perspective, no cast shadow in one direction. The river end (flared hopper) and the bank end (riffled trough) must be clearly different at a glance, and the riffle bars are what tell it from the weir. Same family as the weir, bank stake and silt trap: weathered timber, rope and iron, warm grey-brown palette. Realistic painted natural-history illustration: grounded, believable form and anatomy, matte surface texture, soft painted edges, the silhouette carried by value and colour contrast against a mid-tone ground rather than by a drawn line; never cartoonish, never cute, no outlines.

RimWorld building sprite, seen almost straight down from above: a gold-miner's sluice box two cells long, its long axis running top to bottom of the canvas. The TOP half is the intake mouth standing in shallow moving water: a wide flared timber hopper with a slatted wooden lip, water sheeting in over it. The BOTTOM half lies on the bank: a long narrow plank trough on short legs with evenly spaced low wooden riffle bars across its floor, a few flecks of dark gravel and a glint of gold caught behind the riffles, an iron-banded end board. Weathered grey-brown planks, rusty iron bands, hemp-brown lashing. No water, ground or shadow is painted outside the structure itself; the background is fully transparent.

Sluice gate door: mover panel RM_Sluice / FLOWWORKS_DOOR_FAMILY_1

CURRENT in game

NEW render(s)

facts PASS ; validator skipped; canon_check skipped; 256x256

prompt and notes

One 1x1 door; the game slides and mirrors this panel when the door opens, so the panel is left-right symmetric and spans the full canvas width as a horizontal band through the middle third of the height. Stuffable (wood, stone or steel tints it), so no colour of its own: light greyscale. The see-through slat gaps are the read that liquid passes through while it is shut. Sister of the security grate. Realistic painted natural-history illustration: grounded, believable form and anatomy, matte surface texture, soft painted edges, the silhouette carried by value and colour contrast against a mid-tone ground rather than by a drawn line; never cartoonish, never cute, no outlines.

RimWorld door-leaf sprite seen from above, like the moving panel of a vanilla RimWorld door: a cheap slatted canal sluice gate panel, five vertical planks with clear gaps between them held by two horizontal battens and a diagonal brace, rope-lashed at the joints. Painted in light neutral greys and off-whites only, with value detail in the grain, gaps and lashings, so the game can tint it any material colour. Background fully transparent, and transparent through the gaps between the slats.
