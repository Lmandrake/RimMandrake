## Intended description (the def text)

Camel-tall striders that walk the dry country between pools in single file down the caravan roads, dorsal sail-combs fanned to drink the wind as they come. From the rim, moving lines converging on water: pilgrimage made visible. The RM_ tier's own pilgrim ring — the free mod keeps the pilgrimage identity without a canon herd, which only stacks on top in the campaign.
