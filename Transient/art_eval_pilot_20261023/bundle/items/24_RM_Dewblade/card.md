## Intended description (the def text)

Shin-high tufts of flat grey-green blades standing edge-on to the sun and face-on to the wind, each blade tipped with a drip-point that feeds its own root. Whole hillsides of them align like iron filings -- the wind made visible. The grass-analog and graze base of the biome, densest toward true water.
