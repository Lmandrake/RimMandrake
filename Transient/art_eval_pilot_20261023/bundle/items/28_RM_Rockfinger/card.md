## Intended description (the def text)

A gnarled, wind-stripped hardwood rooted in the rim cracks of the tall stone, branches clawing out over the drop, bark pale as the stone it grips. At this biome's low sun its shadow runs down the rock face and across the ground for many times its height -- you meet the shadow before the tree. Scarce, and hardy the way it has to be. Slow, scattered, precious timber in high stone country; a felled rockfinger is a caravan event.
