## Intended description (the def text)

A slow grey sock of a thing that lies on the pool floor and eats what settles -- silt, dung, dead dew-smoke, the ash blown in from the desert. A pool with duul in it stays clear; a pool without them clouds in a season. Every oasis keeper knows the count. It is edible in the sense that most things are.
