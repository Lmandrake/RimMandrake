## Intended description (the def text)

A placid colossus, round-backed, with a row of dorsal rain-fins like a ship's ventilation cowls. It drinks once per wind-cycle and carries it for days, fins fanned to the wind as it walks — the caravan mount of the oasis string, and the beast the desert roads were beaded for. It replaces the muffalo/dromedary/elephant pack slot a donor mod once filled here.
