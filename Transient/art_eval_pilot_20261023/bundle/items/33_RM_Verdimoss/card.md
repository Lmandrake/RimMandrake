## Intended description (the def text)

Cushions of blue-green moss growing in beaded lines along seep cracks -- and over the old machines, where it takes the colour of oxidised bronze. It marks the seep lines and ages the ruins: every relic in the biome is half-mossed, which is its own sacred ambiguity. Zero yield; pure register, cheap density.
