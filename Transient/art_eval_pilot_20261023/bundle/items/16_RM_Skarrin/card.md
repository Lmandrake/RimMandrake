## Intended description (the def text)

A forearm-long pool fish that spends as much time above the water as in it. When the pool is fed a stocked pool boils with them, silver arcs snapping at the falling dew-smoke. It jumps, and it bites what it lands on — clears a pen wall from standing water; a handler leaning over the pool takes a face-strike.
