## Intended description (the def text)

The dactillions were a reptavian native to the sinkhole world of Utapau. Dactillions shared both avian and lizard traits, with long, thin wings and thick, scaled hides. A dactillion's pair of wings allowed it to ride the thermal updrafts in a Utapau sinkhole, and its prominent limbs allowed it to scale the rocky side. An avian cousin of the speedy varactyl, the dactillion served as a mounts.
