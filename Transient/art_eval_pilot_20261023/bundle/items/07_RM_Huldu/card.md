## Intended description (the def text)

A forearm-length velvet-skinned swimmer, dorsal comb folded flat like wet fur; clings when handled. Warm to the touch — not seep-warm, body-warm, with a pulse you can feel through the skin. Lifted from the water it does not thrash: it grips the handler's forearm and presses close, and it shivers. None whatsoever, which is its own kind of problem: the huldu is the row that makes the handler the uneasy party.
