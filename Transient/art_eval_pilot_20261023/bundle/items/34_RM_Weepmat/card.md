## Intended description (the def text)

A dark green-black mat hugging the cold stone faces, ridged like corduroy, every ridge square to the sea-wind, glistening along the weep-lines. It grows exactly where the stone weeps, so a weep-mat is the wet black streak's green echo -- a face without one is a face that has gone dry. Graze for the sillik.
