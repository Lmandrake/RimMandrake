## Intended description (the def text)

Bomas were ravenous beasts native to planet Onderon and its moon Dxun. In their native environment they hunted Cannoks with ease and were considered dangerous by several Mandalorian clans. They were sometimes introduced into ecosystems on other worlds where Cannoks had become established to help maintain their population.
