## Intended description (the def text)

A palm-wide bladder that hangs at the surface with a comb of fringe trailing below it, sieving the dew-smoke out of the water as it falls. At dusk the whole pool is dimpled with them, tilted to the same side like cups set out. It has no fight in it; a child can lift one out. Thin eating, and nobody minds -- pilgrims cook them for the broth, which tastes faintly of the stones.
