## Intended description (the def text)

The fanback was a cold-blooded predatory amphibian from the swamps of Naboo. Each fanback had a big dorsal spine along with a sensitive snout which allowed them to hunt for fish and other small free swimming prey with ease. They lay a high number of eggs and were known to protect these nest sites by hunting any creature bold enough to attempt to eat or threaten their clutch. It's for this reason that these eggs having been sold (or collected as trophies) has resulted in populations of these animals on other worlds.
