## Intended description (the def text)

A thumb-length shrimp-thing with antennae grown into two fine combs that it holds up into the current and licks clean. Kin to the mirrik above the water, and it shows: the same chitin, the same fringed silhouette, the same habit of mist-dancing at dusk, only underwater. It breeds in the shallows during the truce, in the open, in crowds -- which is why nobody who has sat at a pool at dusk can eat one without a small pang.
