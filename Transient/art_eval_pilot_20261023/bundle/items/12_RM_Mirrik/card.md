## Intended description (the def text)

Thumb-sized insects with water-combing mesh wings that mist-dance over the pools at dusk — from any distance, smoke rising off the water that flows the wrong way. It pollinates the blade-flora, and its cocoons are the source of dewsilk, the biome's signature trade good. The dusk swarm is the biome doing its actual living.
