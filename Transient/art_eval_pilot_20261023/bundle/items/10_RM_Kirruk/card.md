## Intended description (the def text)

A hawk-sized soarer that rides the wind forced up the tall stone, long stiff wings that are themselves combs — slotted trailing edges like comb-teeth — hanging motionless on the updraft off a rock face, wings combing the wet air as it hangs. It drinks the way the biome does, on the wing, and lands only to nest on the rims. The RM_ tier's own sky presence.
