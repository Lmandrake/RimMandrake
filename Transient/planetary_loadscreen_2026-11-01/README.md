Pick one, or rule another iteration. Each candidate sits beside its `seed_*.png`, the same camera on the real tile record
with the real ring (1.4 to 2.0 radii, equatorial), so before/after shows it is this planet.

- `candidate_twilight_terminator.png`: the judge's strongest. Scald and Twilight Sea together on the day/night line.
- `candidate_crater_ring.png`: the Scald crater ocean toward the viewer, with the Rust Cathedral to its right.
- `candidate_grey_sea_limb.png`: the Grey Sea on the terminator. Judge's defect: near-black patches at the upper-right limb.
