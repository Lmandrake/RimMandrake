Canon materials: what each one is good for

Rows are jobs, columns are materials. Each cell says how good that material is at that job, and what that means in plain words. Origin chips under each material say where a Jawa clan gets it. Steel, plasteel and durasteel numbers are MEASURED from today's defs (Core, the Armoury); every other number is a proposal. Every job is a soft preference (the wrong material works, it just costs you) except the key jobs marked HARD RULE, which take only one material.
