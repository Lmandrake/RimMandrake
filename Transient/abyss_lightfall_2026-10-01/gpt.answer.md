### D. The Mercy Engine

**What the player finds.** A circular clinic stands amid heaps of crutches, prosthetics, and keepsakes lowered by generations of desperate families. Its maker is plainly identified as a long-dead humanitarian relief fleet—not the Forsakens or any elder civilization. When a pawn enters its chair, every wound, disease, missing limb, addiction, and age-scar leaves their body as colored light and enters a waiting ceramic effigy. The effigies then rise, each visibly named for the affliction it carries: *Carcinoma*, *Shattered Spine*, *Luciferium Hunger*. Defeating them completes the treatment; retreating returns every surviving burden to the patient.

**What it does in game.**

- An **Anomaly-style ritual encounter** whose threat budget is generated from the patient’s health conditions.
- Effigies inherit readable abilities from those conditions: blindness extinguishes lights, asthma creates choking clouds, pain scars project psychic agony.
- Victory fully restores the chosen pawn, including missing natural parts, and can stabilize unstable **Biotech genes** without replacing the xenotype.
- The engine functions only once; afterward its medical core becomes an installable regeneration implant.

**Cost:** medium, requiring bespoke condition-to-enemy conversion and a ritual arena. **Risk:** A heavily damaged pawn produces the hardest fight, and failure can leave them worse than before—but every returning condition is announced and shown.

### E. The Ship in the Wall

**What the player finds.** A rescue gravship lies driven nose-first into the chasm wall, its decks turned sideways and its hull webbed in centuries of snapped ropes. Logs identify it as the *Kindly Measure*, lost while attempting to retrieve people and cargo lowered into Lightfall. Its crew died during the impact, but the flight computer still repeats its final instruction: **ASCEND WITH ALL SOULS ACCOUNTED FOR**. Once repaired, the ship tears itself free and climbs the shaft while whole ledges shear away around it. It emerges at the lip in a storm of black stone and trailing ropes, becoming the player’s gravship.

**What it does in game.**

- An **Odyssey gravship recovery** across a vertical-wreck pocket map with sideways rooms, broken bulkheads, and hanging cargo.
- Players must restore three systems, route ordinary electrical power, and move every desired corpse, relic, or survivor aboard before launch.
- Ignition starts a timed collapse: marked rockfalls progressively destroy the pocket map while the ship’s safe boarding zones remain clearly visible.
- Success transfers the repaired ship to the surface map with a unique **Rescue Winch** component that extracts pawns and cargo faster from future pocket-map expeditions.

**Cost:** large, needing a custom wreck map, staged collapse, and cross-map gravship transfer. **Risk:** Repairs attract dormant mechanoids embedded in the hull, while premature launch permanently abandons anything left below after an explicit manifest warning.

### F. The Lantern Tide

**What the player finds.** The chasm ends at a black brine lake covered by immense, sunless colonies of yellow glow-flora feeding on dissolved chemicals. A footstep begins a synchronized bloom: light races across the water, climbs the walls, and reveals every rope, cage, coffin, and ladder hanging above like rigging in a drowned harbor. The bloom awakens thousands of krizzaks, which pour from fissures and extinguish the lake one luminous island at a time. If enough colonies survive, the entire lake releases one final golden pulse that travels visibly from the bottom of Lightfall to the sky.

**What it does in game.**

- A territorial defense event in which **krizzaks attack light sources** rather than colonists; the battlefield shrinks into isolated visible pockets as colonies are smothered.
- Surviving colonies improve the quality of a harvestable **Harborheart bud**, using quest-reward quality tiers.
- The bud can be planted once at the colony as a bondable **Ideology-style sacred plant** maintained through pruning.
- A mature Harborheart produces ordinary biological light and vanilla heat, folding the Dark only within a small, sharply bounded pocket; it grants no sight or targeting beyond that boundary.
- Maximum survival also yields a permanent “Witnessed the Lantern Tide” positive memory and a spectacular colony ritual location.

**Cost:** medium, requiring synchronized lighting, objective-targeting krizzak behavior, and one unique plant. **Risk:** Firearms become unreliable as the visible islands collapse, and destroying the attackers carelessly can also destroy the irreplaceable colonies they are clinging to.
