# The biome: the Abyss

A country of obsidian-black crags on the planet's night side, past the terminator, -18 to 0 C typically. Its defining feature is **the Dark**: a real airborne substance (tholin aerosol: fractal organic particles coated in a frozen butane/propane rime, made by the antistellar aurora and blown here). It eats light in transit: sight and ranged accuracy collapse, lamps reach only a short way. **Heat folds the Dark**: in a warm pocket the hydrocarbon coating sublimates and each fractal grain collapses into a compact grain, so sharp-edged clear pockets appear where air is warm, and nothing but a thermometer can tell a clear pocket from the wall of Dark beside it. Where the Dark dies, the collapsed grains settle with acid chemistry and slowly **etch the rock** (the "Etchfall"), which is why the crags are twisted black stone. The only reliable light is biological: blue "darklight" gamma plants and yellow giant glow-flora, the land lit like a harbor at night. Wind comes only in violent gusts, never steadily. Rarely, the Dark lifts wholesale for a few hours: the **Unveiling**, the one time you see the country you live in. Witchfire storms are chemical lightning storms. The biome's giant is the **ghorrumak**, a 16-square dragon (fire breath, regeneration, "the thunder is its voice"). New creatures: the gharrek (gill-fans that open across the map at every gust to feed on stirred air), the durrgak (a shy digger that arranges obsidian rings and neat caches, "someone was here"), the krizzak (a flying moth that eats light and smothers lamps), the etchcap (gourmet fungus grown where etch-grain settles). A gravship landed in the Abyss slowly hides from Imperial pursuit and orbit, though probe droids still come. Whispered cryptid: "the Forsakens", visitors who come for the Dark, never seen; the fearful rumour is that they are Zabrak "Nightbrothers".

# The subject: LIGHTFALL

Lightfall is one named landmark (a single world tile): the deepest etch on the whole planet, a chasm on the terminator suture where damp, milder air (8-17 C) meets the Dark. It is **"where light falls in, the one place the Dark always dies"**: a clear pocket has stood over it for geological time and the grain-acid has eaten a chasm too deep to see the bottom of by lamp. Its in-game text: "Ropes, ladders, and worse have gone down over the years the way rumor always outruns confirmation; what any of them found is not something anyone who came back talks about." The design sheet adds: "whatever generations of the desperate have lowered into it." **What waits at the bottom is unwritten — that is your task.**

- Invented names welcome.
