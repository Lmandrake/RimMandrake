Nothing committed. Mod DLL rebuilt (uncommitted). Existing offline tests already cover SunHeatMath, ShadePatchGraph, WildLeaveMath, TrackPool and SandBuried, so those were not re-fuzzed.

## 1. Survey
- **Picked: moving-shade layer** (`RM_MapComponent_ShadeGrid.RefreshMovingShade/Register/Unregister`). Incremental dirty-rectangle bookkeeping over a shared grid with overlapping casters: the classic "incremental != fresh" bug, impossible to see live (needs several moving giants, sun changes, despawns). Extraction was ~110 lines.
- **Picked: sand swimmer machine** (`RM_CompSandSwim.Evaluate`). Surface/breach/submerge with a post-breach window, melee, droid immunity, storm-blind roll, every 30 ticks over many game days; sequence-dependent, and the live chains only ever hit a few paths.
- Not picked: ParentalEnrage (445 lines, but nearly all Verse: radial scan, relations, mental state; the pure part is a cooldown compare and nearest-wins, low value); TetherPull/DrumLure/DungSeeder/AquaticAmbusher (state interleaved with jobs and Verse effects, high extraction cost); ShadeHop (Verse pathing); VerminPopulation (counter + alert, small).

- **Fuzz**: `.../Source/SelfTestFuzz/{CreatureBehaviorsFuzz.cs,Program.cs,RimMandrakeCreatureBehaviors.Fuzz.SelfTest.csproj}` (net8.0, zero RimWorld refs); wrapper `src/RimMandrake/Utils/selftest_creaturebehaviors_fuzz.py` (picked up by run_selftests.py by name).
- **Invariants shade**: after every refresh the incremental grid equals a from-scratch recast of all live casters (exact float equality); cells in [0,1]; layer caster count == live registered count (dead casters dropped); Unregister never raises any cell; setting-off clears when forced. **bounds**: every cell `CastBody` writes lies inside `ShadowBounds` (random map, position incl. off-map, radius, direction incl. zero/tiny/axis, length).
- **Invariants swim**: never submerged off swim terrain; never submerged inside the surfaced window; off swim terrain always surfaced and a submerged swimmer breaches out; a live in-range target or melee threat never leaves it submerged and breaches; droid-immune (dry pawn target) and storm-blind targets drop the job (EndJob) and are not struck; storm roll consumed only when submerged with a target and chance < 1, at most once; melee threat read at most once; breach only from a submerged swimmer and sets `now + surfacedTicks`; `surfacedUntil` moves only on a breach; with nothing holding it up it submerges once the window passes and not before.
- **Seeds**: default 4,000 shade + 60,000 bounds + 4,000 swim (seeds 1..N) = 68,000 cases / 642k steps, 0.7 s; `--fuzz-scale 10`: 680,000 cases / 6.4M steps, 3 s; `--fuzz-scale 200`: 13.6M cases / 128M steps, 50 s: all 0 failures. - **Finding (tolerated, not fixed, behaviour preserved)**: turning the "Giants cast moving shade" setting OFF mid-game leaves existing moving shade standing: `RefreshMovingShade` sees no mover and returns early; it heals at the next forced recompute (every 2,000 ticks).
