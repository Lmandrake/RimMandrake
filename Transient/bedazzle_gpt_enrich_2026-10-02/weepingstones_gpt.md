## 1. The Last Caravan

- **Marks it serves:** 5; scavenger trade.
- **The player's experience:** **Audacity.** One legendary gorrask, Korrav, wears a freight rack so thoroughly mineralised that its upright ribs resemble another ancient condenser. The manifest identifies an entire surviving merchant caravan: sealed machine tools, trade bars, and the original carrier, which a collector offers to buy intact at the receiving court across its canyon. You can strip the rack for immediate salvage, or clear the old approach and escort Korrav through its one relocation, protecting the valuable cargo on the dry stretches between water. At the receiving pool it settles again; the contract closes permanently, and the creature remains visibly present rather than becoming repeatable transport.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Patch the existing gorrask `ThingDef`; add rack/container `ThingDefs`, a `QuestScriptDef`, and road-interception `IncidentDefs`. Main hook: custom `QuestPart.QuestPartTick()`, reading Korrav’s position, cargo integrity, and authored route checkpoints; writing delivery state, interception signals, payment, and final resting behaviour. Keep its existing single-cell footprint; use a static rack overlay and ordinary pawn movement. **Settings:** quest toggle, escort threat multiplier, cargo value, movement speed.
- **Readable signs:** A inspectable manifest, visible sealed cases, a route overlay, advance letters naming prospective interceptors, and broken cases left on the ground if damaged.
- **Drawn from:** [Death Stranding’s requested deliveries, lost cargo, and cargo damage](https://blog.playstation.com/2019/11/07/hit-the-ground-running-with-these-day-one-death-stranding-tips/) and [VFE Settlers’ caravan-raiding quests](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2052918119). Here the collectible is the surviving caravan **including its living carrier**; completing one ancient commercial journey permanently changes this named creature’s location.
- **Why it is unique here:** Nightside Ice’s sohl also moves once, but Korrav’s movement completes a physical freight contract; unlike the Rot, Fever Wood, and Rust Cathedral giants, it requires neither extraction from a body, ransom, nor repair.
- **Tier:** **Free (`RM_`)**: Korrav and its merchant house are invented; `RUT_` can identify the manifest’s former handlers as a lost Jawa clan without changing the quest.
- **Size:** **L**.

## 2. The Street Through Your Ship

- **Marks it serves:** 6; pilgrim traffic and market-day.
- **The player's experience:** **Exposure.** At one existing, hand-authored oasis, the pilgrim approach and practical landing shelf occupy the same narrow passage. Landing across it produces a queue of vellak and travellers beside your hull: you can depart, or permanently register a free public passage through this ship. Accepting means rebuilding an actual corridor through your home, where pilgrims pass and traders stop beside your merchandise—including representatives of factions you otherwise distrust. The charter follows this particular vessel; closing its passage on later visits becomes a recorded breach, rather than a reversible access toggle.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Corridor-marker and charter `ThingDefs`, arrival `IncidentDefs`, passage `JobDefs`/`ThinkTreeDefs`, and a custom visitor `LordJob`. Main hook: `MapComponent.MapComponentTick()`, reading the landed gravship footprint, authored approach cells, corridor reachability, and local truce radius; writing visitor destinations and charter compliance. Store the charter in an engine `ThingComp`; build on the owed cultural truce implementation. Preserve Odyssey’s visitor-clearance launch flow. **Settings:** toggle, visitor cap, arrival frequency, breach goodwill cost.
- **Readable signs:** A landing-preview approach overlay, queued crests and combs, painted corridor markers, a visible charter plate, and a launch list of remaining visitors.
- **Drawn from:** [Death Stranding’s shared roads and bridges](https://blog.playstation.com/2019/11/07/hit-the-ground-running-with-these-day-one-death-stranding-tips/) and [Odyssey’s removal of neutral visitors before launch](https://ludeon.com/blog/2025/08/update-1-6-4566-improves-gravships-shuttles-and-more/). This creates a lasting public easement through privately owned, mobile living space.
- **Why it is unique here:** Long Shade’s Shipfall Commons provides shade and Fever Wood’s rejected pad sells landing access; this grants a permanent passage through your vessel, while water access remains governed by legitimate residence and defence—landing alone establishes neither.
- **Tier:** **Free (`RM_`)**: invented travellers and an enforceable charter stand alone; campaign factions supply their distinctive market behaviour.
- **Size:** **L**.

## 3. Teach the Fish to Lie

- **Marks it serves:** 7.
- **The player's experience:** **Mischief.** An ancient service lock beside an oasis recognises the local vane array’s chord as its maintenance credential. A stocked loomu can learn that chord; net it into a transport trough and take its living counterfeit to a warehouse whose matching array has fallen silent. Before presenting it, you rehearse and listen: this particular fish keeps inserting its keeper’s name into the response. You get one authentication attempt—the clean imitation opens the service entrance, while the adulterated answer permanently arms the clearly marked exterior sentries.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Lock, trough, and training-target `ThingDefs`; `SoundDefs`; rehearsal/presentation `JobDefs`; a warehouse `QuestScriptDef`. Main hook: `JobDriver_PresentEcho.MakeNewToils()`, reading the loomu’s stored chord ID, rehearsal result, and lock ID; writing the irreversible accepted/rejected state. A loomu `ThingComp` stores a small authored repertoire; sound playback accompanies game-state events rather than requiring microphone analysis. **Settings:** toggle, training work, rehearsal difficulty, sentry strength.
- **Readable signs:** Actual chord playback, matching visual note symbols, exact subtitles for inserted words, a conspicuous “one attempt” warning, and visible sentries outside the water’s truce radius. Hearing ability is never required to solve it.
- **Drawn from:** [Dredge’s foghorn provoking a Night Angler response](https://dredge.wiki.gg/wiki/Abilities) and [VFE Ancients’ sealed, defended vault entrances](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2654846754). Here animal mimicry falsifies a machine credential; it changes access and security rather than locating or attracting a predator.
- **Why it is unique here:** Stillsand reads vibration and the Rust Cathedral responds to manners through its hum; this is a specific acoustic forgery using an already-built fish, with a permanent security consequence.
- **Tier:** **Free (`RM_`)**: invented loomu and condenser infrastructure provide the complete mechanism.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 4. The Stone Lets Go

- **Marks it serves:** 8.
- **The player's experience:** **Dread.** An irregular, heavily saturated sea-wind thickens the black seep lines until water enters the salt-bound seams of particular upright stone fins. Their toes visibly soften: ordinary mining, an explosion, or a destructive blow can now release the whole marked fin into its displayed fall strip. You can suspend quarrying and shore the toe, or deliberately undercut a valuable fin to expose its mineral seam—and possibly crush approaching raiders on dry ground. Once fallen, that fin stays fallen; the weather never creates replacement pillars or dries up an oasis.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** A rainless `WeatherDef`, accompanying `GameConditionDef` and `IncidentDef`, tagged natural-fin `ThingDefs`, rubble `ThingDefs`, and `SoundDefs`. Main hook: the toe’s `ThingComp.PostPreApplyDamage()`, reading active weather, seam saturation, adjacent shoring, and the damage instigator; signalling a `MapComponent` to resolve the authored fall strip into blunt damage and rubble. Attribute deliberate collapse damage to its initiator for existing truce retaliation. **Settings:** toggle, front frequency/duration, warning interval, collapse damage. Declare the biome’s heat as **vanilla overhead-sun heat**, with **ambient spring heat at hot seep sites where shade does nothing**; add no heat hediff.
- **Readable signs:** Broader wet-black seams, a static tilted silhouette, stone ticks, highlighted endangered toes, and an exact fall-area preview before work begins.
- **Drawn from:** [Vintage Story’s unstable rock collapsing when mined](https://wiki.vintagestory.at/Game_Playstiles) and [Against the Storm’s conditional weather hazards](https://eremitegames.com/recipes-cookbook-update/). This combines a temporary weather vulnerability with deliberate, permanent changes to a few authored surface landmarks, without a geological simulation.
- **Why it is unique here:** Abyss etchfall passively erodes exposed construction and Webwork’s rejected sheets fall after storms; these natural combs fall only when their visibly weakened supports are disturbed.
- **Tier:** **Free (`RM_`)**: the physical hazard stands alone; campaign narration may describe Sh’kaar’s dangerous sun without assigning blessings or curses.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 5. The Unspilling Casket

- **Marks it serves:** 2; dewsilk and scavenger commerce.
- **The player's experience:** **Relief.** Field study reveals that a mirrik cocoon’s dewsilk envelope preserves its occupant through an arrested developmental phase. A local experiment using dewsilk and seep-salt unlocks an expensive, portable biostasis casket: seal a willing passenger or downed pawn, then carry the occupied casket as freight. The bleeding trader you could not reach a hospital with becomes a rescue you can finish, and valuable captive animals can travel without being released into another biome’s wildlife. Once learned, manufacture and use work anywhere; buying and reselling dewsilk membranes becomes a lasting trade business.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Cocoon/sample and casket `ThingDefs`, membrane `RecipeDefs`, a `ResearchProjectDef`, and sealing/unsealing `JobDefs`. Main hook: `JobDriver_SealDewCasket.MakeNewToils()`, transferring the identified pawn into a custom cryptosleep-style `IThingHolder`; preserve vanilla suspension behaviour through an occupied, minifiable container. Read occupant mass, equipment, ownership, and discovery completion; write contained-pawn state and consumed membrane charges. Count occupant mass in caravan cargo; retain accessible contents through ship transfers and save/load. Gate research against schematic bypasses. **Settings:** toggle, research work, membrane cost, casing cost, sealing time.
- **Readable signs:** A labelled occupant silhouette, portrait and name in the contents panel, a sealing progress bar, departure manifests, and immediate visible ejection if the casing is destroyed.
- **Drawn from:** [VFE Tribals’ progression unlocked through gatherings and basic research](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3079786283) and [RimWorld’s cryptosleep occupants in ancient bunkers](https://ludeon.com/blog/2025/07/odyssey-preview-4-quests-the-mechhive-endgame-drones-and-more/). This requires a biome-specific biological discovery, then turns suspension into occupied portable freight; substantial research and consumable costs balance its full capability.
- **Why it is unique here:** The Rot’s gut-mother extracts implants and its draught removes living attachments; this preserves the entire pawn unchanged, while differing from Long Shade’s condenser and Leaning Scrub’s vaporator by conserving an occupant rather than producing water.
- **Tier:** **Free (`RM_`)**: invented mirrik materials and independently useful rescue equipment; existing flyers retain genuine 1.6 flight.
- **Size:** **L**.

**Build first: #4, The Stone Lets Go** — a bounded M build that immediately changes what players dare excavate or fight beside.
