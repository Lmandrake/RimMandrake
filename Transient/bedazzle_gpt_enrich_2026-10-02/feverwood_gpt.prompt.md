5. Every idea must be buildable in RimWorld 1.6 (XML defs, C# ThingComps, MapComponents, GameConditions, IncidentDefs, WeatherDefs, SoundDefs, Harmony patches, ritual defs, research projects, quests). Name the def types and the main C# hook.

# Binding project rules (violating one disqualifies the idea)

- RimWorld 1.6 with ALL five DLCs present (Royalty, Ideology, Biotech, Anomaly, Odyssey); depend on them freely.
- One fixed, hand-made, tidally locked planet: a dayside, a terminator, a nightside. NO worldgen features of any kind, no procedural planets, no variants. Content lives on maps, incidents, pawns, buildings, quests.
- ONE kind of heat planet-wide: vanilla temperature, heatstroke. Never invent a new "kind" of heat or a new heat hediff. Every extreme-heat biome declares which vanilla heat it is (overhead sun, low sun, or ambient heat where shade does nothing).
- No animal or pawn ever vanishes without a readable sign (a mark, a letter, a trail, a tell).
- If it flies in the fiction, it flies in the game (real 1.6 flight).
- The free tier (RM_, franchise-free) uses only INVENTED exotic names; genuine Star Wars IP (canon creatures like the dianoga, Jedi/Sith, named canon planets) belongs only in the separate campaign layer (RUT_). The free tier must stand alone, not be a thin fallback.
- One animal, one biome, unless there is an in-game reason (migration, a life stage that moves). Holes in a roster are filled with NEW invented creatures.
- Colour is alien and varied, never Earth-like.
- Every mod ships real Mod Settings: feature toggles, tuning sliders.
- The religion of the campaign (the Jawa "Salvation", nine small jealous gods) is in the campaign layer; a biome can TEACH a rite (found inscription at a site with a reason to be there -> research row -> ritual performable anywhere).
- NO GOD IS EVIL. Sh'kaar (the searing sun) and Zizzik (malfunction, the wrong spark) are "the hungry gods", dangerous, never villains.
- A god's favour shows ONLY through events, world state and subtle odds (and the ship's Narrator speaking of the gods vividly). NEVER a hediff, a stat blessing, a buff or a curse status.
- A rite may not grant a power. "Rituals create cohesion, not material rewards." Its effect MAY be a dramatic, risky world event.
- Powerful tech is good; balance it by cost or gating, never by narrowing what it does.
- Avoid anything that needs new multi-frame animation work. Static art, sound, terrain, map conditions, AI behaviour, quests and UI are cheap; new animated rigs are not.
