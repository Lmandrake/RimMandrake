## 1. Vethr Stitching: Keep the Thing, Replace the Loss

- **Marks it serves:** **2 — discoverable technology.**
- **The player's experience:** At a derelict fracture-testing bench, your researcher makes casts of matching breaks using powder milled from dead smartsteel. Completing the experiments unlocks Vethr stitching: rebuilding a smashed manufactured object around its identifiable remnant. That legendary rifle comes back with its quality, biocoding and history intact—but the colony pays replacement materials, expensive stitching compound and substantial powered work. You bring home a manufacturing technique learned through local experiments.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `ThingDef` for the bench, compound and remnants; `RecipeDef`, `ResearchProjectDef`, `JobDef`, `SoundDef`. Main hook: a Harmony interception of manufactured-object destruction, backed by `CompVethrIdentity`, records the original object’s supported vanilla state in a single persistent remnant; `JobDriver_VethrReconstitute` consumes ingredients and restores it. Preserve faction, quality, biocoding and operational state; rebuilding a hostile machine preserves its hostility. Cost scales with original construction ingredients, with compound surcharge and research gating rather than an equipment whitelist. **Settings:** feature toggle, research work, material multiplier, compound cost, remnant survival probability; compatibility handling for unsupported modded components.
- **Readable signs:** Destruction leaves ordinary wreckage plus a labelled, selectable remnant; the reconstruction bill previews exactly what survives and what rebuilding costs. The compound becomes visibly crazed waste during the job.
- **Drawn from:** **Caves of Qud’s NPC artifact repair**, which makes old equipment worth maintaining, and **VFE Ancients’ signal-discovered technological vaults**, which make discovery precede possession. Here, observation unlocks portable reconstruction that preserves an object’s identity after destruction; neither cited feature supplies that package. [Qud developer notes](https://freeholdgames.itch.io/cavesofqud/devlog/162886/the-tomb-of-the-eaters-is-here), [VFE Ancients](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2654846754).
- **Why it is unique here:** It shares **Pyrelands’** local-learning/global-use structure, but performs costly artifact reconstruction rather than electrical protection, **Webwork’s** traction, **Greentide’s** haemostasis or **Abyss’s** darkness displacement.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**; Vethr is invented, and the entire discovery and manufacturing chain stands alone.
- **Size:** **L**—principally because destruction interception and identity persistence require careful compatibility work.

## 2. The Kharv, Keeper of the Aisle

- **Marks it serves:** **5 — giant.**
- **The player's experience:** A tracked guardian sixteen cells long enters the avenue, its chassis wide enough to conceal your whole expedition. Its inspection fan sweeps across the ground; stopping outside the marked clearance strip and ceasing hostile actions lets it continue its patrol. Its immense opaque body briefly blocks other defenders’ sight, giving you an opportunity to recover a wounded colonist behind it. If challenged, it stops and fires within its guarded sector; withdrawing beyond that sector ends the engagement without pursuit.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Multi-cell `ThingDef`, existing mechanoid weapon/projectile defs, `EffecterDef`, `SoundDef`; custom `ThingComp` plus `MapComponent`. Main hook: `CompKharvPatrol.CompTick`, reading authored patrol nodes, footprint clearance, line of sight, movement and attack stances; writing chassis position, warning phases and bounded weapon targets. Maintain its full physical and sight-blocking footprint as it moves, with interpolated drawing of static directional sprites. Reuse existing exemption logic without adding explanatory text. It has no taming or recruitment interface. **Settings:** spawn toggle, patrol speed, inspection duration, guarded-sector dimensions and weapon damage.
- **Readable signs:** Audible tread impacts precede arrival; inspection lines and a charging weapon identify the exact dangerous sector. The machine stays visible throughout movement and leaves a colossal wreck if destroyed.
- **Drawn from:** **Rain World’s individualistic creature AI** and **Subnautica’s Cyclops attention management through silent running**. Here, readable attention belongs to a non-pursuing territorial guardian whose physical bulk also creates tactical cover; the player manages encounters with its duties. [Rain World](https://store.steampowered.com/app/312520/Rain_World/), [Subnautica: Silent Running](https://unknownworlds.com/en/news/silent-running-update-released).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike **Forge’s giant-on-the-clock**, the Kharv offers no scheduled harvesting window, and unlike **Sump’s tar beast**, its passage requires precise local etiquette rather than evacuation ahead of indiscriminate destruction.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**; an invented guardian with complete territorial behaviour.
- **Size:** **L**—large moving footprints and reliable bounded targeting are the substantial work.

## 3. Irrav: The Sky Grows Teeth

- **Marks it serves:** **8 — interesting weather.**
- **The player's experience:** Rust fines rise vertically from service slots and hang in narrow, glittering sheets above the plateau. White bars appear in the empty sky, and corresponding rectangles on the deck brighten until their edges shimmer violently. You extend overhead screening across an exposed work area before those rectangles reach full intensity, or abandon the job and use an existing roofed passage. The air remains dry and motionless; the immediate sensation is machinery, metal and merciless overhead sunlight.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `WeatherDef`, `GameConditionDef`, `IncidentDef`, `EffecterDef`, `SoundDef`. Main hook: `MapComponentIrrav.MapComponentTick` maintains a small cache of illuminated rectangles; a scoped Harmony postfix on `GenTemperature.GetTemperatureForCell` adds their temperature increment only where the fixed **+79° sun ray** is unobstructed. The resulting value feeds vanilla heatstroke and temperature checks. Existing architectural shadows retain their geometry; the additional highlights brighten and fade. Use a custom weather overlay and static particles, with zero precipitation and wind. **Settings:** toggle, rarity, duration, warning interval, affected area and temperature increment.
- **Readable signs:** Rising metallic glitter, sharp white rectangles and intensifying shimmer precede peak exposure. Cell inspection displays the actual elevated temperature; screening visibly extinguishes the highlight beneath it.
- **Drawn from:** **Oxygen Not Included’s Cosmic Upgrade**, specifically super-bright surface sunlight and protective surface construction, plus **ReGrowth’s atmospheric visual effects**. This makes visible industrial light patterns govern exposure through ordinary RimWorld temperature, without importing ONI’s sunburn condition or ReGrowth’s vegetation. [ONI developer update](https://kleiforums.com/game-updates/oni-alpha/273433-r466/), [ReGrowth](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2260097569).
- **Why it is unique here:** **Long Shade** makes shade an economic resource, whereas Irrav is a rare industrial sky hazard; its suspended fines also differ from **Abyss’s** falling, wall-eroding etchfall.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**; Irrav is an invented phenomenon with a complete visual and survival identity.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 4. The Dorrik Interlock: Choose the Door That Survives

- **Marks it serves:** **Other — irreversible access consequences built on the already-ruled line-cycle.**
- **The player's experience:** Two linked transfer doors stand open across different routes through a factory block. Before an underground line-cycle, their mechanical indicators reveal which passage will close; you can install a heavy brace to preserve that passage, forcing its counterpart into the closed position instead. When the cycle arrives, living things stop as already ruled, and the interlock settles into its new configuration. On later visits, the altered route remains: saving access to a service court may have sacrificed the shortest evacuation corridor.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `ThingDef` for paired doors and braces; `JobDef`, `SoundDef`, `EffecterDef`. Main hook: `MapComponentDorrik.MapComponentTick` reads the existing cycle’s warning/resolution phase, linked fixture IDs and completed brace jobs; it writes the junction’s persistent indexed position and updates doors, pathfinding and sight. This adds a consequence to the cycle rather than another cycle system. Each junction indexes once; HP repair does not reset its saved position. Door sweeps resolve visible collisions through damage and remains rather than deleting occupants. **Settings:** toggle, brace cost/work, warning duration and collision damage.
- **Readable signs:** Direction arrows, moving position indicators, grinding sounds and a highlighted door sweep show the coming change. The resulting sealed doorway remains a physical obstacle with an inspection explanation.
- **Drawn from:** **Dwarf Fortress’s lever-operated drawbridges**, particularly mechanisms that communicate their raising direction, and **Against the Storm’s glade events**, which demand labour while dangerous consequences remain active. Here, intervention permanently changes an authored route instead of providing a reusable gate control or a completed-event reward. [Dwarf Fortress developer update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=975370&enddate=1631213184&feed=steam_community_announcements), [Against the Storm exploration update](https://eremitegames.com/ats-exploration-update/).
- **Why it is unique here:** It shares consequential timing with **Forge**, but the decision concerns a permanent mechanical junction rather than a giant’s recurring timetable.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**; Dorrik interlocks make the factory’s existing cycle consequential without campaign knowledge.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 5. Rekko’s Second Signature

- **Marks it serves:** **9 — a found, portable Salvation rite.**
- **The player's experience:** In a droid enclave’s communal repair court, pilgrims have scratched a Salvation inscription beside the ordinary maintenance sign-off board: *“A repair needs another hand willing to answer for it.”* Copying it opens a research row and subsequently a rite performable anywhere. The congregation gathers around an already repaired possession and names its repairer and witnesses, accepting continuing responsibility for preserving it. Later, deliberately scrapping that visibly mendable object becomes a breach of trust among those named; losing it in battle becomes shared grief.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Inscription `ThingDef`, `ResearchProjectDef`, `PreceptDef`, `RitualPatternDef`, `RitualBehaviorDef`, `RitualOutcomeEffectDef`. Main hook: custom `RitualOutcomeEffectWorker.Apply`, recording witnesses and the pledged object’s identity in a `GameComponent`; destruction/deconstruction notifications distinguish combat loss from deliberate disposal. Ritual quality controls social cohesion only, with bonus rewards disabled. Separately, sustained maintenance conduct subtly weights existing breakdown incidents toward earlier readable warnings; scrapping mendable things removes that favourable weighting. No favour hediff, stat change, repair output or granted ability. **Settings:** rite toggle, attendance/work requirements, social-consequence strength and incident-weight adjustment.
- **Readable signs:** The inscription, research description and ceremony explicitly explain the continuing obligation. A maintenance ledger lists signatures; later letters identify the disposal or loss, while the Narrator may say, “Rekko remembers the hand that reached for the bin.”
- **Drawn from:** **Ideology’s communal ceremonies and social outcomes**, and **VFE Tribals’ gathering-based knowledge progression**. This reverses the latter sequence—finding and research teach the ceremony—and replaces its technological rewards with persistent social responsibility. [Ideology developer preview](https://ludeon.com/blog/2021/07/ideology-adds-social-roles-and-rituals/), [VFE Tribals](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3079786283).
- **Why it is unique here:** **Warscar’s Open Boast** declares an ambition that invites a challenge, whereas the Second Signature witnesses completed work and creates an enduring obligation without dispatching a challenge or granting power.
- **Tier:** **Campaign, `RUT_`**; the inscription, Salvation theology and Rekko narration belong wholly to that layer.
- **Size:** **M**.

**1st — Vethr Stitching:** strongest match for the owner’s favourite: locally learned technology with lasting value everywhere.  
**2nd — Irrav:** comparatively affordable, immediately visible, and makes the Cathedral’s overhead sun mechanically distinctive.  
**Order rationale:** establish the reason to visit and the survival experience first; then invest in the giant’s more demanding movement and targeting.
