**Five verbs:** Stabilize · Contest · Scrape · Declare · Mask — checked: no repeats.  
**Five systems:** Emergency medicine · Megafauna life cycle · Building damage carried by gravships · Ideology and incident history · Acoustic detection — checked: no repeats.

All five operate on the authored planet’s maps, with static art, sound and behaviour. Ambient temperature and vanilla heatstroke remain the thermal rules; shade provides no relief from saturated heat.

## 1. Nethr Lace — Keep the Blood Inside

- **Marks it serves:** 2, discoverable technology.
- **The player's experience:** A doctor examines a freshly severed native branch: its branching vessels close around the wound while sap still pulses through them. Completing that field observation opens a research row for **nethr lace**, manufactured from local vascular film, refined sap and advanced medicine. Later, on any biome, a medic kneels beside a catastrophically wounded pawn and applies one cartridge to stop every existing source of bleeding, including an amputation stump. The wounds still need treatment, but the rescue changes from watching a death countdown to getting someone home.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `ResearchProjectDef`, `ThingDef`, `RecipeDef`, `JobDef`; a saved discovery flag in a `GameComponent`. Main hooks: custom `JobDriver_ObserveClosure.MakeNewToils()` records the qualifying Greentide observation; `JobDriver_ApplyLace` records sealed injuries, with Harmony postfixes on injury and missing-part `BleedRate` getters. It reads individual bleeding wounds and writes closure records that persist until those wounds heal; subsequent wounds require another application. No body-plan whitelist. **Mod Settings:** enable toggle; observation work, manufacturing work and ingredient-cost sliders. Balance the full haemorrhage-stopping effect through discovery and expensive cartridges.
- **Readable signs:** The sample visibly changes from leaking to closed; research receives a discovery letter. Treated wounds gain a “nethr closure” annotation and their displayed bleeding falls to zero.
- **Drawn from:** **Green Hell’s wound inspection and treatment**, which makes bodily damage something the player examines; this adds an alien mechanism that becomes portable colony technology. **VFE Ancients’ recovered technology and nanite workshop** supplies the discovery-to-manufacture progression; this uses field biology and consumable medicine instead of permanent superpowers. [Green Hell](https://store.steampowered.com/app/815370/Green_Hell/), [VFE Ancients](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2654846754).
- **Why it is unique here:** It shares the **Pyrelands’** learn-here/use-anywhere structure, but exports vascular engineering rather than electrical protection; unlike **Miasma’s** fever-forging, it treats injuries without creating survivor qualifications.
- **Tier:** **Free, RM_** — invented material, independent discovery chain and useful medicine without campaign theology.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 2. The Varkhoss — A Giant Escaping Its Own Skin

- **Marks it serves:** 5, giant beast.
- **The player's experience:** A varkhoss arrives with shoulder armour so overgrown that it can barely turn between the trees. After consuming enough vegetation, it begins its single adult moult, smashing trunks to lever enormous plates away from living flesh. Nearby predators attack the exposed body while other animals tear at tissue on the discarded plates; the giant continues crushing, feeding and defending itself throughout. You contest the aftermath with everything else in the jungle, deciding how much valuable plate and meat you can physically extract before the battlefield eats your hunters.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Race `ThingDef`, `PawnKindDef`, `BodyDef`, `LifeStageDef`, `JobDef`, shed-plate `ThingDef` and `SoundDef`. Main hook: `CompVarkhossMaturation.CompTickRare()` accumulates **actual ingested biomass**, then schedules scraping jobs and an irreversible adult transition. Reuse the existing Shatterer felling aura; armour follows the remaining anatomical plates, and local predator AI evaluates the exposed animal and tissue normally. The beast remains damageable before, during and after moulting. Two static body states and detachable plate graphics suffice. **Mod Settings:** spawn toggle; biomass threshold, armour, plate yield and predator-interest sliders; no mastery-based escalation.
- **Readable signs:** Lifting plate edges, a changing inspect description, long scraping sounds, exposed flesh and persistent discarded plates. Death leaves a normal corpse; departure leaves a letter and the wrecked trees.
- **Drawn from:** **Grounded’s creature-part equipment**, especially its armoured Roly Poly equipment, makes anatomy materially valuable; this puts extraction inside a contested life transition. **VFE Insectoids 2’s thumper-summoned bosses** establishes formidable biological opponents; this giant arrives through ordinary ecology and cannot be summoned or recruited. [Grounded](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/time-for-the-backyard-to-get-hotter-and-hazier), [VFE Insectoids 2](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3309003431).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike **Forge’s** giant-on-the-clock, maturation follows feeding rather than a timetable, and unlike **Weeping Stones’** stone-crab, the opportunity is an exposed living body amid unrestricted predation.
- **Tier:** **Free, RM_** — one invented species, naturally resident only in the Greentide; never tameable or mountable.
- **Size:** **L**.

## 3. Skrith Rasp — The Jungle Tastes Your Hull

- **Marks it serves:** 6, gravship touch.
- **The player's experience:** Wet exterior metal acquires small green crescents, followed by filings beneath the wall: a colonial organism called skrith is rasping into it. It attacks ordinary metal colony structures too, so a landed gravship becomes another enormous feeding surface. You send workers outside to scrape threatened sections while they endure the same ambient heat as everyone else. Taking off carries the existing damage home; beyond the Greentide, the rasp visibly blanches and dies, leaving recognisable bite scars on transported buildings.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `ThingDef`, `JobDef`, `WorkGiverDef`, `SoundDef`, plus XML-injected `CompProperties` on eligible metal buildings. Main hook: `CompSkrithRasp.CompTickRare()` reads biome, exterior exposure, material, proximity to wet growth and existing dry-air exclusion; it writes colony coverage, vanilla hit-point damage and a scar seed. `PostExposeData()` keeps those records attached to the transported building, so Odyssey’s ordinary building transfer carries them. Scraping is a designated manual job; no flight-stat penalty or special launch lock. Native growth has zero flammability. **Mod Settings:** enable toggle; establishment rate, damage rate and scraping-work sliders.
- **Readable signs:** Static green bite patterns, metallic grinding, dropped filings and a building inspection warning before serious damage. Departure produces a blanching/death notice; marks never silently disappear.
- **Drawn from:** **Subnautica’s Lava Larvae attaching to vehicles and bases** supplies ecological trespass onto machinery; this consumes structural material and leaves persistent damage. **Odyssey’s travelling colony** supplies the continuity: the same attacked building goes home, without requiring a new ship subsystem. [Subnautica attachment feature](https://unknownworlds.com/en/news/prawn-update-changelog), [Odyssey gravships](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3022790/RimWorld__Odyssey/).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike **Webwork’s** pitched roots through doorframes, skrith attacks exposed material rather than entrances, and unlike **Stillsand’s** buried ship, departure remains possible but cannot undo the feeding.
- **Tier:** **Free, RM_** — a complete invented biological hazard; its living stage stays in its home biome.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 4. Ozzik’s Open Boast — Give the War Your Name

- **Marks it serves:** 9, relationship to the gods.
- **The player's experience:** Inside a ruined assembly hall in the habitable band, you find an inscription crowded with incompatible claims of greatness: people once shouted their ambitions loudly enough to compete with the jungle. Translating it opens a Salvation research row, after which the rite can be performed anywhere, indoors or outside. Each participant declares an ambition and the congregation answers with the colony’s name, committing it publicly to the boast. Days later, a warned hostile challenge may arrive while ordinary threats continue; the emotional turn is proud solidarity followed by the vulnerability of having made yourself heard.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Inscription `ThingDef`, `ResearchProjectDef`, ritual `PreceptDef`, `RitualBehaviorDef`, `RitualOutcomeEffectDef`, `IncidentDef` and `LetterDef`. Main hook: custom `RitualOutcomeEffectWorker` records the completed boast in a `GameComponent` and queues a possible challenge incident from an existing hostile faction. The hall and inscription are hand-placed map content, never worldgen. Completion is **once per colony**, wherever the player chooses; its public record cannot be withdrawn. Outcomes grant ordinary social cohesion only: no research points, recruits, goods, powers or favour status. **Mod Settings:** rite toggle; challenge probability, warning interval and fixed threat-budget sliders.
- **Readable signs:** A permanent entry recording the participants’ words, a challenge letter with an arrival window, and visible attackers entering normally. The Narrator might say, “Ozzik has carried your small names farther than you intended.”
- **Drawn from:** **Ideology’s leader speeches and social festivals** supplies collective performance; this attaches an irreversible public consequence. **VFE Tribals’ gatherings that advance research** supplies ritual progression as a design reference; here archaeology and subsequent research teach the rite, while performing it grants no technical advancement. [Ideology rituals](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1392840/RimWorld__Ideology/), [VFE Tribals](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3079786283).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike **The Sump’s** Mob’Unloo’s Price, this invites a hostile answer instead of holding a faction off, while **Pyrelands’** Struck Glass produces an immediate physical catastrophe rather than lasting public notoriety.
- **Tier:** **Campaign, RUT_** — explicitly Salvation and Ozzik; his dangerous encouragement of ambition is never villainy.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 5. Work Between the Crashes

- **Marks it serves:** 8, soundscape and tactical mastery.
- **The player's experience:** A chewing fight beyond the causeway becomes loud enough to cover your workers’ tools. You manually send them to cut an escape route during that racket, then halt them when the feeding ends. A distant tree crash can cover a brief burst of work, but sound cover never protects a pawn already seen or attacked. The reward is the tense satisfaction of using the jungle’s ongoing violence as an opportunity while every animal remains free to kill you.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `SoundDef`, noise-profile `DefModExtension`s on relevant jobs and weapons, plus `ThinkTreeDef` additions for appropriate existing fauna. Main hook: `MapComponent_GreentideAcoustics.MapComponentTick()` maintains short-lived, spatial sound events from actual work, combat and existing tree-fall callbacks. A custom `ThinkNode_ConditionalHeardWork` compares work noise with competing sound at the **listener’s location**, then permits investigation jobs; it never clears aggression or overrides sight. Stored events are a bounded ring buffer, avoiding continuous whole-map scans. Existing fliers investigate using real 1.6 flight. **Mod Settings:** detection toggle; hearing range, masking strength, mix-volume sliders and directional-caption toggle.
- **Readable signs:** Audible tool noise emerging from the mix, animals turning toward its source, and optional equivalent captions such as “cutting audible northeast.” Muting audio never removes the information; the existing apex-silence cue remains intact.
- **Drawn from:** **Rain World’s layered, spatial environmental audio** supplies the audible ecosystem; this makes competing sounds affect detection. **Project Zomboid’s distinct work sounds and directional sound indicators** supplies readable action noise; this couples that information to simultaneous native activity rather than an abstract stealth meter. [Rain World developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/road-to-the-igf-videocult-s-i-rain-world-i-), [Project Zomboid work sounds](https://projectzomboid.com/blog/news/2014/06/sounds-awesome/), [directional indicators](https://projectzomboid.com/blog/news/2021/08/clutterzed/).
- **Why it is unique here:** It shares sound-mediated danger with **Stillsand**, but uses competing airborne ecological noise to conceal labour rather than geophones locating buried predators; unlike **Fever Wood’s** lure stakes, it creates no decoy target.
- **Tier:** **Free, RM_** — substantial jungle behaviour without relying on campaign Roil or Breaklight.
- **Size:** **M**.

**1st — Nethr Lace:** fills the owner’s favourite gap with a permanent, portable discovery and a clear rescue payoff.  
**2nd — Varkhoss:** makes the existing Shatterer code into a distinctive living giant and a memorable free-for-all encounter.  
**Order rationale:** establish the retained technology first, then invest in the larger creature build; both remain substantial free-tier contributions.
