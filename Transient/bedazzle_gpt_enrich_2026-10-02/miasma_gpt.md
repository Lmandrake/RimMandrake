**Verb check:** Embark · Appraise · Wire · Revoke · Protect — five distinct verbs; no repeats.  
**System check:** Gravship passenger manifests · Salvage valuation · Electrical generation · Ritual-driven incidents · Wild reproduction — five distinct systems; no repeats.

All five use existing, hand-authored maps. The Miasma’s heat remains **vanilla ambient heat**, with vanilla heatstroke: shade does not cool its 43–59°C air. Its permanent haze and rain prohibition remain intact. Builds use static art, existing movement and effects; settings use saved `ModSettings` with an actual configuration window.

## 1. The Last Dry Deck

- **Marks it serves:** 6, 8.
- **The player's experience:** During a breath-tide surge, a flotsam-combing crew retreats along the remaining roots toward your landed gravship, dragging two packed workshop frames. They ask for passage to Bitterleaf **with their livelihood**, not merely permission to stand aboard. Taking them means clearing real deck space, abandoning freight or dismantling a room while the approach disappears beneath brine. Once you depart, their transport commitment is binding; refusing leaves them visibly attempting a worse escape. The emotional beat is **reluctant responsibility**.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `IncidentDef`, `QuestScriptDef`, `PawnKindDef`, workshop-frame `ThingDef`, and `SoundDef`. Main hook: a custom `MapComponent.MapComponentTick()` reads the existing surge state, dry-route connectivity and gravship footprint; a `QuestPart` tracks actual passengers and loaded frames. Harmony at `GravshipUtility.PreLaunchConfirmation` supplies the passenger manifest and abandonment warning; Odyssey performs the flight. Settings: enable, crew size, occurrence chance per surge, workshop footprint and destination deadline.
- **Readable signs:** The crew appears before its route closes; dragging trails, shouted requests and the manifest identify everyone. Departures, casualties and separation produce named letters; bodies remain where deaths occur.
- **Drawn from:** **RimWorld’s refugee quests** supply temporary outsiders with needs, while **Odyssey** carries animals, guests and even incidental occupants on the ship’s footprint. This adds a rescue caused by actual delta geography, with bulky productive assets competing against your freight rather than a generic hosting timer. [Refugee quest introduction](https://ludeon.com/blog/2020/08/1-2-update-with-new-quests-psycasts-gear-and-more/), [Odyssey’s carried colony](https://ludeon.com/blog/2025/06/odyssey-preview-2-gravships-and-space/).
- **Why it is unique here:** The **Weeping Stones’ pitched Pilgrim Passage** permanently ceded a public corridor through the ship; this is a finite evacuation whose cargo and destination become your responsibility.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**: an independent delta salvage crew and transport story; `RUT_` can supply Jawa dialogue without supplying the mechanic.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 2. What the Scuttlers Say It Is Worth

- **Marks it serves:** 7, 3.
- **The player's experience:** Combing crews sell unopened, tangled salvage bundles from the already-ruled Flotsam Yard. Before bidding, your appraiser listens beside a bundle: wet feeding chatter suggests decomposing provisions, while shell taps ringing against broad surfaces suggest substantial metal underneath. Neither sound identifies an exact item; a loud bundle can contain valuable machinery buried beneath worthless fermenting freight. You stake silver on an informed estimate, then cut your purchase open and discover whether your ear earned its keep. The emotional beat is **commercial suspense**.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Bundle `ThingDef`, `SoundDef`, `JobDef`, `WorkGiverDef`, and auction `IncidentDef`. Main hook: `JobDriver_AppraiseBundle` reads a bundle’s persistent `ThingOwner` contents, organic decay and associated share of the existing scuttler population, then presents matching audio evidence; the auction writes ownership and transfers silver once. Sound derives from actual contents, never a fresh loot roll when inspected. Settings: enable, auction frequency, price spread, appraisal duration, sound volume and equivalent descriptive captions.
- **Readable signs:** Scuttlers visibly enter gaps and tap exposed surfaces; captions distinguish feeding chatter from hollow resonance. Opening leaves the severed binding and exposes the actual contents.
- **Drawn from:** **Outer Wilds’ signalscope** makes sound evidence interpretable and worth investigating; **Dredge** turns concealed salvage into saleable discoveries. Here, interpreting animal-made resonance changes what you dare pay **before** recovering the goods, rather than merely locating a signal or collecting treasure. [Signalscope design interview](https://www.mobiusdigitalgames.com/news/separating-the-signal-from-the-noise), [Dredge’s salvage and selling loop](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1562430/DREDGE/).
- **Why it is unique here:** **Stillsand’s geophone** locates approaching subterranean predators; this judges the composition of an already-visible commercial lot, with financial uncertainty instead of an ambush warning.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**: a complete scavenger-trading profession, using the existing invented scuttlers.
- **Size:** **S**, assuming Flotsam Yard containers exist.

## 3. The Spoiled Cargo Lights the Workshop

- **Marks it serves:** 2, 3.
- **The player's experience:** A discarded measuring instrument in a Bitterleaf compost bed still registers current between its buried electrodes. Reproducing that reading across the map’s salinity gradient teaches **vorrsalt cells**: electrochemical beds using processed delta loam, silt ceramics and delta salt to collect electricity from ordinary decomposition. Once researched, you can manufacture and operate them anywhere, buying spoiled provisions cheaply and using the resulting power for any normal electrical load. Large installations are powerful, but consume bulky feedstock and expensive replaceable electrodes. The emotional beat is **practical discovery**: yesterday’s failed delivery pays for today’s workshop.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Instrument and generator `ThingDef`s, `ResearchProjectDef`, `ResearchTabDef`, `RecipeDef`, and study `JobDef`. Main hook: a custom `CompPowerPlant` overrides `DesiredPowerOutput`; its companion `ThingComp.CompTickRare()` reads contained `CompRottable` items, remaining feedstock energy and electrode wear, then writes power output and consumption. A completed field demonstration unlocks the research row; exported processed materials permit manufacture elsewhere. This collects decay’s output without directing the Working or selecting organisms. Settings: enable, feedstock consumption, electrical yield, electrode cost and research effort.
- **Readable signs:** The original meter needle moves; demonstrations display measured current. Operating cells show remaining feedstock, electrode condition and wattage, with visibly exhausted residue after consumption.
- **Drawn from:** **Raft’s biorefiner** converts raw food into engine fuel; **Fertile Fields** converts compost into fertilizer and separately burns plant scraps for electricity. Vorrsalt cells make decomposition itself a transportable electrical industry, accepting failed freight without a combustion or chemfuel intermediate. [Raft’s biorefiner](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appgroupname=Raft&appids=648800&enddate=1575893936), [Fertile Fields’ compost and power chains](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3225843229).
- **Why it is unique here:** The **Rot’s Gut-Mother** digests corpses to recover implants; this consumes decomposing cargo for unrestricted electricity and exports durable equipment rather than a living digestive organ.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**: its invented material, field discovery and manufacturing economy stand alone.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 4. Rekko’s Recall of the Written-Off

- **Marks it serves:** 9.
- **The player's experience:** At a hand-placed river-mouth disposal depot, you find a Salvation inscription beneath generations of condemnation stamps: the dismantlers taught Rekko that “discarded” did not mean “finished.” Studying it opens a research row for a rite performable anywhere with a salvaged disposal docket. The clan strikes through its condemnation verdict and reads the inventory aloud; the rite provokes an old reclamation response that begins collecting loose metal goods, then dismantling powered structures when accessible scrap runs out. Its machines can reach your stores and gravship, and the response cannot be cancelled by ending the ceremony. The emotional beat is **terrible anticipation**: Rekko remembers the discarded; the reclamation machinery remembers its instructions.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Inscription and docket `ThingDef`s, `ResearchProjectDef`, `PreceptDef`, `RitualPatternDef`, `RitualBehaviorDef`, `RitualOutcomeEffectDef`, and `IncidentDef`. Main hook: `RitualOutcomeEffectWorker.Apply()` consumes that docket’s unique code and starts a custom incident; a `LordJob` directs ordinary-sized reclaimers toward visible goods and machinery. Arrival uses existing drop-pod presentation. Each docket resolves once; no blessing, power, goodwill or scripted loot payout accompanies it. Settings: enable, force budget, warning interval and reclamation speed.
- **Readable signs:** The docket’s crossed-out stamp persists; a receiver answers with an obsolete collection receipt. Landing markers precede arrival, and targeted assets acquire visible collection marks. The Narrator speaks of Rekko’s attention without declaring a mechanical favour status.
- **Drawn from:** **Anomaly’s void provocation** establishes a voluntarily triggered dangerous event; **VFE Ancients’ burst signals** connect surviving communications to forgotten war infrastructure. This substitutes an irreversible disposal recall and economically targeted dismantling for a general horror summons or a newly revealed loot vault. [Anomaly’s ritual events](https://ludeon.com/blog/2024/04/anomaly-preview-3-cultists-hate-chanters-and-rituals/), [Ancients’ burst-signal discovery](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2654846754).
- **Why it is unique here:** The **Rust Cathedral’s droid rite** repairs one willing machine; this revokes an institutional verdict and unleashes a dangerous collection operation without repairing or inheriting anything.
- **Tier:** **Campaign, `RUT_`**: its meaning belongs to Salvation and Rekko; the free biome already has substantive independent content.
- **Size:** **L**.

## 5. The First Egg Above Water

- **Marks it serves:** 5, 8.
- **The player's experience:** Among the stranded juveniles, one predefined, small invented species—the violet-and-copper **evrith**—survives its transition onto land. A pair lays eggs in damp delta loam beneath genuinely benign rainbow flowers: the question becomes whether their young can complete the same terrestrial life cycle. A collector offers immediate silver for the founders, while a desperate Wildsteam pilgrim asks you to protect the actual nesting patch through predation, hunger and the next irregular surge. Selling removes those named founders; protecting them requires barriers, nearby scuttler food and keeping brine from their nest. The emotional beat is **earned astonishment** when the first land-born offspring matures and lays its own egg.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Species `ThingDef`, `PawnKindDef`, `LifeStageDef`, egg `ThingDef`, `QuestScriptDef`, and observation `JobDef`. Main hook: `ThingComp.CompTickRare()` tracks one bounded family’s maturity and nesting conditions; a `QuestPart` records the second-generation reproductive milestone. It reads existing salinity and scuttler availability, writes eggs and ordinary offspring, and permits a small persistent wild population after success. No mutation editor or ecology simulation is needed. The sea-to-delta life-stage migration explicitly justifies its cross-biome presence. Settings: enable, encounter rarity, maturation duration and population cap.
- **Readable signs:** The same pawns visibly change life-stage graphics; shed gill cases remain. Eggs, feeding damage, brine intrusion and offspring maturity are inspectable; sales, predation and deaths remain traceable.
- **Drawn from:** **Terra Nil’s wildlife habitat needs** make environmental provision consequential; **Biotech’s natural reproduction** makes successful birth a vulnerable sequence rather than an instant spawn. Here, the milestone proves a new wild life cycle across two generations, while the commercial alternative removes its actual founders. [Terra Nil’s wildlife needs](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1593030/Terra_Nil/), [Biotech’s natural reproduction](https://ludeon.com/blog/2022/10/biotech-preview-3-reproduction-children-genetic-modification-release-date/).
- **Why it is unique here:** The **Slime** records and manipulates genetic information; this protects an undirected success already occurring in the muck, with no gene interface and no involvement from the Warden Mother.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**: invented fauna and a complete ecological quest; the pilgrim’s faction dialogue can stand independently.
- **Size:** **M**.
