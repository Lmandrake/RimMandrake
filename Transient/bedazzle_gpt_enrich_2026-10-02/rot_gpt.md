## 1. Unseaming: Learn What the Jungle Leaves Behind

- **Marks it serves:** 2 — discoverable, portable technology.
- **The player's experience:** In a digestion ruin, you find machinery stripped of its organic binders while delicate metal assemblies remain intact. You expose material coupons to a defended digestive fungus, recording which interfaces it separates; harvesting prematurely triggers its existing spore defence and ruins the experiment. That field evidence unlocks **Unseaming**, an industrial process using fired fungal mineral rind, chemical reagents and precision clamps, manufacturable and usable anywhere. You can now destroy a damaged charge rifle to recover its component assemblies instead of merely smelting it, turning the clan’s knowledge into a profitable salvage specialism: **competence earned through observation**.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `ThingDef`, `ResearchProjectDef`, `RecipeDef`, `JobDef`, plus an authored `RM_TeardownProfileDef`. Main hook: `RM_JobDriver_Unseam.MakeNewToils()` reads the donor’s recipe/profile, material, HP and selected assembly; reserves and consumes the donor exactly once, then creates the declared recovery. Profiles support weapons, equipment and dismantled machinery; ingredient-based recovery supplies the fallback. Balance through expensive tooling, reagent consumption and skilled labour. Settings: feature toggle, research work, reagent cost, labour and recovery sliders.
- **Readable signs:** Exposed seams change static texture; the experiment log records accepted/rejected materials. The dismantling preview names what survives and what is permanently destroyed.
- **Drawn from:** **Anomaly’s fleshsacks**, which digest organic matter while retaining inorganic valuables, supply the selective-digestion seed; this turns observation into transferable industrial knowledge. **Terra Nil’s building recycling** supplies deliberate dismantling; this recovers chosen assemblies from purchased or captured goods rather than clearing your own restoration equipment. [Anomaly preview](https://ludeon.com/blog/2024/03/anomaly-preview-1-fleshbeasts-fleshmass-heart-pit-gate-and-dreadmeld/), [Terra Nil](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1593030/Terra_Nil/).
- **Why it is unique here:** It shares Pyrelands’ local-learning/global-use structure, but its mechanism is selective disassembly; unlike the Rust Cathedral’s fracture-cast rebuilding, it permanently sacrifices a donor to recover existing parts.
- **Tier:** **Free (`RM_`)** — a complete scavenger technology using dead industrial materials, with no exported live preparation.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 2. The Brullith That Cannot Lie Down

- **Marks it serves:** 5 — develops the ratified brullith into the Rot’s own signature giant.
- **The player's experience:** An enormous brullith stands motionless beside a ruin, its animal joints disappearing beneath pale fungal shelves; smaller hybrids gather around its trembling legs. A swallowed loading-frame yoke has grown through its chest, reopening wounds whenever it lowers itself, and the established wound-sharing law spreads that suffering through its attendants—including your nearby tamed hybrids. Saving it means treating the smaller patients first, arranging enough medical capacity, then conducting staged surgery whose incisions also enter the shared network. Success lets this particular giant finally rest; failure leaves named patients dead and the survivors scarred: **tenderness under terrible responsibility**.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Extend its `ThingDef` and `PawnKindDef`; add `BodyDef`/`BodyPartDef` entries, an embedded-object `HediffDef`, surgical `RecipeDef`s and encounter `IncidentDef`. Main hook: `RM_CompBrullithCare.CompTickRare()` reads existing injury-network membership and injury severity, presents the treatment roster and advances the crisis. Surgical workers pass damage through the existing splitter with a transaction guard: damage is conserved and never recursively duplicated. Cutting without preparation provokes defensive spores and protective attendants. Settings: encounter toggle, frequency, initial injury burden and surgery work.
- **Readable signs:** Visible yoke, bloody shelf margins, laboured breathing and individual health entries. Surgery previews identify the recipients of shared incision damage; deaths leave ordinary corpses.
- **Drawn from:** **Dwarf Fortress’s diagnosis, fracture treatment and medical histories** inform treatment sequencing; here several patients form one conserved injury problem. **VFE Insectoids 2’s summoned bosses** provide the contrast of a substantial creature encounter; this encounter is resolved through care, without a boss-summoning device or progression drop. [Bay 12 healthcare development](https://bay12games.com/dwarves/dev_2009.html), [VFE Insectoids 2](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3309003431).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike Nightside Ice’s hessarund, this is a mobile, recognisably embodied patient whose shared wounds demand intervention; its identity comes from a medical relationship rather than a giant landform, heat source or excavation service.
- **Tier:** **Free (`RM_`)** — gives the already-approved hybrid its own anatomy, encounter and history.
- **Size:** **L**.

## 3. The Ship Becomes a Spore

- **Marks it serves:** 6 — gravship touch.
- **The player's experience:** After a long landing, lilac Sheen films gather beneath your ship’s deck edges, then form reproductive pockets around contaminated cargo bays. Early growth can be scraped away, but mature pockets penetrate the decking: removing them now means replacing contaminated sections and unloading their rooms. Your departure decision is concrete—spend another dangerous working day cutting away valuable deck, abandon an infected compartment, or launch carrying a declared birth event that will discharge Sheen inside the ship after landing. The jungle has mistaken your trading vessel for its dispersal body: **dread about what you are taking home**.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `ThingDef`s for attached pockets and scraping tools, `JobDef`s and existing Sheen gas/exposure definitions. Main hook: `RM_CompHullBrood.CompTickRare()` reads landed gravship membership, exposure and pocket maturity; writes contamination stages and affected deck cells. Save brood records on an engine `ThingComp`, carrying them across map transfers; `PostSpawnSetup()` rebuilds their positions and schedules the declared discharge. Patch the launch confirmation to show consequences. Settings: toggle, establishment delay, maturation rate, removal labour and discharge severity.
- **Readable signs:** Static pearl clusters, deck-cell overlay and a manifest listing contaminated compartments. Launch warnings give the expected discharge; exposed crew receive the established Sheen signs and disease handling.
- **Drawn from:** **Death Stranding’s Timefall/container damage** informs inspecting cargo before departure, while **Against the Storm’s dormant Blightrot cysts** inform visible maturation before activation. This adds irreversible deck replacement and transport of a biological event, rather than repair spray or seasonal building purges; it uses **Odyssey’s actual gravship transfer**. [Kojima cargo guidance](https://www.kojimaproductions.jp/en/DSTips), [Blightrot design](https://eremitegames.com/blight-fighter-update/), [Odyssey gravships](https://ludeon.com/blog/2025/06/odyssey-preview-2-gravships-and-space/).
- **Why it is unique here:** It shares hull colonisation with the Webwork, Greentide and Rust Cathedral pitches, but its package is reproductive maturation and transported exposure, rather than door obstruction, hull abrasion or spying.
- **Tier:** **Free (`RM_`)** — Sheen ecology supplies the entire premise; mature pockets are an attached dispersal stage, never a harvestable tea or symbiont.
- **Size:** **L**.

## 4. A Breath You Can Work Inside

- **Marks it serves:** 7 — soundscape with practical meaning.
- **The player's experience:** Beneath the constant hybrid-insect hum, a guardian grove draws a long, wet breath; its caps then begin clicking. Those sounds describe its actual defensive sequence: gathering spores, imminent discharge, then depleted reserves after the hiss. You time the approach, brewing job and retreat around that sequence, keeping protection on because background Sheen and the grove’s other defences remain dangerous. Eventually you recognise a grove’s breathing without selecting it: **the intimacy of understanding something alien**.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `SoundDef`s and `EffecterDef`s attached to existing guardian `ThingDef`s; extend their defensive component. Main hook: `RM_CompGroveBreath.CompTickRare()` reads actual spore reserves, disturbance and queued discharge, then emits the corresponding tell and commits the cloud after its warning interval. Sound follows biological state rather than a separate weather timer. Settings: toggle for added timing behaviour, warning lead, replenishment rate, ambience volume and tell volume; optional caption/glyph duplication.
- **Readable signs:** Directional suction, cap clicks and wet release, with matching static icons. Selecting a grove identifies the current sound’s meaning; distant events never pretend to be nearby.
- **Drawn from:** **Rain World’s shelter-seeking between lethal downpours** informs committing work inside a hazardous interval; here the interval belongs to one defended organism and falls only as fungal emission. **ReGrowth’s distinct weather ambience** informs layered environmental audio; these original sounds additionally report actionable organism state. [Rain World](https://www.rainworldgame.com/), [ReGrowth sound credits and framework](https://github.com/Helixien/ReGrowth2).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike Stillsand’s and Nightside Ice’s predator rumbles, Greentide’s silence cue, Warscar’s Geiger choir or Forge’s voices, this sound exposes a local guardian’s resource expenditure so players can schedule dangerous work.
- **Tier:** **Free (`RM_`)** — makes the existing defended groves legible without adding another resource or creature.
- **Size:** **S**, assuming the existing grove defence code is accessible.

## 5. The Bought Quarrel

- **Marks it serves:** 9 — Mob’Unloo’s remaining rite.
- **The player's experience:** In a digestion site, beneath a defended grove, the Jawa clan uncovers a ceramic weighing slab from an old counting-house; its underside records a Salvation practice: someone must carry the cost that everyone else passes onward. Reading it reveals a research row, and completing that row teaches a rite performable anywhere. During the rite, the clan publicly guarantees a specific, already-encountered unpaid trade claim and invites both living sides to collect at its camp; the guarantee cannot be withdrawn or erased by another performance. Armed delegations arrive with incompatible demands, and your trading, accommodation and security decisions determine whether collection becomes settlement or bloodshed: **accountability made frighteningly physical**.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Inscription `ThingDef`, `ResearchProjectDef`, `PreceptDef`, `RitualPatternDef`, `RitualBehaviorDef`, `RitualOutcomeEffectDef`, `ThoughtDef`, `QuestScriptDef` and arrival `IncidentDef`s. Main hook: `RUT_RitualOutcomeEffectWorker_BoughtQuarrel.Apply()` consumes an unresolved claim ID and records the irreversible guarantee in a `GameComponent`; quest parts schedule delegations on the clan’s current map. Participants receive cohesion memories only. Any favour alters subtle attendance/coincidence odds; no favour status, power, free goods or automatic goodwill. Settings: toggle, claim scale, arrival delay and escort strength.
- **Readable signs:** Readable inscription, named creditor/debtor, exact demands and arrival letters. Delegations enter and leave on foot through map edges. The Narrator says: “Mob’Unloo has found chairs for everyone you hoped would never meet.”
- **Drawn from:** **Pathologic 2’s character-specific barter valuations** inform incompatible demands; this adds a binding third-party guarantee and armed collection. **Ideology’s participant-based ritual outcomes** supply the ceremony framework; material attachable rewards are disabled, and its consequence is an obligation-bearing event. [Pathologic barter development](https://ice-pick.com/progress-report-june/), [Ideology rituals](https://ludeon.com/blog/2021/07/ideology-adds-social-roles-and-rituals/).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike Nightside Ice’s Cold Ledger and the Sump’s Mob’Unloo’s Price, it pays no dead claimant or exclusion fee: the clan assumes a living commercial dispute whose opposing parties must physically meet.
- **Tier:** **Campaign (`RUT_`)** — explicitly belongs to Salvation, Mob’Unloo and Jawa trader identity.
- **Size:** **L**.

**1st — A Breath You Can Work Inside:** cheapest build; immediately adds skill and a recognisable biome voice.  
**2nd — Unseaming:** supplies the missing portable discovery and gives scavenger traders a reason to return.  
**Then — The Brullith:** the strongest signature encounter, once conserved wound-sharing is proven reliable.
