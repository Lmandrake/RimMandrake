**Five verbs:** Split; Berth; Reroute; Admit; Expose — checked: none repeat.  
**Five systems:** Demolition technology; Gravship landing; Weather-driven navigation; Ideology ritual outcomes; Quest evidence and faction relations — checked: none repeat.

All five operate on existing maps and fixed quest sites. Fever Wood’s heat is **ambient vanilla temperature**: shade does not reduce its 45°C air temperature; cooling and vanilla heatstroke remain authoritative. Art uses static states, existing effects and sound.

## 1. Sarruth — the Wedge That Eats a Wall

- **Marks it serves:** 2.
- **The player's experience:** A crown carpenter demonstrates why pulling a swollen drommath loose can split its mounting instead. You reproduce the pressure mechanism using seep-oil, vaulm lacquer and a metal casing, completing a dangerous bench trial before the research row opens. The resulting disposable wedge slowly fractures a selected wall, locked door or rock face without an explosive blast. Later, your scavenger traders sell cartridges across the planet: Fever Wood has taught them how to enter a sealed ruin without shelling its contents.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `ResearchProjectDef`, `RecipeDef`, `ThingDef`, `JobDef`, `SoundDef`. Main hook: custom `JobDriver_RMPressureSplit`, with `CompPressureWedge.CompTick()` advancing the installed cartridge. A discovery job reads the existing drommath’s handled/inflated state and records the witnessed trial in a saved `GameComponent`; research thereafter proceeds anywhere. Installation reads target durability and consumes cartridges; pressure writes targeted structural damage, leaving ordinary roof-collapse consequences intact. Use multiple cartridges for tougher targets rather than arbitrary material exemptions. Cured lacquer and prepared oil remain transportable ingredients. **Settings:** enable technology; research work, cartridge cost, splitting speed and noise radius sliders.
- **Readable signs:** A static amber pressure indicator advances; cracks appear on the target; the tooltip names the expected opening time and roof risk. “Quiet” means quieter than explosives: cracking and collapse still alert the sekkulaath’s existing hearing system.
- **Drawn from:** [Grounded’s resource analysis](https://support.obsidian.net/article/70-what-should-i-do-after-starting-a-new-game) turns found materials into crafting knowledge; [VFE Insectoids 2’s Hivetech](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3309003431) derives practical technology from insect biology. Sarruth requires witnessing a mechanical behaviour and engineering its reproduction; it requires neither a permanent hive nor continuing live-specimen ownership.
- **Why it is unique here:** It shares **Pyrelands’** local-discovery/export structure, but its package is deliberate pressure demolition learned from crown husbandry, while **Webwork’s** traction lance relocates targets.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**: invented technology, complete discovery sequence and export economy.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 2. Urveth — the Berth That Costs a Road

- **Marks it serves:** 6.
- **The player's experience:** Your arrival survey finds a berth on a massive trunk shoulder—but its clearance envelope crosses the only living road serving two herd-groves. The cheap option permanently cuts that limb: your ship fits, and the groves lose their established access. The expensive option commissions a steel saddle spanning two trunks, with lacquer-lined clamps and a separately supported approach. This makes landing an act of responsibility: the crown already carried a civilisation before it carried your home.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `QuestScriptDef`, construction `ThingDef`s, `TerrainDef`, `JobDef`, `SoundDef`. Main hook: a custom berth `MapComponent`, integrated with Odyssey’s landing confirmation and placement through Harmony. It reads the actual ship footprint against marked trunk-supported cells; it writes completed support terrain, permanently severed road cells and the quest’s settlement response. Preview clearance before committing. Construction bears on trunks, never marsh foundations; landing and launch feed their noise into the existing pool-response system. **Settings:** enable berth quests; saddle cost, construction work and pool-response intensity sliders. Vanilla Gravship Expanded needs its own compatibility adapter.
- **Readable signs:** Landing preview distinguishes trunk support, unsupported footprint and the limb scheduled for cutting. Herd-grove representatives stand at the proposed cut; afterward, a visible stump and abandoned toll sign remain.
- **Drawn from:** [Odyssey’s movable colony](https://ludeon.com/blog/2025/06/announcing-odyssey-and-update-1-6/), [Vanilla Gravship Expanded’s maintainable vessel systems](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3609835606), and [Death Stranding’s costly shared roads](https://blog.playstation.com/2019/11/07/hit-the-ground-running-with-these-day-one-death-stranding-tips/). Here, acquiring transport access can destroy another community’s existing route; maintenance alone cannot undo that decision.
- **Why it is unique here:** **Stillsand** takes the ship through moving terrain; Urveth asks what inhabited, load-bearing crown infrastructure you will sacrifice to accommodate it.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**: invented grove communities make the decision complete; `RUT_` can place it at Sporefall.
- **Size:** **L**.

## 3. Nereth — When the Crown Closes

- **Marks it serves:** 8.
- **The player's experience:** A still pressure front arrives, and the giant leaves begin folding downward; the visible sky contracts into narrow amber slits. Their enormous ribs settle across exposed boughway junctions, closing particular routes while leaving the permanent trunk approaches open. You must move herds, reposition defenders and redirect a visiting caravan before its return road closes. The emotional beat is claustrophobia: the safe upper world becomes smaller while the black pools remain the forbidden shortcut.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `WeatherDef`, `GameConditionDef`, leaf-barrier `ThingDef`s, `SoundDef`. Main hook: `MapComponent_RMCanopyClosure.MapComponentTick()` reads weather stage and authored leaf/junction links, then spawns or removes static impassable rib objects and refreshes pathing. Closure has a warning stage and waits for occupied cells to clear; the fixed trunk backbone prevents accidental total entrapment. Existing giant-leaf plants supply the phenomenon—no additional plant roster or spreading vegetation front. Weather has zero precipitation and zero wind; temperature stays vanilla. **Settings:** enable closure; frequency, warning duration, closed duration and affected-junction proportion sliders.
- **Readable signs:** Leaves have two static poses; highlighted junctions show their closure countdown. A dry leathery creak accompanies folding. Bird calls remain audible unless an actual sentinel rises, preserving that warning’s exclusive meaning.
- **Drawn from:** [Against the Storm’s Forest Mysteries and warned seasonal activation](https://eremitegames.com/ancient-hearth-update/) make weather demand preparation; [ReGrowth’s custom weather visuals](https://github.com/Helixien/ReGrowth2) make conditions legible. Nereth expresses its consequence as specific physical road obstructions rather than resolve penalties or a cosmetic overlay.
- **Why it is unique here:** **Webwork’s** creeping web front progressively occupies territory; Nereth is a simultaneous weather response by existing leaves that temporarily changes the crown’s route network.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**: alien canopy physiology needs no campaign mythology.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 4. The Last Customer — Mob’Unloo’s Unfinished Bargain

- **Marks it serves:** 9.
- **The player's experience:** In an abandoned crown tollhouse, you find an inscription beside a counter opening onto the road: its keeper was forbidden to close while a customer remained unheard. Studying it opens a research row and teaches a rite performable anywhere. The clan gathers behind a counter, names its fiercest outstanding enemy and sends an irrevocable invitation to hear that enemy’s price. An armed delegation answers with negotiation, an extortion demand or an attack; the clan must face the customer it deliberately admitted.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Inscribed `ThingDef`, `ResearchProjectDef`, `PreceptDef`, `RitualPatternDef`, `RitualBehaviorDef`, `RitualOutcomeEffectDef`, `QuestScriptDef`. Main hook: custom `RitualOutcomeEffectWorker.Apply()` creates a persistent delegation quest; its `QuestPart` selects demands and its `LordJob` controls parley or violence. It reads faction history, clan cargo and the chosen host; it writes an invitation, arrival and unresolved demands. Arrival uses a valid edge route or an existing vanilla shuttle, including appropriate equipment for orbital maps. This is one irreversible campaign invitation, with no reroll through repetition. **Settings:** enable rite; delegation strength, arrival delay and demand-severity sliders.
- **Readable signs:** The inscription explains the tollhouse’s purpose; the research row previews the risk. A signed acceptance names the delegation before arrival. The ship’s Narrator says, “Mob’Unloo counts the customer you hoped would leave.”
- **Drawn from:** [Ideology’s ceremonies shaped by participants and surroundings](https://ludeon.com/blog/2021/07/ideology-adds-social-roles-and-rituals/) and [Pathologic 2’s barter under threat](https://store.steampowered.com/app/505230/Pathologic_2/). This creates a dangerous visitor event: it grants no power, favourable prices, goods, goodwill or divine status. Ordinary bargaining occurs afterward; the rite itself creates cohesion through shared responsibility.
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike **Nightside Ice’s** Cold Ledger, this neither repays a dead creditor nor seals a counter-gift; it brings a living adversary into a sacred exchange whose outcome remains dangerous.
- **Tier:** **Campaign, `RUT_`**: explicitly Salvation, using Mob’Unloo’s remaining slot; he demands that bargains be heard, without becoming a villain.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 5. Varruth’s Catalogue — Who Is Making the Salvage?

- **Marks it serves:** 5; scavenger-trader identity.
- **The player's experience:** A porter deposits a merchant’s coat whose concealed sleeve contains one of your colony’s numbered delivery receipts. Varruth’s salvage house has already offered that same merchant’s possessions for sale, although its ledger dates their “recovery” before the recorded disappearance. Comparing porter deposits, invoices and witnesses reveals the business: debtors receive forged notices directing them to particular pool margins, where their belongings become bait and subsequently saleable salvage. You can expose the house and rescue its next intended victim, or accept a final hush payment and permanently abandon the case.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** `QuestScriptDef`, evidence `ThingDef`s, `IncidentDef`, `JobDef`, merchant `PawnKindDef`. Main hook: `CompRMEvidence` on selected porter-delivered items sends collection signals to a custom `QuestPart`; the existing porter remains the source of the drops. The quest reads numbered receipts, witness testimony and a named pool ID; it writes evidence stages, the broker’s response, an escorted debtor’s route and lasting trade/faction consequences. Rescue happens on the margin before the snare takes the victim. Exposing the house closes that merchant permanently. **Settings:** enable investigation; evidence opportunities, victim deadline and retaliation-strength sliders.
- **Readable signs:** Every threatened debtor has a name, escort, forged notice and marked destination. Letters identify the deadline; a witnessed taking leaves the existing limb tell and the victim’s identifiable possessions. Evidence persists in a case panel.
- **Drawn from:** [Kenshi’s kidnapping and rescue gameplay](https://lofigames.com/ja/play_kenshi/) supplies the threatened-person intervention; [Anomaly’s Pit Gate](https://ludeon.com/blog/2024/03/anomaly-preview-1-fleshbeasts-fleshmass-heart-pit-gate-and-dreadmeld/) supplies a local threat with escalating, readable consequences. This adds a forensic trading conspiracy: success comes from proving and interrupting exploitation of an unkillable animal.
- **Why it is unique here:** **The Rot’s** giant contains a recoverable ship and navigator; Fever Wood’s giant exposes a criminal supply chain through possessions it already returns, without being killed, entered or fully revealed.
- **Tier:** **Free, `RM_`**: Varruth, its victims and the complete case stand independently; `RUT_` can make the receipt and threatened merchant Wildsteam clan connections.
- **Size:** **L**.

**1st — Sarruth:** the strongest missing progression reward, with portable usefulness and a natural export business.  
**2nd — Varruth’s Catalogue:** gives the existing giant a specific, disturbing story rooted in scavenger commerce.  
**Together:** they make Fever Wood a place that changes both what the clan can manufacture and what it will knowingly sell.
