**Five systems:** animal harvesting · gravship contact records · production acoustics · ritual/quest deadlines · cargo authentication — checked: no repeats.

All five use the existing fixed planet and its Slime maps, retain the mild temperature regime, and need only static art, sound, jobs and UI.

## 1. Qellum, Sold by the Slice

- **Marks it serves:** 5 — the giant’s plot hook; 3 — resources.
- **The player’s experience:** Qellum is a particular titanoslime that grew beside a margin-cook’s waste hatch; feeding reflex still brings it back to that hatch. The cook has died with three enormous raw-slime consignments already sold, and her executor offers the clan the unfinished contracts at a steep discount. You must carve the promised quantities from a moving, engulfing animal, catch its escaping lobes and render them before the buyers’ departures. Leading Qellum onto dry ground makes the operation safer but shrinks the stock you have already promised.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Extend the existing animal `ThingDef`; add `QuestScriptDef`, `JobDef` and rendering `RecipeDef`s. Main hook: a custom carving `JobDriver` calls `CompContractMass`, reading growth stage, remaining mass, hunger and signed quotas; it writes reduced mass, actual lobe spawns, collected quantities and quest signals. Reserve mass atomically so simultaneous cutters cannot duplicate yield; contracts belong to this one named animal and cannot reset. **Settings:** quest toggle, quota multiplier, cutting duration, wound-reflex strength.
- **Readable signs:** Amber bands identify Qellum; the purchase screen shows measured stock against committed quantities. Cutters leave visible incision decals, released lobes remain physical animals, and engulfed pawns stay listed in its inspection panel with visible silhouettes.
- **Drawn from:** **Dwarf Fortress’s butchery** derives products from creature tissues; **Against the Storm’s trade routes** bind desired goods to payment and delivery time. This combines those into a dangerous *live liquidation*, with shrinking inventory and contracts signed before harvesting, rather than ordinary slaughter or stockpile export. [DF butchery](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Butcher), [Against the Storm trade routes](https://eremitegames.com/experimental-trade-routes/).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike the **Weeping Stones’ Last Caravan** or the **Rot’s swallowed navigator**, Qellum carries no recoverable treasure: its own living mass is the freight, and cutting changes the remaining bargain.
- **Tier:** **RM_** — Qellum, the cook’s estate and margin merchants form a complete invented trade story.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 2. The Landing You Cannot Take Back

- **Marks it serves:** 6 — gravship touch.
- **The player’s experience:** The landing preview identifies biological residue on exposed underside surfaces and waste outlets: blood, shed tissue and organic refuse that will physically touch the body. You can clean those contact points before landing, but an Ascendant Helix broker offers excellent payment for leaving a named passenger’s sample outlet open through a complete reading. Accepting permanently adds that previously absent gene to the local archive; the broker’s client subsequently comes to extract it in person. Later, you meet the wealthy rival wearing the rare adaptation you thought made your clan distinctive.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Add outlet and sample-marker `ThingDef`s plus a bounded `QuestScriptDef`. Main hook: `MapComponentTick()` checks the landed gravship footprint, exposed contact cells and recorded sample provenance; it advances the existing reading process and writes an irreversible archive entry and client quest. It never samples through sealed floors, and the client uses the existing open-body extraction procedure, including rider, coma and antidote requirements. **Settings:** contact-disclosure toggle, reading duration, broker frequency, payment scale.
- **Readable signs:** Pink contact patches appear beneath the relevant hull cells; an inspection panel names the exposed sample and reading progress. Completion produces a letter, followed by the visibly equipped extraction party; the later rival’s record cites that purchase.
- **Drawn from:** **Death Stranding’s cargo sensors** make carried material disclose a traveller’s location; **Biotech** makes genetic traits commercially transferable. Here, a landed home discloses biological material through actual contact, with permanent commercial consequences rather than a temporary tracking alert or portable genepack. [Death Stranding cargo sensors](https://www.kojimaproductions.jp/en/DSTips), [Biotech genetic commerce](https://ludeon.com/blog/2022/10/biotech-preview-3-reproduction-children-genetic-modification-release-date/).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike the **Rust Cathedral’s stowaway spies**, this involves no listening agent or hull pet: indifferent biological ingestion creates stock that an outside trader exploits.
- **Tier:** **RM_** — the Helix, physical contact and commercial disclosure stand alone; campaign Narrator commentary is optional.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 3. Two Hammers Make a Mouth

- **Marks it serves:** 7 — sound that changes play.
- **The player’s experience:** One compressor makes its ordinary wet thump; two nearby compressors working together gradually fall into the same beat. Their combined vibration makes the supporting gel yield, and trapped air starts whistling through stretched membrane around their foundations. You must desynchronize production through staggered batches, separation or different foundation arrangements before the workshop slumps. Ignoring the whistle can wreck foundations and spill valuable consignments directly into the reading surface; switching the machines off afterwards does not restore the lost goods.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Add pulse-emitter `ThingComps`, a liquefied `TerrainDef`, warning `SoundDef`s and damaged-foundation `ThingDef`s. Main hook: `MapComponentTick()` reads registered machinery work pulses and evaluates one resonance counter per nearby cluster; it writes warning stages, temporary terrain changes, building damage and exposed dropped stacks. Integrate pulse recording with the existing production work jobs; no waveform simulation or global ecology is needed. **Settings:** resonance toggle, coupling radius, buildup time, damage severity, sound volume and visual equivalents.
- **Readable signs:** Separate thumps become one conspicuous beat, followed by a thin whistle. Membrane-pink rings mark affected foundations, and captions reproduce the warning when audio is muted; engulfing or conversion retains the established readable signs.
- **Drawn from:** **Vintage Story’s mechanically powered helve hammer** makes rhythmic industrial work tangible; **Oxygen Not Included’s visco-gel** makes unusual fluid behaviour consequential to construction. This adds acoustic coupling that changes *ground support*, which neither feature provides. [Vintage Story helve hammer](https://wiki.vintagestory.at/Helve_hammer/en), [ONI visco-gel](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Visco-Gel_Fluid).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike **Stillsand’s geophone**, the **Cauldron’s vibration hunter**, or the **Rust Cathedral’s hum**, sound physically destabilizes a material; nothing hears, hunts or judges the colony.
- **Tier:** **RM_** — ordinary physical resonance, with no restored Assailant function or religious explanation.
- **Size:** **M**.

## 4. Mob’Unloo’s All-Due

- **Marks it serves:** 9 — a found Salvation rite.
- **The player’s experience:** In a drowned farm’s metal clearinghouse, you find an inscription explaining why its last traders gathered: “When the keeper dies, every account comes due.” Deciphering it opens a research row and teaches the clan a rite performable anywhere. Performing it publicly accelerates every eligible outstanding consignment debt to one settlement day, bringing armed collection parties to your current camp. You must find the pledged goods, finish unfinished orders and decide which obligations you can honour before collectors begin taking what their contracts permit.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Use inscription `ThingDef`, `ResearchProjectDef`, `PreceptDef`, `RitualBehaviorDef`, `RitualOutcomeEffectDef`, `QuestScriptDef` and collector `IncidentDef`s. Main hook: a custom `RitualOutcomeEffectWorker` snapshots active campaign consignment `QuestPart`s, permanently advances their deadlines and schedules collector arrivals. This is bounded quest bookkeeping, not a planetary debt simulation; require multiple genuine outstanding debts, permit one invocation per clan, and disable ordinary material ritual rewards. **Settings:** rite toggle, arrival window, collector escort strength, obligation eligibility.
- **Readable signs:** Before commitment, the ritual screen lists every affected debt, pledged object and arriving creditor. Letters announce each party; disputed cargo receives contract-coloured labels, and seizure happens through visible hauling jobs.
- **Drawn from:** **Frostpunk 2’s negotiated promises** turn commitments into future obligations; **Ideology’s rituals** assemble participants around a shared act. This makes existing obligations physically converge on one dangerous day rather than awarding a benefit for successful performance. [Frostpunk 2 promises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frostpunk_2), [Ideology rituals](https://ludeon.com/blog/2021/07/ideology-adds-social-roles-and-rituals/).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike the **Sump’s Mob’Unloo’s Price**, this offers no protection or postponement: the clan voluntarily brings its own enforceable obligations forward.
- **Tier:** **RUT_** — Salvation and Mob’Unloo belong exclusively to the campaign; the Narrator interprets the gathering, while cohesion comes from shared responsibility and favour never becomes a status or reward.
- **Size:** **L**.

## 5. First-Tear Lacquer

- **Marks it serves:** 2 — learned technology; scavenger-trader identity.
- **The player’s experience:** A margin outfitter demonstrates how a thumbstalk’s setting membrane preserves an impression, then teaches you to make an inert lacquer from local slime materials. Applied across a freight crate’s closure, it creates a distinctive translucent microtexture recorded on the seller’s manifest; the first breach fixes a membrane-pink fracture that repainting cannot reproduce. You compare seal and manifest before accepting expensive salvage or medicine consignments, and carry the evidence when a buyer alleges that you substituted the contents. Once learned, the lacquer works everywhere, turning careful custody into something your clan can sell alongside the goods.
- **How it works in RimWorld 1.6:** Add lacquer, manifest and freight-crate `ThingDef`s, `ResearchProjectDef` and manufacture/application `RecipeDef`s. Main hook: `CompCustodySeal` intercepts changes to its crate’s `ThingOwner` while sealed, reading seal identity and closure state; it writes the permanent first-breach record and static fracture variant. A custom trade window compares manifests and supports authored substitution disputes. Manufacturing destroys all living cells: the product stores physical impressions, contains no usable genes and performs no genetic extraction. **Settings:** technology toggle, ingredient cost, application labour, dispute frequency.
- **Readable signs:** Intact and breached seals have clearly different textures; comparison enlarges both the crate seal and original manifest. The UI reports evidence of opening, never magically names a culprit; a stolen intact crate remains stolen intact.
- **Drawn from:** **Death Stranding’s cargo-condition inspection** makes the state of a shipment consequential; **VFE Ancients’ sealed vaults** make a closure distinguish untouched contents from previously accessed ones. This adds portable, irreversible evidence of custody between traders rather than damage ratings or a loot-container category. [Death Stranding cargo condition](https://www.kojimaproductions.jp/en/DSTips), [VFE Ancients sealed vaults](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2654846754).
- **Why it is unique here:** Unlike the **Weeping Stones’ Dewsilk Casket**, this preserves evidence rather than life, and unlike the **Fever Wood’s pressure wedge**, it neither opens nor damages a closure.
- **Tier:** **RM_** — a complete local invention, exportable industry and trader practice using invented materials.
- **Size:** **M**.

**First: Qellum** — gives the existing giant a concrete commercial story and makes its established cutting and shrinking mechanics matter together.  
**Second: First-Tear Lacquer** — delivers the owner’s preferred local learning → portable technology, with a strong scavenger-trader use.  
**Then prioritize the landing disclosure** — it connects ship and living registry through an irreversible consequence, with bounded implementation scope.
