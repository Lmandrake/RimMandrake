BUGS FIXED:
 1. RM_KnockbackMath.Line allocated new List<>(cells) BEFORE its `cells <= 0` guard: a negative count threw ArgumentOutOfRange. Latent (Resolve guards first) but a public trap. Fixed.
 2. The map component's dedupe + per-explosion launch counter were wiped wholesale every 600 ticks (`now - dedupeTick > 600 -> Clear()`), so a blast whose wave straddled that tick
    forgot which (explosion, thing) pairs it had already queued and how many throws it had launched: a repeat callback threw the same thing twice and the per-explosion cap restarted
    (~1% of explosions by the 600-tick period over a few-tick wave). Replaced by KbExplosionLedger: two generations on a fixed window grid (entries live >= window, <= 2 x window), pure

PROVISIONAL: nothing new tuned; the 600-tick memory window is the old literal, now KbExplosionLedger.DefaultWindow.
Design observation: a thrown Item may come to rest ON a pawn's cell (items pass over pawn cells; only pawns stop on pawns). Probably intended (items stack under a pawn) - flagging only.

## Aftermath (C# engine: battle recorder, rule runner, prisoner clock, queue discipline; rule data lives in mandrake.rut.aftermath)
Had: BattleOutcomeClassifier already pure + a net472 selftest (18 checks: classifier + Def-shaped eligibility). Missing: any fuzz of the recorder / runner logic, mutation set, lint, rule-data checks.
Added: Source/Kernel/RM_AftermathKernel.cs (raider observation counts, close rule, prisoner clock, discipline, windows, eligibility; BattleRecord / AftermathRuleRunner / AftermathRuleEligibility delegate),
Source/SelfTestFuzz (classifier over every raid up to 12 + the 60% boundary, scripted raids polled like the recorder - walk-in / drop-pod / flee / die / downed, multi-map prisoner clocks with a negative control,
queue discipline sim, units), 33 mutations all caught (kernel 28 + classifier 5), lint_aftermath_defs.py (engine structure + the 8 shipped rules and 6 alliance pairs: enum names, payload incident exists,
delay sanity, rule-4 pairing, string.Format placeholder safety, alliance factions exist), 20 planted defects.
BUGS FIXED:
 1. Premature battle close on drop-pod raids. MapComponent_BattleRecorder closes a battle when every raider is "accounted for", and BattleRecord counted any raider that was not Spawned as having exited. Raiders still inside a
    drop pod are not Spawned, so a fallback poll (every 250 ticks) landing during the pod's flight closed the battle at once as ROUTED with every raider "survived and exited" - triggering rule 1 (regroup and return) and rule 3
    for a fight that had not started. Now a raider only counts as exited once seen on the map (or after ArrivalGraceTicks = 1000, PROVISIONAL). Found by the scripted-raid fuzz; the original behaviour is the mutation "unarrived raiders count as exited".
 2. Rule 4 (prisoner held >= 3 days) never fired with two or more maps. PollPrisoners dropped from tracking every pawn not in the CURRENT map's prisoner list, i.e. every other map's prisoners, on each map's pass, and re-added them with
    first-seen = now: every clock restarted every poll. Gravship/second-map colonies (Odyssey) never saw it. Now one pass over the union of all maps. The fuzz keeps a negative control that reproduces the per-map behaviour.
PROVISIONAL: ArrivalGraceTicks 1000 (a pod lands within a few hundred ticks; the number is a generous guess, not measured).
Design observations (not changed): (a) OnPayloadLanded matches the FIRST queued marker for the same faction and incident def, so with maxQueuedPerFaction > 1, or a vanilla RaidEnemy for that faction landing for another reason, the wrong rule's letter is sent and the right marker later lands silent;
(b) ResolveTargetFaction takes the FIRST alliance pair whose `a` matches and never reads RM_AlliancePairDef.weight (currently one pair per `a`, the lint holds that); (c) a raider who arrives and leaves between two 250-tick polls is never seen, so a very short raid classifies after the grace rather than at once.
