## Landmark defs (RUT_/Ishko)
- RUT_ComplexStructures: 'complex structures' — Not one ruin but a system of them: blocks, conduits and outbuildings still wired to each other long after whatever ran them stopped. Survey teams mark ground like this because it takes days to walk and longer to understand — whatever was built here was built to work together, and a great deal of it is still standing.
- RUT_LightlessSink: 'lightless sink' — The ground gives way here and does not give it back - a hollow the light never finds the bottom of. Ishko does not hide in it. Ishko is simply already there, and always was.
- RUT_ShadowedOverhang: 'shadowed overhang' — Rock leaning far enough over itself that noon never reaches the ground beneath it. A patient watcher can stand under this stone for a whole day and be seen by no one who was not already looking - which is the whole of Ishko's doctrine, cut into the world.
- RUT_ColdLavaTube: 'cold lava tube' — A vein the world's fire used to run through, long since gone out. Sh'kaar's heat left it whole tile-lengths ago; what is left is a tunnel of his old work, claimed cold, claimed dark, claimed - like everything eventually is - by Ishko.

## FactionDef labels/descriptions (ours)
- RUT_Jawa_AscendantHelix ('Ascendant Helix'): A small, obscenely wealthy gene-cult that believes the body is a rough draft and the species is a project — and despises the manufactured underclass it made itself.\n\nIt does not raid. It retrieves. What it comes for is usually a person, and it has already decided which one.
- RUT_Jawa_DeepwaterCompact ('Deepwater Compact'): The amphibian peoples who live in the deep water and sell it to everyone, including the Empire that is hunting you. Their neutrality is a monopoly with teeth.\n\nTheir wardens cannot follow you inland, and both of you know it. Everything the Compact wants from the desert, it buys.
- RUT_Jawa_FreeDroidEnclaves ('Free Droid Enclaves'): Battle droids abandoned after the war who woke up and decided they belong to themselves. They settle on water and crack it for fuel, so an attacker arrives thirsty at a source it cannot drink.\n\nThey do not take prisoners and they do not hold grudges. What they hold is the water, and a memory of being property that they intend never to repeat.
- RUT_Jawa_GeonosianFoundryHive ('Geonosian Foundry Hive'): A hive mass-producing droids in ancient factories under the rock, ruled by winged aristocrats under one immobile queen.\n\nIt is the only power on the planet that can sustain a siege in deep desert, because it does not need to bring water — it brings a line that never stops walking.
- RUT_Jawa_HuttCartel ('Hutt Cartel'): Wealthy, decentralised and entirely transactional. The Cartel owns the oases and sells water at whatever the buyer can be made to pay, and it holds the only non-Imperial orbital node on the planet — the door off-world, and the reason everyone keeps taking its calls.\n\nIt is hostile the way a creditor is hostile. Nothing about it is personal, and everything about it is negotiable.
- RUT_Jawa_Junkers ('the Junkers'): The bottom of the scrap heap, given weapons and a grudge. Scavengers who arrive second and kill whoever arrived first, welded into warcaskets cut off other people's bodies.\n\nThere is no doctrine down there, only the ladder. Which means there is always somebody who would rather be paid than climb it.
- RUT_Jawa_IndigenousTribes ('Jawa Trade Moot'): Clans of the deep sand who never took a ship. They keep the same nine gods and the same ledger as the crew of the Kolyska, and they will trade with anyone who does not look down at them for long. What they cannot buy they scavenge; what they cannot scavenge they remember the location of, and sell.
- RUT_Jawa_WildsteamClan ('Wildsteam Clan'): A forest people on the wrong planet, hard-sited to the few cool upland springs and holding a covenant that treats every living thing as kin. Devastating at home and near-useless anywhere else.\n\nThey come down out of the highlands for two reasons only: to trade, and to take somebody's slaves back off them.
