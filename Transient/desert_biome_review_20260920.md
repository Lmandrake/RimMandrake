## 1. The verdict in five lines

1. **Neither biome is shippable on the NEXT cold load, for a sequencing reason, not a design one.** The rewired biome tables (`fda35ff14`, deployed 2026-09-20 10:28 — deployed file byte-identical to repo, MEASURED by `diff`) name **16 `RSW_` defs that do not exist in the deployed SWBestiary** (all 6 wild plants of both biomes + 8 fauna in RUT_Desert + 4 in RUT_ExtremeDesert; parsed every def in the live `Mods/SWBestiary`, `Mods/Droidworks`, `Mods/UtinniPatches`, 2,248 defs). 2. **RUT_Desert (2,390 tiles) is, once that is fixed, a real place**: 57 fauna rows, 45 of them the roster's own ruled species, a food web the sheet asked for, and 5 plants. Its remaining defects are quality (donor placeholder art on 13 of 62 rows, a wrong texture on surra grass, forageability that does nothing) and the sheet's signature content (ultracactus, staggerseed, shade-whale, shade grid) having no def and no item.
3. **RUT_ExtremeDesert (3,969 tiles, the planet's largest biome) is thin PAST the sheets' intent, not at it.** One plant species is the sheets' austerity (they want "a bucket" of standing crop) — but the sheets want that bucket to be **glass-nub light-pipes and silverboles**, and it is a donor bloddle with an owed eye-test. The fauna table's two heaviest rows are unruled Fall-Line vermin; the biome's one ruled subsurface-strike predator was removed and never replaced; 3 of the roster's 16 rows (all at 0.5) were never wired.

Caravans crossing 2,390 tiles forage nothing and the World tab hides the line. RUT_ExtremeDesert's 0.0 is deliberate and correct.
5. **Structurally both defs are missing the same 5 vanilla fields** (`allowedPackAnimals`, `foragedFood`, `wildPlantsCareAboutLocalFertility`, `settlementSelectionWeight`/`campSelectionWeight`, `terrainPatchMakers`); only `allowedPackAnimals` and `foragedFood` change what a player sees (§4). Gap list: **18 rows** (§5).

## 3. The numbers

All engine facts below are MEASURED from the decompiled 1.6 source via RimSage on 2026-09-20 (`WildPlantSpawner.GetDesiredPlantsCountAt`, `WildAnimalSpawner.DesiredAnimalDensity`/`DesiredTotalAnimalWeight`, `ForagedFoodPerDayCalculator`, `BiomeDef` field defaults). Vanilla values from `Data/Core/Defs/BiomeDefs/Biomes_WarmArid.xml`, parsed.

| field | RUT_Desert | vanilla Desert | RUT_ExtremeDesert | vanilla ExtremeDesert | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| animalDensity | 0.4 | 0.4 | 0.1 | 0.1 | **right** for both — see below |
| plantDensity | 0.05 | 0.05 | 0.008 | 0.002 | Desert right; ExtremeDesert is already **4× vanilla**, the def comment's "donor's own near-zero" was a GRiNDTerra patch value, not Core |
| forageability | 0.25 | 0.25 | 0.0 | 0 | Desert's is **inert** (no `foragedFood`); ExtremeDesert's is right |
| wildPlantRegrowDays | 35 | 35 | 35 | 35 | right (default is 25; both deserts slow it, as vanilla does) |
| diseaseMtbDays | 90 | 90 | 90 | 90 | right |
| movementDifficulty | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | right |

**What plantDensity means on the ground.** Desired plants per cell = `plantDensity × mutatorFactor × fertility × baseFertility` (i.e. fertility enters SQUARED via `GetBaseDesiredPlantsCountAt` × `num2`), capped at 1. Sand fertility is **0.10** (Core `Terrain_Natural.xml`; SoftSand is 0). So on a pure-Sand cell RUT_ExtremeDesert wants `0.008 × 0.1 × 0.1 = 0.00008` plants per cell → **~5 plants on a 250×250 map (62,500 cells)**, before mutators. That IS "the standing crop would fit in a bucket" — the number is the sheets' intent, and the previous seat's worry ("possibly past the intent after the Stikehr cut") is not about density, it is about **species count and species identity** (one donor bloddle vs. the sheets' light-pipes + silverboles). Recommendation: **leave plantDensity at 0.008**; the fix is the two signature plants, not the dial. RUT_Desert on Sand: `0.05 × 0.01 = 0.0005`/cell → ~31 plants on pure sand, plus real growth on the Soil patches (fertility 1.0 → 0.05/cell). Meagre-but-regular, as desert.md §1 asks. **Leave at 0.05.**

⚠️ **But `RSW_Dunegrass` (surra grass, the top-weighted RUT_Desert plant at 0.6) carries `fertilityMin 0.30`** (`RSW_DesertPortMisc_Plants.xml`), so it can never grow on Sand (0.10) — only on the Soil patches (`terrainsByFertility` puts Soil only above fertility 0.8). Vanilla `Plant_Grass` is `fertilityMin 0.05`. The other five plants carry no `fertilityMin` (default 0.05) and grow on sand. Net effect: 60% of the plant weight is confined to soil islands; on sand the biome shows chak-root, hubba gourd, vellara bloom and dommo tree only. That may even suit "the long shade" (grass in the dew-line halo), but it was not a ruling — the roster says "meagre alien grass, trimmed 2.2→0.6", nothing about confining it. Flagged as a decision, not a defect (§5 row 12).

**What animalDensity means on the ground.** `DesiredTotalAnimalWeight = mapArea / (10000 / density)` = 62,500 × 0.1 / 10,000 = **6.25 ecosystem-weight units** for RUT_ExtremeDesert, **25** for RUT_Desert, scaled by the fraction of the table whose species tolerate the map's temperature (`SeasonAcceptableFor`). ⚠️ That temperature scaling is the number I could not check: at +48 to +70 °C (dune_sea §2), every species whose `ComfyTemperatureMax` is below the map's max is excluded from `num`, shrinking desired density proportionally. If most of the 23 RUT_ExtremeDesert rows are heat-intolerant donor ports, the effective density could be a fraction of 0.1 — see §6. Vanilla ExtremeDesert 0.1 / Desert 0.4 are the right values for the sheets' "sparse to the point of discomfort" and "small and steady"; **no change recommended**, but the tolerance join (`ANIMAL_TOLERANCES_JOIN_BROKEN_1`, resolved today) is what makes the number real.

**wildPlantRegrowDays 35** on both: right. Plants regrow slower than the 25-day default; the sheets' "no fast growth" (deep_desert ban 4) would argue for HIGHER on RUT_ExtremeDesert (e.g. 60), but with ~5 plants per map the dial is invisible. Not worth a ticket.
