## Defects
FIXED while extracting:
- `RM_SandLeviathan.cs:474` - a visit whose entry cell was unusable with no fallback cell was dropped SILENTLY after the letter had promised a muurrok (violates "no animal vanishes without a readable sign"; `EndNeedsSign(ArriveFailed)`). Now posts "The rumble under the sand dies away...". Fuzz: 12,865 of 211,475 visits end this way at scale 25.
- `RM_DuneGale.cs:497-505` - `carried++` ran before the off-map and blocked-at-first-step tests, so the gale log's "carried N" counted pawns that never moved. Now counts only an actual carry (off the map or moved).
REPORTED, not changed (design or other owner):
- **STILLSAND_MUURROK_EMERGENCE_1 (cause not proven, one strong candidate).** `RM_SandLeviathan.cs:519-545` + `Dive` (~L665): a muurrok that arrives OFF swim ground starts its hard-ground dive `30 x (hardGroundGiveUpTicks/30 + 1)` = 630 ticks after arrival (RM_MuurrokEmergence uses 600), seeks sand at Sprint and is `DeSpawn`ed by `Dive` as soon as it is on sand (or after 1,260 ticks); the fuzz proves the exact bounds (an always-off-sand visitor lives exactly 1,890 ticks; one that finds sand lives 630 to 1,890). The arrival is 1,500 (+<=30) ticks after the letter, so it is gone between about +2,130 and +3,390: the harness count at +2,600 can read 0 although the pawn did arrive. `TryFindEntryCell` (L249-270) only PREFERS a sand edge cell (60 edge samples). Decide mod vs harness by reading the `Dive` message ("has gone down into the sand") in the log, or by counting at +1,600..+2,000. The visit's other silent exits are the (now fixed) arrival failure and the killed / despawned drop (no sign needed).
- **STILLSAND_ROOF_COVER_EXPOSURE_1 = STALE CACHE, in CreatureBehaviors (not Stillsand).** `RM_MapComponent_ShadeGrid.cs:72,326`: exposure is only recomputed every `RecomputeIntervalTicks = 2000` ticks (or when gear / a light source registers, L356-440); placing a roof does NOT request a recompute, and `ExposureAt` (L535) reads `exposure[i]` from the last recompute. A read within 2,000 ticks of `set_roof_batch` returns the pre-roof 1.00. The validation step has to wait past the next multiple of 2000 (or the grid should request a recompute on roof change); stepping "at least 1 tick" is not enough.
- `RM_WaterLedger.cs:128-141` - an opening is edge-triggered: a threshold crossing refused by the 30,000-tick throttle is never retried (the debt stays above the threshold and no serial is bumped until it falls and crosses again). 4,166 of 8,426 crossings in the default fuzz were refused. Other opening sources (gale end, krayt kill) exist, so it is not a stall, but the ritual can wait on a debt that is already past its threshold.
- `RM_DuneGale.cs:938` (`Return`) - the pawn is removed from `WorldPawns` BEFORE the "map == null / missing" check, so a living carried pawn is released and never returned when there is no player home map.
- `RM_DuneGale.cs:648-650` (`Emerge`) - a skeleton row sends its letter even when `TryFindSpot` finds no place (nothing is placed).
- Diagonal winds: `ExitEdge` / `ReturnEdge` always pick east or west, but 18,134 of 61,352 fuzzed diagonal carry-offs left through the north or south edge; the return enters on the east / west edge while the letter says "from the northeast". Cosmetic.
- Owed art: `RM_Drazzik.xml:198,213,227` `swimmingGraphicData` points at `Things/Pawn/Animal/RM_Drazzik/RM_Drazzik_Swimming`; only `_east/_north/_south` of the normal body exist, so the half-buried drazzik will draw a missing-texture frame (owner ruling 2026-10-04, LONGSHADE_SHEET_STRUCTURAL_RULINGS_1).
