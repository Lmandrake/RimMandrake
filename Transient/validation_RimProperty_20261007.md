# RimProperty validation (Approach B), 2026-10-07 - DONE, nothing committed

RimProperty is the ownership fabric (claim ledger, claim resolution, event spine, witness / suspicion record) plus the silver verbs on top of it (salvage claim fee, walkable commerce, bribe, hire the placeless, pickpocket, animal theft). It has no rent or property accounting; the "money" invariant is silver conservation across the four paying menus. An older net472 selftest existed (`selftest_property_fabric.py`, 23/23 still pass) that copies the comparator "byte-for-byte"; this work replaces the copy with the production function.

## Defect fixed
- Pay-then-grant race in all four paying menus (salvage claim, buy, bribe, hire): the fee and the carried silver were checked when the menu was BUILT; the click ran `RemoveSilverFromInventory(actor, fee)` (which takes whatever is there, up to the fee) and then granted the claim / purchase / bribe. A pawn who dropped, hauled or spent silver between right-click and click paid less than the fee, down to nothing, and still got the claim. The fuzz reproduced it in the first run of the pre-fix click model ("paid 60 for a fee of 111", "paid 0 for a fee of 37"). Now `SalvageClaimFeeUtility.TryPaySilver` re-checks `CanPay` at click time and the delegate stops with a rejection message otherwise; lint `rp-money` fails if a paying delegate goes back to `RemoveSilverFromInventory` or ignores a failed `TryPaySilver` (planted both: caught).

## Observations, not changed
- Silver is destroyed, not paid: no payee, no faction wealth; a purchase from a settlement's stock pays nobody. Conservation holds (pawn silver + destroyed = start + minted) after every step of the fuzz.
- Ownership by possession: a stolen claim is recorded at strength 1.0, decays linearly over the thing's recognizability-scaled life (3 to 3650 days at the default multiplier), and the holder's situational claim (0.9) overtakes it after 10% of that life: 0.3 days for a plain thing, 365 days for the most recognizable. Lint WARN prints the horizon.
- Two claims at the same strength, claimant kind and tick (two Pawn records written in one tick) tie and `List.Sort` (unstable) picks either; the fuzz shows it on 313 of 2,413 purchases because it advances time in small steps, and asserts the loser is exactly such a tie and nothing weaker.
- `walkableCommerceMarkup` slider goes down to 0.5: buy at half market value. Lint WARN.
- Pickpocket and the animal thief fire the Take event BEFORE moving the item (necessarily: once carried the thief holds the situational claim and the act would read authorized); if the transfer or carry then fails, a theft is already recorded. Practically unreachable for the inventory transfer, reachable for a blocked animal carry.
- A zero-value item is never picked even with the pocket threshold slider at 0 (`BeatsBest` starts from 0).

## Fuzz
`python3 src/RimMandrake/Utils/selftest_property_fuzz.py [--fuzz-scale F] [--fuzz-seed N] [--fuzz-only ledger|suspicion|tables]`
Project `Source/SelfTest/Fuzz/RimMandrakeProperty.Fuzz.csproj` (net8.0, the one kernel; separate from the older net472 selftest in the folder above).
- ledger (seeds 1..5000; 30-200 actions): six pawns (factions -1..2, ghosts after a load), five things (stack limit 1 / 75 / 500, market value 0..2500, quality, named, mechanoid, possessor, owner faction), silver stacks; actions: time, menu open / click (buy, salvage claim, bribe), drop and gain silver, take / strip (unauthorized and authorized), use / sabotage, loot, gift, possess, change owner faction, wipe, settings flips (perception, lifetime x0.25 / 1 / 4, half-life 5 / 45 / 180, fee multiplier, markup, dampen, bribe fee), ghosting. Per step: the production resolution (candidates through the kernel, sorted with `Order`) agrees with a brute-force double-precision spec of "strongest live claim, then more specific claimant, then more recent"; the winner's strength is in (0, 1]; stored records only carry the exception bases at full initial strength; an authorized take writes nothing, an unauthorized take of a claimed thing writes exactly the prior owner's Stolen record at 1.0 and the thief gets none; use / sabotage write nothing; loot writes one or two records; a purchase writes exactly one Purchased record and the buyer holds the winning claim unless another Pawn claim of equal strength was written this tick; a paid claim writes exactly one ClaimFeePaid record; a payment that cannot be made at click time changes nothing; the silver taken equals the fee exactly and total silver (pawns + destroyed) equals start + minted; the wipe keeps exactly the exact claimant and, for a faction's Commons, its known pawns, in order; suspicion stays within 0..1.
- suspicion (4000; 20-140): witnesses (Pawn and Commons suspects, confidences 0 / 0.1 / 0.75 / 1), time, half-life flips, prune, queries, bribes (fractions -1 / 0 / 0.35 / 1 / 2): the total equals the closed form `sum conf x min(1, days/3) x max(0, 1 - days/halfLife)` clamped, reading it before or after a prune gives the same number (an expired entry weighs zero), a prune removes exactly the entries past the half-life, only Pawn suspects are recorded, dampening never raises suspicion, a full bribe reaches zero and a non-positive one changes nothing.
- tables: decay (lifetime vs the closed form, monotone in recognizability, strength linear and non-increasing, zero at the lifetime, a longer life never weakens a claim), comparator vs a lexicographic spec and antisymmetric, spine / authorization / ghost / virtual-basis tables over all acts, enum constants, silver removal conservation over random stackings, fees (salvage fee vs `(5 + 345 recog (0.2 + 0.8 claim)) x mult`, floors, monotone in both, unclaimed priced as claim 0), price formula and monotonicity, configured fee rounding to even, pocket pick (first of the most valuable, threshold inclusive), animal-theft gates, recognizability vs the closed form and monotone in quality / value / name.
- Seeds: default 5000 / 4000 / 6x40 (3 s, 9k cases, 0.9M steps). `--fuzz-scale 40`: 200,000 ledger + 160,000 suspicion seeds, 36.3M steps, 111 s, 0 failures; 95.9k purchases, 188k paid claims, 183k bribes, 15.5k payments refused at click, 1.17M unauthorized takes of owned things, 2.3M witnesses, 790k wipes (blind guard fails the run if any path is zero). A first scale-40 run found three failures that were spec noise, not mod defects: float quantisation (a long-lived claim's decay is finer than a float step near 1.0, so claims a few ticks apart tie and the specificity rule applies), and near-zero strengths the double spec dropped; the tie check now judges the kernel's own float strengths and the independent spec keeps a 1e-4 tolerance.
