- Design observations for the owner (pinned by the fuzz, not changed): (1) `ShadeAt` searches two cells but only counts a score >= 0.5, so a shade caster two cells away NEVER makes a cell shaded - effective rule is "roofed, or next to a caster" (1485 of 2000 random grids contained a two-cells-off-only caster); the 2-cell search is dead range and the shade floor/excellent numbers are tuned to adjacency; (2) the machine is a 2x2, `CellRect.CenterCell` of an even footprint is its upper-right cell, so the pool zone and every ring sit half a cell toward +x/+z (scoring and growth agree, so nothing misfires); (3) a finished ring drops leftover progress (`rungProgressTicks = 0`) - at 250-tick steps that is under 0.01% of a ring; (4) at defaults a full 9-ring oasis takes ~225 days at speed 1 (150 at excellent) - PROVISIONAL tuning.
- NightsideIce mutation proof: `Utils/mutate_nightsideice_fuzz.py` (81 kernel mutations). First run 76 caught / 6 missed; the misses were real gaps and are closed in `NightsideIceFuzz.cs`: `Touches`/`Score` were only exercised through standable cells (now unit-pinned incl. the footprint interior), the path search had no exact oracle for LIMITED budgets (now an independent mirrored queue search must match the kernel path cell for cell at budgets 1..100000), and the test grid reported out-of-bounds cells as unwalkable so the kernel's own InBounds test could be deleted unnoticed (the grid now reads outside the map as standable ice, as the engine's `Standable` does not promise a safe "no").
