## Defects
- FIXED (latent) `ProcessPendingOpens` removed a closed well's pending re-opening even when `FindWellSiteCell` found no site, so each such miss permanently shrank the sea's wells (3-6 at start, never replenished). A pending opening now waits for the next pass until a site exists. Fuzz plants the old behaviour and catches it.
- NOTE (design, not changed) The waning ramp's fourth step is unreachable: `WaningStep` clamps to 0..3 and the colour blend divides by 3, but step 3 needs age == lifespan, which is already Closed. A well's glow bottoms at 60% (0.6 x 6 radius) and its colour stops two thirds of the way to cool-dead before it closes.
- NOTE (design, not changed) In frozen cadence (`twilightDriftCadence == 0`) wells never age but a closed well's pending replacement still opens when due (unchanged behaviour, now covered).
- NOTE (design, not changed) With both lamp-watch toggles off the mod clears the burn clocks but not the watched / scraped / answered latches, so a lamp that was scraped before the toggle will not be scraped again after re-enabling until it goes dark once.
- NOTE `Rewind` floors the clock at 0 but the next-door threshold at 2.5 days, so a long chipping streak resets the "first door" delay as well.
