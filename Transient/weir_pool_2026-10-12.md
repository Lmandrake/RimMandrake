# WEIR_POOL_DIAGNOSE_DAMMED_AT_THE_EDGE — offline fix (BENCH subagent, 2026-10-12)

## Cause (mechanism READ from source; tile 825's own grid shape INFERRED, not measured)
- Vanilla `TileMutatorWorker_River` (end of the mutator) sets each river cell's flow to the AVERAGE of the centreline
  segment tangents within reach: flow runs ALONG the river, never toward the centre or the bank.
- `RM_MapComponent_RiverCurrent.EnsureGrid` quantises that to 8 compass dirs (`RM_RiverMath.Quantise`, 45-degree sectors).
- Old `RM_Building_BankWeir.Upstream`: needs a neighbour whose quantised dir steps EXACTLY onto the cell, else the cell
  straight behind it along the cell's own dir. On a bank-edge wet cell of a river not aligned to the 8 axes, the exact
  step lands beside the cell and the cell behind is bank -> invalid on step 1 -> pool cells=0.
- Offline measure (SelfTest, synthetic rasterised rivers at 9 angles, +-12 deg wander): the old rule pools 0 at
  130 of 864 bank-edge sites (15%); axis-aligned rivers never fail, which is why earlier tiles pooled 14/16/23.

## Fix
- `RM_RiverMath.UpstreamStep/UpstreamWalk` (Verse-free): upstream = the in-flow neighbour whose step onto the cell best
  aligns with the local flow (unit(cell dir) + unit(neighbour dir)), cos >= 0.38 (within ~67.5 deg); lateral, downstream
  and opposed-flow neighbours never qualify; the walk never revisits a cell; length = weirPoolLength (unchanged).
  Straight rivers walk exactly the old path. Pool semantics kept: spine of weirPoolLength cells + 2 lateral wet cells each.
- `AddPoolCells` now calls the walk; old `Upstream` deleted.
- `ProofWeirPool` appends `wetCell= wetDir= nbr=dx,dz:dir|... oldRuleFinds= newFirstStep=` (space-free kv tokens).
