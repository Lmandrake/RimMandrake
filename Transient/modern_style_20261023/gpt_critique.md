**3. Guide corrections**

- **Choose one network now.** White PVC is my first choice on the supplied desert ground: strongest light–dark separation, clear socket silhouettes, and a useful link to the white tank/IBC anchors. Black HDPE ranks second: strong on light sand, weaker in dark soil and shadows. Mid-grey ranks third here: too close to the ground’s value. This is an art-direction judgment; verify with actual 32-pixel renders.
- **White needs restraint.** Use warm white and broad shading, not luminous pure white. At the stated 0.136-cell width, the pipe is only **about 4.4 screen pixels thick**. Printed specifications, galvanised spangle and mould seams cannot carry recognition.
- **Fix the screenshot’s repeated rhythm.** Regular bright collars make the run look segmented. Straight lengths should remain visually continuous; enlarge fittings only at actual connections.
- **S5 contradicts the object list.** Require a shared connection standard and pipe diameter. Brass taps, metal hangers, stainless bolts and grey escutcheons can coexist with white PVC.
- **Remove generator alternatives.** “Rotor or impact,” “electric or gas,” and three pipe materials invite inconsistent outputs. Select one product per asset. The impact sprinkler has the clearest small-scale silhouette.
- **Correct the pump premise.** An ordinary jet pump does not become credible by adding four equivalent casing ports. Use a recognisable pump plus a separate four-direction manifold.
- **Do not infer hose colour from reel colour.** A red reel does not require red hose. Preserve coherent hose diameter, coupling standard and flattened/inflated construction.
- **Replace the seawall assumption.** A removable weir board should reference stoplogs; sheet piling references a retaining wall. Confirm the mechanism before drawing.
- **Relax material bans.** Modern products can contain cast iron, bolts and concrete. Industrial separation should come from mass, construction and proportions.
- **Drop yellow gas-line stripes from water prompts.** Do not use functional identification markings as decoration.
- **“Same shop aisle” is too literal.** These anchors belong to the same present-day environment; plumbing, outdoor electrical equipment and furniture need different forms.

**4. Industrial = brutalist: three changes**

1. **Change mass and silhouette:** thick slab housings, squat plinths, deep recesses, massive piers and chunky structural sections. Establish weight before surface detail.
2. **Change construction:** rough cast concrete for civil structures, coarse cast-metal pump/valve bodies, heavy welded plate and deeply recessed connections. Keep concrete appropriate to the object.
3. **Remove the safety-theme shorthand:** strip most hazard bands, decorative rivets and repeated flange jewellery. Use restrained mineral greys, broad rough shading and sparse functional hardware; reserve warning paint for actual hazards.
