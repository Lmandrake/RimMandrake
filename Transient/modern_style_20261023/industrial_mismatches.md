# Industrial look vs "brutalist" (owner 2026-10-23) — mismatches, not redrawn

Owner: *"Modern should instead be thought of as "modern consumer / commercial" and industrial is "brutalist""*.
Brutalist = raw board-marked concrete, massive cast iron, heavy plate steel, monolithic blocky forms, weight over finish.
Today's Industrial reads as "industrial safety" (black steel + yellow/black hazard stripes). Composites: inventory/inventory_<group>.png, Industrial column.
