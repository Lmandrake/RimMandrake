## UI/UX notes
- Reel: not rotatable, yet its exit side decides every hose's shape (D2); a player cannot tell which way it will leave until it loops.
- "Liquid tap" name promises a faucet on a pipe; it is a ground bleed well (D6). C7's text repeats the promise.
- Machine digging OFF: the dig WorkGiver silently deletes dig-to-depth designations nobody may dig (job_probe designationDeletedByWorkGiver=True for colonist and Tunneler); with it ON the colonist leaves the 2 m+ order standing. An order that vanishes with no message reads as a bug; a "needs machine labour" inspect line (owed) would explain both.
- HoseProbe lay onto a tank's own cell answers "target blocked" (the adjacent cell works). Not checked through the player's Deploy-hose targeting: if a click on the tank itself is refused there too, the player is left guessing where to click.
- Unpowered pump: the vanilla no-power bolt sits over the flange cross and hides where the hose/pipe joins (GPT #2).
- Pipe pole span reads as a power line (D5): a player will assume it carries power.
- GSS (not FlowWorks): a lone 6-cell PowerConduit run renders as a looping coloured tangle 2-3 cells wide (`sheet_girth.jpg`), so there is no vanilla conduit on this tier to compare girth with.
- Keysheet/signs: stale girth DEFERRED rows (see above).
