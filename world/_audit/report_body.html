Post-freeze verification — 2026-09-11

Question: does the frozen authored artifact world/ASHKARR_WORLDMAP_tiles.csv still describe the planet that ships inside the savegame? Offline only — no bridge, no game. Everything below was read straight out of the .rws with xml.etree plus zlib.decompress(data, -15) over the PlanetLayer grids, and biome shortHashes were resolved through the DefDump defs.sqlite capture of 2026-09-10.

Which save this is a verification of

The item named WORLDMAP_V24_consolidated_names_2026-09-09.rws. That file is not in Saves/; it sits in Saves_archive_2026-09-09/. It was measured, and it is not the planet the frozen CSV records — 2,834 tiles disagree on biome. The live canonical save Saves/CANONICAL_ASHKARR_2026-09-09.rws disagrees on zero. V24 predates three passes the CSV already carries (the Blue Desert rename, the desert-band repaint, and WORLD_BOUNDARY_LAND_AT_SEA_ELEVATION_1). The CSV is a record of CANONICAL, not of V24, and the rest of this section verifies against CANONICAL and says so on every line.

UNMEASURED offline — needs the live game

  • River tiles. canon.yml asserts rivers_tiles 254 and carries no _src for it — the only planet figure in the block with no provenance at all. Offline the save yields 326 river edges over 217 distinct origin tiles (Creek 170 · River 62 · HugeRiver 51 · LargeRiver 43), and the CSV's river_flow column is non-zero on 298 tiles. None of the three is 254. The engine's own “tiles with a river” is origins ∪ adjacency, and tileRiverAdjacencyDeflate stores a neighbour index, not a tile ID — resolving it needs the icosphere topology the engine builds at runtime. Not guessed here. One jawa/world_* read settles it and should also give rivers_tiles the citation it has never had.
  • Whether the shortHash table is truly the save's own. Calibration is good but not conclusive. The DefDump capture is 577 mods (6fdca6f582164e2a, 2026-09-10); CANONICAL's meta declares 573, V24 declares 589. Not the same mod set, and the skill is explicit that the table must come from one that is. In favour of the reading: all 29 distinct biome hashes resolved, 0 unknown, and the resulting names matched the CSV on 21,872 of 21,872 tiles — an outcome a drifted table cannot plausibly produce, since drift moves a whole biome wholesale. Against it: agreement is not proof when the failure mode could be shared. The biome reading is MEASURED; the guarantee is not. A dump captured against the canonical mod list would close it.
  • Whether the planet loads clean. Nothing offline can see Could not load reference to Scribe errors, mutator legality, or landmark validity. Untouched by this pass.
  • Mutators. 27,870 (tile, def) pairs are present in tileMutatorTilesDeflate / tileMutatorDefsDeflate and were not decoded — the CSV has no mutator column, so there is nothing offline to verify them against. world/ASHKARR_WORLDMAP_mutators.csv is a separate artifact with its own currency question.

Verdict

The shipped planet and the frozen record are the same planet. Every field the engine actually reads — biome, elevation, hilliness, temperature, rainfall, swampiness — agrees on all 21,872 tiles against CANONICAL_ASHKARR_2026-09-09.rws, and the freeze hash is current. The two mismatches are both in bookkeeping: the CSV's region column missed a rename pass, and canon.yml's planet census still describes a lineage that was deprecated on 2026-09-07. Neither changes a tile of the world. The 2026-08-26 audit is superseded by this section.
